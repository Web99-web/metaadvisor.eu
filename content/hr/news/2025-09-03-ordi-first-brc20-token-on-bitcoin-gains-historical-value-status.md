**ORDI** is the very first **BRC-20 token** ever created on the **Bitcoin blockchain**. Initially launched as a mere experiment, it has grown into a symbol of historical value within the crypto ecosystem.  

The token was introduced in March 2023 by developer **domo**, who unveiled the BRC-20 standard using **Ordinal Inscriptions**. As a proof-of-concept, he minted ORDI with a total supply of **21 million tokens** – mirroring Bitcoin’s maximum supply.  

Although ORDI has limited practical use, it is widely recognized as a **pioneer** and one of the first attempts to bring fungible tokens to Bitcoin. For this reason, it is often compared to **CryptoPunks** on Ethereum – first-of-their-kind assets that later became collectibles of immense value.  

Today, ORDI trades on major exchanges with a market capitalization in the billions. Beyond utility, ORDI represents a **historical artifact** and a reminder of the innovation that sparked the BRC-20 ecosystem.

ORDI demonstrates that in crypto, value can also come from **pioneering and historical significance**. If the BRC-20 standard continues to grow, ORDI could cement its place as one of Bitcoin’s most important “crypto artifacts.”
