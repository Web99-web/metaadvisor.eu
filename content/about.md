**MetaAdvisor** is a small, independent project focused on **technology, AI and crypto**.

Why we started  
Most people still find crypto and modern finance confusing. Our goal is to explain them **simply and clearly** — without jargon, with short summaries and helpful links.

How we write  
- Global news + our short commentary (“what matters & why”).  
- Multi-language publishing: **English, German, Croatian**.  
- Concise format: quick facts first, details below.

What you can expect  
- Daily highlights (prices, major moves, policy, AI x crypto).  
- Beginner-friendly explainers.  
- Occasional deep dives when a trend really matters.
