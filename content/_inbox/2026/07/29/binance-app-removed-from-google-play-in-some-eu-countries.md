Binance’s Android app is unavailable on Google Play in some EU markets amid scrutiny over MiCA compliance.

Update (July 29, 10:50 AM UTC): An earlier version of this article included information about the Binance app’s availability on Google Play in Poland that could not be independently verified. The reference has been removed.

The Binance app has become unavailable on the Google Play Store in certain European Union countries amid questions over the exchange’s compliance with the bloc’s crypto licensing framework.

A user in Spain confirmed to Cointelegraph on Monday that the Binance app was no longer visible in Google Play searches, although it remained available through Oppo’s App Market, an Android app store used on Oppo devices. A separate user in Latvia also confirmed that the app no longer appeared on Google Play.

A Binance spokesperson told Cointelegraph that the company was aware Google Play had updated its policies, affecting the availability of crypto app updates in “certain markets,” and that it was working with Google on a solution. The spokesperson did not identify the markets where the app’s availability changed or specify which policy updates led to the restrictions.

The first reports of Binance’s Android app disappearing from Google Play in Europe surfaced last week, when OKX Europe CEO Erald Ghoos claimed on X that the app had been removed due to Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) licensing requirements.

The reports came weeks after Binance withdrew its MiCA application in Greece , shortly before the framework’s transitional period ended on July 1. The company later notified some EU users that access to certain services would be restricted , while withdrawals would remain available.
