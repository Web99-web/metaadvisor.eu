Today in crypto, Real Trump Coins denied launching or authorizing the Trump Digital GOLD token, blaming “third-party bad actors” for its promotion. Elsewhere, Polygon disclosed previously private security flaws patched through recent hard forks, Stellar’s tokenized real-world asset market climbed to nearly $4 billion, and tokenized stock activity surged over the last 30 days.

Real Trump Coins denied launching, promoting or authorizing the Trump Digital GOLD token, blaming “third-party bad actors” after the Solana-based token appeared on its X account and RealTrumpCoins.com.

“Trump Coins has not authorized and will not launch, promote, or authorize any digital token,” the company said, adding that it was working with authorities to investigate.

The X posts promoting GOLD were later deleted, and the account links to a separate website, TrumpCoins.com. However, it had directed customers to RealTrumpCoins.com as recently as Aug. 25, and that site was still promoting GOLD at the time of publication.

Trump also continued to follow the Real Trump Coins X account, one of 53 accounts he followed on the platform.

Polygon disclosed several previously private security vulnerabilities that could have disrupted its proof-of-stake network after deploying fixes through two recent hard forks.

The vulnerabilities affected Polygon’s Bor and Heimdall clients and included denial-of-service risks, validator resource exhaustion and flaws affecting checkpoint and milestone processing.

Polygon said the flaws were fixed through the Austin and Kyoto hard forks, which were privately deployed and tested before mainnet
