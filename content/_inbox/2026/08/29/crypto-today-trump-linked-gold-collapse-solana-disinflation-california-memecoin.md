Today in crypto, Polygon disclosed previously private security flaws patched through recent hard forks, Stellar’s tokenized real-world asset market climbed to nearly $4 billion, tokenized stock activity surged over the last 30 days, and a Trump-promoted token collapsed after briefly surging.

Polygon disclosed several previously private security vulnerabilities that could have disrupted its proof-of-stake network after deploying fixes through two recent hard forks.

The vulnerabilities affected Polygon’s Bor and Heimdall clients and included denial-of-service risks, validator resource exhaustion and flaws affecting checkpoint and milestone processing.

Polygon said the flaws were fixed through the Austin and Kyoto hard forks, which were privately deployed and tested before mainnet activation and public disclosure. None of the vulnerabilities were observed being exploited on mainnet.

Nodes running older versions of either client past the hard fork activation heights have fallen out of consensus and must upgrade to rejoin the canonical network.

The value of tokenized real-world assets on Stellar has climbed roughly 360% in 2026 to nearly $4 billion, up from $868.8 million at the end of last year.

The network’s RWA market cap stood at $3.996 billion as of Aug. 29, spread across US Treasurys, private and public credit, non-US government debt and other tokenized asset classes.
