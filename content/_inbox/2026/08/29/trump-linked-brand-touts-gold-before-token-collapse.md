Real Trump Coins promoted GOLD before deleting related X posts, while team-linked wallets sold 224.5 million tokens as its market value fell about 99%.

Update Aug. 30: This article has been updated to include Real Trump Coins’ denial that it launched or authorized GOLD and to clarify references to associated domains.

A Solana-based token promoted by a Trump-linked coin brand collapsed within hours of its launch, raising questions over who was behind it and its unusual trading activity.

Real Trump Coins, a brand US President Donald Trump publicly promoted in 2024, touted the “Trump Digital GOLD” token on X before deleting related posts on Saturday, according to blockchain analytics platform Lookonchain.

RealTrumpCoins.com continued promoting GOLD as of the article’s initial publication, advertising a 4% trading fee and pledging to use 99% of trading fees to buy back the token. A separate website, TrumpCoins.com, is currently linked from the Real Trump Coins X profile and does not display the GOLD promotion.

The launch has left crypto observers questioning GOLD’s legitimacy and who was behind it, with some suggesting Real Trump Coins may have been compromised.

The token surfaced early Saturday when the Real Trump Coins X account, which Trump’s official account follows, announced the GOLD launch and directed users to RealTrumpCoins.com to buy the token.

Lookonchain flagged the launch shortly afterward, noting that the developer held 600 million GOLD while 15 newly created wallets spent $18,657 to acquire another 224.5 million tokens. “The team currently controls 82.45% of the total supply,” Lookonchain said, advising users to be cautious.

Lookonchain later reported that the 15 wallets, which it linked to the team, sold all 224.5 million GOLD for 3,178 Solana
