Keel Infrastructure (KEEL) a digital energy and infrastructure company raised $458 million through a convertible senior notes offering due 2032, generating approximately $445 million in net proceeds. The debt, which carries a 1.25% coupon, provides additional flexibility to fund data center development, equipment purchases, and other growth initiatives as the company pivots toward AI and high-performance computing infrastructure. Alongside the debt raise, Keel has also been monetizing its bitcoin holdings, selling 269 BTC for approximately $20 million during the first four months of 2026. The Bitcoin sales form part of management's broader strategy to redeploy capital from mining operations into higher-growth digital infrastructure
