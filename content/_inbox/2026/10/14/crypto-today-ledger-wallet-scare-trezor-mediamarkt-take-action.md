Today in crypto, Ledger asked resellers across Southeast Asia and Hong Kong to suspend hardware wallet sales amid an investigation into tampered devices, European electronics retailer MediaMarktSaturn removed Ledger products from stores and online platforms, and rival Trezor began cutting its authorized reseller network as a precaution.

Ledger has requested sellers and distributors of its devices in six Southeast Asian countries and Hong Kong to suspend sales and remove its hardware wallets from shelves as it investigates reports of tampered devices.

In a post on X on Tuesday, Ledger said the request covers resellers in Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, the Philippines, Vietnam, Malaysia and Hong Kong.

“To be clear, as the investigation continues, the only confirmed incidents remain contained to Southeast Asia. We have not verified a drain linked to a device bought elsewhere,” said Ledger.

“The investigation is ongoing to determine who tampered with these devices and where in the distribution chain it happened,” it added.

The wider sales suspension request expands on Ledger’s earlier request for reseller CryptoBilis to stop sales and shipments. The investigation became public on Oct. 9 after reports of stolen crypto linked to devices purchased through the reseller.

Hardware wallet maker Trezor is reducing its authorized reseller network after reports that a Southeast Asian reseller modified Ledger devices to steal users’ recovery phrases.
