Investors in Donald Trump's memecoin are getting another shot at being in a room with the U.S. president, the third such event following others that drew criticism from Democratic lawmakers as an openly corrupt selling of the White House.

The top 185 registered investors in $TRUMP will be invited through the doors of his Trump National private club in Washington, D.C., on November 22, according to the website that manages $TRUMP events. The VIP status — determined by investor engagement in the token — will be locked on November 12, the organization said.

When the "gala dinner" was announced , the price of the coin popped up about 10% to $2.25 before sinking again near the level it's been at in recent days — still a small fraction of the all-time high of around $74.

The event is being billed as the "most exclusive dinner in the world," and is advertising that three other "legends" will join the president, though none have yet been identified.

One thing is for sure, according to the club: "No attendees will be granted a private meet & greet with the President."

That had been a point of disappointment for some participants in past events: While Trump briefly appeared, they didn't get a chance to meet him.

Since his memecoin's launch in the days before he took office again, investors have lost billions of dollars , and those losses have occupied the spotlight during the recent debates over the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act — the crypto market structure bill in the Senate. With Trump's crypto empire in mind , Democratic lawmakers had insisted that the bill limit the digital asset ties of senior government officials, but that provision could never be worked out, and the bill failed to advance last month.
