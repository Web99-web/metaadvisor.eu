The U.S. is offering up to 40 million barrels of oil for purchase from its Strategic Petroleum Reserve.

This comes one day after the government reported SPR stocks had fallen to 283.8 million barrels, the lowest level since October 1982.

Nevertheless, the 40 million-barrel offer has knocked WTI crude oil prices down by about $1 per barrel in the past few minutes, with WTI now trading at $90.50, down 2.25%
