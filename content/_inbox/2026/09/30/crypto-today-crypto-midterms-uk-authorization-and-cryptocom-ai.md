Need to know what happened in crypto today? Here is the latest news on daily trends and events impacting Bitcoin price, blockchain, DeFi, Web3 and crypto regulation.

Today in crypto, a Coinbase-backed advocacy group endorsed candidates in key congressional races ahead of the US midterms, the United Kingdom opened applications for crypto firms seeking authorization under its new regulatory regime and Crypto.com’s personal AI agent platform remains in “stealth mode” nearly eight months after its Super Bowl debut.

Stand With Crypto has endorsed candidates in several key Senate and House races as the crypto industry steps up its involvement ahead of the US November midterm elections.

The Coinbase-backed advocacy group is supporting Republican Sen. Jon Husted in Ohio, Republican Rep. Ashley Hinson in Iowa and Democratic Rep. Chris Pappas in New Hampshire. It also endorsed House candidates from both parties, including Democrat Shomari Figures in Alabama and Republican Mariannette Miller-Meeks in Iowa.

The endorsements come two weeks after the Senate failed to advance the CLARITY Act, which would establish a federal regulatory framework for crypto markets. Stand With Crypto has since updated its candidate scorecard to reflect lawmakers’ votes on the bill and plans to increase advertising in six House races.

Crypto money is already flowing into several races. Fairshake, the industry-backed super PAC funded largely by Coinbase, Ripple and Andreessen Horowitz, has committed $30 million to support Husted against Democrat Sherrod Brown in Ohio.

The UK’s Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) opened applications Wednesday for crypto businesses seeking authorization under a regulatory regime due to take effect on Oct. 25, 2027.
