Today in crypto, MoonPay launched a credit card that enables users and AI agents to spend stablecoins through the Mastercard network. MoonPay is rolling out a virtual debit card that allows users and AI agents to spend stablecoins directly from self-custodied wallets at merchants that accept Mastercard .

The card connects onchain wallets to traditional payment rails and converts stablecoins into fiat at checkout. Transactions are authorized via smart contracts at the point of purchase, meaning users do not need to preload funds or move assets offchain, and declined payments return funds immediately.

The launch comes as companies, including Coinbase , build infrastructure for AI-driven payments. Credit card network Visa has also released tools that enable developers to integrate programmatic payments for AI agents, pointing to broader industry efforts to support automated transactions.

Crypto VC funding plunged to a near two-year low in April as investors pulled back from crypto start-ups and early-stage companies.

Crypto VC funding fell to $659 million across 63 funding rounds in April, down 74% from the $2.6 billion seen across 84 rounds in March, according to Cryptorank data . This brings the total year-to-date investments to $5.64 billion so far in 2026.
