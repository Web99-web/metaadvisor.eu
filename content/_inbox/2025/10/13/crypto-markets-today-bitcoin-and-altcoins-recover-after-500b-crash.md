The crypto market staged a recovery on Monday following the weekend's $500 billion bloodbath that resulted in a $10 billion drop in open interest.

Bitcoin BTC $ 115,431.03 rose by 1.4% while ether ETH $ 4,232.50 outperformed with a 2.5% gain. SNX $ 2.4888 , meanwhile, stole the show with a 120% rally as traders anticipate "perpetual wars" between the decentralized trading venue and HyperLiquid.

Plasma XPL $ 0.4417 and aster ASTER $ 1.4280 both failed to benefit from Monday's recovery, losing 4.2% and 2.5% respectively.

Combined spot and derivatives trading on centralized exchanges surged 7.58% to $9.72 trillion in August, marking the highest monthly volume of 2025

XLM Rises 6% to Recover From Weekend Plunge

Stellar posts dramatic intraday recovery from $0.33 support to $0.35 resistance as institutional money flows in.
