Encrypted messengers are having a second wave. Apps like WhatsApp, iMessage and Signal made end-to-end encryption (E2EE) a default expectation. But most still hinge on phone numbers, centralized servers and a lot of metadata, such as who you talk to, when, from which IP and on which device. That is what Vitalik Buterin is aiming at in his recent X post and donation . He argues the next steps for secure messaging are permissionless account creation with no phone numbers or Know Your Customer (KYC) and much stronger metadata privacy. In that context he highlighted Session and SimpleX and sent 128 Ether ( ETH ) to each to keep pushing in that direction. Session is a good case study because it tries to combine E2E encryption with decentralization . There is no central message server, traffic is routed through onion paths, and user IDs are keys instead of phone numbers. Did you know?
