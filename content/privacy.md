## Who we are
MetaAdvisor is an informational website about technology, AI and crypto.

## What we (don’t) collect
- We don’t ask you to create an account and we don’t track individual behaviour across sites.
- If you contact us or subscribe to our newsletter (when available), we’ll process the data you provide (e.g. email address) only to reply or to deliver the newsletter.

## Newsletter (future)
- If/when we offer a newsletter, email addresses will be stored with our email provider (e.g. MailerLite). You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in each email.

## Legal bases (GDPR)
We rely on:
- **Legitimate interest** to keep the site secure and measure basic performance.
- **Consent** for optional features (e.g. newsletter) where required.

## Data retention
- Contact emails/newsletter data: kept until you unsubscribe or we delete the list.
- Server/CDN logs: kept for short periods for security and troubleshooting.

## Sharing your data
We do not sell personal data. Service providers (hosting/CDN, email provider) may process data strictly to provide their service and under a data-processing agreement.

## International transfers
Our providers may operate globally. Where applicable, transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

## Your rights
You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection under GDPR, and you can withdraw consent at any time (for consent-based processing).

## Advertising (future)
We may display advertising provided by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on general location or context, in accordance with its own privacy policies. We do not receive personally identifiable information from advertisers.

## Contact
For privacy-related requests, please contact us via the Contact page.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Significant changes will be noted on this page.

## Analytics and your choices
We use Google Analytics 4 to measure visits, page use and referrals. Where prior consent is required, Google Analytics is not loaded until you accept analytics. In reviewed regions where service analytics may lawfully start without prior consent, it can start automatically with an easy opt-out. Uncertain locations use the prior-consent mode. Country is estimated through our hosting/CDN, not inferred from page language.

Analytics permission does not permit advertising tracking. Advertising consent parameters stay denied, Google advertising signals are disabled, and this implementation does not load the advertising-network scripts. Reject analytics stops analytics; Cookie settings lets you change your choice at any time. We also respect browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track by disabling analytics.

Acceptance is remembered for six calendar months across EN/DE/HR. A new refusal normally lasts for the current browser visit, with a new visit after 30 minutes of inactivity or the end of the browser session. France and the UK retain refusal for six months following regulator guidance; unresolved locations also retain it for six months as a protective default. Previously saved refusals keep their original validity. Browsers that restore sessions may also restore a session refusal. If preference storage is blocked, automatic analytics stays off. Analytics cookies, if allowed, have a maximum configured lifetime of 180 days. We omit URL query strings, URL fragments and form data from our analytics commands. Google processes permitted analytics data; property retention and processing terms must be reflected in the approved provider configuration. Hosting security logs are separate from analytics. Withdrawing consent does not erase earlier lawful processing.

### Regional analytics rules
For ordinary, non-sensitive service statistics, our prepared regional policy permits automatic analytics without an initial banner in Australia and reviewed US states where a reliable regional signal is available, subject to the applicable processing, disclosure and provider conditions. You can turn it off at any time using **Privacy / Cookie settings** in the footer. An earlier refusal, Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track takes priority. Advertising consent stays denied; no advertising profiles are created by this implementation.

In the EEA (including Croatia and Germany), the UK, Switzerland, Japan pending further assessment, and other unreviewed or unknown locations, analytics starts only after acceptance. Japan is not treated as automatically exempt: recipient linkage and overseas-transfer conditions require assessment. Page language never determines location. US states are never inferred from browser language. The prepared automatic profiles cover California, Colorado, Connecticut and Texas only when reliable hosting regional metadata is available and the stated conditions are met. Maryland, Washington, other unreviewed states and an unknown state use prior consent.

Google is the analytics provider. Permitted data include a cookie identifier, page path/title, a sanitized referral and approximate geography plus ordinary technical analytics data. Query strings and fragments are omitted from our page commands. Provider settings must limit collection to the declared purpose; analytics is not a tool for sensitive-data or known-child profiling. Refusal stops future analytics and clears its cookies; it does not delete data already processed.
