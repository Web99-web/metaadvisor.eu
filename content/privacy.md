_Last updated: 06 Sep 2025_

## Who we are
MetaAdvisor is an informational website about technology, AI and crypto.

## What we (don’t) collect
- We don’t ask you to create an account and we don’t track individual behaviour across sites.
- If you contact us or subscribe to our newsletter (when available), we’ll process the data you provide (e.g. email address) only to reply or to deliver the newsletter.

## Cookies & local storage
- The site itself uses only essential cookies required by the hosting/CDN (e.g. to serve pages securely and quickly).
- We avoid third-party tracking pixels. If we add any in the future, we will update this page.

## Analytics
- We use privacy-friendly aggregated analytics (e.g. via CDN/hosting) to understand page performance (page views, bandwidth, country). These analytics do **not** give us a profile of you as an individual.

## Newsletter (future)
- If/when we offer a newsletter, email addresses will be stored with our email provider (e.g. MailerLite). You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in each email.

## Legal bases (GDPR)
We rely on:
- **Legitimate interest** to keep the site secure and measure basic performance.
- **Consent** for optional features (e.g. newsletter) where required.

## Data retention
- Contact emails/newsletter data: kept until you unsubscribe or we delete the list.
- Server/CDN logs: kept for short periods for security and troubleshooting.

## Sharing your data
We do not sell personal data. Service providers (hosting/CDN, email provider) may process data strictly to provide their service and under a data-processing agreement.

## International transfers
Our providers may operate globally. Where applicable, transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

## Your rights
You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection under GDPR, and you can withdraw consent at any time (for consent-based processing).

## Advertising (future)
We may display advertising provided by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on general location or context, in accordance with its own privacy policies. We do not receive personally identifiable information from advertisers.

## Contact
For privacy-related requests, please contact us via the Contact page.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Significant changes will be noted on this page.
