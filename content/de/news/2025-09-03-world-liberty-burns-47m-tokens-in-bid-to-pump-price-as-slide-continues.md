**World Liberty Financial (WLFI)** has burned **47 million tokens** in an attempt to boost its price amid a continued slide.  

The move, known as a **“token burn”**, is a common strategy in crypto projects to reduce supply and theoretically increase value. However, the market has not responded as hoped – WLFI’s price continued to fall despite the significant reduction in circulating supply.  

Analysts note that token burns can sometimes spark short-term rallies, but without genuine demand and sustainable adoption, the long-term impact remains limited.  

---

**Our take:**  
Burning 47 million tokens reflects a desperate bid to reverse the downtrend. Without real utility and investor confidence, WLFI risks becoming another cautionary tale of projects relying on gimmicks over fundamentals.
