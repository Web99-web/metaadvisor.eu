**Monero (XMR)** has released **CLI v0.18.4.3 “Fluorine Fermi”**, described by the team as a *highly recommended upgrade*. It strengthens protection against so-called **spy nodes**.

Monero’s privacy stack includes:
- **Stealth addresses** – each transaction generates a unique recipient address.  
- **Ring signatures** – sender’s transaction mixed with decoy inputs.  
- **RingCT** – amounts hidden by cryptographic proofs.  

Researchers recently flagged **non-standard nodes** posing as legitimate but designed to monitor the network. The **Fluorine Fermi** update counters this with:  
- 🛡️ **improved peer selection** (avoiding multiple peers in the same subnet),  
- 🚫 **reduced connections to suspicious IP clusters**,  
- 🔒 **safer defaults**.
