Gold’s surge to a new record underscores defensive positioning, while Bitcoin’s behavior remains mixed: in stress episodes it sometimes tracks gold, but in liquidity-rich rallies it trades more like a high-beta tech asset.

Institutional flows still favor gold via ETF inflows and physical demand, whereas BTC participation is more cyclical and momentum-driven. Technically, prior highs and the 200-day average remain key references for BTC; gold finds support above former peaks.

**Our comment:** A barbell allocation — gold for stability, BTC for upside — can work, provided position sizing and risk controls are strict.
