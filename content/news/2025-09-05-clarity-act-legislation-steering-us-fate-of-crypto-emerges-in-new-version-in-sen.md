The **U.S. Senate** is advancing work on the industry’s top policy priority — the **Clarity Act**, aimed at establishing a regulatory framework for crypto markets in the United States. On Friday, a new draft circulated that outlines stronger developer protections, bankruptcy provisions, and the role of regulators in supporting tokenization of financial markets.  

While the **House of Representatives** already passed its own version — the **Digital Asset Market Clarity Act** — the Senate’s draft is expected to set the direction for the final law.  

### Key updates in the draft
- **Developer protections**: legal safeguards for those building and maintaining blockchain systems and DeFi applications.  
- **Bankruptcy rules**: explicit clarification that “ancillary assets” and digital commodities must be treated as customer property.  
- **Tokenization**: SEC and CFTC are tasked with jointly studying standards for tokenizing securities and real-world assets.

## Our take
This draft marks a major milestone in U.S. crypto regulation. By protecting developers and clarifying bankruptcy treatment, the **Clarity Act** builds the foundation for trust and stability. If enacted, it could position the U.S. as a **global leader in crypto market oversight**, bridging the gap between traditional finance and digital assets.
