The CME’s regulated ether (ETH) futures market is heating up as the ongoing rotation out of bitcoin (BTC $113,025.87) accelerates.

The total notional open interest (OI) in ETH futures recently surpassed **$10 billion** for the first time on record, according to data shared by the exchange with CoinDesk. Earlier this month, the number of large open interest holders reached a record 101.

Notional OI represents the dollar value of active or open contracts at a given time. The CME offers standard contracts sized at 50 ETH and micro contracts sized at 0.1 ETH. Large holders are those with at least 25 ether futures contracts open at the same time.

The new high in open interest accompanies other record-breaking metrics, including the number of open micro ether contracts, which exceeded 500,000, and ether notional options open interest topping $1 billion. In contract terms, ether options OI reached a year-to-date high of more than 4,800 contracts.

> “We’re certainly seeing a resurgence and renewed enthusiasm in Ether futures – especially regarding institutional participation. Our Large Open Interest Holders (LOIH) hit a record of 101 during the week of August 5. This is a critical indicator for market participants as it signals a strengthening of the institutional and professional ecosystem around ether,” Giovanni Vicioso, global head of cryptocurrency products at CME Group, told CoinDesk.

> “As far as broader trends driving the surge, increased network activity, corporate treasury accumulation of ether, and positive regulatory developments have all contributed to a broad-based rally in ether and ether-based derivatives,” Vicioso added.

While ether markets are booming, open interest in standard bitcoin futures lags behind, underscoring the shift in institutional attention toward Ethereum.
