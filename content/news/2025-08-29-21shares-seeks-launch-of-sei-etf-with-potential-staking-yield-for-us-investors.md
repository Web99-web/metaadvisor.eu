**21Shares**, a leading asset management firm, has filed with the **U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)** to launch an **ETF tracking SEI**, the native token of the Sei blockchain.

The proposed **21Shares SEI ETF** would provide investors with exposure to SEI while offering potential additional yield through **staking rewards**, according to the filing.

On X, 21Shares posted:  
> “We’re excited to announce that we’ve filed with the SEC for a SEI ETF in the U.S.—a key milestone in our vision to expand ETF access linked to @Seinetwork.”

Following the successful rollout of **spot Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs** in 2024, fund managers are now turning their attention to smaller digital assets, including **Solana, Dogecoin, XRP, and other altcoins**, supported by a crypto-friendly Trump administration.

The Trust’s primary goal is **“to track the performance of SEI,”** with a secondary aim to **“generate rewards from staking a portion of the Trust’s SEI.”**  

According to **Krishnendu Chatterjee**, CEO and co-founder of **A2ZCryptoInvestment**, it is “highly likely that the SEI ETF will be accepted and join Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs.”  
> “This marks a step toward broader regulated alt investments, including staking benefits,” he added.

Still, 21Shares acknowledged that it **has not yet determined whether staking can be offered** under a public trust structure.

**Coinbase Custody Trust Company** will serve as custodian for SEI holdings, while **Coinbase Inc.** will act as the prime broker for trading activities.

The move follows **Canary Capital’s filing in May**, highlighting the growing race to expand ETFs beyond Bitcoin and Ethereum.
