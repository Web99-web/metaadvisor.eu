Over the past 24 hours, XRP surged approximately 3%, reaching a high near $2.82. Whales have accumulated around 340 million tokens (about $960M), signaling institutional conviction amid broader market stress.

**Key technical indicators:**
- **RSI** sits comfortably in the mid-50s, suggesting a neutral-to-bullish bias.
- **MACD** is nearing a bullish crossover above its signal line, which often heralds a shift in momentum.
- A symmetrical triangle is forming beneath $3.00; a breakout above $3.30 could spur a rally to $4.00+.

**Macro and regulatory context:**
- September historically underperforms for crypto, compounded by uncertainty around XRP ETF approvals from Grayscale, Bitwise, and others.
- Analysts are split: some warn of downside toward $1.00, while others eye long-term targets in the $7–$8 range upon a breakout.

---

**Our comment:**  
Whale accumulation combined with neutral-bullish technical signals offers a compelling setup—but traders should wait for confirmation. A solid break above $3.30 is key; a break below the $2.70 support range could reignite selling pressure.
