**Kraken** met with the US **SEC**’s **Crypto Task Force** on Monday to discuss **tokenization of traditional assets** and the legal/regulatory requirements for a **tokenized trading system**, according to a memorandum filed with the Commission.

Attendees included representatives from **Payward, Inc.**, **Kraken Securities LLC**, and law firm **WilmerHale**. Discussion points covered compliance requirements, the potential benefits of tokenization, and next steps.

The meeting comes as industry groups and global regulators urge the SEC to take a stricter stance on **tokenized stocks**. Kraken launched tokenized stocks for non-US investors in May and recently **expanded its offering** to additional networks.
