**Eric Trump** said the family turned **pro-crypto** after being **“debanked”** in the aftermath of the **January 2021 Capitol** events. Multiple banks allegedly **shut hundreds of accounts** tied to the **Trump Organization**, forcing a temporary shift to regional banks before a new, unnamed partner was found — remarks attributed to a **Wall Street Journal** interview. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

He argued the episode showed **how easily the financial system can be weaponized**, and said industry insiders claimed the **Biden administration** restricted **bank access** for crypto firms via regulatory pressure. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}

In **March**, the **Trump Organization** **sued Capital One**, alleging politically motivated account closures. A month later, Eric Trump said banks must **adopt crypto** or face **extinction** within ten years. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

He also advocated **tokenizing real-world assets**, floating the idea of **tokenizing Trump Tower** to broaden investor access. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}

**Family ties to crypto:** the official **TRUMP memecoin**, **World Liberty Financial** (USD1 stablecoin; Donald Trump listed as co-founder emeritus, sons as co-founders), and **American Bitcoin** (a **Hut 8** subsidiary) which **raised $220M** to purchase BTC and mining equipment. Reports peg Donald Trump’s **crypto fortune at ~$2.4B**; Eric Trump **denies** the family profited from the presidency and hinted someone from the family might run in **2028**. :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}
