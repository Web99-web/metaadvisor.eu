Asia-focused **Tiger Research** has set a **Q3 target of $190,000 for bitcoin**, pointing to record global liquidity, structural ETF demand, and new access via **401(k) retirement accounts** as the strongest setup since 2021.

Tiger’s model calculates a **base price of $135,000**, then applies multipliers for **fundamentals (+3.5%)** and **macro conditions (+35%)** to reach the $190,000 forecast — a 67% gain from this week’s average of $113,000.

The report cites three main drivers:  
- M2 money supply exceeding **$90 trillion**,  
- ETFs and corporate accumulation now making up **6% of bitcoin supply**,  
- a regulatory green light opening U.S. retirement accounts to crypto.  

Trump’s executive order allowing 401(k) exposure is described as **“a definitive signal of bitcoin’s transition to a core institutional holding.”** Even a 1% allocation from the $8.9 trillion pool would equal nearly $90 billion in demand.

Accumulation is evident: ETFs collectively hold **1.3 million BTC**, while **MicroStrategy (MSTR)** owns over **629,000 BTC** valued at $71 billion. Block activity has shifted toward larger institutional trades, with fewer but bigger transfers.

Still, the report concedes the network remains unbalanced. Daily transactions and active users are well below last year’s highs, and retail participation has faded. New initiatives like **BTCFi** will be needed to reignite activity beyond institutional wallets.

On-chain indicators also warn of caution. **MVRV-Z** stands at 2.49, a zone that has historically preceded corrections as profits pile up.
