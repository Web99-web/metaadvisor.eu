**Figure Technologies**, the blockchain lender founded by SoFi co-founder Mike Cagney, is targeting a valuation of up to **$4.13 billion** in its upcoming **Nasdaq IPO**, according to filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

The company and its investors are seeking to raise as much as **$526 million** via share sales, making Figure one of the latest crypto-linked firms to test equity markets as investor demand for new listings shows signs of recovery.

Shares are expected to trade under the ticker **FIGR**, with Goldman Sachs, Jefferies, and BofA Securities acting as lead underwriters.

Figure has been a key player in the **real-world assets (RWA)** sector, originating more than **$16 billion** in home equity lines of credit using its **Provenance blockchain**.

Earlier this year, the company merged with **Figure Markets**, another venture by Cagney, which runs a blockchain marketplace and issues **YDLS**, a yield-bearing stablecoin structured as a tokenized money market fund.
