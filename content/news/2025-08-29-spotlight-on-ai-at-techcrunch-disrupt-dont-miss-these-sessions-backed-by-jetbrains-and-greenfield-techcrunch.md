TechCrunch Disrupt isn’t just about showcasing the startups of tomorrow — it’s also about surfacing the boldest ideas shaping technology today. Thanks to the support of partners JetBrains and Greenfield, the **TechCrunch Disrupt 2025** program (October 27–29 at Moscone West, San Francisco) includes two can’t-miss sessions that put AI front and center.

**Monday, Oct 27 — Builders Stage (1:40–2:10 p.m. PT)**  
*Who’s Defining AI’s Future in 2025? The AI Disruptors 60 Unveiled* (Greenfield Partners) — a list of early- and growth-stage startups leading AI infrastructure, applications and go-to-market innovation.

**Tuesday, Oct 28 — AI Stage (1:55–2:15 p.m. PT)**  
*Vibe coding? Cute. Now let’s get real and talk about AI built for developers* (JetBrains) — JetBrains CEO Kirill Skrygan explains why code quality — not just velocity — will define the next wave of intelligent software, and how developers can harness AI to deliver at scale with reliability and precision.
