## A joint crypto benchmark for institutional markets

The **Nasdaq Stock Exchange** and the **CME Group (Chicago Mercantile Exchange)** have announced the unification of their existing crypto benchmarks under a single name: the **Nasdaq-CME Crypto Index**. The move creates a consolidated reference index designed specifically for institutional market participants.

The index tracks a basket of leading cryptocurrencies, including **Bitcoin (BTC)**, **Ethereum (ETH)**, **XRP**, **Solana (SOL)**, **Chainlink (LINK)**, **Cardano (ADA)** and **Avalanche (AVAX)**. Its purpose is to provide a transparent, professionally maintained benchmark that reflects the core of the digital asset market.

## Why crypto index products are gaining traction

As the crypto market matures, demand is growing for **index-based investment products** that offer diversified exposure without requiring investors to analyze or manage individual tokens. For institutions such as asset managers, pension funds and family offices, indices offer a regulated and familiar entry point into digital assets.

Crypto indices help reduce technical complexity, spread risk across multiple assets and are particularly well suited to **passive investment strategies**. In a market with millions of listed tokens, they provide clarity, structure and comparability.

## Inside the index: why these tokens matter

The Nasdaq-CME Crypto Index focuses on established networks with strong liquidity and long-term relevance:

- **Bitcoin (BTC)** as digital store of value  
- **Ethereum (ETH)** as the backbone of smart contracts and DeFi  
- **XRP** for payment and settlement infrastructure  
- **Solana (SOL)** for high-performance blockchain applications  
- **Chainlink (LINK)** as the leading oracle network  
- **Cardano (ADA)** with its research-driven development approach  
- **Avalanche (AVAX)** as a scalable platform for DeFi and tokenization  

This composition highlights a clear institutional preference for **market-leading and infrastructure-grade projects**, rather than speculative assets.

## Implications for ETFs and institutional adoption

Analysts expect the new benchmark to serve as a foundation for future **crypto index ETFs** and structured investment products. In both the United States and Europe, interest is growing in regulated vehicles that allow crypto exposure in a format similar to traditional equity indices.

For many investors, such products offer a practical way to participate in the crypto market without managing wallets, private keys or direct on-chain activity.
