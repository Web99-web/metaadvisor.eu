# When Sleep Becomes Data

**OZLO**, known for its **Sleepbuds** that help users fall asleep by blocking external noise, is quietly shifting its strategic focus. The company is moving beyond hardware and building an **AI-powered platform for collecting and analyzing sleep data**.

This marks a broader shift in how sleep is perceived: no longer just a personal experience, but an increasingly **measurable and monetizable data stream**.

## From Gadget to Ecosystem

Founded by former Bose employees, OZLO has always aimed to build more than a single product. A core element of its strategy is a proprietary **SDK (software development kit)** that allows partners to access certain sleep-related data.

One early partner is the meditation app **Calm**, which uses OZLO’s data to determine whether its audio and meditation content actually helps users fall asleep — something traditional app analytics alone cannot reliably measure.

In other words, OZLO does not just track usage; it measures **outcomes**.

## How the Data Is Collected

The Sleepbuds and their smart charging case rely on sensors that monitor:
- body movement,
- breathing patterns,
- transitions between wakefulness, relaxation and sleep.

This data is processed locally and analyzed using **machine learning algorithms** to assess whether a user is awake, relaxed or asleep.

Such insights could be valuable for:
- wellness and health applications,
- sleep and stress research,
- digital therapeutics,
- future medical use cases.

## Moving Toward the Healthcare Sector

OZLO’s recent acquisition of a **neurotech startup** signals ambitions that go beyond consumer electronics. Subscription-based services, AI-driven insights and medically adjacent applications could follow, pushing the company into more regulated territory.

In this context, sleep becomes a **biometric signal**, not just a subjective feeling of rest.

## A Meta Perspective: Technology Not Everyone Wants

Not everyone wants their sleep analyzed. Even people who struggle with sleep may feel uncomfortable having their breathing, movements and nightly routines continuously monitored.

That tension is precisely what makes this a meta-level story. Technologies like OZLO shape **industry standards and expectations**, regardless of whether individuals choose to adopt them.
