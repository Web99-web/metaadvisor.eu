# PEPE is rising faster than the market

The start of 2026 has brought early signs of a revival in the crypto market after a prolonged period of stagnation and heightened caution. While most large-cap tokens moved only modestly and lacked decisive momentum, PEPE posted a much stronger price increase in a short time frame, standing out as one of the most active tokens during this phase.

Over the past seven days, Bitcoin, Solana and XRP recorded single-digit percentage gains, while PEPE climbed by roughly 35%. This contrast clearly illustrates how, in the early stages of a market recovery, capital often gravitates toward tokens that exhibit higher volatility and a greater sensitivity to shifts in market sentiment.

On average, weekly gains hovered around 35%, with some exchanges and specific time windows even showing moves closer to 45%. This further underlines how pronounced PEPE’s price action has been compared to the broader market.

## Why PEPE often reacts more strongly

For readers who are not deeply immersed in crypto on a daily basis, it is important to understand that markets do not treat all assets equally. When optimism begins to return, investors frequently avoid moving immediately into the most stable assets and instead look for those with the potential for faster price moves. In this environment, PEPE has repeatedly proven to react earlier and more forcefully than the market average.

This behavior is not necessarily driven by short-term news or technical upgrades, but rather by market structure, token distribution and investor psychology. As the market transitions from a passive phase into a more active one, PEPE often becomes a focal point of rising trading volumes and renewed interest.

## The strength of the community and long-term holders

One of PEPE’s defining characteristics is its exceptionally large and active holder base. A significant portion of users hold the token over longer periods, while a share of supply remains concentrated among larger addresses, commonly referred to as whales, which did not show signs of mass selling during the recent rally.

This combination often creates a more stable foundation for strong yet relatively controlled price movements. When available supply remains limited and demand increases, prices tend to respond faster than in projects with more fragmented ownership structures.

## What this rally says about the market

It is important to stress that such a rally does not necessarily mark the beginning of a long-term trend. However, experience from previous cycles shows that when the market starts to turn upward gradually, PEPE often rises faster and more sharply than many other tokens. That dynamic is precisely why it has once again moved into the spotlight at the start of 2026.

This example also highlights how quickly capital continues to rotate within crypto markets and why volatility remains a defining feature, even at a time when the industry is often described as entering a more mature phase.

## Our view (MetaAdvisor)

We view PEPE as a kind of modern crypto “institution” in terms of market behavior and community strength, rather than through traditional technological narratives. Its ability to attract attention and liquidity in the early stages of a market recovery stems less from functionality and more from holder structure and strong recognition among users. While maintaining a neutral analytical stance, it is evident that PEPE commands an unusually strong user base, making it relevant whenever the market begins to move higher.
