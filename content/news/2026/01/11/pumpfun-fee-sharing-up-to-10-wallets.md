## Pump.fun redesigns creator incentives

Pump.fun, one of the most active memecoin launchpads on the Solana blockchain, has announced a significant overhaul of its creator fee model. The new system enables teams to **share fees across up to 10 different wallets**, marking a clear departure from the previous setup that largely incentivized short-term token creation.

The update follows an internal review showing that while the earlier dynamic fee model successfully boosted activity, it **failed to produce sustainable market behavior** or long-term liquidity.

## Why the previous model fell short

According to the platform’s co-founder, the earlier incentive structure encouraged low-risk token deployment rather than meaningful market building. Traders — the primary source of liquidity and volume — were often sidelined, leading to brief spikes in activity followed by rapid declines.

This dynamic resulted in frequent community takeovers and eroded user confidence across parts of the memecoin ecosystem.

## Greater control and clearer accountability

Alongside fee sharing, Pump.fun is rolling out additional governance tools:
- transparent distribution of fees among team members  
- transfer of token ownership  
- revocation of update authority  
- improved controls for CTO (Community Takeover) administrators  

The goal is to promote more responsible creator behavior while improving the overall experience for traders.

## A maturing memecoin market

Pump.fun played a central role in Solana’s memecoin boom, but these changes suggest the market is entering a new phase. Attention is gradually shifting away from pure pump-and-dump dynamics toward **more structured and sustainable incentive models**.

If successful, the new fee system could serve as a benchmark for other launchpad platforms across the crypto industry.
