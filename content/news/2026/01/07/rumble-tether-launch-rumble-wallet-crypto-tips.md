# Rumble and Tether Launch Rumble Wallet

Video platform Rumble and stablecoin issuer Tether have launched **Rumble Wallet**, a new **non-custodial crypto wallet** that allows users to tip content creators directly within the app. The move brings cryptocurrencies closer to real-world usage, shifting the focus away from pure speculation toward practical digital payments.

## Crypto Tips as a New Monetization Model

Rumble Wallet enables direct value transfer between users and creators without traditional intermediaries. This introduces an alternative monetization model where creators are supported directly by their audience, rather than relying solely on advertising revenue or platform-controlled payout systems.

At launch, the wallet supports **USDT**, **Bitcoin**, and **Tether Gold**, giving users the choice between a stable digital dollar, the most widely recognized cryptocurrency, and tokenized gold exposure.

## Focus on Real-World Crypto Use

Unlike many crypto narratives that center on price action and volatility, this initiative highlights functionality and everyday usability. Stablecoins such as USDT are particularly well suited for small, frequent transactions, while Bitcoin — despite higher fees and slower settlement — maintains strong symbolic and network value.

### Easy Fiat-to-Crypto Access

Integration with MoonPay allows users to move seamlessly between fiat currencies and crypto assets. This removes one of the biggest barriers to broader adoption: technical complexity and fragmented user experience.

## The Bigger Picture: Returning to Internet-Native Money

Rumble Wallet fits into a broader trend of cryptocurrencies returning to their original **peer-to-peer payment** roots. Tipping content creators is a clear, practical example of how digital value can move instantly, globally, and without centralized control.
