# XRP is now live on Hyperliquid

**Spot trading for XRP** is officially live on **Hyperliquid**, enabled through the listing of **FXRP**, a tokenized representation of XRP developed within the **Flare ecosystem**. This marks XRP’s entry into a fully-fledged **on-chain spot trading environment with an order book structure**.

FXRP is designed to make XRP liquidity usable within DeFi and trading platforms without relying on centralized custodians. The model is considered *trust-minimized* and aims to bring assets without native smart contract functionality into modern blockchain-based markets.

## Why Hyperliquid matters for XRP

Hyperliquid has quickly emerged as one of the fastest-growing on-chain trading platforms. Unlike traditional DeFi exchanges that rely on AMM models, Hyperliquid operates with a **real order book**, offering an experience closely resembling centralized exchanges.

For the XRP ecosystem, this means:
- immediate access to on-chain spot trading  
- additional sources of liquidity  
- deeper integration of XRP into the broader DeFi landscape  

This development represents a strategic shift beyond XRP’s long-standing focus on banking and cross-border payments.

## How Hyperliquid works technically (MetaMask access)

Hyperliquid does not use traditional user accounts, email registration, or KYC. Access is provided exclusively through **self-custody wallets**, most commonly **MetaMask**.

The process works as follows:
1. the user connects a MetaMask wallet to the Hyperliquid platform  
2. wallet ownership is verified without personal data  
3. assets (typically USDC) are transferred from the wallet to Hyperliquid’s infrastructure  
4. users can immediately trade spot markets, including FXRP  

While the interface feels similar to centralized exchanges, trading remains **on-chain**, and full responsibility for wallet security stays with the user.

### Benefits and risks of this model

Removing centralized custody reduces counterparty and regulatory risk. However, it increases the importance of **wallet security**, as lost private keys, compromised browser extensions, or malicious software can result in irreversible fund loss.

## XRP as a DeFi asset: the broader context

FXRP and its integration with Hyperliquid represent a broader effort to position XRP as an **active DeFi asset**, not just a value transfer mechanism. While short-term price reactions may be limited, the move is structurally significant for XRP’s long-term utility.
