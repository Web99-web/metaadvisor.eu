# Family task calendar: Skylight Calendar 2

At CES 2026, Skylight introduced **Calendar 2**, a device designed to act as a central **family task calendar** and **smart family organizer**. While Skylight originally started as a digital photo frame company, its focus has now clearly shifted toward helping families manage everyday life more efficiently.

Notably, the announcement does **not** state that Calendar 2 is free. Based on the presentation, it is positioned as a **paid device and software product**, although pricing details were not disclosed at launch.

## One shared calendar for the whole family

The core idea behind Skylight Calendar 2 is to merge all family schedules into one shared view. The system can sync calendars from services such as Google Calendar, iCal, Microsoft tools, as well as school and sports apps commonly used by children.

Events are **color-coded**, making it easy to understand everyone’s commitments at a glance — from school activities and sports practices to work meetings and family plans.

## AI support for everyday family organization

What sets Calendar 2 apart is its use of **AI-powered features**. The device can recognize dates and events from photos of paper notes, school flyers, or printed reminders and automatically add them to the calendar.

### More than just a calendar

Beyond scheduling, the device also supports:
- grocery lists  
- meal planning and recipes  
- reminders for appointments and tasks  
- simple chores and to-do items designed to be usable even by young children  

The interface is intentionally simple and visual, allowing children to interact with it even before they can read.
