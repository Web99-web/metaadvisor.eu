# Bitcoin Near $100,000: Is the Bull Market Returning?

In mid-January 2026, Bitcoin once again demonstrated notable strength, climbing toward the $97,000 level and reigniting debate about whether the market is entering a new bull phase. Price levels like these attract attention not only because of the psychological importance of $100,000, but also because of what is happening beneath the surface of the market.

Unlike many previous rallies, this move has not occurred in isolation from institutional investors. On the contrary, strong capital inflows suggest that larger financial players are increasingly involved, giving this rally a more solid foundation than purely speculative spikes.

## Institutional capital as fuel for the rally

One of the most significant signals supporting the recent rise is the scale of inflows into Bitcoin exchange-traded funds. Roughly $750 million flowed into Bitcoin ETFs in a single day, a figure that typically points to structured institutional positioning rather than short-term retail speculation.

Such inflows tend to have a dual effect: they create genuine demand for Bitcoin while also sending a broader signal that Bitcoin is being treated as a more mature and credible asset class. This perception often encourages additional participation from smaller investors.

## Technical picture: strength with early signs of caution

From a technical perspective, Bitcoin has broken above several key resistance levels. Short-term indicators such as the 20-day exponential moving average and the relative strength index (RSI) indicate that buyers currently maintain control of the market. At the same time, selling pressure has emerged above the $96,000 area, suggesting that some investors are taking profits after the rapid advance.

This does not necessarily mark the end of the rally, but it does indicate that the market has not yet entered a phase of unchecked euphoria.

## What is FOMO and why does it matter now?

In fast-rising markets, the term FOMO appears frequently. FOMO stands for *Fear Of Missing Out* and describes the behavior of investors who buy primarily because prices are rising rapidly and headlines are turning bullish, rather than because of careful analysis.

If Bitcoin begins to “tease” the $100,000 level, the FOMO effect could intensify, particularly among retail investors. Historically, such phases often lead to short-term overheating, followed by corrections or extended consolidation.

## Altcoins follow, but selectively

Bitcoin’s rise has lifted parts of the altcoin market as well, though without the broad-based euphoria seen in earlier cycles. Some projects have shown relative strength, while many major altcoins have recorded only moderate gains. This selective behavior suggests a more mature market, where capital is allocated with greater caution.

## A new bull market or just a powerful rally?

Some analysts draw parallels to the 2020–2021 cycle and argue that, if history repeats itself, Bitcoin could eventually reach significantly higher levels. Others urge caution, pointing out that the traditional four-year cycle may have changed, potentially leading to higher volatility and longer consolidation phases rather than a sustained upward trend.

What is clear is that Bitcoin currently displays stronger fundamentals than during many previous rallies. Whether this momentum develops into a full-fledged bull market will likely depend on whether the $100,000 level can be decisively reached and held.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor):

- Institutional inflows add credibility to the current rally.  
- FOMO could drive prices higher in the short term but also increases correction risk.  
- The market appears more mature and selective, especially among altcoins.  
- The $100,000 level remains the key psychological and technical test.  
- The coming weeks should clarify whether this is the start of a new cycle or a strong interim rally.
