# Privacy Coins as New Surprises at the End of 2025: Zcash, Monero and Dash

## The end of 2025 brings an unexpected shift

As much of the crypto market struggled toward the end of 2025 with declining prices, weaker liquidity and heightened investor caution, one segment quietly began to attract attention. These are so-called privacy coins — cryptocurrencies designed with a strong focus on anonymity, identity protection and transaction privacy. During this period, Zcash, Monero and Dash showed relative strength compared with the broader market.

*The image is symbolic.*

For many market participants, this came as a surprise. For years, privacy coins had remained in the shadow of dominant narratives such as DeFi, NFTs and artificial intelligence. Yet as the market cooled, attention gradually returned to the foundational values of crypto.

## Why privacy is coming back into focus

The year 2025 was defined by rapid technological advancement, but also by a growing sense of surveillance. Digital identities, the tracking of financial flows and increasing regulatory pressure became everyday realities. In this environment, investors and users alike began to reconsider what cryptocurrencies were originally meant to represent.

Privacy coins offer a technical response to these concerns. They enable transactions in which the sender, recipient and transaction amount are not publicly visible, setting them apart from transparent blockchains like Bitcoin or Ethereum. In times of market uncertainty, these characteristics regain importance.

## Zcash as a leading force in the return of privacy

Zcash stood out particularly toward the end of 2025. Its strong price performance was accompanied by increased use of so-called shielded addresses, suggesting that interest was not purely speculative. A portion of users actively turned to Zcash to benefit from its advanced privacy mechanisms based on zero-knowledge cryptography.

Notably, Zcash is often viewed more as cryptographic infrastructure than as a conventional cryptocurrency. This perception makes it especially appealing in periods when the market seeks stability and conceptual clarity.

## Monero and Dash in a different but stable role

Monero, on the other hand, has maintained its reputation as the most consistent privacy coin. Privacy protection is deeply embedded in its protocol and not optional, which makes Monero particularly attractive to users for whom anonymity is a top priority. Although Monero did not experience dramatic price spikes like Zcash, its relative stability in a turbulent market further strengthened its position.

Dash played a somewhat different role in this narrative. While it offers fewer privacy features compared to Zcash and Monero, its emphasis on ease of use and fast transactions allowed it to align with the broader trend toward privacy-focused solutions.

## The broader context: defensive investor behavior

Market analyses suggest that toward the end of 2025, a portion of capital adopted a more defensive stance. Rather than chasing high-risk returns, investors increasingly favored projects with clear utility and long-term relevance. In this context, privacy coins emerged as a logical choice.

This does not mean that these projects are without risk. Regulatory pressure, exchange availability and reputational challenges remain significant. However, their relative strength during a weak market phase demonstrates that privacy is not a forgotten theme, but rather a cyclical value that resurfaces when confidence in broader systems declines.

## New stars or a temporary comeback?

The key question now is whether this marks the beginning of a new trend or merely a temporary resurgence of interest. The answer likely lies somewhere in between. Privacy coins may not dominate the market, but they have once again proven that they hold a meaningful place within the crypto ecosystem.

In a world where the boundary between private and public continues to blur, cryptocurrencies offering genuine technical privacy remain relevant. The end of 2025 made that abundantly clear.

## **Our Take (MetaAdvisor)**

**Toward the end of 2025, it became increasingly evident that projects focused on privacy were once again moving into the spotlight.** Zcash, Monero and Dash are not new projects, yet their resilience and relative strength during a period of broad market uncertainty suggest that some investors are re-evaluating the importance of cryptographic solutions centered on privacy.

Zcash, in particular, stands out due to the sharp increase in the use of shielded transactions, highlighting that privacy is more than a theoretical concept — it reflects a real demand in the digital financial space. Monero’s long-standing commitment to anonymity and Dash’s evolution from a payment-focused coin into a hybrid solution further reinforce the idea that, in certain phases, the market favors stable and functional concepts.

In our view, this development does not necessarily signal a lasting “privacy coin season,” but it does reaffirm that privacy remains a foundational value of the crypto ecosystem — especially during periods when trust in infrastructure, regulation and transparency weakens. Observing how these projects evolve throughout 2026 will therefore be particularly interesting.
