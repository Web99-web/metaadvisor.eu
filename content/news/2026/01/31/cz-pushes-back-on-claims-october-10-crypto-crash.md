# CZ Pushes Back on Claims: Who Is Really Responsible for the October 10 Crypto Crash?

Former Binance CEO Changpeng Zhao, widely known as **CZ**, has rejected allegations that the world’s largest crypto exchange played a key role in the **largest liquidation event in crypto history**. On October 10, roughly **$19 billion** in leveraged positions were wiped out within hours.

Since then, multiple narratives have circulated — ranging from stablecoin stress to liquidity withdrawal and structural market failures. Due to its size and dominance in derivatives trading, Binance quickly became the focal point of speculation.

## Why did Binance become the target?

As the largest global venue for spot and futures trading, Binance naturally draws attention during extreme market events.

A key issue is perception:
- Liquidations look like aggressive selling,
- but they are automated position closures,
- triggered by margin requirements and risk algorithms.

This often fuels the search for a single culprit, even when market mechanics offer a more straightforward explanation.

## CZ: “These claims are far-fetched”

During a Q&A session on Binance’s social media channels, CZ described the allegations as **far-fetched**. Some market participants even suggested Binance should compensate losses linked to the crash.

Zhao dismissed these accusations, emphasizing that Binance did not cause the collapse but was merely one of the venues where its effects were most visible.

## Speaking as a shareholder, not a CEO

CZ no longer runs Binance. After stepping down in November 2023 following U.S. charges related to anti-money laundering violations, he has stressed that he now speaks **solely as a shareholder and user**, not as a representative of the exchange.

## Liquidations, leverage and structural risk

The October 10 event once again highlighted core vulnerabilities in crypto markets:
- excessive leverage
- thin liquidity during stress
- cascading algorithmic liquidations

In such conditions, both long and short positions can be wiped out simultaneously, regardless of directional bias — often leaving traders searching for someone to blame.

## Our take (Metaadvisor):

- There is currently no confirmed evidence of regulatory action tied specifically to the October 10 event.
- CZ’s statement is expected given the accusations and aligns with the well-known mechanics of crypto derivatives markets.
- The crash appears to be driven by market structure rather than deliberate manipulation.
- Large platforms inevitably become targets during periods of extreme volatility.
- The episode once again highlights the risks of leverage in crypto trading.
