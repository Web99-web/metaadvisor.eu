## Berlin as a global hub for precious metals

As **gold and silver experience sharp rallies followed by swift corrections**, Berlin once again becomes a focal point for the global precious metals industry.  
The **World Money Fair 2026** takes place against this backdrop, turning the German capital into a meeting point for international market participants.

The emphasis is on **physical metals**, real delivery, and long-term market structures rather than short-term price action.

---

## What is the World Money Fair and why it matters

World Money Fair is **Europe’s most important event** dedicated to:
- precious metals  
- investment coins and bars  
- numismatics  
- minting and refining technology  

Held **annually in Berlin**, it brings together national mints, refineries, distributors, and institutional partners.

---

## Key exhibitors and European participants

### Argor-Heraeus
One of the world’s leading gold refineries, playing a central role in the European investment gold supply chain.

### Heimerle + Meule
Germany’s oldest precious metals refinery, with more than 180 years of history and strong presence across industrial and investment markets.

### Croatian Mint
The national mint of Croatia, active in international minting projects and investment-grade coin production.

---

## What happens at the fair

World Money Fair is more than an exhibition:
- **B2B partnerships and distribution agreements**
- Launches of new investment and collectible coin series
- Market discussions on gold and silver dynamics
- Analysis of investor behavior during volatile market phases

---

## A fair held amid gold and silver volatility

This year’s edition coincides with:
- strong price rallies in gold  
- elevated volatility in silver  
- subsequent market pullbacks  

Such conditions often increase demand for physical precious metals as long-term stores of value.
