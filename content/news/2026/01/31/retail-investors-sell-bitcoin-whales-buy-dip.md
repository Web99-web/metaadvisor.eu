# As Retail Investors Panic Sell, Bitcoin Whales Buy the Dip

Bitcoin has **once again dropped sharply below $77,000 in the last 2–3 hours**, intensifying market pressure. While the narrative of whales buying the dip had already emerged earlier, this renewed decline likely created **even more attractive entry levels** for large holders.

At the same time, a familiar pattern persists: **retail investors sell**, while **whales accumulate**.

## Who are retail investors — and why are they selling?

Retail investors are everyday market participants:
- smaller holders  
- people who do not follow markets daily  
- individuals who may need liquidity  

During sharp declines:
- fear of further losses takes over  
- some sell to reduce risk  
- others sell because they need cash  

These sales are rarely strategic — they are **emotional or driven by necessity**.

## What whales are doing instead

Glassnode on-chain data shows:
- wallets holding **1,000 BTC or more** are accumulating  
- wallets with **10,000+ BTC** remain neutral to slightly positive  
- the number of large holders tends to rise during corrections  

For whales, breaks below key levels like $80,000 and $77,000 signal **opportunity, not panic**.

## Why whales stay calm

Large investors:
- are not forced to sell  
- use little or no extreme leverage  
- ignore short-term headlines  

Their strategy focuses on:
- long-term positioning  
- accumulation during fear  
- buying when liquidity is thin and selling pressure is high  

This often leads to a **quiet redistribution of Bitcoin** from smaller holders to larger ones.

## Our view (Metaadvisor):

- Retail investors sell out of fear or necessity, not strategy  
- Whales deliberately accumulate during downturns  
- The renewed drop below $77,000 likely increased whale activity  
- The market is being cleaned up, while its core structure remains intact
