# Wikipedia anniversary: an indispensable tool, but not infallible

Recently, Wikipedia marked another anniversary — a milestone for one of the most influential digital projects in modern history. Today, it is difficult to imagine education, work or everyday information-seeking without it. From quick fact checks and historical context to biographies of public figures and explanations of complex topics, Wikipedia is often the first stop.

Because of this reach, Wikipedia plays a powerful role in shaping how people understand the world.

## How Wikipedia helps us in everyday life

Wikipedia fundamentally changed access to knowledge. Before it, information was scattered across books, encyclopedias and academic databases, often behind paywalls or physical libraries. Today, that knowledge is available within seconds.

Its key strengths include:
- free and open access  
- constant updates  
- an enormous range of topics  
- contributions from a global community  

For most users, Wikipedia is not a final authority but a **starting point** for deeper research — and in that role, it is unmatched.

## Openness as both strength and vulnerability

Wikipedia’s open editing model was a radical departure from traditional encyclopedias and the main reason for its success. At the same time, openness creates vulnerabilities. Articles are not static; they evolve through edits, discussions and sometimes disputes.

This is particularly sensitive when it comes to:
- political topics  
- historical events  
- wars, conflicts and ideologies  
- biographies of powerful individuals or states  

In such cases, subtle changes in tone, emphasis or sourcing can significantly influence how readers perceive an issue.

## When information becomes a tool: the Portland Communications case

These risks became especially visible in a recent case involving the UK-based PR firm **Portland Communications**. An investigation by the **Bureau of Investigative Journalism** linked the agency to practices commonly referred to as “wikilaundering”.

According to the investigation, Portland Communications allegedly relied on external contractors to commission favorable edits to Wikipedia articles related to certain clients. Rather than inserting blatant falsehoods, the changes reportedly focused on:
- softening critical sections  
- pushing negative information further down articles  
- altering context and emphasis  

Some of the edits were said to relate to articles connected to the state of Qatar ahead of the 2022 FIFA World Cup.

While there is no evidence that the company’s founder personally ordered such edits, the case highlights how attractive Wikipedia has become as a target for reputation management.

## Why this matters — especially in politics and history

Wikipedia is widely perceived as neutral and authoritative. Yet it is, in reality, a compilation of sources and interpretations. When the selection or framing of those sources is influenced, public understanding can shift over time.

In political and historical contexts, this is particularly dangerous. History is not only about facts, but about how those facts are presented and remembered. Subtle manipulation can blur responsibility, soften accountability or reshape narratives.

## Using Wikipedia responsibly

None of this diminishes Wikipedia’s value. It remains an extraordinary achievement. But it should be used wisely:
- treat it as a starting point, not a final verdict  
- review the cited sources  
- compare information with other reputable outlets  
- apply extra caution with political and historical topics  

Open knowledge is a major step forward — but only when paired with critical thinking.
