# Pump.fun launches a $3 million 30-day hackathon

Pump.fun, the platform that became synonymous with the memecoin frenzy throughout 2024 and early 2025, has announced a **30-day hackathon worth a total of $3 million**, marking its most explicit move yet toward longer-term, more serious projects. The initiative signals a clear attempt to reposition the platform beyond short-lived speculative cycles.

## From memecoins to real products

According to the announcement, **up to 12 projects** will receive funding, with each selected team eligible for **$250,000 in investment** at a **$10 million valuation**. The hackathon forms the foundation of Pump.fun’s newly created investment arm, known as *Pump Fund*.

Unlike traditional venture capital funds, Pump.fun emphasizes that participating projects **do not need to be crypto-native**. Instead, the focus is on teams that can ship products quickly, communicate openly with users and demonstrate potential for long-term relevance.

## Letting the market decide

A defining feature of the hackathon is its evaluation model. Rather than relying on closed-door juries or pitch committees, Pump.fun proposes that **the market itself acts as the judge**. Participants are expected to launch tokens, publish regular progress updates and allow community engagement and market response to signal which ideas gain traction.

This approach reflects the broader crypto ethos, where trust and value are often established publicly and in real time rather than through institutional gatekeepers.

## A response to falling volumes and speculation fatigue

The timing of the hackathon is notable. Trading volumes on Pump.fun have declined significantly from their early-2025 peak, as memecoin enthusiasm has cooled across the market. Instead of attempting to reignite the same speculative dynamics, the platform appears to be pursuing a **strategic pivot**.

As the crypto market matures, many projects are being forced to reassess growth models that rely purely on hype. Pump.fun’s move aligns with a wider industry shift toward sustainability after a prolonged period of excess speculation.

## Can Pump.fun change its image?

Despite the ambitious announcement, skepticism remains. Critics argue that a platform built on rapid speculation and short-term incentives may struggle to redefine itself as a credible incubator for long-term ventures. The success of Pump Fund will likely depend on the quality of projects it supports and whether it can maintain a clear separation between investment activities and its memecoin launchpad.

If the hackathon results in viable products and teams that endure beyond initial hype, Pump.fun could emerge as an unusual hybrid — blending open market dynamics with early-stage venture funding.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor)

- The hackathon signals Pump.fun’s recognition that pure memecoin speculation has limits.
- Market-driven evaluation fits crypto culture but introduces additional volatility.
- Long-term credibility will depend on execution, not marketing.
- Pump Fund represents an interesting experiment at the intersection of speculation and innovation.
