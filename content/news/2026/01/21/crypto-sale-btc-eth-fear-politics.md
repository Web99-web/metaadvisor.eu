# Is crypto on SALE again?

The crypto market is once again showing clear signs of nervousness. **Bitcoin is trading around $87,500**, while **Ethereum has fallen below the $2,900 level**, prompting caution among some investors and renewed buying interest among others looking for lower entry prices.

The pullback is not driven by technical failures within blockchain networks, but rather by **political and geopolitical uncertainty** currently dominating global markets.

## Why is the market nervous?

Several key factors are weighing on sentiment at the same time:

- **The World Economic Forum meeting in Davos**, where global security, war risks, and economic power shifts are being discussed  
- Rising **political uncertainty in the United States**, which traditionally impacts risk assets  
- Growing **concerns about the expansion of existing conflicts or the emergence of new ones**  
- Lack of clarity regarding future decisions by major global powers  

As a highly volatile asset class, crypto tends to react faster and more sharply to these signals than traditional markets.

## Current prices of major cryptocurrencies

At the time of publication, prices are approximately:

- **Bitcoin (BTC):** around **$87,500**  
- **Ethereum (ETH):** roughly **$2,850 – $2,900**  
- **Solana (SOL):** around **$126**  
- **Bittensor (TAO):** around **$232**  
- **Zcash (ZEC):** around **$36**  
- **XRP:** around **$1.89**

These figures are indicative only, as market conditions remain highly volatile.

## Fear or opportunity?

The market appears divided. **Some investors are stepping aside**, unwilling to take additional risk amid geopolitical tensions and war-related uncertainty. **Others see the current pullback as an opportunity**, pointing out that many assets are trading well below their recent highs.

Experienced market participants often remind that **Bitcoin has survived wars, pandemics, financial crises, and political shocks**. Ten years ago, Bitcoin traded at **around $320**, and even then it was widely considered risky, unstable, and “doomed to fail.”

## Our take (MetaAdvisor)

The current correction looks more like a **reaction to fear and uncertainty** than a loss of confidence in crypto technology itself. History shows that periods of elevated anxiety have often marked important turning points in the crypto market.

Whether this phase turns out to be a genuine “SALE” or just a temporary pause within a broader trend will become clearer in the weeks ahead.
