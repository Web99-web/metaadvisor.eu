# Strategy continues its aggressive Bitcoin strategy in 2026

Strategy began 2026 with another bitcoin purchase, adding 1,283 BTC for a total value of approximately $116 million. This move pushed the company’s total bitcoin holdings above 673,000 BTC, further cementing its position as the world’s largest corporate holder of bitcoin.

## Buying despite major paper losses

The latest acquisition comes as Strategy reported an unrealized loss of roughly $17.4 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, driven by bitcoin’s price decline toward the end of the year. These losses are accounting-based and do not reflect any sale of bitcoin holdings.

Despite the short-term pressure, the company continues to finance additional purchases through equity issuance, signaling that market volatility has not altered its long-term conviction.

## A long-term bet on bitcoin as a treasury asset

Strategy treats bitcoin as a core strategic asset rather than a speculative trade. Alongside ongoing bitcoin accumulation, the company has strengthened its U.S. dollar reserves to support liabilities and maintain balance sheet flexibility, while positioning bitcoin as a long-term hedge against inflation and monetary instability.

- Strategy remains fully committed to long-term bitcoin accumulation.  
- The move reinforces bitcoin’s role as a corporate treasury asset.  
- Volatility remains high, but the strategy clearly targets a multi-year horizon.  
- Such actions continue to divide long-term investors from short-term traders.
