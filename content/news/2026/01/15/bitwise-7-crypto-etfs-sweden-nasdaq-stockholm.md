# Bitwise Lists 7 Crypto ETFs in Sweden

US-based digital asset manager Bitwise has taken another major step in its European expansion by listing **seven crypto ETFs** on Nasdaq Stockholm. The move gives European investors, particularly in the Nordic region, a regulated and familiar way to gain exposure to cryptocurrencies through standard brokerage accounts.

Instead of managing wallets, private keys or interacting directly with crypto exchanges, investors can access crypto markets via exchange-traded products that fit neatly into traditional investment portfolios. This structure is especially attractive to institutional investors and more conservative market participants.

## Which crypto ETFs were listed?

Bitwise introduced a mix of **spot and staking-related crypto ETFs**, including:

- Bitcoin-focused ETFs  
- Ethereum-focused ETFs  
- staking-linked products connected to Ethereum and Solana  
- a diversified crypto index tracking the largest digital assets  
- a hybrid product combining exposure to Bitcoin and gold  

According to the company, all products are **fully backed by the underlying crypto assets**, held in institutional-grade cold storage and verified through regular independent audits.

## Why Sweden matters

Sweden is considered one of Europe’s most advanced financial markets, with a strong investment culture and a streamlined regulatory framework. Of particular importance is the **ISK (Investment Savings Account)** system, which simplifies taxation for investors. Depending on the brokerage platform, some of these crypto ETFs may qualify for such accounts, making crypto exposure significantly easier to manage from a tax perspective.

This stands in contrast to direct crypto ownership, where tax reporting can be complex and burdensome.

## Part of a broader European strategy

The Stockholm listings are part of Bitwise’s wider European expansion. The firm has already established a presence on other major exchanges, including London and Switzerland, positioning itself as one of the leading providers of regulated crypto investment products across the continent.

Rather than operating outside the financial system, Bitwise is embedding crypto into existing market infrastructure.

## Crypto without hype, but with long-term impact

Unlike price spikes driven by speculation or FOMO, the listing of crypto ETFs represents a quieter but more meaningful development. Institutional capital typically follows regulation, custody standards and transparency — not social media trends.

Bitwise’s move into Sweden highlights how crypto adoption is increasingly shaped by regulation and market structure, laying foundations that may prove more important than short-term price movements.
