# ElevenLabs and the Rise of Voice AI

ElevenLabs, a startup specializing in AI-powered voice generation, has emerged as one of the fastest-growing companies in artificial intelligence. According to CEO Mati Staniszewski, the company surpassed **$330 million in annual recurring revenue** in 2025 — a milestone that underscores how voice AI has moved far beyond experimentation.

This trajectory reflects a broader shift: voice is becoming a core layer of digital infrastructure, not just a creative add-on.

## A Rare Growth Curve in AI

Founded in 2022 and launched publicly in 2023, ElevenLabs scaled at a pace rarely seen even in the AI sector:
- approximately **20 months** to reach $100 million in annual revenue  
- another **10 months** to reach $200 million  
- just **five additional months** to exceed $330 million  

Such acceleration highlights both strong product-market fit and surging enterprise demand.

## Who Is Using ElevenLabs?

ElevenLabs’ technology is now used by:
- **Fortune 500 companies**
- startups building customer support and sales tools
- media organizations and content creators
- call centers deploying AI voice agents
- developers integrating voice into autonomous AI systems

The company says its voice agents already handle **more than 50,000 calls per month**, signaling real-world, production-scale adoption.

## Funding, Valuation, and Strategic Importance

In 2025, ElevenLabs raised **$180 million in Series C funding** at a valuation of **$3.3 billion**. Just months later, additional investors acquired employee shares, pushing valuation higher and reinforcing institutional confidence.

Backers such as a16z, ICONIQ Growth, and Sequoia view voice as a foundational interface for the next generation of AI applications.

## Voice, Identity, and Regulation

Rapid growth also brings new challenges:
- misuse of AI-generated voices
- deepfake risks and impersonation
- ownership and licensing of vocal identity  

ElevenLabs has begun partnering with public figures to license voices legally, signaling an industry-wide recognition that regulation and ethical frameworks are unavoidable.

## Our Take (MetaAdvisor)

- Voice AI is becoming critical digital infrastructure  
- ElevenLabs demonstrates how quickly AI can generate real revenue  
- Voice will play a central role in support, media, and AI agents  
- Regulation around identity and misuse is inevitable  
- Compared to crypto, AI is currently monetizing at a much faster pace
