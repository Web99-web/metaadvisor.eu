# Dubai Insurance Launches Crypto Wallet

**Dubai Insurance**, one of the oldest insurance providers in the United Arab Emirates, has officially launched a **crypto-enabled digital wallet** that allows policyholders to **pay insurance premiums and receive claims settlements in digital assets**.

The initiative represents the **first such offering in the UAE insurance sector** and marks another step toward integrating blockchain technology into traditional financial and insurance systems.

## How the crypto wallet works

The new wallet enables:

- payment of insurance premiums in digital assets  
- settlement of insurance claims in cryptocurrency  
- institutional-grade custody of assets through a regulated partner  

The infrastructure is built in partnership with **Zodia Custody**, an institutional digital asset custodian backed by major financial institutions and designed to meet strict regulatory and compliance standards.

## Regulation as a foundation, not a barrier

Dubai Insurance emphasized that the wallet operates **fully within the existing regulatory and compliance frameworks of the UAE**.

This includes:

- strict KYC and AML requirements  
- treatment of digital assets as regulated financial instruments  
- secure, institutional custody of client funds  

Over recent years, the UAE has positioned itself as one of the most crypto-friendly yet regulated jurisdictions globally, and this launch reinforces that strategy.

## Insurance companies embrace digital assets

Dubai Insurance’s move is part of a broader trend in which insurers worldwide are experimenting with:

- crypto-denominated insurance products  
- blockchain-based claims processing  
- digital assets as part of reserves or investment strategies  

In late 2025, for example, U.S.-based insurer Meanwhile raised $82 million to expand its Bitcoin-denominated insurance and savings products, signaling growing confidence in crypto-native financial models.

## Open questions remain

Dubai Insurance has not yet disclosed:

- which specific cryptocurrencies will be supported  
- whether the wallet will be available across all insurance products  
- plans for international expansion  

Nevertheless, the direction is clear: **digital assets are steadily becoming part of mainstream insurance infrastructure.**

- A major step for institutional crypto adoption beyond banking  
- Strong focus on regulation and custody reduces operational risk  
- Insurance may emerge as a trusted gateway for broader crypto usage
