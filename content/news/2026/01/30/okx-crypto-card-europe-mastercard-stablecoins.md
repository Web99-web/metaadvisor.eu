# OKX Launches Crypto Card in Europe

Crypto exchange **OKX** has officially introduced the **OKX Card** for users across the European Union, allowing them to spend **stablecoins** anywhere **Mastercard** is accepted. The move marks another step toward integrating crypto into everyday payments.

## How does the OKX Card work?

The OKX Card is issued in partnership with **Monavate**, a regulated Electronic Money Institution (EMI), and operates on the **Mastercard network**. It can be used online and offline at millions of merchants across Europe.

At launch, the card supports:
- **USDC (Circle)**
- **USDG (Global Dollar, Paxos)**

Payments are made through **OKX Pay**, a **self-custodial wallet**, meaning users retain control over their assets.

## Regulation and MiCA compliance

OKX is registered as a **Crypto-Asset Service Provider (CASP)** in the EU and complies with the **MiCA regulatory framework**. The card itself is regulated via Monavate and follows strict **AML and KYC standards**.

## Why this matters

The OKX Card highlights several key trends:
- Stablecoins are increasingly used for **real-world payments**  
- Crypto is integrating into existing financial rails  
- Europe is emerging as a regulated yet innovation-friendly crypto market  

## The bigger picture: Crypto cards return

After earlier setbacks, crypto cards are making a comeback — this time with clearer regulations and stronger institutional backing. This could mark a turning point for everyday crypto adoption.
