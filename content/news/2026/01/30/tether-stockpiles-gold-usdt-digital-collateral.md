# Tether Stockpiles Gold: 1–2 Tons per Week, Bunkers, and a New Role for USDT

Tether, the issuer of **USDT**, the world’s largest stablecoin, is quietly building one of the **largest private gold reserves on the planet**. According to company statements, Tether adds **1 to 2 tons of physical gold every week**, bringing total holdings to approximately **140 tons**, worth tens of billions of dollars.

The gold is not stored in traditional financial centers, but in a **high-security bunker**, a former nuclear shelter in Switzerland — infrastructure typically reserved for governments and central banks, not crypto companies.

## Gold in focus: volatility, demand, and new highs

Gold prices have recently shown both strong momentum and sharp volatility. At one point, prices **surged to around $5,500 per ounce**, signaling intense global demand. A correction followed, with the **current price hovering around $4,980 per ounce**.

These movements suggest a market in transition. Gold is no longer seen solely as a passive safe haven, but increasingly as an actively traded financial asset. In this context, Tether’s aggressive gold accumulation becomes even more significant.

## USDT, XAUT, and the collateral question

Tether has long maintained that USDT is fully backed by reserves. What is changing is the composition of those reserves — with a growing emphasis on **hard, physical assets**. Alongside cash and government bonds, gold is becoming a strategic pillar of Tether’s balance sheet.

At the same time, Tether issues **XAUT**, a token directly backed by physical gold. Rising interest in XAUT highlights growing demand for **digital access to physical security**.

Unlike traditional gold ETFs, XAUT enables direct tokenization of gold without banks or legacy brokers.

## Why this matters

Tether’s strategy signals a deeper paradigm shift:

- stablecoins are no longer just “digital dollars”
- physical collateral is regaining central importance
- the boundary between crypto, gold, and traditional finance is fading

If a private crypto company can accumulate gold reserves comparable to those of smaller nations, a critical question emerges: **Who defines monetary stability in the future — states or infrastructure?**

## Our Take (MetaAdvisor)

- Tether is positioning itself as a hybrid between a crypto issuer and a “digital central bank”
- Gold is returning to the core of the global financial system
- Stablecoins are evolving beyond simple trading instruments
