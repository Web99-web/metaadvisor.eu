# Who really controls TikTok in the US?

TikTok has spent years at the center of political and regulatory debate. While users see entertainment, governments see **data security, algorithmic power, and geopolitical influence**.

Under sustained pressure, TikTok quietly restructured its US operations — a move with major implications.

## Why was TikTok a concern?

The issue centered on **ByteDance**, TikTok’s Chinese parent company. US lawmakers feared:

- potential access to American user data  
- algorithmic influence on public discourse  
- lack of US legal oversight  

In 2024, Congress passed legislation forcing a **separation of TikTok’s US operations**.

## The new structure: TikTok USDS Joint Venture

TikTok formed **TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC**, responsible solely for US operations.

Key points:

- About **80% owned by non-Chinese investors**  
- **ByteDance retains 19.9%**, losing control  
- US entity manages:
  - content moderation
  - data protection
  - algorithm oversight
  - technical operations

## Who owns it now?

A consortium leads ownership:

- **Oracle** – technology and security partner  
- **Silver Lake** – private equity firm  
- **MGX** – AI-focused investment firm  

Each holds roughly **15%**. Oracle also oversees data storage and audits algorithm security.

## What about the algorithm?

The algorithm is **licensed** from ByteDance but:

- runs on US infrastructure  
- undergoes security audits  
- operates under US oversight  

## What does this mean for users?

For users, little changes. Behind the scenes, TikTok now operates **under US jurisdiction and security rules**.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor)

- TikTok US is no longer under Chinese control  
- The model may shape future tech regulation  
- Control over algorithms is becoming a geopolitical issue
