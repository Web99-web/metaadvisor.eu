# AI agents will soon negotiate, trade, and collaborate on their own — Ethereum makes it possible

Ethereum is entering a new phase where artificial intelligence is no longer just a passive tool. With the introduction of **ERC-8004**, AI agents gain something fundamental: **a digital identity, reputation, and the ability to act autonomously on-chain**.

In short: AI gets an ID.

## Why does AI need an identity?

Modern AI systems are powerful, but they lack a crucial element:  
they don’t know **who they are**, **who to trust**, or **how to prove reliability**.

Inside closed corporate systems, this is handled via API keys and permission lists. But once AI needs to operate across platforms, companies, or jurisdictions, those models break down.

Without identity:
- AI can’t sign contracts  
- can’t build trust  
- can’t cooperate independently  

## What does ERC-8004 introduce?

The standard defines three lightweight on-chain registries:

### 1️⃣ Identity registry  
Each AI agent receives a **unique on-chain identifier**, similar to an NFT, describing:
- its purpose  
- supported protocols  
- interaction methods  

### 2️⃣ Reputation registry  
Performance feedback is stored in a transparent, verifiable way, allowing trust to emerge organically.

### 3️⃣ Discovery registry  
AI agents can find, compare, and select other agents to work with — autonomously.

## What does this unlock?

- AI negotiating prices  
- AI executing smart contracts  
- AI collaborating across organizations  
- AI building long-term economic reputation  

## Why Ethereum?

Ethereum provides:
- decentralization  
- smart contracts  
- global accessibility  
- censorship resistance  

This makes it the natural foundation for **a global autonomous AI economy**.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor)

- ERC-8004 is foundational infrastructure  
- Identity and reputation are essential for autonomous AI  
- Ethereum positions itself at the core of AI-driven markets
