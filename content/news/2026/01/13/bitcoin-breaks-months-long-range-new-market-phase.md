# Bitcoin exits a prolonged consolidation phase

After months of trading within a relatively narrow range, Bitcoin showed clear signs of breaking out of its long consolidation phase in mid-January 2026. The period during which price oscillated roughly between $90,000 and $92,000 was marked by hesitation, but also by gradual liquidity accumulation beneath the surface.

The move above that range has not come as a sudden euphoric spike, but rather as a controlled and technically structured advance — a pattern many analysts associate with a maturing market environment.

## Macroeconomic backdrop and inflation

One of the key catalysts behind Bitcoin’s move higher was the latest U.S. inflation data. The CPI (Consumer Price Index) confirmed a continued cooling of inflationary pressures, reinforcing expectations that monetary policy may become less restrictive in the coming months.

Such conditions are traditionally supportive of risk assets. In this environment, Bitcoin is increasingly viewed not merely as a speculative instrument, but as an asset sensitive to global liquidity trends and monetary expectations.

## Market structure and reduced volatility

Unlike previous cycles, Bitcoin’s current advance is accompanied by noticeably lower short-term volatility. A major reason lies in the evolving market structure, where open interest in options now exceeds that of futures contracts.

Options allow for more sophisticated risk management and reduce reliance on aggressively leveraged positions. The result is smoother price behavior, suggesting a stronger presence of institutional and professional market participants rather than purely speculative trading.

## Bitcoin and gold: a meaningful divergence

Additional attention is drawn to the renewed divergence between Bitcoin and gold. Historically, periods in which Bitcoin decoupled from gold have often preceded strong upside phases for BTC.

While gold delivered significant gains earlier, a growing number of analysts believe Bitcoin could take the lead in 2026 — particularly if global liquidity continues to expand and monetary conditions ease further.

## Psychological levels and the path toward $100,000

Holding above newly established support zones opens the door to higher psychological price levels. The $100,000 mark is increasingly discussed not as an extreme scenario, but as a plausible next milestone should macroeconomic and structural factors remain supportive.

Notably, the market has yet to display signs of excessive euphoria, suggesting the current move is driven more by fundamentals and structure than by emotion.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor)

- Bitcoin’s current advance appears technically healthier than many prior rallies.
- Lower volatility points to market maturation, but also limits short-term speculative swings.
- Macroeconomic conditions and monetary policy are becoming more influential than narrative-driven momentum.
- A renewed influx of retail investors could significantly alter market dynamics.
- The $100,000 level looks increasingly realistic, but still requires confirmation from broader market strength.
