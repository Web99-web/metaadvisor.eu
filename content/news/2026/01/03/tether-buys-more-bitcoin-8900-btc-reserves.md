# Tether keeps accumulating Bitcoin as markets hesitate

Tether, the issuer of the world’s largest stablecoin USDT, opened 2026 with a strong signal to the crypto market. In the fourth quarter of 2025, the company acquired **exactly 8,888.88 BTC**, an investment worth approximately **$780 million** at the time of purchase.

The acquisition follows a policy introduced in 2023, under which Tether allocates up to **15% of its realized quarterly operating profits** to systematic Bitcoin purchases.

## How Tether funds its Bitcoin purchases

Unlike companies that issue debt or raise equity to buy Bitcoin, Tether relies exclusively on **operating profits**. These profits primarily stem from managing the reserves backing USDT, which are largely invested in **short-term U.S. Treasuries** and repo instruments.

In a high interest-rate environment, these assets generate substantial income. That income, in turn, allows Tether to expand its Bitcoin holdings without compromising liquidity or the stability of its stablecoin liabilities.

## Why this purchase matters

Following the latest acquisition, Tether’s Bitcoin reserves now exceed **96,000 BTC**, placing the company among the largest corporate Bitcoin holders globally.

The timing is also notable. Bitcoin traded in a relatively narrow range toward the end of 2025 and into early 2026, with thinner liquidity and cautious investor sentiment. Tether’s move signals long-term conviction in Bitcoin as a strategic reserve asset rather than a short-term trade.

## Bitcoin as part of a treasury strategy

Tether’s approach sets it apart from opportunistic corporate buyers. Bitcoin plays a defined role within a **systematic treasury strategy** focused on diversification and long-term value preservation.

As demand for USDT grows and macro conditions remain favorable, the company could continue accumulating Bitcoin without external financing or headline-grabbing capital raises.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor)

- Tether is increasingly acting like a long-term institutional Bitcoin holder.  
- Funding purchases from profits significantly reduces financial risk.  
- Higher interest rates indirectly support ongoing Bitcoin accumulation.  
- Tether is emerging as one of the market’s most influential “silent” BTC buyers.  
- Sustained accumulation tightens Bitcoin’s available supply over time.
