# Coinbase enables loans of up to $1 million using staked Ethereum

US-based crypto exchange **Coinbase** has introduced a new financial service that allows users to **borrow up to $1 million by using staked Ethereum (ETH) as collateral — without having to sell their cryptocurrency**.

The move represents another step toward integrating crypto assets into familiar financial services such as lending and collateralization, but within a **regulated, centralized framework**.

## How does a loan backed by staked ETH work?

Users who already have **Ethereum staked** (staking means locking ETH to help secure the network and earn rewards) can now **borrow against the value of that ETH instead of selling it**.

The ETH remains locked as **collateral**, while the user receives liquidity in fiat currency or stablecoins. This allows borrowers to maintain exposure to potential upside in Ethereum’s price.

## Can users with smaller amounts participate?

It’s important to distinguish between two things:

### Ethereum staking
- available even with **small amounts of ETH**
- accessible to a wide range of users

### Loans backed by staked ETH
- technically possible, but  
- in practice typically aimed at users with **larger collateral values**
- subject to minimum thresholds and loan-to-value (LTV) requirements

In short: **staking is for everyone**, while **loans are realistically geared toward higher-value portfolios**.

## Why does this matter?

This development highlights how crypto assets are increasingly treated as **legitimate financial collateral**, similar to stocks or real estate.

By offering this product in a **regulated and familiar environment**, Coinbase is bringing features long associated with DeFi into a centralized platform — an approach that may appeal especially to institutional and more conservative users.

## What are the risks?

If Ethereum’s price drops sharply, users may:
- be required to **add more collateral**, or  
- face **partial liquidation**, similar to margin positions in traditional finance

As with any leveraged product, this service requires a **clear understanding of market and liquidation risks**.
