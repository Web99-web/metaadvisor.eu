Gold briefly **touched the 5,093 level** in a short upward impulse before **pulling back toward 5,065**. The move comes amid heightened volatility across global markets and raises a key question: **is this merely profit-taking, or the setup for another attempt to break above 5,100?**

## Why did gold pull back from the high?

When prices reach **psychologically significant levels**, many market participants lock in profits. This behavior is typical among short-term traders and funds reacting to rapid price movements, especially after a strong rally.

In this case, the pullback does not resemble panic selling, but rather a **controlled cooling-off phase** following a test of a key resistance zone.

## How is gold behaving compared to crypto?

During the same period, **Ethereum fell by roughly 4% in a single day**, while other risk-on crypto assets also remained under pressure. This divergence once again highlights gold’s role as a **relative safe haven during periods of increased uncertainty**.

While crypto assets tend to react quickly and aggressively, gold typically moves more slowly and with greater discipline — even during short-term pullbacks.

## Why is the 5,100 level so important?

The area around **5,100** represents a clear **psychological resistance level**. A break above such zones often:
- attracts new capital  
- increases trading volume  
- accelerates short-term momentum  

If gold stabilizes after this pullback, the market is likely to test this level again in the near term.

## Our view (MetaAdvisor)

- The pullback from 5,093 looks like **profit-taking, not a trend reversal**
- Gold remains resilient while risk assets weaken
- The 5,100 psychological level remains the key short-term focus
- The next move may be driven by continued volatility in crypto markets
