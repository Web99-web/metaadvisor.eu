# Who controls Bitcoin today?  
## From 10.10 to 25.01 — price stagnation, soaring metals, and silent institutional accumulation

More than three months have passed since the **mysterious, almost choreographed crash on October 10**, an event many analysts still describe as **a coordinated institutional strike**.  
Today, on January 25, 2026, the picture has become clearer — we can now see **what actually happened afterwards, and who is taking control of the market.**

The answer is not surprising:

### **Institutions are accumulating aggressively. Retail investors are stepping aside. Bitcoin’s price is flat — but the power structure is shifting.**

---

## 🔥 1. Bitcoin has not recovered — even after briefly touching 97,000 USD

Between 10.10 and today:

- Bitcoin moved between **88,000 and 97,000 USD**,  
- several times it looked ready to break **100,000 USD**,  
- but each time it faced a sudden drop triggered by global uncertainty.

The pattern is clear:  
Bitcoin behaves like an asset whose price is being **kept in a controlled range**.

A market not driven by free movement — but by **systematic accumulation**.

---

## 🔥 2. Meanwhile — gold and silver are exploding upward

While Bitcoin remains stuck:

- **gold broke above 5,000 USD**,  
- **silver surged past 100 USD.**

This signals that global markets expect:

- rising macro risks,  
- deepening uncertainty,  
- and demand for traditional “hard” stores of value.

At least for now, Bitcoin is treated more like a **risk asset**, not a safe haven.

---

## 🔥 3. And what are institutions doing? Buying — more heavily than ever.

The biggest paradox of the past three months is simple:

### **The price is flat. Institutions are buying. Retail investors are exiting.**

During this period we observed:

- the launch of new **bank-issued stablecoins**,  
- the expansion of **blockchain settlement networks** between major banks,  
- new institutional **BTC/ETH trading desks**,  
- consistent inflows into Bitcoin ETFs,  
- rising interest in tokenization and digital asset infrastructure.

All of it confirms what was suggested in earlier reporting:

### **institutions are quietly taking positions — while retail investors panic and withdraw.**

This is not a market cycle — it is a **transfer of ownership.**

---

## 🔥 4. The statements from the November 25 analysis now sound even more accurate

In the analysis published two months ago —  
**<a href="https://metaadvisor.eu/news/who-attacked-bitcoin-10-10-banks-hostile-takeover/" style="text-decoration:none; color:inherit;">Was the ‘Big Short’ on 10.10 a hostile takeover attempt by banks and institutions trying to seize control of Bitcoin?</a>** —  
well-known analysts **Ran Neuner, Simon Dixon and Arthur Hayes** issued clear warnings:

- **“The crash was not accidental.”**  
- **“MSTR has become a systemic risk.”**  
- **“When the system feels threatened, it attacks.”**

With the benefit of hindsight, their statements now ring **even more true.**

Since 10.10, Bitcoin has entered what can only be described as:

### **a phase of strategic accumulation under the oversight of major financial players.**

A phase where:

- volatility decreases,  
- price is held in an accumulation band,  
- retail capitulates,  
- institutions expand their exposure.

---

## 🧠 Our view (MetaAdvisor)

As of January 25, 2026, something can be said openly:

### **Bitcoin currently behaves like a market in the middle of a power struggle — not a freely moving asset.**

The 10.10 crash was:

- **a stress test**,  
- **a structural market strike**,  
- **a pressure move targeting MSTR and similar players**,  
- **a signal that TradFi is now entering the game aggressively.**

And the past three months strongly resemble well-known historical patterns:

### **first break the price, shake confidence, seize positions — then dictate the next phase of growth.**

If this interpretation is correct, then 2026 could become:

👉 **the year of final institutional accumulation**,  
👉 **the year Bitcoin shifts into the hands of major players**,  
👉 **and the year the foundation is laid for the next major cycle — under a new power dynamic.**
