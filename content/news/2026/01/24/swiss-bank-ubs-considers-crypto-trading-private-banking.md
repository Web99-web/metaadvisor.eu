One of the world’s largest and most prominent **Swiss banks**, **UBS**, is reportedly considering allowing its **wealthiest private banking clients** to trade cryptocurrencies such as **Bitcoin (BTC)** and **Ether (ETH)**. The move would further highlight the growing interest of traditional financial institutions in digital assets.

According to a Bloomberg report, UBS plans to initially offer crypto trading to selected clients in Switzerland, with a potential expansion to the **Asia-Pacific region** and the **United States** at a later stage. While the bank has not officially confirmed the details, it is said to be in the process of selecting technology and service partners for the offering.

UBS is widely recognized as the **world’s largest wealth manager**, serving ultra-high-net-worth individuals, families, and institutional clients. When a bank of this scale considers direct crypto trading, it is generally seen as a strong signal that cryptocurrencies are becoming part of the **financial mainstream**, rather than remaining a fringe or experimental asset class.

## UBS and blockchain: more than just trading

Even before considering crypto trading for clients, UBS has been actively exploring the use of **blockchain technology** in traditional finance. The bank has participated in the development of **tokenized financial products**, where conventional investment instruments are represented digitally on blockchain networks.

One such initiative is **uMINT**, a tokenized US dollar money market fund built on the **Ethereum blockchain**. Projects like these aim to improve efficiency, transparency, and transaction speed while reducing operational costs.

## Collaboration with Ant International and the Alipay ecosystem

On the payments side, UBS has expanded its blockchain efforts through a collaboration with **Ant International**, a global financial technology company best known for **Alipay**, one of the world’s largest digital payment platforms.

As part of this partnership, UBS and Ant International are testing **tokenized bank deposits** via the **UBS Digital Cash** platform in Singapore. The goal is to enable **near-instant cross-border payments**, settling transactions in minutes rather than days, as is often the case with traditional banking systems.

Ant International provides payment and financial services to millions of businesses and users worldwide, particularly within the **Alipay+ ecosystem**, which connects numerous local and international payment networks. The collaboration illustrates how established banks are using blockchain technology to modernize global payment infrastructure.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor):

- The focus on ultra-wealthy clients reflects a cautious, risk-managed approach to crypto adoption.  
- In this context, blockchain is being positioned primarily as an infrastructure upgrade rather than a radical transformation of the financial system.
