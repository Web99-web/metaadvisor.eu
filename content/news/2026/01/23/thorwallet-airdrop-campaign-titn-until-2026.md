# ThorWallet launches new airdrop campaign with rewards of up to 5,000,000 TITN tokens

ThorWallet displayed an in-app notification today, January 23, announcing a **new airdrop campaign** that includes a total of **5,000,000 TITN tokens** allocated for participating users. The campaign is fully integrated within the application, with detailed rules and conditions available through the official documentation.

According to clearly stated information, **Airdrop One runs until April 25, 2026**, confirming that this is not a short-term or weekend-only promotion, but a longer-term reward and activity-based program.

## What the ThorWallet airdrop includes

The airdrop campaign is based on **user activity within the ThorWallet ecosystem**. While exact scoring weights are defined by campaign rules, it is evident that rewards are linked to:

- active use of the wallet  
- interaction with supported crypto services  
- participation in DeFi features available within the app  

The application also features a **leaderboard**, displaying wallet addresses and estimated rewards. This further confirms that the campaign is continuous rather than a one-time token distribution.

Based on information visible within the app, **Airdrop One rewards are distributed among the top 300 ranked users** on the leaderboard, with rankings determined by the level of activity across the ThorWallet ecosystem.

## Who can participate

Participation in the campaign is not limited to existing users only. Based on available information:

- users must have the **ThorWallet application installed**
- registration and acceptance of **Terms & Conditions** are required
- rewards are accumulated through normal usage of the app  

While no publicly stated minimum transaction amount is specified, the leaderboard structure and reward distribution strongly indicate that **user activity level plays a role in scoring and ranking**.

## Campaign duration

The official documentation explicitly states:

> **Airdrop One ends on April 25, 2026**

This means that users planning **future crypto purchases, sales, swaps, or staking activities** during this period may simultaneously participate in the airdrop program — without requiring additional steps beyond regular app usage.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor)

ThorWallet positions this campaign as a long-term engagement initiative rather than a short-lived marketing event. The clearly defined duration, visible leaderboard, and published rules suggest an emphasis on **consistent wallet usage** rather than short-term hype.

For users already planning to use a wallet or DeFi services over the coming months, this campaign may represent an additional incentive — but not a guarantee of any financial outcome.
