# Crypto enters a hot phase: DOGE +15%, PEPE +20% while Bitcoin traded above $87,300

**The crypto market has entered a much more speculative phase of the rally. Bitcoin traded above $87,300 during the latest move and was still holding above $85,000 on the morning of September 22. Dogecoin gained more than 15%, PEPE more than 20%, XRP around 7%, Solana about 5%, and Ethereum roughly 3%. Behind that wave of gains is one particularly important figure: more than $1 billion in leveraged crypto positions were liquidated over the past 24 hours, with about $844 million, or roughly 82% of the total, coming from short positions.**

A large part of the latest upward move therefore did not come only from new buyers. Rising prices forced many traders who had bet on a decline to close their shorts, creating additional buying pressure and pushing prices even higher. CoinGlass data cited by CoinDesk showed that around 135,000 trader positions were closed, while the largest single liquidation was a Bitcoin position worth nearly $21 million on Hyperliquid.

## Bitcoin traded above $87,300, but altcoins are taking the spotlight

Bitcoin reached levels above $87,300 during the rally and was still trading above $85,000 on the morning of September 22. That is a strong move on its own, but what is happening beneath the surface of the market is even more interesting.

Dogecoin gained more than 15% and once again became one of the strongest major tokens, while XRP rose around 7%, Solana about 5%, and Ethereum roughly 3%. That breadth shows that capital is no longer staying only in Bitcoin but is spreading into more volatile parts of the crypto market.

That is usually a positive sign while a rally is developing, but it can also become a warning when the riskiest tokens begin to outperform Bitcoin by a wide margin. At that point, part of the market is no longer buying only because of long-term conviction, but because traders expect fast gains to continue.

## PEPE jumps more than 20% and confirms the return of speculation

PEPE is another clear sign of how much market sentiment has changed. The token gained more than 20% in 24 hours, while some market sources recorded a move of around 22%. CoinGecko data showed its market capitalization climbing back above roughly $2 billion, while daily trading volume also rose sharply.

The comparison with Bitcoin is even more important. When BTC gains around 5% while PEPE rises more than 20%, the market is showing a classic high-beta rotation in which investors increasingly seek tokens capable of producing much larger short-term moves.

PEPE does not currently have a confirmed new fundamental catalyst that would independently explain such a surge. The move fits more naturally into the broader memecoin rally and the short-squeeze dynamics affecting the wider crypto market.

## $844 million in shorts liquidated in one day

The most important number in the current rally may not be Bitcoin's price or the double-digit gains in memecoins, but the structure of the liquidations.

More than $1 billion in leveraged crypto positions were liquidated over the past 24 hours, with around $844 million tied to bearish or short positions. That represents roughly 82% of all liquidations.

Bitcoin alone accounted for about $608 million in liquidations, while Ethereum accounted for roughly $181 million. When prices rise quickly enough, traders with leveraged shorts can run out of sufficient collateral, forcing exchanges to close their positions by buying back the underlying asset.

That forced buying pushes prices higher, puts another group of shorts under pressure, and can create a chain reaction known as a short squeeze.

## The short squeeze has already done a lot of the work

This is where the most important question for the next phase of the rally begins. If a large part of the latest move was driven by forced short covering, the market loses an important automatic source of demand once those positions have been cleared.

CoinDesk reported that the intensity of liquidations had already started to decline. That means further gains will increasingly require genuine new buyers rather than traders being forced to exit bearish positions.

That does not mean the rally has to end. In fact, several calmer sessions of consolidation above roughly $84,000 to $85,000 could be healthier than another vertical jump.

If Bitcoin holds that area while ETH, SOL, XRP and other altcoins maintain solid momentum, it would suggest that real demand remains in the market even after the largest part of the short squeeze has passed.

## Dogecoin and PEPE show the rally is entering a later phase

Double-digit daily gains in memecoins matter because they often accompany a shift in market psychology. In the early stage of a rally, capital typically moves first into Bitcoin, then into Ethereum and larger altcoins, and later into higher-risk tokens.

DOGE above 15% and PEPE above 20% are therefore not just isolated price moves. They show that investors are once again becoming willing to take significantly more risk.

That phase can continue and produce further large gains, but historically it is also more volatile because an increasing share of buying comes from the expectation that prices will keep rising simply because they are already rising fast.

A strong memecoin rally can therefore be both confirmation of bullish sentiment and a warning that the market is no longer in an early stage of capital rotation.

## Ethereum, Solana and XRP confirm the breadth of the rally

So far, the move is not limited to Bitcoin and memecoins. Ethereum, Solana and XRP are also participating, showing that market breadth remains strong.

That is healthier than a scenario in which only a few tokens rise while the rest of the market stagnates. The real test, however, begins when short liquidations stop being one of the main drivers.

If altcoins continue to rise, or at least hold their newly gained ranges while liquidation activity declines, that would suggest fresh capital is still entering the market.

If breadth deteriorates quickly as the short squeeze fades, the latest jump could turn out to have been a short-lived euphoric phase.

## Bitcoin now has to prove that $85,000 can become support

The area around $84,000 to $85,000 is now especially important. Holding that range through several calmer sessions would allow the market to reduce leverage and confirm the newly reclaimed level.

A move back below roughly $82,000 to $83,000, especially if accompanied by simultaneous weakness in ETH, SOL, XRP and memecoins, would be the first more serious sign that the current momentum is losing strength.

On the other hand, stabilization above $85,000 with continued broad participation across the market would show that the rally can continue even without constant short liquidations.

## Crypto is no longer just strong, it is getting hot

The market has moved through several phases in a very short period of time. Bitcoin first reclaimed key levels, then Ethereum and larger altcoins joined the move, and now DOGE and PEPE are posting double-digit daily gains.

At the same time, around $844 million in short positions were liquidated. That is a very clear sign that the market has shifted from a simple recovery phase into one dominated by strong momentum trading and rising speculation.

Such a phase can continue, but the market now has to answer the most important question: what happens after the short squeeze ends?

If fresh capital arrives, the current rally could enter another phase. If it does not, the latest double-digit gains may turn out to be a sign that the market has moved too far too quickly.
