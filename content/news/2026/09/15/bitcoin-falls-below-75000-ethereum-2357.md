# Bitcoin falls to $74,914, Ethereum to $2,357: what hit the crypto market today?

**The crypto market went through a sharp wave of selling on September 15. Bitcoin fell during the day to around $74,914, Ethereum to $2,357, Solana to about $95.7, Hyperliquid to $75.14 and Bittensor to around $213. By 9 p.m., parts of the market had already bounced from their daily lows, but the main reasons behind today’s decline had not disappeared.**

This time, the move was not driven by a single problem inside the crypto industry. The selloff coincided with rising U.S. Treasury yields, high oil prices, expectations surrounding the Federal Reserve’s next decision and disappointment over a U.S. bill intended to create clearer rules for digital assets.

## Bitcoin breaks below $75,000 as altcoins fall harder

Bitcoin fell during the day to approximately **$74,914**, briefly losing the psychologically important $75,000 level, while Ethereum dropped at the same time to around **$2,357**. Pressure was even more visible across several major altcoins: Solana fell to about **$95.7**, Hyperliquid to approximately **$75.14**, and Bittensor to the **$213** area.

A first recovery appeared by the evening. Bitcoin moved back toward $76,000, Ethereum climbed above $2,400 again, while TAO, HYPE and SOL also bounced from their daily lows. The move shows that buyers appeared after the sharp decline, but it does not yet confirm that the market has found a final bottom.

## Brent near $103 as WTI trades around $101

One of the important factors behind today’s risk-off environment came from the energy market. Brent traded at approximately **$103 per barrel** during the day, while WTI was around **$101**.

The move has been linked to new supply problems from Saudi Arabia, disruptions affecting the East-West Pipeline and loading difficulties at the port of Yanbu. War and transport disruptions across the region have added further pressure, while some physical oil cargoes in Europe have reportedly traded above $130 per barrel.

For financial markets, such a rise in oil prices is not only an energy story. Higher energy costs increase inflationary pressure at exactly the moment when investors are trying to assess how long the Federal Reserve may need to keep monetary policy restrictive.

## U.S. yields above 5% add pressure to risk assets

The yield on the 10-year U.S. Treasury moved above **5%**, reaching its highest levels in almost two decades. For crypto, this is an important problem because high yields on U.S. government debt create serious competition for riskier assets.

When investors can earn a high return from instruments considered significantly safer than cryptocurrencies, part of the capital naturally moves away from more volatile markets. The combination of elevated energy prices and U.S. Treasury yields above 5% therefore created an especially difficult environment for Bitcoin, Ethereum and technology stocks.

## Markets await the Federal Reserve decision

The Federal Reserve is holding its two-day FOMC meeting on September 15 and 16, with the monetary policy decision and press conference scheduled for September 16. Markets are not only trying to determine whether the Fed will change interest rates again, but more importantly what the central bank will say about the months ahead.

If high energy prices and inflation pressures lead the Fed to conclude that rates need to remain elevated or rise further, pressure on risk assets could continue. A softer message could quickly change sentiment in the opposite direction. After a day like today, when part of the leveraged long positioning has already been forced out of the market, even a change in expectations can trigger a sharp counter-move.

## CLARITY Act fails key procedural step

The crypto market also followed the U.S. **Digital Asset Market Clarity Act**, a bill intended to create a clearer regulatory framework for digital assets. The U.S. Senate failed to secure enough votes to advance the legislation: 60 votes were required for the procedural step, while the proposal received 49.

For the crypto industry, this represents a significant disappointment because it once again delays the creation of a clearer regulatory framework in the United States. The reaction was also visible in the market itself, with Bitcoin falling further after the vote and pressure spreading to companies closely linked to crypto.

## Liquidations added momentum to the decline

When a market begins falling quickly, leveraged long positions can accelerate the move. If the price reaches a level where an exchange automatically closes a trader’s leveraged position, the selling is no longer voluntary. The position is liquidated and creates additional selling pressure. If many positions are concentrated around similar levels, a relatively normal decline can quickly turn into a much sharper move lower.

The same mechanism can later work in the opposite direction. After long positions have been flushed out and new short positions have been opened, a sudden recovery can force short traders to close their positions and accelerate the rebound.

## Evening rebound is not yet confirmation of a reversal

After the daily lows, the market recovered somewhat by the evening. Bitcoin moved back above $75,000, Ethereum above $2,400, while SOL, HYPE and TAO also moved away from their lowest levels of the day. That is a positive short-term sign, but a recovery of a few percent after a strong selloff is not yet enough to conclude that the trend has turned positive again.

For Bitcoin, it will be particularly important to see whether the price can reclaim the **$77,000** area on a sustained basis, while Ethereum will be watched for a move back toward approximately **$2,450–$2,500**. If prices return to today’s lows, a second test of those levels could show whether buyers are prepared to defend them or whether another wave of selling is ahead.

## Our view

* Brent traded at around **$103 per barrel**, while WTI was near $101.
* **A 10-year U.S. Treasury yield above 5% creates serious competition for risk assets.**
* The CLARITY Act failed a key procedural step in the Senate, adding further pressure to the crypto market.
* **Leverage and forced liquidations likely accelerated the decline after important short-term levels were broken.**
* SOL, HYPE and TAO fell alongside BTC and ETH, showing that today’s move was not limited to the two largest cryptocurrencies.
* **The rebound from the daily lows shows that buyers have appeared, but it is not yet enough to confirm a new uptrend.**
* The next major event is the Federal Reserve decision on September 16, which could determine the market’s next significant move.
