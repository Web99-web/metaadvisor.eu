# ZEC moves against the entire market, nearing $1,400 while BTC and ETH fall: futures volume almost ten times higher than spot

**While Bitcoin, Ethereum and much of the crypto market stagnated or moved lower on September 16, Zcash went in the opposite direction. ZEC gained roughly 18–20% during the day and again approached the $1,400 area. Even more striking than the price itself was the gap between spot and derivatives trading: ZEC futures volume approached $10 billion over 24 hours, while spot volume was around $1 billion.**

That difference shows how strongly short-term ZEC trading is currently influenced by derivatives. Spot demand is clearly present, but with futures activity this large, shorts, leverage, stop-loss orders and liquidations can significantly amplify an initial price move.

## ZEC rises while BTC, ETH and SOL stall or fall

During the evening, ZEC was trading around $1,340 and was roughly 18% higher over 24 hours. Bitcoin was near $75,500, Ethereum around $2,390 and Solana just below $98, while Bittensor was trading lower.

ZEC was therefore not simply outperforming the market but moving almost directly against its broader direction. That divergence is particularly notable because Zcash has repeatedly shown strong upward moves in recent weeks even when Bitcoin and much of the altcoin market were not showing similar momentum.

## Futures volume almost ten times higher than spot

CoinGlass showed approximately **$9.8 billion in ZEC futures volume** during the evening, compared with around **$990 million in spot volume**, while open interest was close to **$2.9 billion**. Futures trading value was therefore almost ten times higher than trading in actual ZEC on spot markets.

The $9.8 billion figure does not mean that $9.8 billion of fresh capital bought ZEC. Futures volume includes the repeated opening and closing of long and short positions, often with leverage and sometimes several times within the same day. This is exactly why a relatively modest move in the spot market can trigger stop-loss orders and liquidations, after which the derivatives market can accelerate the move further.

## Large holders can have greater influence

ZEC's market capitalization is above $20 billion, but that does not mean $20 billion of actual liquidity is sitting in exchange order books. If sell-side liquidity is thin at certain price levels, a larger order can move the market price quickly, while the much larger futures market can amplify the effect through shorts and liquidations.

The influence of large holders is also being discussed within the Zcash community itself. In coinholder voting, voting power depends on the amount of ZEC rather than the number of people, giving the largest holders potentially much more influence. One participant on the Zcash Community Forum previously described this structural risk as a possible **“whale oligarchy”**, referring to the potential concentration of voting power among a relatively small number of large holders.

Because of Zcash's privacy model, the public can see the total amount that participated in the vote but cannot easily determine how many separate economic actors were behind it. We know that around **2.4 million ZEC** participated in the latest vote, but it is not straightforward to determine whether that amount was spread across many smaller holders or concentrated among fewer large holders.

## Voting results were public on September 14, media coverage surged on September 16

The NU7 coinholder vote ended on **September 14 at 19:00 UTC**, and the results were published almost immediately after voting closed. By then, it was already known that around **2.4 million ZEC out of roughly 3.6 million eligible shielded ZEC** had participated.

Major international crypto media, however, began covering the story much more heavily on **September 16**, the same day ZEC sharply accelerated. The wider media wave may therefore have placed already-public information in front of a much larger group of investors and momentum traders, generating a fresh burst of attention. The vote alone, however, is unlikely to explain the entire move, particularly with futures trading almost ten times larger than spot activity.

## What does the move from 75 to 25 seconds mean?

One of the main NU7 questions concerned reducing the Zcash block time from roughly **75 seconds to 25 seconds**. The network would produce blocks about three times more frequently, allowing transactions to receive confirmations faster and creating additional spare capacity if Zcash usage grows in the future.

It does not automatically mean more transactions. If network usage remains unchanged, the same activity would simply be spread across a larger number of blocks. More blocks also do not mean three times more new ZEC, because the reward per block would be adjusted to preserve the existing issuance schedule.

## ZEC also has real sources of new demand

Not all of the recent strength can be explained by derivatives. During 2026, Zcash gained several institutional catalysts, particularly the Grayscale Zcash ETF, which gives traditional investors exposure to ZEC without requiring them to buy and self-custody the privacy coin directly.

In early September, Digital Currency Group contributed approximately **85,705 ZEC** to the fund, worth around $100 million at the time. If larger amounts of ZEC move into ETFs, institutional wallets or long-term custody, part of the supply is no longer actively available on exchanges. Combined with heavy derivatives activity, a smaller freely traded float can make the price more sensitive to larger orders.

## EU and Zcash in 2027: the regulatory risk already has a date

While ZEC gained an ETF and greater institutional interest in the United States during 2026, the European Union already has adopted regulation that creates a longer-term regulatory risk for privacy coins. **Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 was published on June 19, 2024, and key provisions begin applying on July 10, 2027.**

The regulation restricts certain accounts and products linked to so-called **anonymity-enhancing coins** for regulated financial institutions and crypto-asset service providers. The EU definition also covers cryptoassets where privacy features are optional, which is particularly relevant to Zcash because ZEC supports both transparent and shielded transactions.

This is not a complete ban on privately holding ZEC in the European Union. The more important question is how regulated exchanges and custodians will react before **July 10, 2027**. If Binance, Kraken, Coinbase, OKX or other platforms conclude that they can no longer offer ZEC to European users in the same way, restrictions or delistings could be announced months before the regulation's application date.

Zcash therefore currently has two very different stories: institutional access is expanding in the United States, while a known regulatory deadline is approaching in the EU.

## ZEC is not behaving like a typical altcoin following Bitcoin

The latest move once again shows that ZEC cannot currently be analyzed solely through Bitcoin's direction. On one side are ETF demand, institutional interest, the NU7 upgrade and a potentially smaller freely traded float; on the other is an unusually large derivatives market where futures volume is almost ten times higher than spot trading.

That combination means larger orders, shorts and liquidations can have a much stronger impact than spot demand alone might suggest. For ZEC, it currently makes sense to watch spot volume, open interest, funding, short liquidations, ETF flows, whale transfers and future regulatory decisions by major exchanges at least as closely as the price itself.
