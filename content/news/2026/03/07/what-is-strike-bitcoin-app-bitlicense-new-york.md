# What is Strike? Bitcoin fintech app enters one of the world’s most regulated markets

Bitcoin payments platform **Strike** has received regulatory approval to operate in the U.S. state of New York after obtaining a **BitLicense** and a **Money Transmitter License** from the **New York State Department of Financial Services (NYDFS)**.

This approval allows the company to offer its Bitcoin-based financial services to individuals and businesses in New York, widely considered one of the **most tightly regulated digital asset markets in the world**.

For many crypto companies, gaining access to New York represents a major regulatory milestone. As **Metaadvisor.eu** notes, companies that successfully pass this process often gain stronger credibility with regulators and institutional investors.

## What exactly is Strike?

Strike is a fintech application that allows users to **buy, sell and use Bitcoin (BTC)** for everyday financial transactions.

Unlike many crypto platforms focused primarily on trading, Strike was designed with the goal of turning Bitcoin into a **global payment infrastructure**.

Users can:

- buy and sell Bitcoin  
- set recurring BTC purchases  
- convert part of their salary into Bitcoin  
- pay bills using BTC balances  
- send money via the **Lightning Network**  
- withdraw Bitcoin to **self-custody wallets**

One of the platform’s most notable features allows users to **automatically convert a portion of their paycheck into Bitcoin**, effectively creating a long-term digital savings strategy.

## Who founded Strike?

Strike was founded by **Jack Mallers**, a well-known entrepreneur in the Bitcoin ecosystem.

Mallers gained global attention in 2021 when he played a significant role in helping **El Salvador adopt Bitcoin as legal tender**, the first country in the world to do so.

Strike’s Lightning Network infrastructure was used as part of the implementation of Bitcoin payments in the country.

Since then, Mallers has positioned Strike as a platform designed to **bridge traditional financial systems with Bitcoin infrastructure**.

## Why the BitLicense matters

The **BitLicense** is a regulatory license issued by the **New York State Department of Financial Services (NYDFS)** that allows companies to legally operate digital asset businesses in the state of New York.

To obtain it, companies must meet strict requirements including:

- maintaining capital reserves  
- implementing **Anti-Money Laundering (AML)** compliance systems  
- cybersecurity standards  
- regular regulatory audits

Because of these strict rules, many crypto companies have chosen not to operate in New York at all.

For those that do obtain the license, it often serves as a **strong signal of regulatory credibility**.

## Expansion of Bitcoin financial services

With the BitLicense approval, Strike can now provide its Bitcoin services to users and businesses in New York.

These include:

- buying and selling Bitcoin  
- automated accumulation strategies  
- paycheck-to-Bitcoin conversion  
- paying bills using BTC balances  
- transferring funds to self-custody wallets

Strike also offers **price-triggered orders**, allowing users to automatically purchase Bitcoin when it reaches a specific price level.

The company also states that **customer funds remain fully backed and are not lent out**, a point emphasized after the collapse of several crypto lending platforms in 2022.

## Regulation and the future of crypto finance

New York has long been known as one of the most heavily regulated jurisdictions for digital assets.

While some industry participants criticize these rules, others argue that they can help **build a more stable and trusted financial ecosystem for digital assets**.

Strike’s entry into this market demonstrates how parts of the crypto industry are increasingly choosing to **operate within regulatory frameworks rather than outside them**.

As Metaadvisor.eu has frequently noted, the next phase of crypto market development may involve **greater institutional integration and regulated financial platforms**.
