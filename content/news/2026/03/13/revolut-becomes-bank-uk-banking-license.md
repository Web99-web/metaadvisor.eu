# Revolut becomes a bank: fintech giant receives full UK banking license

One of Europe’s most well-known fintech companies, **Revolut**, has taken a major step toward becoming a traditional bank. The company has received a **full banking license in the United Kingdom**, marking a new phase in its evolution from a digital finance app into a regulated financial institution.

For users, this change has a very concrete implication: **money held on Revolut will now receive the same type of regulatory protection as deposits held at traditional banks**. In other words, a fintech platform that began as a low-cost international payments tool is gradually transforming into a fully regulated banking institution.

## What the banking license means

Receiving a banking license allows Revolut to offer **fully regulated banking products** within the UK financial system. This includes deposit accounts, lending products and other services typically associated with traditional banks.

The most important change for customers is deposit protection. Customer funds will now be covered by the **Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS)**, the UK government-backed system that protects bank deposits if a financial institution fails.

In practice, deposits are protected up to **£85,000 per person**, which is the standard level of protection for UK banks.

For many customers this is a significant milestone, as Revolut now operates with **a full regulatory banking structure**.

## The new entity: Revolut Bank UK

To implement the change, the company created a new institution called **Revolut Bank UK Ltd.** Existing customer accounts will gradually be migrated to this new structure.

Revolut said customers will be notified through email or in-app messages once their accounts are ready to move. For most users, the everyday experience of using the app will remain largely unchanged.

**Account numbers, IBAN details and transaction history will remain the same**, while most of the changes take place on the regulatory and infrastructure side.

In other words, the app will look almost identical — but behind it there will now be **a fully licensed bank**.

## Regulation and supervision

Revolut Bank UK will be supervised by two key UK financial regulators:

- **Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA)** – responsible for the stability of banks  
- **Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)** – responsible for financial market oversight and consumer protection  

The approval follows a lengthy regulatory process. Revolut previously received a **restricted banking license** and entered a stage known as the **mobilisation phase**, during which new banks must demonstrate they can safely manage deposits, capital and operational risks.

## Services that remain outside the bank

Despite becoming a bank, several Revolut services will remain outside the new banking entity.

These include:

- **cryptocurrency trading**
- **stock trading**
- **precious metals investing**
- certain investment products

These services will continue to operate through **separate entities within the Revolut group**, which is common practice in the financial sector.

## Revolut’s global ambitions

Revolut is currently one of the fastest-growing fintech companies in the world. The company has **tens of millions of users** and operates in more than 30 countries.

In its latest funding round, the company was valued at roughly **$75 billion**, placing it among the most valuable private fintech firms globally.

The UK banking license is only part of a broader international strategy. Revolut has already applied for regulatory approvals in other markets, including the **United States**.

## Fintech and traditional banks are converging

Revolut’s development reflects a broader shift within global finance. The line between **fintech platforms and traditional banks** is becoming increasingly blurred.

A decade ago, fintech startups attempted to bypass banks. Today many of them are seeking to become **regulated banks themselves**.

According to analysis by **Metaadvisor**, the financial industry is moving toward a model in which mobile apps become the central hub for managing money — from payments and savings to investments and cryptocurrencies.

## Our view (Metaadvisor)

Revolut obtaining a banking license represents a major milestone for the fintech industry. For users it increases the safety of deposits and strengthens trust in digital financial platforms.

At the same time, it will be interesting to see how regulators manage the long-term interaction between traditional banking, fintech innovation and the crypto services that Revolut continues to offer through separate entities.
