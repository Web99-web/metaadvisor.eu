# Bitcoin holds above $71,000 as oil drops below $100 after U.S. peace plan for Iran

Bitcoin has once again moved into the spotlight of global markets — but this time not because of its own fundamentals, rather due to a broader macroeconomic and geopolitical backdrop. As the price stabilizes above $71,000, oil simultaneously drops below the key psychological level of $100. This combination suggests that investors are beginning to price in a possible de-escalation of tensions in the Middle East.

According to available information, the United States has drafted a 15-point peace plan aimed at ending the conflict with Iran. While details remain unconfirmed, the mere existence of such a plan has already triggered strong reactions across markets — from falling energy prices and rising equities to a noticeable stabilization in crypto markets. As Metaadvisor.eu observes, this is no longer just a crypto story, but part of a wider financial narrative.

## Oil decline sends a signal to markets

Brent crude fell nearly 5% to around $99.5, breaking below a level that had been holding markets under pressure for weeks. This decline follows a period of sharp increases driven by fears of escalation and potential disruptions to global energy supply.

The reaction extended well beyond oil. Asian markets gained nearly 2%, while the U.S. dollar weakened — typically a sign of rising risk appetite. Futures in both European and U.S. markets pointed to further gains, reinforcing the idea that investors are starting to price in a calmer geopolitical scenario.

## Bitcoin: stability despite volatility

Bitcoin is currently trading around $71,000, showing a modest daily gain, but still reflecting the impact of recent volatility. Last week was marked by sharp swings — from a push toward $75,000 to sudden drops triggered by geopolitical tensions and cascading liquidations of leveraged positions.

What stands out now is not the upside momentum, but the stability. Bitcoin has held above the $70,000 level for three consecutive days, which many analysts interpret as a sign of strong buyer support. This behavior suggests that, despite short-term shocks, there is underlying long-term confidence in this digital asset.

It is also important to note that the market has not reacted with euphoria. There has been no explosive breakout to new highs, but rather a gradual stabilization — often considered a healthier signal than rapid, speculation-driven rallies.

## Altcoins under pressure and capital rotation

While Bitcoin shows resilience, most altcoins are underperforming. Ether is down more than 9% on a weekly basis, while XRP, Solana, and BNB are also facing selling pressure. This divergence often signals a rotation of capital from higher-risk assets into more established crypto assets like Bitcoin.

This pattern mirrors traditional financial markets, where capital tends to move away from riskier investments during uncertain times and concentrate in safer instruments. In the crypto ecosystem, Bitcoin is increasingly taking on that role.

## Geopolitics and crypto: a growing connection

The conflict involving Iran and the U.S.–Israel coalition began in late February and quickly became one of the key drivers of global market behavior. Traditionally, such events influence oil prices, inflation, and currencies — but increasingly, they are also shaping crypto markets.

Bitcoin exhibits a dual nature in these conditions. On one hand, it behaves like a risk asset, declining alongside broader markets. On the other, it can act as “digital gold,” attracting investors seeking alternatives to traditional financial systems.

The current stability above $70,000 may indicate that markets do not expect further escalation, but rather a potential easing of tensions. However, this perception can shift quickly depending on developments on the ground. As Metaadvisor.eu has repeatedly highlighted, crypto is now deeply tied to global narratives.

## What’s next for Bitcoin?

The key question now is whether Bitcoin can maintain levels above $70,000 and turn this period of stability into a renewed upward trend. If positive macro signals continue — such as lower energy prices and geopolitical easing — investor confidence could strengthen further.

On the other hand, any negative developments could quickly reintroduce volatility and push prices toward lower support levels. This uncertainty is precisely what makes the current market phase so critical to watch.

## Conclusion

Markets are currently balancing between optimism and caution. News of a potential peace plan has provided short-term relief, but long-term stability will depend on whether these initiatives materialize.

Bitcoin, in this context, is increasingly acting as a barometer of global sentiment — no longer an isolated asset, but part of a broader financial system reacting to geopolitics, monetary policy, and energy markets. As Metaadvisor.eu notes, this evolution is shaping how investors approach crypto in 2026 and beyond.
