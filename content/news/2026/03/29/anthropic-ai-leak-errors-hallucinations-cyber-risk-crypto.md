# Anthropic AI leak: new model raises questions about errors, hallucinations and cyber risks — crypto markets react

## AI is no longer just a tool — it is becoming a risk factor

Crypto and tech markets are no longer reacting only to macroeconomic conditions. A new and increasingly important factor has entered the picture: artificial intelligence. At the center of attention is Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI system, after information about a new model was leaked.

The focus is no longer just on what these systems can do, but on what can go wrong. A key question is emerging: what happens when AI systems become powerful enough to identify vulnerabilities — but still hallucinate, make mistakes, or fail to follow instructions correctly?

## A leak that raised more questions than answers

According to available reports, internal materials became publicly accessible due to an exposed data repository. These included draft announcements and technical documentation referring to a new model that could surpass previous versions.

The concern lies in the model’s ability to identify software vulnerabilities. While this capability is not inherently negative, combining it with potential errors, hallucinations, and unpredictable behavior significantly increases the risk.

## AI errors: hallucinations, incorrect outputs and unpredictability

AI is increasingly shifting from a productivity tool to a systemic risk factor. Systems can hallucinate, generate incorrect information, or fail to execute instructions as intended — raising serious concerns about their use in sensitive areas like cybersecurity.

In the context of this new model, that means a system capable of detecting vulnerabilities might also misinterpret situations or produce flawed outputs. When combined with high capability, such behavior could have serious real-world consequences.

## Market reaction: tech stocks and Bitcoin decline

Markets reacted quickly. Shares of cybersecurity companies dropped between 4% and 6%, reflecting concerns that advanced AI systems could alter the balance between attack and defense in the digital space.

At the same time, Bitcoin pulled back toward the $66,000 level. This decline is not directly caused by the AI model itself, but by a shift in market sentiment. In periods of uncertainty, investors tend to reduce exposure to risk assets, including crypto and technology stocks.

## A new reality: AI as a market and security driver

This situation highlights a broader shift. AI is no longer just a tool for innovation — it is becoming a factor that influences security, business operations and financial markets.

The boundaries between technology, finance and security are becoming increasingly blurred. Events in one sector now have immediate consequences in others, contributing to higher volatility and uncertainty.

## Conclusion: AI enters a new phase of responsibility

The Anthropic AI leak is not just a technical story — it signals a structural change. As AI systems grow more powerful, so do the responsibilities and risks associated with them.

The key question is no longer whether AI can help, but whether it can be trusted in critical systems.

* The combination of power and unpredictability creates real risk
* Markets are reacting faster than ever to AI-related developments
* Bitcoin and tech sectors are increasingly correlated
* AI is evolving from a tool into a systemic market factor
