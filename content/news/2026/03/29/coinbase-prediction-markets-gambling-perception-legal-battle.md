# Coinbase under pressure: users accuse platform of pushing gambling as legal battle escalates in the US

## When a crypto app starts to look like a betting platform

Coinbase, one of the largest crypto exchanges in the world, is facing growing controversy after users began criticizing a new feature within its app — so-called prediction markets, which allow users to bet on the outcomes of events, including sports results.

The backlash is not only about the feature itself, but also how it is being promoted. Users report being flooded with push notifications encouraging them to place bets, which many see as inappropriate for a platform originally designed for cryptocurrency trading.

## User backlash and erosion of trust

During the popular March Madness tournament in the US, complaints intensified. Some users reported receiving more notifications about sports betting than about crypto markets within a short period of time.

Criticism quickly escalated. Users accused Coinbase of trying to hook its customer base into gambling to generate additional fee revenue, rather than focusing on its core purpose. In an industry already struggling with trust issues, such moves are amplifying concerns.

## Prediction markets: innovation or disguised gambling?

Prediction markets are often presented as innovative financial tools where users speculate on event outcomes, with prices reflecting collective probability estimates.

In practice, however, the line between prediction and gambling is increasingly blurred. Platforms such as Kalshi, Polymarket and now Coinbase offer models that closely resemble traditional betting, attracting growing regulatory scrutiny.

## The legal battle: states vs Coinbase

At the same time, Coinbase is engaged in a legal dispute with several US states, including Michigan, Connecticut and Illinois. The central issue is who has authority to regulate prediction markets.

Coinbase argues that these markets fall under the jurisdiction of the CFTC (Commodity Futures Trading Commission), a federal regulator. States, on the other hand, view them as a form of gambling that should be regulated at the state level.

## Detroit steps in — a potential precedent

The city of Detroit is preparing to join the case in support of the state of Michigan, highlighting the growing importance of the dispute. This move signals that the outcome could have implications far beyond Coinbase itself.

A court decision could set a precedent for the entire industry. If states prevail, prediction markets could face significant restrictions. If Coinbase succeeds, it could open the door to broader expansion of this model.

## Where is crypto heading?

This situation raises a broader question: where is the crypto industry heading? Expanding into new revenue streams may be strategically logical, but it comes with reputational risks.

The boundary between financial innovation and gambling is becoming increasingly unclear, and user reactions suggest that the market is not fully ready to accept such changes without resistance.

## Conclusion: growth with reputational risk

Coinbase finds itself at a crossroads. On one side is expansion and diversification; on the other, the risk of undermining user trust.

The outcome of the legal battle and ongoing user sentiment may ultimately shape not only Coinbase’s future, but also the trajectory of prediction markets within the crypto ecosystem.
