# Pudgy Penguins launches its game – a Web3 project trying to feel like a normal game

The NFT project **Pudgy Penguins** has launched its first major browser game called **Pudgy World**, and many players were surprised by their first reaction: the game **doesn’t feel like a crypto project at all**.

Unlike most Web3 games, which often require a wallet, token, or NFT before players can even start playing, Pudgy World was designed to function like **a traditional online game**. Players can enter the virtual world, explore locations, play mini-games and follow simple storylines without needing to understand blockchain technology.

This approach represents a significant shift in how Web3 projects attempt to reach a broader audience.

## A virtual world of penguins

Pudgy World is a **browser-based game** set in a world called **The Berg**, a virtual environment divided into twelve different towns. Players can explore the world, complete small quests and participate in mini-games, while the story follows a penguin named Pengu searching for someone named Polly.

Early impressions from players and journalists suggest that the gameplay is **smooth, responsive and intuitive**. Navigation is simple and the game has been optimized to run even on lower-end devices.

The development team explained that the game was built using **open-source web technologies**, allowing rapid development and optimization. Artists can work with tools such as **Maya, Cinema4D or Blender**, while specialized pipelines automatically convert assets into web-optimized formats.

The goal was to create a game that feels like **a modern browser game**, not an experimental crypto product.

## Inspired by classic internet games

Many players quickly compared the game to **Club Penguin**, Disney’s virtual world that ran between 2005 and 2017. At its peak, Club Penguin had more than **200 million registered users**, mostly children and teenagers who customized their penguin avatars and played mini-games.

Pudgy World appears to draw inspiration from this model — **a simple virtual world with recognizable characters and casual gameplay**.

Such an approach could be crucial for Web3 gaming, as most blockchain games have struggled to attract audiences beyond the crypto community.

## The problem with Web3 gaming

The blockchain gaming industry has long faced criticism that many projects are not actually games. Instead, they often resemble **financial products with minimal gameplay**, where token economics take priority over player experience.

In many of these projects players must purchase NFTs or tokens before they can even start playing, limiting the audience to those already familiar with crypto ecosystems.

Pudgy Penguins is attempting the opposite strategy: **build a game that works without crypto**, while the blockchain infrastructure remains mostly invisible in the background.

If successful, this model could represent **a new generation of Web3 games**.

## From NFT collection to global brand

Pudgy Penguins originally launched in 2021 as an **NFT collection of 8,888 digital penguins** on the Ethereum blockchain. During the NFT boom the project gained significant attention, but like many other projects it faced challenges after the market downturn in 2022.

A new team later took over the project and shifted the strategy toward **building a brand rather than focusing only on NFTs**.

One notable move was launching **physical Pudgy Penguins toys** that are now sold in major retail stores such as Walmart. These toys include QR codes that connect users to the project’s digital world.

This allows people to first discover **the characters and the brand**, before interacting with the digital ecosystem.

## A new model for Web3 projects

The strategy behind Pudgy Penguins differs from many Web3 projects that focus primarily on token communities. Instead, the project follows a **brand-first strategy**, where characters, games and community come before blockchain technology.

In this model crypto becomes **invisible infrastructure**, while the user experience remains simple and accessible.

Many analysts believe this approach could become essential for the long-term future of Web3.

## Can the game reach a mainstream audience?

The biggest question for Pudgy World is not the technology but **the audience**. If the game succeeds in attracting players who do not even realize they are interacting with blockchain infrastructure, it could become one of the first examples of mainstream Web3 gaming.

However, if the game remains limited to existing crypto communities, it may face the same fate as many other Web3 gaming projects.

Regardless of the outcome, the launch of Pudgy World shows how the industry is beginning to move away from models based purely on tokens and NFTs.

For platforms such as **Metaadvisor.eu**, which monitor technology and crypto trends, projects like this offer a fascinating example of how blockchain technology might be integrated into products capable of reaching **a global audience beyond the crypto community**.
