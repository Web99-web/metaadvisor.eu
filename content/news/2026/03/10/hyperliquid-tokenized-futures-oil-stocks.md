# Hyperliquid tokenized futures reach $1.2B

The decentralized exchange **Hyperliquid** has been gaining significant traction in the crypto derivatives market. According to recent market data, the platform’s **open interest** — the total value of active futures contracts — recently surpassed **$1.2 billion**, marking a new record for the rapidly growing platform.

Open interest is widely used as an indicator of market activity and liquidity. Rising open interest often signals that more traders are entering the market and placing bets on future price movements.

Interestingly, much of this activity on Hyperliquid is not tied directly to cryptocurrencies. Instead, traders are increasingly using the platform to speculate on **tokenized versions of traditional financial assets** such as oil, stock indices and precious metals.

## Tokenized markets expand beyond crypto

Hyperliquid enables trading through **perpetual futures contracts**, a type of derivative that allows traders to speculate on the price of an asset without owning the asset itself. Unlike traditional futures contracts, perpetual futures **do not have an expiration date**, allowing positions to remain open indefinitely.

One of the platform’s key innovations is its **permissionless market structure**, which allows users to create new trading markets for almost any asset.

This opens the door to trading **tokenized representations of real-world assets (RWA — Real World Assets)** directly on blockchain-based platforms.

## Most markets are not crypto pairs

According to analysis highlighted by investment firm **Arca**, only **7 of the top 30 markets on Hyperliquid are crypto pairs**. The majority of trading activity involves futures linked to **commodities and equity indices**.

Among the largest markets on the platform are:

- **XYZ100-USDC**, a tokenized equity index futures market with around **$213 million in open interest**
- **CL-USDC**, a futures contract tracking oil prices with about **$169.8 million in open positions**
- futures tied to **Brent crude oil**
- futures tracking the **S&P 500 index**
- futures linked to **gold and silver**

The **CL-USDC market** has seen particularly strong activity, recording roughly **$1.62 billion in trading volume within 24 hours**.

## Why traders use decentralized futures markets

One of the key advantages of decentralized trading platforms is that they operate **24 hours a day, seven days a week**. Traditional financial markets close on weekends and follow fixed trading hours.

When global events occur outside those hours, decentralized platforms often become the first place where traders react to new developments. This process is commonly known as **price discovery**.

## Oil volatility and geopolitical tensions

Part of the surge in activity on Hyperliquid is linked to developments in global energy markets. Prices for commodities such as oil, gold and silver have experienced increased volatility in recent months.

For example, certain crude oil grades such as **Murban crude** recently traded at around **$103 per barrel**. These price movements are often tied to **geopolitical tensions in the Middle East**, which can significantly impact global energy markets.

In such conditions, traders often seek markets where they can **react immediately to new information**, and decentralized derivatives platforms are increasingly fulfilling that role.

## DeFi and traditional finance are converging

The rise of platforms like Hyperliquid reflects a broader shift in the financial industry. The boundaries between **traditional finance (TradFi)** and **decentralized finance (DeFi)** are gradually becoming less distinct.

Blockchain infrastructure is increasingly being used to trade digital representations of financial instruments that were once exclusive to traditional markets.

Many industry analysts believe that **tokenized financial markets could become one of the most important trends in the crypto sector in the coming years**. If adoption continues to grow, platforms like Hyperliquid may eventually serve as a **bridge between global financial markets and the blockchain ecosystem**.

For projects and media platforms such as **Metaadvisor.eu**, this trend highlights how quickly the crypto ecosystem is expanding beyond its original scope and into broader financial infrastructure.
