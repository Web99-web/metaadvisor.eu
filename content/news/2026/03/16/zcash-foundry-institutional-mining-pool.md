# Zcash enters a new phase: Foundry launches institutional mining pool

The cryptocurrency **Zcash**, known for its strong focus on privacy and anonymous transactions, is entering a new phase of development. **Foundry Digital**, one of the key infrastructure companies in the crypto industry, has announced the launch of an **institutional mining pool for Zcash** in April 2026.

This move represents a significant shift not only for Zcash but also for the broader sector of privacy-focused cryptocurrencies. While such projects have long been perceived as niche and often controversial, the growing interest from institutional players suggests that this perception is beginning to change.

## What an institutional mining pool means

A mining pool allows multiple miners to **combine their computing power** and participate together in block validation, sharing rewards proportionally. Compared to solo mining, this model increases income stability and reduces risk.

However, what makes this project unique is its focus on **institutional and publicly traded mining companies**. Foundry plans to offer infrastructure that includes:

- advanced reporting systems  
- transparent payout models  
- compliance with regulatory requirements  

This means that Zcash is, for the first time, gaining infrastructure capable of meeting the standards of large investors and companies.

## Why Zcash

Zcash differs from most other cryptocurrencies because it uses **zero-knowledge proofs**, a technology that allows transactions to be validated without revealing information about the sender, receiver, or amount.

This technology enables a high level of **financial privacy**, which is becoming an increasingly important topic in the digital economy. While Bitcoin and most blockchains offer transparency, Zcash provides the option of privacy.

According to Foundry representatives, this combination of **privacy and regulatory compatibility** was the key reason for entering the project. The idea that privacy and compliance can coexist is becoming increasingly relevant in the world of digital finance.

## Broader context: privacy vs regulation

The issue of privacy in cryptocurrencies has been debated for years. On one side, advocates argue that **financial privacy is a fundamental right**, while regulators warn about potential misuse.

What Foundry’s move suggests is that the industry is attempting to find a **balance between privacy and regulatory compliance**. This balance could ultimately define the future of projects like Zcash.

If institutional investors begin to participate more actively in such networks, it could lead to broader acceptance of privacy-focused cryptocurrencies.

## Foundry expanding beyond Bitcoin

Foundry Digital has so far been best known for its role in the Bitcoin ecosystem, particularly through the **Foundry USA Pool**, one of the largest mining pools by hashrate share.

Its entry into Zcash clearly signals that the company aims to diversify its operations and expand beyond Bitcoin. It also indicates that the market is becoming mature enough to support infrastructure across multiple blockchain networks.

This move could encourage other infrastructure companies to explore similar directions.

## Technological and market potential

Zcash has been developing privacy-focused technology for years, but it has not yet had broad institutional backing. The launch of a mining pool tailored to large players could increase:

- network security  
- hashrate stability  
- developer and investor interest  

At the same time, the development of new wallets and applications for Zcash shows that the ecosystem continues to grow.

## Metaadvisor analysis: why this matters

According to analysis followed by **Metaadvisor**, this move could have broader implications for the entire crypto industry. If it proves that privacy and regulation can coexist, it could open the door to a new generation of digital financial products.

It is particularly interesting that this development is happening at a time when global interest in decentralized technologies is rising, including regions such as Asia and markets frequently seen in analytics (e.g., Singapore).

## Can privacy become mainstream

The key question remains whether privacy-focused cryptocurrencies can move from a niche segment into broader adoption.

If institutional capital begins to flow into such projects, it could significantly change market perception. Instead of being seen as a risk, privacy could become a **standard of the digital economy**.

## Our view (Metaadvisor)

The launch of an institutional Zcash mining pool by Foundry can be seen as one of the more important steps in the evolution of the crypto industry. The combination of privacy, technology, and institutional capital creates a new dynamic that could reshape how we view digital finance.

If this model proves successful, Zcash and similar projects could gain significantly more visibility and legitimacy on the global market.
