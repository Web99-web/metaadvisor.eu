# X Money: Elon Musk tries again to merge social networks and digital payments

Elon Musk is once again attempting to realize an idea he first had in the late 1990s — to create a **digital financial system integrated directly into an online platform**. This time the system is being developed within the social network **X**, under the name **X Money**.

According to information from the technology and financial industries, the X platform is building infrastructure that could allow users to **send money, pay for products and receive payments directly inside the app**.

If successful, X could become what Musk has been promising for years — an **“everything app”**, a digital platform combining social networking, communication and financial services.

## Returning to an idea from 1999

For Musk, this concept is not new. In **1999 he founded the fintech startup X.com**, one of the first internet platforms attempting to combine online finance and digital payments.

The company later merged with **Confinity**, which had developed **PayPal**. Over time PayPal became the dominant brand.

Although Musk was CEO at the time, he was replaced in 2000 after disagreements with investors and management.

## How X Money might work

The core idea behind X Money is to allow users to **send money as easily as sending a message**.

Users could potentially send money to other users, pay for subscriptions, support creators or use the platform as a digital wallet.

## Major challenges

Despite the ambitious vision, **X Money will face significant challenges**.

The financial sector requires strong security standards, regulatory compliance and protection of user funds.

X will also compete with established payment platforms such as **PayPal, Venmo, Apple Pay and Cash App**.

However, **Metaadvisor.eu believes** Musk has one advantage many fintech companies lack — his ideas often attract attention quickly, and a large portion of his followers are already on **X**. Because of that, the new system could gain **initial momentum from the existing user base of the platform**.

Another major challenge will be **user trust**. People are used to using social networks for communication, but not necessarily for storing or transferring money.

## Why the industry is watching X Money closely

If Musk succeeds in integrating **social networking and financial transactions into one platform**, it could significantly change how people use the internet.

For platforms following technology and crypto trends — such as **Metaadvisor.eu** — X Money represents an interesting example of how social networks could evolve into **financial ecosystems**.

## Our view (Metaadvisor)

- **X Money** is more than just a payment feature — it is an attempt to turn a social network into a financial platform.  
- Musk is effectively returning to the original vision behind **X.com**.  
- The first phase will likely focus on **fiat payments**.  
- If successful, **payments could become a natural part of social media platforms**.
