# X Money: Elon Musk once again tries to combine social networks and digital money

Elon Musk is once again trying to realize an idea he originally had back in the late 1990s — to create a **digital financial system integrated directly into an internet platform**. This time the system is being developed inside the social network **X**, and the project is called **X Money**.

According to information from the technology and financial industry, the X platform is building infrastructure that would allow users to **send money, pay for products and receive payments directly within the app**. If the project succeeds, X could become what Musk has been talking about for years — the so-called **“everything app”**, a digital platform combining social networks, communication and financial services.

Such a model already exists in some parts of the world. For example, Chinese super-apps allow users to chat, shop, pay bills and send money to friends within one platform. Musk has been saying for years that he wants to build a **Western version of such a super-app**, and X Money could be a key step toward that vision.

## A return to the idea from 1999

For Musk this idea is not actually new. Back in **1999 he launched a fintech startup called X.com**, one of the first internet platforms that attempted to combine online financial services and digital payments.

X.com was not a traditional bank, but it offered a range of financial services online. Users could open accounts online, send money via email and use digital financial services that were quite revolutionary at the time. In today’s terms it could best be described as an **early fintech project**, long before the word fintech became widely used.

In 2000, X.com merged with the company **Confinity**, which had developed an online money transfer system called **PayPal**. After the merger PayPal quickly became the most popular part of the business and the company eventually adopted that name completely.

During that period Musk served as **CEO of the company**, but in 2000 he was removed from that position after disagreements with investors and management over the technological strategy. PayPal later grew into one of the world’s largest payment platforms, and in 2002 the company was acquired by eBay.

The domain **X.com** did not remain in Musk’s ownership after those events and stayed with PayPal for many years. Only in **2017 did PayPal sell the domain back to Musk**, symbolically closing the circle of an idea that began almost two decades earlier.

## How X Money could work

The core idea behind X Money is to allow users to **send money as easily as they currently send messages or comments**.

In practice this could mean that users on the platform would be able to send money to other users, pay for digital products or subscriptions, receive payments for content or services, donate to creators and use the platform as a type of digital wallet. If the system develops fully, X could become a place where **communication and financial transactions take place within the same digital platform**.

Such a model could open a completely new type of internet economy in which social networks gradually transform into **financial infrastructures**. Instead of clicking a link, moving to another application and completing the payment there, the entire process could remain inside one digital ecosystem.

## Partnerships with major payment networks

For such a system to work, the platform must build serious financial infrastructure. According to some industry reports, X is already working on integration with major payment networks, including **Visa**.

Such integration could enable systems such as **Visa Direct**, which allows almost instant transfers of money between bank accounts. That would mean users could send money **in near real time**, which is a key feature of modern digital payment systems.

If X Money truly enables fast peer-to-peer payments, that would be an important signal that the platform is seriously entering a space currently dominated by established fintech and payment services.

## Regulatory steps in the United States

Financial services are among the most regulated sectors in the world. Because of that, the X platform has in recent months begun obtaining so-called **money transmitter licenses** in different U.S. states.

Such licenses allow a company to **legally provide money transfer services**. According to available information, X has already obtained several dozen such approvals in the United States, which indicates that the X Money project is not just an idea but part of a serious infrastructure that is already being developed.

This is an important detail because it shows that Musk and his team are not only talking about a future vision but are building the operational framework required for such a system to function in a large market.

## Will X Money include cryptocurrencies

One of the most common questions surrounding X Money is **whether the platform will include cryptocurrencies**.

Elon Musk has shown interest in the crypto industry for years, particularly in **Bitcoin and Dogecoin**, and Dogecoin has frequently appeared in his posts on social networks. Because of that, part of the crypto community has long expected that X might eventually introduce some form of **crypto payments**, at least as an optional feature.

However, there is currently no confirmation that cryptocurrencies will be part of the X Money system from the very beginning. It is possible that the platform will first develop **traditional fiat payment functions**, and only later add options connected to blockchain technology. Such an approach would be logical because it would allow the platform to first build **a large user base for digital payments**, and only then experiment with crypto integration.

For Metaadvisor and readers who follow fintech and crypto trends, this remains one of the most interesting open questions: will X Money remain a traditional payment system or later become a bridge between conventional payments and digital assets.

## Musk’s vision of the “everything app”

Musk has been speaking for years about the idea of an application that combines multiple internet functions into one digital space. In his vision, users would not need to use ten different apps for communication, social networks and payments. Instead, everything would be available within a single platform.

If X Money becomes a functional system, X could become a place where users communicate, follow news, publish content and **carry out financial transactions** without leaving the application. Such a model could significantly change how people use social networks, especially if payments become integrated as naturally as private messages, subscriptions or creator support.

It could also transform the business model of the platform itself. Instead of relying almost exclusively on advertising and subscriptions, X could gain an additional revenue stream through **payment infrastructure and transaction fees**.

## Major challenges

Despite the ambitious vision, **X Money will face a number of serious challenges**. The financial sector requires a high level of security, regulatory compliance and user protection. The platform will need to ensure **transaction security, protection of user funds and systems to prevent fraud and money laundering**.

At the same time, X will compete with already established digital payment platforms such as **PayPal, Venmo, Apple Pay and Cash App**, which already have millions of users, strong brand recognition and well-developed infrastructure.

However, **according to the assessment of Metaadvisor.eu**, Musk has one advantage that many fintech companies do not. His ideas often find an audience very quickly, and a large number of his supporters and followers are already present on the **X** platform. Because of this, there is a realistic possibility that the new payment system could gain **rapid initial momentum thanks to the platform’s existing user base**.

Another major challenge will be **user trust**. People are used to using social networks for content and communication, but not necessarily for storing or transferring money. That is why **the first impression of the X Money system will be extremely important**. If the system turns out to be slow, complicated or involved in security controversies, user trust could be very difficult to restore. **However, considering Musk’s previous projects and his strong follower base, it is possible that a large number of users will still be willing to quickly try the platform’s new features.**

## Why the industry is closely watching X Money

Despite these challenges, the development of the X Money system is attracting significant attention from both the technology and financial industries. If Musk manages to integrate **a social network and digital money into one platform**, it could significantly change how people use the internet. Social networks would then no longer be just a place for communication, but also a space where part of the digital economy takes place.

For platforms that follow technology and crypto trends, such as **Metaadvisor.eu**, the development of the X Money project represents an interesting example of how social networks could transform into **financial ecosystems**. In that sense, X Money is not just another feature within a single app, but a test of a broader idea — that communication, content, identity and payments could merge into one digital experience.

## Our view (Metaadvisor)

- **X Money** is more than just a payment feature — it is an attempt to turn a social network into a financial platform.  
- Through this project Musk is effectively returning to the idea from the days of **X.com**, only within a modern and much larger digital environment.  
- The most likely first step will be **fiat payments**, while the question of crypto integration will probably remain open for a later phase.  
- If the project succeeds, it could open the door to a model in which **payments become an integral part of social networks**.  
- The real test, however, is still ahead: whether users will be willing to use the X platform not only for content and communication, but also as a place for their digital money.
