# Robert Kiyosaki shocks the market: Bitcoin at $750,000?

## Ambitious forecast or warning of a crisis?

The author of the well-known book *“Rich Dad Poor Dad”*, Robert Kiyosaki, has once again stirred financial markets by stating that Bitcoin could reach as much as $750,000. His prediction comes with a strong warning — according to him, the global financial system is on the verge of a major bubble burst.

While such statements often attract attention, public reactions are divided. While part of the crypto community sees this prediction as confirmation of Bitcoin’s long-term potential, others — including crypto investors themselves — respond with a degree of skepticism.

## What does $750,000 actually mean?

At first glance, a price of $750,000 per Bitcoin seems extremely optimistic. However, Kiyosaki does not base his prediction solely on Bitcoin’s growth, but rather on the expected collapse of the traditional financial system.

In other words, the question is not just how much Bitcoin rises — but how quickly fiat currencies and purchasing power decline.

If strong inflation or monetary expansion were to occur, the real value of that figure could be significantly different from what we imagine today. In such a scenario, Bitcoin’s price increase could be more a reflection of a weakening dollar than actual value growth.

## Historical context: when money loses value

Similar patterns have already been seen in the past. During the period between 2020 and 2021, global monetary expansion led to a strong rise in asset prices. The S&P 500 gained more than 50%, while real estate prices in the US rose significantly in a very short time.

Bitcoin often behaves as a “safe haven” in such conditions for investors seeking protection against inflation.

Kiyosaki goes a step further and predicts that gold could reach $35,000 per ounce, further reinforcing his thesis of a major monetary shift.

## The problem with predictions: time and market reality

Despite the strong narrative, one key question remains — the timeframe.

Kiyosaki mentions a period of roughly one year after a financial bubble burst. However, such predictions often overlook the complexity of markets and the many factors influencing prices, including regulation, liquidity and global economic stability.

Metaadvisor emphasizes in this context that such statements are often interpreted without the broader picture — and that broader picture is crucial for understanding the true potential of the market.

## It’s hard to ignore the actual trend

This brings us to the core issue.

Looking at real market data, Bitcoin has been under pressure for months. Since October 10, 2025, the market has shown a continuous trend of decline or stagnation, making it even harder to imagine a scenario of rapid and explosive growth in a short timeframe.

Such an environment does not support the narrative of a quick rise to $750,000. On the contrary, it points to a market going through a phase of uncertainty, consolidation, or even prolonged decline.

For this reason, many analysts see such predictions more as a reflection of long-term optimism than realistic short-term projections.

## Market psychology and investor expectations

Large numbers like this often have a strong psychological effect. They create narratives, drive discussions, and attract new participants to the market.

At the same time, they can also create unrealistic expectations.

If investors begin making decisions based solely on such projections, without understanding the risks involved, this can lead to increased volatility and potential losses.

## Our view

Kiyosaki’s $750,000 Bitcoin prediction is not impossible — but the timeframe remains unclear, and under current market conditions it is difficult to justify.

In an environment where Bitcoin has been stagnating or declining for months, such a scenario would require an exceptionally strong catalyst, such as a global financial crisis or radical monetary changes.

In other words, his prediction says more about potential problems within the system than about the actual state of the market today.
