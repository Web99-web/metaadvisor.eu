# AltStore PAL joins the Fediverse: alternative iOS app store becomes part of the decentralized social network

The alternative **iOS app store AltStore PAL**, which emerged as a result of new regulatory rules in the European Union and several other markets, has announced an interesting technological integration — connecting with the **Fediverse**, a decentralized network of social platforms powered by the open **ActivityPub** protocol. This move makes AltStore PAL one of the first projects attempting to combine mobile app distribution with decentralized social networks.

This announcement comes at a time when the mobile ecosystem is gradually beginning to change. For years Apple maintained almost complete control over app distribution on iOS devices through its **App Store**, but regulatory changes such as the European **Digital Markets Act (DMA)** have opened the door for alternative app marketplaces. AltStore PAL is among the first projects trying to take advantage of this new regulatory environment and introduce a different model of software distribution on the iPhone.

## What is AltStore PAL

AltStore PAL is an alternative marketplace for apps that allows the installation and distribution of applications **outside Apple’s App Store**. Such a model was nearly impossible for years on the iOS platform due to Apple’s strict control over the software ecosystem. However, regulatory pressure in the European Union and several other jurisdictions forced Apple to open parts of its system and allow competition.

AltStore was already known among developers as a tool that enabled the installation of apps Apple often does not allow in the official store, such as emulators or experimental applications. The new version, **AltStore PAL**, goes a step further and aims to become a true alternative app marketplace for iOS.

In this context, integration with the Fediverse represents an interesting experiment that could potentially change how users discover and follow mobile applications.

## What is the Fediverse

The Fediverse is a decentralized network of social platforms that communicate with each other using the open **ActivityPub** protocol. Instead of a single centralized platform, the Fediverse consists of a large number of independent servers that exchange content with one another.

The best-known platform using this system is **Mastodon**, but the Fediverse ecosystem also includes applications like PeerTube, Flipboard, and even newer social networks trying to avoid the centralized platform model.

In such a system, users on one server can follow users and content from other servers. This means the network functions as a **federation of interconnected platforms**, rather than a single centralized social network.

It is important to understand that the Fediverse is not one platform but a network of interconnected servers. The most well-known example is **Mastodon**, a decentralized social platform that has grown significantly in recent years, particularly among developers, journalists, and the open-source community.

## The first federated app marketplace

AltStore PAL plans to launch its own **Mastodon server** which will act as a communication platform for app developers and users. Developers will be able to publish information about their apps, new versions, security alerts, or feature changes.

Interestingly, these posts will not be limited only to AltStore users. Because the server uses **ActivityPub**, posts will also be visible to users across other Fediverse platforms.

This means that someone using Mastodon or another compatible platform could **follow a specific app like a profile**, receiving updates about new versions or changes directly in their feed.

In this way, an app marketplace gains elements of a social network.

## Interaction between users and developers

AltStore PAL also plans to enable **social interaction within the app itself**. Users may be able to react to developer posts, like apps, or comment on new updates.

These interactions will be connected to Fediverse accounts, meaning users could sign in with existing profiles from platforms such as Mastodon or even Bluesky, if their accounts are bridged with the Fediverse network.

This model could potentially create a **more open communication channel between developers and users**, something traditional app stores often lack.

## Why this matters for the mobile ecosystem

Apple’s App Store has long been **the only official distribution platform** for iOS apps. This gave Apple complete control over which apps could be published, how they were distributed, and how they were monetized.

Regulatory frameworks such as the Digital Markets Act aim to reduce that level of control and allow greater competition. AltStore PAL is one of the first projects attempting to operate within this new environment.

The integration with the Fediverse goes even further by trying to combine **the decentralized social web with software distribution**.

## The broader trend of internet decentralization

This move fits into a broader trend within the technology industry. More and more platforms are exploring models that reduce dependence on centralized services and rely on open protocols.

ActivityPub has become one of the key protocols behind such projects, and the Fediverse has expanded significantly in recent years, especially after major changes across large social networks.

According to analyses followed by **Metaadvisor**, decentralized platforms are increasingly appearing as alternatives to centralized technology giants. While still relatively small compared to mainstream social networks, their influence and developer interest continue to grow.

## Will Apple allow such a model

One open question is how long Apple will tolerate alternative distribution models such as AltStore. Although the regulatory framework in Europe currently requires more openness, Apple still tries to maintain a significant level of control over security and app distribution.

AltStore PAL therefore operates in a sensitive space between innovation and regulation. If successful, the project could demonstrate how **alternative app stores might function within a decentralized digital ecosystem**.

## Our view (Metaadvisor)

The integration of AltStore PAL with the Fediverse represents an interesting technological experiment combining two major shifts on the internet: the opening of mobile platforms and the rise of decentralized social networks. Although the project is still in an early stage, the idea that apps could have their own profiles within an open network may eventually change how users discover and follow mobile applications.

If such a model proves successful, it could encourage the development of new decentralized marketplaces and further reduce reliance on centralized platforms.
