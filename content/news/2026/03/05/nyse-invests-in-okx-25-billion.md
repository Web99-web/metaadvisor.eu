# New York Stock Exchange enters crypto with investment in OKX valued at $25 billion

One of the most significant developments in digital assets today comes from the heart of the global financial system. Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), the company that owns the **New York Stock Exchange (NYSE)** — the world’s largest stock exchange — has announced a strategic investment in crypto exchange **OKX**, valuing the platform at approximately **$25 billion**.

The announcement has drawn attention across both the cryptocurrency sector and traditional financial markets, as it comes from one of the most established institutions in global finance.

## A partnership bridging crypto and traditional finance

According to the announcement, **Intercontinental Exchange (ICE)** invested an undisclosed amount in OKX and will receive a seat on the exchange’s board of directors.

The investment forms part of a broader strategic partnership designed to develop financial products that connect **traditional finance (TradFi)** with digital assets.

The collaboration includes plans for:

- crypto futures products  
- tokenized equities  
- digital trading infrastructure  
- new solutions for institutional investors  

ICE will use **spot crypto prices from the OKX platform** as reference data for its crypto futures products.

At the same time, OKX will provide its users with access to certain markets and products built by ICE, including potential **tokenized versions of equities and financial instruments**.

## OKX and a market of 120 million users

OKX is one of the largest global cryptocurrency exchanges, with more than **120 million registered accounts worldwide**.

The platform is known for:

- its strong derivatives trading ecosystem  
- large trading volumes  
- development of Web3 infrastructure  
- increasing focus on institutional clients  

The partnership with ICE further strengthens OKX’s role as a bridge between blockchain infrastructure and traditional financial markets.

Haider Rafique, global managing partner at OKX, said the two companies quickly aligned on their vision for the future of financial markets.

According to him, the next stage of financial innovation lies in the **integration of blockchain technology with traditional financial infrastructure**.

## Tokenization as the next evolution of markets

One of the key pillars of the partnership is the development of markets for **tokenized securities**.

Tokenization refers to the process of converting traditional assets — such as equities — into digital tokens recorded on blockchain networks.

These tokens could enable:

- faster global trading  
- lower transaction costs  
- increased liquidity  
- broader investor access  

Major financial institutions have increasingly been exploring tokenization, as it could significantly transform how capital markets operate.

If the model succeeds, investors could eventually **trade digital representations of equities almost as easily as cryptocurrencies**.

## ICE accelerating blockchain infrastructure

Jeffrey C. Sprecher, chairman and CEO of ICE, said the partnership with OKX will expand global investor access to regulated markets.

The company also plans to accelerate the development of infrastructure for **tokenized assets and blockchain-based financial products**.

Star Xu, founder and CEO of OKX, noted that the investment represents only the beginning of a deeper strategic collaboration.

## Crypto and traditional finance moving closer

Just a few years ago, many major financial institutions were skeptical of the crypto industry.

Today the situation looks very different.

Large banks are building blockchain projects, investment funds are buying **Bitcoin ETFs**, and exchanges such as the NYSE are exploring tokenized securities.

The partnership between ICE and OKX is therefore widely seen as another sign that **crypto markets are gradually integrating into the global financial system**.

The valuation of OKX at **$25 billion** also signals that institutional investors continue to see strong potential in digital asset infrastructure.

## What it means for the market

For the cryptocurrency market, the deal carries several important implications.

First, it confirms that major financial institutions are expanding their involvement in blockchain infrastructure.

Second, it shows that crypto exchanges are becoming key partners in developing new financial products.

Third, it opens the possibility of a new market structure where **digital assets and traditional equities could coexist on the same technological platforms**.

If the plans from ICE and OKX materialize, this partnership could represent an important step toward a future financial system where the boundaries between crypto markets and traditional exchanges become increasingly blurred.
