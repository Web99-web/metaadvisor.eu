# FTX returns billions: how is it possible that some users receive 100%?

## Repayments after one of the largest collapses

More than three years after the dramatic collapse of the crypto exchange FTX, users are finally receiving concrete repayments. According to the latest information, the FTX Recovery Trust plans to distribute around $2.2 billion to creditors on March 31, 2026 — the fourth payout round within a lengthy bankruptcy process.

So far, approximately $10 billion has been returned, making this one of the rare cases in the crypto industry where a significant portion of funds has been successfully recovered. However, what draws particular attention is the fact that reports suggest some users are receiving up to 100% recovery — or even more in certain categories. Payments are expected within 1 to 3 business days via BitGo, Kraken, or Payoneer. Unlike cases such as Celsius, where users received payouts in BTC and ETH based on predefined values, FTX payouts are made in USD. In both cases, one key point remains the same — users do not benefit from the actual crypto market growth that occurred after the collapse.

## How is 100% — or even 120% — possible?

Particularly notable is the category of so-called “convenience claims,” meaning smaller claims, where users in certain cases receive more than 100% — primarily to close such cases more quickly from an administrative standpoint.

At first glance, such a scenario seems almost unbelievable. After losses and a multi-year process, a partial recovery would be expected. However, the structure of the bankruptcy process and the specifics of the crypto market led to a different outcome.

The key point to understand is that payouts are not made in the original cryptocurrencies, but in US dollars, based on the value at the time of the 2022 bankruptcy. According to available information, percentages such as 100% or 120% refer to that USD value — not to today’s market prices of cryptocurrencies.

This means that users who held Bitcoin or other cryptocurrencies do not receive their current market value, but rather the value determined at that time — which, in a rising market, results in a significantly smaller number of coins.

In the meantime, part of FTX’s assets — including crypto, investments, and other financial instruments — has significantly increased in value or has been successfully liquidated, allowing the total pool available for distribution to exceed initial expectations.

## Comparison with Celsius

A similar effect was already seen in the Celsius case, where approximately 70% recovery was discussed. However, a large number of users did not initially understand that this percentage referred to the USD value at the time of bankruptcy in July 2022, when Bitcoin was around $19,000. Since payouts were made later — during a period of significantly higher prices — the actual number of coins received was much lower.

In one specific example, a user who originally held 0.30 BTC ultimately received a single payout which — through a combination of BTC and ETH distributions — amounted to approximately 0.06 BTC in value, or about 20% of the original amount. In other words, regardless of how the percentage is formally presented, the real outcome for many users was significantly below the expected “70%”.

After the payout, the user received a notification stating that the distribution had been completed — without further explanation or the possibility of additional communication. In such cases, where the calculation method and all phases of distribution are not transparent, some users are left wondering whether all expected payouts were fully executed.

At the same time, the lack of additional information and the abrupt end of communication following the “payment completed” message leaves room for uncertainty — especially in situations where users have no way to verify the structure and completeness of the final amount.

## Structure of payouts and what the percentages mean

The fourth payout round includes different categories of creditors. For example, some groups receive an additional 18%, while others receive 5% or 15%. In some cases, total recovery reaches 100%.

It is important to emphasize that these percentages do not represent profit in the traditional sense, but rather a return based on the recognized claim value from 2022 — not current cryptocurrency market prices.

## FTX as a lesson: how it all began

FTX was once one of the largest crypto exchanges in the world, holding billions in user funds and having a strong influence on the market. However, in November 2022, it collapsed suddenly after serious irregularities in fund management were uncovered.

The founder of the exchange, Sam Bankman-Fried, was later sentenced to 25 years in prison for fraud and conspiracy, giving the case a legal conclusion.

## What comes next?

After this round of payouts, another distribution is planned for May 2026, meaning the process is not yet finished. At the same time, questions remain about how these repayments will impact the market.

If some users decide to reinvest their funds into cryptocurrencies, this could increase liquidity and potentially affect prices in the short term. On the other hand, some may choose to exit the market entirely after this experience, which would have the opposite effect.

That is why this story is not only about repayments, but also about trust — and how it is rebuilt after a major collapse.

## Our take

The FTX case shows that even after one of the largest financial collapses in the crypto industry, partial or near-complete recovery of funds is possible. However, it is essential to understand the context — most users do not receive the actual amount of cryptocurrency they originally held, but rather its historical USD value.

The comparison with Celsius further highlights how large the gap between USD-based accounting and actual coin amounts can be. Recovery percentages may sound high, but the real outcome for users is often significantly lower.

FTX remains both a symbol and a warning — not only because of what happened, but also because of how the consequences are being resolved.
