# Ondo Finance in focus: are stocks moving to blockchain and is the market changing forever?

## The crypto market is falling — but infrastructure is growing

While Bitcoin and Ethereum prices have been stagnating or declining in recent months under pressure from macroeconomic factors such as high interest rates, a strong dollar, and geopolitical tensions, something far more important is happening beneath the surface. A new financial infrastructure is being built that could redefine how people invest, trade, and access markets in the long run.

At the center of this shift is the tokenization of real-world assets (RWA), and one name keeps appearing more frequently — Ondo Finance. Unlike traditional crypto projects driven by hype and short-term sentiment, Ondo is positioning itself as a bridge between traditional finance and blockchain, focusing on real products, real liquidity, and real users.

{{< support1 >}}

## MetaMask integration: stocks enter the crypto wallet

One of the most significant steps came when MetaMask integrated Ondo Finance, enabling access to more than 200 tokenized U.S. stocks, ETFs, and commodities directly within the wallet. This means users no longer need to open brokerage accounts or rely on fragmented platforms to access traditional markets.

Users in supported jurisdictions can now trade tokenized versions of stocks like Tesla, Apple, and Nvidia, as well as ETFs linked to gold, silver, or the Nasdaq — all within a single interface. This move clearly shows how quickly the line between crypto and traditional finance is disappearing.

## MEXC partnership: tokenized stocks reach exchanges

At the same time, the crypto exchange MEXC expanded its offering of tokenized stocks through its partnership with Ondo Finance. More than 17 new trading pairs were introduced, along with additional tokens tied to sectors such as energy and defense.

These tokens are issued as ERC-20 assets on the Ethereum network and traded against USDT, while the underlying shares are held in regulated custody accounts. This structure combines the security of traditional finance with the flexibility of blockchain — a key factor for broader adoption.

{{< support2 >}}

## Tokenization in numbers: growth that cannot be ignored

The data shows that tokenized stocks are no longer an experiment. Ondo Global Markets has already surpassed $500 million in total value locked (TVL) and more than $7 billion in trading volume since its launch in September 2025.

The overall tokenized stock market is approaching $1 billion in size, growing by around 27% in just one month. Ondo had previously already dominated the tokenized U.S. Treasuries market, with more than $2 billion in assets under management.

These figures clearly indicate that this is not a short-term trend, but a structural transformation of financial markets.

## Why stocks?

According to Ondo Finance leadership, the key advantage of stocks in the tokenization process lies in their clarity and liquidity. Unlike real estate or alternative assets, stocks have clear pricing, deep markets, and are widely understood by investors.

Tokenization enables two key benefits — easier access and the ability to use assets as collateral within DeFi systems. This combination makes stocks an ideal candidate for blockchain integration.

## 24/7 markets: the end of trading hours?

One of the most compelling ideas emerging from this trend is the possibility of trading stocks 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. If exchanges like the NYSE were to adopt such a model, tokenization could receive a massive boost.

Traditional markets are still limited by fixed trading hours, while blockchain operates continuously. The combination of these two worlds could solve one of the biggest inefficiencies in finance — time constraints and fragmentation.

## Privacy as a key for institutional adoption

While blockchain brings transparency, privacy is what will ultimately determine whether institutions fully enter this space. Banks and large financial players cannot operate on fully transparent networks where sensitive data is publicly visible.

That is why “privacy-preserving” blockchain solutions are being developed, combining transparency with data control. Examples like Canton Network and the involvement of major players such as Visa show that the infrastructure is already evolving in this direction.

## Ondo moves further: from assets to financial infrastructure

Ondo Finance is not aiming to remain just a tokenized asset issuer. Its long-term vision is to build a full financial infrastructure on-chain, including elements such as prime brokerage services.

This means enabling trading, collateralization, lending, and risk management — all within a single on-chain ecosystem. In other words, the goal is not just to tokenize assets, but to reconstruct the entire financial system.

## Why price doesn’t follow development

Despite all these advancements, the price of the ONDO token does not always reflect the project's progress. The reason lies in the nature of markets, which tend to react short-term, while the real value of such infrastructure develops over years.

Macroeconomic conditions, such as monetary policy and global liquidity, still heavily influence the entire crypto market. In such an environment, infrastructure projects are often overshadowed by more speculative narratives.

## A new phase of the market

More and more signals suggest that crypto is entering a new phase. The focus is shifting from “tokens” to real-world application — from DeFi experimentation to integration with the global financial system.

If the tokenization trend continues, the line between crypto and Wall Street could nearly disappear. But as always, the path forward will not be linear and will depend on regulation, trust, and execution.
