# RAVE drops from 28 to 1.23 in 24 hours as Binance and Bitget investigate market activity

The crypto market has once again witnessed an extreme scenario — the **RAVE** token dropped from around **28 dollars to approximately 1.23 dollars within just 24 hours**, following a sharp surge beforehand.

The sudden volatility quickly attracted the attention of major exchanges, and **Binance and Bitget confirmed they are investigating trading activity** related to the token.

## What actually happened

According to available data, RAVE followed a classic pattern:

* rapid capital inflow
* extreme price surge
* sharp collapse after the peak

Such behavior often raises concerns about:

👉 market manipulation
👉 coordinated price pumping
👉 token concentration among a small number of wallets

## Suspicions of manipulation

On-chain analysts, including well-known investigator **ZachXBT**, pointed to potential irregularities.

According to these analyses:

* a large portion of the supply may be controlled by insiders
* suspicious token flows toward exchanges were observed
* coordinated trading activity is possible

Important:

👉 these claims are not yet confirmed
👉 the investigation is ongoing

## Project response

RaveDAO publicly denied the allegations.

In a statement, they said:

* they were not involved in price manipulation
* they are not responsible for recent market movements

At the same time, the team outlined plans to:

* sell a portion of tokens
* fund development and marketing
* expand their team

## Role of exchanges

The most important part of this story is the reaction of major exchanges.

**Binance** and **Bitget** confirmed they are:

* analyzing trading data
* monitoring suspicious activity
* evaluating whether manipulation occurred

Such investigations can lead to:

* trading restrictions
* delistings
* additional regulatory scrutiny

## What this pattern means for the market

RAVE is not an isolated case.

This is a typical scenario involving:

* low liquidity
* high token concentration
* rapid retail investor inflow

This combination often leads to:

👉 extreme price spikes
👉 equally sharp crashes

## Reality: between hype and risk

Events like this highlight how unstable the crypto market can be — especially when it comes to smaller projects.

On one hand:

* new projects attract attention
* communities grow quickly

On the other hand:

* lack of transparency
* concentrated ownership
* aggressive trading

create significant risks for investors.

## Conclusion

The RAVE case is still unresolved.

The investigation is ongoing, and final answers have yet to emerge.

However, one thing is clear — such extreme price movements rarely happen without deeper underlying factors.

For investors, it comes down to one reality:

👉 high potential = high risk
