# Google AI Mode: the end of traditional search or a new phase of the internet?

Google has introduced a new feature that could significantly change how we use the internet. It’s called AI Mode — a system that combines traditional search with artificial intelligence into a unified experience.

Unlike the previous model, where users switch between multiple pages and tabs, web content and AI are now displayed side by side on the same screen.

## How AI Mode works

When a user searches on Google and clicks a result, the page no longer opens in isolation. Instead, it appears alongside an active AI panel.

This means:

* you can read the page content  
* ask questions to the AI at the same time  
* get additional explanations without losing context  

The AI uses both the content of the open page and broader web data to generate answers in real time.

## Real-world use case

For example, if you’re searching for a coffee maker:

* describe what you’re looking for  
* open a product  
* ask the AI additional questions  

Such as:

👉 “how easy is this to clean?”  
👉 “how does this compare to other models?”  

This significantly speeds up and simplifies the research process.

## Searching across multiple tabs

One of the most interesting features is the ability to include multiple open tabs in a single AI query.

This means users can:

* combine multiple information sources  
* include documents, images, or notes  
* receive unified answers  

For example:

* you’re researching hiking trails  
* you have several tabs open  
* the AI analyzes everything and suggests new options  

{{< support1 >}}

## What is actually changing

This shift is not just technical — it changes how information is consumed.

The internet is moving from:

👉 “search and click”  

to:

👉 “ask and get answers”

Users no longer need to browse dozens of pages to reach a conclusion.

## Impact on websites and media

This brings us to a key question.

If AI provides direct answers:

* will users still click on websites?  
* will smaller sites lose visibility?  

There is a real possibility that:

* traffic will decline  
* large sources will be favored  
* smaller publishers will struggle to reach audiences  

{{< support1 >}}

## Google vs AI platforms like Perplexity

Unlike some AI platforms like Perplexity, which are experimenting with value-sharing models for publishers, Google continues to rely primarily on its own ecosystem.

This means:

* it provides answers  
* keeps users within its platform  
* does not directly share revenue with content creators  

## What this means for the future

AI Mode signals the direction the internet is heading.

Artificial intelligence is no longer just a tool — it is becoming the main layer through which users access information.

This brings:

✔ faster search  
✔ fewer tab switches  
✔ higher efficiency  

But also:

⚠️ reduced visibility for smaller websites  
⚠️ potential traffic decline  
⚠️ increased dependence on major platforms

## Conclusion

Google AI Mode represents one of the biggest changes in search in recent years.

Combining the web and artificial intelligence into a single interface can significantly improve the user experience, but it also raises important questions about the future of the internet as we know it.
