# Alameda Research (FTX) moves $16 million in SOL — are creditor payouts coming?

## A technical move that carries a clear signal

As the restructuring process of FTX continues, another concrete signal is emerging from within the system.

Alameda Research, a firm linked to FTX, unstaked around **$16 million worth of SOL tokens** and moved them to an address associated with potential creditor payouts.

At first glance, this appears to be a technical action. However, the context suggests something more.

## What “unstaking” means in this case

In Proof-of-Stake networks like Solana, staking means locking tokens to support the network and earn rewards.

Unstaking is the opposite process:

* tokens are unlocked
* they become liquid
* they can be sold or distributed

In the context of FTX, such moves often precede payouts.

## A repeating pattern the market recognizes

This is not the first time Alameda has made a similar move.

About a month ago, a nearly identical transaction was observed:

* unstaking tokens
* sending them to the same address
* linked to the distribution process

Such repetition is rarely random — the market interprets it as a continuation of a payout plan.

## Are payouts imminent?

There is currently no official confirmation that these specific funds will be distributed immediately.

But the pattern is clear.

When funds are moved to addresses associated with distribution, it usually signals preparation for the next phase.

In other words, this is not an isolated move, but part of a broader strategy.

## How much remains

According to available data, Alameda still holds a significant amount of SOL.

This includes:

* around **3.5 million SOL tokens**
* worth approximately **$294 million**

This suggests the process is far from over.

Each new transfer can impact both the market and creditor expectations.

## The bigger picture: FTX still impacts the market

Although FTX collapsed in 2022, its effects are still unfolding.

The restructuring involves:

* gradual liquidation of assets
* management of large token holdings
* balancing speed and value

Every decision in this process can have long-term consequences.

* Alameda (FTX) continues preparing funds for creditor payouts
* Unstaking and transfers indicate a coordinated process
* The repeating pattern increases the credibility of the signal
* A large amount of SOL is still undistributed
* The FTX restructuring continues to actively influence the market
