# TAO under pressure: AI ‘Bitcoin’ and the question of real decentralization

The cryptocurrency TAO, associated with the Bittensor project, is sometimes described as a kind of “AI Bitcoin.” This narrative is based on the idea of decentralized artificial intelligence development, as well as technical characteristics such as a limited maximum supply of 21 million tokens, which further reinforces the perception of scarcity and long-term value.

Such a market position also carries certain risks. When a project is built not only on technology but also on a strong narrative, any shift in trust can have fast and visible consequences. This is exactly what happened to TAO following events within its own ecosystem, which triggered a debate about the project’s actual structure.

## The exit of a key team as a trigger

The key moment was the exit of the Covenant AI team, one of the more active development groups within the Bittensor network. This team was involved in building so-called subnets, subsystems where artificial intelligence models are trained and evaluated.

The exit alone would not have had such a strong impact if it had not been accompanied by public criticism. In its statements, Covenant AI questioned the way the network is governed, including the role of co-founder Jacob Steeves. In this context, the term “decentralization theatre” was used, suggesting that decentralization exists more at the level of presentation than in the system’s actual functioning.

{{< support1 >}}

## When the narrative becomes vulnerable

Such accusations have a particularly strong effect in projects that rely on the idea of decentralization. TAO is not just a technological project, but part of a broader vision of a future of artificial intelligence without central control, which attracts both investors and developers.

When that idea is challenged, market reactions can be swift. Alongside its exit from the project, Covenant AI liquidated around 37,000 TAO tokens, worth approximately $10 million at the time, further increasing market pressure and accelerating the price decline. Following the announcements, increased selling and liquidations added to the downward movement. However, it is important to note that this drop did not occur in isolation from the broader market, which was also experiencing increased volatility and declines during the same period.

## Price recovery and broader market context

After the initial drop, TAO’s price movement did not continue to diverge from the rest of the market. The subsequent stabilization coincided with overall crypto market trends, with TAO behaving similarly to most other tokens, albeit with somewhat stronger fluctuations.

This indicates that, despite internal developments, the market continues to view TAO within the broader AI and crypto narrative. In other words, the token has not detached from the market but reacts within it.

{{< support2 >}}

## Can the project recover

It is important to emphasize that the exit of a single team does not mean the end of the project. Bittensor is designed as an open network in which various teams and developers can participate, theoretically allowing replacement and continued development.

In practice, however, such transitions take time. Teams like Covenant AI bring specific knowledge and experience that cannot be easily replaced, and their departure raises additional questions about governance structure and long-term sustainability. At the same time, the broader market context supports the possibility of continued development — growth in artificial intelligence, combined with layoffs in the IT sector, is creating a pool of skilled professionals seeking new opportunities. Such teams could eventually take over parts of development and strengthen the ecosystem, although this process is rarely immediate.

## Conclusion

The situation around TAO highlights how sensitive crypto projects are to the combination of technology, narrative and trust. The price decline was neither solely the result of market movements nor purely internal issues, but rather a combination of both.

---

### Our take (Metaadvisor)

TAO continues to position itself as a project with strong potential in the AI space, but the current situation shows how quickly perception can change. The exit of one team does not break the system, but it does raise questions that the market does not ignore.

In the short term, TAO will likely continue to follow the broader market with increased volatility, while its long-term direction will depend on whether it can maintain trust and attract new high-quality participants.
