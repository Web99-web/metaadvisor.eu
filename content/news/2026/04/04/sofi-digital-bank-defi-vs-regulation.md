# Banks enter crypto: SoFi is building what DeFi still lacks

While decentralized finance has been promising a world without intermediaries for years, more and more users — especially companies — are running into the same issue: systems are complex, difficult to understand, and lack clear instructions. This is exactly the gap fintech companies like SoFi Technologies are now trying to fill. Instead of experimenting with technology that requires technical expertise, SoFi is attempting to offer something DeFi still fails to deliver — a system users can actually understand.

SoFi, a US-based digital bank, has introduced a platform that allows companies to manage both fiat and crypto transactions within a single regulated system. This means businesses no longer need to rely on multiple services for custody, transfers, and settlement, but can instead operate within one unified system available 24/7.

## One system instead of four

Until now, managing finances in the crypto space required combining several different solutions. Companies had to use banks for fiat currencies, crypto exchanges for trading, custodians for asset storage, and additional payment providers for transfers. This fragmentation increased complexity and the risk of operational mistakes.

SoFi aims to remove this friction by bringing all these functions into a single experience. This not only simplifies the underlying technology but also makes financial tools easier to understand and use.

## Stablecoin as the key bridge

A central part of this system is SoFiUSD, a dollar-backed stablecoin that acts as a bridge between traditional finance and blockchain infrastructure. Companies can convert fiat into digital assets, use blockchain for fast transactions, and still keep reserves within a regulated banking environment.

This approach removes the need for complex transitions between different platforms and reduces the technical barriers that have so far limited broader crypto adoption.

## While DeFi requires expertise — SoFi simplifies usage

The difference between these two worlds is becoming increasingly clear. DeFi often requires understanding technical concepts, navigating complex interfaces, and making decisions without clear guidance, while SoFi focuses on simplifying the entire experience and making it accessible to users without technical backgrounds.

👉 While DeFi still lacks clear user instructions, banks like SoFi are building systems that look like the future — but under control.

## The future of finance: integration, not replacement

This model suggests that the future of finance may not be fully decentralized, but hybrid. Instead of replacing traditional finance, crypto is increasingly being integrated into it.

This brings greater stability, clearer rules, and easier usability, but also introduces more control and regulatory oversight. For many companies, this trade-off is acceptable because it offers predictability and operational security.

## Conclusion: two worlds, one direction

DeFi and SoFi represent two different philosophies. One emphasizes freedom and decentralization, while the other focuses on security and usability. In practice, most users choose systems they understand and trust.

If DeFi wants to become mainstream, it will need to solve its biggest issue — not technology, but accessibility.

## MetaAdvisor take:

* SoFi shows what a regulated crypto future could look like
* DeFi still struggles with UX and usability
* Stablecoins are becoming the key bridge between two financial systems
* Companies prefer simplicity and security over full decentralization
* The future will likely be a hybrid of DeFi and traditional finance
