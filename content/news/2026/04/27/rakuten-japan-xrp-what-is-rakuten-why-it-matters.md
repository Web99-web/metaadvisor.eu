# Rakuten (Japan) introduces XRP: what this tech giant is and why it matters more than it seems

The news that Rakuten is integrating XRP into its payment system quickly gained traction within the crypto community. Social media posts generated tens of thousands of views and strong engagement, showing that those following the market clearly recognize the potential of this move.

Outside that circle, however, the reaction has been much quieter. The reason is not a lack of importance, but a lack of context. Many people simply do not know what Rakuten is — and without that understanding, it is difficult to grasp the significance of this integration.

## What is Rakuten and why it matters

Rakuten is one of the largest digital ecosystems in Japan. It is not just a single app, but a platform that combines e-commerce, digital payments, mobile services, streaming, and financial products into one integrated system.

For millions of users in Japan, Rakuten is part of everyday life — a place where they shop, pay, earn points, and manage their finances. Its loyalty program, Rakuten Points, represents billions of dollars in value and is actively used across the entire ecosystem.

Outside Asia, Rakuten is less recognizable than major US tech companies, but many people have still seen it. The company has been highly visible through sports sponsorships, including football clubs and global events, which is why its logo often appears on TV.

In Europe and beyond, Rakuten is present through e-commerce and services such as Rakuten TV, but its core strength remains in Japan and the broader Asian market.

## From loyalty points to crypto

By introducing XRP, Rakuten is connecting its existing loyalty system with a cryptocurrency. Users can use points to buy XRP and potentially use it within the digital ecosystem.

This is what makes the move different from many other crypto announcements. Instead of remaining confined to exchanges, XRP becomes part of a system with millions of active users and daily transactions.

It marks a shift from speculation to real-world use.

## In parallel: banks testing Ripple

While Rakuten is bringing crypto into consumer payments, the banking sector is exploring its use in cross-border transactions.

According to recent reports, Kasikornbank is working with Ripple to test on-chain cross-border transfers between regions such as the UAE and Thailand.

The goal is clear: faster, more efficient, and potentially cheaper international payments compared to traditional systems.

## Two worlds, one direction

What makes this development particularly interesting is that XRP is appearing in two very different contexts at the same time:

* retail (Rakuten and everyday payments)
* banking (Ripple and international transfers)

This parallel expansion suggests that crypto technology is spreading across multiple layers of the financial system.

## Conclusion

Rakuten is not just another platform — it is a digital giant connecting millions of users and real financial flows. That is why its integration of XRP carries more weight than many other crypto announcements.

Combined with ongoing tests in the banking sector, this development shows that crypto is gradually becoming part of the real economy — across multiple layers at once.

For the crypto community, this is a clear signal. For the broader public, it is only the beginning of understanding.
