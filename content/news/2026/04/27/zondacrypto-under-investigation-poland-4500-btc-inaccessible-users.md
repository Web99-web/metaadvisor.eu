# Zondacrypto under investigation in Poland: 4,500 BTC inaccessible, hundreds of users affected

The crypto market has once again been shaken by news from Europe, where Polish authorities have launched an investigation into the platform Zondacrypto after a serious issue with access to funds was reported. At the center of the case is a cold wallet holding approximately 4,500 Bitcoin, whose availability is now in question.

Although all details are not yet fully confirmed, it is already clear that this is not just an internal technical issue, but a situation that may directly impact users who held funds on the platform.

## What actually happened

According to available information, the situation escalated when it became known that the platform had lost access to part of its crypto reserves. The issue involves a so-called cold wallet, meaning funds that should have been stored offline for enhanced security.

When such a wallet becomes inaccessible, the situation quickly shifts from a technical problem to a serious financial and security risk. Estimates suggest the amount involved is around **4,500 BTC, currently valued at approximately $347 million**, depending on Bitcoin’s market price.

## Investigation and potential victims

Polish prosecutors have already launched an official investigation into suspected fraud and investor losses. Reports mention hundreds of potentially affected users, making this one of the more serious cases in the region.

It is important to note that this is not a decentralized system where users control their own funds, but a centralized platform. In such a model, users entrust their cryptocurrencies to the platform, which manages them on their behalf.

If the platform loses access to those funds or faces internal issues, the consequences are directly transferred to users.

## CEO and additional concerns

The situation is further complicated by reports that the company’s CEO, Przemysław Kral, left Poland for Israel at the time the issue escalated. It is also reported that he holds Israeli citizenship, which could complicate legal proceedings or potential extradition.

Additionally, communication with him has reportedly been cut off, increasing uncertainty and raising further questions about the situation.

{{< support2 >}}

## Why this matters for the wider market

Events like this rarely affect just one platform. Every time a centralized exchange loses control over funds, it raises broader concerns about security and trust across the crypto ecosystem.

The difference between holding crypto on an exchange and in a private wallet becomes especially clear in such cases. In a self-custody model, users control their own keys and assets, while exchange users rely entirely on a third party.

## Conclusion

While the investigation is still ongoing and no final conclusions have been reached, the Zondacrypto case already highlights how vulnerable the crypto market can be when centralized platforms encounter problems. If the allegations prove true, the consequences will not be limited to the company alone, but will also affect users who entrusted it with their funds.
