# eToro acquires Zengo for around $70 million: a move toward self-custody and an onchain future

Investment platform eToro has made a significant strategic move into the self-custody segment by announcing the acquisition of crypto wallet provider Zengo in a deal valued at around $70 million.

This acquisition comes at a time when the crypto industry is increasingly shifting toward solutions where users maintain full control over their assets, rather than relying on centralized platforms.

## What Zengo brings

Zengo is known as a non-custodial wallet, meaning users control their own private keys — a core principle in the crypto ecosystem.

Unlike traditional wallets, Zengo uses MPC (Multi-Party Computation), a technology that enables secure access without a seed phrase, reducing the risk of losing access to funds.

The platform already has more than 2 million users and offers:

* token swaps
* staking
* fiat on-ramp options

## eToro expands beyond traditional trading

With the acquisition of Zengo, eToro is moving beyond its role as a traditional investment platform and entering the onchain ecosystem.

The company plans to expand its offering to include:

* tokenized assets
* decentralized markets
* perpetual futures
* yield products

This clearly shows eToro’s intention to position itself at the intersection of traditional finance and decentralized technologies.

## From regulated platform to crypto-native users

CEO Yoni Assia emphasized that the goal is to attract “crypto-native” users — those who actively use blockchain products and want greater control over their assets.

Zengo will remain separate from eToro’s regulated services, meaning users will interact directly with blockchain protocols rather than through a traditional brokerage model.

## Crypto as a core revenue driver

The numbers highlight how important crypto has become for eToro. In 2025, the company reported total revenue of $13.8 billion, of which $12.98 billion came from crypto-related activities.

This confirms that crypto is no longer a side business, but a central pillar of the platform’s model.

## Bitcoin to $250,000?

In addition to the acquisition, attention was drawn to the CEO’s statement that Bitcoin, after another quarter of market weakness, could enter an accumulation phase and eventually rise above $250,000.

This prediction comes at a time when the market is divided — some expect continued growth, while others question the relevance of the traditional four-year cycle.

## Conclusion

By acquiring Zengo, eToro is clearly signaling the direction of the industry — toward greater user control, decentralization, and deeper integration with blockchain technologies.

At the same time, optimistic Bitcoin forecasts suggest that industry leaders remain confident in long-term market growth, despite short-term volatility.
