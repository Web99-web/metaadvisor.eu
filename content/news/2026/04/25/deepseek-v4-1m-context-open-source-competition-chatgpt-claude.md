# DeepSeek V4 and ‘1M context’: can open AI compete with ChatGPT and Claude

## A new player in a market dominated by closed models

The AI market is currently dominated by closed models like ChatGPT and Claude. They offer strong performance, but come with limitations — controlled access, API costs, and dependency on large companies.

DeepSeek is trying to change that model.

## What “1M context” means and why it matters

Context defines how much information an AI can keep in memory during a task. Most models were limited to around 128k tokens.

DeepSeek V4 pushes that to 1 million tokens.

This allows AI to process hundreds of pages at once without losing context.

## The difference vs ChatGPT and Claude

Models like ChatGPT and Claude are powerful, but:

* not open source
* paid APIs
* closed ecosystems

DeepSeek takes a different approach: openness and accessibility for developers.

This means:

* more control
* less dependency
* potentially lower costs

## “Free” changes the dynamics

Open source doesn’t mean fully free, but it gives developers flexibility to build and optimize their own solutions.

## More context doesn’t mean better AI

More data doesn’t guarantee accuracy. It increases capability, but errors are still possible.

## Bigger picture: a shift in the industry

Open models could reshape the AI landscape:

* more competition
* lower costs
* faster innovation
