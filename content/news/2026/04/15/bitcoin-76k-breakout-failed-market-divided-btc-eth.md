# Bitcoin between decline and growth: breakout attempt above $76K failed, market divided

Bitcoin has once again shown in the past two days how indecisive the market is. After attempting to break above the $76,000 level, the price quickly fell back below $74,000 and is currently trading around $73,700, confirming that key resistance is still holding.

Although there was a short-term surge, the momentum did not last. The breakout attempt turned into a pullback, and the market has once again entered a zone of uncertainty where investors are increasingly struggling to choose a direction.

## Analysts still see a possible drop to $50K

Despite occasional price increases, some analysts still expect a so-called “final flush” that could push Bitcoin down to around $50,000 before any meaningful recovery begins. This scenario is interpreted as the final phase of accumulation and a form of market “reset” before a new growth cycle.

According to this view, recent price bounces are considered weak compared to the broader trend, which still shows signs of weakness. From this perspective, the market has not yet completed its corrective phase.

## At the same time: potential for a strong upside move is growing

At the same time, opposing signals are emerging. While the price is trying to find direction, data from the derivatives market shows an increase in short positions.

Funding rates remain negative, and open interest is rising, indicating that more traders are positioning for a decline. This combination has historically often led to short squeeze scenarios — rapid price increases caused by forced closing of short positions.

Such setups can result in fast and unexpected moves — going against the dominant market sentiment.

## Ethereum is sending a different signal

While Bitcoin is stagnating, Ethereum is showing signs of relative strength. Over the past two days, ETH reached around $2,415 and is currently trading near $2,312, outperforming Bitcoin in the short term.

An increasing ETH/BTC ratio is often seen as a signal of rising risk appetite, suggesting capital rotation toward more volatile parts of the market. Historically, such movements have often preceded broader crypto market recoveries.

{{< support2 >}}

## A market between two scenarios

The current situation clearly shows how divided the market is. On one side, there are expectations of further downside toward lower levels, while on the other side, technical and derivatives signals point to the possibility of a sudden upward move.

Bitcoin is now positioned between a potential “final flush” and a possible surprise rally — a scenario that will largely depend on market behavior in the coming days.

## Conclusion

The question is no longer whether a major move will happen.

The real question is — in which direction.
