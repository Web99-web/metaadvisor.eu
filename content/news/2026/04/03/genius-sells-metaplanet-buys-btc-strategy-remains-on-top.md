# Genius sells, Metaplanet buys BTC, while Strategy remains on top

## Three companies, three completely different strategies

As the crypto market remains under pressure and searches for direction, companies around the world are making completely opposite decisions when it comes to Bitcoin. The latest moves reveal a clear divide — Singapore-based Genius is exiting Bitcoin, Japan’s Metaplanet is aggressively accumulating, while U.S.-based Strategy remains firmly at the top without changing course.

This situation is not just a difference in business decisions — it reveals a deeper truth: Bitcoin is no longer a universal strategy, but a financial tool each company uses depending on its own balance sheet, risk tolerance, and long-term outlook.

## Genius sells despite business growth

Singapore-based Genius Group decided during the first quarter of 2026 to fully liquidate its remaining Bitcoin reserves in order to repay debt. This decision comes despite strong financial results — revenue surged by 171%, and the company moved from a loss into a significant profit.

However, despite business growth, balance sheet stability and risk reduction became the priority. In doing so, Genius moved away from its earlier “Bitcoin first” strategy, under which it planned to hold the majority of its reserves in Bitcoin.

## Metaplanet buys while others exit

In contrast to Genius Group, Japan’s Metaplanet is moving in the opposite direction. During the first quarter of 2026, the company purchased an additional 5,075 BTC worth nearly $400 million, increasing its total holdings to more than 40,000 BTC.

This move positioned Metaplanet as the third-largest corporate Bitcoin treasury in the world, overtaking several major players that have reduced their positions. The strategy clearly signals long-term confidence in Bitcoin, even amid uncertain market conditions.

It is important to note that Metaplanet is currently sitting on unrealized losses based on its average purchase price, but is clearly betting on long-term recovery and growth.

## Strategy remains the dominant player

Throughout this landscape, U.S.-based Strategy continues to stand out, holding by far the largest Bitcoin treasury in the world, with more than 760,000 BTC.

Unlike other companies that adjust their strategies based on market conditions, Strategy remains consistent in its accumulation approach. This consistency makes it a benchmark for corporate Bitcoin strategy.

While some are exiting positions and others are just entering, Strategy continues to play the long-term game regardless of short-term volatility.

## A market without a single narrative

What these three examples clearly show is that the market no longer has a single narrative. In the past, the dominant idea was that companies would accumulate Bitcoin as a hedge against inflation, but today’s reality is far more complex.

Today, we see a market where:

* some companies sell to stabilize their business
* others buy and increase exposure
* others remain consistent regardless of conditions

Such diversity of strategies is typical for periods of uncertainty and transitions between market cycles.

## What does this mean for Bitcoin?

This divergence shows that Bitcoin is currently in a testing phase. It is not simply about rising or falling prices, but about a market searching for a new equilibrium.

Geopolitical tensions, macroeconomic pressure, and changes in investor behavior further complicate the situation. In such an environment, companies are making decisions driven more by their own financial needs than by overall market sentiment.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)

This story clearly reflects a new market reality: Bitcoin is no longer an ideological decision, but a financial instrument.

Metaadvisor.eu sees a clear divide — companies with strong liquidity and long-term vision continue to buy, while those under pressure are selling, regardless of previous strategies.

This distinction may define the next cycle.

Because the question is no longer whether everyone will buy Bitcoin — but who can actually afford to.
