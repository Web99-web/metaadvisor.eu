# Pepe celebrates 3 years today: over 517,000 holders and a project that survived all meme cycles

On this day, **April 14, 2023**, the pool for Pepe was created — a moment that marked the beginning of one of the most unusual, yet resilient phenomena in the crypto world.

Three years later, Pepe still has more than **517,000 holders**, placing it among the rare meme coins that did not disappear after the initial hype. In a market where most similar projects last only days or weeks, this number is significant — it reflects continuity.

![](static/images/news/Pepe-3-years.jpeg)

## A meme that had a community from the start

Unlike most meme coins, Pepe did not begin without support. From the very beginning, it gathered a large number of holders and built a strong community that encouraged one another. That early phase was crucial — many chose not to sell, but instead held their positions and built a sense of collective belief.

In the crypto world, where projects often collapse at the first major drop, such behavior is uncommon. Yet for Pepe, it helped it survive early cycles and remain visible.

## A meme that survived the market

At first glance, Pepe looked like just another meme coin — without clear utility and without complex technology. But that is exactly where its uniqueness lies: it created a rare level of hype that continues within its community.

Many cryptocurrencies start at extremely low prices, sometimes at 0.00000001, which for people outside crypto may seem illogical. However, such beginnings are often part of broader market dynamics. When Pepe launched, it coincided with a strong market rise that generated significant gains — especially for those who entered early.

Today, the situation is different. The current price of Pepe is 0.00000358. The market is in a correction phase, and many coins, starting with BTC, which is now about 50% lower than it was 6 months ago, are trading significantly below previous levels. But it is exactly in such conditions that the difference becomes visible between projects that disappear and those that endure.

## Not every meme coin is the same

Most meme coins disappear almost as quickly as they appear. Without community and attention, such projects simply fade away.

Pepe, however, shows a different pattern. Over three years, it has gone through multiple market cycles — rises, drops, and periods of stagnation — and has not disappeared. In fact, it continues to follow market sentiment: when the market rises, Pepe rises; when it falls, Pepe falls with it.

Maybe some Pepe holders will support our knowledge.

## More than a project: digital culture

Pepe cannot be viewed as a traditional crypto project. Its value does not come from technology, but from community, attention, and internet culture. It functions as a symbol — something that exists because people believe in its presence.

In that sense, Pepe is closer to digital art than to a financial instrument.

## Conclusion

Three years after launch, Pepe is no longer just another meme coin.

It is proof that in crypto, it is not always the most complex projects that survive — but those that manage to hold attention.

And while most meme coins disappear without a trace, Pepe remains.
