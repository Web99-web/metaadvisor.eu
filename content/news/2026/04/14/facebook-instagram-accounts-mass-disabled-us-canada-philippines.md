# Thousands of Facebook and Instagram accounts of users from the U.S., Canada and the Philippines were mass-disabled

**Imagine not being able to read this news — because you were banned from the “social” platform you use every day.** Suddenly, you no longer have access to information from around the world, your city, or even your own network.

At first glance, this may look like just another technical platform issue. But for many users, this was not a temporary glitch. It was a sudden and complete disappearance from the digital world.

Users report being logged out simultaneously, without a clear explanation and without any real chance of recovery. Attempts to log back in lead only to a message that the account has been disabled — with no option to appeal or contact human support. For profiles built over years — personal, business, entire communities — the result is immediate: silence. They were removed from the platform. And this pattern has been repeating for months, worldwide.

![](/images/Meta/Facebook-banned-people-US-Canada-Phillippines-27.03.2026.png)

## This is not an isolated case

Over the past few months, as we have been tracking this issue, similar cases have been quietly accumulating. What makes this situation different is not just the scale, but the pattern. Accounts are not being restricted or temporarily limited — they are being deleted. Instantly, without warning, and without any real possibility to respond. The appeal window is 180 days, but only on paper — in reality, it is nearly impossible to reach support, and time simply runs out… until it runs out. Users in the EU may still have the strongest option through DSA institutions and the Appeals Centre Europe. Some users from the U.S. have contacted us — they were banned back in 2025 and have not been able to reach support since.

Some users try to share their cases on platforms like Reddit or X. But many people don’t use those platforms at all and are not even aware that others have been removed — they are likely questioning whether they did something wrong… For them, Facebook or Instagram is the only digital connection point — and once removed, they lose the ability to even say what happened. Their voice is — turned off.

## A growing problem without answers

At the same time, public attention around wrongful account disabling is increasing. Petitions and discussions are gaining momentum, pointing to a broader issue: lack of transparency and absence of human oversight in critical decisions. Since we started tracking this petition, the number of people signing it has only been increasing. When we first checked it on April 4, 2026, the number was below 61,800 — and since then it has been rising daily, now approaching 63,000.

![](/images/Meta/Meta-wrongfully-disabling-accounts-Facebook-Instagram.png)

## What is the real problem

The core issue is not just that accounts are being disabled. The problem is how it happens.

AI systems are increasingly taking over moderation on large platforms. In theory, they are designed to detect abuse, spam, and harmful content. In practice, they can also remove real users — without explanation, without accountability, and without any real possibility to appeal. As we have been pointing out, AI makes mistakes and requires human oversight. But what no one is talking about is — someone designed that AI, and someone is responsible for it.

## When you disappear from the digital world, you lose access to real, current and uncensored information

AI is removing **real human beings** from “social” platforms — cutting them off from the modern digital world and all the information that comes with it. It happens silently and instantly. No warning. No message. **Your friends may not even notice for months that you’ve disappeared.**

And when people are removed from social platforms, it’s not just their voice that disappears — it’s like someone switches off the light and leaves them in a dark room, in a black hole without access to today’s information. They lose access to digital information. They can no longer easily exchange messages and photos with friends across the world, or stay informed about what is happening in their city or neighborhood. **By being removed from the network, they are effectively cut off from the flow of today’s information and modern life.**

But the consequences go even further. People are cut off from current and uncensored information, which directly affects their ability to function in today’s society. They lose access to job opportunities — whether they are offering or seeking — as well as access to knowledge, markets, and everyday information that is essential for decision-making. In the digital world, **being excluded no longer means just being offline — it means being completely locked out of the system and the digital society.**

## Conclusion

The question is no longer whether mistakes happen.

The real question is:
how many real users have been removed — and how many will never come back?
