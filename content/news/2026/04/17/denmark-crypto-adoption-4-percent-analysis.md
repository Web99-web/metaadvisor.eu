# Only 4% of Danes hold crypto despite global growth: central bank reveals the reasons

Despite the strong global growth of the cryptocurrency market, Denmark is showing an exceptionally low level of digital asset adoption. According to data from the Danish central bank, **only 4% of citizens own cryptocurrencies** — a figure that has not significantly changed since **2023**.

The survey, conducted on a sample of more than 3,000 citizens, shows that crypto remains a marginal investment category in one of Europe’s most developed economies.

## Most investments remain small

Even among those who own crypto, most invest relatively small amounts. The majority of respondents hold less than 10,000 Danish kroner (around 1,570 US dollars), confirming that cryptocurrencies are still not widely accepted as a serious investment class.

The total value of crypto assets in Denmark is estimated between 317 and 847 million dollars, further confirming that crypto still does not play a significant role in citizens’ financial portfolios.

## Denmark lags behind the rest of Europe

Compared to other European countries, Denmark ranks at the lower end of crypto adoption. In countries such as Norway, Finland and the United Kingdom, more than 10% of the population holds digital assets.

This data highlights significant differences within Europe, despite similar levels of economic development and digital infrastructure.

## Banks and taxes as barriers

The Danish central bank notes that domestic banks have traditionally taken a cautious stance toward cryptocurrencies. In many cases, customers were not given easy access to purchase crypto through banking platforms, and such investments were often labeled as high risk.

This approach may have limited broader adoption of digital assets among citizens, especially those who rely on traditional financial institutions.

In addition, the earlier tax treatment of cryptocurrencies in Denmark was less favorable compared to other types of investments, which further discouraged investment and reduced interest among the wider population.

{{< support2 >}}

## Demographic picture: younger and wealthier lead

Data shows that cryptocurrency ownership is concentrated among younger citizens and higher-income individuals. However, even within these groups, investment sizes remain relatively modest.

This suggests that crypto in Denmark is still not widely accepted as a long-term investment strategy, but rather as a supplementary or experimental asset.

## A stable financial system reduces incentives

One of the key conclusions of the report is that a stable financial system and high trust in institutions can reduce the need for alternative forms of investment.

Denmark has a well-developed banking system, low inflation and a strong social safety net, which reduces the motivation for citizens to enter higher-risk asset classes such as cryptocurrencies, and therefore interest in crypto remains naturally limited.

## Conclusion

Findings from the Danish central bank show that crypto adoption depends not only on the availability of technology, but also on economic, regulatory and social context.

Despite global market growth, Denmark remains an example of a country where crypto still plays a limited role — at least for now.
