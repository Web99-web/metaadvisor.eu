# OKX launches regulated crypto derivatives in Europe: a new step toward market institutionalization

Crypto exchange OKX has taken a major step in the European market by launching a regulated derivatives product called X-Perps. The offering is available across the European Economic Area (EEA) and is based on the company’s MiFID license in Malta.

This move marks a further integration of the crypto market into the traditional financial system, especially in the derivatives segment, which already dominates trading activity.

## What are X-Perps and what do they offer

OKX’s new X-Perps product is designed as a long-term derivatives instrument with maturities of up to five years. The platform allows trading with leverage of up to 10x, representing a balance between accessibility and risk control.

A key advantage is support for multiple types of collateral, including:

* euro (EUR)
* US dollar (USD)
* cryptocurrencies

This enables flexibility for different investor profiles, from retail users to institutional participants.

## Regulation as a key differentiator

Unlike many global platforms, OKX offers this product in compliance with the European regulatory framework MiFID (Markets in Financial Instruments Directive).

MiFID sets rules for:

* investor protection
* market transparency
* risk management

With this approach, OKX is clearly targeting investors who prioritize security and regulatory clarity.

## Broad offering — from Bitcoin to memecoins

The platform includes a wide range of trading pairs, from leading cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin (BTC), Ether (ETH), and XRP to memecoins like Dogecoin (DOGE) and Pepe (PEPE).

This combination shows that OKX aims to attract both institutional investors and speculative traders.

{{< support2 >}}

## OKX among leading derivatives players

OKX is already one of the leading global players in crypto derivatives, ranking just behind the largest exchanges in terms of trading volume.

This confirms a broader trend: derivatives are becoming a core segment of the crypto industry, often surpassing spot trading.

## What this means for Europe

The introduction of regulated derivatives in Europe could significantly increase investor confidence and open the door for institutional capital.

At the same time, stricter regulation may limit flexibility and reduce attractiveness for high-risk traders.

## Conclusion

OKX’s move highlights how the crypto market is increasingly aligning with traditional financial frameworks.

The focus is shifting from rapid growth toward stability, regulation, and long-term sustainability.
