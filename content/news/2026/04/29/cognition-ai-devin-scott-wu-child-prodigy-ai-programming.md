# Cognition AI (Devin) and Scott Wu: A Child Prodigy Building AI That Can Code

A video of a young boy at a math competition came across our feed — answering questions so quickly that we hadn’t even had time to understand what the question was. At first, it looked like just another viral trick. But curiosity took over — we started digging into who this boy actually was.

That search led us to the name Scott Wu. And at that point, the story stopped being just an interesting video — and started pointing toward one of the most ambitious young minds and AI projects today.

![Scott Wu Mathcounts 2010](/images/companies/Scott-Wu-2010-Mathcounts-National-Competition.png)

*Scott Wu as a young competitor at the MATHCOUNTS National Competition*

## From a Math Competition and “Child Prodigy” to the Top of Programming

The video now circulating online originates from the U.S. math competition MATHCOUNTS, where Scott Wu demonstrated remarkable speed and precision at a very young age.

What followed later is even more impressive. Wu became one of the best young programmers in the world, winning three gold medals at the International Olympiad in Informatics, including first place globally in 2014 with a perfect score of 600/600.

He studied at Harvard University before fully dedicating himself to building technology and launching his startup.

## Cognition AI: A Startup Targeting the Core of the IT Industry

Cognition AI was founded in 2023 and quickly attracted attention from investors and the tech community. The reason is not just the team — but the ambition behind the project.

Their main product, Devin AI, is described as the first “AI software engineer.”

Unlike traditional AI tools that assist developers, Devin goes a step further. The goal is not assistance — but autonomy.

The system is designed to write code, understand tasks, identify and fix bugs, test solutions, and deploy applications. In other words, this is not a tool for developers — but an attempt to build a digital developer.

## What Devin Means for the Future of Work

If this technology proves successful, the impact could be significant. Software development is one of the most in-demand and best-paid professions today. Companies compete for talent, and many projects depend on the availability of skilled developers.

In such an environment, AI that can take over part of that work changes the rules of the game. But Devin is not just about automating simple tasks — the ambition is much bigger: solving complex problems independently.

This raises questions about the future of the industry — whether AI will take over part of junior developer roles, whether senior roles will shift, and whether the entire model of software development will need to evolve. These changes could also affect education systems and the decisions young people make about their future. If the perception of the profession shifts, the question becomes whether interest in computer science will rise or fall — and what role humans will play in a world where AI increasingly handles technical tasks.

## Hype vs Reality

Despite the strong interest and impressive demonstrations, it is important to stay grounded.

Devin is not yet a perfect system. Like most current AI models, it still has limitations — from errors in complex scenarios to the need for supervision and control.

In practice, it currently resembles a capable junior developer more than a fully autonomous engineer.

## Why Investors Are Pouring in Billions

According to available data, Cognition AI is valued at several billion dollars, in some cases up to around $4 billion. This valuation is not based solely on current capabilities — but on potential.

If AI reaches a point where it can reliably develop software, the impact could be enormous — from reducing development costs to accelerating innovation and reshaping the labor market.

## A New Direction for Artificial Intelligence

Cognition AI is part of a broader shift in which AI is no longer just a support tool, but a system capable of executing tasks independently.

Instead of asking how AI can help humans, the question is increasingly becoming what tasks AI can perform on its own.

In this context, software development is one of the first fields where this transformation is becoming visible.

## Conclusion

Scott Wu’s story shows how a path that begins at math competitions can lead to the very top of the technology industry — and how the boundary between human work and automation is rapidly shifting.
