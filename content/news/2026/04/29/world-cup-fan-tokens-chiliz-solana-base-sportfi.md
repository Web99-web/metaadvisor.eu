# World Cup hype: Fan tokens in focus as Chiliz expands to Solana and Base

As the World Cup approaches, interest in sports and digital assets is rising again. Metaadvisor highlights how Chiliz, a sports-focused blockchain platform, is expanding its portfolio of over 70 fan tokens to Solana and Base — aiming to capture the wave of global attention.

The timing is not accidental. The World Cup brings massive global exposure, billions of viewers, and a surge in engagement. Chiliz is clearly betting that this attention can translate into real trading volume.

This move represents a strategic shift — from a closed ecosystem to an “omnichain” model, where tokens become available across multiple blockchain networks.

Fan tokens allow supporters to engage with their favorite teams, access rewards, and even participate in minor decisions. Chiliz already works with major clubs such as PSG, Barcelona, and Manchester City.

## Omnichain strategy and real market push

This is not just a technical upgrade — it’s a repositioning. Instead of keeping fan tokens inside its own ecosystem, Chiliz is pushing them into active markets like Solana and Base, where liquidity and users already exist.

The goal is clear: turn fan tokens into real trading assets.

Until now, the issue was obvious. Fan tokens existed, but markets around them were thin, fragmented, and largely ignored by serious traders.

With the omnichain approach, Chiliz is trying to fix that. One token, multiple networks — without wrapped versions and without splitting supply.

In theory, this should simplify trading and increase volume.

## The hype is real — but the market is not convinced

Despite the strong narrative and World Cup hype, market reaction remains cautious.

The CHZ token dropped between 5% and 9%, signaling that investors are not buying into the story blindly.

There are clear concerns:

* liquidity is still fragmented
* multi-chain can introduce more complexity
* more networks do not automatically mean more demand

👉 In simple terms: hype ≠ price growth.

## SportFi — the next big thing or just a narrative?

Behind this expansion lies a broader concept: SportFi.

SportFi (sports finance on blockchain) refers to the combination of sports, tokenization, and decentralized finance (DeFi — Decentralized Finance).

The idea is ambitious:

* fan tokens as tradable assets
* integration into DeFi systems
* potential links to real-world sports revenue

But for now, the market is not fully convinced this transition is happening yet.

## Conclusion

Chiliz is making a bold move — trying to push fan tokens from a niche product into a global crypto category.

But the market is sending a clear message: attention alone is not enough.

The World Cup may act as a catalyst, but it won’t automatically change the underlying economics.
