The crypto market is entering a new phase of tension. While Bitcoin has been trading in a relatively narrow range between $60,000 and $70,000 for weeks, a more serious institutional risk-management strategy is unfolding beneath the surface. At the same time, long-term chart structures are reappearing — patterns that previously marked the final and deepest stages of bear markets in 2014 and 2022.

## ETF Funds and Corporate Treasuries Are Buying “Insurance”

According to derivatives data, particularly from Deribit — which accounts for roughly 80% of global crypto options activity — demand has surged for put options with a $60,000 strike price and expiries ranging from six to twelve months. Open interest at that level has climbed to approximately $1.5 billion, making it the most concentrated options level across the board.

A put option functions like insurance: it allows the buyer to sell Bitcoin at a predetermined price ($60,000) even if the market falls significantly lower. In other words, major players are not exiting their positions — but they are preparing for a downside scenario.

This becomes even more significant when considering ownership concentration. U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs have accumulated over 1.26 million BTC, while publicly listed companies hold an additional 1.14 million BTC. Combined, that represents more than 11% of Bitcoin’s circulating supply. When participants controlling such a large share of supply begin systematically buying downside protection, it signals disciplined caution.

## Selling Pressure Is Easing — But Liquidity Remains Fragile

Some analysts suggest that the intense wave of selling seen earlier this month may have run its course. Bitcoin has stabilized temporarily, yet attempts to reclaim the $70,000 level continue to face resistance. The market appears exhausted, but without a clear catalyst for a decisive breakout.

Liquidity across both spot and futures markets remains thin. Historically, Bitcoin rarely sustains rallies when both liquidity sources deteriorate simultaneously. In such conditions, volatility becomes the dominant theme.

More cautious voices warn that if global macroeconomic conditions were to deteriorate significantly, the $30,000 region could emerge as a major support zone, with $16,000 representing a long-term structural floor required to preserve the broader multi-year bull trend.

## The 2014 and 2022 Bear Pattern Is Back

Adding to market unease is the reappearance of a long-term technical structure that previously preceded deep bear market extensions in 2014 and 2022.

In both instances, once this pattern emerged, Bitcoin experienced significant declines within a relatively short period. While past performance is never a guarantee of future results, experienced traders understand that recurring long-term formations cannot simply be ignored.

Interestingly, U.S.-listed spot ETFs have still attracted strong inflows during recent sessions, suggesting that some long-term investors are using corrections as accumulation opportunities. This creates a paradox: institutional capital is simultaneously hedging and buying.

At Metaadvisor.eu, we view this dual behavior as a hallmark of mature capital management rather than emotional decision-making.

## Nasdaq Correlation and the Nvidia Effect

Bitcoin’s recent decline coincided with a pullback in technology equities following Nvidia’s earnings reaction. Crypto markets continue to show increasing correlation with the Nasdaq. When equities enter a “risk-off” phase, leveraged crypto positions are often the first to be reduced.

Despite short-term pressure, several altcoins — including Cardano and Solana — have outperformed Bitcoin on a weekly basis. This suggests that risk appetite has not vanished entirely; instead, it is being redistributed selectively across narratives.

## Capital Rotation Toward AI and Deflationary Models

While Bitcoin consolidates, tokens tied to artificial intelligence and innovative governance models are attracting attention. Decred posted notable gains after treasury governance adjustments, and Internet Computer (ICP) moved higher following a proposal to burn 20% of network revenue, introducing a usage-linked deflationary mechanism.

This capital rotation indicates that the market is not devoid of momentum — it is searching for long-term sustainability themes.

AI-linked tokens, governance reform models, and tokenomics innovation are increasingly shaping the next wave of speculative and structural flows. Metaadvisor has observed similar behavior in previous cycles where capital temporarily rotates before returning to core assets.

## A Market Between Caution and Structural Accumulation

The current environment can be summarized as a balancing act:

- Institutions are buying protection below $60,000  
- Long-term charts signal historically bearish patterns  
- ETF inflows remain resilient  
- Altcoins and AI tokens show relative strength  
- Macro uncertainty continues to dominate  

This is not panic. It is calculated caution.

The coming months will likely test whether ETF demand remains strong enough to absorb supply if volatility intensifies. If institutional inflows persist, Bitcoin may stabilize and rebuild momentum. If they slow, the protective positioning currently visible in options markets may prove prescient.

At Metaadvisor, we believe the key variable is not short-term price action, but whether long-term capital maintains conviction in the face of macro uncertainty.
