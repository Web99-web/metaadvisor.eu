# Truth Social Seeks SEC Approval for Two Crypto ETFs — Including Staking Yield

<small>Image is symbolic.</small>

Yorkville America Equities, linked to the Truth Social brand, has filed registration documents with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) for two new cryptocurrency ETFs, expanding its ambitions in the digital asset market.

The proposed **Truth Social Bitcoin and Ether ETF** would provide exposure to BTC and ETH. A second product, the **Truth Social Cronos Yield Maximizer ETF**, would invest in the CRO token and generate potential returns through staking on the Cronos blockchain.

## Staking inside an ETF structure

Unlike traditional spot ETFs that passively track asset prices, the Cronos fund would integrate staking rewards — income earned by supporting proof-of-stake blockchain networks.

If approved, this could represent a significant regulatory milestone, as yield-generating ETF structures remain a debated topic within U.S. crypto policy discussions.

## Infrastructure partnership

Crypto.com is expected to serve as custodian, liquidity provider and staking services partner. Distribution would occur through Foris Capital US LLC, a registered broker-dealer affiliated with Crypto.com.

## Broader implications

Truth Social previously filed for a spot Bitcoin ETF and a digital asset basket ETF in 2025, neither of which has launched yet. The new filings indicate continued expansion into institutional crypto products.

If approved, these ETFs could signal further convergence between politically branded financial vehicles and the growing digital asset ecosystem.
