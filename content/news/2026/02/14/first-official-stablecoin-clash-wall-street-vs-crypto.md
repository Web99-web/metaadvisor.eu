# First Official Stablecoin Clash: Wall Street vs Crypto — Who Controls Yield?

<small>Image is symbolic.</small>

For the first time, the disagreement between traditional banking institutions and the crypto industry over stablecoin yield has been formally documented in competing policy papers.

At the center of the dispute is a crucial question: **Should stablecoins be allowed to offer yield or rewards?**

## Banks push for yield prohibition

Major financial institutions submitted a document titled **“Yield and Interest Prohibition Principles”**, arguing that stablecoins offering returns would directly compete with bank deposits.

In their view, allowing yield could undermine the core structure of the U.S. banking system.

## Crypto industry calls for flexibility

The Digital Chamber responded with its own principles, signaling willingness to restrict traditional interest-like payments while insisting that **certain incentive mechanisms must remain permissible**.

The group also supports a two-year study on stablecoins’ impact on deposits — provided it does not trigger automatic regulatory bans.

## Political backdrop

The debate unfolds within the framework of the **GENIUS Act (Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins)**, which seeks to define the regulatory environment for stablecoins in the U.S.

Stablecoins have evolved from technical instruments into a strategic battleground over the future of the digital dollar.
