# ARK rotates out of Coinbase and into Bullish

**ARK Invest**, led by well-known crypto bull **Cathie Wood**, made an unusual move during the latest crypto market selloff: it sold **Coinbase (COIN)** shares worth approximately **$17.4 million** and reallocated nearly the same amount into **Bullish (BLSH)**.

According to trade disclosures, ARK offloaded **119,236 Coinbase shares**, just one day after making a small additional purchase. This marked **ARK’s first Coinbase sale of 2026** and its first since August 2025.

## Why this move stands out

ARK is widely known for **buying crypto-related equities during market drawdowns**, not selling them. Reducing exposure to Coinbase in a pronounced risk-off environment therefore represents a **rare change in behavior**, especially given that Coinbase has long been a core holding across ARK’s crypto-focused funds.

Coinbase shares fell roughly **13% in a single session** and are down about **37% year-to-date**, prompting what appears to be a reassessment of near-term risk exposure.

## Bullish becomes the new bet

The capital freed up from the Coinbase sale was almost entirely redirected into **Bullish**, an institutionally focused crypto trading platform that listed on the **New York Stock Exchange in August 2025**.

ARK purchased approximately **716,000 Bullish shares**, valued at around **$17.8 million**. Since its public listing, Bullish shares have declined by more than **60%**, placing this trade firmly in **contrarian territory**.

Notably, ARK was also among the **largest investors in Bullish’s IPO**, alongside **BlackRock**, suggesting a longer-term conviction in the platform’s institutional trading model.

## Broader context: Bitcoin pressure and crypto equity stress

This portfolio shift comes as **Bitcoin fell below $70,000**, briefly touching the **$60,000 level**, intensifying pressure across crypto-linked equities and ETFs.

While many investors are reducing risk exposure, ARK appears to be **rebalancing rather than exiting the sector**, trimming exposure to a highly visible and regulation-sensitive exchange like Coinbase while increasing exposure to Bullish’s institutional infrastructure.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor)

- Selling Coinbase during a drawdown is an unusual move for ARK  
- Buying Bullish points to a long-term bet on institutional crypto infrastructure  
- The shift looks like portfolio rebalancing, not a loss of faith in crypto  
- Focus appears to be moving from retail exposure to institutional platforms
