# ZachXBT, Axiom and Polymarket: How an investigation generated $400,000 in profit

The crypto industry has once again demonstrated how valuable information – or even speculation about information – can be. While the well-known on-chain investigator ZachXBT was preparing his announcement regarding alleged insider trading within Axiom Exchange, a parallel story was unfolding: tens of millions of dollars were flowing into the prediction platform Polymarket.

At the moment ZachXBT announced that an Axiom employee, Broox Bauer, and others had allegedly been involved in insider trading activities since early 2025, the betting market already had its winners. One Polymarket user earned approximately $400,000 by correctly predicting the outcome of the investigation.

## An investigation that shook trust

According to statements ZachXBT published on the social media platform X, an employee of Axiom Exchange allegedly used internal tools to access sensitive user data. These tools reportedly allow deeper insight into wallet activity, potential identity linkage, and transaction tracking before the broader public understands what is happening.

If the allegations prove accurate, such conduct would represent a classic case of insider trading – the use of privileged information for personal gain. In traditional financial markets, such activities are subject to strict regulatory penalties. In the crypto space, where the boundaries between decentralized and centralized structures are often blurred, the situation is more complex.

ZachXBT also shared audio recordings in which a person he identifies as Bauer discusses the ability to track users. This added further weight to the accusations and triggered strong reactions within the community.

Axiom Exchange responded by stating that it was “shocked and disappointed,” that access to the relevant tools had been removed, and that an internal investigation was underway. The company emphasized that such behavior “does not represent the team” and that user protection remains its priority.

## $40 million before the reveal

While the public debated ethical and legal implications, Polymarket recorded a surge in activity. Users were placing bets on which company ZachXBT would expose. The total betting volume reached nearly $40 million.

Part of the market believed the target would be Meteora, a decentralized liquidity platform. Others positioned themselves in favor of Axiom. At the time of the announcement, those who had taken the correct position secured substantial gains.

According to available data, one user collected around $400,000.

It is important to note that it has not been confirmed who controls the wallet associated with that profit. In crypto markets, a single address may represent an individual trader, a coordinated group, or a structured entity. Public on-chain visibility does not automatically reveal the underlying identity.

## Meta-speculation: betting on exposure

This case raises a new question: have prediction markets become instruments for trading informational expectations? While some allegedly used internal exchange data, others profited by predicting whether such practices would be publicly exposed.

The market therefore operated on three levels:

1. Potential misuse of information within the platform  
2. Reaction of investors and the community  
3. Betting on the outcome of the investigation  

Such multi-layered dynamics further complicate the regulatory landscape. US lawmakers are already closely monitoring prediction markets, and events like this could intensify scrutiny.

## Trust as the industry’s foundation

The crypto industry frequently highlights blockchain transparency. Yet this episode serves as a reminder that on-chain transparency does not necessarily translate into transparency within internal systems. The greatest risk is not always in the code – but in access to data.

If insider allegations are confirmed, the reputational damage for Axiom could be significant. At the same time, Polymarket demonstrated how markets monetize uncertainty itself.

Information has value. The expectation of information can be even more valuable.

## Broader implications

This episode shows that crypto markets are maturing – but also becoming more sophisticated. Investigations, disclosures and regulatory debates are no longer isolated events; they are part of an ecosystem where capital rapidly moves toward whatever carries the strongest narrative momentum.

ZachXBT once again reinforced his reputation as an investigator who uncovers irregularities. Simultaneously, the market demonstrated that it does not wait for investigations to conclude – it already trades the possible outcome.

In such an environment, the speed of information becomes just as important as its accuracy.
