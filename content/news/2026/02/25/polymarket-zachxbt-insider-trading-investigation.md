# Polymarket Bets: Who Will Be Named in ZachXBT’s Insider Trading Investigation?

The crypto industry is currently focused not only on price action but also on an upcoming announcement from on-chain investigator ZachXBT. More than $7 million has been wagered on Polymarket regarding which company will be named in what he described as a major insider trading investigation.

ZachXBT, known for uncovering hacks and scams, stated that his investigation targets one of crypto’s “most profitable businesses.” Allegations suggest that internal data may have been abused over an extended period to trade digital assets before public disclosure.

As of now, no company has been officially named.

## Why Polymarket?

Polymarket is a decentralized prediction market platform where users trade event-based contracts. Prices reflect collective expectations rather than confirmed facts.

Participants can choose between several potential targets, including:

- Meteora  
- MEXC  
- Axiom  
- Jupiter  
- Pump.fun  
- Other  

The odds shift based on trading activity and sentiment. Importantly, they do not represent evidence, but aggregated belief.

## Speculation Around Meteora

At the time of writing, Meteora appears among the leading options in market sentiment. However, this reflects user expectations, not verified information. On-chain patterns, prior controversies, or community speculation may influence these bets.

The inclusion of centralized exchanges such as MEXC suggests that market participants are not limiting their assumptions to a specific business model. Insider trading concerns can arise wherever information asymmetry exists.

## Prediction Markets as Sentiment Indicators

Prediction markets act as psychological mirrors. A single well-informed trader can influence odds, but so can a large group reacting to rumors.

In crypto’s fast-moving ecosystem, such dynamics can amplify volatility and reputational risk even before facts emerge.

## Regulatory Implications

This episode unfolds amid increasing regulatory scrutiny in the United States. The Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) has asserted jurisdiction over certain prediction markets, while several states have pursued legal actions against similar platforms.

If insider trading allegations were substantiated, regulatory consequences could extend beyond one company, potentially impacting broader industry oversight.
