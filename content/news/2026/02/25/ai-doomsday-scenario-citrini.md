# AI doomsday scenario: Is the ‘feedback loop without brakes’ a real threat?

Citrini Research’s Substack publication titled “The 2028 Global Intelligence Crisis” triggered noticeable turbulence in US markets. Although clearly labeled as a scenario rather than a forecast, the narrative was powerful enough to unsettle investors.

According to Guardian Tech, companies explicitly mentioned in the report — including Uber, Mastercard, American Express and DoorDash — fell between four and six percent. The broader S&P 500 index weakened, and the software sector touched multi-month lows.

## The “feedback loop without brakes”

The model assumes autonomous AI agents increasingly replace white-collar functions. Companies cut labor costs, unemployment rises, consumption declines.

Lower demand pressures revenues, leading to further layoffs — a self-reinforcing downward spiral.

The scenario even projects unemployment above 10% and stress in credit and mortgage markets.

## Why specific companies were highlighted

The inclusion of recognizable brands amplified the shock. Uber and DoorDash depend heavily on consumer spending. Mastercard and American Express rely on transaction volume.

If purchasing power contracts, their revenue streams weaken — reinforcing the domino logic.

## Real concerns behind the narrative

Automation pressure is real. Administrative work, basic programming and customer service are already shifting.

However, the scenario assumes limited regulatory or monetary intervention. In practice, central banks and governments possess stabilization tools.

## Transformation rather than collapse?

Historically, technological revolutions restructured economies rather than destroyed them.

AI accelerates change, but it operates within economic and political systems.

## Conclusion

Citrini Research’s scenario is a powerful thought experiment. The market reaction demonstrates how sensitive investors are to AI-driven narratives.

The key question is not whether AI will reshape the economy — but how quickly and under what governance.
