# Solana Sees Activity Surge With Over 5 Million Daily Active Addresses

The Solana blockchain experienced a strong surge in activity at the end of January, with **daily active addresses consistently exceeding 5 million**, according to data from Nansen. This represents a **115% increase** compared to previous months.

The growth stands out amid broader market volatility, where many participants have scaled back risk.

## What’s driving the surge?

The spike is fueled by a mix of **AI-driven tools**, **memecoin expansion**, and **Solana’s technical efficiency**.

A key catalyst was the use of *Claude Cowork*, an AI agent capable of controlling desktop actions. Developers leveraged it to rapidly launch new tokens — particularly memecoins — using Solana-based launchpads such as Bags.

## Fees jump — but remain competitive

On January 16, Solana recorded **$4.5 million in daily fees**, a sharp increase from late 2025, when daily fees often remained in the low thousands.

### How fees really compare: Solana vs. Ethereum

While we recently reported that **Ethereum fees dropped to just a few cents after the Fusaka upgrade**, context matters.

On Ethereum, low fees are primarily achieved **via Layer 2 networks** like Base, Arbitrum, and Optimism. The Ethereum main layer (L1) can still become expensive during peak usage.

Solana, by contrast, maintains **very low fees directly on its base layer (L1)**, where a typical transaction often costs **fractions of a cent**, without relying on additional scaling layers.

## Memecoins, speculation, and risk

A large share of activity stems from speculative memecoin trading, which brings:
- high volatility
- short project lifespans
- rapid liquidity shifts

High activity does not necessarily imply long-term sustainability.
