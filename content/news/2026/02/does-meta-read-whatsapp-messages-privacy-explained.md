# US authorities investigate claims Meta can read encrypted WhatsApp messages

US authorities are reportedly investigating claims that **Meta can access WhatsApp messages that are officially described as end-to-end encrypted**.

The allegations stem from a lawsuit filed last week, based on testimony from **whistleblowers in Australia, Brazil, India, Mexico and South Africa**.

## Pegasus, NSO Group and legal context

The case is closely linked to Israel-based **NSO Group**, the developer of **Pegasus spyware**, previously used to target journalists and activists.

Meta strongly denies the allegations, calling them false and misleading.

## Why this matters

If proven, the claims would:
- undermine trust in encrypted messaging
- raise major privacy concerns
- trigger global regulatory consequences

- This is an investigation, not a verdict
- Multiple whistleblower sources add weight
- Outcome could reshape expectations around private messaging
