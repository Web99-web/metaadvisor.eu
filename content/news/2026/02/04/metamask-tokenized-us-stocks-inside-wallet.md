# MetaMask moves stock trading on-chain

MetaMask, one of the most widely used self-custody crypto wallets, is expanding beyond digital assets by adding access to tokenized US stocks, ETFs and commodities through an integration with Ondo Finance.

Users in supported jurisdictions can now gain exposure to tokenized shares of companies such as Apple, Tesla, Nvidia and Microsoft — directly inside their wallet and without opening a traditional brokerage account.

## What tokenized stocks mean

Tokenization allows real-world financial assets to be represented as blockchain-based tokens. These tokens track the value of the underlying asset while benefiting from on-chain settlement, transparency and programmability.

This model is increasingly viewed as a bridge between traditional finance and decentralized markets.

## One wallet, multiple asset classes

According to Consensys, the company behind MetaMask, the goal is to replace fragmented financial infrastructure with a single self-custodial wallet that gives users full control over crypto, tokenized equities and commodities.

No intermediaries, no trading-hour restrictions, and no surrendering of asset custody.

## RWA adoption accelerates

Real-world asset tokenization has grown into a market exceeding $22 billion, becoming one of the strongest narratives of the current crypto cycle.

MetaMask’s move signals a future where wallets evolve into full financial hubs connecting blockchain and capital markets.
