# Five European “unicorn” startups in 2026

Despite a cautious global investment environment, Europe has already delivered **five new startup unicorns** in the first month of 2026 — technology companies with **valuations exceeding $1 billion**. This signals that venture capital is still flowing toward businesses with real products, growth traction and global relevance.

## What does “startup unicorn” mean?

A **“unicorn”** is a privately held startup valued at **over $1 billion**. The term originally reflected how rare such companies once were. Even today, unicorns remain an elite segment of the startup ecosystem.

Importantly, valuation does **not equal profitability**. It reflects investor expectations around market size, technology and long-term growth potential.

## Why are some of these companies considered European despite global operations?

Many European startups:
- incorporate in the **United States (e.g., Delaware)** for fundraising efficiency  
- while keeping **founders, engineering and product development in Europe**

As a result, ecosystem analyses focus more on **origin and technological roots** than on legal headquarters alone.

## The five new European unicorns

### Aikido Security (Belgium) – Cybersecurity  
Aikido Security builds a platform that integrates security across the entire software development lifecycle. The product is widely adopted globally and shows strong revenue and customer growth.

### Cast AI (Lithuania / US) – Cloud and AI optimization  
Cast AI helps companies optimize cloud and AI workloads, reducing infrastructure costs. Its engineering roots are firmly European, particularly in Lithuania.

### Harmattan AI (France) – Defense and security AI  
Harmattan AI develops artificial intelligence systems for defense and security applications, reflecting Europe’s growing focus on technological sovereignty.

### Osapiens (Germany) – ESG and compliance software  
Osapiens provides software to help enterprises meet ESG and regulatory requirements. As regulation tightens, such tools are becoming critical corporate infrastructure.

### Preply (Ukraine / EU) – Digital language education  
Preply is a global language-learning platform with strong European roots, using AI-driven personalization to scale education worldwide.

## What does this tell us?

These five unicorns highlight Europe’s strength in **cybersecurity, AI, infrastructure, defense, ESG and education** — areas driven by long-term demand rather than short-term hype.

## Our take (Metaadvisor):
- Europe continues to produce globally relevant tech companies.  
- Unicorn status signals investor confidence, not guaranteed success.  
- Regulation is increasingly becoming a competitive advantage.  
- AI and security remain dominant investment themes in 2026.
