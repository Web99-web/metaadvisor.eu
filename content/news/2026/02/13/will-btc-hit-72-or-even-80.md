# Will BTC Hit $72,000 — or Even $80,000?

<small>Image is symbolic.</small>

Bitcoin is once again at a pivotal moment. Following the latest US inflation data, BTC briefly surged above $69,000, reigniting hopes of a short-term rally. Traders are now watching the $72,000 resistance level, with $80,000 seen as a potential upside target if momentum accelerates.

## CPI, inflation and market reaction

The latest CPI (Consumer Price Index) data signaled easing inflationary pressure. Lower inflation expectations often support risk assets such as cryptocurrencies.

However, Bitcoin must firmly break the $68,000–$70,000 resistance range before a move toward $72,000 becomes technically convincing.

## MVRV indicator: undervaluation signals?

The MVRV (Market Value to Realized Value) ratio has returned to levels not seen since 2023.

Values near 1 historically indicate fair value or potential undervaluation. Some analysts interpret the current reading as a possible sign that the downtrend is losing strength.

## Technical outlook

A potential V-shaped recovery pattern is forming. Key levels to watch include:

- $72,000 as the first breakout target,
- $76,000 if momentum continues,
- $80,000 as a psychological barrier,
- $85,000 in the event of a broader trend reversal.

Failure to hold above $65,000 could increase the risk of deeper downside.
