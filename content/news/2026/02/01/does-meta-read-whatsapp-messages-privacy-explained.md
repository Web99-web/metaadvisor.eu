# Can Meta Read WhatsApp Messages?

US authorities are currently investigating serious allegations that Meta may be able to access private WhatsApp messages, despite the platform’s official claims that all communications are end-to-end encrypted.

The allegations stem from a lawsuit based on testimony from whistleblowers in multiple countries and focus on the technical implementation of encryption, particularly in relation to optional AI features.

## Whistleblowers from multiple countries

The lawsuit was filed by the law firm **Quinn Emanuel Urquhart & Sullivan** and is said to rely on information provided by whistleblowers from:
- Australia  
- Brazil  
- India  
- Mexico  
- South Africa  

According to the claims, Meta could under certain conditions access WhatsApp communications.

## Connection to NSO Group and Pegasus spyware

Meta argues that the lawsuit is part of a broader legal strategy to defend the Israeli company **NSO Group**, known for developing the spyware **Pegasus**, which has been used to surveil journalists, activists and politicians.

WhatsApp previously won a lawsuit against NSO Group, in which a US court ordered the company to pay **$167 million in damages** for the unlawful surveillance of more than 1,400 users.

Notably, the same law firm, Quinn Emanuel, **simultaneously represents NSO Group in an appeal**, which Meta has pointed to as a potential conflict of interest.

## What does Meta officially say?

Meta insists that:
- WhatsApp messages remain end-to-end encrypted  
- the company does not have routine access to message content  
- the allegations are intended to create legal and media pressure  

However, the fact that an **official investigation is underway** gives weight to the claims.

- This is one of the most sensitive technology–legal cases of recent years
- It is not a proven abuse, but an investigation involving serious allegations
- End-to-end encryption remains the standard, but trust has been shaken
- The outcome could have global regulatory consequences
