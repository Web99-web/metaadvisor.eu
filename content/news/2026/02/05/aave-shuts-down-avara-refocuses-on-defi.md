# Aave Returns to Its DeFi Roots

One of the most established DeFi protocols, **Aave**, has announced a clear strategic shift: the **Avara** umbrella brand is being discontinued, and the **Family crypto wallet** is being gradually phased out. The move marks a renewed focus on **decentralized finance (DeFi)** and core infrastructure products.

According to founder and CEO **Stani Kulechov**, Avara was a transitional structure that no longer fits Aave’s long-term vision. Simplification and clarity are now the priority.

## What’s Being Phased Out — and What Remains

Avara previously grouped multiple initiatives, including Family Wallet and earlier the **Lens protocol**. Under the new structure:

- All products operate under **Aave Labs**
- **Aave App, Aave Pro and Aave Kit** remain core offerings
- The iOS-based Family Wallet is being sunset gradually

No new users will be onboarded after **April 1, 2026**, while existing users can continue using the app until **April 1, 2027**. Accounts and infrastructure will remain supported within the Aave ecosystem.

## Why Aave Is Moving Away From Generic Wallets

Aave says the Family Wallet experience highlighted a key lesson: **mass adoption does not come from generic, open-ended wallets**, but from **purpose-built financial products**.

Users are looking for:
- savings and yield  
- borrowing and liquidity access  
- transparent risk and rates  

These are the areas where Aave has built long-standing credibility.

## Back to DeFi Fundamentals

The move follows Aave’s reduced operational role in **Lens**, freeing up resources to concentrate on DeFi. The project is positioning itself as **mature financial infrastructure**, rather than a broad experimental Web3 platform.

## What This Means for Users and the Market

For users, the transition will be gradual and predictable. For the broader market, it’s another sign that DeFi is entering a phase of **optimization, consolidation and professionalization**.

Aave is returning to what made it relevant in the first place.
