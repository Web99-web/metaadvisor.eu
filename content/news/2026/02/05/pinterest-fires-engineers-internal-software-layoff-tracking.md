# Pinterest Fires Engineers Over Internal Software Used to Track Layoffs

Pinterest has fired two engineers after they created internal software designed to identify which employees had been laid off during the company’s latest round of job cuts, according to a report by **The Guardian**.

Earlier this month, Pinterest announced plans to reduce its workforce by approximately **15%**, affecting around **700 employees** globally. At the same time, the company said it was doubling down on an **AI-focused strategy**, emphasizing automation and personalized content.

## What happened?

According to Pinterest, the two engineers wrote **custom scripts** that improperly accessed internal company systems. These scripts allowed them to detect **which employee accounts were being deactivated**, a strong indicator that those individuals had been terminated.

Pinterest stated that this activity constituted a **clear violation of internal policies and employee privacy**, leading to the engineers’ dismissal. The company did not disclose the identities of the individuals involved and did not specify whether the information was shared beyond a limited internal audience.

At this time, there is **no public evidence** that the data was misused or distributed externally.

## Technology, fear and uncertainty

In large technology companies, internal systems often make it technically possible to observe changes such as account removals or access restrictions. However, such systems **cannot reliably predict future layoffs**, as accounts are typically disabled **only after termination decisions have already been made**.

This has led some observers to suggest that the engineers’ actions may have been driven by **curiosity or self-protection** — an attempt to understand their own job security during a period of uncertainty. Since Pinterest has not provided details about how the information was shared or used, such interpretations remain **speculative**.

## AI strategy and market pressure

Pinterest has framed the layoffs as part of a broader shift toward an **“AI-first” operating model**, aimed at improving content personalization and advertising efficiency.

The restructuring comes amid financial pressure. Pinterest shares have fallen **more than 20% year-over-year**, as investors increasingly demand cost control and higher margins across the tech sector.

- The case highlights the **growing anxiety among tech workers** during large-scale layoffs.
- Technology is increasingly used as a tool for **self-assessment and risk awareness**, but it also creates new ethical boundaries.
- In times of workforce reductions, the line between **legitimate curiosity and policy violations** becomes blurred.
- Without transparent details on intent and data usage, definitive judgments are difficult.
