## Continued downside pressure after the correction

The crypto market continues its downward trend on Thursday, with investor sentiment remaining highly cautious. **Bitcoin (BTC)** is currently trading around **$63,000**, after touching an intraday low of **$62,235**. Despite the move back above this key psychological level, volatility remains elevated.

A similar pattern is visible among major altcoins. **Solana (SOL)** rebounded toward **$80** after hitting a low of **$77.6**, while **Ethereum (ETH)** recovered to around **$1,884**, following a dip to **$1,823**. These moves suggest the market is still searching for equilibrium after the recent correction.

## Crypto Metals also under pressure — HYPE stands out

The sell-off was not limited to traditional cryptocurrencies. **Crypto Metals**, including **gold** and **silver**, also came under significant pressure. **Gold fell from around $4,885 to approximately $4,735**, marking a **drop of more than $150**, while **silver declined from about $77 to near $71**.

This indicates that **selling pressure spread across the broader market**, including assets often viewed as safer havens during periods of instability.

### HYPE as a rare exception amid the sell-off

Against this backdrop, the **HYPE token stands out as a rare exception**. Instead of a sharp decline, the price briefly dipped toward **$31** before **returning to around $33**, a level it has hovered around for several days. Unlike **Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and most Crypto Metals**, HYPE currently **shows no signs of panic selling**.

This **divergence** suggests that the current move is **not a simple “risk-off” flight from all assets**, but rather a **selective market reaction**, driven by liquidity, narrative, and structural factors.

## Why the market still looks fragile

Although short-term stabilization may appear encouraging, analysts caution that this does not yet signal a clear trend reversal. Breaks below previous support levels indicate the market remains sensitive to macroeconomic developments, regulatory signals, and shifts in liquidity.

Notably, the recent declines occurred relatively quickly, without the kind of volume that would suggest full capitulation. In such conditions, brief rebounds often serve as opportunities for position unwinding rather than the start of a new uptrend.

## Scenarios: $58,000… or even $50,000?

Previous analyses have repeatedly highlighted that **Bitcoin may not yet have reached the bottom of this correction**. Key levels frequently cited include **$58,000**, and in a more pessimistic scenario, **$50,000**.

These scenarios are based on technical indicators as well as historical market behavior following strong rally phases. If Bitcoin were to lose the $60,000 level again, the psychological impact could be significant, potentially dragging Ethereum, Solana, and the broader altcoin market lower.

## What this means for investors

For long-term investors, periods like this are often viewed as accumulation phases, while short-term traders must contend with heightened risk and rapid directional shifts. The market currently sits between residual optimism from the prior rally and growing concern that the correction is not yet complete.

In this environment, Bitcoin’s behavior around key support levels — and upcoming macroeconomic or regulatory developments — will be critical.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor):

- Short-term stabilization does not necessarily mark the end of the correction.
- The $58,000 area remains a key technical zone for Bitcoin.
- Altcoins continue to follow Bitcoin without clear independent strength.
- Elevated volatility is likely to persist in the near term.
- Risk management is currently more important than chasing quick gains.
