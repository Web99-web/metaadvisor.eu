# JP Morgan and Malaysia’s Central Bank Signal Institutional Commitment to Crypto

While retail traders react to short-term volatility, major financial institutions are positioning for the long game. JP Morgan has taken a constructive stance on crypto markets for 2026, while Bank Negara Malaysia is piloting stablecoin and tokenization initiatives under regulatory supervision.

## JP Morgan: Institutional Flows May Drive the Next Recovery

Despite recent price corrections, JP Morgan analysts expect institutional inflows to underpin the next leg higher in digital assets. Bitcoin’s estimated production cost historically acts as a soft floor, often leading to miner capitulation and eventual market stabilization.

The bank also notes that Bitcoin’s relative attractiveness versus gold is improving amid rising volatility and macro uncertainty.

## Malaysia’s Central Bank Tests Stablecoins and Tokenized Deposits

Bank Negara Malaysia has launched sandbox pilots focused on:

- Ringgit-backed stablecoins  
- Tokenized bank deposits  
- Real-world asset (RWA) tokenization  

These initiatives may also inform the potential development of a wholesale CBDC (Central Bank Digital Currency).

## A 15-Year Institutional Strategy

At Consensus Hong Kong, venture capital leaders emphasized that crypto is not a short cycle but a multi-decade infrastructure shift. Stablecoins, tokenization and institutional trading rails remain core themes.
