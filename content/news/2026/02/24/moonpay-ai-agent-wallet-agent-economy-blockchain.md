# MoonPay Launches AI Agent Wallet: Building the Agent Economy

MoonPay has unveiled “MoonPay Agents,” a new infrastructure layer that allows artificial intelligence systems to directly access blockchain financial networks. For the first time, AI agents can create wallets, hold stablecoins, and execute on-chain transactions without human intermediaries once funded.

This marks a structural step toward what many call the “agent economy.”

## From reasoning to economic action

As CEO Ivan Soto-Wright stated, AI systems can reason, but without capital infrastructure they cannot act economically. MoonPay aims to bridge that gap.

With programmable, non-custodial wallets, AI agents can:

- execute payments  
- trade digital assets  
- interact with DeFi protocols  
- manage capital flows autonomously  

Metaadvisor observes that this evolution transforms AI from an analytical tool into an economic participant.

## Institutional interest grows

According to Bloomberg, Intercontinental Exchange (parent company of the NYSE) has reportedly held early-stage discussions about a potential investment in MoonPay, which is said to be seeking funding at a $5 billion valuation.

Metaadvisor.eu notes that this level of institutional attention signals that AI-driven wallet infrastructure is being taken seriously beyond crypto-native circles.
