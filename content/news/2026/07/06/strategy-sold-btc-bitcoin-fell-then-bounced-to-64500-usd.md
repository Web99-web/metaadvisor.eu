# Strategy sold BTC, Bitcoin fell then bounced to $64,500

**Bitcoin briefly fell toward $61,000 after news that Strategy had sold part of its BTC holdings. The price then quickly recovered toward $64,500. This shows that the market reacted nervously, but the short-term sentiment has not been fully broken so far.**

According to Cointelegraph, Bitcoin saw sharp volatility on Monday after the market reacted to news that Strategy had sold **3,588 BTC** through July 5. The sale was reportedly linked to funding preferred stock dividend payments and replenishing cash reserves.

After the news, BTC fell toward the **$61,000** area, representing a daily loss of more than 4 percent. After the initial reaction, however, the price recovered and moved back toward the **$64,000 to $64,500** range.

## Strategy is no longer only a Bitcoin buyer

Strategy, formerly known as MicroStrategy, has for years been a symbol of corporate Bitcoin accumulation. That is why any larger BTC sale by the company has a strong psychological effect on the market.

CoinDesk writes that Strategy also reported a large unrealized loss on its Bitcoin holdings in the second quarter, after the BTC price fell from around $68,000 at the beginning of April toward roughly $60,000 at the end of June.

It is important to distinguish between two terms. An unrealized loss means that the value of an asset has fallen on paper, without the asset necessarily being sold. A realized loss occurs when an asset is actually sold below its book value.

In this case, the market did not react only to the number of BTC sold, but also to a change in perception. If a company that was for years the best-known institutional Bitcoin buyer now sells part of its holdings, investors immediately start asking whether the broader strategy has changed.

## Short-term shock, but not a full breakdown

Bitcoin’s recovery toward $64,500 shows that the market has not remained in panic so far. Strategy’s sale was a trigger for the drop, but it does not necessarily mean the start of a new major breakdown.

Some analysts argue that weaker signals were already present in the market before the news itself. That means the sale did not create the pressure on its own, but amplified existing nervousness.

Situations like this are common in crypto. When the market is already sensitive, one major headline can trigger a fast drop. But if buyers respond quickly, the price can recover just as fast toward previous levels.

## What to watch now

The most important thing now is whether Bitcoin can hold above the $64,000 area. If that zone is confirmed as short-term support, the market may conclude that the news about Strategy’s sale has already been absorbed.

If BTC falls back toward $61,000, the sale could remain an important psychological pressure, especially because Strategy has very large Bitcoin exposure.

For now, the clearest description of the situation is this: Strategy sold BTC, the market briefly reacted with a drop, but Bitcoin recovered quickly.

## Our view

- Strategy sold **3,588 BTC**, which triggered short-term nervousness in the market.
- Bitcoin fell toward **$61,000** after the news, but then recovered toward **$64,500**.
- The rebound shows that the market has not fully lost confidence so far.
- Still, Strategy is no longer only a symbol of buying Bitcoin, but now also of selling part of its holdings.
- The key level to watch is whether BTC can hold the area around **$64,000**.
- Conclusion: this is not a clearly bearish signal, but it is a warning that the market is watching every move by large corporate Bitcoin holders very closely.
