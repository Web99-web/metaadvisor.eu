# Did you know `.me` is actually a country’s domain?

Many people assume that `.me` is an ordinary international domain created because of the English word “me”. However, it is actually the official national internet domain of Montenegro.

This fact may have come into focus today after Telegram’s `t.me` domain was unavailable for a period of time. Telegram is not a Montenegrin company, nor are WhatsApp, Facebook, WordPress, Proton Mail or PayPal. Nevertheless, all of them use or have used short internet addresses ending in `.me`.

Montenegro has therefore transformed its international country code into one of the region’s most recognisable digital export products.

## Why does Montenegro have the `.me` domain?

National internet domains generally follow the two-letter country codes defined by the international ISO 3166-1 standard. After the State Union of Serbia and Montenegro ceased to exist, Montenegro received the international code `ME` in 2006, derived from the name Montenegro.

Based on that code, Montenegro was able to receive its own national internet domain. The `.me` domain was delegated in 2007, while global registration began in 2008. Before that, Serbia and Montenegro used the former Yugoslav domain `.yu`. After the creation of two separate states, Serbia received `.rs` and Montenegro received `.me`.

## How did a country code become a global product?

Montenegro quickly recognised that the code `ME` had a market value extending far beyond its original role as a national domain. The English word “me” makes the extension ideal for personal websites, portfolios, marketing campaigns, calls to action and short internet addresses.

Instead of restricting registration to residents, institutions and companies from Montenegro, the country opened `.me` to users and businesses around the world. In practice, the national domain began functioning almost like an international generic domain.

Today, there are more than one million registered `.me` domains used by individuals and companies in more than 200 countries and territories. The number of registered domains is therefore considerably higher than Montenegro’s own population.

## Why do Telegram, WhatsApp and PayPal use it?

Short domains are extremely valuable because they are easy to remember, simple to share and look good in messages, advertisements and social media posts.

Telegram uses `t.me` for links to channels, groups, profiles and bots. WhatsApp uses `wa.me` to open conversations directly, Messenger uses `m.me`, Facebook uses `fb.me`, WordPress uses `wp.me`, and Proton Mail offers its users short addresses ending in `pm.me`.

PayPal built an entire system of personal links for sending and receiving money around the PayPal.Me name.

This does not mean that these companies are Montenegrin or that Montenegro controls their applications. They use individual internet addresses registered within Montenegro’s national domain zone. The functionality of their `.me` addresses therefore depends on the proper operation of the registry managing the entire `.me` zone, just as every domain depends on the registry responsible for its particular extension.

## What does Montenegro gain from it?

Every owner of a `.me` domain pays for the initial registration and subsequent renewals through an authorised registrar. The revenue is divided between the retail registrar, the registry operator, technical partners and the state according to the agreed model.

Montenegro therefore does not receive the full amount that a customer pays to GoDaddy or another registrar. Nevertheless, the large number of registrations and regular annual renewals have generated substantial overall revenue.

In 2015, the Government of Montenegro said that the `.me` project had brought the country more than €15 million during its first several years. An official statement published in 2016 said that the total financial benefit for Montenegro had **exceeded €20 million**. A precise and more recent cumulative figure has not been published with the same clarity. Since domain registrations and renewals continue, the project’s total value is probably significantly higher today, but an exact amount cannot be stated without newer official data.

## Who actually manages the `.me` domain?

The Government of Montenegro is listed as the manager of the national domain in IANA’s official records. The day-to-day registry and technical operations are handled by the authorised `.ME` Registry, operated by the company doMEn in cooperation with international technical partners and a network of registrars.

Users do not register domains directly with the Government of Montenegro. Instead, they use a retail registrar. The registrar collects the registration fee, provides the customer interface and manages the relationship with the buyer, while the central registry maintains the official database of all domains within the `.me` zone.

The owner of an individual domain controls its content, servers and applications. The registry, however, maintains the central record that allows the domain to appear in the DNS and remain accessible to users around the world.

## What did today’s Telegram incident reveal?

Only after the problem Telegram experienced with its `t.me` domain this morning did many people discover for the first time that `.me` is actually the identifier of Montenegro’s national domain zone.

When `t.me` temporarily received the `serverHold` status, links to Telegram channels, groups, profiles and bots stopped opening normally, even though the Telegram app itself did not completely stop working.

The incident showed that `t.me` is not merely a creative Telegram abbreviation. It is an address registered within Montenegro’s national domain, whose operator maintains the record required for the address to function correctly within the global DNS.

Montenegro does not read Telegram messages, manage its channels or control the app’s content. Nevertheless, as with every national domain, the registry operator has an important technical role in maintaining domain records.

## Our view

- `.me` is not a generic domain created because of the English word “me”. It is Montenegro’s official national domain, which became globally popular precisely because of the meaning of those two letters.
- Montenegro recognised the commercial value of its international country code early and opened registrations to users around the world.
- Telegram, WhatsApp, Facebook, PayPal and other companies are not Montenegrin, but their `.me` addresses operate within Montenegro’s national domain zone.
- More than one million registered domains show that `.me` has developed from a country code into a valuable global digital product.
- Montenegro had officially reported more than €20 million in financial benefits by 2016, but a precise and more recent cumulative amount has not been publicly confirmed.
- The Telegram incident revealed to many people for the first time how dependent global platforms are on domain registries and the largely invisible DNS infrastructure.

**Disclaimer:** This article is based on publicly available information from IANA, the `.ME` Registry and the Government of Montenegro at the time of publication.
