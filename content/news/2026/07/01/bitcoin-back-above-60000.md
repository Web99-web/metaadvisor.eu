# Bitcoin Back Above $60,000

**After several weaker days, the crypto market looks livelier today. Bitcoin moved back above the $60,000 level, Ethereum is also in the green, while Solana and Cardano posted stronger daily gains.**

Still, this looks more like a **relief bounce** than confirmation of a new major rally. Bitcoin is still trading around the psychologically important $60,000 level, after the market faced pressure in recent days from weaker sentiment, ETF outflows and macro uncertainty.

According to current market data, Bitcoin traded today in a range of roughly **$57,891 to $60,377**, while Ethereum moved between approximately **$1,557 and $1,626**. Solana jumped above **$77**, and Cardano was among the stronger daily gainers.

That shows buyers are trying to defend key levels, but the market has not yet clearly moved out of the caution zone.

## Solana and Cardano stronger than Bitcoin

The most interesting part of today’s move is not only that Bitcoin is back above $60,000. It is also that some altcoins moved more strongly than Bitcoin itself.

Solana was among the stronger large-cap gainers today, rising by around six percent, while Cardano posted an even stronger daily move. Ethereum also gained, but more moderately than those altcoins.

This kind of movement often shows that the market is testing riskier positions again. When Bitcoin first stabilizes sentiment, part of the capital often tries to move into altcoins that can react more sharply to a short-term recovery.

But that is still not the same as a new bull market.

In other words, investors are coming back — but carefully.

## The other side: Citi cuts Bitcoin and Ether forecasts

The other side of the story is more negative. Reuters reports that **Citi cut its 12-month forecasts for Bitcoin and Ether** due to weaker investor interest, negative ETF flows and slow progress in U.S. crypto regulation.

According to Reuters, Citi lowered its Bitcoin target from **$112,000 to $82,000**, while its Ether forecast was cut from **$3,175 to $2,240**.

That is an important signal because it shows that some major financial institutions are no longer looking at the crypto market with the same optimism as they did a few months ago.

The reference to ETF flows is especially important. If institutional money is leaving Bitcoin and Ether ETFs, the market loses one of the key growth drivers that previously helped keep prices elevated.

## What is really happening today?

Today’s rise should therefore be read carefully.

On one side, Bitcoin moved back above $60,000, Ethereum stabilized, and Solana and Cardano are showing stronger risk appetite. That is positive for short-term sentiment.

On the other side, the bigger picture is still not strongly bullish. ETF outflows, weaker investor interest and regulatory uncertainty in the United States continue to weigh on the market.

So the best description of today’s market is: **crypto is livelier, but it has not yet clearly changed direction**.

## Our view

* Bitcoin moved back above **$60,000** today.
* Ethereum is also in the green, but without a strong breakout.
* Solana and Cardano gained more strongly than BTC and ETH.
* Today’s move looks more like a short-term recovery than confirmation of a new bull market.
* Citi cut its 12-month forecasts for Bitcoin and Ether, showing that institutional sentiment remains cautious.
* The biggest pressures on the market are still ETF outflows, weaker investor interest and slow progress in U.S. crypto regulation.
* Bottom line: the market is livelier today, but it is not yet out of the risk zone.
