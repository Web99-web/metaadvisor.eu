**Zcash (ZEC) came under intense selling pressure on November 11, 2026, losing approximately 12.3% in 24 hours and more than 21% over seven days. The sharp decline coincided with substantial futures liquidations and falling open interest. Meanwhile, a warning displayed in the MEXC trading app indicated that the maximum position quantity for ZECUSDT futures had been adjusted to zero contracts. It remains unclear whether the restriction applies to all traders.**

## What triggered the sharp ZEC decline?

One of the most significant factors behind the market turbulence appears to be the unwinding of leveraged futures positions. According to available market data, approximately $33.3 million in ZEC futures positions were liquidated over a 24-hour period. Of that total, roughly $29.3 million involved LONG positions, while approximately $4 million involved SHORT positions. This means nearly 88% of recorded liquidations affected traders betting on higher ZEC prices.

When cryptocurrency prices decline rapidly, leveraged LONG positions can reach their liquidation thresholds. Exchanges may then automatically close those positions when the remaining margin becomes insufficient. These forced closures can create additional selling pressure, potentially triggering further liquidations and accelerating the decline. Such liquidation cascades help explain why cryptocurrency prices sometimes fall much faster than broader market conditions alone might suggest.

## ZEC futures volume dramatically exceeds spot trading

Another notable feature of the recent selloff is the unusually large amount of derivatives activity compared with direct cryptocurrency trading. During the observed period, ZEC futures trading volume reached approximately $9.5 billion, compared with around $689 million in spot trading volume. Futures activity was therefore almost fourteen times larger than spot trading.

At the same time, total futures open interest declined by approximately 11.4% over 24 hours. Falling prices combined with declining open interest are consistent with traders closing existing positions and reducing leverage. However, these figures do not establish precisely how much of the reduction resulted from voluntary exits rather than forced liquidations. They also do not prove that the market has reached a bottom.

## What does the MEXC futures warning mean?

A warning appeared in the MEXC app for the ZECUSDT perpetual futures contract, stating that the maximum permitted position quantity had been adjusted to **0 contracts**. The message also specified that existing positions were not affected by the adjustment.

Depending on the restrictions applied to an account, this could prevent traders from opening additional positions or increasing the size of positions they already hold. However, the warning does not mean that existing positions will necessarily be closed automatically. MEXC's publicly available trading information continues to list the ZECUSDT perpetual futures contract.

At the time of writing, there has been no confirmed public announcement establishing that a zero-contract position limit applies to every MEXC user. It is therefore important to distinguish an account-level warning from a platform-wide suspension of trading. The reason for the displayed restriction remains unconfirmed. Its potential impact on previously submitted but unfilled orders also requires clarification.

## Zcash NU7 upgrade preceded the selloff

The recent market downturn followed a significant technical upgrade to the Zcash blockchain. Network Upgrade 7, commonly known as NU7, was activated on November 6, 2026, at block height 3,543,405. Among the changes was a reduction in the targeted block interval from 75 seconds to approximately 25 seconds.

Although such improvements may strengthen the network's technical capabilities, they do not guarantee immediate increases in the market value of its cryptocurrency. Digital asset markets frequently experience a phenomenon known as *sell the news*, in which traders accumulate positions ahead of an anticipated positive event and take profits once the event occurs.

Profit-taking following the NU7 upgrade may have contributed to the selling pressure. However, no direct causal relationship between the upgrade and the sharp price decline has been established. There is also no confirmed evidence that a new Zcash network attack or technical outage caused the selloff.

## What comes next for Zcash?

Three indicators are particularly important for assessing whether the market is beginning to stabilize: the pace of futures liquidations, changes in open interest, and demand in the spot market. Sharp rebounds can follow major liquidation events as forced selling subsides and traders attempt to rebuild positions. However, a short-term recovery does not necessarily confirm that a broader downtrend has ended.

For traders using leverage, additional uncertainty comes from changing exchange risk limits and the possibility that new orders may be restricted during periods of heightened volatility. Monitoring liquidation thresholds, available margin, and exchange-specific trading conditions remains especially important.
