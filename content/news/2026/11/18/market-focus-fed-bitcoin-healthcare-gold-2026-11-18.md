**November 18, 2026 was about more than another Bitcoin selloff. Cryptocurrencies, precious metals, crude oil and parts of the U.S. stock market weakened at the same time. Newly released Federal Reserve minutes showed that almost all participants considered further interest rate increases likely to be appropriate under their baseline outlooks. Bitcoin fell toward $62,500 during the session, while heavy losses among U.S. health insurers added pressure to the Dow Jones Industrial Average.**

## The Fed keeps higher rates on the table

The Federal Reserve released minutes from its October 27–28 meeting, when policymakers held the federal funds target range at 3.75%–4.00%. Two voting members preferred a quarter-point increase. Looking ahead, almost all participants considered additional tightening likely to be appropriate under their baseline inflation and labor-market assumptions. **This is not a decision to raise rates in December.** The next meeting is scheduled for December 8–9, and policymakers stressed the role of incoming data.

For investors, the shift in rate expectations matters. Higher borrowing costs can undermine valuations for growth stocks, while higher yields make interest-bearing assets more attractive. The CME FedWatch Tool indicated a very high market-implied probability of a quarter-point December hike during the session. That estimate comes from futures pricing and is not a Fed commitment.

## Bitcoin trades near $62,500 as ETH slides

An intraday snapshot showed Bitcoin near $62,453, down about 3.7%, and Ethereum around $1,741, down roughly 4.5%. Gold, silver and oil were also lower. Such a cross-market move is consistent with broader risk reduction and a potential unwinding of leveraged positions, although shared price direction does not prove every asset had the same immediate catalyst. These quotes are time-specific snapshots, not official closing prices.

## Health insurers deepen pressure on the Dow

U.S. managed-care stocks faced a separate regulatory shock. The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services proposed broader auditing of Medicare Advantage plans, while investors weighed renewed political discussion of healthcare reform. Shares of UnitedHealth, Humana and CVS Health fell sharply during the day. These sector-specific pressures should not be mistaken for a direct consequence of Fed policy, even though they reinforced the market's weakness.

## NVIDIA's record earnings are not enough

NVIDIA reported record fiscal third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, a rise of 103% year over year. AI data center spending remains an extraordinary growth engine. Yet record corporate earnings cannot guarantee rising stock prices when financing conditions tighten and valuations already reflect ambitious expectations. The Fed minutes also discussed the financial-stability risks of disappointingly low returns on AI investment.

## Gold falls alongside risk assets

Gold fell below $3,800 an ounce and silver approached $45 during the session. Higher expected yields and a stronger dollar can weigh on non-yielding precious metals, while crude oil faced worries about demand and supply. Simultaneous declines may signal a demand for liquidity or broad portfolio de-risking, but they do not establish one cause for every price move.

## Our Perspective (Metaadvisor)

* **The Fed sets the tone.** December inflation and employment data are crucial, but no rate decision has been made.
* **Bitcoin is not moving alone.** Its decline coincided with weakness in several other markets.
* **Healthcare has a separate catalyst.** Regulatory uncertainty is central to the insurer selloff.
* **NVIDIA confirms AI demand.** Strong earnings do not remove valuation or financing risks.
* **Gold is not always a short-term haven.** Rising yields and dollar strength can overwhelm defensive demand.
