**Former Robinhood software engineer Huaisong Xiang has pleaded guilty to fraud after using confidential cryptocurrency listing information to trade perpetual futures on Hyperliquid. His illicit trading profit totaled $125,523. An FBI complaint identifies two substantial transfers into the wallet involved, but does not disclose the margin or leverage used for each trade.**

On November 16, 2026, the U.S. Department of Justice announced that Huaisong Xiang, also known as Jerry Xiang, had pleaded guilty to wire fraud. The 30-year-old former Robinhood employee repeatedly opened long positions before public announcements that particular crypto tokens would be listed on Robinhood Crypto.

## How much money entered the Hyperliquid wallet?

According to the FBI criminal complaint, Xiang transferred approximately **18 ETH, worth around $34,000 at the time**, from a cryptocurrency exchange account registered in his name to a Hyperliquid wallet on **March 12, 2025**.

The following day, a second person sent approximately **33,395 USDC** to that same wallet. Investigators linked the person to Xiang because the individual, believed to reside outside the United States, had named Xiang as a contact on a visa application. The public complaint does not establish that this person knew about or participated in the fraud.

Those two recorded transfers totaled approximately **$67,395**. However, this figure is **not a verified trading margin or position size**. The filings do not specify the leverage used for individual positions or account for every possible later deposit and withdrawal.

## POPCAT: A long position before the public announcement

On March 10, 2025, Xiang learned through a confidential internal channel that Robinhood was considering listing POPCAT on March 13. After transferring ETH to Hyperliquid, he received a further internal message confirming the planned listing time.

On March 13, Xiang opened **long positions in POPCAT perpetual futures** on Hyperliquid. He closed those positions at a profit once POPCAT became available for trading on Robinhood but before Robinhood made its public listing announcement.

According to the FBI, a cryptocurrency can become tradable on Robinhood as much as an hour before the platform publicly announces the listing. Availability on a major exchange can itself generate demand and upward price pressure.

## At least ten additional listing-related trades

Investigators identified at least ten further occasions between May 2025 and February 2026 when the same wallet traded perpetual futures ahead of public Robinhood listing announcements. The complaint specifically describes:

- **MEW and MOODENG:** Long positions opened in May 2025.
- **ONDO:** Long positions opened in August 2025.
- **RENDER:** Long positions opened in January 2026.

Xiang belonged to a restricted group of employees known as *Coin Aware Individuals*. Members had access to a private Slack channel containing confidential listing plans and dates. Robinhood's internal rules prohibited them from trading relevant assets before a listing announcement and for 24 hours afterward, including on other platforms.

## $125,523 in illicit profits

The Justice Department said Xiang made **$125,523 in illicit trading profits** and agreed to forfeit that amount. The figure represents his gain across a series of transactions, **not his initial deposit**.

Sentencing is scheduled for **February 25, 2027**. The wire fraud charge carries a statutory maximum of 20 years in prison, but the actual sentence has not yet been determined.

## Was Hyperliquid involved in the fraud?

**No published evidence establishes that Hyperliquid participated in Xiang's fraud.** The decentralized exchange was the venue where he placed trades. The confidential information came from Robinhood.

The case also demonstrates that decentralized trading is not necessarily anonymous. Public blockchain records, combined with information from cryptocurrency exchanges and other evidence, can help investigators connect wallet activity to an individual.

## Our Perspective (Metaadvisor)

- **Information advantage:** Knowing a listing date before the public can give a trader a substantial edge over the market.
- **Unknown leverage:** The $125,523 profit does not reveal how much margin Xiang used for any particular long position.
- **Second transfer:** The 33,395 USDC deposit is significant, but it does not prove the sender participated in any crime.
- **Blockchain transparency:** Decentralized trading leaves transaction records that investigators may be able to trace.
- **Accountability:** The case concerns misuse of Robinhood's confidential business information, not a hack of Hyperliquid.

**The central lesson is that Xiang did not need to predict which token Robinhood would list next. He had access to a material piece of information that other market participants did not possess.**
