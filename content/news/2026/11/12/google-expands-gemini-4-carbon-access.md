**Google expanded access to Gemini 4 Carbon on November 10, 2026, making its advanced AI model available to paying developers and businesses through the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and related enterprise offerings. The model is designed for demanding tasks including multi-step coding, complex analysis and scientific research.**

## What makes Carbon different?

Google positions Carbon as a model for problems requiring extensive reasoning and repeated verification. Gemini 4 Argon remains the company's recommended choice for many ordinary software engineering and knowledge tasks, while Carbon targets particularly difficult coding projects, debugging and open-ended research.

More computation can help with challenging tasks but can also increase latency and operating costs. The most powerful model is not necessarily the most economical choice for routine work.

## Coding, research and security

Google highlights Carbon's abilities in advanced programming, multi-step analysis and scientific research. Published evaluations also examine security-related tasks. Such capabilities may assist professionals who analyze software, but they raise concerns about misuse and the importance of safeguards.

Benchmark results describe performance under specific conditions. They do not guarantee reliable behavior in every real-world workflow.

## How much does Carbon cost?

Google's introductory API pricing through November 30, 2026, is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. After that, the announced prices rise to $4 and $20 respectively. Actual expenses depend on request size, response length and the number of model calls.

AI agents may make several calls to complete one complex assignment. Evaluating the total cost of a workflow is therefore more useful than looking at token prices alone.
