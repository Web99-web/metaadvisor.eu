**Jensen Huang, Nvidia's co-founder and longtime chief executive, resigned on November 12, 2026, following a controversy raising questions about corporate governance, possible foreign intelligence activity and the security of American technology. Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress has been appointed interim CEO. The announcement triggered a sharp market reaction and raised questions about the future leadership of one of the most important companies in the global artificial intelligence industry.**

## Resignation follows days of mounting pressure

The crisis began with reports about Huang's personal relationship with Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman and former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed. Media reports said U.S. authorities suspected Alice Huang might have links to Chinese intelligence activity. Questions also emerged about possible access to sensitive information and shipments of advanced Nvidia processors to China.

**Suspicions of espionage are not proof of espionage.** Huang publicly acknowledged marital infidelity and apologized to his family, employees and shareholders. Admitting to a personal relationship does not establish involvement in intelligence activity or the disclosure of trade secrets. Further reports about an alleged relationship with an Nvidia employee added to the pressure on the board. Directors faced the question of whether Huang could remain in charge during a security-related investigation.

## Nvidia launched an independent investigation

In an official statement on November 8, Nvidia confirmed an independent investigation into reports that its then-CEO might have been targeted by foreign intelligence. The company said it had no reason to believe that U.S. national security or Nvidia trade secrets had been compromised.

On November 10, independent directors announced that former acting U.S. Attorney Joon H. Kim and law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz had been retained for the investigation. **Huang's resignation does not mean the investigation has concluded or that every allegation has been substantiated.** The company must establish whether sensitive information, business relationships or technology-export decisions were exposed to improper influence.

## Who is interim CEO Colette Kress?

Colette Kress has led Nvidia's finances for more than a decade. She is familiar with the company's operations, investment strategy and relationships with major institutional investors. Her appointment is intended to provide continuity during the leadership transition.

However, financial management represented only one part of Huang's role. He shaped Nvidia's technology strategy, introduced new processor architectures and developed relationships with chip manufacturers, cloud providers and AI developers. Reuters reported that Harvey Jones will take the newly created role of Nvidia board chairman. The company plans to consider internal and external candidates for a permanent CEO.

## How did financial markets react?

Investors responded by selling Nvidia shares. According to Reuters, the stock fell more than 10% in premarket trading on November 12. That move is especially significant because Nvidia, with a market capitalization of approximately $4 trillion, carries considerable weight in major U.S. equity indices.

The decline illustrates how much investors value leadership stability at a company central to the global AI investment cycle. Yet **a leadership change does not mean demand for Nvidia processors has disappeared**. Major technology companies continue to invest heavily in data centers, computing infrastructure and advanced AI models. The underlying question is whether Nvidia can maintain its development pace without the executive who has led it since its founding.

## Why Huang's departure matters for the AI industry

Jensen Huang founded Nvidia in 1993 alongside Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem. The company began as a graphics processor manufacturer and evolved into one of the world's leading suppliers of AI infrastructure. Nvidia chips are used to train and run large AI models, while its CUDA software platform forms an important part of the broader technology ecosystem.

Huang played a central role in that transformation. His departure raises questions about future product strategy, relationships with major customers, management stability and capital investment. At the same time, Nvidia retains established product roadmaps, strong supplier and customer relationships and substantial financial resources.

## The relationship with China faces renewed scrutiny

Nvidia has long operated at the center of U.S.-China technology competition. Washington restricts exports of certain advanced processors to reduce the risk that they could be used in Chinese military or intelligence systems.

Huang advocated maintaining some American technology access to China's market, a position that had drawn debate in Washington even before the current controversy. President Donald Trump confirmed that the FBI was examining circumstances involving Huang's relationship and potential intelligence concerns. Investigations and political suspicions, however, do not demonstrate that Huang knowingly endangered U.S. national security.

## Next test: Nvidia earnings on November 17

The resignation comes just days ahead of Nvidia's scheduled quarterly results. On November 17, 2026, the company plans to report earnings for the third quarter of fiscal 2027. Beyond revenue and profitability, investors will focus on operational continuity and the direction of the new leadership. Demand for AI accelerators, spending by major cloud providers and guidance for upcoming quarters will be closely watched. Those results may help distinguish a short-term confidence crisis from changes in the underlying business.
