**CoreWeave reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $3.661 billion, up 168% year over year, but its net loss widened to $709 million. The results illustrate both strong demand for AI computing capacity and the substantial financial burden of rapidly expanding the infrastructure required to deliver it.**

## Rapid growth comes at a cost

Revenue increased from $1.365 billion in the third quarter of 2025, while the net loss rose from $110 million. CoreWeave supplies computing capacity used to train and operate AI models, requiring major investments in processors, data centers, networking and electricity.

## Financing expenses weigh on earnings

The company reported approximately $846 million in net interest expense. At the operating level, it generated about $207 million in income and $379 million in adjusted operating income. These figures underline an important distinction: positive operating performance does not necessarily translate into overall net profitability after financing and other costs.

## Full-year revenue outlook rises

CoreWeave raised its 2026 revenue guidance to between $13.0 billion and $13.3 billion. It also reported roughly $132.5 billion in revenue backlog, suggesting substantial contracted future demand. However, backlog is not revenue already recognized or cash already collected. The company still needs to deliver the services under the relevant contracts.

Delivering future capacity requires completing facilities, securing electricity and deploying equipment on schedule. Delays, higher financing expenses or changing customer demand could affect financial performance.
