**Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) approved investments totaling up to $1 billion in OpenAI and Anthropic on November 10, 2026. The world's largest contract chipmaker could expand its position beyond semiconductor manufacturing by becoming an investor in two major AI developers driving demand for advanced computing infrastructure.**

## What did TSMC approve?

The board authorized the purchase of up to $500 million in OpenAI Group PBC preferred shares and up to $500 million in Anthropic common shares. The Anthropic investment is tied to the price of the company's planned initial public offering. The two share classes may carry different economic rights, voting arrangements and risks.

**Board approval does not confirm that the full $1 billion has already been invested.** The resolutions establish maximum authorized amounts; actual transactions depend on their respective terms and implementation.

## Why invest in AI developers?

TSMC manufactures advanced chips designed by its customers. Training and operating large AI models require significant computing resources, which in turn increase demand for powerful processors, memory systems and data centers. OpenAI and Anthropic contribute to that demand through their AI products and services.

Potential ownership stakes could provide TSMC with financial exposure to the developers' future performance in addition to manufacturing revenue. However, an equity investment does not establish a new manufacturing contract or exclusive supply agreement. Since OpenAI and Anthropic compete in the same market, approving investments in both could also diversify TSMC's strategic exposure.

## Anthropic's IPO and the wider AI market

Anthropic's planned IPO will test investor appetite for rapidly growing AI businesses that also face exceptionally high development and infrastructure costs. A substantial valuation reflects expectations about future results and does not guarantee profitability.

At the same board meeting, TSMC approved approximately $40.93 billion in capital appropriations for manufacturing capacity, technology development and related operations. **That amount is separate from the proposed OpenAI and Anthropic investments.** The board also approved a plan to establish an investment company with up to $500 million in capital for strategic semiconductor-sector investments.

## Our Take (Metaadvisor)

* **The $1 billion figure is an authorization limit.** Full payment has not been established by the board resolution.
* **The investments have different structures.** OpenAI involves preferred shares, while Anthropic involves common shares at the IPO price.
* **TSMC is broadening its role.** It could become both an infrastructure supplier and an investor in AI developers.
* **Anthropic's IPO will test market valuations.** Investors must weigh strong growth against high costs.
* **The $40.93 billion allocation is separate.** It concerns TSMC's own industrial development.
* **Strategic investment still involves risk.** Rapid AI spending does not guarantee sustainable returns.

**TSMC's decision illustrates the growing financial connections between AI developers and their infrastructure suppliers. The long-term question is whether the industry's extraordinary investments can translate into sustainable profits.**
