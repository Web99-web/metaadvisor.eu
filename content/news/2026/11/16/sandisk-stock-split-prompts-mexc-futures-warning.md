**SanDisk Corporation (NASDAQ: SNDK) has announced an eight-for-one stock split, with trading on a split-adjusted basis expected to begin on November 20, 2026. The split itself does not create additional value for shareholders, but it will trigger an important technical adjustment in the futures market. Cryptocurrency exchange MEXC plans to temporarily suspend trading in SanDisk-linked perpetual contracts and automatically cancel all open orders, including Take Profit and Stop Loss instructions. For leveraged traders, that operational warning matters more than the change in the displayed share price.**

## What does SanDisk's eight-for-one stock split mean?

On October 29, SanDisk confirmed that shareholders would receive seven additional shares for every share already held, bringing the total to eight. The additional shares are scheduled to be distributed after the US market closes on November 19, with split-adjusted trading expected to begin on Friday, November 20. For example, if one share were worth $800 before the split, the eight shares would theoretically be worth about $100 each afterward. The investment would still be worth $800, assuming the market price did not change for other reasons.

The split does not alter existing shareholders' percentage ownership or the company's market capitalization. A lower price per share may make whole-share purchases more accessible, but it is not an economic discount on SanDisk's value and does not guarantee future gains.

## Strong earnings provide the business backdrop

The split announcement accompanied SanDisk's results for the first quarter of fiscal 2027. The company reported revenue of $11.03 billion, up 23% from the previous quarter and 378% year over year. GAAP net income reached approximately $8.1 billion, while SanDisk highlighted growing demand for its data center products.

Management said the split was intended to make the shares more accessible, including to employees participating in its stock purchase plan. Those financial results provide important context, but future investor returns will depend on SanDisk's revenue, profitability and competitive position rather than the split itself.

## MEXC to temporarily suspend SNDK futures trading

MEXC has announced a rebase of its SNDKUSDT, SNDKUSDC and SNDKUSD1 perpetual futures contracts. According to the exchange, the adjustment is scheduled for November 20 from 01:00 to 05:30 UTC, equivalent to **02:00 to 06:30 in Zagreb (CET)**. The window is an estimate and may change depending on the technical process. During the suspension, users will be unable to trade, modify orders or close positions in the affected contracts.

The exchange will carry out a *rebase*, proportionally adjusting contract quantities and prices to reflect SanDisk's post-split shares. Contract quantities are expected to increase roughly eightfold while the reference price falls proportionally. The final adjustment ratio could vary slightly with market conditions and rounding.

## What happens to long and short positions?

The critical distinction between a technical adjustment and a genuine market decline is that both the price and the number of contracts change. If the displayed price is mathematically reduced to one-eighth, **a short position does not automatically earn an 87.5% profit**. Similarly, a long position should not suffer a loss of that magnitude solely because of the split. Risks from real market movements, leverage and liquidity remain in place.

MEXC says existing unrealized profit or loss will be settled as realized P&L, after which the adjusted position's unrealized P&L display will restart at zero. Account equity is intended to remain unchanged by the rebase itself, apart from possible minor rounding differences. Existing positions are generally expected to be reopened, although insufficient funds or extreme volatility could result in positions being settled without a replacement position being established.

## Biggest risk: Take Profit and Stop Loss orders will be canceled

For traders who hold positions over longer periods and rely on protective orders, the most important part of MEXC's notice is the automatic cancellation of all open orders. This includes Take Profit and Stop Loss instructions regardless of whether the underlying position is long or short. Related Grid, AI and Copy Trading bots and strategies will stop and will not restart automatically. Once trading resumes, users may need to recreate strategies and protective orders.

MEXC also notes that liquidations will be suspended during the adjustment window, while scheduled funding fee settlements will continue. When trading resumes, the opening futures price may differ from the adjusted reference price and volatility may increase. Traders should therefore distinguish the predictable mathematical change in contract quantity from genuine market risk after reopening.

## Could the stock split attract more investors?

Stock splits often draw attention, particularly when a company's nominal share price has become relatively high. A lower per-share price may influence how smaller investors perceive accessibility, but the underlying economic value of the business is unchanged immediately before and after the split, excluding market movements. SanDisk's longer-term performance will depend on demand for memory products, competition, earnings and investor expectations, not on the mathematical increase in its share count.
