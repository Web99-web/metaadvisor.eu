**Anthropic, the US company behind the Claude AI assistant, has raised approximately $81.31 billion in the largest initial public offering in history. Its shares were priced at $83 on November 18, 2026, above the previously announced range. Trading on Nasdaq under the symbol ANT is expected to begin on November 19, but the first public-market transactions will provide the real test of investor demand.**

## Demand pushes the offering above expectations

Anthropic confirmed an IPO price of $83 per Class A share and a total offering of 980 million shares. The company itself is selling approximately 956.35 million of those shares, with existing stockholders selling the remainder. The earlier marketing range was $70 to $78 per share. The public offering is valued at approximately $81.31 billion.

The company expanded the offering following strong demand. The number of shares increased from an earlier plan of 825 million to 980 million, a rise of roughly 19%. Pricing above the indicated range signals considerable appetite for an investment in a leading developer of advanced AI models.

## Anthropic overtakes SpaceX's IPO record

The offering surpassed the previous record held by SpaceX, which raised approximately $75 billion in June 2026. Anthropic therefore leads in terms of money raised through an IPO, not necessarily in terms of total company valuation. SpaceX had a higher overall estimated valuation at the time of the comparison.

Anthropic's estimated value of approximately **$1.5 trillion** uses a fully diluted share count that includes certain options and other equity instruments. Excluding those instruments, the valuation is approximately $1.1 trillion. These figures describe different measures and should not be treated as interchangeable.

## Nasdaq ANT: the IPO price is not the opening price

Anthropic announced that its Class A shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on Thursday, November 19, 2026, under **ANT**. The $83 offering price applies to investors who received shares through the IPO allocation. The first price established in public trading can be higher or lower.

At the time this article was prepared, the company had announced an expected trading date, but the first transaction had not yet been confirmed. Nasdaq futures rose by nearly 2% in morning trading. That is a broader market signal and must not be confused with a return on ANT shares, which had not yet opened for trading.

## A record amid concerns about AI spending

The offering comes as shares of semiconductor manufacturers and AI infrastructure providers face pressure. Investors are scrutinizing the enormous capital needed for data centers, computing capacity and the development of increasingly powerful AI models. Anthropic's listing is therefore a significant test of appetite for exposure to AI model developers themselves.

A separate $26 billion private placement at the IPO price is also planned. That amount should not be added to the $81.31 billion public-offering record. The transactions form part of a broader financing package, but they are structured differently.

Strong demand for Anthropic shares does not resolve concerns over the industry's path to profitability. Public-market investors will gain additional opportunities to compare the company's growth against infrastructure commitments and operating costs.
