# Richard Heart, Founder of HEX, Reportedly Detained in Istanbul

**Richard Heart, founder of the HEX cryptocurrency and the PulseChain blockchain network, was reportedly detained on October 14, 2026, in Istanbul's Beşiktaş district, according to Turkish media. The operation was reportedly carried out by Turkish police and the National Intelligence Organization (MİT) in connection with an international search initiated at the request of Finnish authorities. The news quickly spread across the cryptocurrency market, where digital assets associated with Heart's projects recorded substantial price declines.**

Richard Heart, whose legal name is Richard James Schueler, has been one of the most controversial figures in the cryptocurrency industry for years. Turkey's Anadolu news agency and other local media reported on the police operation, while several international cryptocurrency publications identified the detained individual as the founder of HEX.

At the time this article was prepared, however, no separate publicly available statement from Turkish or Finnish authorities had been identified that explicitly confirmed his detention and legal status under his full name.

## Police Operation in Beşiktaş and Conflicting Reports About Seized Assets

According to Turkish media reports, police searched a property in Istanbul's Beşiktaş district during the morning and detained two individuals, one of whom international publications identified as Heart. Reports also mentioned the detention of his wife.

Authorities reportedly seized cash, gold, luxury watches, computers, mobile phones, electronic equipment and firearms during the search.

However, published figures regarding the seized assets vary considerably. Some reports, citing Turkish news agencies, mention approximately $600,000 in cash and 24 luxury watches, while others refer to around $4 million and 64 watches.

Given these discrepancies, neither figure should be treated as the definitively confirmed value of the seized property. Complete official search and seizure records have not been made publicly available.

## Why Was Richard Heart Subject to an Interpol Red Notice?

Finnish authorities have been seeking Heart in connection with allegations of aggravated tax fraud. An Interpol Red Notice associated with the case was published in December 2024.

A Red Notice is not an international arrest warrant. Instead, it is a request to law enforcement agencies in member countries to locate a wanted individual and, where permitted under national law, provisionally arrest that person pending further legal proceedings.

The existence of a Red Notice does not establish criminal guilt.

Likewise, reports of detention in Turkey do not automatically mean that pretrial detention has been ordered or that extradition to Finland has been approved. Such measures would require separate decisions by the competent authorities.

## Who Is Richard Heart and How Does HEX Work?

Richard Heart gained international attention in 2019 when he launched HEX, a cryptocurrency token operating on the Ethereum blockchain.

The project was marketed as a blockchain-based alternative to traditional fixed-term savings deposits. Users can lock their HEX tokens for a selected period and receive rewards calculated in additional HEX tokens.

However, these rewards are not equivalent to guaranteed interest paid by a bank.

An investor may accumulate more tokens while simultaneously losing a substantial portion of the investment's market value if the price of HEX declines. The project does not provide deposit insurance or guaranteed repayment in euros or US dollars.

In 2023, Heart expanded his ecosystem by launching PulseChain, an independent blockchain network, and PulseX, a decentralized cryptocurrency exchange.

His public image has been characterized by intensive marketing, provocative statements and frequent displays of luxury watches, expensive clothing and other high-value possessions.

This approach attracted a substantial community of supporters, while critics questioned the economic sustainability, transparency and long-term value of the projects.

## What Happened to the US SEC Lawsuit?

In 2023, the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) filed a civil lawsuit against Heart and entities associated with his cryptocurrency projects.

The regulator alleged that more than $1 billion had been raised through unregistered crypto asset offerings. It also alleged that some investor funds had been used to finance luxury purchases.

These were regulatory allegations, not judicially established findings of criminal wrongdoing.

A US federal court dismissed the lawsuit in February 2025, and the SEC did not file an amended complaint in April.

The dismissal did not amount to a judicial determination that all of Heart's activities were lawful. Equally, the SEC's allegations did not constitute proof of guilt.

The Finnish investigation is separate from the American civil case and must be considered independently.

## HEX and PulseChain Record Sharp Losses Following the Reports

According to market data and reports published on October 14, HEX and PLS, the native coin of the PulseChain blockchain, experienced significant price declines.

The Block reported losses of approximately 25% for HEX and 35% for PLS during the period it examined.

The precise percentages may differ depending on the time of measurement, trading platform and version of the token being tracked. In particular, HEX on Ethereum must be distinguished from its corresponding version on PulseChain.

The situation illustrates how strongly confidence in a cryptocurrency ecosystem can depend on the reputation of its founder.

A decentralized blockchain may continue to operate technically even when its founder is no longer actively involved. Nevertheless, token prices, market liquidity and future development can remain heavily influenced by that individual's legal and public status.

The distinction between technological decentralization and economic dependence on a prominent founder is particularly important when evaluating the resilience of cryptocurrency projects.

## What Happens Next?

Several important questions remain unresolved following the reported detention.

The first concerns official confirmation of the detained individual's identity and legal status. Further developments may include decisions by Turkish judicial authorities and a potential extradition request from Finland.

It also remains unclear which of the conflicting media reports concerning the seized assets will be supported by official documentation.

None of these possible outcomes should be assumed in advance.

Meanwhile, investors holding HEX, PLS and PLSX should be aware of the potential for increased volatility and limited liquidity in individual markets.

The reported detention does not automatically mean that the associated blockchain networks will cease functioning, nor does it establish criminal responsibility.

## Our Take

* **The most important issue is not the number of luxury watches, but the founder's legal status.** Reports of the detention represent a significant development, but official confirmation of identity, potential judicial measures and any extradition proceedings will be essential to understanding the case.
* **Conflicting figures regarding seized assets require caution.** When some publications report $600,000 and others mention $4 million, it would be misleading to present either amount as definitive without official documentation.
* **The Finnish and American cases are separate matters.** The dismissal of the SEC lawsuit in 2025 does not resolve the allegations being investigated by Finnish authorities.
* **HEX rewards paid in additional tokens are not guaranteed investment returns.** An increase in token holdings does not protect investors against a decline in the market value of their investments.
* **Technical decentralization does not eliminate founder-related risks.** When market confidence is closely tied to one prominent individual, developments involving that person can trigger substantial price movements even if the underlying blockchain continues to function.
* **Official statements will be more important than online speculation.** Information from Turkish and Finnish authorities, together with any future court decisions, will provide a stronger basis for evaluating the situation than viral posts on social media.

The case involving Richard Heart is therefore simultaneously a legal matter, a cryptocurrency market event and a reminder that investors should distinguish between the technical functionality of a blockchain project and the reputational and regulatory risks associated with the individuals who promote it.

For investors, the broader lesson extends beyond HEX and PulseChain. The long-term resilience of any cryptocurrency ecosystem depends not only on its technological architecture but also on transparency, market liquidity, genuine utility and its ability to operate independently of any single influential figure.
