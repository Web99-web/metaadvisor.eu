# One Institutional OTC Buyer Takes the Entire HYPE Package Worth About $329 Million

**Hyperliquid Labs has started unstaking 3.75 million HYPE tokens allocated to the team for October, and according to published information, one institutional buyer is expected to take the entire package through a private OTC deal. The market value of the tokens was estimated at about $329 million at the time of reporting. The buyer's identity, the actual transaction price and any period during which the buyer may be required to hold the tokens have not been disclosed.**

The news has attracted attention because a large team unlock usually raises concerns about additional sell pressure. In this case, the 3.75 million HYPE tokens are not expected to move directly into public order books, but instead to be transferred to a single institutional buyer outside the open market. That could significantly reduce the immediate market impact of the unlock, but it does not answer the question of what the new owner will do with the tokens after receiving them.

## Hyperliquid is unstaking 3.75 million HYPE

The tokens represent Hyperliquid's October allocation to the team. According to available reports, the plan was published within the Hyperliquid community, after which Hyperliquid Labs began unstaking a total of 3.75 million HYPE.

Under Hyperliquid's mechanism, unstaking takes seven days, so the announcement does not mean the tokens are immediately available to the buyer. Once that period is complete, the tokens can be transferred to the institutional buyer, and their subsequent movement should then be visible onchain.

## The entire package goes to one buyer off-market

The most important part of the deal is that a buyer has been found for the full amount. An OTC, or over-the-counter, transaction is negotiated privately between two parties and bypasses public exchange order books.

For a package of this size, that can be especially important because selling millions of tokens through the open market can itself create significant price pressure. If all 3.75 million HYPE tokens are indeed transferred to one buyer through an OTC deal, the team does not need to seek liquidity for that package through public exchanges.

## About $329 million is not necessarily the OTC price

The estimated value of roughly $329 million is based on HYPE's market price at the time of reporting. That does not necessarily mean the institutional buyer will pay exactly that amount.

OTC transactions often include privately negotiated terms. The price may involve a discount, a premium, a lock-up or other obligations that are not visible to the public market. The $329 million figure should therefore be treated as the market value of the package, not as the confirmed price of the deal itself.

The fact that a single institutional buyer is willing to take 3.75 million HYPE is still significant because it shows that demand exists for a very large package without requiring the tokens to be sold through a public order book.

## The buyer remains unknown

The identity of the institution has not been disclosed. It is not known whether the buyer is a crypto fund, treasury company, market maker, traditional financial institution or another type of large investor.

A potential lock-up period is another important unknown. An OTC deal can remove immediate team sell pressure from the open market, but it does not automatically mean the purchased HYPE will never be offered for sale.

If there are no selling restrictions, the buyer could choose to hold the tokens, stake them or gradually move them to the market later.

## The real test comes after the transfer

Once unstaking is complete, the blockchain should provide an important additional signal. If the package remains on one address after the transfer or is staked again, that would suggest that a large amount of HYPE is staying outside active market supply for the time being.

If, however, a significant share of the tokens starts moving toward centralized exchange addresses, the market could interpret those movements as potential preparation for selling.

The OTC deal therefore changes the immediate supply risk, but does not eliminate it completely. Instead of asking how much the Hyperliquid team will sell after the unlock, the market now has to watch what one large new owner does with the entire package.

## An unlock does not automatically mean falling prices

A large token unlock is often associated with expectations of lower prices because more tokens may become available for sale. In practice, the reaction depends on how many tokens actually reach the market and whether there is enough demand to absorb that supply.

In this case, institutional demand is exactly the key part of the story. If the entire package has already found one buyer, the amount that might otherwise have reached the open market could be significantly smaller.

That does not mean the unlock is automatically bullish. Price direction still depends on the broader market structure, liquidity, buyer behavior and the actions of other large HYPE holders.

## The OTC deal changes the nature of supply risk

Without a large institutional buyer, the market would mainly be asking how much of the 3.75 million HYPE the team might sell once the tokens become available.

Now the question is different: who is taking such a large package and what strategy does the buyer have?

If the new buyer plans to hold or stake HYPE for the long term, a large part of the October unlock could remain outside active supply. If the investment horizon is shorter, some of that supply could appear on the market later.

In either case, the immediate setup differs from a typical unlock in which a larger number of recipients receive tokens and individually decide whether to sell.

## HYPE is already attracting large pools of capital

The OTC deal comes at a time when Hyperliquid has already attracted significant treasury and institutional buyers. Large HYPE accumulations and growing interest in the token over recent months make the identity of a buyer willing to take a package worth hundreds of millions of dollars even more interesting.

Still, until the institution's identity is officially confirmed, it would not be justified to link this transaction to any specific company, fund or wallet.

For now, the most important known fact is the structure of the deal itself: a very large team package is expected to be absorbed through a single private institutional transaction rather than sold through the open market.
