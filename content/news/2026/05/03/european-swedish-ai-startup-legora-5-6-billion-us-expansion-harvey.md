# Swedish legal AI startup Legora reaches $5.6B valuation and targets U.S. expansion where Harvey leads

As artificial intelligence continues to move deeper into real-world business operations, one of the most traditionally closed industries — law — is beginning to shift. In that space, Swedish startup Legora is emerging as a fast-growing global contender.

Founded in 2023 in Stockholm, Legora has reached a valuation of approximately **$5.6 billion in less than two years**, positioning itself among the most valuable European AI startups in the legal tech sector. As Metaadvisor notes, this rapid growth reflects a broader shift toward AI-driven professional services.

## A startup less than two years old — already global

Since its launch in 2023, Legora has:

* expanded into more than 50 markets
* onboarded over 1,000 law firms and legal teams
* surpassed **$100 million in annual recurring revenue (ARR)**

For an industry known for slow change, this pace is remarkable.

## What Legora actually does

Legora develops AI systems specifically designed for legal workflows, aiming to improve efficiency rather than replace lawyers.

Its platform enables:

* contract analysis and drafting
* legal research
* processing large volumes of documentation
* automation of repetitive tasks

Importantly:

👉 with AI support, lawyers can work faster and handle more cases

## 🔥 Nvidia enters legal AI — for the first time

The most significant development in Legora’s story is the involvement of Nvidia.

Legora raised approximately **$600 million through its Series D round and extension**, with Nvidia participating in that extension.

👉 the investment comes through **NVentures**, Nvidia’s official venture capital arm

👉 this marks **Nvidia’s first known investment in the legal AI sector**

Alongside Nvidia, the round included:

* Atlassian
* and other institutional investors

This signals a strategic shift:

👉 Nvidia is not only investing in chips and infrastructure
👉 but also in AI applications that run on top of that infrastructure

## Competition with Harvey — Europe vs U.S.

Legora is entering direct competition with U.S.-based startup Harvey.

Harvey:

* founded in the United States
* focuses on AI-powered legal services
* is valued at around **$11 billion**

The competitive dynamic is becoming global:

* Legora is expanding from Europe into the U.S.
* Harvey is expanding from the U.S. into Europe

👉 creating a direct transatlantic battle in the legal AI market

## The legal industry enters a new phase

The legal sector has historically been resistant to technological change due to:

* high accuracy requirements
* regulatory frameworks
* responsibility toward clients

However, AI is beginning to reshape how legal work is done.

The focus is shifting toward:

* faster processing
* higher case throughput
* more efficient workflows

This does not necessarily reduce costs, but it changes how legal services are delivered.

## The bigger picture

Legora is not an isolated case.

It reflects a broader trend:

* AI is entering traditional professions
* markets are becoming global
* competition is accelerating

Most importantly:

👉 capital from major players like Nvidia suggests this transformation is just beginning
