# Gency AI raises $20 million: AI and blockchain are reshaping digital advertising

Digital advertising is one of the largest global markets today, yet it still operates on a model built around trust in centralized platforms. Gency AI is attempting to change that by combining artificial intelligence and blockchain technology to create a more transparent and automated system.

The project has raised $20 million, with participation from investors such as **TikTok**, **HF0**, **XYZ**, **Streamlined Ventures**, **Hat-Trick Capital**, **ArkStream**, **MH Ventures**, **ViaBTC**, and **Basics Capital**.

{{< support1 >}}

## From platform trust to protocol trust

Today’s advertising ecosystem relies on what is often described as a “platform trust” model — advertisers must trust the data provided by large platforms. Gency AI introduces the concept of “protocol trust,” where key data can be independently verified.

Ad impressions, clicks, and conversions can be recorded on-chain, making them transparent and verifiable without relying on intermediaries.

## Automation and transparency

One of Gency AI’s main goals is to automate processes that are currently slow and complex. Through the use of smart contracts, payments between advertisers, agencies, and publishers can be executed automatically once campaign conditions are met.

This leads to faster financial flows, reduced administrative overhead, and lower risk of errors or data manipulation.

{{< support2 >}}

## Technology behind the system

The platform combines multiple layers of technology to enable this model:

* on-chain verification of campaign data
* smart contract-based revenue distribution
* privacy-preserving computing to protect sensitive data
* AI engine for ad optimization and performance analysis

Together, these components allow the entire advertising process to operate in a secure and transparent environment.

## What this means for the market

If implemented at scale, this model could significantly change how digital advertising works.

Potential impacts include greater data control, faster settlements, and reduced dependence on large centralized platforms.

At the same time, the challenges remain substantial, as the advertising industry is already dominated by powerful incumbents.

## Our take (Metaadvisor):

The idea of verifiable advertising aligns with the broader trend toward transparency and data ownership. The combination of AI and blockchain serves a clear functional purpose here rather than just a marketing narrative. However, entering an industry dominated by major platforms will be difficult. The project’s success will depend on its ability to attract adoption beyond the crypto ecosystem.
