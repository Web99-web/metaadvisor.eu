# AI boom drives SNDK higher: SanDisk surges from ~$250 to nearly $1,200 in just months

While most investors are focused on AI models and software, the real story is happening behind the scenes — in the infrastructure that makes it all possible.

SanDisk (SNDK) has become one of the clearest examples of this shift. As Metaadvisor highlights, the stock was trading around **$250 at the end of 2025**, only to surge to nearly **$1,200 within a few months**.

Moves like this don’t happen by accident.

## What is driving the surge

The reason is simple:

* AI needs massive amounts of data
* data needs storage

SanDisk produces NAND memory, a critical component for:

* data centers
* cloud infrastructure
* AI servers
* storing and processing large datasets

As major companies rapidly scale AI infrastructure, demand for storage solutions is accelerating.

{{< support1 >}}

## AI is not just software — it’s hardware

Much of the market attention is on AI models, but without infrastructure, those systems cannot function.

Within the AI ecosystem:

* GPUs process data
* cloud platforms distribute it
* storage holds it

Without memory, the entire system breaks down.

That’s why capital is increasingly flowing into this segment.

## A surge that signals a cycle shift

SNDK’s move is not linear — it’s a rapid revaluation often seen in the early stages of major technological cycles.

Such movements typically indicate:

* institutional capital entering the market
* a shift in investor perception
* a transition from niche to mainstream

This is no longer just “tech hype” — it reflects a structural need.

{{< support2 >}}

## Is the growth sustainable?

After such a strong rally, the key question becomes — what comes next?

On one side:

* AI demand continues to grow
* investments in data centers are accelerating

On the other:

* part of the growth is already priced in
* investors are taking profits
* volatility is increasing

This creates a classic market tension between momentum and caution.

## The bigger picture: a new phase of the AI market

This is not an isolated story.

We are seeing a broader trend:

* massive investment in infrastructure
* rising demand for storage
* growing focus on the “back-end” of AI systems

In other words:

👉 it’s not just AI that is growing — it’s the entire ecosystem behind it
