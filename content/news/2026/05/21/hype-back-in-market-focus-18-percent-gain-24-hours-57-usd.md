# HYPE back in market focus: around 18% gain in 24 hours and a test of the 57 USD zone

Hyperliquid’s token HYPE has once again moved into the center of attention on the crypto market after making a strong upward move over the last 24 hours. According to available market data, HYPE moved from the area around **48 USD** to the area around **57 USD**, which means it gained around **18%** in one day and added roughly **9 USD** to its price.

Such a move immediately attracted market attention because HYPE is no longer just another altcoin in a short-term momentum wave. Hyperliquid is increasingly being viewed as one of the most interesting on-chain trading infrastructures, especially because of perpetual futures markets, synthetic assets, the real-world asset narrative and growing interest in 24/7 trading for assets that were once tied almost exclusively to traditional financial channels.

What makes HYPE especially interesting is that the market no longer sees it only as the token of a decentralized exchange. Increasingly, it is being treated as a possible proxy for the future of on-chain trading, where crypto infrastructure is used for price discovery, leverage, perpetual futures and new forms of exposure to assets that were previously difficult for smaller investors to access.

{{< support1 >}}

## Hyperliquid is no longer just a DEX story

Hyperliquid initially attracted attention as a decentralized platform for perpetual futures trading. But the current narrative around HYPE goes far beyond the classic DEX story. The market now increasingly sees Hyperliquid as infrastructure for on-chain trading, meaning a platform that tries to combine speed, liquidity, decentralized execution and new types of markets.

In the crypto world, this is an important shift. For a long time, decentralized exchanges were mostly seen as useful for spot trading, while more advanced derivatives markets remained strongly connected to centralized platforms. Hyperliquid is trying to change that relationship by building an environment where perpetual futures and more complex trading products can run directly on blockchain infrastructure.

That is why HYPE is not only rising as a token connected to one platform. It is also rising as part of a broader question: can part of professional trading, price discovery and derivatives markets move onto on-chain infrastructure?

## The SpaceX pre-IPO narrative added more attention

One reason Hyperliquid has attracted additional attention in recent days is the story around the SpaceX pre-IPO perpetual market. Trade.xyz launched a synthetic perpetual futures product on Hyperliquid infrastructure linked to the expected valuation of SpaceX before a possible IPO. This opened an interesting question for the market: can decentralized platforms enable early price discovery for private companies that are not yet available to ordinary investors on traditional exchanges?

Of course, such products do not mean ownership of real shares. They are synthetic markets and perpetual futures contracts that allow speculation on a price or expected valuation. But that is exactly why they are interesting to the crypto market, because they show how blockchain infrastructure can be used to create new types of markets outside the usual framework of traditional finance.

{{< support1 >}}

For HYPE, this narrative is important because it connects several major themes at once: on-chain trading, real-world assets, synthetic markets, private equity price discovery and the desire of the retail market to gain earlier access to stories that were once reserved for institutional investors.

## The price rise also raises the question of FOMO

When a token rises by almost 20% in a single day, the market almost always enters a sensitive phase. On one hand, such a rise can confirm strong momentum and show that capital is returning to a project. On the other hand, these exact moves often attract FOMO buyers who enter only after a large part of the short-term move has already happened.

HYPE is now in a phase where it is being watched by long-term Hyperliquid supporters, active traders, momentum buyers and investors looking for the next major crypto infrastructure story. That can further increase volatility, especially if the price holds near the 57 USD zone or if the market starts testing whether room for higher levels could open.

In such situations, it is important not to look only at price. Volume, open interest, liquidity, the reaction after the first strong move, Bitcoin’s behavior and broader altcoin sentiment also matter. For tokens connected to trading infrastructure, it is especially important to watch real activity on the platform, because token price and protocol usage do not always grow at the same pace.

## HYPE as an infrastructure narrative for the crypto market

Unlike some altcoin moves that are based almost entirely on short-term hype, HYPE has a clearer market story. Hyperliquid is positioning itself around trading, liquidity, perpetual futures markets and new forms of on-chain financial infrastructure. That makes it interesting at a time when the crypto market is once again turning toward projects that can explain their function in one simple sentence.

For HYPE, that sentence is: the market sees it as a token connected to one of the most visible on-chain trading platforms. This does not mean the risk is low. On the contrary, trading platforms, derivatives products and leverage always carry a high level of risk. But that is also why the market is watching Hyperliquid so closely, because it is where crypto volatility, professional trading tools and experiments with new markets come together.

## Why the market is now watching the 57 USD zone

The area around 57 USD has become important because it marks the level where HYPE has reappeared among the most closely watched crypto gainers. Such zones often become short-term psychological points around which a new market story forms. If the price manages to hold near higher levels, the market may begin to talk about trend continuation. If it quickly returns lower, the move may be interpreted as an overheated momentum wave.

For investors and market observers, this is now more than just one daily candle. HYPE has become a test for a broader question: can on-chain trading infrastructure become one of the main crypto narratives in this phase of the market?

The answer will become clearer only in the coming days. If Hyperliquid continues to attract volume, new products and market attention, HYPE could keep its place among the most interesting infrastructure tokens. If interest cools quickly, the rise could be remembered as another strong but short-lived altcoin impulse.

## The broader message for the crypto market

The rise of HYPE shows that the crypto market is not currently looking only for meme coins or short-term viral stories. Projects connected to infrastructure are receiving more attention again: trading rails, settlement, liquidity, perpetual futures, tokenized assets and real-world asset markets.

That is why Hyperliquid is interesting even to those who do not trade HYPE. Its rise shows how crypto infrastructure is trying to move closer to traditional financial markets, but in a way that is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week and built around blockchain logic.

For now, one thing is clear: HYPE is back in market focus. The price has risen by around 18% in 24 hours, touched the area around 57 USD and opened a new debate about whether Hyperliquid could become one of the main symbols of on-chain trading infrastructure.
