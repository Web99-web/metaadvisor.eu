# JPMorgan and Mastercard test a new type of financial infrastructure using the XRP Ledger blockchain

Large financial institutions have been experimenting more aggressively with blockchain technology, tokenization and digital settlement systems in recent years, but a new pilot transaction involving JPMorgan, Mastercard, Ripple and the XRP Ledger blockchain has attracted major attention across the crypto and fintech industries.

The story is more complicated than it first appears because it involves:
- one of the world’s largest banks
- a global payment network
- blockchain infrastructure
- tokenized US government bonds
- and several different digital systems.

👉 To understand what actually happened, it is probably best to first explain:
➡️ who is who in this story.

## Who is JPMorgan?

JPMorgan Chase is one of the largest financial institutions in the world and one of Wall Street’s most important players.

The bank dominates areas such as:
- investment banking
- interbank transfers
- treasury markets
- derivatives
- and institutional finance.

Interestingly, JPMorgan was highly skeptical of crypto for years. The bank’s CEO Jamie Dimon frequently criticized Bitcoin and the crypto market publicly, calling Bitcoin a “fraud” and an extremely risky market.

At the same time:
➡️ JPMorgan was quietly building its own blockchain infrastructure.

Its blockchain division was previously known as:
- Onyx
- the JPM Coin platform
- or JPMorgan blockchain division.

Today the entire system operates under a new name:
👉 Kinexys.

## What are Kinexys and JPM Coin?

Kinexys is JPMorgan’s blockchain and digital asset system for:
- institutional settlement
- digital liquidity
- tokenization
- blockchain payments
- and interbank transfers.

JPM Coin is not a traditional cryptocurrency like Bitcoin or XRP. Instead, it is a digital banking token used internally within JPMorgan’s institutional network.

In other words:
➡️ JPMorgan has been building its own digital financial ecosystem for years.

## Who is Ripple and what is the XRP Ledger blockchain?

Ripple is a fintech and blockchain company focused on modernizing international payments and settlement systems through blockchain technology.

Its ecosystem is connected to:
- the XRP Ledger blockchain
- and the XRP token.

It is important to understand:
➡️ XRP Ledger is not the same thing as the XRP coin.

The XRP Ledger (XRPL) is a blockchain network and infrastructure layer, similar to:
- Ethereum
- Solana
- or the Bitcoin network.

The network can be used to:
- process transactions
- transfer tokenized assets
- and handle settlement operations.

XRP itself is simply a digital token that exists on that network.

That means:
⚠️ this story does not necessarily mean JPMorgan is using XRP coin as its main settlement currency.

As far as currently known:
➡️ the XRP Ledger blockchain was used as infrastructure.

## Who is Ondo Finance?

Ondo Finance is one of the best-known companies in the real-world asset tokenization sector (RWA — real-world assets).

Its goal is to turn:
- government bonds
- funds
- and other financial products
into blockchain-based tokens.

In this case, Ondo participated through its tokenized US Treasury fund called OUSG.

Ondo has been increasingly mentioned in stories involving tokenized financial assets and blockchain infrastructure, and we previously covered the company’s participation in projects involving stock tokenization on blockchain systems:
➡️ https://metaadvisor.eu/hr/news/ondo-finance-tokenizacija-dionica-blockchain-buducnost/

## What role did Mastercard play?

Many people initially assumed Mastercard was introducing XRP payments or crypto cards here, but that is not the case.

Mastercard used:
👉 the Multi-Token Network.

This is the company’s new system for:
- tokenized finance
- digital asset settlement
- and connecting blockchain networks with banking infrastructure.

In this pilot:
➡️ Mastercard essentially acted as a “bridge” between the blockchain layer and traditional banking settlement systems.

{{< support2 >}}

## What actually happened in the pilot project?

The pilot transaction involved Ondo Finance’s tokenized US Treasury fund called OUSG.

That tokenized asset was processed through:
👉 the XRP Ledger blockchain.

At the same time:
- Mastercard’s infrastructure transmitted the settlement instructions
- while JPMorgan’s Kinexys system handled the fiat settlement and US dollar transfer to Ripple’s bank account in Singapore.

In other words:
➡️ for the first time, a:
- public blockchain
- banking settlement system
- and global payment infrastructure
were combined in a real-time process.

## Why is this story important?

Because it shows that Wall Street is no longer ignoring blockchain infrastructure.

At the same time:
➡️ banks clearly do not want to completely replace the existing financial system.

Instead, large institutions are attempting to combine:
- blockchain networks
- tokenization
- stablecoins
- banking settlement
- and traditional finance
into a new hybrid financial model.

## Tokenization is becoming Wall Street’s next major theme

The story also shows how seriously large financial institutions are now taking:
👉 real-world asset tokenization.

This includes:
- government bonds
- funds
- real estate
- stocks
- and other financial products.

Many analysts believe tokenization could become one of the biggest transformations of the global financial system over the next decade.

## Our view (Metaadvisor):

* Wall Street is increasingly testing blockchain settlement infrastructure
* The XRP Ledger blockchain was used as a public blockchain layer, not necessarily as an XRP coin settlement system
* JPMorgan’s Kinexys shows how major banks are building their own digital asset networks
* Mastercard is attempting to become a critical bridge between blockchain systems and traditional finance
* Tokenization and multichain financial systems could become one of the biggest transformations of future finance
