# Hyperliquid, Coinbase and Circle connect the HYPE and USDC ecosystem as markets watch a major DeFi shift

The crypto market has started paying much closer attention to Hyperliquid after Coinbase and Circle announced a new collaboration tied to the AQAv2 model and USDC infrastructure.

According to Hyperliquid’s official announcement, Coinbase will act as the treasury deployer for AQAv2 on USDC, while Circle will serve as the technical deployer responsible for CCTP and cross-chain infrastructure. Both companies also plan to stake HYPE in order to activate AQAv2.

This is where the story becomes far more significant than it may initially appear. Hyperliquid no longer looks like just another crypto-native perpetual trading platform. Instead, it increasingly resembles a serious institutional DeFi layer attracting some of the biggest names in the crypto industry.

## USDC is becoming Hyperliquid’s central stablecoin

One of the biggest changes is that USDC is expected to become the canonical quote asset for Hyperliquid markets through the upcoming HIP-4 upgrade. In other words, Hyperliquid is attempting to solve one of modern crypto trading’s biggest problems — fragmentation between stablecoins, liquidity pools and bridge infrastructure.

Many users still deal with:
- multiple networks,
- wrapped assets,
- bridge transfers,
- cross-chain confusion,
- and funds temporarily getting “stuck” during transfers.

Because of this, Hyperliquid now appears focused on simplifying the ecosystem and concentrating liquidity around one dominant stablecoin — USDC.

## Many users did not realize Coinbase was deeply connected to USDC

A large part of the younger crypto community primarily associates USDC with Circle today. However, Coinbase was actually one of the key partners behind the stablecoin from the very beginning.

USDC was launched back in 2018 through the Centre Consortium, which was jointly created by Coinbase and Circle. While Circle later became the primary issuer of the stablecoin, Coinbase remained deeply tied to the entire USDC ecosystem and reserve revenue structure.

The new Hyperliquid announcement now suggests that Coinbase no longer wants to function only as a crypto exchange, but also as a major infrastructure and treasury player within the DeFi sector.

## What happens to the USDH stablecoin?

The announcement also confirmed the gradual sunset of the USDH stablecoin. Native Markets agreed to terms allowing Coinbase to acquire the USDH brand assets, while existing USDH markets will remain active during the transition period.

Hyperliquid stated that USDH remains fully backed and that users will be able to convert funds into USDC or fiat without fees during the migration process. The project also announced grants for developers and teams that previously integrated USDH infrastructure in order to support the transition.

Although USDH is slowly leaving the spotlight, Hyperliquid says many of the mechanisms and ideas pioneered by USDH will continue through the AQAv2 model.

## Hyperliquid is moving toward institutional DeFi

For a long time, Hyperliquid carried a reputation as:
- crypto-native,
- anti-establishment,
- and almost underground within the trading world.

But the involvement of Coinbase, Circle and USDC infrastructure is clearly changing the perception of the project.

Part of the market views this as a bullish signal because it could bring:
- deeper liquidity,
- a stronger stablecoin layer,
- and potentially much larger institutional capital flows.

At the same time, some members of the crypto community warn that stronger dependence on USDC could also increase regulatory centralization within the ecosystem.

## Our view (Metaadvisor)

- Hyperliquid is clearly attempting to simplify its stablecoin ecosystem around USDC.
- USDH is gradually entering its sunset phase, while parts of its mechanics will continue through AQAv2.
- Coinbase appears increasingly interested in becoming a major infrastructure player within DeFi.
- Hyperliquid is slowly moving away from its “underground trading” image and closer toward institutional crypto infrastructure.
- The market may still underestimate how significant this transition could become over the long term.
