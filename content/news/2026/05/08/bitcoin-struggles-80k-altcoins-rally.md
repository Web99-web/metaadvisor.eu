# Bitcoin Struggles at $80K While Altcoins Rally Hard: Is the Market Ignoring Iran?

Bitcoin has started to look strange again.

While parts of the altcoin market continue posting aggressive gains and much stronger momentum, the world’s largest cryptocurrency is once again struggling around the psychologically important $80,000 level.

BTC briefly pushed toward the $83,000 area earlier this week before sliding back toward $80,000, leaving the market divided between two completely different narratives:
👉 Is Bitcoin preparing for another breakout toward $85,000, or is the market slowly entering a cooling phase?

## Markets Expected Weaker US Data

Part of the nervousness came from the United States.

New US labor market data showed a much stronger economy than expected. Instead of the forecasted 65,000 new jobs, the market saw 115,000 jobs added.

That matters because the Federal Reserve (FED — the central bank of the United States) has spent months trying to fight inflation through higher interest rates.

If the economy remains strong, the FED has fewer reasons to cut rates.

And lower rates are often viewed as fuel for:
* crypto
* stocks
* and higher-risk assets.

That is why Bitcoin initially weakened after the data release.

## Altcoins Are Telling a Completely Different Story

What many traders now find even more interesting is the fact that several altcoins currently appear much stronger than Bitcoin itself.

While BTC looks heavy and slower, capital appears to be rotating into riskier projects where traders are searching for higher upside potential.

That can often signal:
* rising risk appetite
* capital rotation
* or the market searching for the “next big move.”

Ethereum, Solana and several other major altcoins continue showing relatively solid momentum, while some smaller projects have recently posted much more aggressive moves than Bitcoin.

## Iran, Hormuz and the Question Confusing the Market

A large part of the market expected far more panic surrounding the latest tensions involving Iran and the Strait of Hormuz.

The Strait of Hormuz remains one of the world’s most important energy corridors, with a significant portion of global oil shipments passing through the region.

Under normal circumstances, a major escalation there would likely:
* send oil prices sharply higher
* increase fear across financial markets
* and pressure risk assets.

This time, however, markets are reacting far more calmly than many expected.

Although Brent crude moved higher in the short term, investors still appear to believe the conflict remains limited and unlikely to trigger major global trade disruption.

That is a very important signal.

Because if markets truly expected a serious escalation:
* oil prices would likely be exploding much higher
* the US dollar would be rallying aggressively
* and crypto markets would probably look far weaker.

## Why Is Everyone Suddenly Talking About Funding Rates?

One of the most interesting signals currently comes from the futures market.

Bitcoin funding rates have now remained negative for 67 consecutive days — the longest streak in nearly a decade according to K33 Research data.

Funding rates are periodic payments exchanged between long and short traders in futures markets.

When funding rates are negative:
👉 short traders are paying long traders.

In other words:
a large portion of the market is still betting against Bitcoin.

That is exactly why some analysts continue warning about the possibility of a major short squeeze.

## What Is a Short Squeeze?

A short squeeze happens when too many traders expect prices to fall, but the market suddenly moves sharply higher instead.

Short sellers are then forced to close their positions to limit losses, creating additional buying pressure that pushes prices even higher.

That is why some traders still believe Bitcoin could make another move toward:
* $83,000
* or even $85,000.

## But There Are Still Reasons for Caution

Despite optimism from part of the market, Bitcoin still does not look like the fully convincing leader of this rally.

In classic strong bull market phases, BTC usually leads the entire market while altcoins follow later with more aggressive moves.

This time, the situation looks different:
* altcoins appear more aggressive
* Bitcoin continues struggling with key resistance levels
* while markets simultaneously monitor the FED, inflation and geopolitical risks.

That creates the impression of a market still searching for clear direction.

## The Key Levels Everyone Is Watching

Markets are now closely watching the zone between:
* $78,000 and $80,000.

If Bitcoin manages to hold above those levels consistently, some analysts believe another attempt toward $85,000 becomes possible.

But if BTC loses the $78,000 area:
👉 short sellers could regain control of the market.

That is exactly why markets have recently become extremely sensitive to:
* macroeconomic data
* FED comments
* war-related headlines
* and large futures positions.

## Our View (Metaadvisor):

* Bitcoin currently looks weaker than parts of the altcoin market
* Negative funding rates could become fuel for a major short squeeze
* Markets do not currently appear to expect a major Hormuz escalation
* BTC remains trapped inside a crucial 78K–80K zone
* The market currently looks more like a battle for direction than the beginning of a major trend
