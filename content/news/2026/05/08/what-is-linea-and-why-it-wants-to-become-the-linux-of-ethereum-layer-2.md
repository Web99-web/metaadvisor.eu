# Who is Linea and why it wants to become the Linux of Ethereum Layer 2 networks

If you have never heard of Linea — you are not alone.

Even though it is considered one of the more serious Ethereum projects, most people outside hardcore crypto and developer communities barely know it exists. The reason is simple: Linea is not focused on hype or viral marketing, but on blockchain infrastructure mainly used by developers and tech companies.

## Who is behind Linea?

Behind Linea stands **Consensys — one of the most important companies in the Ethereum ecosystem**.

If you have ever used the **MetaMask wallet**, you have already used one of their products. Consensys also develops **Infura** and several other tools widely used by Ethereum developers and blockchain projects.

In crypto circles, Consensys is often considered an “OG” project — an old internet/slang term meaning “original gangster,” now commonly used for veterans and pioneers of an industry.

In other words:

👉 this is not a startup that appeared yesterday.

## What are Layer 1 and Layer 2?

This is where many people get lost because the crypto industry constantly talks in “layers.”

The simplest explanation:

**Ethereum is the main network — also known as Layer 1.**

The problem:

* Ethereum can become slow
* and transactions can become expensive.

That is why Layer 2 networks exist, such as:

* Arbitrum
* Base
* Optimism
* and Linea.

Their goal is:

👉 to make Ethereum faster and cheaper.

The easiest comparison:

Ethereum is the main highway.

Layer 2 is an additional fast lane running beside it to reduce traffic congestion.

## Why is Linea suddenly in the news?

Linea announced that part of its technology stack will move under Linux Foundation governance.

That sounds technical, but the idea is actually quite simple.

👉 they want the project to look less like a “private blockchain controlled by one company” and more like open infrastructure maintained by a wider developer and enterprise community.

## Why is Linux being mentioned?

Linux is one of the most famous open-source projects in the world.

It is not controlled by a single company, but by a foundation model and a broad developer community.

Now Linea is trying to build something similar in the blockchain world.

That is why the description fits:

👉 “Linea wants to become the Linux of Ethereum Layer 2 networks.”

## What do they gain from this?

With this move, Linea wants to appear:

* more serious
* more neutral
* more decentralized
* and more attractive to enterprises and institutions.

Because today many crypto projects talk about decentralization, while in reality a single company still controls:

* the code
* upgrades
* infrastructure
* and key security decisions.

Linea is trying to show that it wants a more open and long-term development model.

{{< support2 >}}

## But there is one important detail

This does not mean Linea is now fully decentralized.

There are still parts of the network controlled by a smaller group and centralized infrastructure.

That is why this move is more about:
👉 building trust and legitimacy

than launching a complete revolution.

## Why does this story actually matter?

Because it shows how the blockchain industry is increasingly trying to evolve into serious technological infrastructure.

Linea was never a meme or hype project. From the beginning, the focus has been on:

* Ethereum infrastructure
* network scaling
* developers
* and long-term development.

That is exactly why this move is interesting.

👉 the goal is not virality — but trust.

By moving part of its technology under Linux Foundation governance, Linea is trying to show that it does not want to depend entirely on a single company forever, but instead build a more neutral and open system.

This is a model that has existed successfully in the traditional open-source world for years.

And that is probably why this story matters far more to developers and the tech industry than to the average crypto audience.

## Our take (Metaadvisor):

* Linea is trying to gain more legitimacy through the Linux Foundation model
* This is more of an infrastructure and developer story than traditional crypto hype
* Layer 2 networks are becoming increasingly important for Ethereum’s future
* Open-source governance could become a major trend in blockchain infrastructure
* Projects like this show how crypto is increasingly trying to look like serious technological infrastructure
