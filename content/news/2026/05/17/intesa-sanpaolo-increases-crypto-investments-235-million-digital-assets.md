# Intesa Sanpaolo increases crypto investments: the European banking giant now holds around $235 million in digital assets

One of Europe’s largest banking groups, **Intesa Sanpaolo**, significantly increased its cryptocurrency exposure during the first quarter of 2026. According to reports from several crypto and financial media outlets, the total value of its crypto portfolio grew from approximately **$100 million** at the end of 2025 to around **$235 million** by the end of March 2026.

According to the reports, most of the growth came from additional **Bitcoin exposure** through ETF products connected to **BlackRock** and ARK 21Shares funds. The bank also reportedly opened positions linked to **Ethereum and XRP** for the first time.

At the same time, Intesa Sanpaolo almost completely reduced its exposure to **Solana**. According to the report, its position connected to a Solana ETF product dropped from more than **266,000 shares** to only a few thousand.

It is also interesting that the bank entered the **crypto derivatives** segment for the first time through call options tied to Bitcoin ETFs. That type of strategy resembles the approach used by major **Wall Street institutions** more than traditional direct crypto investing.

## Intesa Sanpaolo operates through a large network of European and international banks

Intesa Sanpaolo is not a small regional bank, but one of Europe’s largest banking groups with a broad network of banks and financial institutions across Europe and beyond.

The group operates through:
- PBZ - Privredna banka Zagreb
- Intesa Sanpaolo Bank Slovenia
- Intesa Sanpaolo Banka Bosnia and Herzegovina
- Banca Intesa Beograd
- CIB Bank in Hungary
- VUB Bank in Slovakia
- Intesa Sanpaolo Bank Romania
- Intesa Sanpaolo Bank Albania
- Alexbank in Egypt
- Pravex Bank in Ukraine
- Eximbank Moldova in Moldova.

Because of that, crypto and ETF-related stories are no longer just topics for crypto startups and technology enthusiasts — they are increasingly becoming part of the broader European banking and financial industry.

## European banks are increasingly moving into digital assets

This story comes at a time when Europe is simultaneously trying to regulate the crypto market through the **MiCA framework** (Markets in Crypto-Assets), the European regulatory framework for digital assets.

For years, European banks were significantly more cautious than American institutions when it came to crypto. However, over recent months there has been a visible shift toward institutional adoption of:
- Bitcoin ETF products
- Ethereum exposure
- tokenization of assets
- stablecoin infrastructure
- and digital financial products.

What is especially interesting is that Intesa Sanpaolo is not aggressively moving into smaller altcoin projects, but instead remains focused on **institutionally accepted products** such as Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs.

## Crypto is no longer just a “startup story”

These developments show how much the crypto market has changed over the past few years. Digital assets were once mostly associated with startups, retail investors and crypto exchanges. Today, the market increasingly includes:
- major banks
- asset management firms
- ETF products
- institutional treasury models
- and regulated blockchain-based financial products.

It increasingly appears that crypto is becoming part of the traditional financial system rather than remaining only an alternative sector outside conventional banking.

# Our view (Metaadvisor):

- European banks clearly no longer ignore the crypto market the way they did a few years ago.
- The focus on Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs shows that institutions currently prefer a more regulated approach to digital assets.
- It is interesting that Intesa Sanpaolo is almost fully exiting Solana while increasing exposure to BTC, ETH and XRP.
- The crypto market increasingly looks like it may split into institutional and retail segments over the long term.
- Moves like this from major banks could further accelerate institutional crypto adoption across Europe.
