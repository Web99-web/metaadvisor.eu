# Japan: SBI, Rakuten and Nomura launch crypto funds without wallets

Major Japanese financial institutions are preparing a new wave of institutional and retail entry into the crypto market. According to reports from several financial and crypto media outlets, some of Japan’s largest brokerages and financial groups are developing crypto investment trusts and funds connected to digital assets.

The main names involved reportedly include:
- SBI Securities
- Rakuten Securities
- Nomura
- Daiwa Securities
- SMBC Group
- and Mizuho Financial Group.

This story is especially interesting because Japan has long been known as one of the most strictly regulated financial markets when it comes to crypto. That is precisely why moves by major Japanese financial institutions are being seen as another sign that institutional adoption of digital assets is entering a new phase.

## Crypto investing without wallets or exchanges

The biggest change these products could bring is the ability for ordinary investors to gain crypto exposure without:
- opening crypto wallets
- managing seed phrases
- using crypto exchanges
- or having advanced blockchain knowledge.

Instead, investments could be made directly through existing brokerage accounts and mobile apps already used for traditional financial products.

SBI Securities reportedly plans to develop and distribute products through its own group company SBI Global Asset Management, while Rakuten is preparing a model integrated directly into its mobile investment applications.

## Bitcoin and Ethereum remain the main focus

According to reports, the first products will likely focus on more liquid and institutionally accepted digital assets such as Bitcoin and Ethereum.

That suggests major financial players still prefer a more regulated and conservative approach to digital assets instead of aggressively moving into riskier altcoin projects.

Interestingly, this story appears almost simultaneously with reports about European banking giant Intesa Sanpaolo significantly increasing its crypto exposure through Bitcoin, Ethereum and XRP ETF products.

{{< support2 >}}

## Japan is preparing a regulatory framework for crypto funds

According to reports, Japan’s Financial Services Agency (FSA) is preparing regulatory changes that could formally allow funds directly holding crypto assets by 2028.

That could significantly change how Japanese retail investors access digital assets.

Japan was already among the first major countries to formally regulate Bitcoin and crypto exchanges following several major security incidents over the past decade. Because of that, moves by Japanese regulators and financial institutions are often closely watched far beyond Asia.

## Crypto is increasingly becoming part of the traditional financial system

Over recent months, there have been growing signs that the crypto market is gradually integrating into the traditional financial system:
- Bitcoin ETFs in the United States
- European banks increasing crypto exposure
- Japanese brokerages preparing retail crypto funds
- tokenization of assets
- stablecoin infrastructure
- and institutional treasury models.

It increasingly appears that the future of crypto may become a combination of blockchain technology and traditional finance rather than a completely separate parallel system as many expected only a few years ago.

# Our view (Metaadvisor):

- Japan is one of the most important markets for institutional crypto adoption because of its strict regulatory environment.
- Products like these could introduce crypto exposure to millions of new retail investors who have never used a wallet or crypto exchange.
- The focus on Bitcoin and Ethereum shows that institutions currently prefer more stable and regulated digital assets.
- Crypto increasingly appears to be integrating into the existing financial system rather than fully replacing it.
- If Japan officially opens the door to crypto investment trusts, the impact could extend far beyond Japan into the broader Asian market.
