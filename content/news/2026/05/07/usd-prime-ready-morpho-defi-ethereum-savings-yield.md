# USD Prime: Ready and Morpho Want to Turn Ethereum Into a DeFi Savings Account

Crypto app Ready has unveiled a major platform redesign and new earning opportunities for users, with the spotlight currently focused on **USD Prime — a product developed in collaboration with the Morpho protocol**.

At first glance, this may look like just another DeFi yield product.

But in reality, the story is much bigger.

👉 Ready and Morpho are trying to turn Ethereum into something that feels more like a modern savings account than a traditional DeFi experience.

According to Ready’s announcement, users can now open the “Invest” section, choose USD Prime and begin earning yield on their USD balances within seconds.

Without:
* complex wallets
* multiple applications
* manually using DeFi protocols
* advanced technical knowledge

And that may be one of the most important trends in the next generation of crypto apps.

## DeFi Is Becoming Invisible

For years, decentralized finance was mostly reserved for technically advanced users.

Using DeFi often required understanding:
* MetaMask
* gas fees
* bridges
* liquidity pools
* lending protocols
* security risks

For average users, it felt complicated and intimidating.

But Ready and Morpho are trying to completely change that approach.

The idea is no longer:
👉 “learn how to use DeFi”

Instead, it becomes:
👉 “tap Invest and watch your USD grow”.

In other words, the DeFi infrastructure is slowly disappearing from the visible part of the user experience.

## Morpho Is Becoming a Key Player in the New DeFi Era

The partnership with Morpho is also no coincidence.

In recent months, Morpho has increasingly positioned itself as one of the most interesting next-generation DeFi protocols, especially in areas such as:
* lending markets
* yield optimization
* capital efficiency
* simplified user experience

The goal is no longer just attracting hardcore crypto users.

👉 The goal is bringing DeFi to mainstream audiences.

That includes:
* easier onboarding
* mobile-first apps
* lower costs
* simple investing
* experiences similar to modern fintech platforms

{{< support1 >}}

## Ethereum as a Savings Account?

Perhaps the most interesting part of the story is the changing perception of Ethereum itself.

For years, Ethereum was seen by most people as:
* a blockchain network
* a smart contract platform
* a place for NFTs and DeFi experiments

But products like USD Prime are trying to present Ethereum in a completely different way.

👉 As infrastructure for digital savings and passive yield.

That represents a major psychological shift.

Users may no longer even think about:
* what a blockchain is
* how lending protocols work
* where liquidity comes from
* how smart contracts function

They simply see:
* a USD account
* yield
* a mobile app
* a simple experience

And that could become the key to mainstream crypto adoption.

## Fintech and DeFi Are Blending Together

Over the past year, the line between:
* fintech apps
* digital banking
* stablecoins
* DeFi

has become increasingly blurred.

Many new crypto apps no longer want to look like “crypto”.

They want to feel:
* simple
* secure
* mobile
* familiar

In other words:

👉 the crypto industry is trying to hide its own complexity.

{{< support2 >}}

## Yield Is Becoming the New Fintech Battleground

Yield-generating stablecoin products are becoming one of the biggest trends of 2026.

More and more projects are trying to offer users:
* passive USD yield
* alternatives to traditional savings
* simple access to DeFi markets
* lower transaction costs

What’s especially interesting is that younger users increasingly no longer distinguish between:
* a fintech app
* a crypto wallet
* a digital bank account

If an app allows:
* simple money transfers
* savings
* yield
* cards
* mobile usability

👉 many users simply no longer care whether the backend uses a blockchain or a bank.

## Our Take (Metaadvisor):

* Ready and Morpho are trying to hide DeFi complexity behind a simple mobile experience
* USD Prime shows how crypto apps are increasingly starting to resemble modern fintech platforms
* Ethereum is gradually evolving into infrastructure for digital savings and passive yield
* The future of DeFi may belong not to the most technical apps — but to the simplest ones
* The “tap and earn” model could become the key to mainstream crypto finance adoption
