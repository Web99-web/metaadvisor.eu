# Injective (INJ) rises again after a strong breakout

The cryptocurrency Injective (INJ) has once again attracted market attention after a very strong price rally and a breakout from a multi-month consolidation zone.

Charts are showing a sharp increase in trading volume, stronger momentum and a continuation toward higher levels after the price broke through several important technical resistance zones.

## Volatility is increasing again

INJ had been relatively quiet compared to some other altcoins in recent months, but market activity has increased significantly over the past few days.

Traders are now closely watching:
- trading volume
- funding rates
- open interest
- price behavior around key breakout levels

Such combinations often attract short-term momentum traders and increase volatility across the market.
