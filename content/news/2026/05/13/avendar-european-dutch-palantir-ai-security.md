# Avendar: Dutch AI security startup building Europe’s answer to Palantir

While much of the AI industry remains focused on chatbots, image generators and virtual assistants, another part of the artificial intelligence sector is rapidly growing in Europe — AI for security, investigations and advanced data analysis.

One of the startups currently attracting significant attention in the European tech scene is Dutch company Avendar, which develops AI systems for fraud detection, cybercrime investigations and large-scale data analysis for government institutions and security organizations.

After securing a €2.2 million investment round, many observers have already started describing Avendar as a potential “European Palantir.”

## Europe is increasingly talking about digital sovereignty

In recent years, Europe has increasingly used the term “digital sovereignty.” The idea is that European countries want greater control over:
- their own AI systems
- cloud infrastructure
- government data
- cybersecurity
- critical digital tools

Some European politicians and investors believe the continent should not become fully dependent on American technology companies when it comes to sensitive state data and security infrastructure.

That is one of the main reasons why startups like Avendar are becoming increasingly interesting to European investors and institutions.

## Why is Avendar being compared to Palantir?

American company Palantir has spent years building software for governments, police departments, defense agencies and intelligence organizations capable of analyzing enormous amounts of data.

Its systems are used for:
- connecting different data sources
- identifying suspicious patterns
- analyzing criminal networks
- supporting investigations
- financial monitoring

Avendar is attempting to build a similar concept, but within a European regulatory and legal framework.

According to available reports, the startup is already working with Dutch police, the City of Amsterdam and programs linked to the Dutch Ministry of Justice and Security.

## AI for investigations and fraud detection is becoming a major industry

AI is no longer just about generating text or creating images.

An increasing amount of investment capital is now moving into:
- AI security
- cybercrime investigations
- AML systems
- fraud detection
- large-scale data analytics
- automated intelligence systems

AML stands for Anti-Money Laundering — systems designed to detect suspicious financial activity and potential money laundering operations.

Government institutions today process enormous amounts of information that humans alone often cannot efficiently analyze anymore. This is where AI systems are increasingly finding their role.

Avendar is developing platforms designed to:
- accelerate investigations
- connect fragmented data
- automatically identify suspicious behavior
- support institutional decision-making

## Europe is trying to build its own AI model

Unlike the United States or China, Europe traditionally applies much stricter rules regarding privacy and data processing.

Because of this, European AI companies often try to balance:
- security
- efficiency
- GDPR regulations
- citizen privacy
- AI transparency

This could eventually become one of the biggest differences between European and American AI platforms.

While many American companies prioritize speed and market dominance, Europe is attempting to build AI systems that operate more closely within regulatory and privacy frameworks.

## AI security could become one of Europe’s largest future tech sectors

Although Avendar is still a relatively small startup, the fact that it is already working with government institutions demonstrates how quickly demand for AI security systems is growing in Europe.

If Europe continues investing heavily in domestic AI infrastructure and digital sovereignty, companies like Avendar could become an important part of the continent’s future technology ecosystem.

At the same time, major concerns are also emerging:
- how much access AI should have to personal data
- where security ends and surveillance begins
- how governments may use AI systems to monitor citizens
- whether AI systems can produce dangerous false conclusions

One thing is becoming increasingly clear: AI security and data analysis are evolving into a much larger market than many people expected only a few years ago.

Metaadvisor.eu believes this trend may become one of the most important long-term developments in the European AI industry as governments search for more advanced digital investigation and security systems.

## Our Take (Metaadvisor)

- Avendar shows that Europe is trying to build its own AI security ecosystem.
- The AI industry is increasingly shifting toward investigations, cybersecurity and large-scale data analysis.
- Digital sovereignty is becoming a major strategic topic in Europe.
- AI for fraud detection and AML could become one of the fastest-growing European AI sectors.
- At the same time, privacy and AI surveillance concerns are likely to grow significantly.
