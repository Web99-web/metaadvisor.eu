# NEAR Extends AI Rally: Is the $5 Target Realistic or Is the Market Already Entering FOMO?

**NEAR extends its AI rally: why analysts mention $5 while the price has not yet broken above $3**

NEAR Protocol has once again caught the attention of the crypto market after a strong move that fits into the broader recovery of AI-related tokens. In a short period of time, NEAR recorded a very strong price movement, and the market connected that rise with a combination of the AI narrative, optimism around Nvidia, technical upgrades to the NEAR network and liquidations of short positions.

The headline about a possible $5 target sounds attractive, but it should be read very carefully. NEAR has not yet broken firmly above $3, so $5 is currently more of a bullish scenario than a confirmed direction. For that target to become more serious, the price would first need to move convincingly above $3, maintain strong volume and show that the rally is not only short-term FOMO.

## Why Did NEAR Rise?

The first reason is AI momentum. The market has started buying tokens connected to artificial intelligence again, and NEAR has been trying for some time to position itself as infrastructure for AI agents, autonomous applications and a digital economy in which software can perform tasks independently.

The second reason is broader technology optimism. When positive sentiment appears around Nvidia and the AI sector, part of that mood often spills over into crypto projects that have an AI story. That does not mean every AI token has the same value, but in such phases the market often buys the narrative first and only later checks how ready the technology really is.

The third reason is technical upgrades to the NEAR network. NEAR is developing solutions focused on privacy, AI integration and scaling. This part gives the story a more serious foundation, because the rise is not connected only to hype, but also to an attempt to make the network better prepared for higher traffic and more complex applications.

## What Is a Shard and Why Is Dynamic Resharding Being Mentioned?

A shard is a separate part of a blockchain network that processes part of the transactions and data. Instead of the entire network doing everything in one place, the work is divided into several smaller parts. It is like a supermarket with multiple cash registers: when there is no crowd, one register is enough, but when many customers arrive, additional registers are opened.

In a blockchain, those additional “cash registers” are shards. Their purpose is to allow the network to process more traffic without congestion. This matters because blockchains often become slower and more expensive when the number of users, applications and transactions suddenly increases.

Dynamic resharding means that the number of those shards can adapt to traffic. If demand rises, the network can add more capacity. If traffic is lower, it does not have to maintain an oversized system all the time. For NEAR, this is important because the network wants to show that it can scale without constant manual adjustment.

In the AI context, that sounds especially interesting. If AI agents one day frequently pay for data, buy computing resources, use applications or send microtransactions, a network will need a lot of capacity for a large number of small and fast operations. That is why the market reads dynamic resharding not only as a technical upgrade, but as part of broader AI infrastructure.

## Is $5 a Realistic Target?

The $5 target is being mentioned because NEAR has strongly broken through previous levels and because the market in such phases quickly starts looking for the next major psychological target. But the distance between levels below $3 and $5 is still large. This would not be a small continuation of the move, but an ambitious scenario.

For such a scenario to open, NEAR would first need to move convincingly above $3, keep buyers involved, avoid sudden profit-taking and show that volume is not fading. If the price rises only because people do not want to “miss the train,” the rally can easily turn into a short-term pump followed by a cooldown.

That is why the question in the title matters: has the market started valuing real infrastructure for AI applications, or is it simply buying the next popular story? The answer is probably not black and white. NEAR has concrete technical elements, but after a strong rally there is always some FOMO buying as well.

## Our View (Metaadvisor)

NEAR is currently interesting because it combines three things the market loves: an AI narrative, a technical upgrade and a strong chart. That is a combination that can quickly attract attention, especially when headlines about a possible $5 target appear.

Still, the fact that $5 is being mentioned while the price has not even broken above $3 shows how quickly the crypto market moves from caution to optimism. Such targets can be useful as scenarios, but they should not be read as guarantees.

The most important point is to understand why NEAR could be different from a simple hype story. Shards and dynamic resharding are not just attractive technical terms, but an attempt to make a blockchain network more scalable. If AI applications and autonomous agents really do create many transactions, this kind of infrastructure could become important.

For smaller investors, the risk is clear: NEAR may have a good story, but after a strong rally, the entry becomes much more sensitive. The technology is worth watching, but the price should not be chased only because the market has rediscovered AI tokens.
