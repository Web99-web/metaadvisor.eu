# US Senate Releases Final CLARITY Act Draft

The crypto market reacted strongly today after the US Senate Banking Committee released the final draft of the long-awaited CLARITY Act — legislation that could fundamentally reshape how cryptocurrencies are regulated in the United States.

Although the bill has been discussed for months, today’s release is being viewed as the biggest concrete step forward so far because the full legislative text has now been published ahead of the official markup vote scheduled for May 14.

Across social media, crypto influencers and major industry companies are already describing the moment as historic, while part of the market believes the United States may finally be moving toward the regulatory framework that Wall Street and institutional investors have been waiting for.

## What exactly is the CLARITY Act?

The CLARITY Act attempts to answer one of the biggest questions in the crypto industry — who actually regulates cryptocurrencies in the United States?

For years there has been tension between the SEC (Securities and Exchange Commission) and the CFTC (Commodity Futures Trading Commission), while many crypto companies argued that regulatory rules remained unclear and inconsistent.

During the Joe Biden administration and under former SEC Chair Gary Gensler, large parts of the crypto industry accused the SEC of trying to regulate digital assets primarily through lawsuits instead of through clearly written laws.

As a result, some companies even considered moving operations outside the United States toward more crypto-friendly jurisdictions.

## What does the new draft include?

The newly released draft spans 309 pages and defines several key areas of the crypto market in much greater detail.

Some of the most important topics include:
- stablecoin regulation
- AML and KYC requirements
- definitions of decentralized platforms
- tokenization of financial assets
- fundraising rules for crypto projects
- division of authority between the SEC and CFTC

One of the most controversial areas involves stablecoin “yield,” meaning rewards and interest offered to users for holding stablecoins.

According to reports, the banking sector strongly lobbied against such models due to fears that stablecoins could pull deposits away from traditional banks.

## The crypto industry sees a “major step forward”

Coinbase, the Blockchain Association, Solana Policy Institute and several other major organizations reacted positively to the publication of the draft.

Many believe the CLARITY Act could:
- allow larger institutional participation in crypto
- reduce regulatory uncertainty
- further open the door to Wall Street
- accelerate tokenization of financial assets

Some analysts believe the legislation could become one of the most important regulatory moments in the history of the US crypto market.

Many industry participants also point out that institutional investors have been waiting for clearer rules before increasing exposure to digital assets.

## The bill is still not finalized

Despite the massive hype across crypto social media, it is important to emphasize that the CLARITY Act has not yet become law.

The next steps include:
- the Senate Banking Committee markup on May 14
- potential amendments and revisions
- additional Senate votes
- possible political conflicts between Republicans and Democrats

Several controversial topics remain unresolved, including:
- stablecoins
- DeFi regulation
- SEC authority
- the relationship between traditional banks and crypto markets

Because of this, some analysts warn that the bill could still face major resistance before final approval.

## Wall Street is waiting for regulatory clarity

Still, the market clearly shows that major investors want a more stable regulatory framework.

Spot Bitcoin ETFs have attracted billions of dollars in recent months, while banks and investment funds are becoming increasingly active in digital assets.

Many within the crypto industry believe the CLARITY Act could open the door to a new wave of institutional adoption and bring the crypto sector closer to the traditional financial system.

Some even believe that 2026 could become the year when crypto finally transitions from a “Wild West” industry into a fully regulated financial market.
