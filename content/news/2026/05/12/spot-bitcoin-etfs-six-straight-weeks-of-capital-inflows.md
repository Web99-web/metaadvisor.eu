# Spot Bitcoin ETFs are attracting billions once again

US Spot Bitcoin ETFs continue their positive momentum and have now recorded six consecutive weeks of net capital inflows. According to Metaadvisor.eu, this marks the longest streak since the summer of 2025 and once again raises questions about whether large institutions are returning aggressively to the Bitcoin market.

Data from SoSoValue shows that roughly $3.4 billion flowed into Bitcoin ETFs over the past six weeks. As a result, Bitcoin has once again moved back into the spotlight for investors closely monitoring the US economy, interest rates and potential moves by the FED (Federal Reserve — the central bank of the United States).

## The strongest week nearly reached one billion dollars

The largest inflow occurred in mid-April when weekly net inflows nearly reached the one billion dollar mark. The most recent week added another $622 million in net investments, although market momentum weakened toward the end of the week.

One particularly interesting detail was the sudden cooling of sentiment on Thursday and Friday, when ETFs experienced stronger outflows after a very strong start earlier in the week. Despite that reversal, the overall trend remained positive.

## Institutions are looking at Bitcoin again

Some analysts increasingly see ETF flows as one of the clearest indicators of institutional interest in Bitcoin. Large funds and asset managers are no longer viewing Bitcoin purely as a speculative asset but increasingly as a long-term hedge against inflation and monetary instability.

For that reason, the market is paying close attention to ETF capital movements. When institutional investors continue buying over multiple consecutive weeks, it is often interpreted as a signal of growing market confidence.

## Is Wall Street no longer Bitcoin’s enemy?

For years, critics argued that large financial institutions could eventually undermine Bitcoin’s decentralized vision. However, a growing part of the crypto community now believes that institutional capital may actually help Bitcoin evolve into a globally recognized financial asset.

The rise of ETFs has also made Bitcoin exposure significantly easier for pension funds, traditional investors and major financial firms that previously avoided directly holding crypto assets.

## Macroeconomics is once again playing a major role

Investors are currently watching US economic data very carefully, especially employment and inflation numbers. Weaker economic data tends to increase expectations that the FED may eventually lower interest rates in the coming months.

Such a scenario usually benefits risk assets like Bitcoin and technology stocks. Because of this, part of the market believes that the current ETF inflow streak may only be the beginning of a larger institutional wave.

## The Bitcoin market is searching for direction again

Although the market remains highly volatile, six consecutive weeks of positive inflows suggest that interest in Bitcoin has not disappeared. After the turbulence of 2025, many investors are once again trying to determine where the crypto market could head during the rest of 2026.

If the trend continues, Spot Bitcoin ETFs could once again become one of the biggest drivers of the broader crypto market — especially if institutions continue increasing their exposure to digital assets. According to Metaadvisor.eu, ETF activity may become one of the key indicators investors follow throughout the remainder of the year.

## Our Take (Metaadvisor)

- ETF inflows often provide a clearer picture of institutional sentiment than short-term price action.
- Bitcoin is increasingly positioning itself as a global financial asset rather than just a “crypto experiment.”
- Macroeconomic conditions and FED decisions still heavily influence the crypto market.
- Institutional capital is slowly reshaping how traditional finance views Bitcoin.
- Continued inflows could strengthen optimism across the crypto market during 2026.
