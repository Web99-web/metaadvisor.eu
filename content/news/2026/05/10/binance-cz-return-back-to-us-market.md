# CZ claims rival exchanges tried to block Binance’s return to the US

Binance founder Changpeng “CZ” Zhao has once again sparked major attention across the crypto industry after claiming that rival US crypto exchanges allegedly tried to block his presidential pardon because they fear Binance returning to the American market.

During an interview with Crypto Banter, CZ said he believes there was strong “anti-lobbying” against his pardon request, although he admitted he has no direct evidence. According to Zhao, some American crypto companies are worried that a full Binance comeback could dramatically shift the balance of power inside the US digital asset market.

The comments came only days after CZ openly discussed the possible strengthening of Binance.US during Consensus Miami in an effort to restore American users’ access to global crypto liquidity.

## Binance wants a stronger return to the US

According to CZ, American users currently do not have access to the best global liquidity available outside the United States. That is why Binance is now exploring multiple options to strengthen Binance.US and potentially restore a much larger presence in the American market.

“The best liquidity in crypto is outside of the U.S.,” CZ said during the Miami conference.

In other words:
👉 Binance believes American users currently receive worse trading conditions than much of the rest of the world.

That is a very important message because liquidity remains one of Binance’s greatest advantages. The exchange still dominates a large portion of global crypto trading volume, and many traders believe Binance offers:
- the best spreads
- the deepest liquidity
- the largest token selection
- and one of the strongest trading infrastructures globally.

## Why would competitors be worried?

If Binance seriously re-enters the US market, competition could become significantly more aggressive for companies such as:
- Coinbase
- Kraken
- Gemini
- and other American crypto exchanges.

After regulatory problems and Binance’s $4.3 billion settlement with US authorities, Binance.US lost much of its market strength. CZ stepped down as CEO in 2023, pleaded guilty to violations of certain US regulations and later served a four-month prison sentence during 2024.

But the situation now appears to be changing again.

Binance.US is once again attempting to expand its business, including derivatives and prediction markets, while CZ openly says the US regulatory climate has become far more favorable toward crypto than it was several years ago.

## The Trump administration changed the tone toward crypto

One of the most interesting aspects of the story is that CZ is now openly praising the current US crypto policy environment. During his appearance, he said the United States is now “leading the world in crypto policy,” especially following new legislative proposals such as the CLARITY Act.

According to CZ, many developers and crypto companies left the US over the past few years because of regulatory pressure and moved to:
- Abu Dhabi
- Hong Kong
- Singapore
- and other crypto-friendly jurisdictions.

Now, parts of the industry believe that trend may begin reversing.

That helps explain why many major crypto companies are aggressively trying to:
- secure licenses
- expand their regulatory footprint
- and institutionalize operations.

## The battle for the US crypto market is only beginning

What once looked like the “Wild West” of crypto increasingly resembles a battle between major financial companies fighting for control over future digital infrastructure.

This is no longer just about ordinary crypto exchanges.

Today, the fight involves:
- stablecoin payments
- institutional custody
- derivatives markets
- crypto banking
- ETF infrastructure
- and global access to liquidity.

In that context, Binance potentially returning to the US becomes much bigger than the story of a single company.

It becomes a question of:
👉 who will control the future global crypto infrastructure.

## Binance no longer looks like a normal exchange

Over the years, Binance has evolved from a standard crypto exchange into a global crypto ecosystem that includes:
- trading
- wallet infrastructure
- stablecoins
- staking
- launchpad projects
- payments
- and institutional services.

That is likely why competitors continue watching every attempt Binance makes to strengthen its US position again.

Because a serious Binance return to the United States could significantly reshape the balance of power across the entire crypto industry.
