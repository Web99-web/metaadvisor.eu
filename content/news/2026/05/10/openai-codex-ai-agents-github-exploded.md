# OpenAI Codex explodes as AI coding agents enter a new phase

OpenAI Codex installation numbers have attracted massive attention across developer communities and AI social media after viral screenshots revealed growth figures that appear almost unreal.

According to screenshots circulating on LinkedIn and X, installations of the OpenAI Codex package reportedly jumped from around 5.7 million to more than 163 million within just one week.

While some experts caution that such statistics may include automated systems, CI/CD pipelines, cloud deployments and dependency installs, the explosion of interest in AI coding tools is becoming impossible to ignore.

What looked like an experiment just one or two years ago is now increasingly turning into a completely new way of building software.

## AI is no longer just a chatbot

The biggest shift is that AI tools are no longer limited to conversation or text generation.

New AI coding agents are now entering:
- development workflows
- terminals
- GitHub repositories
- DevOps systems
- debugging
- deployment automation
- and real-world project editing.

In other words:
👉 AI is no longer simply “advising” developers.

AI is increasingly beginning to directly perform parts of development work itself.

That is why interest has exploded around:
- OpenAI Codex
- Cursor
- Claude Code
- Windsurf
- Devin
- GitHub Copilot
- and other AI agent systems.

## The numbers may not be perfectly “clean” — but the trend is real

Many developers point out that 163 million installs likely does not mean that many humans manually installed Codex.

Such statistics often include:
- automated systems
- build servers
- Docker containers
- cloud infrastructure
- enterprise pipelines
- dependency refreshes
- and various CI/CD processes.

But even so:
➡️ the trend is extremely clear.

AI coding tools are rapidly becoming one of the fastest-growing segments of the entire AI industry.

## Big tech companies are already fighting for AI developers

Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and numerous startups are currently locked in a massive race to dominate the future AI development tooling market.

The reason is simple:
👉 developers are the heart of the future AI economy.

Whoever controls the AI coding workflow:

- cloud usage
- API economies
- enterprise infrastructure
- and future AI agents.

That is why billions of dollars are now flowing into:
- AI coding models

Many analysts believe AI coding tools may become some of the first AI products to truly replace portions of everyday work at scale.
