# What Does Grok (xAI) Think About MetaAdvisor.eu? Honest AI Review of the Project

MetaAdvisor.eu is an independent media project focused on artificial intelligence, cryptocurrency, blockchain, and emerging technologies. From the very beginning, the goal of the project has been simple: explain complex topics in a practical and understandable way that larger groups of people can follow — not just AI, crypto, and financial experts.

Over the last few months, MetaAdvisor has actively explored AI tools, their capabilities, limitations, mistakes, and the way they understand technology, crypto markets, and everyday topics that interest ordinary users.

That is why we decided to run an interesting experiment: ask Grok, the AI model developed by xAI and connected to the X platform, what it thinks about the MetaAdvisor.eu project itself. The question was asked completely naturally — in Croatian. And that turned out to be one of the most interesting parts of the entire conversation.

![](/images/companies/we-asked-Grok-about-Metaadvisor.eu-1.png)

## Grok Surprised Us With Its Understanding of Croatian

The first thing that genuinely surprised us was how well Grok understands the Croatian language.

The conversation flowed naturally from the start, without any need to switch to English. The AI understood the context of the questions, analyzed specific articles, provided detailed answers, and even recognized the difference between hype-driven topics and practical content that can genuinely help ordinary users.

For smaller languages like Croatian, this is still relatively rare among many AI models, which is why the quality of the responses positively surprised us. The conversation itself lasted quite a while — these are only summaries.

Grok described MetaAdvisor as:

> **“a solid small independent project”**

and stated that the project attempts to:

* explain complex topics clearly and simply
* make AI and crypto easier to understand for ordinary people
* avoid the aggressive hype approach often seen in this space

## What Did Grok Like About MetaAdvisor?

According to Grok, the project has real potential and several clear advantages compared to typical AI and crypto news websites.

**It particularly highlighted the focus on explaining complicated topics to ordinary people without the aggressive “guru” style often seen in the crypto industry. It also positively mentioned the multilingual approach — publishing content in English, Croatian, and German.**

**Positive points:**

The project is independent — funded through BuyMeACoffee donations (without major sponsors or aggressive advertising).

It covers current topics (scams, AI news, regulations, security...).

It has a presence on Instagram, TikTok, X, and Facebook under the same name.

The website design looks clean, without excessive advertising or suspicious pop-ups.

There are no signs that the site is a scam or fake website (according to Grok).

Among the articles Grok specifically highlighted were:

* “Operation Atlantic” — a major crypto permission scam that affected more than 20,000 users
* articles about the EURC stablecoin and the growth of the digital euro in Europe
* stories about AI risks, including the case of an AI agent deleting an entire database within seconds
* analyses of major Bitcoin predictions, such as Robert Kiyosaki’s claim that BTC could eventually reach $750,000

According to Grok, these are exactly the types of topics that provide the most value for “ordinary” users trying to understand what truly matters — and what is simply hype.

## Would Grok Recommend MetaAdvisor.eu?

The answer was — yes.

**Grok stated that it would recommend MetaAdvisor to:**

* readers in Europe who prefer content in their own language
* people looking for practical scam and security warnings
* beginners who want to understand AI and crypto without heavy technical jargon

Interestingly, Grok also pointed out that the website feels authentic and useful precisely because it attempts to explain complex subjects without overly technical language.

At the same time, it mentioned that the project still feels like an independent media project in development rather than a large corporate news platform — which is actually completely true.

## Areas That Still Need Improvement

Since we asked for honest feedback, Grok was direct here as well.

The biggest weaknesses it pointed out were:

* the generic appearance of the website
* a homepage that feels visually “flat”
* lack of stronger branding and visual identity
* the need for better workflow organization and more efficient content production

One particularly interesting observation was that MetaAdvisor already has a large amount of content and custom visuals, but the content itself is not yet fully “packaged” through stronger layout and visual hierarchy.

And honestly — we agree with some of those comments.

The project developed organically through everyday research into AI, blockchain, crypto markets, and emerging technologies.

## How Did MetaAdvisor Actually Start?

MetaAdvisor did not start as a traditional crypto news portal or marketing project.

The project originally started from personal curiosity and the need to better understand the world of artificial intelligence, blockchain, and cryptocurrency. At first, many topics felt confusing — filled with technical jargon, abbreviations, and hype that ordinary people often struggle to understand.

Through years of research, countless questions, ups and downs — including losses on certain projects that were not fully understood at the time — the idea emerged that this type of content might also help others.

Today, after hundreds of hours of research and daily monitoring of the market and AI industry, MetaAdvisor does not try to act like an “instant expert” platform, but instead attempts to explain what is really happening in the world of technology and digital finance in a simpler and more understandable way.

## What Happens Next?

Following this conversation with Grok, the project will focus on:

* improving the website design and homepage layout
* building a stronger visual identity
* creating more efficient workflows for content and visuals
* continuing to publish practical and useful topics without excessive hype
