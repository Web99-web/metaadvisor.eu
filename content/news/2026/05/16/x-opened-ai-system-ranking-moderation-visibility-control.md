# X Opened Its AI System: Ranking, Moderation and Visibility Control Are Now Public

## X opened the “black box”: AI now decides what the internet sees — and the internet may never be the same again

Elon Musk announced that part of the X recommendation system is now available as an open-source project on GitHub. At first glance, many people saw it as just another developer repository, but a deeper analysis reveals something far bigger than a typical “social media algorithm.”

The published code shows how modern platforms have evolved into complex AI systems that:
- analyze content
- evaluate post quality
- moderate comments
- analyze audio and video
- evaluate trust signals
- and decide content visibility.

## AI no longer counts only likes and engagement

One of the most interesting parts of the system is called:

`BangerInitialScreenClassifier`

Its job is to evaluate whether a post has the potential to become interesting, high-quality or viral. The code shows that AI:
- analyzes text
- reviews images
- assigns quality scores
- categorizes topics
- evaluates “AI slop”
- and generates its own reasoning explaining why content may be considered high quality or problematic. Interesting, isn’t it?

In other words, the recommendation engine no longer functions only as a mathematical engagement calculator. AI is genuinely trying to understand content.

## “AI slop” is becoming a new internet problem

One of the most fascinating details in the code is:

`slop_score`

This suggests the platform is already attempting to evaluate whether content is:
- generic
- spammy
- low effort
- or mass AI-generated.

In other words, one AI is now trying to recognize another AI’s content. This could become one of the internet’s biggest topics over the next few years as social networks become increasingly flooded with:
- AI images
- AI videos
- AI articles
- AI bots
- and automatically generated comments.

## AI moderation is becoming a multi-layered system

The published code also reveals a highly sophisticated moderation pipeline. Content goes through:
- safety screening
- spam classification
- policy analysis
- trust scoring
- reply ranking
- and multimodal understanding.

What is especially interesting is that the system does not analyze only a single post, but also:
- the user
- account history
- previous posts
- safety signals
- and reputation data.

This means AI decisions are no longer necessarily tied to just one post. The platform may be evaluating an entire user “trust profile.”

## AI now listens to video content as well

One module also reveals an Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) pipeline. This means AI:
- extracts audio from videos
- converts speech into text
- and then integrates the transcript into recommendation and moderation systems.

In other words, AI no longer analyzes only captions or hashtags. It can also analyze what a person is actually saying in a video.

## The internet is becoming an “embedding space” — AI is trying to understand the true meaning of content

Perhaps the most important part of the system involves multimodal embeddings. Posts are transformed into:
- semantic vectors
- AI representations of meaning
- and content “fingerprints.”

This allows the recommendation engine to:
- understand topics
- connect similar content
- find relevant posts
- and recommend content even without matching hashtags or keywords.

In other words, AI is trying to understand the real meaning behind posts.

## Small accounts are also going through AI evaluation

Another very interesting detail involves the so-called “Min Traction” pipeline. This suggests X has dedicated AI processes for:
- small accounts
- low-engagement posts
- and content that has not yet become popular.
- They still haven’t discovered us even though we’re trying.

AI is essentially attempting to determine whether a post has the potential to become a “banger.” This is a major shift compared to older social networks where reach depended almost entirely on account size. And honestly, this part is fascinating because it could finally give smaller creators a chance to be discovered.

## AI is no longer just a tool

What this entire open-source project actually shows is that social networks are entering a completely new phase.

Today AI:
- reads
- listens
- interprets
- ranks
- moderates
- evaluates trust
- and decides visibility.

In other words, the internet is increasingly becoming an AI-mediated system. This is already the next generation of the internet.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)

• Recommendation algorithms are no longer just mathematical engagement systems.
• Modern social networks are evolving into AI-mediated visibility management systems.
• AI is now attempting to understand the true meaning behind content — not just hashtags and keywords.
• Audio, video and images are increasingly becoming part of recommendation pipelines.
• The future of the internet will increasingly depend on AI interpretation and trust signals.
