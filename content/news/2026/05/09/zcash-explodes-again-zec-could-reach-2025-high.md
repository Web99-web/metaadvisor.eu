# Zcash explodes again: ZEC could reach its November 2025 high

Zcash (ZEC) is once again drawing attention across the crypto market after a powerful rally pushed the price from below 200 USDT to more than 600 USDT within just a few weeks.

On some futures exchanges, ZEC briefly moved above 640 USDT, while the market now faces increasing volatility and growing pressure from leveraged futures trading.

It is one of the most aggressive rallies among older altcoins this year.

For a long time, ZEC remained outside the main market spotlight while investors focused primarily on AI tokens, Layer 2 projects and new DeFi narratives.

However, in recent days, capital appears to be rotating back into older low-supply coins with heavy leverage exposure.

## Could ZEC revisit its November 2025 high?

Traders are once again discussing the major November 2025 peak, when ZEC reached around 754 USDT on the futures market.

What made that rally particularly interesting was the fact that ZEC became one of the few older altcoins surging aggressively while much of the broader market remained relatively weak.

Even today, the chart structure looks strikingly similar:

* strong breakout
* explosive volume
* rapidly growing futures positions
* and increasing retail FOMO.

Because of this, part of the market is now trying to determine whether the current rally could retest that zone once again.

Opinions remain divided.

Some traders believe that continued short squeeze pressure could push ZEC toward previous highs, especially if Bitcoin and the broader crypto market remain stable.

Others warn that the market already looks heavily overheated and that the 700–750 USDT area could become a major resistance zone with aggressive profit-taking.

## The futures market is becoming increasingly aggressive

Daily charts now show an almost parabolic rally.

After months of consolidation, ZEC experienced a major breakout accompanied by explosive trading volume and a sharp increase in open futures positions.

Funding rates on some exchanges have turned positive, suggesting that many traders are aggressively entering long positions expecting further upside.

At the same time, large wicks on daily candles indicate that some participants are already taking profits after the rapid pump.

## Return of interest in privacy coins?

The crypto community on social media is increasingly discussing the possible return of interest in privacy-focused projects.

Zcash has long been one of the best-known privacy coins in the crypto industry, recognized for technology designed to enable more private blockchain transactions compared to traditional networks.

At the same time, that focus also exposed the project to years of regulatory pressure and gradually reduced mainstream attention.

Now some investors are asking whether ZEC can once again reclaim a position among the market’s most closely watched altcoins — or whether this is simply another short-lived wave of speculation and leveraged trading.

## The market is entering a higher-risk phase

One of the biggest problems with vertical rallies like this is that markets often become extremely sensitive to sudden changes in sentiment.

If momentum weakens or a larger wave of long-position closures begins, futures markets could quickly accelerate a correction.

That is why some analysts believe the next several days could be crucial in determining whether this is the beginning of a more sustainable ZEC comeback — or simply a temporary volatility explosion.

## Our view (Metaadvisor)

Sudden comebacks from older altcoins often attract strong retail attention because they feel like a “second chance” opportunity returning to the spotlight.

However, these types of parabolic rallies also tend to end in brutal corrections once profit-taking and overleveraged liquidations begin.

The major question now is whether ZEC can revisit the 754 USDT area from November 2025 during this rally.

If momentum remains strong, the market could attempt another major push toward that zone.

At the same time, any visible slowdown in volume or larger profit-taking activity could rapidly shift sentiment across the futures market.
