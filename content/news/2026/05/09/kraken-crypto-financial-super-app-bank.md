# Kraken wants to become a crypto financial super-app after buying Reap for $600 million

Kraken has been doing far more in recent months than simply expanding its crypto exchange business. Increasingly, the company looks like it wants to become a complete crypto financial platform — something between a bank, a payments network, a trading platform and an institutional custody provider.

The latest major step in that strategy is the acquisition of Hong Kong-based payments company Reap Technologies in a deal reportedly worth around $600 million. Reap specializes in stablecoin payments, international transfers, business-to-business payment infrastructure and connecting traditional financial systems with digital assets.

In other words:
👉 Kraken no longer wants to be just a place to buy and sell cryptocurrencies.

The company now appears focused on building a much larger financial ecosystem.

## Stablecoins are becoming the next payment infrastructure

The Reap acquisition shows how seriously major crypto firms now view the stablecoin market. Stablecoins are increasingly being used for international payments, faster settlement, business transfers and even as alternatives to parts of traditional banking systems like SWIFT.

Asia is particularly important in this strategy because stablecoin adoption across the region continues growing rapidly. Through Reap, Kraken is entering the Asian payments market in a much more aggressive way than before.

According to Kraken CEO Arjun Sethi, Asia currently represents one of the fastest-growing regions for digital assets and crypto infrastructure.

## Kraken is also trying to become a “crypto bank”

Just one day after the Reap acquisition news, another major development emerged. Kraken’s parent company Payward officially applied to the US OCC regulator (Office of the Comptroller of the Currency) for a federal trust charter license.

If approved:
👉 Kraken could become one of the first large federally regulated crypto trust institutions in the United States.

That is extremely important because large institutions:
- do not want to self-custody crypto
- require regulated custody providers
- need compliance infrastructure
- and want protections similar to traditional banking systems.

Custody is increasingly becoming one of the most important sectors within institutional crypto markets.

## Kraken is assembling a much larger financial puzzle

When all of Kraken’s recent acquisitions and strategic moves are combined, it becomes clear the company is building something far larger than a traditional exchange.

Recently, Kraken has been:
- acquiring payments companies
- expanding into derivatives
- growing institutional staking services
- building custody infrastructure
- preparing for a potential IPO
- and pursuing banking-related licenses.

{{< support1 >}}

Kraken also recently acquired Bitnomial, a crypto derivatives platform that significantly strengthened the company’s regulatory position in the US market.

At the same time, Kraken continues developing partnerships such as its collaboration with MoneyGram in an effort to connect traditional financial systems with crypto transfer infrastructure.

Taken together, all of this increasingly resembles:
➡️ an attempt to build a “crypto financial super-app.”

In other words, a platform capable of offering:
- trading
- custody
- stablecoin payments
- staking
- derivatives
- international transfers
- and institutional financial services
within a single ecosystem.

## The Trump administration opened new doors for crypto

One of the most interesting aspects of this story is the timing. Much of the crypto industry now believes the regulatory climate in the United States has become significantly more favorable toward digital assets.

As a result, many crypto firms are rushing to:
- obtain licenses
- institutionalize operations
- and expand their regulatory footprint
before the political environment potentially changes again.

That is why some analysts believe the industry is entering a completely new phase:
➡️ a transition from the “Wild West” era into serious financial infrastructure.

And Kraken appears determined to secure a leading position in that transformation.

## Crypto exchange or future digital bank?

What once looked like a standard crypto exchange is increasingly evolving into something much more complex.

Kraken no longer appears focused only on competing with Binance or Coinbase. Instead, the company seems to be building its own financial ecosystem that could eventually function as a digital crypto bank for:
- retail users
- businesses
- investment funds
- and institutional clients.

If successful, this model could significantly reshape how traditional financial institutions view the crypto sector in the future.
