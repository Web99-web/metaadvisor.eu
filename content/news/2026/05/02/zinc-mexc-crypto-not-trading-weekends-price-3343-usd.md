# Crypto that doesn’t trade on weekends? ZINC on MEXC reveals a new market reality at $3,343

There’s an unwritten rule in crypto — markets never sleep. Bitcoin, Ethereum, and most altcoins trade 24/7 without interruption. But one instrument on MEXC breaks that rule entirely, raising an important question: is everything we see on crypto exchanges truly “crypto”?

The instrument in question is ZINC (XZN). At first glance, it behaves like a typical crypto asset. It shows volatility, price swings, and chart patterns that resemble altcoins. However, there is one crucial difference — this “crypto” does not trade on weekends.

At the time of writing, ZINC is trading around $3,343. What stands out even more is its price action over recent weeks.

<img src="/images/daily-charts/zinc-xzn-02.05.2026.png" alt="ZINC XZN chart 02.05.2026" />

Since late March 2026, ZINC has consistently held above approximately $3,165, reaching a high near $3,495. This kind of movement may look like a classic altcoin pump, but the reality behind this instrument is very different.

## This is not a traditional crypto asset

Unlike most tokens, ZINC does not have its own blockchain, whitepaper, or developer community. It is not a decentralized project, but rather a derivative or synthetic instrument.

In simple terms:

👉 ZINC is a trading instrument, not a coin meant for long-term holding

It most likely tracks the price of industrial metals such as zinc, but through an interface that looks like a standard crypto exchange.

## Why it doesn’t trade on weekends

This is the key part of the story. While crypto markets operate non-stop, traditional markets follow fixed trading hours. Weekends are closed, and trading happens within defined sessions.

ZINC clearly follows that structure.

This means:

* the market closes
* no trading occurs during weekends
* it reopens at the start of the week

{{< support1 >}}

## The hidden risk: the “gap” effect

What many crypto traders don’t expect is the risk that comes with such instruments.

If the market closes and global conditions change over the weekend, the price can reopen with a sharp move up or down.

👉 without any ability to react

This is known as a “gap,” and it can be especially dangerous for leveraged positions.

Unlike traditional crypto trading, where positions can be adjusted at any time, here there is a period of complete uncertainty.

## Why the price looks “explosive”

The move from $3,165 to nearly $3,500 may look like a typical pump. However, several factors contribute to this behavior:

* lower liquidity
* leveraged trading
* fewer market participants

In such environments, even relatively small volumes can cause significant price movements.

That’s why the chart appears more aggressive than in large markets like Bitcoin.

## A new reality: crypto meets TradFi

ZINC is not an isolated case.

It reflects a broader trend:

👉 crypto exchanges are evolving into hybrid platforms combining crypto and traditional finance

Today, users can trade on the same platform:

* cryptocurrencies
* metals
* indices
* various derivatives

The line between these worlds is becoming increasingly blurred.

## What this means for traders

For users, this changes the rules of the game.

It’s no longer enough to rely solely on charts and indicators. It becomes essential to understand:

* what is actually being traded
* how the market functions
* which factors influence price movements

Without that understanding, it’s easy to misinterpret price action and make poor trading decisions.

## Not everything is “crypto” anymore

ZINC is a perfect example of how markets are evolving.

At first glance, it looks like just another coin. In reality, it behaves more like a traditional financial instrument than a decentralized crypto asset.

And that’s exactly what makes it so interesting.
