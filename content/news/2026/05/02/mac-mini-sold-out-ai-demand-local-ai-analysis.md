## An unexpected problem: Mac mini disappears from the market

Mac mini, which had long been a niche product in Apple’s lineup, has suddenly become one of the most in-demand devices. According to multiple reports, the base model with the M4 chip and 16 GB of RAM is sold out through official channels, while delivery timelines for higher configurations are being pushed back.

At the same time, secondary markets like eBay are seeing rising prices, with units selling above retail. A similar pattern was seen with GPUs during the crypto boom — but this time, it’s happening with a different category of hardware.

## Why is Mac mini suddenly in demand?

The reason is not traditional usage, but a shift in how the device is being used. More and more users are turning to Mac mini to run local AI models instead of relying on cloud services.

This trend is closely tied to growing concerns around privacy and data control. Instead of sending information to remote servers, users are increasingly looking for AI that runs directly on their own machines.

## What Mac mini actually offers (and why it matters for AI)

Apple describes the Mac mini as a “small powerhouse” — a compact desktop measuring just 12.7 × 12.7 cm, delivering strong performance through the M4 chip.

In practice, it’s a quiet and energy-efficient machine that can run continuously, making it well-suited for persistent workloads like local AI systems. A key component is the Neural Engine within Apple Silicon, designed specifically for AI processing.

With support for up to 64 GB of unified memory and multiple terabytes of storage, it can handle larger datasets and models. Modern connectivity options like Thunderbolt, HDMI, and USB-C also allow it to function both as a standard desktop and a compact home server.

## Local AI: a trend reshaping hardware demand

What sets this situation apart from previous shortages is the underlying demand. Mac mini isn’t being purchased for gaming or design — but for its ability to run AI models locally.

This includes:

* document processing without cloud dependency
* local AI assistants
* data analysis and automation

This approach is becoming especially relevant for users who want to retain control over their data while avoiding recurring cloud costs.

## Pricing varies by country: EU vs US

The price of Mac mini varies significantly by region, mainly due to differences in taxation.

In Croatia, where VAT is 25%, the entry-level model already exceeds around €800. In the United States, the base price starts at $599 — but this does not include tax.

Importantly, the US does not have a unified VAT system. Instead, each state applies its own sales tax:

* Oregon → 0%
* California → ~7–10%
* Texas → ~6–8%

This means the final price can vary depending on where the purchase is made.

## Our take

Mac mini hasn’t become scarce by accident. It’s the result of multiple converging factors — the AI boom, supply constraints, and growing interest in local data processing.

What’s particularly interesting is the shift in perception: a device originally designed as a compact desktop is now being seen as an entry point into local AI.

That said, for most users, cloud-based AI remains more practical and powerful. Local AI makes sense primarily for specific use cases — privacy, automation, and experimentation.
