# Solana news: Germany’s AllUnity expands EURAU to Solana as euro stablecoins gain traction

The European crypto market is entering a new phase of development. Germany’s **AllUnity**, backed by **DWS (part of the Deutsche Bank group)**, **Flow Traders**, and **Galaxy Digital**, has expanded its euro stablecoin EURAU to the Solana blockchain.

At first glance, this may look like a technical upgrade. In reality, it signals something much bigger — Europe’s attempt to build its own digital financial layer based on the euro.

In practical terms, this means the euro can now move across blockchain networks in seconds instead of taking days through traditional banking systems. As Metaadvisor highlights, this shift is not just technological — it’s structural.

## What is AllUnity and why it matters

**AllUnity** is not a typical crypto project. It is a regulated financial initiative designed to bridge traditional banking and blockchain infrastructure.

It is backed by:

* **DWS (part of the Deutsche Bank group)** — one of Europe’s largest asset managers
* **Flow Traders** — a global trading firm
* **Galaxy Digital** — a leading crypto investment company

Unlike many stablecoins, EURAU is:

* fully backed by real euro reserves
* issued in line with EU regulatory frameworks
* compliant with **MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets)** regulations

This makes it one of the first serious attempts to integrate crypto into the regulated financial system.

## Why Solana

Until now, EURAU was available on Ethereum. Expanding to Solana opens new possibilities.

Solana offers:

* near-instant transactions
* extremely low fees (often below $0.01)
* scalability for high-volume payments

For businesses, this means blockchain can finally be used efficiently without high costs or delays.

## What this means for real-world payments

The biggest impact is not on exchanges, but in everyday business use.

Examples include:

* companies sending cross-border payments in seconds
* freelancers receiving funds instantly
* financial institutions using euro-based liquidity on-chain

In simple terms, EURAU enables something traditional banking still struggles with — real-time global euro transfers.

## Euro vs dollar in crypto

The stablecoin market is currently dominated by the U.S. dollar, with over $300 billion in value.

Euro stablecoins remain small but are growing rapidly:

* nearly doubled since early 2025
* approaching €1 billion in market size

According to S&P projections, the market could reach €570 billion by 2030.

This raises a key strategic question:
👉 Can Europe build its own digital monetary infrastructure, or will it remain dependent on the dollar?

## Regulation as a competitive advantage

Unlike many crypto projects, AllUnity emphasizes regulatory compliance.

European policymakers have already encouraged banks to explore euro-denominated stablecoins and tokenized deposits.

This shows that the shift is not only technological, but also political and economic.

## What comes next

AllUnity plans to expand EURAU across multiple blockchains to increase adoption.

As demand for regulated euro stablecoins grows, projects like this could form the foundation of future financial systems. As Metaadvisor notes, this could redefine how payments and liquidity move across borders.
