# Bitcoin rises above $77,400, but institutional hedging and selling pressure limit further upside

Bitcoin has once again moved above the $77,400 level, but what looks like a continuation of the rally at first glance actually reveals a much more complex picture. While the price is rising, market data suggests that large players are becoming more cautious.

Over the past 24 hours, Bitcoin gained roughly 1.5% to 1.7%, with trading volume about 15% above its average. This points to steady market participation, but not necessarily to strong bullish momentum.

## Institutions are hedging against downside

One of the key signals comes from the derivatives market. A sharp increase in demand for put options (contracts that profit when the price falls) indicates that institutional investors are actively hedging against potential downside.

Specifically, there has been a notable rise in open interest near current price levels, suggesting that large market participants are:

* locking in profits  
* expecting a short-term correction  
* balancing risk after the recent move up  

This is not typical behavior of a market expecting an unchecked breakout.

## Selling pressure is building

Another signal comes from on-chain data. More than $770 million worth of Bitcoin has been sent to exchanges over the past week.

Such transfers are often seen as a pre-sale step, since Bitcoin needs to be moved to exchanges before it can be sold. This increases the likelihood of rising selling pressure in the near term.

In addition, order book data shows significant sell orders between $76,700 and $79,300, making it harder for the price to break higher.

## Technical picture: battle around key levels

From a technical perspective, Bitcoin has successfully confirmed the $75,000 level as support, which is a positive signal. It has also moved back above the 20-day moving average, which supports the bullish case in the short term.

However, the $77,000 and $79,000 levels remain key resistance zones.

For the rally to continue, the market needs:

* a stronger increase in volume  
* fresh capital inflows  
* a reduction in selling pressure  

Without these factors, any upward move may be quickly rejected.

## Bigger picture: Bitcoin follows macro trends

Interestingly, Bitcoin is currently moving almost in sync with the broader digital asset market, with minimal deviation.

This suggests that price action is not driven solely by crypto-specific factors, but also by broader macro trends such as:

* interest rate movements  
* inflation  
* global risk appetite  

In other words, Bitcoin is currently behaving more like a traditional risk asset rather than a fully independent asset.

Metaadvisor highlights that this macro correlation remains one of the key drivers behind current Bitcoin price behavior.

## What comes next?

In the short term, Bitcoin is in a zone where two strong forces are colliding:

* bullish momentum and technical support  
* profit taking and institutional hedging  

If the price manages to break above the $79,000–$80,000 range with strong volume, it could signal a continuation of the uptrend.

If not, there is a real possibility of a move back to lower levels and a short-term correction.
