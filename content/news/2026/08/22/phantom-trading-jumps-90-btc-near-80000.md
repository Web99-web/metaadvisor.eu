# Phantom: trading jumps 90%, BTC near $80,000

The crypto market accelerated sharply this week, and another sign of how quickly sentiment has changed came from Phantom. The wallet platform reported that trading this week was around **90 percent higher than last week**, showing how rapidly activity returned following the strong rise in prices of major digital assets.

Bitcoin was once again at the centre of attention. Its price has climbed almost **25 percent** in recent days and, at the time of publication, is trading around **$77,500**, already close to the $80,000 level. Such a strong move in a short period was enough to revive interest among retail investors and more active traders, while also accelerating the rotation of capital into other parts of the crypto market.

## Ether and HYPE are also moving higher

The rally has not been limited to Bitcoin. **Ether** is trading at around **$2,440** at the time of publication, while **HYPE** is around **$79**, putting it among the stronger performers of the week.

This kind of combination usually suggests that the market is not simply seeing a rise in one asset, but a broader return of risk appetite. When Ether and tokens such as HYPE begin rising alongside Bitcoin, it often indicates that traders are no longer staying only in the relatively “safer” part of the crypto market and are again looking for more aggressive opportunities.

## Higher trading activity shows volatility is back

Phantom’s figure of around 90 percent more trading is particularly interesting because it comes precisely at a time when prices have moved sharply in a very short period. In practice, this usually means more buying, more position closures, more rotation between coins and significantly more overall activity than during quieter weeks.

In other words, the market has woken up again. After a period of slower price action, Bitcoin’s rise, Ethereum’s recovery and strong interest in HYPE have brought volatility back – and with it a much higher level of trading activity.

## What is behind this week’s move?

This week’s rally does not appear to be the result of a single factor. Bitcoin’s surge, heavy closing of short positions and increased market activity have together created an environment in which momentum quickly attracted additional capital.

When Bitcoin rises by almost a quarter within a matter of days, such a move almost inevitably pulls much of the wider market with it. Ether benefits as the largest altcoin, while HYPE has been among the tokens attracting even more aggressive interest.

## The market looks “alive” again

The numbers themselves describe the week well: **Bitcoin around $77,500, Ether around $2,440, HYPE around $79 – while Phantom reports 90 percent more trading than last week**.

That does not mean this pace of price growth will necessarily continue without interruption. It does, however, mean that the market has regained a level of momentum and activity in a very short period that had not been seen at this intensity for some time.
