# Bitcoin back near $80,000: US demand strengthens as markets wait for the Fed

Bitcoin is once again trading around the psychologically important **$80,000 level**, but this time several interesting signals behind the price suggest the move is not being driven only by futures and a short squeeze. The Coinbase Premium has turned positive for the first time since May, US spot Bitcoin ETFs continue to attract capital, and Bitcoin is still trying to hold much of the strong gains made over the past few days.

The broader crypto market is not moving uniformly. Ethereum is trading near $2,500, Solana and Hyperliquid are showing relative strength, Zcash remains close to $800 after a strong multi-day rally, while Bittensor is once again posting a significantly larger daily decline. Above all, the market is now waiting for a new signal from **Kevin Warsh and the Fed**.

## Bitcoin tests the $80,000 area again

Bitcoin is trading at around **$79,500–$80,000** during the morning session after recovering sharply from much lower levels over the previous days. The price is now sitting just below an area where market analysis indicates a larger concentration of supply between approximately $81,000 and $86,000.

That means the rally has brought Bitcoin into an area where more sellers could emerge, including longer-term holders who previously bought at higher prices and are now approaching their entry levels again.

At the same time, the futures market indicates that the previous rise was not simply the result of aggressive new long positions. Part of the move came from short positions being closed, while funding remained relatively neutral. That makes it increasingly important to see whether there is enough genuine spot demand for Bitcoin to hold this new higher price zone.

## Coinbase Premium turns positive for the first time since May

One of today's more interesting signals is coming from Coinbase. **The Coinbase Premium has returned to positive territory for the first time since May.**

The Coinbase Premium measures the difference between the Bitcoin price on US-based Coinbase and prices on other major international exchanges. When Bitcoin trades at a slightly higher price on Coinbase, it can indicate relatively stronger demand from US buyers.

The price difference itself does not have to be large, but the indicator is closely watched because Coinbase is used by US institutional investors, companies and investors buying Bitcoin directly with dollars.

A positive Coinbase Premium is therefore particularly interesting when it appears at the same time as strong inflows into US Bitcoin ETFs.

## More than $2.8 billion flowed into Bitcoin ETFs

US spot Bitcoin ETFs have recorded **eight consecutive trading days of net inflows**, bringing more than **$2.8 billion** into the funds during that period.

On August 27 alone, net inflows reached approximately **$242 million**. BlackRock's IBIT recorded around $278 million in inflows, while several other funds saw smaller withdrawals on the same day.

The streak matters because it shows that institutional and investor demand has continued even after Bitcoin's strong price increase.

The combination now looks like this:

**Bitcoin near $80,000 + Coinbase Premium positive again + more than $2.8 billion in ETF inflows.**

That does not guarantee that the rally will continue, but it does show that there is genuine buying demand behind the market, rather than only leveraged positions on derivatives exchanges.

## SOL and HYPE stronger, ETH and XRP slightly weaker

Ethereum is trading at around **$2,495**, down approximately 1.5% on the day, while XRP is near **$1.43**.

Solana is one of the stronger major cryptocurrencies. SOL is trading at around **$106**, with a daily gain of approximately 2–3%.

Hyperliquid's HYPE also remains strong at around **$84**, up approximately 2% on the day.

BNB is trading near **$708**, with a relatively small daily decline.

This distribution once again shows that capital is not currently flowing evenly across the entire market. Bitcoin remains dominant, while individual altcoins are simultaneously making much stronger moves of their own.

## ZEC remains near $800 while TAO comes under pressure again

Zcash remains at very high levels after the strong rally of recent days, trading at around **$790–$800**. Its daily move is relatively small, which currently looks more like consolidation of the previous strong advance than a new major impulse.

Bittensor is behaving differently. TAO is trading near **$245**, down more than 5% over the past 24 hours.

The difference between ZEC and TAO is once again interesting because both tokens frequently make strong moves that are not fully aligned with Bitcoin or the broader market. At the moment, ZEC is still holding much of its previous rally, while TAO is once again more sensitive to selling pressure.

The total crypto market capitalization is approximately **$2.7 trillion**, while Bitcoin dominance remains very high at around **59%**.

## Markets wait for Kevin Warsh and the Fed

As the crypto market tries to assess how strong current US demand really is, the next important macro signal is expected from **Kevin Warsh and the Fed**.

Investors are primarily interested in what Warsh will say about inflation, the state of the US economy and the future direction of monetary policy.

Inflation in the United States remains above the Fed's target, while several US central bank officials have recently warned again that the fight against inflation is not over.

For Bitcoin, this matters because high or further rising interest rates can make the US dollar and Treasury bonds more attractive while reducing investors' appetite for riskier assets.

A softer stance from the Fed could have the opposite effect and give Bitcoin, technology stocks and other risk-on markets additional room to move higher.

## Our view

* **More than $2.8 billion in ETF inflows across eight consecutive trading days provides further evidence that US capital continues to move into Bitcoin.** This does not mean the price cannot fall, but it shows that the rally is not based only on leverage and short covering.
* **The area above $80,000 is now becoming the real test.** If Bitcoin can absorb selling in the $81,000–$86,000 zone while ETF inflows continue, the market would receive a much stronger confirmation that demand has increased.
* **The broader crypto market continues to show strong capital rotation.** SOL and HYPE are rising while ETH and XRP lag, ZEC is consolidating its previous strong rally and TAO is once again posting a significantly larger decline.
* **The Fed remains the biggest short-term external risk.** If Kevin Warsh delivers a tougher message on inflation and interest rates, it will be especially important to watch whether current spot and ETF demand can keep Bitcoin near present levels.
