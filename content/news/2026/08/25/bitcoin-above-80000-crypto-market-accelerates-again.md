# Bitcoin back above $80,000

Bitcoin has once again climbed above **$80,000**, for the first time since May, and is holding around **$80,500** in morning trading. BTC has gained approximately **4% over the past 24 hours**, continuing its strong multi-day recovery after the June decline.

Bitcoin's rise is once again at the center of the market, but this time what is particularly interesting is that the move is becoming increasingly visible across other large and mid-sized cryptocurrencies as well.

## Bitcoin leads the market again

The return above $80,000 is an important psychological level after several months in which Bitcoin went through several sharp corrections. From the June lows around $58,000, BTC has risen by nearly 40%.

Bitcoin's market dominance remains very high at around **59.3%**, showing that the largest share of capital is still concentrated in BTC. However, over the past several days there have been increasing signs that part of the rally is beginning to spread into other areas of the crypto market.

## Solana back above $100

Among the major altcoins, **Solana (SOL)** is showing particular strength. It has moved back above **$100** and is trading around **$101**, with a gain of almost **8% over the past 24 hours**.

At the same time, **Ethereum (ETH)** has returned toward **$2,500**, while **Dogecoin (DOGE)** is around **$0.092**. **Stellar (XLM)** is trading near **$0.196** and is also participating in the broader market recovery.

## ZEC remains above $850, HYPE around $80

After an exceptionally strong multi-day rise, **Zcash (ZEC)** is still holding around **$853**. This is particularly interesting because ZEC has so far not given back a large part of its previous jump, instead remaining at very high levels.

**Hyperliquid (HYPE)** is trading around **$80.5**, after also posting strong gains over the previous several days. ZEC and HYPE therefore remain among the tokens that have moved far away from the levels at which they traded before the latest market wave.

## The rally spreads to other altcoins

Moves are also visible beyond the largest projects. **Chainlink (LINK)** is trading around **$11.53**, **Injective (INJ)** around **$5.8**, while **Aave (AAVE)** is near **$129**.

**Sui (SUI)** is trading around **$0.80**, and gains are visible across a number of other tokens as well. That does not yet mean the entire altcoin market is rising at the same pace, but it does show that the current market move is no longer limited to Bitcoin alone.

## SEI and RUNE may have gone unnoticed

While Bitcoin, Solana and Zcash have attracted most of the attention over the past several days, strong moves have also appeared in some smaller tokens.

**Sei (SEI)** was trading around **$0.03831 on August 19**, and by August 22 it had reached approximately **$0.05104**. That represents a gain of around **33% in just a few days**.

**THORChain (RUNE)** made a similar move. Its price was around **$0.397 on August 19**, before climbing to approximately **$0.5005**, a gain of around **26%**.

Such moves provide further evidence that the current rally is not limited to the largest cryptocurrencies. While Bitcoin continues to lead the market, capital is clearly appearing in smaller projects as well, which can easily go almost unnoticed while attention remains focused on BTC above $80,000.

## Strategy follows Bitcoin again

Bitcoin's move is also visible outside the crypto market itself. **Strategy (MSTR)**, a company whose stock is strongly linked to Bitcoin's performance because of its large BTC treasury, is trading around **$124**.

MSTR is not a cryptocurrency, but the market often uses it as one indicator of sentiment toward Bitcoin. When BTC rises or falls sharply, the reaction in Strategy shares is often even more pronounced.

## Has rotation into altcoins begun?

The overall crypto market is rising, but the move is still not evenly distributed. Bitcoin is up around **4% over the past 24 hours**, while Solana is gaining almost twice as much. At the same time, several other altcoins are showing considerably more moderate moves.

With Bitcoin dominance still around 59%, it is too early to speak of a broad altseason. The current picture looks more like a **BTC-led rally in which capital is gradually rotating into selected altcoins**.

If this pattern continues, it will be important to watch whether the rally spreads to an increasing number of tokens over the coming days, or whether Bitcoin once again draws most of the market's liquidity back toward itself.

## Next focus: $82,000

After Bitcoin's return above $80,000, the next level the market is quickly beginning to watch is **$82,000**.

That level is also attracting attention in the derivatives market. Bitcoin options traders have already begun placing multi-million-dollar bets on a rapid continuation above $82,000, while another part of the market is simultaneously increasing protection against a possible decline.
