# Unitree robots: IPO still ahead, but prices are already going wild

Unitree Robotics has not yet started regular trading on the Shanghai STAR Market, but several parallel markets have already formed around its future price — and each one gives a very different estimate of what the Chinese humanoid robot maker could be worth once trading finally begins.

The official IPO price is **150.80 yuan per share**, or a little over $22. At that price, Unitree is valued at approximately **$9 billion**.

But investors outside the official IPO process are already paying — or are willing to pay — much more.

## Scalpers offer around 410 yuan for IPO shares

Reuters reported, citing Chinese media, that some investors who managed to secure scarce IPO allocations were receiving offers from scalpers of around **410 yuan per share**.

That is approximately **170% above the official IPO price of 150.80 yuan**.

Such a premium is not particularly surprising considering how difficult it was to obtain Unitree shares through the IPO. The retail portion of the offering was more than **8,000 times oversubscribed**, a record level of demand for the Shanghai STAR Market.

Nearly ten million retail investors attempted to secure part of the relatively small number of available shares, meaning scarcity created a kind of unofficial market even before regular trading began.

## Hyperliquid goes even further — around four times the IPO price

Crypto derivatives traders have set an even more aggressive price. Pre-IPO perpetual contracts on Unitree trading through Hyperliquid infrastructure were recently priced at around **$92 to $94**.

Allium cited approximately **$93**, about 4.2 times the official IPO price of roughly $22.37.

Converted roughly into yuan, that implies a price of more than **600 yuan per share**.

But there is a crucial difference: this is not an actual Unitree share.

A pre-IPO perpetual is a derivative. The trader is speculating on the company's future value but does not receive ownership in Unitree, and the position cannot simply be converted into a real share after the IPO.

The Hyperliquid price should therefore not be interpreted as “Unitree is now worth 630 yuan.” It is better understood as an indication of how optimistic speculators currently are about the company's first day of public trading.

## Three markets, three completely different prices

Unitree now effectively has three prices before regular trading has even started.

The official IPO price is **150.80 yuan**.

Unofficial offers from Chinese scalpers are around **410 yuan**.

The crypto derivatives market is implying more than **600 yuan**.

The gap is enormous. Even if Unitree jumped 170% on its first trading day and reached around 410 yuan, that would be a spectacular market debut — yet the price would still remain well below the level currently implied by Hyperliquid.

## Unitree now has to justify enormous hype

The expectations are not coming from nowhere. Unitree is one of China's best-known humanoid robotics companies, it is profitable and its revenue has grown rapidly.

In 2025, the company generated around **1.7 billion yuan in revenue**, while the IPO has already attracted record demand.

But even the official IPO valuation is already demanding. Reuters reported that the 150.80 yuan offer price represents a valuation of around **219 times projected 2025 earnings**.

That shows that the official price already incorporates very high expectations for the future growth of robotics.

If the price immediately moved toward the levels currently implied by Hyperliquid, Unitree's valuation would approach **$38 billion**.

At that point, the market would no longer be paying only for Unitree's current business, but for a highly aggressive forecast of what the company might become in the future.

## The story has moved beyond crypto circles

At the beginning of August, the Unitree pre-IPO perpetual was still a relatively niche crypto derivatives story. Major international financial media are now paying attention to the huge gap between the IPO price and expectations on Hyperliquid.

Reuters published a report on August 14 about Unitree's expected strong market debut, while CoinDesk highlighted a day later that Hyperliquid traders were valuing the company at more than four times its IPO price.

That means the market is now in a very different phase. Early traders were buying the expectation before the story became widely known. New buyers are entering after it has already been reported that the IPO was 8,000 times oversubscribed and that the crypto market is pricing in almost a fourfold increase.

## The real test is still ahead

Unitree is expected to begin trading on the Shanghai STAR Market during the coming week. Only then will a real public market price appear for the first time, allowing us to compare which group came closest to reality.

Will the traditional market confirm the relatively conservative 150.80 yuan IPO price?

Will the stock move closer to the roughly 410 yuan being offered by scalpers?

Or were Hyperliquid traders closer to reality when they pushed the synthetic price above 600 yuan?

The answer could be important not only for Unitree, but for the entire emerging market for pre-IPO crypto derivatives.

## Our take

* **410 yuan and roughly 630 yuan are not two competing official prices for Unitree shares.** The first represents reported scalper offers for real IPO allocations, while the second comes from the price of a synthetic pre-IPO derivative.
* **The official reference price remains 150.80 yuan.** Everything above it currently represents market expectations before regular trading begins.
* **A price of 410 yuan would already represent an extraordinary IPO result.** That would be about 170% above the offer price.
* **Hyperliquid is pricing in an even more extreme scenario.** A pre-IPO perpetual around $93 implies a valuation of roughly $38 billion, compared with about $9 billion at the IPO.
* **The biggest risk now is that a spectacular debut is already priced into the derivative.** Unitree does not merely need a good IPO to justify the Hyperliquid price — it needs an exceptionally strong first trading day.
* **The first day of trading will be a fascinating test for pre-IPO perpetual markets.** It will show whether crypto traders achieved early price discovery or simply pushed the robotics hype too far.

Unitree is therefore no longer just a story about humanoid robots. It has become an experiment in which a traditional IPO, China's unofficial market and crypto derivatives are all trying to answer the same question at the same time — how much is this company really worth before its shares even begin regular trading?
