# Crypto yesterday-today: XRP around $1, Bitcoin below $63,000, Tether gets first full audit

The crypto market has lost some momentum over the past 24 hours. Bitcoin slipped below $63,000 on Friday morning, XRP is once again trading around the psychologically important $1 level after stronger intraday moves the previous day, while Ethereum and Solana are also not yet showing a broad return of risk appetite.

This time there is no single event driving the market. Several unfavorable regulatory, institutional and macroeconomic signals have appeared at the same time, while individual projects are still producing some important fundamental developments.

## Bitcoin falls below $63,000 again

Bitcoin was trading around the $63,000 level on the morning of August 14 after spending much of the previous day near $64,000. The broader crypto market also weakened, with pressure coming from several directions.

Progress on the U.S. CLARITY Act has slowed again, while the SEC is delaying regulatory initiatives intended to make trading tokenized securities easier and provide a clearer framework for crypto projects.

At the same time, U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs are seeing capital outflows again. So far this week, they have recorded around **$333 million in net outflows**, after approximately $853 million flowed into the products the previous week.

Additional pressure is coming from the U.S. Treasury market. Higher long-term yields increase the cost of capital and traditionally make riskier assets such as cryptocurrencies less attractive.

## XRP tests the $1 level again

XRP has attracted particular attention in recent days because of sharp short-term price moves and strong positioning in derivatives markets, but on August 14 the token is once again trading around the psychologically important **$1 level**.

That does not necessarily make the previous day's intraday rise meaningless, but the market has not yet confirmed a sustainable breakout. Stronger derivatives activity while the price continues to struggle around $1 can increase volatility and create sharp moves in either direction.

XRP therefore remains one of the more interesting large altcoins to watch, precisely because it is trading close to a level that has attracted significant market attention in recent months.

## MSCI opens another issue for Bitcoin treasury companies

Additional uncertainty is coming from traditional finance. MSCI is considering a rule change that could remove certain companies from its equity indices if their business model is primarily based on holding assets.

Among the companies mentioned are **Strategy and Metaplanet**, both known for holding large Bitcoin reserves. If such a change were implemented, funds and other investment products that automatically track MSCI indices would have to adjust their positions.

This is therefore not only a technical question about corporate classification. It could raise a much broader issue: how willing will traditional financial markets be to accommodate companies whose strategy and market value depend heavily on accumulating Bitcoin?

## Tether receives its first full financial audit

While the market is digesting several negative developments, a major positive story has emerged from the stablecoin sector. Tether has completed the **first full independent audit of its annual financial statements**, with KPMG U.S. issuing an unqualified, or “clean,” opinion for the 2025 financial year.

This is a major step beyond Tether's previous practice. The company has published quarterly reserve attestations for years, but a full financial audit goes much deeper, covering the balance sheet, income, cash flows, transactions, ownership records, asset valuations and counterparties.

According to the published figures, Tether's reserves exceeded its liabilities by **$6.814 billion** at the end of 2025. As part of the audit, KPMG also physically inspected and counted Tether's gold bars rather than relying solely on custodian records.

For USDT, the world's largest stablecoin, this is one of the most important steps toward addressing a question that has followed Tether almost since the beginning: how transparent and verifiable are the assets backing the tokens in circulation?

## Solana discusses a major reduction in future SOL issuance

A different story is developing around Solana. It is not directly related to today's price action, but it could become much more important over the longer term.

Validators are discussing proposals that could accelerate the decline in SOL inflation while also increasing the amount of tokens burned through network activity. The goal is to reduce the rate at which new SOL enters circulation while permanently removing more existing tokens through burn mechanisms.

One proposal would accelerate the annual reduction in inflation, allowing Solana to reach its long-term target inflation rate earlier. Another part of the proposed changes could significantly increase the amount of SOL burned each day.

If such a package were approved, the change would affect both sides of supply: **fewer new SOL tokens would enter circulation while more existing tokens would be burned.**

It is important to note, however, that these changes have not yet been finally approved and remain part of the governance process.

## The market is sending mixed signals

The crypto market therefore enters August 14 with an unusual combination of developments. Bitcoin is back below $63,000, XRP is fighting around the $1 level, capital is leaving Bitcoin ETFs again and U.S. regulatory initiatives are facing further delays.

At the same time, some of the most interesting fundamental developments in recent months are taking place. Tether has completed the full audit that had remained one of the biggest open questions around USDT for years, while Solana is considering changes that could significantly affect future SOL supply.

The market currently looks more like a period of selective capital rotation than a situation in which Bitcoin simply rises or falls and every other token follows in the same direction.

## Our take

* **Bitcoin is once again showing how sensitive crypto remains to macroeconomic conditions.** Higher U.S. Treasury yields, weaker ETF flows and regulatory delays are creating an environment in which broad risk-on momentum is difficult to sustain.
* **XRP is particularly interesting around the $1 level.** The previous day's rise showed that buyers are present, but a sustainable breakout has not yet been confirmed and heavy derivatives activity may increase volatility further.

* **The Solana story is less about today's SOL price and more about future supply.** If inflation declines faster and more tokens are burned, the network's long-term tokenomics could look significantly different.
* **The market is currently giving neither a clear signal of a new strong rally nor broad capitulation.** It looks more like a period in which individual fundamental developments may matter more than Bitcoin's daily move.
