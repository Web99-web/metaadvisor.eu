# Bitcoin back above $80,000 as ETFs buy for the eighth day in a row

Bitcoin moved back above $80,000 on August 27, with more than short-term trading now sitting behind the rally: U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded their **eighth consecutive trading day of net capital inflows**.

At the same time, the broader crypto picture is strengthening. Ethereum jumped to approximately **$2,566** during the day, Solana posted a strong daily gain among the larger altcoins, and XRP also traded higher. In traditional markets, the Asian session did not show a classic broad risk-on move, but rather a **rotation of capital toward AI, semiconductors and parts of the technology sector**.

## Bitcoin ETFs buy for the eighth day in a row

U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded approximately **$232.2 million in net inflows on August 26**, extending their positive streak to eight trading days.

Across those eight days, approximately **$2.8 billion** flowed into Bitcoin ETFs. The significance is not only in the size of the amount but also in its consistency. One large inflow day can reflect short-term positioning, but eight consecutive days point to more persistent demand for spot Bitcoin exposure through U.S. funds.

The largest share of capital on August 26 again went to **BlackRock's IBIT, with approximately $200.8 million in net inflows**. Fidelity's FBTC added around **$25.6 million**, Bitwise's BITB around **$6 million**, while Grayscale's GBTC recorded approximately **$50.4 million in net outflows**.

Despite outflows from individual products, the overall result for the group remained strongly positive.

## Ethereum ETFs follow the same pattern

Bitcoin is not the only crypto asset currently attracting institutional capital.

U.S. spot Ethereum ETFs also recorded their **eighth consecutive day of net inflows**, with approximately **$192 million in new capital on August 26**.

That changes the broader market picture. Institutional buying is no longer concentrated only in Bitcoin but is again spreading toward Ethereum. Smaller ETF products linked to other crypto assets also recorded inflows: XRP funds attracted approximately **$28 million**, while around **$9 million** flowed into Solana products.

## Bitcoin again tests $80,000

Bitcoin climbed back above **$80,000** during August 27 before retreating toward the $79,000–$80,000 area.

The relationship between price and ETF inflows is now one of the more important market signals. Bitcoin has already made a strong move over the previous several days, so continued ETF buying shows that new capital is still arriving even after the large increase in price.

However, moving above $80,000 and holding above that level are not the same thing. The area around $80,000 therefore remains an important short-term test.

If ETF inflows continue and Bitcoin manages to stabilize above $80,000, the market would receive additional confirmation that buying did not end with the first major jump. The opposite scenario would also be important: continued large ETF inflows alongside a weakening Bitcoin price would indicate that substantial selling pressure is emerging and absorbing institutional demand.

## Asian Market Watch: capital rotates toward AI and chips

Asian markets did not send a completely uniform signal on August 27.

The MSCI Asia-Pacific equity index rose by around **0.5%**, while South Korea's KOSPI was approximately **1.8% higher** during the session. Chipmakers and companies linked to AI infrastructure were again among the stronger parts of the market.

One of the main reasons was Nvidia, whose results and very strong longer-term outlook again strengthened expectations that the major AI infrastructure investment cycle is not yet over. Positive sentiment spread to parts of the Asian supplier and semiconductor sector.

Japan was weaker at the same time, showing that this was not a classic everything-rises session. A more accurate description of today's Asian session would be **rotation**: capital is moving into selected technology and AI segments while the broader market remains mixed.

U.S. index futures were also positive during the European part of the day, while European markets showed a similar split — technology was stronger, but the entire market did not rise with the same intensity.

## Ethereum, Solana and XRP show crypto strength broadening

Ethereum reached approximately **$2,566** during the day, an important move because it shows that the latest crypto momentum is not limited to Bitcoin.

Among the larger altcoins, **Solana** stood out in particular, trading around $105 during the day and posting a daily gain of approximately 9%. **XRP** traded around $1.44, up several percent over 24 hours.

Stronger gains among major altcoins, alongside the eight-day inflow streak into Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs, currently point to a broadening of positive sentiment. That does not yet mean the entire crypto market has entered an equally strong new rally, but capital is no longer concentrated exclusively in Bitcoin.

It is particularly notable that this is happening at the same time as renewed strength in the AI and technology segments of traditional markets. For now, this does not look like a complete shift of capital from one asset class into another, but rather a broader return of risk appetite toward assets and sectors with strong momentum.

## U.S.–Canada trade conflict remains in the background

Trade tensions between the United States and Canada remain an active risk, particularly for autos, steel, aluminum and other sectors directly affected by tariffs and counter-tariffs.

However, **they were not the dominant driver of global markets today**. Investors reacted much more strongly to AI and semiconductor momentum, expectations around U.S. monetary policy and continued strength in crypto.

For now, the U.S.–Canada trade dispute therefore remains an important background risk to monitor, but it does not explain the main direction of today's market.

## What we are watching now

The most important signal for Bitcoin in the coming sessions will be the combination of **price and ETF flows**.

If ETFs continue recording hundreds of millions of dollars in daily inflows and Bitcoin stabilizes above $80,000, it would indicate that institutional demand continues to follow the rise in price.

If inflows continue while Bitcoin again loses $80,000 and begins moving back toward lower levels, however, that divergence could become more important than the inflow figure itself.

The second signal is the behavior of Ethereum and major altcoins. Continued relative strength in ETH, SOL and XRP would indicate that crypto momentum is spreading beyond Bitcoin.

The third signal comes from traditional markets: if capital continues flowing into AI, semiconductors and technology after the U.S. market opens, today's Asian rotation would receive confirmation in the United States as well.

## Our view

* **Eight consecutive days of Bitcoin ETF inflows matter more than one large inflow day.** The consistency shows that institutional buying continued even after the strong increase in price.
* **Approximately $2.8 billion across eight trading days provides significant spot demand for Bitcoin**, but BTC still needs to show that it can remain sustainably above $80,000.
* **Ethereum ETFs have also recorded eight positive days**, showing that institutional interest is no longer limited to Bitcoin.
* **Inflows into XRP and Solana products further indicate that interest is spreading across a larger part of the crypto market.**
* **The Asian session looks more like rotation than broad risk-on.** AI, chips and technology are attracting capital, but not all markets and sectors are rising strongly at the same time.
* **U.S.–Canada tariffs were not the main market driver today.** They remain a risk to monitor, but today's moves were driven more by AI, technology, monetary policy expectations and crypto.
* **The next important signal will be the relationship between ETF inflows and Bitcoin around $80,000.** Continued buying alongside a breakout would confirm strength, while continued inflows with a falling price would indicate that strong selling pressure is absorbing new institutional demand.
