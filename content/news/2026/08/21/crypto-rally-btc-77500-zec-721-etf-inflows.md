# Crypto rally broadens: BTC 77,500, ZEC 721

The crypto market continued its strong rise on August 21, but this is no longer just a story about Bitcoin and short positions being forced to close. At the time of writing, Bitcoin is trading around **$77,500**, Ethereum around **$2,425**, Zcash has reached approximately **$721**, AAVE around **$113**, Chainlink around **$11.85**, and XRP about **$1.43**.

Bitcoin climbed to nearly **$79,500** during the day, its highest level in several months, before pulling back toward the $77,000–$78,000 area. BTC is up more than 20% on the week, and the question is increasingly whether this is still just an unusually powerful short squeeze or the beginning of a more serious market recovery.

One figure in particular changes the picture today: **US spot Bitcoin ETFs are buying heavily again.**

## Bitcoin ETFs: $1.6 billion in four days

US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded around **$606 million in net inflows on August 20**, their strongest daily inflow since early May. It was also the fourth consecutive positive trading day.

Altogether, spot Bitcoin ETFs attracted approximately **$1.6 billion in fresh capital** from Monday through Thursday.

BlackRock’s **IBIT** stood out in particular, attracting around **$503 million** on Thursday alone, accounting for more than 80% of the day’s total net inflow.

That matters because the first part of this rally was not driven only by fresh buying. A large part of the initial move came from short liquidations. When prices rise sharply, traders betting on a decline are forced to buy Bitcoin to close their positions, pushing prices even higher.

ETF inflows show something different: **fresh capital is actually entering the market.**

## More than $4 billion in shorts wiped out

According to market data, more than **$4 billion in crypto short positions** have been liquidated since Wednesday. That is one of the main reasons the move has been so fast.

Bitcoin moved from the low-$60,000 area to nearly $80,000 within just a few days. A move like that is difficult to sustain at the same pace.

But the appearance of strong ETF inflows after the initial short squeeze suggests the engine behind the rally may now be changing.

In other words, short sellers were initially forced to buy. Now traditional investors are also buying again through regulated spot ETFs.

That is exactly why analysts are increasingly divided over whether this is still just a short-term relief rally. Some see signs that Bitcoin may be emerging from a prolonged period of weakness, while others want to see whether the price can hold higher levels once the wave of forced short liquidations fades.

## Bitcoin now looks toward $80,000

BTC reached roughly **$79,400–$79,500** during the day before pulling back toward $77,500.

The **$80,000** level is now the obvious psychological test.

But after such a rapid rise, the more important question may be what happens during the first serious pullback. If Bitcoin can hold higher levels and continue attracting capital through spot ETFs, the case for a more durable recovery becomes much stronger.

If ETF flows suddenly reverse back into outflows and BTC quickly loses the levels it has just regained, the past few days would look more like an extreme squeeze than the start of a new bull market.

## ZEC explodes to around $721

While Bitcoin is attracting most of the attention, **Zcash** has delivered one of the strongest moves in the market.

ZEC reached approximately **$721** during the day after already posting a powerful rise over the previous several sessions. The privacy coin has become one of the biggest outperformers of the current crypto rally.

Unlike many altcoins simply following Bitcoin higher, ZEC currently has its own catalysts.

Grayscale filed an updated set of documents related to converting its **Zcash Trust into a product that could trade on NYSE Arca**, under the ticker ZCSH.

More notably, the filing revealed non-binding discussions between Grayscale and a company connected to its parent group, Digital Currency Group, regarding a possible contribution of around **200,000 ZEC** to the trust.

At prices before the latest surge, that amount of ZEC was worth approximately **$110 million**.

A potential purchase or contribution of 200,000 ZEC is a very large amount for a market that is far smaller and less liquid than Bitcoin, so it is not surprising that the development attracted attention.

## ETH 2,425, XRP 1.43, AAVE 113 and LINK 11.85

Another important signal is the breadth of the rally.

At the time of writing, Ethereum is trading around **$2,425**, XRP around **$1.43**, AAVE around **$113**, and Chainlink around **$11.85**.

That means capital is no longer concentrated only in Bitcoin.

In healthier crypto rallies, BTC often moves first, followed by Ethereum, before capital begins rotating into larger and then smaller altcoins. A similar pattern is now beginning to appear.

That does not yet mean a new altseason has started, but it is an important difference compared with a rally where only Bitcoin rises while the rest of the market stagnates.

## Ethereum ETFs are also attracting capital again

Positive ETF flows are not limited to Bitcoin.

US spot Ethereum ETFs attracted around **$221 million in net inflows on August 20**, also marking a fourth consecutive positive day.

That helps explain why ETH has returned toward the $2,400 area and why momentum is beginning to spread through the broader crypto market.

The combination of Bitcoin and Ethereum ETF inflows is a much stronger signal of institutional interest than rising prices on crypto exchanges alone.

## Why are Bitcoin and gold rising at the same time?

Interestingly, Bitcoin is not rising alone this time.

Gold also reached around **$4,630 per ounce**, approaching fresh multi-month highs.

One of the key macro developments was a move by the US Treasury to expand buybacks of longer-dated government debt. That followed significant pressure in the US bond market and a sharp rise in longer-term yields.

US government debt is already approaching **$40 trillion**, and the combination of large deficits, heavy government borrowing and interventions in the bond market has revived the so-called **debasement trade**.

In that environment, investors look for assets whose supply cannot simply be expanded by political decisions or additional currency issuance.

That is one reason Bitcoin and gold are rising together again.

## Short-term jump or something bigger?

It is still too early to say that a new long-term bull market has begun.

Bitcoin has gained more than 20% in just a few days. ZEC has exploded toward $700, while many altcoins have gained double-digit percentages in a very short period.

Moves like that increase the probability of a correction even when the medium-term trend is positive.

But compared with the situation only a few days ago, something important has changed.

The rally began as a major short squeeze. Now we have four consecutive days of Bitcoin ETF inflows totaling around **$1.6 billion**, strong Ethereum ETF inflows, a broadening move into altcoins, and a macro environment in which both Bitcoin and gold are rising.

That already looks more substantial than a one-day crypto pump.
