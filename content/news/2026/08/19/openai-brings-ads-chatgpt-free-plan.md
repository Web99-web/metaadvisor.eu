# OpenAI brings ads to ChatGPT Free plan

ChatGPT is no longer completely ad-free for every user. During 2026, OpenAI began introducing advertisements for users on the free ChatGPT plan and the lower-cost ChatGPT Go plan, and on August 11 the company confirmed another major expansion of its advertising system.

After initial testing in the United States, ChatGPT ads were also launched in the United Kingdom, Mexico, Brazil, Japan and South Korea. OpenAI says it plans to expand advertising to additional markets during the year.

## What do ads in ChatGPT look like?

OpenAI has emphasized that ads should be clearly separated from the answers generated by ChatGPT.

An ad can appear below a response when the system determines that there is a relevant sponsored product or service connected to the current conversation. It should be clearly labeled as advertising and visually separated from the actual ChatGPT response.

Users can also hide an ad or provide feedback about why it is not relevant to them.

In other words, ChatGPT is gradually adopting a business model that internet users already know well from Google, Facebook, Instagram and other major digital platforms.

## Who will see ads and who will not?

Under OpenAI's current policy, advertising applies to users on ChatGPT Free and Go plans.

ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu remain ad-free.

OpenAI says advertising revenue should help keep ChatGPT broadly available for free or at lower-cost price points, rather than limiting access to advanced artificial intelligence only to users who can pay for more expensive subscriptions.

This also marks an important shift in how ChatGPT is funded. After years in which subscriptions and business customers provided a major part of the revenue, one of the internet's largest business models is now entering ChatGPT as well: advertising.

## Can advertisers read your conversations?

That is likely to be one of the first questions many users will ask.

OpenAI says advertisers do not receive access to users' private conversations and that the company does not sell user conversations or personal data to advertisers.

The company also says advertisers cannot influence the answers ChatGPT provides.

That distinction is particularly important because the boundary between an AI response and a commercial recommendation could quickly become a trust issue if users begin to believe that a certain answer was given only because someone paid for it.

OpenAI therefore says ads will remain clearly separated from ChatGPT's organic answers.

## Why is OpenAI introducing ads?

Developing and operating large AI models is extremely expensive. ChatGPT is used every day by a huge number of people, including many users who do not pay for the service.

OpenAI says advertising should help finance broader access to ChatGPT and allow the company to maintain a free version of the product.

From a business perspective, the move is understandable, but it also opens a completely new question for the AI industry.

Google built one of the world's largest business models by combining search and advertising. Meta generates a large share of its revenue from ads on Facebook and Instagram. If hundreds of millions of people increasingly ask AI assistants directly for information, recommendations and help making decisions instead of using traditional search engines, the space inside AI conversations becomes extremely valuable to advertisers.

## Can an ad influence ChatGPT's answer?

OpenAI says no.

According to the rules published by the company, advertisements should remain separate from responses, and a commercial relationship with an advertiser should not influence what ChatGPT tells a user.

Over the long term, this is likely to be one of the most important commitments OpenAI will have to maintain convincingly.

An AI chatbot is not the same as an ordinary website. People ask it questions about shopping, travel, finances, work, technology and many personal decisions. If ads begin appearing alongside those answers, user trust will depend heavily on how clearly the line between the answer and the advertisement remains visible.

## Ads continue to expand

OpenAI began testing advertising in the United States in early 2026, initially with a limited number of signed-in adult users on Free and Go plans.

By August, the system had expanded to five additional major markets, and OpenAI says it plans to continue the rollout during the year.

So far, OpenAI has not officially announced that ads have launched in Croatia or across the entire European Economic Area. It would therefore be inaccurate to claim that Croatian or all EU users will begin seeing ads on August 19.

The direction, however, is now clear: ChatGPT is no longer an AI product funded only by subscriptions. Advertising is becoming part of its business model.

## Our view

* The introduction of advertising is not unexpected. Free access to highly expensive AI systems must ultimately be financed in some way.
* More important than the mere existence of ads will be how clearly they remain separated from ChatGPT's answers.
* OpenAI says advertisers do not gain access to private conversations and that ads do not influence AI responses. Maintaining that separation will be crucial for user trust.
* Advertising currently applies to Free and Go plans, while Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu remain ad-free.
* After Google Search and social networks, the advertising industry is gaining another potentially enormous market: conversations with artificial intelligence.
