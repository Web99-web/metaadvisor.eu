# SpaceX Falls to Around 165 USD, Crypto Market Mixed: BTC 64.5K, ETH Around 1,760, SOL 73, HYPE Around 68

SpaceX fell today to around **165 USD**. This shows that the market has started to cool after the initial IPO excitement. The stock remains in focus because SpaceX is one of the most closely watched technology and space companies, but today’s decline shows that investors are looking more carefully at the price after the big hype.

The crypto market did not have one clear direction today. **Bitcoin** moved around **64.5 thousand USD**, while **Ethereum** was around **1,747 USD** and also reached around **1,760 USD** during the day. This means ETH did not look especially weak, but stayed above the 1,700 USD level.

**Solana** was around **73 USD**. That is stronger than a few days ago, when SOL was around **67 USD**. For Solana, this shows a recovery from recent lower levels.

For **HYPE**, the picture is different. The token was above **70 USD** a few days ago, possibly even close to **75 USD**, while today it moved up and down around **68 USD**. This means HYPE is weaker today compared with its recent highs, although it is still holding at a relatively high level.

## Today’s Market Picture

Today, it is therefore not possible to speak about one clear rise or one clear decline. **SpaceX fell to around 165 USD. Bitcoin held around 64.5K. Ethereum was around 1,747–1,760 USD. Solana rose toward 73 USD, while HYPE moved down toward around 68 USD after previously trading above 70 USD.**

This looks more like a mixed market than full panic. Some parts of the market are cooling, some are stabilizing, and some are recovering from recent lower levels.

## Our View (Metaadvisor)

SpaceX was under pressure today and fell to around **165 USD**, while the crypto market was uneven. **BTC** held around **64.5K**, **ETH** around **1,747–1,760 USD**, **SOL** strengthened toward **73 USD**, and **HYPE** fell toward around **68 USD** compared with levels above 70 USD a few days ago.

For now, this does not look like a complete flight from risk assets, but more like a selective market move: SpaceX is cooling after the hype, Solana looks stronger than a few days ago, and HYPE is weaker compared with its recent highs.
