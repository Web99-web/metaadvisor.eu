# Crypto Whale Shorts ETH With $19.7 Million: Ethereum Under Pressure Again

A large Ethereum trader, or “crypto whale”, has once again caught the market’s attention after opening a short position on ETH worth almost $20 million. The move comes at a time when Ether is under technical pressure, while questions around Ethereum’s funding, governance and the role of the Ethereum Foundation are also resurfacing.

After eight months of silence, the wallet identified as “0xf83f...6728” became active again — and immediately opened a 20x leveraged ETH short position worth around $19.72 million. The position was opened as Ether moved toward the $1,500 support zone, after falling more than 18% over the past two weeks.

{{< support1 >}}

What makes this story especially interesting is not only the size of the position. The same wallet has previously been linked to a successful Ether short during the crypto crash in October 2025. In other words, the market is not looking at just another large leveraged position, but at the return of an address that had already correctly positioned itself for a major downside move in ETH.

According to available data, the position was opened at an average entry price of around $1,565. At the time of reporting, the trader already had more than $100,000 in unrealized profit, while ETH was trading around the $1,550 area. If Ether continues to fall toward $1,375 — a level cited in the article’s technical analysis as a possible scenario if the bear flag breakdown continues — the whale’s unrealized profit could rise to roughly $2.39 million before fees and funding costs.

## Why Did This Short Attract Market Attention?

A large short position does not, by itself, mean that ETH will definitely fall. Whales can be wrong, and they sometimes lose large amounts as well. Positions with high leverage also carry significant risk. With 20x leverage, even a smaller price move in the opposite direction can quickly change the entire picture.

But this move comes at a sensitive moment. Ethereum has been under pressure in recent weeks together with the broader market for risk assets. Weaker sentiment in the technology sector, pressure on the Nasdaq and chip stocks are also spilling over into the crypto market. When investors reduce exposure to risk assets, ETH often feels additional pressure because the market sees it both as technological infrastructure and as a speculative asset.

This matters because ETH is not viewed only as another token. Ethereum is the foundation for a large part of the DeFi market, stablecoin infrastructure, layer-2 networks and many projects connected to tokenization. When sentiment around Ethereum weakens, the effect can be felt beyond the ETH price itself.

## Ethereum Foundation and the Question of Trust

Alongside technical pressure on price, Ethereum is also facing an internal debate over funding and governance. CoinDesk writes that Trent Van Epps, a former important member of the Ethereum Foundation, warned that Ethereum’s long-term decentralization strategy is entering a critical transition phase. His message is not that Ethereum is in an existential crisis. It is more about a practical question: how development, research, security and public goods will be funded over the long term if Ethereum governance becomes increasingly distributed outside the Foundation’s own structure.

That is an important distinction. For years, the Ethereum Foundation has played an important role in coordinating development and supporting the ecosystem. If that role changes, the market naturally asks who takes over part of the responsibility for the network’s long-term stability. For developers and investors, this is not only an organizational question, but also a question of trust. Still, this does not have to be bad news. More distributed governance can be a sign of Ethereum’s maturity over the long term. The problem is that markets do not like uncertainty. When a price decline, a large short, weaker technical signals and questions around ecosystem funding appear at the same time, traders can interpret it as a reason for caution.

## A Short Does Not Mean a Guaranteed Drop, but It Shows Sentiment

The latest move by this whale should not be read as a guaranteed forecast for the ETH price. Large traders can be right, but they can also quickly end up on the wrong side of the market, especially when they use high leverage. With leveraged positions, risk is always higher because the market does not need to make a large move for a position to become problematic.

Still, positions like this show how part of the market currently views Ethereum: not as a failed project, but as an asset under pressure. That is an important difference. Ethereum remains one of the most important blockchain networks, but in the short term it is going through a period in which technical signals, macro sentiment and internal ecosystem questions are overlapping in the same direction.

That is why this story has two layers. On the surface, a known whale is shorting ETH with a position of almost $20 million and betting on further downside. Beneath that is a broader story: Ethereum is going through a phase in which it must prove that decentralization can work even when the network becomes serious global infrastructure.

## Our Take

* The technical analysis cited by Cointelegraph mentions a possible drop toward $1,375 if the negative chart pattern continues.
* Ethereum is under pressure from both a broader selloff in risk assets and weaker sentiment around its own ecosystem.
* The debate around the Ethereum Foundation is not necessarily a sign of crisis, but it does raise questions about long-term funding and governance.
* A short position does not mean ETH will definitely fall, but it shows that part of the market currently sees Ethereum as an asset under pressure.
