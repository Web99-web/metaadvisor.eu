# Bitcoin and Ethereum at key support zones

The crypto market has entered another nervous phase. Bitcoin briefly fell below 60,000 USD and touched the area around 59,200 USD, while Ethereum slipped toward the 1,500 USD zone. These are not just ordinary round numbers on a chart. They are levels where the short-term sentiment of the entire market is now being tested.

The most important question right now is not only whether Bitcoin and Ethereum can recover a few percent higher, but whether they can defend their nearest support zones. If these zones hold, the market may still attempt a short-term rebound. If they break, a new wave of selling could quickly spread to altcoins, meme coins and stocks connected to the crypto narrative.

## Bitcoin: 59,000–60,000 USD as the most important zone

For Bitcoin, the key zone is now around 59,000–60,000 USD. After falling below 60,000 USD, BTC recovered toward the 60,500–60,800 USD area, but that is still not enough to call the market stable. In this kind of environment, one short recovery does not necessarily mean that the pressure is over. It often only means that some traders are closing short positions or that buyers are trying to defend the first important level.

If Bitcoin manages to stay above the 59,000–60,000 USD zone, the market may get some breathing room. In that case, altcoins could attempt a short-term bounce, especially those that have fallen the most. But if BTC loses 59,000 USD again, the problem is no longer only psychological. Stop-loss orders, leveraged position liquidations and a new wave of selling could be triggered, quickly opening the door to lower levels.

## Ethereum: 1,500–1,550 USD as a test of confidence

For Ethereum, the situation is even more sensitive. ETH fell toward the 1,500–1,550 USD area and, according to market data, touched its lowest level in the last 13 months. That means Ethereum is no longer falling only because Bitcoin is weak. Pressure is also coming from inside the Ethereum ecosystem: derivatives data shows weaker sentiment, funding has turned negative, and demand for downside protection through options has risen sharply.

The area around 1,500 USD is now crucial for ETH. If it holds, the market can talk about an attempt at stabilization. If it breaks, traders will increasingly look toward 1,400 USD as the next dangerous zone. That does not mean Ethereum must fall there, but it does mean that this level now appears as a realistic technical and psychological test.

## Why does Ethereum look weaker than an ordinary altcoin pullback?

Ethereum’s decline is not only the result of a broadly red crypto market. In the background, there are also specific signs of weakness. The funding rate on ETH perpetual futures contracts turned negative, which means demand for short positions has increased. In other words, part of the market is actively betting on further downside or at least wants protection against another drop.

In addition, demand for put options on Deribit has risen strongly. Traders use put options when they want protection from falling prices or when they expect weakness to continue. When such a signal appears at the same time as a price decline, it shows that the market is not only thinking about a short bounce, but is also preparing for the possibility of a deeper move lower.

## Falling TVL adds more pressure to the Ethereum story

Another important element is the decline in total value locked, or TVL, in Ethereum decentralized applications. TVL shows how much capital users keep in DeFi protocols, lending platforms, staking systems and other applications. When TVL falls, it often means that capital is being withdrawn from smart contracts and that activity inside the ecosystem is declining.

That matters because Ethereum is not only a token for trading. A large part of its value comes from the idea that it is the base layer for DeFi, stablecoins, staking, restaking, NFT infrastructure and smart contract applications. If capital is leaving those applications, sentiment toward ETH weakens further. Lower activity can mean lower fees, lower ecosystem revenue and weaker demand for ETH as a network asset.

## Altcoins depend on whether BTC and ETH can hold

Bitcoin remains the main guide in this story. When BTC falls below 60,000 USD, altcoins rarely stay calm. Ethereum, Solana, HYPE, LINK, AAVE, INJ, ADA, JTO, DOGE, SHIB and PEPE then react not only to their own news, but to a broader exit from risk. If Bitcoin loses its key support, altcoins usually fall faster because investors first leave the riskiest positions.

For now, the market does not look like a complete collapse, but rather like a very unstable attempt at a rebound. Bitcoin is trading around 60,800 USD, Ethereum around 1,556 USD, Solana around 62.3 USD, HYPE around 58.4 USD, LINK around 7.93 USD, AAVE around 60.93 USD, TAO around 196 USD, INJ around 5.139 USD, ADA around 0.159 USD and JTO around 0.507 USD. This shows that buyers still exist, but also that the market remains very fragile.

## Zcash as a short example of panic and fast recovery

Zcash was a special case, but it should not become the main topic of this story. After reports of a serious issue connected to the Zcash blockchain, ZEC fell sharply toward the area around 260 USD, but then recovered relatively quickly toward the 340 USD area. That rebound shows that panic has not completely destroyed demand, but also how quickly a price can collapse when security news appears in an already nervous market.

What matters for the broader crypto picture is that security news in one project can further damage sentiment across the whole sector. In a calmer market, such news might have remained isolated. In a market that is already falling, it becomes another reason to reduce risk, even in tokens that have no direct connection to Zcash.

## Solana, HYPE, XRP and meme coins are watching Bitcoin

For HYPE, the 56–58 USD zone now looks important. This is the area where the market is trying to defend itself after the earlier decline and after news that Arthur Hayes sold his HYPE and NEAR positions. That sale alone does not bring down the market, but it is psychologically important because it comes from someone who had previously been very bullish on those tokens. When a well-known investor publicly takes profit, part of the market reads it as a signal to reduce risk.

Solana is currently holding around 60–62 USD, which is also a sensitive area. If SOL loses 60 USD, the market could next look toward 55–57 USD. XRP is trading around 1.10 USD, with the first more important support zone around 1.05–1.08 USD. With meme coins such as DOGE, SHIB and PEPE, the problem is that they often do not fall calmly. If Bitcoin loses support again, meme coins can very quickly search for lower liquidity zones.

## MSTR shows that pressure is spilling into stocks too

MSTR, or Strategy, is especially interesting because pressure on Bitcoin often spills into stocks connected to the Bitcoin narrative. MSTR is trading around 118–120 USD, showing that fear is visible not only in tokens, but also in equities that the market links to large Bitcoin exposure.

That matters because the crypto market is no longer completely separate from traditional financial markets. When Bitcoin weakens, tokens are not the only assets that fall. Pressure can spread to companies holding Bitcoin, miners, crypto exchanges, ETF flows and the broader risk-on sentiment. That is why the BTC support zone is now important not only for crypto traders, but also for part of the traditional market connected to the crypto story.

## What should be watched now?

The most important relationship is simple: BTC 59,000–60,000 USD and ETH 1,500–1,550 USD. If these zones are defended, the market may attempt a pause. If they break, the story changes quickly. Then ETH toward 1,400 USD, SOL below 60 USD, HYPE toward 50–52 USD and weaker meme coins become realistic risks again.

This decline should not be viewed only as a technical problem. Behind it are weaker sentiment, liquidations, fear of interest rates, falling risk assets and uncertainty around major crypto and AI narratives. When all of that comes together, even a small negative signal can trigger a fast reaction.

## Bitcoin sets the pace, Ethereum shows the depth of the problem

For ordinary investors, the most important thing is not to look at only one token. Ethereum may look like the main story because it fell to a 13-month low, but if Bitcoin loses its support, ETH will struggle to defend 1,500 USD on its own. The same applies to altcoins. If BTC stabilizes, altcoins may get room for a bounce. If BTC moves below 59,000 USD again, the market will probably become much more nervous.

That is why the question now is not only whether the price has already fallen enough. The question is where the selling pressure stops. If buyers defend BTC and ETH at these levels, the market can attempt short-term stabilization. If they fail, the next wave could be sharper because it would include additional liquidations, loss of confidence and exits from riskier altcoin positions.

- Ethereum is in an even more sensitive position because it is fighting for the 1,500–1,550 USD area, while weaker derivatives signals and falling TVL are visible in the background.
- The Zcash issue added more nervousness, but the quick recovery of ZEC toward the 340 USD area shows that the market still reacts to extreme drops.
- Solana, HYPE, LINK, AAVE, INJ, ADA, JTO, DOGE, SHIB and PEPE now depend mainly on whether Bitcoin can defend its key support.
- The key message of the day: if BTC and ETH defend their current zones, a short-term recovery is possible; if they lose them, the next wave of selling could become much more aggressive.
