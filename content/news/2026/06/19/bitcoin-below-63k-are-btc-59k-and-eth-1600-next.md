# Bitcoin Below 63k: Are BTC 59k and ETH 1,600 Next?

Bitcoin has fallen below $63,000 again, and the crypto market has lost part of the optimism seen earlier this week. The short rally linked to easing geopolitical tension and the US-Iran agreement did not turn into a stronger recovery. Instead, the market returned to the same question traders have been watching for days: can Bitcoin defend the $59,000–$60,000 area?

The weakness was not limited to Bitcoin. Ethereum also remained under pressure near the $1,700 area, while several major altcoins showed weakness as well. That suggests the market is not reacting to one single headline, but to a broader cooling of risk appetite. When global markets become more cautious, crypto often feels the pressure quickly because it remains one of the riskier asset classes.

## Bitcoin tests the lower edge again

Bitcoin has been moving in a relatively tight range over the past several days. The area below $63,000 now matters because the price is moving closer to the lower edge of that range. If buyers do not return with enough strength, the market’s focus could quickly shift toward the $59,000–$60,000 zone.

That level is not only technical. It is also psychological. If Bitcoin breaks the area traders have been watching as support, part of the market may conclude that the short recovery did not have enough strength and that a new correction phase could follow.

## Ethereum has its own problem

Ethereum does not look weaker only because of its price. Exchange and derivatives data also show a more cautious picture. According to available market analyses, more ETH flowed into Binance over the past few days, while the number of new depositors remained weak. At the same time, open interest in ETH futures fell from around $15 billion to about $10.3 billion.

This combination often means the market does not have enough fresh capital for a strong recovery. Larger ETH inflows to exchanges can suggest possible preparation for selling, while lower futures activity shows that part of the leverage and speculative energy has left the market.

## Why ETH 1,600 is now in focus

If Ethereum fails to hold the area around $1,700, the market could start looking at lower levels, including the zone near $1,600. That does not mean another drop is certain, but it shows where market fear is currently concentrated. ETH needs new buyers and better momentum to avoid additional pressure.

Ethereum is also important because it often acts as a signal for the broader altcoin market. If ETH remains weak, it is harder to imagine a strong and sustainable altcoin rally. In previous crypto cycles, altcoins often had their moment after a stronger Bitcoin move. Now, however, the question is whether that pattern will repeat in the same way.

## Broader markets are not giving clear support

The crypto market is not isolated right now. Global markets started cooling again after a short recovery, while investors continue to watch interest rates, the dollar, geopolitical headlines and oil prices. The fall in oil after the easing of tension around the Strait of Hormuz reduced part of the inflation fear, but it was not enough to help crypto hold a stronger upward move.

This shows that the market currently needs more than one positive headline. For the recovery to continue, crypto needs fresh capital, better liquidity and a clearer signal that buyers are ready to defend key levels. Without that, every bounce can look more like a short pause than the beginning of a new trend.

## What to watch now

For Bitcoin, the key area is around $59,000–$60,000. If that zone is defended, the current drop can remain just a correction inside a broader range. If it breaks, the market could start looking for a new lower support level.

For Ethereum, the focus is on the area around $1,700 and then on a possible test near $1,600. The important question is whether real demand will increase or whether exchange inflows and weaker futures data will continue to create selling pressure.
