# Croatian tech at the 2026 World Cup: Aircash and Sofascore collaboration

The 2026 World Cup is not only a football tournament. It is also a major digital moment in which fans increasingly follow results, schedules, statistics, campaigns, rewards and payments through their smartphones. That is why the collaboration between two Croatian digital products, Sofascore and Aircash, is especially interesting.

Sofascore and Aircash are not the same company. Sofascore is a Croatian sports app and platform for live scores, statistics and sports data. Aircash is a Croatian fintech app, digital wallet and regulated electronic money institution. Their collaboration is interesting because it connects two worlds that naturally meet during major sports events: sports attention and digital payments.

## What is Sofascore?

Sofascore is one of the best-known Croatian technology products with global reach. For users, it is an app where they can follow live scores, schedules, tables, statistics, match details and information about teams or players. In the sports space, Sofascore has become much more than a simple score display.

Today, users expect speed, accuracy and context from sports apps. They do not only want to know the score. They also want to know who scored, which statistics matter, what is happening in the group, who is moving forward and how the wider tournament picture is changing. That is why apps such as Sofascore become especially important during major competitions.

For the 2026 World Cup, such an app has an even larger role because the tournament has an expanded format, more national teams and more matches than before. That means more schedules, more results, more groups and more reasons for users to check the app several times during the day.

## What is Aircash?

Aircash is a Croatian fintech app and digital wallet. It allows users to use digital money, make payments, send funds and access other related financial services. Unlike a sports app, Aircash belongs to the world of electronic money and digital payments.

It is important that Aircash is not just an ordinary payment app, but a regulated fintech product. That means it has to operate within rules related to electronic money, user safety, compliance and data protection. Such products depend on user trust because they are not only about an app on a smartphone, but also about money, identity and transactions.

Aircash is also interesting because it shows how Croatian fintech can grow beyond the local market. If a digital wallet has a clear function and can be used in multiple countries, then sport, travel, fan campaigns and partnerships become a logical space for expansion.

{{< support1 >}}

## Why is the collaboration interesting?

The collaboration between Sofascore and Aircash is interesting because it connects user attention with the possibility of digital transactions. Sofascore has an audience that actively follows results and statistics during matches. Aircash has a fintech solution that can fit into fan promotions, payments, reward campaigns or other forms of user activity.

This is not only a question of advertising inside an app. Major sports events create moments in which users are emotionally involved and frequently active on their phones. When someone follows a match, checks the score or looks at the schedule, they are already inside a digital environment. If a fintech partner appears in that moment, the collaboration has a broader meaning.

Sofascore brings the sports context. Aircash brings the fintech layer. Together, they show how sport, apps and payments are increasingly overlapping. The fan is no longer only a viewer in front of the television. The fan is also an app user, a recipient of notifications, a participant in campaigns and a potential user of a digital wallet.

## Our view (Metaadvisor)

The collaboration between Sofascore and Aircash is interesting because it shows how sport, fintech, apps and fan campaigns are increasingly meeting in the same user space: the smartphone.

* Sofascore and Aircash are two separate Croatian digital products from different industries, but both show that Croatian tech can compete in a wider European and global market.
* Sofascore is built on sports technology, live scores, statistics and sports data.
* Aircash is built on digital payments, fintech infrastructure and everyday user transactions.
* The 2026 World Cup gives both products a shared context: fans, apps, smartphones and moments of high attention.
* Football is the trigger, but around it a broader digital sports economy is forming: apps, services, campaigns, payments and user habits.
* For Sofascore, the 2026 World Cup is an opportunity to further strengthen the habit of following sport through the app.
* For Aircash, it is an opportunity to be present at a moment when users are active, emotionally involved and open to digital campaigns.
* Most importantly, this is not about copying foreign models, but about two Croatian products that already have their place in the digital economy.
* If such products can connect around a major global event, that is a positive signal for Croatian tech.
