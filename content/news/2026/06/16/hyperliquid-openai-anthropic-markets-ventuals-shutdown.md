# Hyperliquid loses OpenAI and Anthropic markets

Hyperliquid is losing its OpenAI and Anthropic markets because Ventuals, the project behind them, is shutting down. These markets allowed users to trade synthetic perpetual futures contracts linked to the estimated valuations of private AI companies OpenAI and Anthropic.

They were not real shares. Users were not buying ownership in OpenAI or Anthropic, but speculating on their possible private valuations before a potential IPO.

## Hyperliquid had already done this with SpaceX

This is not the first time Hyperliquid has entered the pre-IPO story. Before the OpenAI and Anthropic markets, Hyperliquid had already attracted attention with a SpaceX pre-IPO market. At that time, users were not trading real SpaceX shares, but synthetic contracts that tracked market expectations around SpaceX’s value before its public listing.

The SpaceX pre-IPO perp on Hyperliquid launched with a reference price of around 150 USD, implying a valuation of about 1.78 trillion USD. After launch, the price quickly moved toward 203 USD. Later, before the IPO, the price was mentioned around 165 USD, while the IPO price was 135 USD.

That means the Hyperliquid pre-IPO market was close enough to show real market interest, but also far enough above the IPO price to show how much hype can push prices before the official market opens.

## OpenAI and Anthropic were a similar experiment

The OpenAI and Anthropic markets followed a similar logic. Since these companies are not publicly listed, ordinary users cannot buy their shares on a stock exchange. Ventuals therefore enabled trading on Hyperliquid through contracts that tried to track the estimated valuations of these private AI companies.

It is an interesting concept because it allows users to speculate on the value of the most watched AI companies before an IPO. But it also carries significant risk because it is not real ownership, but a synthetic market that depends on project rules, liquidity and the way prices are determined.

## What is being shut down now?

Ventuals is now shutting down, and the OPENAI and ANTHROPIC markets have been stopped. Existing positions were automatically settled. According to published data, the OPENAI market was settled at around 1,341.80 USD, while the ANTHROPIC market was settled at around 1,618.90 USD.

Those numbers do not represent the price of a real OpenAI or Anthropic share. They are the prices of synthetic contracts linked to the estimated valuation of private companies.

## Why is this interesting?

Because it shows that crypto markets are increasingly trying to trade what has traditionally been unavailable to small investors. SpaceX, OpenAI and Anthropic are not ordinary public companies with a stock ticker. They are private companies with enormous public interest, but small investors usually have no direct access before an IPO.

Hyperliquid and projects like Ventuals tried to open this space through pre-IPO / private-company perpetual markets. That can be interesting for price discovery, but it is not the same as a real stock market product.

The biggest problem is that private companies do not have a clear daily exchange price. Their value comes from funding rounds, secondary transactions, media reports and estimates. When such a valuation is translated into a crypto perp market, the question immediately becomes: who determines the reference price, and what happens if the project running the market shuts down?

Ventuals now shows exactly that risk. A market can be interesting, and the volume can be large, but if the project shuts down, positions are closed and users exit according to the settlement rules.

## Our view (Metaadvisor)

* Hyperliquid had already shown with the SpaceX pre-IPO market that there is interest in crypto trading of private companies before an IPO.
* The OpenAI and Anthropic markets were part of the same idea: speculation on the valuation of private AI companies, without buying real shares.
* Ventuals is now shutting down, and the OPENAI and ANTHROPIC markets were settled at prices of around 1,341.80 USD and 1,618.90 USD.
* The main lesson is simple: a pre-IPO crypto market can look like early access to a major company, but it is not ownership. It is a synthetic bet on an estimated valuation.
* That is why these products can be interesting, but users must understand that the risk is not only in the price. The risk is also in the market itself, the model and the project operating it.
