Spot Ether exchange-traded funds in the U.S. gained a staggering **$1.83 billion in net inflows over five trading days**, dwarfing the $171 million that spot Bitcoin ETFs garnered during the same period, according to CoinGlass data ([cointelegraph.com](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2025/08/28/doge-faces-selling-pressure-as-usd200m-whale-transfer-to-binance-caps-rally?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

On the latest trading day, nine ETH funds registered inflows totaling $310.3 million, while eleven Bitcoin funds only managed $81.1 million.

Ethereum’s price outperformed Bitcoin this week, climbing roughly 5% from its Tuesday low, compared to Bitcoin’s 2.8% gain.

Since early July, Ether ETFs are nearing $10 billion in cumulative inflows, and have amassed $13.6 billion over their 13-month lifespan.

Bitcoin ETFs, live for 20 months, have accumulated $54 billion in total inflows.

VanEck CEO Jan van Eck dubbed Ethereum “the Wall Street token,” and Bloomberg ETF analyst James Seyffart cites investment advisors as the largest stakeholders—Goldman Sachs holds a leading $712 million in exposure to Ether ETFs.

### Context & Significance:
This extraordinary inflow trend marks a clear shift in institutional sentiment toward Ethereum, driven by its broader utility and regulatory tailwinds. Legislation like the GENIUS Act is likely reinforcing Ethereum’s evolution from a speculative crypto asset into a heavyweight in traditional finance.
