While digitization has transformed **consumer banking in India**, corporate banking remains stuck with outdated systems, paper-heavy processes, and spreadsheet-driven workflows. **TransBnk** wants to change that. The three-year-old startup has raised **$25 million** in a round led by Bessemer Venture Partners to accelerate its mission.

Over the past decade, India’s consumer fintech boom has been powered by innovations like the **Unified Payments Interface (UPI)** and the rise of payment aggregators. But these advances have had little impact on transaction banking for businesses, where payments, collections, and statements remain largely manual. Business clients juggle multiple online banking portals and reconcile accounts with Excel sheets. This inefficiency persists despite India being home to nearly **75 million SMEs**, the largest SME market in the world.

The untapped opportunity is massive. According to Chiratae Ventures and The Digital Fifth, India’s **B2B fintech market could reach $20 billion by 2030**. JM Financial reported last year that India already hosts 26 fintech unicorns worth a combined $90 billion, though most focus on payments and lending rather than core banking infrastructure.

Mumbai-based TransBnk was founded by former bankers **Vaibhav Tambe, Lavin Kotian, Pulak Jain, and Sachin Gupta**. Their platform aims to connect banks and corporate clients through APIs and cloud infrastructure, streamlining transaction processes and reducing manual work.

### Context & significance:  
If successful, TransBnk could redefine how corporate banking operates across India’s digital-first economy and unlock new efficiencies for millions of businesses.
