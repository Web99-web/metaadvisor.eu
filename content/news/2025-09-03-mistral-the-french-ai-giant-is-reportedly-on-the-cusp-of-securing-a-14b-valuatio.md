French AI startup **Mistral AI** is finalizing a €2 billion investment at a post-money valuation of $14 billion, Bloomberg reports, positioning the company as one of Europe’s most valuable tech startups.  

The two-year-old company, founded by former **DeepMind and Meta researchers**, develops **open source language models** and **Le Chat**, an AI chatbot built for European audiences.  

If confirmed, this would mark Mistral’s first major funding round since June 2024, when it was valued at €5.8 billion. The company has already raised over €1 billion from major investors including **Andreessen Horowitz and General Catalyst**.  

The deal comes as European AI startups are gaining unprecedented momentum. Investments surged 55% year-on-year in Q1 2025, with 12 European startups reaching unicorn status in the first half of the year.
