**CIMG Inc.** has raised **$55 million** through a stock issuance, allocating the funds to purchase **500 Bitcoin (BTC)**. The move underscores a growing trend of corporations adding crypto directly to their balance sheets.

The company follows in the footsteps of **MicroStrategy**, now holding over 200,000 BTC, and Japan’s **Metaplanet**, which recently adopted Bitcoin as a core reserve asset.

This signals a shift in corporate finance, where Bitcoin is increasingly seen not only as a speculative investment but as a **strategic store of value** amid global monetary uncertainty.

Such moves also tie corporate treasuries more closely to crypto markets, creating potential benefits in diversification but also exposing firms to Bitcoin’s volatility.

**Our take:**  
CIMG’s purchase is another milestone in institutional adoption. If more companies follow, Bitcoin could evolve into a mainstream treasury reserve asset, much like gold did in the 20th century.
