Web3 startups raised **$9.6 billion** in venture funding during Q2 2025, the second-largest quarter on record, even as deal counts hit multi-year lows, according to **Outlier Ventures**.  

The report points to a maturing market where investors are putting more money into fewer projects, emphasizing infrastructure and experienced teams over speculative bets.  

Only 306 deals were disclosed, the lowest since mid-2023, but the median deal size rose at every stage.  

- **Series A**: median $17.6M, 27 deals totaling $420M, the strongest since 2022.  
- **Seed**: median $6.6M, also rising.  
- **Token fundraising**: private sales raised $410M across 15 deals (best since 2021), while public token sales plunged 83% to $134M.  

Sectors like **crypto infrastructure, mining/validation, and compute networks** captured the largest rounds (median $70–112M), while consumer-facing areas such as marketplaces lagged.  

“Capital is consolidating around projects that can provide the rails for the next phase of adoption,” Outlier wrote, adding that infrastructure-first bets are seen as “indispensable” for Web3.
