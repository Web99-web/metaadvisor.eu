**Ark Invest**, the New York-based investment firm led by **Cathie Wood**, added more than 100,000 shares of **Figma** to its **ARKW ETF** after the stock plunged nearly 20% following its first earnings report since going public.

The disclosure came via the popular Ark Invest Tracker X account, but has not yet been independently confirmed by ARK’s official filings. Decrypt reached out to Ark Invest for comment but did not immediately receive a response.

The stock purchase aligns with Ark’s past strategy of adding to high-growth names during periods of weakness.  
“This is a textbook Cathie Wood move: leaning into volatility and backing companies she sees as long-term disruptors,” said Dan Dadybayo of Unstoppable Wallet.

In July 2025, Ark bought about 143,000 **Tesla** shares after an earnings selloff, while trimming roughly 34,000 **Coinbase** and 68,000 **Roku** shares, according to a Barchart report.

Despite the selloff, ARK appears to signal conviction that Figma’s collaborative design moat, product momentum, and high margins outweigh short-term risks.

**Figma**, which went public in July, reported quarterly revenue of **$249.6 million**, up 41% year-over-year. Rising expenses and thinner margins fueled doubts about sustainability, sending shares down nearly 20% to **$54.56**.

## Our take

This is a classic **Ark Invest** play – buying when others sell. Cathie Wood has long championed “disruptive innovation,” and Figma fits squarely into that vision.  
Still, the 20% drop shows the market isn’t convinced about quick profitability. Long-term, if Figma sustains 40%+ revenue growth, it could become another **Tesla-like** success story in Ark’s portfolio.
