**HONG KONG** — Binance founder **Changpeng “CZ” Zhao** believes that the **convergence of equity markets and crypto** is ushering in a new era for digital assets, expanding institutional access and crypto’s global footprint.  

But he cautioned that the industry still faces **significant risks**, especially as it enters its first major bull cycle since such structures gained momentum.

Speaking at **Bitcoin Asia** in Hong Kong, CZ highlighted the move by public companies to hold **bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies** on balance sheets — following **MicroStrategy’s** lead — as a turning point.  

> “In the world’s largest economy, 90–95% of the money is managed by institutions. Until ETFs and treasury companies, those players couldn’t participate in crypto at scale,” he said.

By opening equity markets in the U.S., Hong Kong, and Japan to crypto, CZ said the industry is effectively “bringing equity markets to crypto, or bringing crypto to them — depending on perspective.”

Beyond **bitcoin treasuries and ETFs**, Zhao pointed to the **tokenization of real-world assets (RWAs)** as another transformative trend: stablecoins, treasury bills, commodities, real estate, and even **personal income streams** are being tokenized, channeling “hundreds of millions and billions” into the crypto economy.

> “We’re going both ways,” CZ said. “Equity markets now have access to crypto, and we’re bringing real-world assets into crypto. This is fantastic.”

Despite his optimism, CZ warned that not every company will succeed.  
Some firms may use crypto treasuries as a way to **inflate their stock price**, while others lack the expertise to manage complex portfolios of digital assets.  

Such failures, he added, should not overshadow the **structural transformation** underway: the growing integration of traditional markets with crypto is becoming unavoidable.
