With Bitcoin (BTC) hovering just above $110,000 and Ether (ETH) consolidating after fresh records, **Solana (SOL)** has emerged as a standout performer.  

On Monday, SOL traded at around $211 — a 33% increase from August lows. Over the past month, it gained 34% against BTC and 14% against ETH.  

Analysts point to a broader rotation into altcoins. Sergei Gorev from YouHodler noted that liquidity has been moving from BTC into second-tier tokens like SOL.  

Jeff Dorman, CIO of Arca, highlighted that SOL could replicate ETH’s playbook earlier this year, driven by stablecoin adoption, ETF inflows, and strong demand from digital asset treasuries (DATs). ETH surged nearly 200% since April.  

The first U.S.-listed Solana ETF launched in July, but it was futures-based. Spot filings from VanEck and Fidelity are pending later this year. Meanwhile, three Solana-focused DATs are raising as much as $2.65 billion.  

---

**Our take:**  
Solana is cementing its role as a serious altcoin. If ETF approvals and DAT funding deliver, SOL may well mirror — or even surpass — ETH’s trajectory.
