**Nvidia launched an independent internal investigation on November 8 after reports suggested that CEO Jensen Huang may have been targeted by a foreign intelligence operation. Media reports link the controversy to Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman formerly associated with Nvidia customer Megaspeed. No public evidence establishes that corporate secrets were compromised or that espionage occurred.**

## Who is Alice Huang?

Alice Huang, also known as Huang Le, is a Chinese citizen who was previously involved with Singapore-based cloud computing company Megaspeed International. Online claims that she has “no past” are misleading: corporate records document parts of her business history, including a leadership role at Megaspeed in 2023.

Other accounts of her education and earlier work in media and finance have not all been independently verified. Her current location and precise ongoing relationship with Megaspeed remain uncertain.

## What connects her to Jensen Huang?

Photos and videos place Alice and Jensen Huang together at industry events in Taipei in 2024 and 2025. Jensen Huang acknowledged infidelity in a message to employees, but did not identify the other person in that statement. News organizations reported allegations from US intelligence sources that Alice Huang may have had links to Chinese intelligence.

Those allegations are unproven, and China has rejected them. A confirmed admission of an affair must not be confused with proof of spying or the release of confidential information.

## Why does Megaspeed matter?

Megaspeed provides cloud computing capacity involving high-end AI processors and describes itself as an Nvidia partner. US authorities have scrutinized whether associated arrangements allowed Chinese customers to access restricted AI technology. Such scrutiny does not establish that unlawful chip transfers took place, or that Alice Huang personally directed them.

Advanced AI chips sit at the center of US-China technological competition. Washington restricts exports of some processors because of potential military and intelligence applications.

## What exactly is Nvidia investigating?

Nvidia's board established a committee of independent directors, supported by outside counsel, to review allegations of possible foreign intelligence targeting. Jensen Huang has said he will cooperate. Nvidia says it has no reason to believe its trade secrets or US national security were compromised and that Huang will continue leading the company.

**This is an internal corporate inquiry, not confirmation of criminal charges against Jensen Huang.**

## Why are investors watching?

Nvidia is central to global AI infrastructure, while Jensen Huang is closely associated with its strategy and relationships. The investigation arrives as investors already question the scale and profitability of AI spending. Uncertainty over leadership can therefore add to existing pressure on technology shares.

US lawmakers have requested a closed-door briefing from Huang on November 18. Neither the congressional response nor Nvidia's internal findings can be treated as predetermined.
