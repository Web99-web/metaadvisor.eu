According to **Julio Moreno**, Head of Research at **CryptoQuant**, **US-based spot Bitcoin ETFs** now regularly generate **$5–10 billion in daily trading volume** on active days, sometimes **surpassing most cryptocurrency exchanges** :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}.

Nevertheless, **Binance remains the leader** in spot trading, with Bitcoin volumes soaring to **$18 billion** and Ether hitting **$11 billion** on peak activity days :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}.

Data from **CoinGlass** shows that the 11 US spot Bitcoin ETFs collectively record **$2.77 billion in daily trading volume**, which is **about 67 % of Binance’s daily Bitcoin spot volume** (~$4.1 billion) :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}.

Together, these figures underscore the growing role of ETFs in Bitcoin's institutional adoption and their evolving impact on spot market liquidity.
