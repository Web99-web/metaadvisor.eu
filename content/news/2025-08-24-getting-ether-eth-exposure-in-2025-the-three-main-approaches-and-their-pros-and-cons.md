Ether (ETH) is trading near all-time highs, and bullish forecasts — including Tom Lee’s $15,000 year-end target — have put the spotlight on **how** to gain ETH exposure most effectively.

Per CoinDesk Data (~$4,783), strong institutional demand underpins price action. Tom Lee (Fundstrat; chairman of BitMine Immersion Technologies — BMNR) points to stablecoins, DeFi and real-world asset (RWA) tokenization as key drivers.

### 1) Direct ETH ownership
**Pros:** full control; access to DeFi/NFT/staking; 24/7 liquidity.  
**Cons:** custody & security (self-custody vs. third-party); regulatory uncertainty; fees (exchange, gas).

### 2) Spot ETH ETFs
Regulated exposure via brokerage accounts; some issuers are seeking SEC approval to add **staking**.  
**Pros:** simple, regulated; potential extra yield (if staking approved).  
**Cons:** fees; no on-chain utility; regulatory timing risk.

### 3) Corporate treasuries via equities (ETH treasury)
Invest in shares of companies holding ETH (e.g., BMNR).  
**Pros:** potential combo of corporate growth and ETH exposure (incl. staking income).  
**Cons:** **double volatility** (equity + crypto); dilution/capital-raising risk; governance factors.

**Bottom line:** With ETH near ATHs, the decision is **which vehicle** best fits your risk tolerance — direct tokens, spot ETFs or equity exposure to ETH treasuries.
