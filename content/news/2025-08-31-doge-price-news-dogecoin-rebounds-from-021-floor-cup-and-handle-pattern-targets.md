Dogecoin (**DOGE**) bounced from the **$0.21 floor**, forming a potential **cup-and-handle pattern** that targets a move toward **$0.30**, according to CoinDesk analysis.  

The token posted a late-session rally on Aug. 30–31, with whale and exchange flows pointing to sustained institutional participation despite broader macro uncertainty.  

Market data showed DOGE stabilizing above the **$0.21–$0.22 support zone**, after which the pattern emerged that technical analysts often interpret as bullish. If confirmed, the projected target stands near **$0.30**.  

Analysts cautioned that trading volume will be critical for confirmation and noted that while demand could accelerate a breakout, risks remain as the broader crypto market continues to face macroeconomic headwinds.
