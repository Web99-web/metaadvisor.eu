XRP advanced sharply during the Aug. 22–23 session, testing multi-month resistance near $3.10 after weeks of sideways trading.

The move coincided with dovish commentary from Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell at Jackson Hole, reinforcing expectations of a September rate cut and lifting risk sentiment across digital assets.

On-chain settlement volumes on the XRP Ledger surged 500% earlier this week, adding to optimism around institutional adoption despite ongoing whale distribution.

**Price Action Summary**
- XRP rose 8.56% across the 23-hour session (Aug. 22, 11:00 to Aug. 23, 10:00), from $2.83 to $3.03.  
- The token swung between $2.79 and $3.10, creating an 11% intraday range.  
- The breakout occurred at 14:00 UTC on Aug. 22, with XRP jumping from $2.84 to $3.03 on 667.4M volume — five times session averages.  
- Late-session volatility capped the move, with XRP easing 0.47% to settle near $3.01.  
- Support has formed at $2.97–$3.00 while resistance remains firm at $3.08–$3.10.

**Technical Structure**
- **Breakout zone:** $2.84–$2.97 accumulation triggered upside impulse.  
- **Resistance:** $3.08–$3.10 supply blocked follow-through.  
- **Support:** psychological $2.97–$3.00 floor, repeatedly defended intraday.  
- **Volume:** breakout candle logged 667.4M trades, 72% above weekly average.  
- **Pattern:** continuation setup if $3.00 holds; fading volume suggests consolidation first.

**What to Watch**
- Whether $3.00 holds during profit-taking or a deeper retrace tests $2.95.  
- Breakout confirmation above $3.08–$3.10 opening a path toward $3.25.  
- Fed policy signaling into September; on-chain activity and whale flows.
