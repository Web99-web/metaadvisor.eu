This is a daily analysis by CoinDesk analyst and Chartered Market Technician Omkar Godbole.  

Bitcoin (BTC) at $108,005 has breached key support levels, signaling growing bearish momentum and the risk of sliding toward $100,000.  

The leading cryptocurrency fell 6.5% in August, ending a four-month winning streak as U.S.-listed spot ETFs saw $751 million in outflows (SoSoValue).  

The latest drop pushed BTC below several crucial supports: the Ichimoku cloud, the 50-day and 100-day SMAs, and horizontal zones at $111,965 (May high) and $109,364 (December high).  

These breakdowns highlight weakening market structure, confirming a bearish shift in indicators such as the GMMA and MACD histogram.  

The short-term GMMA EMA bands (green) crossed beneath the long-term bands (red), while the weekly MACD histogram dipped below zero — clear bearish signals.  

Taken together, the signals suggest a sustained sell-off is possible, with downside targets near the 200-day SMA at $101,366 and possibly $100,000.  

Seasonal trends reinforce the outlook: since 2013, BTC has averaged -3.49% in September, closing lower in 8 of the last 12 years (Coinglass).  

**Our comment:** Unless bulls reclaim $113,510, “Red September” could once again live up to its reputation as Bitcoin’s weakest month.
