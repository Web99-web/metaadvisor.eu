The **Sky Glass Air** is Sky’s latest attempt to deliver a **budget smart TV** – and it succeeds.  

It is **lighter, slimmer and cheaper** than the Glass gen 2, priced from **£309** or just £6/month on 48-month interest-free credit. Available in three sizes, its strength lies in the combination of **Sky’s software and broadband TV service**.  

Full use requires a Sky subscription (from £15/month), though it can still function as a basic smart TV with apps, HDMI ports and tuner.  

Unlike pricier siblings, the Air lacks the integrated soundbar and aluminium body, but gains from **significant reductions in weight and thickness**. Only a power cable and internet connection are needed.  

It also features **Sky’s “Hello Sky” voice control**, allowing users to power on, search, and adjust settings with simple commands.  

**Our comment:** Sky Glass Air shows that a decent smart TV doesn’t need to break the bank – a strong choice for those planning to use Sky’s ecosystem.
