## Celestia (TIA): modular innovation on the rise

For years, the blockchain industry has searched for a way to combine **true scalability** with decentralization. Celestia (TIA) is pioneering this shift with its **modular blockchain design**.

### Why Celestia stands out

- **Modularity at its core:** Celestia separates *data availability* from execution. Developers can build custom rollups while Celestia ensures secure and decentralized data access.  
- **Scalable by design:** With data availability sampling, the network expands with demand while keeping fees low.  
- **Ecosystem friendly:** Rollups and applications can plug into Celestia, fostering diversity and innovation.

### Strong financial base

Celestia holds a **$100M treasury**, enough to fund **six years of development**. This stability is rare in the crypto world and strengthens its long-term outlook.

- The $1.30–1.60 range is seen as a key support zone for patient investors.

### Conclusion

Celestia (TIA) is more than just another token – it’s a **platform redefining blockchain design**. With modular architecture, strong reserves, and long-term vision, Celestia is positioned as a potential backbone of the next decentralized wave.
