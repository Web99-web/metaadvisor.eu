El Salvador, the first country in the world to adopt Bitcoin as legal tender, has taken a step to safeguard its national BTC reserves. The government has distributed **$678 million worth of Bitcoin** across 14 different wallets to mitigate potential **quantum risks**.

The Ministry of Finance explained that this wallet-splitting strategy serves as a precautionary measure against possible future threats posed by quantum computing, which could eventually undermine the cryptographic algorithms securing the Bitcoin network.

According to the statement, distributing funds across multiple wallets does not affect the daily use of BTC in the country, but it strengthens the resilience of the national reserves against global security challenges. This move further underlines that El Salvador views its Bitcoin strategy as a long-term, security-focused endeavor.
