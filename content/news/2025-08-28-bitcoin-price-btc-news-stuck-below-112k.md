Crypto markets attempted a rally on Thursday, but steady **selling pressure** across the U.S. afternoon cut gains short.

Bitcoin (BTC) briefly topped $113,000 before retreating to **$111,800**, down 0.7% over the past 24 hours. Ether (ETH) and XRP saw heavier losses, sliding 2.1% and 1.4% to $2.8945, respectively.

Among the majors, **Solana’s SOL** outperformed, up 3.1% on the day.

Meanwhile, **gold continued its quiet climb**, rising 0.8% to **$3,477 per ounce**, edging closer to its record high of $3,534 hit earlier this month. For August, gold is up nearly 4%, while bitcoin is down 5.2%.

Despite macro conditions that should support bitcoin—lower interest rates and a weaker dollar—the narrative of digital gold is failing to gain traction.

### Looking ahead:
September could bring resumed **Federal Reserve rate cuts** and the appointment of one or two new (likely dovish) Fed officials by President Trump, setting the stage for a potentially volatile final stretch of 2025.

Stephen, CoinDesk’s Managing Editor for Markets, noted that **10x Research favors a short strangle strategy**, as market signals point to near-term calm in bitcoin’s price action.
