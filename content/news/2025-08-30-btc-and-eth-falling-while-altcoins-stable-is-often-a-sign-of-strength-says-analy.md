Crypto analyst and macroeconomist **Alex Krüger** believes the market looks so broken and bearish that it could actually turn bullish.  

On Saturday, he wrote on X that “most crypto charts now look so broken and bearish that it’s bullish.” He argued that when price action looks this bad, panic has usually gone far enough for a reversal to be near.  

He shared bitcoin (BTC) and ether (ETH) spot charts showing both had fallen below short-term upward trendlines, creating a bearish technical picture, while solana (SOL) showed relative resilience.  

Alongside those, he posted BTC-USDT and ETH-USDT derivatives charts combining futures data (funding rates, long liquidations) with options metrics like skew, showing traders had turned heavily defensive.  

Krüger noted that bitcoin and ether absorbed most of the selling pressure, while many altcoins had already stopped falling earlier in the day — a divergence he said is “often a sign of upcoming strength,” suggesting panic selling may be winding down.
