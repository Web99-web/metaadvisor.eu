**Antonio Gracias** has emerged as part of a new Silicon Valley movement—especially among right-wing billionaires—drawn to the **therapeutic and commercial promise of MDMA**.

Months before Gracias assumed a central role in dismantling U.S. federal agencies under the so-called “Department of Government Efficiency” (Doge), he was at **Burning Man**.

In the Nevada desert, at a sunrise tribute rave, Gracias danced alongside **Rick Doblin**, the nation’s most prominent advocate for psychedelic drugs and founder of **MAPS (Multidisciplinary Association for Psychedelic Studies).**

Doblin has spent decades campaigning for the legalization of MDMA as a therapy tool. Gracias, convinced by the evidence, donated **$1 million** to MAPS in 2020 and another **$16 million** to Harvard in 2023 for psychedelic research.

That same year, MAPS’ pharmaceutical arm, **Lykos Therapeutics**, raised **$100 million** while awaiting **FDA approval**. Once dismissed as a mere party drug, MDMA is now being positioned as a powerful therapeutic aid with massive market potential.

Dressed more for an investment boardroom than a rave, Gracias exemplifies the crossover of **politics, business, and new therapies**, while reinforcing his close alliance with Elon Musk.
