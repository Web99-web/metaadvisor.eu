A Florida man, already serving a 47-year prison sentence for orchestrating a series of violent home invasions targeting crypto holders, has been handed more time for attacking a witness, according to the U.S. Justice Department.  

**Remy St Felix (25)** was sentenced to an additional seven years for assaulting a witness who testified against him. The attack occurred at a North Carolina detention facility, where St Felix struck the shackled witness in the face and body while calling him a “rat.”  

Although 36 months of the new sentence will run concurrently with his existing term, the ruling effectively adds nearly four years to his incarceration.  

The case comes amid a rise in **“wrench attacks”** — physical assaults on crypto holders aimed at bypassing even the most advanced security protections.
