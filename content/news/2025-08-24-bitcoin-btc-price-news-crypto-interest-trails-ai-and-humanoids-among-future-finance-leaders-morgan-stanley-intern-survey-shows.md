The “**we’re still early**” line still rings true in 2025: even with **bitcoin (BTC)** above **$100,000**, broader **adoption** remains nascent.

A **Morgan Stanley** intern survey backs that up (U.S.: 500+ from June 10–27; Europe: 147 from June 26–July 7). Only **18%** **own or use** crypto (up from 13% a year earlier). **Interest** has risen to **26%** (from 23%), while **55%** remain **uninterested** (down from 63%). That’s striking given Wall Street’s embrace of BTC via **spot ETFs**. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

**ETFs & prices.** **11 spot BTC ETFs** have amassed roughly **$53.7B**, while **ether ETFs** have taken in **$12.4B**. Corporates are steadily adding both assets to balance sheets. **BTC** has cleared **$100K** this year; **ether** set a fresh high above **$4,800** on Friday. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

**AI out in front.** **AI** is already commonplace among future finance leaders: **96%** of U.S. interns and **91%** of European interns use it at least occasionally. Nearly all say it **“saves me time”** and is **“easy to use,”** though **88%** think it still **needs better accuracy**. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

**Humanoids as a market wave.** Most interns would like to own **humanoids** (human-like robots) but are wary of social impacts. **Morgan Stanley** estimates a **>$5T** humanoid market by **2050**, with **>1B** units, **90%** for industrial/commercial use. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}
