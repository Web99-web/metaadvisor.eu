The **Bitcoin (BTC)** bull run has already stalled as long-term holder wallets continue selling and ETF inflows slow. To make matters worse, another lesser-known but significant market variable is turning against bitcoin bulls, signaling new challenges ahead.

That variable is the **MOVE index**, created by Harley Bassman, a former managing director at Merrill Lynch. The index calculates implied volatility using a weighted average of option prices on one-month U.S. Treasury options across maturities (2, 5, 10, and 30 years). This method captures the collective expectations of market participants about future interest rate movements.

The **MOVE index** surged from 77 to 89 in just three days, marking the sharpest rise since early April, when President **Donald Trump’s** tariffs rattled global markets — including bitcoin, which dropped to **$75,000**.

More importantly, momentum indicators like the **MACD** are signaling a clear bullish shift, suggesting the index is poised for continued gains. That calls for caution from bitcoin bulls, as periods of elevated Treasury market volatility, as captured by the MOVE index, are known to cause **global liquidity tightening**.

U.S. Treasuries are widely regarded as high-quality liquid assets and form the cornerstone of the global collateral system, reducing credit risk and ensuring smooth capital flows across financial markets.

However, heightened Treasury volatility tends to disrupt liquidity, raise borrowing costs, and create ripple effects across credit markets and the broader financial system. In such conditions, lenders demand higher risk premiums, and market participants retreat from riskier assets — ultimately slowing capital flows and adding systemic stress.

## Our take

**Bitcoin (BTC)** is currently facing pressure from macroeconomic headwinds, and the rise in the **MOVE index** clearly shows that global liquidity can dry up quickly. Historically, whenever the bond market destabilizes, crypto markets feel the shockwaves.  
For investors, this means: short-term volatility, but long-term — another resilience test for **Bitcoin** as a global store of value.
