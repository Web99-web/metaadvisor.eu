**BitMine**, a digital asset investment firm, has purchased $65 million worth of Ethereum (ETH), according to a statement released this week.  

The company’s chairman compared the move to the “1971 moment” when the U.S. under President Richard Nixon ended the dollar’s convertibility to gold, marking a historic monetary shift. He argued that Ethereum is now playing a similar transformative role in global finance.  

> “Ethereum represents the next phase of trustless value transfer and programmable money. Just as 1971 changed the future of currencies, ETH today is reshaping finance for the digital era,” he said.  

The firm suggested that institutional adoption of ETH is accelerating, with decentralized finance (DeFi), tokenization, and treasury demand forming the backbone of long-term growth.  

Market analysts noted that such large-scale acquisitions could strengthen ETH’s position as a reserve digital asset alongside Bitcoin, especially with the rise of ETFs and corporate digital treasuries.
