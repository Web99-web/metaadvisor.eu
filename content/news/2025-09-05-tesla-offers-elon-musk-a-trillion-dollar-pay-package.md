**Elon Musk** could become the world’s first **trillionaire** if he achieves the targets set by Tesla under the most ambitious compensation plan ever proposed.  

Tesla outlined this in its latest shareholder report with the lead-in: *“Yes, you read that correctly.”*  

Under the plan, Musk must grow Tesla’s market value from just above **$1 trillion** today to **$8.5 trillion** over the next decade.  

If successful, he would receive new shares that increase his stake from nearly 16% to beyond 25%, pushing his net worth beyond **$2 trillion**.  

**Forbes** currently estimates Musk’s net worth at around **$430.9 billion**, while **Larry Ellison** sits second with **$272.3 billion**.  

The plan was filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission and comes at a time when critics argue Musk’s political statements may be dragging on Tesla’s performance. European sales have dropped by 40%, a decline partly attributed to his outspoken views and brief political alliance.  

Moreover, Musk juggles Tesla along with other ventures like **SpaceX**, **Neuralink**, **xAI**, and **The Boring Company**, while maintaining an active presence on **X** (formerly Twitter).

## Our take
Tesla is clearly betting on **Musk’s personal brand** as a core asset. But targeting a market cap of $8.5 trillion exceeds the combined value of Apple, Microsoft, and Google. This raises a critical question: is the company anchoring too much of its future on Musk’s volatility and charisma, especially during periods when investors are craving stability?
