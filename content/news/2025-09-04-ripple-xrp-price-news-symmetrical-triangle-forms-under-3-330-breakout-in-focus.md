XRP is trading in a narrowing range, with a **symmetrical triangle pattern** forming below the **$3.00 resistance**.  

The token rebounded from lows near **$2.76**, supported by whale accumulation, while institutional liquidations created downward pressure. Current resistance levels at **$2.86** and **$3.30** remain critical for momentum.  

Analysts note that breaking above **$3.30** could trigger fresh buying interest, potentially targeting the $3.60–$3.80 zone. However, failure to hold support at $2.70 would risk a correction toward $2.50.  

On-chain data shows over **340 million XRP accumulated by large wallets** in the past two weeks, highlighting continued long-term interest despite weak sentiment.

**Our take:**  
XRP has struggled to deliver strong performance compared to ETH and SOL, but whales seem committed. The $3.30 breakout level could be a turning point — otherwise, XRP risks staying stuck in sideways action.
