Webull has rolled out crypto trading services in Australia just days after re-launching its crypto offering in the U.S. — having previously pulled out in 2023. Through a partnership with Coinbase Prime, Webull Australia now offers access to **240 cryptocurrencies** :contentReference[oaicite:21]{index=21}.

Rob Talevski, CEO of Webull Securities Australia, said, “The addition of cryptocurrencies and digital tokens ... underlines our ongoing mission to provide Australian investors with the freedom to trade what they want” :contentReference[oaicite:22]{index=22}.

Webull CEO Anthony Denier explained that removing crypto services in 2023 “was against client expectations,” and the relaunch is a natural realignment with customer demand :contentReference[oaicite:23]{index=23}.

The expansion complements Webull’s global strategy, following crypto launches in Brazil earlier in 2025, with more regions expected to follow :contentReference[oaicite:24]{index=24}.

**Our twist:** Webull’s strategic push underscores a growing trend: traditional brokers increasingly evolve into hybrid platforms blending conventional asset trading with robust digital asset services. However, this model also raises regulatory complexity—especially in markets like Australia, where the legal framework for crypto products remains in development.
