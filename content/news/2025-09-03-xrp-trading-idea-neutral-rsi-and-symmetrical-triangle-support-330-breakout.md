**XRP** saw a volatile session, moving between **$2.76 and $2.86**, as geopolitical and monetary policy uncertainty weighed on crypto markets.  

Data indicates that over **340 million XRP** were accumulated by whales, suggesting institutional interest despite ongoing selling pressure.  

Analysts are split on the outlook, but key resistance levels are at **$2.86** and **$3.30**. Traders are watching for a sustained close above these levels to confirm further upside momentum.  

Across the broader market, traders warn of a potential **12% monthly drop in Bitcoin**, while **Solana (SOL)** has led recent gains among major cryptocurrencies.  

---

**Our take:**  
Institutional buying supports XRP, but the battle is at the $3.30 resistance. Without a breakout, September’s bearish seasonality could keep gains limited despite whale activity.
