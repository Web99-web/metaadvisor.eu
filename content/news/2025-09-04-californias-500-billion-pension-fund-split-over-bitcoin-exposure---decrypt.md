California’s $506 billion public pension system CalPERS revealed a sharp split among board candidates over Bitcoin exposure.  

The fund already holds 410,596 shares of Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy), valued at nearly $166 million, giving it significant indirect Bitcoin exposure. Still, some candidates strongly opposed direct investments.  

Candidate Dominick Bei countered, saying CalPERS “owns shares in the world’s largest Bitcoin-holding company,” while opponents insisted that investing in a Bitcoin-related business differs from buying BTC directly.  

Michael Saylor’s Strategy controls over 636,000 BTC worth more than $70 billion.  

Kadan Stadelmann of Komodo Platform told Decrypt: “Bitcoin is certainly not too volatile for pensions, especially given inflation. The market has clearly chosen Bitcoin as a store of value.”

The CalPERS debate highlights that institutions are already exposed to Bitcoin but lack the political will to acknowledge and expand it.
