Taco Bell’s Chief Digital and Technology Officer says the company is having an “active conversation” about when AI makes sense at the drive-through – and when it doesn’t.  

The chain has rolled out **voice AI-powered ordering** at more than 500 locations. But the rollout has sparked viral criticism, such as one customer ordering **18,000 water cups** just to bypass the AI and reach a human worker.  

Dane Matthews admitted that even he has mixed experiences: “Sometimes it lets me down, but sometimes it really surprises me.”  

For now, Taco Bell appears undecided on whether to go all-in on AI or allow flexibility across franchises. Matthews suggested that at busier locations with long lines, it might make sense for humans to take orders instead of AI.  

“For our teams, we’ll help coach them: at your restaurant, at these times, we recommend you use voice AI, or closely monitor it and jump in as needed,” he said.
