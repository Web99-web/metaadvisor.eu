U.S. stocks are flashing signs of euphoria, contrasting with a muted crypto market as traders look for clues on what’s next.

Bank of America’s Global Equity Risk-Love indicator, which tracks investor sentiment, suggests that positioning, volatility, and technicals in equities are turning dangerously bullish.

“BofA’s Global Equity Risk-Love indicator jumped to 1.4, its highest in 13 months,” The Kobeissi Letter wrote Monday. “This metric has surged from panic to euphoria in just four months. Since 1987, sentiment has only been higher 7% of the time.”

Since April, both U.S. equities and crypto have surged, buoyed by dovish economic data and ETF flows.

Bitcoin and Ethereum have remained flat over the last seven days, with BTC up less than 1% and ETH down 0.4%, CoinGecko data shows.

If sentiment tips into excess, a risk-off move could spark an equities pullback that spills into digital assets, deepening Bitcoin’s recent slide.

In its August report, BofA acknowledged that the rally in the S&P 500 and meme stocks “has been enough to raise some eyebrows.” Still, it said that despite the “disconnect between investor enthusiasm and fundamentals, it is not a risk we’re overly concerned about for now.”

Retail investors, meanwhile, remain cautious. A survey by the American Association of Individual Investors found that only 15.5% of respondents stayed bullish.
