**OpenAI** is reorganizing its **Model Behavior team**, a small but influential research unit shaping how the company’s AI models interact with people, TechCrunch reports.  

In an August memo, chief research officer **Mark Chen** announced that the 14-person team will join the larger **Post Training group**, which is responsible for refining models after their initial pre-training. The team will now report to Post Training lead Max Schwarzer.  

The team’s founding leader, **Joanne Jang**, is moving on to launch a new project within the company. In an interview with TechCrunch, she revealed she is building a new research group called **OAI Labs**, focused on “inventing and prototyping new interfaces for how people collaborate with AI.”  

The Model Behavior team has been instrumental in shaping ChatGPT’s “personality,” reducing **sycophancy** (when AI merely agrees with users), addressing political bias in responses, and helping define OpenAI’s position on questions like AI consciousness.  

Chen emphasized that now is the right time to bring this work closer to core model development, signaling that the “personality” of AI is now viewed as a critical factor in how the technology evolves.

## Our take
This reorganization highlights how strategically important the **personality of AI systems** has become. OpenAI is signaling that the way AI interacts with users is not a side feature but a central part of its products. With OAI Labs, the company may push further into research on collaboration and interaction, shaping how people will use AI in everyday life.
