Futures tied to **World Liberty Financial (WLFI)**, the Trump-linked DeFi token, surged to nearly $1 billion in open interest just hours before the token’s unlock.  

Data from **Hyperliquid DEX** shows traders rushing into both long and short positions as anticipation builds. WLFI had been non-transferable during its presale phase, and the unlock marks the first time tokens will circulate freely on centralized exchanges.  

Heavy shorting last week already dragged the token’s implied valuation down from $44 billion to $24 billion. Analysts warn that the unlock could amplify volatility as large holders gain the ability to sell.  

Beyond Binance, South Korea’s Upbit has confirmed it will also list WLFI, with the token launching across Ethereum, Binance Smart Chain, and Solana.  

**Our comment:** WLFI is evolving into one of the most politically charged assets in crypto — and its unlock could define whether it gains legitimacy or collapses under speculation.
