Rivian is cutting about 150 jobs — its second small workforce reduction in a matter of months — as the company gears up for the critical launch of its more affordable R2 SUV next year.  

The company confirmed to TechCrunch that the new cuts mostly affect its “commercial” team, responsible for sales and service operations. Affected employees will be eligible for rehire and encouraged to apply for other open positions.  

This follows a similar 1% workforce reduction in June that targeted Rivian’s manufacturing division.  

Over the past two years, Rivian has repeatedly adjusted its workforce. In early 2024, it laid off about 10% of staff, followed by another small cut in April. The company entered 2025 with approximately 15,000 employees worldwide.
