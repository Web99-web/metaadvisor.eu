Jan van Eck, CEO of investment management firm VanEck, speculates that Ethereum is destined to become the premier blockchain for banks handling stablecoin transfers—calling it “the Wall Street token.”

In a Fox Business interview, van Eck stated: “It’s very much what I call the Wall Street token. What I mean by that is, because of stablecoins, every bank and financial services company has to have a way of taking in stablecoins.”

He added: “So the winner is, who’s going to be building on these blockchains? It’s going to be Ethereum, or something that uses Ethereum’s methodology, which is called EVM.”

Last month, the US House passed the GENIUS Act, which was signed into law and stands as the first federal legislation focused exclusively on payment stablecoins, while total stablecoin supply has surpassed $280 billion :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}.

A May report from Fireblocks found that 90 % of institutional players are exploring the use of stablecoins in their operations. Van Eck emphasized that banks must adopt technology to facilitate stablecoin usage within the next 12 months—otherwise they risk falling behind :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}.

**Context & significance:**  
This highlights the broader shift underway: Ethereum is being positioned as a core financial infrastructure. VanEck already offers an Ethereum ETF, approved by the SEC, managing over $284 million in assets :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}.
