Tether has announced that it is **scrapping its previously revealed plan** to phase out support for USDT across five blockchain networks.  

The company said the decision followed consultations with ecosystem and institutional partners, who raised concerns about potential negative impacts on liquidity and interoperability.  

According to Tether, its focus remains on strengthening the stability, transparency and resilience of USDT while continuing to support existing networks. The move is seen as an effort to maintain market confidence and avoid fragmentation of the stablecoin ecosystem.
