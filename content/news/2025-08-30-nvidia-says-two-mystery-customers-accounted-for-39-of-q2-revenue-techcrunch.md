Nearly 40% of Nvidia’s second-quarter revenue came from just two customers, according to a filing with the SEC.  

On Wednesday, the chipmaker reported record revenue of **$46.7 billion** for the quarter ended July 27 — up 56% year-over-year, largely fueled by the AI data center boom.  

One customer represented 23% of total Q2 revenue, while another accounted for 16%. Nvidia did not disclose their names, referring to them only as “Customer A” and “Customer B.”  

CFO Nicole Kress noted that **large cloud service providers** represented 50% of Nvidia’s data center revenue, which itself accounted for 88% of the company’s total revenue.
