**Paraphrased recap:**  
- **Why Meta:** Wouldn’t have joined if he thought **Zuckerberg/Sandberg** were “monsters”; points to reform steps (independent **Oversight Board**, election-time measurement, **Instagram parental controls**).  
- **Trump ban:** Substantively justified but troubling as a private platform’s limit on public discourse.  
- **Speech vs harm:** Pandemic moderation sometimes “over-did it”; warns against elastic “lawful but awful” categories. Evidence of social media’s blanket harm to teens is mixed; backs **stricter age gates** via app stores.  
- **Valley culture:** Behind disruption rhetoric lies **conformism**; critiques macho posturing (e.g., **Musk/Rogan** debate).  
- **UK politics:** UK can’t embrace both the **EU** and **Trump’s America**; urges Labour to pin **Brexit** costs on **Nigel Farage**. Calls **Starmer/Reeves** decent but cautious — in office you often have only **one shot**.  
- **AI outlook:** Next five years bring **agentic AI**, more **AI-generated content** and **infrastructure concentration**; overreach risks losing **social licence** and even **nationalisation** talk.  
- **Personal:** Family reasons for leaving Palo Alto; notes **Miriam González Durántez’s** civic/political work.
