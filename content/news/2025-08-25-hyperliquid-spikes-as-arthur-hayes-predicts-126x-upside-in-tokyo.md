**HYPE**, the native token of **Hyperliquid** (a decentralized perpetuals exchange), outperformed after **Arthur Hayes** in Tokyo projected a **126×** upside over the next **three years**. He argued stablecoin growth could lift the DEX’s annualized **fee revenue** to around **$258B** (from roughly **$1.2B** now).

Platform metrics are at records: **open positions** hit **198,397**, **open interest** climbed above **$15B**, and **total wallet equity** peaked at **$31B**. Over the weekend, **DEX volume** reached ~**$1.56B** (per DefiLlama) and monthly **fees** are near July’s ATH (~**$93M**). **TVL** sits around **$685M**.

A recent **Redstone** report says Hyperliquid captured **>75%** of the **decentralized perps** market in under two years (previously led by dYdX), now processing up to **$30B** daily — approaching **Binance** on some pairs. **HYPE** notched an ATH just under **$50** on **July 14** and was ~**7%** below that at publication.
