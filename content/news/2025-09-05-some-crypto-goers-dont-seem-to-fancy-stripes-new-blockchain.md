**Stripe**, the global online payments giant, has launched **Tempo**, its own blockchain project designed for stablecoin transactions and digital payments. Yet, reactions across the crypto community reveal a split opinion.  

### Mixed reactions
Some see Stripe’s entry into blockchain as a major step toward **mainstream Web3 adoption**, while others criticize it as a **centralized solution** that goes against the ethos of decentralization. Critics argue that Tempo primarily benefits large partners and institutions, leaving **smaller projects and startups sidelined**.  

### Bigger picture
Stripe has already partnered with **Visa, Deutsche Bank, Revolut, Shopify, and OpenAI** to test its network. The blockchain aims to power fast payments, global payouts, and microtransactions, but questions remain about how open Tempo will be to the wider crypto ecosystem.  

Currently, the stablecoin market is dominated by **USDT (Tether)** and **USDC (Circle)**. Stripe’s move could shift the balance of power — but whether the crypto community embraces a fintech-driven chain remains uncertain.  

---

## Our take
Stripe’s blockchain launch underscores how fintech and traditional finance are merging with crypto. Still, the split reactions highlight that the community values **decentralization** over **corporate control**. If Tempo succeeds, it could redefine global payments. If not, it will serve as proof that even the biggest fintech players can’t bypass the core principles of Web3.
