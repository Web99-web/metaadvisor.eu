Binance has become the first centralized crypto exchange to list **World Liberty Financial (WLFI)**, a DeFi project linked to the Trump family. Trading began Monday afternoon UTC, with deposits already open and withdrawals starting Tuesday.  

Previously, WLFI tokens were non-transferable, a compliance measure preventing presale tokens from being freely traded. Binance’s listing marks the first time WLFI is fully tradable on a centralized exchange. The exchange has applied its “seed tag” label, reserved for innovative but risky assets.  

Following Binance’s move, South Korea’s **Upbit** also announced plans to list WLFI. The token will be accessible across three blockchains: Ethereum, Binance Smart Chain, and Solana.  

Last week, WLFI futures launched on Hyperliquid DEX, where heavy shorting cut its implied valuation from $44 billion to $24 billion within hours.  

On Binance, users must pass quizzes acknowledging volatility and risk disclosures before accessing WLFI markets.  

**Our comment:** WLFI may become one of the most politically charged tokens in crypto — with market performance tied as much to Trump’s brand as to blockchain fundamentals.
