Crypto investment platform **Unicoin** has responded to the SEC’s **$100 million fraud lawsuit**, urging a New York federal judge to dismiss the case. The company argues the regulator:
- “**Plucks snippets of communications out of context**,”  
- treats normal financial projections as fraud,  
- and ignores **Unicoin’s own risk disclosures** :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}.

Unicoin alleges that the SEC distorted its own disclosures to serve as evidence of deception :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}. In its motion to dismiss, the company also calls out the SEC’s use of “shotgun pleading” and a failure to prove *scienter*—a necessary element in securities fraud :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}.

According to SEC allegations, Unicoin misled investors by overstating the asset backing and selling unregistered certificates :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}. Unicoin refutes these claims, emphasizing transparency in disclosures and seeking a dismissal **with prejudice** :contentReference[oaicite:15]{index=15}.
