Nasdaq-listed healthcare firm Kindly MD has filed an automatic shelf registration with the SEC to issue up to $5 billion in stock, just days after disclosing a $679 million Bitcoin purchase through its subsidiary, Nakamoto Holdings.

The company said Bitcoin will now serve as its main treasury reserve asset, emphasizing a long-term strategy. The new WKSI status gives Kindly MD more flexibility in raising capital but also brings pressure in times of volatility and heavy issuance.

Distribution of the offering is expected to involve Cantor Fitzgerald, TD Securities, B. Riley Securities in the U.S. and Canaccord Genuity in Canada, among others.

Kindly MD argued that Bitcoin represents the “ultimate reserve asset” for corporates, but the expansion of Digital Asset Treasuries (DATs) has stirred concerns that liquidity could drain from altcoins into Bitcoin.

Analysts note that institutional exposure to crypto is no longer experimental but embedded in balance sheets. Kelvin Koh of Spartan Group said this wave of adoption gained momentum after U.S. Bitcoin ETFs were approved in early 2024, alongside pro-crypto policy moves from the Trump administration.
