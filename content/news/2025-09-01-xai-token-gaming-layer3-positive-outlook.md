**What is XAI?**  
XAI is the native token of the **Xai blockchain**, a Layer-3 solution built on **Arbitrum Orbit**. It functions as a **gas token** for transactions and validator rewards in an ecosystem designed specifically for gaming and digital assets.  

The gaming industry requires fast and affordable transactions, and Xai delivers – enabling ownership and trading of in-game items without complex wallet setups.  

**Market and Price**  
According to CoinMarketCap and CoinGecko, XAI trades around **$0.047**, with a market cap between **$60M–$95M**. Daily trading volume exceeds **$12M**, showing healthy liquidity.
