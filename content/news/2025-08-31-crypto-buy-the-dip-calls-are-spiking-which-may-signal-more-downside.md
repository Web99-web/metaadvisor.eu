Analytics firm **Santiment** reported that “buy the dip” chatter on social media has surged to multi-month highs as the crypto market faces mounting sell pressure.  

According to its August 30 analysis, these spikes in bullish calls often historically signal that the downturn is not yet over, with premature optimism frequently leading to continued declines.  

Santiment highlighted that historical data shows sharp increases in such calls usually precede further losses, as too many retail traders expect a rebound too early.  

Bitcoin, Ether and major altcoins continued to hover near key support levels, while analysts warned that September — historically a weak month for markets — could provide another stress test for investors.  

Santiment’s conclusion: while “buy the dip” sounds optimistic, its current intensity may in fact mean the opposite — **that more downside is likely before a real recovery can take hold**.
