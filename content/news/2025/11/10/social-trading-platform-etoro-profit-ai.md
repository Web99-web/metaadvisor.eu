**Social trading and investment platform eToro** (Nasdaq: ETOR) reported a **48% profit increase** and announced a **$150 million stock buyback program**, pushing its share price up nearly 7% on the Nasdaq.

In Q3 2025, client assets reached **$20.8 billion**, up **76% year-over-year**.  
Net income under GAAP rose to **$57 million**, with funded accounts expanding to **3.73 million users**.

Additionally, **crypto trades increased 84%**, and the **average trade amount** rose 52% to $320.  
The company plans to launch a **new crypto wallet** in upcoming quarters, offering access to tokenized markets, prediction platforms and DeFi services.

eToro is best known for its **“social trading”** model, which allows users to **copy the strategies of successful investors**.  
Beginners can learn by following experts, while experienced traders earn additional revenue when others mirror their trades.  
This approach merges **social networking and investing**, making eToro one of the world’s most recognizable fintech brands.

The company also launched **“Tori,” an AI-powered investment assistant**, providing personalized insights and portfolio analytics to enhance decision-making.

> “We focus on innovation that creates real value for our users,” said CEO **Yoni Assia**.  
> “AI helps us better understand markets and guide investors toward smarter choices.”

In October, eToro users executed **5 million crypto transactions**, up 84% year-on-year — a clear signal of **renewed market confidence** after a volatile first half of the year.

eToro now operates in over **100 countries**, bridging traditional finance with **AI-driven investment tools** and **tokenized assets**.
