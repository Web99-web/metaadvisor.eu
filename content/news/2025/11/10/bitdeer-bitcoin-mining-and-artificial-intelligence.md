Bitdeer Technologies, based in Singapore, reported a **$266 million net loss** for Q3 2025 — even as its revenue and Bitcoin production surged.  
Company shares dropped nearly **20 %** after the earnings release.

Revenue climbed to **$169.7 million**, up **174 %** year-over-year, fueled by the expansion of self-mining operations.  
At the same time, Bitdeer booked its first **AI and high-performance cloud services revenue**, totaling **$1.8 million**.

👉 The firm is now shifting part of its computing power toward **artificial intelligence (AI)**.  
Chief Business Officer Matt Kong said Bitdeer plans to dedicate **200 MW of power capacity** to AI cloud services — potentially reaching an **annualized revenue run rate of $2 billion** by the end of 2026.

Bitdeer currently manages **241,000 mining rigs** and holds **2,029 BTC**, a sharp rise from just 258 BTC a year earlier.

### 💬 Our view (Metaadvisor)
The fusion of **Bitcoin mining and AI** signals a new direction in digital infrastructure.  
Companies like Bitdeer are looking to repurpose their mining farms into **AI compute hubs**, where energy and GPU power can be monetized far beyond crypto.  
This shift could transform Bitdeer into a major “compute-as-a-service” provider in the next wave of decentralized cloud computing.
