**At a glance:** On Thursday evening (around 23:00 CET), the crypto market experienced one of the most violent **flash crash + bounce** events ever. Roughly **$16B** in positions were liquidated, with extreme wicks across major pairs — erased almost instantly.

### Examples
- **BTC**: 122.5k → **101.7k** → 111–112k  
- **ETH**: 4,388 → **3,405** → ~3,820  
- **SOL**: 224 → **142** → ~184  
- **ZEC**: 289 → **148** → ~258 (notably stable)  

### Our take
- Highlights the fragility of markets to **leverage cascades and whale orders**.  
- Raises the question whether **whales and exchanges can trigger/manipulate such moves**.  
- ZEC stood out as one of the few coins holding relative strength.
