# Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) would sell Bitcoin only if every other option fails

Strategy CEO **Phong Le** says the company would sell Bitcoin *only in one scenario*:  
➡️ **if the stock price drops below mNAV and capital markets shut down completely.**

He stressed that such a move would be a *“mathematical decision”* — not a pivot away from Bitcoin.

In other words:  
**they do not want to sell Bitcoin**, but they *would*, if that is the only rational way to protect “Bitcoin yield per share.”

---

## 🧩 What exactly is mNAV?

mNAV = *multiple to net asset value*  
→ the ratio between the company’s share price and the value of its underlying assets (primarily Bitcoin).

- If mNAV is **above 1** → the stock trades *above* asset value → raising capital is easy and non-dilutive.  
- If mNAV is **below 1** → the stock trades *below* asset value → raising capital becomes expensive and punitive for shareholders.

Phong Le explains:

> “If mNAV is under one and financing options dry up, selling Bitcoin becomes mathematically justified to protect yield per share.”

---

## 📉 When would a BTC sale be justified?

According to Le, three conditions must occur:

### ✔️ 1. The stock trades below NAV  
Issuing new shares would drastically dilute existing shareholders.

### ✔️ 2. Capital markets are no longer accessible  
No option for low-coupon convertibles or ATM equity issuance.

### ✔️ 3. The company must cover fixed obligations  
Strategy has **$750M–$800M** in annual payments tied to preferred instruments.

If all three conditions overlap → selling a portion of BTC becomes a rational choice.

---

## 🧱 The business model is designed to avoid selling Bitcoin

Strategy’s entire capital structure aims to *never* sell BTC unless absolutely necessary:

### **1. Long-dated debt maturities**  
The first major maturity arrives only in late 2025.  
→ Plenty of breathing room.

### **2. At-the-market equity programs (ATMs)**  
Used only when mNAV is strong.

### **3. Zero- and low-coupon convertible notes**  
Allow capital raising without near-term dilution pressure.

Le summarizes their position:

> “We have more flexibility than ever to continue accumulating Bitcoin.”

---

## 🏛️ Why are investors nervous?

Markets are watching several factors closely:

- rising fixed annual obligations  
- interest rate environment  
- BTC volatility  
- relationship between share price and NAV  

If mNAV stays below 1 for long, the theoretical risk of selling BTC increases — even if it is not the plan.

As Le said:

> “I don’t want to be the company that sells Bitcoin.  
> But financial discipline must override emotion.”

---

## 📈 What does this mean for everyday investors?

This statement sends two important signals:

### **1. Strategy remains extremely long-term bullish on Bitcoin**  
Their model is built around continuous accumulation.

### **2. Short-term volatility may increase**  
Even the *possibility* of a BTC sale can move markets.

### **3. Institutional demand is still a major force**  
Strategy holds over **158,000 BTC** — one of the largest corporate holdings in the world.
