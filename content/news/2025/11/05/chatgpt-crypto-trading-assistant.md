### How ChatGPT can help traders

According to Cointelegraph, users can transform ChatGPT into a **personal trading assistant** with just a few prompts and simple tools.  
Here are the main ways to use it:

1. 🧠 **Market analysis** — ChatGPT can review news, monitor trends, and explain technical indicators like RSI, MACD, and EMA.  
2. 💬 **Creating trading plans** — generate personalized strategies (e.g., swing trading or scalping) with recommendations for stop-loss and take-profit levels.  
3. ⚙️ **API integration** — advanced users can connect ChatGPT to exchanges like Binance or Bybit (via API keys) to receive **automated signals or simulated trades.**  
👉 Detailed guide: [**How to connect ChatGPT with Binance or Bybit using API keys**](https://metaadvisor.eu/news/chatgpt-binance-bybit-api-integration/)  
4. 📊 **Risk and portfolio evaluation** — ChatGPT can assess risk ratios, volatility, and diversification based on user goals.  
5. 🔍 **Sentiment detection** — by analyzing news and social media, the model can gauge how the market reacts to specific events.

---

### Paid vs. free version of ChatGPT

Although the article doesn’t state that the paid version is mandatory, users should know that:  
- Basic features (prompts, analysis, strategy generation) work in the **free version**,  
- while advanced functions such as **API connectivity, real-time data, and agent mode** usually require **ChatGPT Plus (GPT-4)** or **Team** versions.

*Note: Always check which features are available in your version before trying to automate trading.*

---

### Advantages and limitations

AI tools like ChatGPT can boost productivity and reduce emotional bias in trading, but it’s important to remember that:

- AI **cannot predict the market** with full accuracy,  
- responsibility still lies with the user,  
- and incorrect settings can lead to **financial losses**.

Cointelegraph therefore recommends using ChatGPT as a **supporting tool**, not as a substitute for personal market understanding.

---

### Artificial intelligence in trading — a new standard

More and more traders are using ChatGPT as an addition to their existing analyses.  
Advanced versions can combine **AI models with real-time data** and create **personalized bots** that monitor markets 24/7.

Thanks to this combination, even small investors can access professional-grade tools that were once reserved for hedge funds and institutions.
