In crypto’s constant evolution, mining is once again being redefined.  
Two projects — **ORE** and **Tari** — are taking very different paths but share one vision: **to make mining more accessible, efficient, and meaningful.**

---

### ORE — Mining on Solana

**ORE** is an experimental project that blends **Proof of Work (PoW)** logic into a **Proof of Stake (PoS)** network.  
Running entirely on **Solana**, ORE lets users “mine” new tokens by sending cryptographic attempts (hashes) through smart contracts.

When a user’s hash meets the target, they earn **1 ORE token**.  
The aim is to **democratize mining** — giving everyone a chance to participate without expensive hardware.

Some call ORE a “gamified version of mining,” while others see it as an **innovative token distribution model** inside a PoS system.  
Regardless of perspective, ORE represents **an experiment worth following** — bringing PoW principles to an otherwise PoS ecosystem.

---

### Tari — Privacy and utility meet Proof of Work

**Tari**, on the other hand, is building its own **Layer-1 blockchain** with a focus on **privacy and digital assets**.  
It uses **Proof of Work** and supports *merge mining* with **Monero (XMR)**, allowing miners to secure both networks simultaneously.

Tari’s structure is two-layered:  
- **Layer 1** provides network security via PoW  
- **Layer 2** handles NFTs, tickets, and other tokenized assets  

By combining utility and privacy, Tari creates a **real-world use case** for modern PoW infrastructure.

---

### ORE vs Tari — two paths, one goal

| Feature | ORE | Tari |
|----------|------|------|
| **Blockchain** | Solana (PoS-based program) | Native PoW Layer 1 + Layer 2 |
| **Mining model** | Hash attempts via Solana transactions | Traditional PoW + Merge Mining with Monero |
| **Goal** | Token distribution & accessibility | Digital assets & privacy |
| **Status** | Experimental, growing community | Infrastructure project under development |
| **Risk** | Transaction costs, unclear sustainability | Complexity & competition |

---

### What it means for crypto’s future

Both ORE and Tari prove that **mining isn’t dead — it’s evolving**.  
ORE experiments with *PoW within PoS*, while Tari builds a *modern PoW platform* for real applications.  
Together, they open the door to **hybrid models** that merge distribution, privacy, and security in new ways.
