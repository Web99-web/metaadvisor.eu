As blockchain technology evolves, **Monad** positions itself as one of the **most advanced Layer-1 projects**, built for **speed, efficiency, and full EVM compatibility**.

The project claims to handle **10,000 transactions per second (TPS)** with **sub-second finality** and low hardware requirements — aiming to make decentralization more inclusive and accessible.

---

### What is Monad?

**Monad** is an **Ethereum-like blockchain** designed to overcome Ethereum’s main limitations — scalability and cost.  
Instead of relying solely on raw performance, Monad combines **parallel execution and AI-driven optimization** to achieve speed without compromising security.

The project is led by the **Monad Foundation**, a non-profit entity supporting development, governance, and ecosystem growth.

---

### Key Features

- ⚙️ **High throughput** — up to 10,000 TPS  
- ⚡ **Fast finality** — block confirmations in under one second  
- 🤖 **AI optimization** — predictive network management and smart scheduling  
- 🧩 **Full EVM compatibility** — Ethereum apps can run without code changes  
- 💻 **Low hardware requirements** — accessible to regular participants  
- 🌐 **Decentralized governance** — DAO structure planned for the future  

---

### Why Monad matters

While many Layer-1 projects focus solely on speed, **Monad aims for balance — performance, accessibility, and sustainability.**  
EVM compatibility lowers the barrier for developers, and the AI component improves efficiency in real time.

With these traits, Monad stands as a **real alternative to Ethereum and Solana**, especially for projects needing speed without centralization.

---

### Roadmap and outlook

The **mainnet launch** is expected in the coming months, alongside new developer tools and grants.  
According to co-founder **Keone Hon**, the goal is to **bridge Web3 performance with Web2 simplicity** — making Monad a potential foundation for the next generation of blockchain apps.
