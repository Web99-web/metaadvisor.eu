The **Monad Foundation** officially confirmed via X that the **airdrop of its MON token will take place on November 24**, giving users a firm date for one of the **most anticipated airdrop events of the year.**

This announcement marks the start of preparations for the distribution of MON tokens to early and active contributors, ahead of the project’s upcoming **mainnet launch**.

---

### Airdrop claim portal

Back on **October 14**, the Foundation launched its official **airdrop claim portal**, inviting users to connect their wallets, verify eligibility, and link their social accounts ahead of distribution.

The portal allowed eligible users from **five categories**, including:
- core Monad community members,  
- on-chain DeFi users,  
- and the wider group of active crypto participants.

---

### Allocation and criteria

According to the Foundation’s blog post, the allocation model aims to **reward around 5,500 key community members** and approximately **225,000 broader crypto users**, following a **“stacked allocation” system** for those qualifying under multiple categories.

The total share of tokens to be distributed has not been disclosed, but Monad co-founder **Keone Hon** previously stated that the project’s **total supply will be 100 billion MON tokens.**

---

### Why it matters

The Monad airdrop comes as the crypto market slowly recovers and new projects are working to re-engage users through **transparent and fair token distributions.**  
The project positions itself as a **technological competitor to Ethereum**, emphasizing performance, scalability, and EVM compatibility.
