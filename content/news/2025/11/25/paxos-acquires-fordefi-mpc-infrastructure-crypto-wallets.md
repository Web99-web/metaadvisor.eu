Paxos, one of the world’s most strictly regulated blockchain infrastructure companies, has acquired Fordefi — a New York–based startup specializing in institutional MPC (multi-party computation) crypto wallets and onchain governance controls. The deal, reportedly valued at more than $100 million, marks a major step toward secure, regulated and institution-focused blockchain infrastructure.

## What is Paxos?

Paxos operates as a regulated backbone for enterprises entering the digital asset space. The company provides compliant blockchain infrastructure to some of the largest financial and fintech institutions in the world, bridging the gap between traditional finance and blockchain technology.

Paxos works with:

- PayPal  
- Mastercard  
- Interactive Brokers  
- Mercado Pago  
- PicPay  

It is also the issuer of multiple major digital assets:

- **PYUSD** — PayPal USD  
- **USDP** — Pax Dollar  
- **PAXG** — tokenized gold  
- **USDG** — Global Dollar  

With regulatory approval in the United States, Europe and Singapore, Paxos has become one of the most trusted institutions for secure onchain operations.

## What is Fordefi?

Fordefi, founded in 2021 in New York, quickly became one of the leading providers of institutional security tools in the digital asset industry. The company developed:

- institutional-grade MPC wallets  
- governance controls for authorizing and signing onchain transactions  
- DeFi-integrated infrastructure with strict permissioning  
- tools for funds, exchanges and custodians managing high-value assets  

MPC (multi-party computation) ensures that private keys are never held in a single location and cannot be compromised by one actor — a critical requirement for institutional security.

## Why did Paxos buy Fordefi?

The acquisition brings several major advantages:

### 1️⃣ MPC security integrated into a regulated environment  
This positions Paxos as one of the safest stablecoin and tokenization infrastructures available.

### 2️⃣ A unified platform for institutions  
Enterprises can now:

- issue stablecoins  
- tokenize assets  
- run onchain transactions  
- access DeFi tools in a secure and compliant framework  

### 3️⃣ A significant boost to institutional tokenization  
Tokenized assets are expected to become one of the fastest-growing segments in finance, and Paxos is positioning itself as a key infrastructure provider.

### 4️⃣ A competitive edge over Circle  
While Circle leads the stablecoin market with USDC, Paxos is strengthening its position in the highly regulated institutional space.
