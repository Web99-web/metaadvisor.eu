## 🔬 Science + Blockchain = The rise of DeSci

The traditional scientific funding model suffers from:
- slow approval processes  
- centralized funding sources  
- limited accessibility  
- dependence on grants, institutions and bureaucracy  

Blockchain introduces a faster, global and more transparent alternative.

---

## 🧬 Portage Biotech evolves into a Toncoin treasury company

In September, Portage Biotech transformed into a **Toncoin (TON)** treasury firm.

Its revenues now come from:
- staking TON to secure the network  
- investing in the Telegram ecosystem, including mini-apps, games and services  

A portion of those revenues will be used to **fund cancer research**, AlphaTON CEO Brittany Kaiser told Cointelegraph.

The company is also exploring **real-world asset tokenization (RWA)** as a new funding mechanism for scientific development.

---

## 💬 Scaramucci: “This is not a typical crypto treasury”

Anthony Scaramucci, strategic advisor to AlphaTON, emphasized:

> “Most crypto treasury companies absorb a shell and shut down the original operating business.  
> Here it’s the opposite — the shell contains valuable real-world assets and a functioning operation.”

In other words:  
This is one of the first models where **biotech and crypto treasury operations merge with real industrial value**.

## 📊 Prediction markets for scientific funding

Decentralized science startup **Ideosphere** is investigating how **prediction markets** can finance early-stage research.

This model allows:
- scientific hypotheses to gain a “market price”  
- community and investors to support research through capital  
- funding to flow to the most promising scientific directions  

DeSci creates a **global, decentralized, market-driven** research ecosystem — free from centralized gatekeepers.
