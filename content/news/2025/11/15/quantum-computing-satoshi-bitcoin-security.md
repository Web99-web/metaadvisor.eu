**Satoshi Nakamoto** — whose identity remains one of the biggest mysteries in tech  
(*read our full breakdown here:*  
**[Who is Satoshi Nakamoto?](https://metaadvisor.eu/news/who-is-satoshi-nakamoto/)**) — left behind an estimated **1.1 million BTC**.

These coins have not moved in more than 15 years, forming one of the largest and most iconic digital treasures in the world.

But as quantum computing advances from theory to functioning prototypes, cryptographers warn that **early Bitcoin addresses may one day become vulnerable**.

## Why early Bitcoin addresses could be at risk

Bitcoin’s oldest addresses use formats that are less secure than today’s standards.  
A sufficiently powerful quantum computer could theoretically derive private keys from certain early public keys.

This is not about hackers or server breaches —  
it’s about a completely new kind of computational power.

Across the world:

- tech giants are investing billions in quantum hardware  
- cryptography labs are building quantum-resistant algorithms  
- Bitcoin developers are preparing long-term upgrades  

## Could quantum computers “steal” Satoshi’s 1.1M BTC?

Today: no.  
Current quantum machines are far too weak.

Long-term: a realistic concern.

Satoshi’s wallets are a potential target because:

- they hold more BTC than any other single set of addresses  
- they were created using early cryptographic patterns  
- they carry enormous symbolic weight  

## Is the threat real or exaggerated?

Experts generally agree:

- short term — no immediate danger  
- long term — quantum-secure cryptography will be essential  
- Bitcoin must evolve to stay secure in the quantum era  

The question is not *if*, but *when* a major cryptographic upgrade will become necessary.

### Conclusion

Satoshi’s untouched 1.1M BTC represent:

- a historic digital treasure  
- a central myth in Bitcoin culture  
- and a potential vulnerability in a future with quantum computing  

Quantum computers aren't a threat today — but the race to prepare for tomorrow has already begun.
