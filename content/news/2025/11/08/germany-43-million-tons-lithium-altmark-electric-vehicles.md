Germany has confirmed one of **Europe’s largest geological discoveries** — **43 million tons of lithium carbonate equivalent (LCE)** found in the **Altmark region** of **Saxony-Anhalt**.  
The discovery was announced by *Neptune Energy*, with an estimated value exceeding **$1 trillion USD**.

Often called the **“white gold” of the energy transition**, lithium is critical not only for **electric vehicle (EV) batteries**, but also for **energy storage systems**, **electronics**, **telecommunications**, **aerospace**, and **medical technology**.  
Its strategic importance is growing rapidly, especially with the rise of **AI infrastructure and data centers** that demand long-term, efficient energy storage.

Until now, Europe has heavily depended on lithium imports from **China, Chile, and Argentina**, but this discovery could mark the start of **European self-sufficiency in critical minerals**.

The extraction will use a **sustainable Direct Lithium Extraction (DLE)** method from geothermal brines — avoiding traditional open-pit mining and minimizing the **environmental footprint**.  
According to early estimates, **commercial production could begin around 2030**, after the exploration and permitting phases are completed.

This breakthrough comes as Germany and the EU accelerate investments in **green energy and electric mobility**.  
Beyond automotive applications, the Altmark lithium find could also drive **new growth in battery manufacturing, energy storage, and industrial modernization** across Europe.
