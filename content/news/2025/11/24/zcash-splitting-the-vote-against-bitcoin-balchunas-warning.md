Bloomberg Senior ETF Analyst **Eric Balchunas** has warned that Zcash may “split the vote” at a critical moment for Bitcoin.  
His comparison is straightforward: ZEC has “third-party candidate vibes,” much like political outsiders who drain support from main contenders.

With **ZEC trading around 550 USD**, interest in the privacy coin is rising again — not just because of price action, but because of the technology behind it.

## 🔹 Why Zcash — and not hundreds of other coins?
The debate reignited on X.  
Arman Meguerian wrote: *“I don’t know a single Bitcoin maxi who thinks about Zcash at all.”*  
Samson Mow added that Bitcoin maximalists *“only look at ZEC to roll their eyes.”*

So why does Zcash trigger such reactions while other privacy coins barely get mentioned?

The answer:

### **Zcash is one of the first cryptocurrencies to implement Zero-Knowledge (ZK) cryptography.**

This makes it a **technological outlier**, not just another altcoin — a “third candidate” with its own narrative.

## 🔹 Zero-Knowledge (ZK) — why Zcash is seen as an alternative to Bitcoin
ZK technology allows proving information *without revealing the underlying data*.  
It is ideal for:

- privacy  
- identity  
- security  
- blockchain scaling  

Many analysts believe **ZK solutions could become the dominant crypto trend by 2030**.  
Zcash was among the earliest real-world implementations of this technology.

## 🔹 Claims of “manufactured hype”
Investor Mark Moss posted screenshots showing marketing agencies offering paid ZEC campaigns.  
Analyst Rajat Soni warned that recent excitement looks like an attempt to “find exit liquidity,” including fake headlines predicting ZEC at 100,000 USD.

On the other hand, the **Winklevoss twins**, founders of Gemini, openly support Zcash and ZK technology as protection against rising AI-driven privacy risks.

## 🔹 Conclusion
- Zcash attracts attention because it is an early pioneer of ZK technology.  
- That’s why analysts say it acts like a “third candidate” that could split political and cultural support for Bitcoin.  
- ZK is becoming a defining trend for the coming decade.  
- Support from the Winklevoss brothers amplifies the debate even further.
