## Was 10.10 a coincidence — or a coordinated attack?

The crypto community has been debating the dramatic Bitcoin crash on 10.10 — a drop that erased billions in minutes.  
Many well-known analysts now argue that this was **not a normal market move**, but a **coordinated institutional strike** on Microstrategy and the broader Bitcoin market.

### What did the top analysts say?

Over the past days, three major voices — all deeply familiar with Wall Street mechanics — made waves:

- **Ran Neuner (Crypto Banter)**:  
  *“Microstrategy was a target. This looks like an attack, not regular volatility.”*

- **Arthur Hayes (BitMEX)**:  
  *“Institutions know exactly where the liquidation levels are. This was the 2025 version of the Big Short.”*

- **Simon Dixon (BankToTheFuture)** went even further:  
  *“Banks and MSTR are on the same board — just with different incentives.”*

And the most viral quote on X:  

> **“They are on the same team.” — Simon Dixon**

In other words:  
**Banks, funds, and institutional capital may not hate Bitcoin — they want to own it and control it.**

## Microstrategy under the strongest pressure in years

The 10.10 crash hit almost perfectly:

- the known MSTR risk zones  
- margin pressure levels  
- the best possible areas for large funds to open short positions  

While retail traders were liquidated,  
**institutions were buying aggressively.**

On-chain data confirms that several dormant whale addresses accumulated BTC **between 10.10 and 12.10**.

## A JP Morgan pattern?

Some analysts go further, suggesting the financial sector sent a very clear message:

### “If we want Bitcoin — we’ll take it.”

Signals include:

- sharp increase in short positions before the drop  
- coordinated reports from major banking research desks  
- pressure on Microstrategy’s preferred stock  
- large BTC outflows from exchanges immediately after the crash  

All of this resembles a **takeover operation**, not a natural correction.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor)

The events of 10.10 represent a clash of two monetary worlds:

- **traditional finance** (banks, funds, credit institutions, regulators)  
- **the decentralized monetary system** led by Bitcoin  

Institutions have realized they cannot ignore Bitcoin anymore.  
If they cannot stop it — **they will try to buy it, control it, or reshape it to fit their system.**

The attack on Microstrategy may only be the first round.

One thing is becoming increasingly clear:  
**the battle for Bitcoin has begun — and 2025 will be a turning point.**
