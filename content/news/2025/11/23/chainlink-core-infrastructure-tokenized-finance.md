## Chainlink enters a new phase — becoming the backbone of tokenized finance

According to a new research report from Grayscale — the world’s largest digital asset manager — **Chainlink is no longer just an oracle network. It is rapidly becoming a core piece of infrastructure for the global market of tokenized assets**.

This conclusion comes at a moment when tokenization is moving from hype to real adoption: across banks, hedge funds, asset managers, and regulated pilot programs around the world.

Grayscale writes that **LINK is “the connective tissue between crypto and traditional finance (TradFi)”**, placing Chainlink in the same category as the data and settlement standards used in the traditional financial system.

---

## Why is Chainlink so critical?

### 🔹 1. Smart contracts need reliable data  
Chainlink has long been the dominant provider of price and data feeds for DeFi protocols.  
Without these feeds, large parts of the ecosystem simply wouldn’t function.

### 🔹 2. CCIP — the communication layer between blockchains  
The report highlights the **Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP)**, which enables secure transfer of tokens and messages across different blockchain networks.

Tests conducted with **J.P. Morgan’s Kinexys platform** and **Ondo Finance** demonstrated how important CCIP is for institutional scenarios such as:

- on-chain settlement  
- transferring tokenized securities  
- real-world asset (RWA) operations  
- multi-chain workflows for banks  
- cross-market liquidity routing  

### 🔹 3. Deepening integrations with major financial institutions  
Grayscale points to expanding collaborations with:

- **S&P Global**  
- **FTSE Russell**

These are not experimental pilots — they are early signs of standardization.

---

## Tokenization is still early — but accelerating fast

The report estimates the current market for tokenized assets at **$35 billion**, which is:

- just **0.01%** of global capital markets,  
- but growing steadily.

In other words:  
**We are in phase one — with enormous upside ahead.**

As regulation matures and banks shift more processes on-chain, Chainlink naturally becomes the infrastructure layer connecting everything.

---

## What Grayscale says

> “Chainlink is essential infrastructure for blockchain-based finance. It connects crypto with traditional markets.”

This is the strongest institutional endorsement yet that LINK is moving from “altcoin category” to **infrastructure category** — something akin to “AWS for on-chain data and interoperability”.
