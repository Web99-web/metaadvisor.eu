## XRP rebounds from capitulation zone

XRP climbed back above **$2.00** today after yesterday’s sharp drop triggered by aggressive whale selling, which pushed the token into the **$1.90–$1.95** zone.  
Bitcoin also stabilized around **$86,600**, easing the extreme volatility seen over the past two days when BTC briefly traded as low as the $79,000–$83,000 range.

---

## Whale selling slows — buyers step back in

Across a 48-hour window, large holders offloaded nearly **200 million XRP**, worth roughly **$400 million**.  
This heavy distribution caused:

- a breakdown of the **$1.96** resistance area  
- a capitulation low at **$1.895**  
- trading volume soaring **67% above average**

Late in the session, buyers aggressively defended the key **$1.90–$1.95** support range.  
This demand helped XRP regain **$2.05** this morning, signaling that the worst of the sell-off pressure may be over.

---

## Early technical signs of recovery

RSI and short-term stochastic indicators fell deep into oversold territory yesterday, creating ideal conditions for a technical bounce.  
A **bullish divergence** is beginning to form — often an early sign that a capitulation event has exhausted sellers.

The rising morning volume suggests:

- weakening sell-side pressure  
- early accumulation behavior  
- stabilization above the psychological $2 level  

---

## What’s next?

XRP is now entering a new consolidation phase:

- **Support:** $1.95  
- **Key resistance:** $2.15  
- **Bullish confirmation:** breakout above $2.22 with strong volume

If XRP holds above $2.00, the market could transition back into a bullish structure — provided Bitcoin remains stable above $86,000.
