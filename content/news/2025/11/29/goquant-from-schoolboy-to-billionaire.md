Denis Dariotis is one of the most striking examples of what happens when **curiosity + data + AI + discipline** collide.  
Born in Montreal, he was nine years old when he told his teacher he needed “10 minutes out of class” to check his portfolio. When she asked to see his screen, he closed the laptop and said: *“That’s private.”*

Twenty-two years later, he is the founder and CEO of **GoQuant**, a quantitative trading firm valued at **over $1 billion**.

His story is not just inspirational — it demonstrates how ordinary people can use AI-powered tools without needing to write a single line of code.

---

# 🧠 How Denis became a quant-driven billionaire before turning 23

### 1) He started extremely young  
He was fascinated by tickers and charts on CNBC while other kids watched cartoons.

### 2) Programming became his superpower  
Age 11 — first lines of code  
Age 12 — building websites  
Age 13 — moving to Python & C++ and discovering automation

### 3) He was doing quant work before he even knew the term  
He taught himself backtesting, data scanning, pattern detection and statistics.

### 4) He automated everything that could be automated  
“If I do it manually too many times, the computer should do it better.”

This became the foundation of GoQuant.

### 5) Dark-pool tech & institutional infrastructure  
GoQuant now builds high-level trading systems for professional and institutional markets.

# 🌍 Can *ordinary people* benefit from AI and quant trading?

Yes — but not by trying to “become Denis”.

Instead, by using tools that bring automation to everyone.

### 1) No-code AI platforms

### 2) Copy-trading / agent trading  
For people who prefer following proven strategies instead of designing their own.

### 3) Start with small capital  
Denis began with literal piggy-bank savings.  
Discipline beats capital.
