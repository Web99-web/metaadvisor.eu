AI trading is no longer limited to hedge funds or programmers — today anyone can use it by connecting an account, selecting a strategy, and letting the algorithms work.  
This **30-day plan** is designed for beginners who want to start safely, with minimal stress and zero coding.

---

# 🗓️ Week 1 — Learn the Basics With Zero Risk (0 €)
Goal: understand the core concepts.

- learn five fundamentals: trend, volatility, risk, signals, stop-loss  
- open demo accounts (TradingView Paper Trading, Hyperliquid Testnet)  
- watch how bots behave without entering trades  
- spend 10 minutes a day observing price action  

This week builds the foundation for “quant thinking.”

---

# 🗓️ Week 2 — First AI Models (still no risk)
Goal: activate your first strategies on DEMO.

Recommended combination:
- **Trend-Follower AI bot**  
- **Mean-Reversion AI bot**

Watch:
- win rate  
- average win vs loss  
- drawdown  
- trade frequency  

Still no real capital used.

---

# 🗓️ Week 3 — Small Live Portfolio (20–50 €)
Goal: experience real market conditions.

Suggested allocation:
- 20 € into a trend strategy  
- 20 € into a mean-reversion bot  
- 10 € into small manual trades for market feel  

Here the goal is *experience*, not profit.

---

# 🗓️ Week 4 — Automation and a Stable Plan
Goal: build a structure you can scale later.

Optimal 3-strategy portfolio:
1. Trend bot  
2. Mean-reversion bot  
3. Volatility breakout bot  

Risk rules:
- max 1–3% risk per position  
- max 30% in any single strategy  
- weekly review (10 minutes)

# 🔍 Platform Guide: Hyperliquid, Kryll and MEXC

## ✅ **1. Hyperliquid AI Agents (DeFi L2, no KYC)**
Hyperliquid is a **decentralized exchange (DEX)** on its own Layer-2 blockchain.  
Its standout feature: **AI Agents** that trade automatically on your wallet.

**How to start using Hyperliquid AI Agents:**  
1. Install MetaMask  
2. Open https://app.hyperliquid.xyz  
3. Bridge USDT/USDC to Hyperliquid  
4. Open the menu → **AI Agents**  
5. Select a bot → click *Activate*

Pros:
- no KYC  
- ultra-fast L2  
- AI bots work 24/7  

Cons:
- requires basic wallet handling  

---

## ✅ **2. Kryll.io — Marketplace for AI Trading Strategies**
Kryll is a no-code automation platform used by beginners and pros.

On Kryll you can:
- choose pre-built AI or quant strategies  
- connect your MEXC/KuCoin/Binance account via API  
- start a bot with the amount you choose  

**How to start on Kryll:**  
1. Visit https://kryll.io  
2. Create an account  
3. Connect your MEXC API keys  
4. Choose a strategy from the Marketplace  
5. Click *Start*

Pros:
- extremely beginner-friendly  
- large strategy marketplace  

Cons:
- strategies must be tested first
