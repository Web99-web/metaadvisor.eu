**Where to find the bots:**  
➡️ app.hyperliquid.xyz → **AI Agents**

Available models:
- Trend AI  
- Mean Reversion  
- Volatility AI  
- Market-Making AI  
- historical charts, win-rate, performance metrics

- trend strategies  
- scalping bots  
- breakout models  
- mean reversion

- no coding required  

**Cons:**

**Best for:**  
👉 users who want professional strategies without technical complexity.

# 📱💻 Mobile or Desktop — which is better for AI trading?

Short answer:

### 👉 **Start your bots on desktop — monitor them on mobile.**

### ✔ Desktop is better for:
- launching bots  
- adjusting parameters  
- backtesting and analysis  
- Kryll Marketplace  
- Hyperliquid AI Agents (needs screen space)

### ✔ Mobile is better for:
- quick PnL checks  
- notifications  
- monitoring existing bots  
- MEXC Spot Grid & DCA  

**Why?**  
Setup is clearer and safer on a computer, but daily monitoring is easier on a phone.
