# 🛑 Why a Bot Sometimes Fails to Start (Most Common Reasons)

### ❌ “Insufficient Balance”  
Futures Grid requires **200+ USDT** minimum margin.

### ❌ Leverage too high (50x, 100x)  
Grid strategies work properly only at **3–5x**.

### ❌ Wrong futures type (Coin-M instead of USDT-M)  
Grid only works on **USDT-M Futures**.

### ❌ Hedge Mode enabled  
Grid requires **One-Way Mode**.

### ❌ Coin too volatile  
Higher volatility requires higher margin → bot refuses to start.
