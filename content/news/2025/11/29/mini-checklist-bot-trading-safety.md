**Bots are not a substitute for knowledge — they are a tool.**  
If used blindly, they can create heavy losses instead of automation benefits. This checklist gives beginners everything they need to avoid the most common bot-trading mistakes.

---

# ✅ 1. Do NOT start a bot during extreme volatility

Worst times to launch a bot:

- sudden drops of –5% to –10%  
- mass liquidations  
- negative macro news  
- FOMC meetings (Federal Reserve – FED)  
- inflation releases (CPI, PCE)

➡️ **Start bots in calm market conditions — not during chaos.**

---

# ✅ 2. Keep leverage low (1–3x)

Simple, safe rules:

- **1–3x** → ideal for beginners  
- **5x** → only if you understand the risk  
- **10–20x** → only for experts  

➡️ High leverage = fast liquidation.

---

# ✅ 3. Check the minimum capital required

Examples:

- MEXC Futures Grid → **200–300 USDT minimum**  
- MEXC Spot Grid → works from 10–20 USDT  
- Hyperliquid AI Agents → best with 30–100 USDC  
- Kryll strategies → 50–200 USDT  

Too little capital → bot stops or behaves inconsistently.

---

# ✅ 4. Check your Position Mode: One-Way vs Hedge

MEXC has two modes:

- **One-Way Mode** → bots work  
- **Hedge Mode** → bots *cannot* run  

If you can hold long and short at the same time → you are in Hedge Mode.

➡️ **Grid bots require One-Way Mode.**

---

# ✅ 5. Don’t run too many bots at once

Typical beginner mistake:

❌ 5 bots on 5 different coins  
❌ capital spreads too thin  
❌ margin becomes unstable  

Recommendation:

👉 **Start with 1–2 bots maximum.**

---

# ✅ 6. Always check parameters — never run bots blindly

Grid bots use:

- upper price limit  
- lower price limit  
- number of grids  
- starting capital  

If price exits the grid zone → bot stops working properly.

---

# ✅ 7. Combine one safe bot and one aggressive bot

**Safe bots:**
- MEXC Spot Grid  
- DCA bot  
- Hyperliquid Mean Reversion  

**Aggressive bots:**
- Hyperliquid Volatility AI  
- MEXC Futures Grid  
- Kryll breakout strategies  

➡️ A good balance of stability and profit potential.

---

# ✅ 8. Do NOT use bots on illiquid or pump-and-dump coins

Best assets for bot trading:

- BTC  
- ETH  
- SOL  
- BNB  
- TON  
- AVAX  

Worst:

- new memecoins  
- low-volume “shitcoins”  
- pump & dump tokens  

➡️ AI performs best on stable, liquid markets.

---

# 🧭 How to know if your bot is performing well

**Good signs:**  
- balance increases steadily  
- grids fill regularly  
- volatility doesn’t kill margin  
- no large drawdown  

**Bad signs:**  
- price constantly exits the grid  
- bot inactive for 24h  
- loss > –10% with no recovery  
- constant manual intervention required  

---

# 🟦 Our take (MetaAdvisor)

- Bots are mathematical tools — not magic.  
- 70–80% of beginner losses come from poor risk management, not bad bots.  
- Best workflow: launch bots on desktop → monitor on mobile.  
- Ideal for beginners: one safe bot + one more aggressive bot.
