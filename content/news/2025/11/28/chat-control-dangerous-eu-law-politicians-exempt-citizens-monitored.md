# 🇪🇺 CHAT CONTROL: The Biggest Surveillance Precedent in EU History — and Why It’s Especially Dangerous in Former Eastern Bloc States

**The most alarming part of Chat Control is not only the mass scanning of private messages — but the fact that politicians are explicitly exempt from surveillance.  
Citizens lose privacy.  
Elites gain immunity.  
And Europe gains a law without democratic precedent.**

The EU is preparing to introduce a regulation that would automatically scan private messages, photos, videos and attachments of all citizens — on WhatsApp, Signal, Messenger, Telegram, Viber and all encrypted platforms.

For the first time in European history, *active digital surveillance* would be enabled without a court order, without suspicion and without individual assessment.

---

## 🔵 1. Who is pushing Chat Control the most?

Unexpectedly — **Denmark**.

Despite being one of Europe’s most liberal societies, Denmark is the strongest proponent of Chat Control, arguing that:

- online child exploitation is increasing  
- 80–90% of illicit material circulates through encryption  
- “protection of children is impossible without scanning”

The intention may be good — the consequences are not.

---

## 🔵 2. Who is firmly against it?

The most outspoken opponents include:

- **Germany**  
- **Netherlands**  
- **Austria**  
- **Finland**  
- **Luxembourg**  
- **Czech Republic**  
- **Poland (digital rights & legal institutions)**

Their position is clear:

### “Mass scanning of private messages is not democratically acceptable.”

Key reasons:

- unprecedented mass surveillance  
- weakening of end-to-end encryption  
- high potential for political abuse  
- criminals simply move to harder-to-track channels  
- further collapse of trust in EU institutions

# 🔵 3. Highest misuse risk: Former Eastern Bloc countries

Experts warn that **the highest political misuse risk exists in states with a history of political surveillance, weaker institutions and limited judicial independence**.

These include:

- **Hungary**  
- **Slovakia**  
- **Romania**  
- **Bulgaria**  
- **Croatia**  
- **Slovenia**

This is NOT an accusation — but a risk analysis based on democratic indicators.

---

## 📌 Freedom House  
– frequently lists these states as “democratically vulnerable”  
– notes declining judicial independence and rising political influence  

## 📌 Transparency International  
– many of these states rank among the weakest in the EU for corruption control  
– political influence over institutions increases the risk of misuse  

## 📌 European Digital Rights (EDRi)  
– warns Chat Control is especially dangerous in states with authoritarian legacy  
– highlights risks of monitoring political opponents  

## 📌 Reporters Without Borders (RSF)  
– documents declining media freedom in Hungary, Bulgaria, Slovenia  
– states with weakened media oversight are more vulnerable to surveillance abuse  

## 📌 European Parliament – LIBE Committee  
– several documents warn that Chat Control is “especially risky in states with lower democratic standards”

# 🔵 4. The biggest scandal: Politicians are exempt from surveillance

The most controversial element:

### **Citizens — monitored.  
Politicians — protected.**

A surveillance mechanism that scans the population but excludes political elites creates the most dangerous precedent:

### **The people under scrutiny. The powerful above scrutiny.**

This is not a European democratic standard — it is a red alert.
