💥 **Bitcoin is under pressure again.**  
The price fell below **$98,000**, marking the lowest level in several weeks and the **third significant pullback in just a few months**.

According to **CoinGlass data**, more than **$1.3 billion in long positions** were liquidated in the past 48 hours – since **November 11**, when Bitcoin was still trading above $106,600. This sharp wave of forced sales drove the price under the critical $100,000 level.

---

### 📉 Market is cleansing — and repositioning

Analysts describe this as a classic correction **within a larger up-trend**.  
Many investors — both institutional and retail — are using the decline as a “buy-the-dip” opportunity.

> “Liquidations do not necessarily mark the end of a trend – rather the market often seeks a new equilibrium,” comments MetaAdvisor.  
> “Following short-term panic, a consolidation phase usually comes before a new upward move.”

---

### 📊 Technical zone around $98,000

Trader *Daan Crypto* pointed out that just below **$98,000** lies a large liquidity cluster — where many stop-loss orders and leveraged long positions were placed.  
If this zone holds, a **short squeeze toward $108,000-$112,000** could unfold.

On the flip side, if Bitcoin decisively breaks below 98K, analysts see **$92,000** as the next significant support zone, where institutional buyers may again step in.
