### 📉 Technical view: pressure on $14.40 support

LINK fell from $15.26 to $14.29, with **trading volume surging 118% above average**.  
A fast three-wave liquidation between **17:05 and 17:41 UTC** saw more than **360,000 tokens** trade in minutes, pushing price toward new support near **$14.40**.  
CoinDesk research’s model indicates the range **$15.00–$15.26** now acts as strong resistance, while a break below **$14.20** could trigger further losses.

---

### 🟢 Accumulation despite drawdowns

Interestingly, the **Chainlink Reserve** continues to buy its own tokens.  
On Thursday it added **74,049 LINK**, pushing total holdings above **800,000 tokens**.  
The average acquisition cost is around **$20**, meaning the reserve is currently **~27% under water**, yet still accumulating — a strong sign of long-term confidence.

---

### 📊 Broader picture: market consolidation

The drop in Chainlink is not isolated — most altcoins are following Bitcoin, which recently fell below $98,000.  
Analysts view this as a **healthy market reset**, with many investors waiting for **buy-the-dip** opportunities at lower levels.

---

### 💬 Our take (MetaAdvisor)

This correction does *not* necessarily mark the end of the trend, but rather a **market rebalancing**.  
Despite losses, Chainlink shows strength through **active accumulation** and growing demand for oracle solutions in DeFi.
