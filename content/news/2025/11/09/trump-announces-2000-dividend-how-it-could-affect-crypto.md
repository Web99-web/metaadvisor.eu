U.S. President **Donald Trump** announced on Sunday that most Americans will receive a **“dividend” of $2,000**, funded by tariff revenues.  
The measure, he said, is meant to stimulate spending and “return money to the American people.”  

> “A dividend of at least $2,000 per person, excluding high-income earners, will be paid to everyone,” Trump posted on Truth Social, while criticizing opponents of his tariff policies.

The proposal still faces legal hurdles: the **U.S. Supreme Court** is currently reviewing the constitutionality of the tariffs.  
Prediction markets place the odds of approval at just **23%** on Kalshi and **21%** on Polymarket.

Despite that uncertainty, **investors reacted positively**, viewing the announcement as a potential **stimulus for risk assets** like **Bitcoin and equities**.  
Analysts at *The Kobeissi Letter* estimate that around **85% of U.S. adults** could qualify for the payments, mirroring the distribution model used during the COVID-19 relief era.

> “If you don’t put that $2,000 into assets, inflation will simply eat it away,” warned Bitcoin advocate **Simon Dixon**, adding that much of the money will end up servicing debt or in banks.

Short term, the stimulus could **boost market sentiment and prices**, but analysts warn of **inflationary risks and dollar devaluation** in the long term.  
As with previous stimulus rounds, part of the funds may flow into **digital assets**, temporarily lifting demand for Bitcoin and altcoins.
