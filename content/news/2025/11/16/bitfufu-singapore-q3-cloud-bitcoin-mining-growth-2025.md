Singapore’s cloud-Bitcoin mining leader **BitFuFu** has reported exceptionally strong results for the third quarter of 2025, more than **doubling its revenue** year-over-year. The company attributes the surge to the global recovery of crypto markets and rapidly rising demand for cloud hashrate services.

According to BitFuFu, the growth came from three main drivers:

- increased demand for **cloud hashrate packages**,  
- expansion of mining operations in the United States,  
- Bitcoin price recovery and stronger institutional interest.

While 2024 was marked by volatility and lower profitability across the mining sector, 2025 has seen major investors return to Bitcoin mining infrastructure. Cloud-mining models are expanding the fastest, as retail users seek mining exposure without purchasing physical hardware.

Regulatory developments in the US — including the **Genius Act**, **Clarity Act**, and the ban on CBDCs — have further strengthened investor confidence and helped stabilize the mining environment.

BitFuFu is simultaneously expanding partnerships in Texas and across the Middle East, while demand in Asia continues to grow due to affordable cloud-mining plans.

## 🧠 Key terms explained

**Cloud mining** — renting external computational power to mine BTC, without physical hardware.  
**Hashrate** — total computational power securing the Bitcoin network.  
**Q3** — third quarter (July–September).
