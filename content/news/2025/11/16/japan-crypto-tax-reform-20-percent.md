Japan is preparing the most comprehensive crypto reform in its history — digital assets may soon be officially classified as *financial products*, accompanied by strict disclosure rules and a dramatic reduction of the tax rate from 55% to only 20%.

Japan’s Financial Services Agency (FSA) is working on a new legislative framework that would place cryptocurrencies under the same regulatory umbrella as stocks and other traditional financial instruments. Bitcoin, Ether and another 103 tokens would fall under the **Financial Instruments and Exchange Act (FIEA)** — Japan’s strictest financial regulatory regime.

---

## 🔍 New classification: “financial products”

The reform covers **105 cryptocurrencies** listed on Japanese exchanges and introduces mandatory disclosures for each asset, including:

- whether the token has an identifiable issuer  
- technical description of the blockchain  
- volatility and risk assessment  
- token structure  
- level of centralization  
- governance and security details  

For the first time in Japan, digital assets would also become subject to **insider trading rules**, making it illegal to trade based on non-public information such as:

- upcoming listings  
- planned delistings  
- undisclosed technical risks  
- financial distress of the issuer  

The FSA plans to submit the proposal to parliament in **2026**.
