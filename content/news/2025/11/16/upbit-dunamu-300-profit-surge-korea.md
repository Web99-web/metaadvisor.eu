South Korea’s largest crypto exchange operator **Dunamu**, the company behind **Upbit**, has reported exceptionally strong financial results for the third quarter of 2025. The firm posted more than **300% year-over-year profit growth**, marking one of the most significant quarterly jumps in the Asian crypto sector this year.

According to a report from Chosun Biz citing regulatory filings submitted to Korea’s Financial Supervisory Service, Dunamu recorded **239 billion won** in Q3 net income (around **165 million USD**). In the same period a year earlier, the figure was approximately 40 million USD.

Consolidated revenue climbed to **266 million USD** (+35% QoQ), while operating profit reached **162 million USD**, representing a 54% quarter-over-quarter increase. All major financial indicators showed broad-based improvement.

## 🇰🇷 Why is the Korean market so important?

Upbit dominates the Korean crypto market with a share of more than **80%**.  
In one of the world’s most active crypto-trading nations, any shifts in Upbit trading volumes often influence broader sentiment across Asia.

Korean traders are known for:

- high altcoin activity  
- rapid portfolio rotations  
- strong influence on tokens such as XRP, SOL, SAND, TON and others  

When the Korean market moves, global liquidity often follows.

## 📈 What drove Dunamu’s massive profit surge?

Regulatory filings and local reports point to several contributing factors:

### • Increased trading activity  
Market conditions were more stable in 2025, boosting confidence among both retail and institutional investors.

### • Regulatory clarity from the United States  
New U.S. legislation — including the **Genius Act**, **Clarity Act** and **Anti-CBDC Bill** — helped reduce uncertainty and supported trading activity.

### • Higher institutional participation  
A stronger presence of larger market players contributed to increased volumes and revenue for exchanges.

## 🌍 Other crypto firms also posted strong results

Several companies operating in mining and infrastructure reported notable growth:

- **TeraWulf** (USA): doubled its Q3 revenue compared to last year  
- **BitFuFu** (Singapore): reported strong cloud-mining revenue growth  

This suggests a broader recovery across the digital asset industry.

## 🧠 Key terms explained

**YoY (Year-over-Year)** – comparison with the same period one year earlier.  
**Q3** – third quarter (July–September).  
**Consolidated revenue** – combined revenue of all subsidiaries and affiliated entities.
