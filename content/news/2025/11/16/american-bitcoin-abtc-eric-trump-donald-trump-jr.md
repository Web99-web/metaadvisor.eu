**American Bitcoin Corp (ABTC)** has become one of the most talked-about crypto-linked stocks in the United States — not because of its technology, but because of its politics.  
The company is **directly connected to Donald Trump’s sons — Eric Trump and Donald Trump Jr.**, making it the most politically charged crypto stock of the year.  
Since the stock is also available on platforms like **[eToro](/en/news/platforma-drustveno-trgovanje-etoro-profit/)**, retail interest is rapidly increasing.

---

## 🇺🇸 What is American Bitcoin (ABTC)?

American Bitcoin Corp is a Bitcoin mining company that went public through a **reverse merger**, a faster and cheaper alternative to a traditional IPO.

The company brands itself as:

- “American Bitcoin for the American economy”  
- “a national strategic resource”  
- “domestic Bitcoin production that stays in the U.S.”

This narrative is gaining traction in political circles advocating for U.S. dominance in global Bitcoin mining and infrastructure.

---

## 👔 The Trump sons and political influence

Eric Trump and Donald Trump Jr. are directly involved in the project.  
No other mining company on NASDAQ has this level of political proximity.

For investors, this adds both opportunity and significant risk, as political events can sharply move the stock.

---

## 💼 Scaramucci: Trump administration + TON ecosystem

Another major element is **Anthony Scaramucci**, who briefly served as the White House Communications Director under Donald Trump in 2017.

Beyond politics, Scaramucci has been active in Web3 for years and is tied to early investment structures connected to the **TON ecosystem**.  
We covered this angle in detail here:

👉 **[Biomedicine, blockchain and Web3 — financing and development](/en/news/biomedicina-blockchain-crypto-financiranje-razvoj/)**

This combination of political influence, capital and Web3 exposure amplifies ABTC’s profile.

---

## 💰 Scaramucci family invests over 100 million USD

The biggest surprise came when **Solari Capital**, led by AJ Scaramucci, revealed an investment of **over 100 million USD** in American Bitcoin.

Other notable investors include:

- Tony Robbins  
- Charles Hoskinson (Cardano)  
- Grant Cardone  
- Peter Diamandis  
- Anthony Scaramucci (smaller amount)

A uniquely diverse investor group for a mining company.
