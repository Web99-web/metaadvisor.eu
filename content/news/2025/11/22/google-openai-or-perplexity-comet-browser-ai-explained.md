## Google, OpenAI or Perplexity?  
### The new Comet browser is redefining AI search

Perplexity, one of the fastest-growing AI startups in the U.S., is asking a question that once sounded impossible:

**Can anyone replace Google as the primary search platform?**

With its new **Comet browser**, Perplexity is launching the biggest challenge to traditional web search in 20 years.  
After launching on desktop, Comet is now arriving on **Android** — introducing a completely new way of navigating the internet with AI assistance.

---

## 🔍 What exactly is Perplexity?
Perplexity is an **AI search engine** that responds like ChatGPT but with **real sources**, up-to-date information, and live web analysis.  
It is already used by millions — especially developers, researchers and people who need verified information.

---

## 🚀 What is Comet?
Comet is a browser that:

- performs AI search in real time  
- analyzes full web pages  
- understands, summarizes and compares tabs  
- conducts research autonomously  
- assists with shopping, reading and learning  
- automatically blocks ads  

On Android, additional features include:

- voice queries across all tabs  
- cross-tab summaries  
- live assistant actions  
- a built-in password manager coming soon  

---

## 📱 Why launch on Android first?
Perplexity confirms that **several major smartphone manufacturers** (carriers & OEMs) have contacted them to preinstall Comet.  
Only Motorola is officially confirmed — but interest is rising quickly.

This means:  
**For the first time in years, Google is getting real competition on its own Android devices.**
