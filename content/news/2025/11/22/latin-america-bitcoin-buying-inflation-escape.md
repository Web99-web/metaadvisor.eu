## 🇦🇷 Why Bitcoin is exploding across Latin America

For many countries in the region, financial crises are not an exception — they’re the baseline:

- Hyperinflation (Argentina, Venezuela)  
- Capital controls  
- Deep distrust in banks  
- Rapid devaluation of local currencies (Peso, Real)

When your salary loses half its purchasing power in a month, there’s only one rational move:  
**you protect your savings with Bitcoin.**

## 🇧🇷 Ripio: The hidden crypto giant of Latin America

Argentinian exchange **Ripio** revealed that its corporate crypto treasury exceeds **$100M**, mainly in **BTC** and **ETH**.

What surprises most analysts is that Ripio, relative to its company size,  
**holds more crypto than MicroStrategy**, the famous Bitcoin giant led by Michael Saylor.

Other major treasuries in the region include:

- **OranjeBTC (Brazil):** 3,713 BTC — larger than many U.S. corporate holders  
- **Méliuz:** $54M in BTC  
- **Mercado Libre:** $51M in BTC  

In countries with chronically unstable financial systems, Bitcoin is no longer speculation —  
**it’s defense.**
