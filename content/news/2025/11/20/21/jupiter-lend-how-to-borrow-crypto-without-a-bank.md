## Introduction

Many people still believe that borrowing money is only possible through traditional banks, with paperwork, income verification and long waiting times.  
In the crypto world, a completely different model exists: **borrowing against your own crypto collateral**, directly on the blockchain, without banks and without delays.

On Solana, this is enabled by **Jupiter Lend**, a module inside the Jupiter ecosystem. Most users know Jupiter only as a DEX, yet within the interface there is also a powerful lending and borrowing tool.

## How Jupiter Lend works

After connecting your wallet, clicking *Lend* opens a module with two actions:  
**Supply** (deposit tokens and earn yield) and **Borrow** (take a loan and use your assets as collateral).

Collateral options include SOL, wBTC, wETH, USDT, USDC, JitoSOL, bSOL and other liquid Solana assets.  
Once you select a token, the system instantly shows:

- maximum borrow amount  
- interest rate  
- current *Health Factor* (your position’s safety rating)

## Managing your borrowing position

All changes are visible in real time. If the market drops sharply and your health factor weakens, you can stabilize your position by **adding more collateral** or **repaying part of your loan**.  
If the market rises, liquidation risk naturally decreases.

On Solana, all actions settle within seconds — making this model extremely fast and easy to manage.

Many users prefer this approach because it allows them to access liquidity **without selling their crypto**, often avoiding a taxable event. For smaller loans, this is a faster, more flexible and more transparent option than a traditional bank loan.

## How to avoid liquidation

Liquidations happen when your collateral loses too much value.  
Therefore, similar rules apply as with BTC-collateral loans:

**1️⃣ Never borrow the maximum allowed amount**  
If the limit is 75%, borrow only 30–40% to stay safe.

**2️⃣ If the market drops — add more collateral**  
This instantly strengthens your position.

**3️⃣ If you can’t add collateral — repay part of the loan**  
Even a small repayment can significantly increase stability.

**Golden rule:**  
**Always assume the market can drop 30–70% in a single day — because this has already happened multiple times in crypto history. If it falls even further, your position almost certainly enters automatic liquidation.**

### 1) Manually type **jup.ag**  
If the domain is not *exactly* jup.ag — close it immediately.

### 2) Connect a Solana wallet  
Phantom, Solflare, Backpack… Jupiter only requests a *Sign*, never a seed phrase.

### 3) Click “Lend”  
Sometimes located under “More”.

### 4) Choose Supply or Borrow  
Supply = deposit and earn yield  
Borrow = loan backed by collateral

### 5) Select your collateral  
SOL, wBTC, wETH, USDT, USDC, JitoSOL, bSOL…

### 6) Check the Health Factor  
Higher = safer.

### 7) Confirm the transaction  
Click → Sign → Done.

### 8) If the market drops — add collateral  
If it rises — risk decreases.

### 9) Repay via “Repay”  
The collateral unlocks automatically.
