## How do Bitcoin-backed loans work?

Bitcoin-backed loans give you immediate liquidity without having to sell your BTC. This is especially useful for long-term holders who want access to capital while keeping their exposure to Bitcoin’s future upside.

The mechanics are simple: you deposit BTC as collateral, and the platform lends you USD, USDT, or USDC. Your BTC remains your property but is locked until the loan is repaid. If you maintain a healthy LTV, the full collateral is returned once the loan is settled.

## Why take a BTC-backed loan?

- access liquidity without selling BTC  
- avoid triggering a taxable event  
- stay exposed to long-term BTC price appreciation  
- buy additional BTC using leverage  
- more flexible terms than traditional bank loans  

The key metric behind every crypto loan is the LTV ratio.

## What is LTV and why does it matter?

LTV (Loan-to-Value) measures the ratio between the value of the collateral and the borrowed amount.

Example:  
If you borrow 2,000 USD and deposit 0.05 BTC, and Bitcoin trades at 90,000 USD, the collateral is worth ~4,500 USD.  
This gives an LTV of about 44%.

Higher LTV = higher liquidation risk.

## The biggest risk: liquidation

Liquidation can occur even when you:

- have been paying interest consistently for months or years  
- already repaid part of the principal  
- kept your loan stable for a long time  

If BTC suddenly drops and your LTV exceeds the platform’s threshold, the system automatically liquidates part or all of your collateral to cover the loan.

The system protects the platform — not the borrower.

This is why experienced users keep LTV **below 30–35%**.

## Can you add more collateral when the price drops?

Yes — as long as the network is functioning normally.  
During sudden crashes, however, liquidations can be faster than blockchain confirmations, meaning collateral may be lost before your top-up arrives.

## How is the loan repaid?

Depending on the platform:

- interest may be charged daily or monthly  
- principal repayment is usually flexible  
- the loan can run indefinitely  
- the only critical factor is keeping LTV safe  

The process is flexible but sensitive to market volatility.

## Where can you get BTC-backed loans?

Bitcoin-backed loans are available on:

### **1. Conservative, specialized lenders**
- Ledn  
- Unchained Capital  
- CoinLoan  
- Anchorage Digital  

### **2. Centralized crypto exchanges (CeX)**
- Binance  
- OKX  
- Bitget (Crypto Loans)  
- Bybit  
- KuCoin  

### **3. DeFi protocols**
- Aave  
- MakerDAO  
- Compound  
- Liquity  
- Spark  
- Jupiter Lend (Solana)  

DeFi offers high flexibility but carries the highest liquidation risk.

## When does a BTC-backed loan make sense?

- when you believe in long-term BTC growth  
- when you don’t want to sell your Bitcoin  
- when you can maintain very low LTV  
- when you have liquidity to add collateral if needed  
- when you understand volatility and risk  

Without these conditions, the likelihood of losing the BTC collateral becomes much higher.
