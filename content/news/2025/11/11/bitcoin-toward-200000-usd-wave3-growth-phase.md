The price of Bitcoin (BTC) is once again trading in a narrow range between **US$102,000 and US$103,000**, after a brief spike to US$107,000 on Monday. According to exchange data, the largest cryptocurrency has dropped around **2.5% over the past 24 hours**, wiping out gains triggered by [Trump’s plan for a “tariff dividend”](https://metaadvisor.eu/news/trump-announces-2000-dividend-how-it-could-affect-crypto/) and optimism about the end of the US government spending standoff.

Ethereum (ETH) fell 2.3% below US$3,500, while larger altcoins such as **Solana (SOL)**, **XRP**, and **SUI token** recorded declines of 4-5%.  
The sell-off extended into crypto-stocks, particularly among Bitcoin-mining firms that had benefited from the AI-boom.  
CleanSpark (CLSK) dropped 8%, Hut 8 nearly 9%, and Core Scientific (CORZ) more than 11%.  
TeraWulf (WULF) and [Bitdeer (BTDR)](https://metaadvisor.eu/news/bitdeer-bitcoin-mining-and-ai/) also logged double-digit losses following weaker earnings and warnings of delays in new ASIC chip rollouts.

Additional pressure on the market came from news that [SoftBank sold its stake in Nvidia](https://metaadvisor.eu/news/softbank-exits-nvidia-ai-market-shocked/) worth US$5.8 billion, causing the stock of the world’s largest AI company to fall 3.5%.  
After several months of strong gains, investors appear to be entering a phase of **profit-taking** and consolidation as the market attempts to stabilise.

---

### 🧭 Analysts forecast “Wave III” — move toward US$200,000?

Despite the correction, Bitcoin’s long-term structure remains bullish.  
Analyst **Gert Van Lagen** argues that the corrective Wave II phase is nearing completion, and that the third impulse wave — *Wave III Expansion* — may be about to begin, potentially driving Bitcoin’s price toward **US$200,000 or even US$240,000**.

His model draws on the **Elliott Wave Theory**, which posits that each major consolidation phase lays the base for the next parabolic breakout.  
Crypto-trader **Jelle** adds that BTC is currently testing the midline of its long-term ascending price channel, and a breakout above could open the path to the channel’s upper boundary — near **US$350,000**.

Macroeconomic analysts highlight that a recovery in the US economy and a rise in risk-on investor sentiment could further fuel the next leg of Bitcoin’s rally.

### 🐋 Whale opens long US$195 million on Ethereum

According to data shared on X by **Crypto Rover**, the same crypto “whale” who previously shorted Bitcoin ahead of Trump’s tariff dividend announcement has now opened a **long position worth US$195.7 million on Ethereum (ETH)**.  
Many analysts view this move as a signal of potential trend reversal and the beginning of a new phase of institutional accumulation.

At a time when retail investors are increasingly selling, “smart money” appears to be entering — reinforcing the case that the market might be laying the groundwork for the next up-leg.

### 🧩 Consolidation and institutional repositioning

The market is increasingly resembling a phase of **controlled consolidation**: weaker participants are exiting, while large **ETF-funds and institutional players** solidify their positions.  
Growing institutional investment via **Bitcoin ETFs** (e.g., BlackRock, Fidelity, VanEck) is making the market more resilient to abrupt drops, as these funds tend to act strategically rather than impulsively.

Furthermore, the recent [IRS approval of staking and crypto-ETF framework](https://metaadvisor.eu/news/irs-approves-crypto-etf-staking-framework/) adds another institutional leg under the market.

As a result, more analysts consider the range between **US$100,000 and US$103,000** as the new support zone — and the base from which the next growth wave toward **US$200,000+** could launch.
