### 🐋 Who is the “whale”?

The term *whale* refers to investors managing tens or hundreds of millions of dollars whose moves often impact the market.  
In this case, the address is known for timing — the previous Bitcoin short was opened **just hours before** the tariff announcement.

The new long position on Ethereum valued at nearly **$200 million** suggests accumulation and expectation of a market rebound.

---

### 📈 Signal of confidence in Ethereum

Ethereum has been under pressure recently due to lower trading volumes and pullbacks by institutional funds.  
Such a move by a major player could be interpreted as **a shift in sentiment** — especially given rising interest in AI tokens and projects leveraging Ethereum’s network for data processing.

> “Institutions enter when noise is loudest and confidence lowest. That’s often when a new trend begins,” comments MetaAdvisor.
