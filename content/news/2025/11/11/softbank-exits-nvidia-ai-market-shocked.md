💥 **Masayoshi Son shocks markets once again.**  
The SoftBank founder has decided to **sell his entire $5.8 billion Nvidia stake**, announcing plans to redirect capital into *artificial intelligence*.  
The decision shook global markets, given Nvidia’s symbolic status as the face of AI’s explosive growth and semiconductor dominance.

Nvidia’s stock fell 3.5% after the announcement, sparking increased volatility.  
Investors are now asking — **if SoftBank is exiting Nvidia, what does that mean for the entire AI sector?**

### 📉 A history of extreme bets

Masayoshi Son is not known for caution.  
His career has been defined by massive risks — from **the largest personal loss in history** during the dot-com crash (over $70 billion) to his **legendary $20 million investment in Alibaba**, later worth more than **$150 billion**.

Now 68, Son still plays “all or nothing.”  
After his SoftBank Vision Fund success — backed by billions from Saudi investors — he is returning to his roots: **AI and automation**, calling it “the new internet revolution.”

---

### 💻 Nvidia — the engine of every AI revolution

Without Nvidia’s chips, modern AI wouldn’t exist.  
GPU processors like **H100**, **A100**, and **B100** power ChatGPT, Midjourney, Claude, Gemini, and most other major AI models.  
Yet Son’s sale doesn’t mark a retreat — he’s **shifting focus from hardware to innovation**.

> “I don’t want to just hold shares of chipmakers. I want to invest in companies that *use* those chips,” Son reportedly said.

---

### 📊 An opportunity for retail investors

Despite Nvidia’s brief 3.5% drop, analysts believe this could be **an entry point for smaller investors**.  
The AI sector remains fundamentally strong, and Nvidia’s chips are indispensable to the industry.

> “Institutional exits often mark short-term peaks, creating room for new participants,” notes MetaAdvisor.  
> “Retail investors may now have the chance to enter during the second wave of AI growth.”

---

### 🧠 SoftBank goes all-in on AI

Son is preparing new funds focused on **generative AI, next-gen chips, data centers, and decentralized computing**.  
Selling Nvidia aligns with a broader capital rotation from **hardware toward AI software and cloud infrastructure**.

---

### 🌐 AI and crypto grow closer

The Nvidia correction coincided with volatility in the crypto market and a growing overlap between AI and blockchain sectors.  
Companies like [**Bitdeer**](https://metaadvisor.eu/news/bitdeer-bitcoin-mining-and-ai/) are already combining **Bitcoin mining and AI cloud services**, showing how both ecosystems are beginning to merge.

Capital isn’t disappearing — it’s **evolving** into new forms.

### 💬 MetaAdvisor’s take

SoftBank’s sale isn’t weakness — it’s a shift of strategy.  
The company is seeking the **next stage of the AI revolution** — beyond chips, into intelligent data and autonomous systems.  
For retail investors, it could be a signal to look at long-term exposure to AI-linked tech stocks and startups.

This isn’t the end of Nvidia’s story — it’s **the start of a new cycle** where AI, blockchain, and compute infrastructure converge.
