Texas has officially become the first U.S. state to purchase Bitcoin through a spot BTC ETF. According to public financial records, the Texas Treasury acquired **$5 million USD** worth of Bitcoin via an ETF — a move already described as a historic milestone.

At the same time, several crypto analysts and podcast commentators noted that the **state’s total Bitcoin allocation could be closer to $10 million USD**. This suggests that the initial $5 million purchase may represent the first tranche, with additional acquisitions possible — either through ETFs or through directly held Bitcoin, depending on regulatory and custody decisions.

### Why this matters  
For the first time, a U.S. state is considering digital assets as part of its official reserves. While cities like Miami, New York, and Fort Worth have experimented with Bitcoin, Texas is the first to act at the **state level**, and it comes at a moment when many states are reassessing long-term financial resilience.

Buying through an ETF offers advantages: transparency, simplified accounting, and lower operational risk. But the key limitation remains — the state **does not hold the private keys**, raising the question of whether Texas will eventually shift toward direct BTC custody in the future.

Texas officials describe the move as “a step toward the future,” positioning the state for the emerging digital economy, tokenization era, and blockchain-driven infrastructure.
