Robinhood, together with Susquehanna International Group, has announced the acquisition of **90% of MIAXdx/LedgerX** — a fully regulated derivatives exchange and clearinghouse that once formed a core pillar of the FTX ecosystem. This marks the company’s most radical strategic shift since it entered crypto several years ago.

MIAXdx (formerly LedgerX) is uniquely important because it was one of the **very few FTX-linked entities that remained solvent** after the exchange’s collapse in 2022, largely thanks to strict CFTC oversight and segregated customer funds.

### Entering prediction markets: a rapidly growing sector
With this move, Robinhood is entering **prediction markets** — a sector where users trade contracts based on the probability of future events:

- political outcomes  
- economic decisions  
- societal trends  
- sports events  
- technological developments  

This step puts Robinhood in direct competition with Kalshi and Polymarket, both of which have gained significant momentum.

Crucially, this move shifts Robinhood from a brokerage interface to becoming an **infrastructure provider** with its own exchange and clearing capabilities.

### Why LedgerX matters
LedgerX is often viewed as a rare “success story” in the FTX universe:

- it remained fully operational during the FTX meltdown  
- it was tightly regulated by the CFTC  
- all customer funds were segregated  
- it followed strict compliance and reporting obligations  

In other words: LedgerX was the one part of the FTX empire that worked exactly as it should have.

### A reminder of the FTX collapse
Robinhood’s entry into the derivatives and prediction market business cannot be separated from the context of 2022.

The contrast is crucial:  
FTX attempted to dominate the same sectors through aggressive expansion but collapsed due to mismanagement, commingled customer funds and a lack of regulatory protection.

### Robinhood + Susquehanna: a strategic reboot
Through the acquisition, Robinhood gains:

- a licensed exchange  
- a regulated clearinghouse  
- infrastructure for prediction markets  
- institutional-grade liquidity from Susquehanna  

Susquehanna is one of the world’s largest market-makers and will provide liquidity and credibility to the new venture.

### Why this matters for the industry
Prediction markets are one of the fastest-growing areas of fintech.  
They could become a “real-time information network” — a kind of social signal engine powered by price data.

Robinhood wants to position itself as one of the first large, regulated players in this field.

### What it means for investors
There are significant advantages:

- access to new financial instruments  
- regulated event contracts  
- deeper liquidity  
- institutional infrastructure  

But also meaningful risks:

- political and ethical concerns around event betting  
- volatility based on real-world events  
- the potential for tighter regulation  

### Bottom line
Robinhood is making its boldest strategic move yet — transforming from a trading interface into a **market infrastructure operator**.  
The acquisition of MIAXdx/LedgerX could reshape the future of regulated prediction markets.

The memory of FTX serves as a reminder of why transparency, compliance and strong oversight matter more than ever.
