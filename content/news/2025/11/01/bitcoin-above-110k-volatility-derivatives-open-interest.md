## What’s happening under the hood?

| Term | Meaning | Why it matters above 110k |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Interest (OI)** | Outstanding futures/options contracts | High OI = more fuel for moves via forced flows both ways |
| **Funding rate** | Cost of leverage on perpetuals | Extremes imply crowded side; contrarian moves are common |
| **Automatic liquidations of leveraged positions** | System closures when margin is insufficient | Accelerate moves; can “clean” the market before trend continuation |
| **IV (implied volatility)** | Expected volatility in options | Higher IV = pricier options, tendency to large candles |
| **Max pain (options)** | Theoretical level of maximum option losses | Price gravitates there, esp. near expiries |

**Psychological levels** (100k, 110k, 120k) often cluster stops and option barriers. Price therefore tests multiple supports/resistances in short order, with quick flushes of over-levered longs/shorts.

## Tech & sentiment — our checklist

- **Daily trend/structure** matters more than a single spike.  
- **Lower-TF divergences** (RSI/MACD) often signal pauses, not guaranteed reversals.  
- **Spot vs. perps**: Spot volume confirmation increases the odds a move sticks.  
- **OI distribution** across venues/pairs (BTC-USDT vs. BTC-USD) shows where leverage sits thin.
