U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent marked the 17th anniversary of the Bitcoin whitepaper with a post on X praising the **resilience of the Bitcoin network**, stressing that “Bitcoin never shuts down” — unlike government systems.

> “17 years after the white paper, the Bitcoin network is still operational and more resilient than ever. Bitcoin never shuts down.  
> @SenateDems could learn something from that.”

The post doubled as a **policy signal, a crypto nod and a provocation** toward his opponents in the Senate.

---

## Why it matters

📌 It’s rare for a **Treasury Secretary** to praise Bitcoin publicly.  
📌 Bessent isn’t a “crypto influencer” — he’s an **official of the world’s reserve currency**.  
📌 It reinforces a broader **political shift toward Bitcoin** in the U.S.
