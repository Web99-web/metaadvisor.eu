NFT marketplace **OpenSea** has confirmed its **SEA token** for **Q1 2026**. **Half of supply** goes to the **community**, partly via an **initial claim** and additional **rewards programs**. SEA will be integrated into the **core product**; users can **stake** behind favorite collections/projects. At launch, **50% of platform revenue** will be used to **buy back SEA**.

## Key details
- **Timeline:** **Q1 2026** launch.  
- **Allocation:** **50%** to the community (claim + other categories).  
- **Eligibility:** special consideration for **historical OpenSea users** and **rewards participants**.  
- **Product integration:** **collection/project staking** as an in-app feature.  
- **Buyback:** **50% of revenue** earmarked for an initial **SEA buyback**.

## Why it matters
Signals a shift **beyond NFTs** toward **token trading** and **DeFi features**. The combo of **community allocation** plus **revenue-funded buybacks** targets **early demand and liquidity**.

## Risks & watch-outs
- Precise **claim criteria**, **vesting/lockups**.  
- **Buyback mechanics/timing** (one-off vs. phased).  
- **Sustained utility** for SEA beyond launch hype.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Strong opener:** community + buyback can spark momentum.  
- **Utility is king:** if **collection staking** gains traction, SEA has legs.  
- **Keep tracking:** tokenomics specifics, claim rules, buyback plan and product rollout.
