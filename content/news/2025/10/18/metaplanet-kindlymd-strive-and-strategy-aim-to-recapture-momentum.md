**What’s happening.** After a run of listings in 2025, **Bitcoin treasury companies (BTCTC)** — firms that put BTC at the center of their strategy — have suffered **sharp equity selloffs**. The latest Bitcoin drop added pressure, but many names were **sliding beforehand**. In response, executives at **Metaplanet, KindlyMD, Strive and Strategy** have gone on the offensive (e.g., capital structure tweaks, prioritizing preferred stock) to **win back momentum**.

## The bigger picture
- **Turbo beta to BTC:** BTCTC equities **amplify** Bitcoin cycles — faster up in bull markets, faster down in drawdowns.  
- **Dilution risk:** Funding more BTC via **share/debt issuance** **dilutes** holders and raises timing risk.  
- **Narrative vs. numbers:** Social threads don’t replace **cash flow, cost control and net BTC position**.

## Quick glossary
- **BTCTC:** a listed company using **BTC as core asset/strategy**.  
- **Dilution:** new shares reduce existing holders’ percentage ownership.  
- **Momentum:** short-term trend strength (here: attempt to restore confidence).
