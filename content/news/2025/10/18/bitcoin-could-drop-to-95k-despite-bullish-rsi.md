**The scenario.** While the **RSI indicator** (Relative Strength Index) shows bullish hints – divergences and oversold conditions – analysts caution the market could still retest lower zones near **$95,000**.  

### Key points
- **Trend structure:** a series of lower highs and lows keeps the door open for further downside.  
- **Liquidity under 100k:** stop clusters and thin order book areas attract “flush” moves.  
- **RSI can mislead:** it may signal weakening momentum, but price often dips one more time before a real rebound.

### Why $95K?
- **Psychological level:** $95,000 is a logical support zone just below the round-number 100k.  
- **Technical gaps:** previous rallies left gaps that markets often revisit.  

### Bigger picture
- **Derivatives & leverage:** crowded long positions can trigger liquidation cascades.  
- **Macro pressure:** broader risk-off sentiment (rates, USD liquidity) weighs on BTC regardless of bullish signals.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **$95K is not the end of the world.** It would be a support test, common in volatile cycles.  
- **RSI may be right – just later.** Often a final “flush” comes before a sustainable recovery.  
- **Watch for:** volume at the lows, buyer reactions, and derivative metrics (funding, open interest).
