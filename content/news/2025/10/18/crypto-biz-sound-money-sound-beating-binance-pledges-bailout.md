**Context.** A **sudden drawdown** and **cascading liquidations** defined the week. The **‘sound money’** idea (hard rules, e.g., Bitcoin) met a **‘sound beating’** — in practice, **liquidity, leverage and sentiment** drive price. **Binance** meanwhile **pledged support** to ease liquidity stress and calm order books.

## What broke
- **Leverage washouts:** over-levered longs/shorts were force-closed, deepening the selloff.  
- **Thin books:** especially in altcoins, **spreads** blew out and **depth** vanished.  
- **DeFi stress:** volatile collateral lost value; edge cases tipped into liquidations.

## What Binance’s pledge implies
- **Goal:** **stabilize liquidity** in key pairs and curb panic.  
- **Typical tools (in general):** incentives for **market makers**, temporary **fee relief**, targeted **liquidity lines** for solid projects.  
- **Watch for:** **eligibility**, **duration** and **terms** (collateral, transparency).

## Myths vs. facts
- **“Sound money fixes everything.”** → **Doesn’t** fix leverage cycles or behavior. Price follows **liquidity & sentiment**.  
- **“Bailouts save everyone.”** → **No**; they’re **bridges**, not destinations.

## What it means for users
- **Stress plan:** set **leverage rules** and **loss limits** beforehand.  
- **Liquidity over thesis:** in turmoil, **stables** and **smaller risk** often beat the perfect narrative.  
- **Partners & rules:** know where assets sit (exchange vs. self-custody) and the **halt/interrupt policies**.
