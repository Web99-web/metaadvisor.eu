We heard the claim that Wi-Fi can “see” us. With misinformation everywhere, we dug in. The finding: research has been ongoing for **years** across multiple teams — and now **IEEE 802.11bf** makes it official: **Wi-Fi sensing is coming to routers**.

---

## What “Wi-Fi can see” actually means
No cameras, no magic. **Radio waves** from a router bounce off people/objects. Those changes show up in metrics like **CSI (Channel State Information)**. With ML models, systems can **detect presence**, **track motion**, and — in controlled settings — recognize **gestures**, **falls**, or rough **posture**.

### Myth vs. fact
- **Myth:** “A router knows who you are.”  
  **Fact:** Outside the lab and without extra data — **no**. Sensing is strong at **that someone is there** and **how they move**, not personal identity.  
- **Myth:** “Any neighbor can scan you.”  
  **Fact:** Useful sensing typically needs **hardware/data access**, calibration and trained models.

---

## Why 802.11bf matters
**802.11bf** (2025) defines Wi-Fi **sensing** as an official capability, giving:
- a **common framework** for presence/motion/gesture features,  
- a path from **lab demos to mainstream** devices.

## What it can do today
- **Presence & movement detection**  
- **Gesture & fall detection**  
- **Breathing monitoring** in calm, controlled environments

## Where it falls short
- **Identity:** not reliably “who” outside controlled trials  
- **Clutter & interference:** multiple people/metal/room changes **reduce accuracy**  
- **Operational needs:** often requires **calibration** and **CSI access**

---

## Use cases on the horizon
Smart homes (lights/HVAC by presence), elder care (fall detection), security (motion alerts), offices/retail (people counting), healthcare (passive breathing).

---

## Privacy & protection
Keep firmware updated, use **WPA3**, disable **remote management**, **segment IoT** networks, and check if sensing features exist and can be **turned off**. In the EU, presence data may be **GDPR-relevant**.

---

## Glossary
**Wi-Fi sensing:** using Wi-Fi for **environment perception**; **CSI:** detailed channel fingerprints; **Doppler:** motion signatures in phase/frequency.

---

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
**This is the mainstream moment.** With 802.11bf, sensing leaves the lab. **No panic, but higher standards.** We expect **transparent settings** from vendors and will test real-world benefits and limits as devices ship.
