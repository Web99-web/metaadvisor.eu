According to Bloomberg, **Tether** (issuer of **USDT**) is partnering with **Antalpha**—known for financing within Bitmain’s supply chain—to raise **at least $200M** for a **tokenized gold treasury** centered on **XAUT** (Tether Gold), backed by **physical bars** held in a Swiss vault.

### Key points
- **Asset & market:** **XAUT** is the largest tokenized gold product (≈ **$1.5B** market cap). The vehicle aims to **accumulate XAUT**, offer **collateralized lending**, **custody**, and **redeem** tokens for **physical gold**.  
- **Expanded partnership:** Dedicated hubs for **XAUT lending/custody/redemption** are planned. **Antalpha** says it will open **vaults in major financial centers** to enable **physical redemption**.  
- **Beyond USDT:** With ≈ **$174B** USDT in circulation, Tether has been investing in **bitcoin mining, payments, energy and AI**; it backed **XXI Capital** and is reportedly seeking funds at a **$500B** valuation.  
- **Balance sheet & narrative:** CEO **Paolo Ardoino** has championed gold; Tether held **$8.7B** in gold per its **June attestation**.

### Why it matters
- **RWA momentum:** A full **operating stack** (lending, custody, redeem) for **tokenized commodities**.  
- **On-chain ↔ physical bridge:** Token-to-gold **redemption** is a trust anchor for institutions.  
- **Competitive angle:** Tether expands **beyond stablecoins**, entering a space served by **traditional bullion/custody providers**.

### Metaadvisor’s take
- **Strategically positive:** Diversifies revenue via **RWA (gold)** and strengthens institutional appeal.

- **Next to watch:** Structure of the **$200M** raise, **fee model** (lending/custody/redeem), and **transparency** of XAUT reserves.
