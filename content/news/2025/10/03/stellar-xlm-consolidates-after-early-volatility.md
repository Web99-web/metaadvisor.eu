**XLM (Stellar)** saw sharp swings on **Oct. 3**. After briefly touching **$0.4041**, price retreated to around **$0.4015** after **14:00 UTC**, wiping earlier gains.  
During the pullback, more than **1.4M tokens** traded in a single minute, pointing to **institutional selling** at resistance and potential near-term downside.

### Bigger picture
- **Bitcoin.com Wallet integration** brings Stellar and its DeFi protocols to a wider audience, expanding **payments** use cases for **XLM**.  
- **Seasonality**: October has historically been supportive for crypto, though short-term risks remain.  
- **Market context**: Combined spot + derivatives volume on CEX hit **$9.72T** in August 2025 — a yearly high.

### What is Stellar?
Launched in 2014, **Stellar** targets **fast, low-cost cross-border payments**.  
- **XLM** is the native token used for fees and value transfer.  
- The community frames XLM as **“digital cash for everyday payments,”** while **Bitcoin** is more often viewed as **“digital gold”/store of value**.
