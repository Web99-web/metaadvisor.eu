**What’s happening?**  
Naveen Rao, former Head of AI at **Databricks** and founder of **MosaicML** (acquired by Databricks in 2023 for **$1.3B**), has launched **Unconventional, Inc.** — an AI **hardware startup** aiming to raise up to **$1B** at a **$5B valuation**. Lead investor is **Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)**, with **Lightspeed** and **Lux Capital** also joining; **Databricks** is said to be involved too.

**Why it matters?**  
- **AI hardware race**: Soaring cost and latency of training/inference push the market toward new chips and architectures.  
- **Rao’s pedigree**: MosaicML experience plus investor ties give credibility and know-how.  
- **“Brain-Scale Efficiency”**: Rao envisions rethinking computers to match biology’s efficiency without its constraints.

**Funding model**  
The round is **tranched**: the company moves forward with hundreds of millions already secured, with the rest raised in installments. A common strategy in capital-intensive deep-tech.

**What might they build?**  
Still undisclosed, but hints suggest:  
- **Specialized accelerators** with superior performance/energy/cost balance.  
- **System architecture** (HW+SW) maximizing memory throughput and parallelism.  
- Possibly **scalable clusters** for “brain-scale” workloads.
