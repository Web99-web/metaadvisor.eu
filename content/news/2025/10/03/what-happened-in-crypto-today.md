**Quick overview:** Today’s highlights include **Samsung × Coinbase integration**, a Unity patch for **Android wallet security**, **altcoin ETF deadlines**, declining **hack losses in Q3**, and **stablecoins breaking $300B**.

### Samsung × Coinbase: 75M Galaxy users onboard Coinbase One
- **Coinbase One** launches with ~**75M Galaxy users in the US**, global rollout planned.
- Could onboard a **new wave of mainstream crypto users** via Samsung Wallet.

### Unity (Android) – Wallet vulnerability patched
- **Unity** patched a flaw that allowed **unauthorized code injection** in Android apps.  
- Critical for **mobile wallets** – devs urged to update immediately.

### Altcoin ETFs: October deadlines
- SEC’s **commodity ETP listing standards** pave way for **Solana, XRP and other altcoins**.
- October holds **key decisions** – potential fast-tracking of long-delayed approvals.

### Q3 Security: Losses down 37%
- Losses dropped from **$803M (Q2)** to **$509M (Q3)**.  
- Hackers shifting from **smart contract exploits** to **wallet/ops vulnerabilities**.

### Stablecoins cross $300B
- Market cap for stablecoins exceeds **$300B**, up ~47% YTD.  
- Growth driven by **USDT, USDC, USDe** and stronger **institutional demand**.

### FDIC & “reputation risk”
- US FDIC is considering redefining how **reputation risk** is assessed for banks serving crypto firms.  
- Could directly impact **crypto–bank relationships**.
