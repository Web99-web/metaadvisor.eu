**Summary**  
**Chainlink (LINK)** dipped slightly on Friday but held a **higher low**, closing the week up about **+6.7%**. Momentum is supported by **Plasma (XPL)** joining **Chainlink SCALE** and integrating **CCIP**, **Data Streams** and **Data Feeds**; meanwhile **UBS** launched a **pilot** that links **CCIP** with **SWIFT** messaging for tokenized fund operations.

**Plasma (XPL): stablecoin-first L1 with Chainlink stack**  
Plasma’s move brings **cross-chain interoperability (CCIP)**, **low-latency Data Streams**, and **reference Data Feeds** from day one — giving developers **enterprise-grade** infrastructure for **stablecoin payments and treasury** use cases with faster time-to-production and lower integration risk.

**Institutions: UBS + SWIFT + CCIP**  
Banks and funds are accelerating **tokenization** while plugging into existing rails. **UBS** is piloting **CCIP** alongside **SWIFT** messages for **tokenized fund** workflows — signaling that **on-chain** protocols can fit within **standardized messaging flows** and compliance routines used by institutions.

**Token economics: Chainlink Reserve buys LINK**  
The **Chainlink Reserve** purchased another **46,441 LINK** on Thursday, bringing holdings to **>417,000 LINK** (about **$9.5M**). Buybacks funded by integration/service revenues can act as **additional demand**, especially when oracle and interoperability services see rising usage.

**Technical picture**  
Model-based indicators point to **bullish momentum returning** with a **higher low**, but **resistance near $23** remains the zone to clear for confirmation of the next leg up.

### Our take (Metaadvisor):
- Adoption is coming from both ends: **new L1s (Plasma)** and **institutions (UBS/SWIFT)** lift demand for **CCIP/Oracle** services.
- **Reserve buybacks** tie **revenues** to **token demand**, though the current size is small relative to total supply.
- Core fundamental remains **real usage**: integration count, CCIP route volumes, and paid **Data Streams/Feeds**.
- Technically, a clean break **above $23** would likely turn the higher-low structure into a **clearer uptrend**.
