## 🔹 What happened?
Investors sued **Yuga Labs** (Bored Ape Yacht Club/BAYC), claiming the **NFTs were unregistered securities**.  
The **judge dismissed the case**, holding that the NFTs **do not satisfy the Howey test**.

## 🔹 Reminder: What is the Howey test?
The **Howey test** is a U.S. legal standard that determines whether something is an **“investment contract”** (and thus a security). It requires **all four** elements:

1. **Investment of money**  
2. **In a common enterprise**  
3. **With an expectation of profits**  
4. **Primarily from the efforts of others**

If **all four** are met, the product is a **security** and must be **registered with the SEC**.

## 🔹 Why did the court dismiss the case?
The court found insufficient evidence that BAYC buyers purchased primarily **as an investment** expecting profits **from Yuga Labs’ efforts**.  
The NFTs were treated more like **digital art/collectibles**, **not** securities.  
Thus, the **Howey test was not met**, and the suit was **dismissed**.

## 🔹 Why it matters
- **Signal for NFTs:** NFTs can be viewed as **collectibles**, not automatically as securities.  
- **Limits for the SEC:** Narrows securities oversight in the NFT space — still **case-by-case**.  
- **Not a blanket pass:** Projects promising **returns** or profit schemes may still trigger Howey.

> *Note: This ruling turns on the specific facts of the case; other NFT projects may be assessed differently.*
