**What happened?**  
U.S. Bitcoin miner **CleanSpark (CLSK)** reported **629 BTC** produced in September, **445 BTC** sold worth approximately **$48.7M**, and a **treasury of 13,011 BTC** as of Sept. 30. The company continues to expand capacity while improving fleet efficiency.

**Operational metrics (Sept 2025)**  
- **BTC produced:** 629  
- **BTC sold:** 445 (~$48.7M)  
- **BTC treasury (Sept 30):** 13,011  
- **Avg operational hashrate:** ~45.6 EH/s  
- **Peak fleet efficiency:** ~16.07 J/Th  

**Why it matters**  
A **partial-sell** approach (monetize a portion of output, accumulate the rest) helps stabilize **cash flow**, while a growing treasury enhances **strategic flexibility** (capex, acquisitions, financing). With BTC volatility, energy efficiency and disciplined sales are key to public miners’ competitiveness.

- Operationally strong: higher output and stable hashrate point to solid execution.
