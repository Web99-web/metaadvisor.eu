**TL;DR:**  
Over the last ~12 hours crypto bounced: **Bitcoin ~$115,000**, **Ethereum ~$4,164**. The move follows historic liquidations and geopolitical headlines. The key question: a **short-lived pop** on thin liquidity or a **bull-trend continuation** with real demand?

## What just happened?
- **Crash & liquidations (Oct 10–12)**: Billions in leveraged positions wiped; several majors printed their largest single-day drops in a while.  
- **Geopolitical trigger**: US tariff saber-rattling vs. China and Beijing’s **rare-earth export controls** boosted volatility.  
- **Quick rebound (Oct 12–13)**: Traders eye **$114k–$116k** as a potential **liquidity-grab** zone; early dip-buys and short-covering emerged.

## Where are BTC and ETH now?
- **BTC**: around **$115,000** (±) — back into the **$114k–$116k** pocket often associated with stop clusters/liquidity.  
- **ETH**: around **$4,164**; recent tech notes highlight rebound areas and relatively tight spot supply on exchanges.

## Bear case: short-lived pop
- **Post-liquidation bounce**: Big forced unwinds are often followed by **technical retracements** without trend confirmation.  
- **Macro risk**: Tariffs/rare-earth headlines can quickly cap buyers and flip sentiment.  
- **Derivatives**: A push to **$114k–$116k** can be mere **stop-fishing** before the next decision.

## Bull case: continuation
- **Real demand in range**: Signs of **genuine bids** as key ranges are reclaimed.  
- **ETH narrative**: Thin exchange supply + momentum **can** support further recovery if volume follows.  
- **Stats**: Sharp dumps frequently see **several days** of mean-reversion.

## What to watch next
1) **BTC reaction at $114k–$116k**: rejection with growing sell volume = likely **relief bounce**; break & hold with **volume** opens **$120k+** again.  
2) **ETH above ~$4.2k**: durability over $4.1k–$4.2k and rotation back into ETH/majors.  
3) **Derivatives & funding**: normalization of funding and extreme long/short tilts.  
4) **Macro headlines**: more noise around **tariffs** and **rare earths**.

---

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Neutral-cautious**: The bounce looks **convincing short-term**, but without volume/accumulation it stays **fragile**.  
- **BTC:** Watch **$114k–$116k** and the closes; no confirmation = no trend resumption.  
- **ETH:** **$4,164** is a decent check-back, but trend durability hinges on exchange supply and rotation into majors.  
- **Headline risk**: Geopolitics remains the **wild card**; any rally can be cut short quickly.
