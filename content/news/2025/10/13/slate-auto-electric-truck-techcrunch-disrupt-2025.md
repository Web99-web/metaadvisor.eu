**San Francisco, Oct 27–29, 2025.** **TechCrunch Disrupt 2025** will bring together **10,000+ founders, investors, operators and tech visionaries**. **Christine (Chris) Barman**, CEO of **Slate Auto**, **plans** to unveil the company’s new **electric truck** live on the main stage — **before** anyone sees it on the road.

### Live premiere (announcement)
The session will feature a **live reveal** plus an inside look at how Slate aims to rethink the **commercial EV market** — from **design** and **manufacturing** to **scale-up** in one of the most capital-intensive industries.

### What to expect
An **unfiltered conversation** about **hardware innovation**, **supply-chain complexity**, and **startup-scale production**, alongside the reveal of one of the year’s **buzziest EVs**.

### About the CEO
**Christine (Chris) Barman** leads Slate’s mission to build **affordable, personalizable vehicles**. Previously **Global VP** at **HCL Technologies** (transport solutions) and **CTO** of **Eaton’s Industrial Sector**, she spent **20+ years** at **Chrysler** across **engineering, systems and product development** (including **ADAS, electrical and autonomy**). She holds a **Mechanical Engineering** degree from **Purdue** and an **MBA** from the **University of Michigan**, and mentors **women engineers**.
