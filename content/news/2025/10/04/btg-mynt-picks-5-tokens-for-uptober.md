**Mynt**, the crypto platform of **BTG Pactual** (Brazil’s largest investment bank), highlighted five tokens it sees well-positioned for October (**“Uptober”**): **bitcoin (BTC)**, **ether (ETH)**, **solana (SOL)**, **avalanche (AVAX)** and **sky (SKY)**. The list comes from analysts **Matheus Parizotto** and **João Galhardo**, citing improving macro conditions (easing US rates) and rising expectations for new crypto ETFs.

> _Note: Image is symbolic._

### Why these five?

**BTC – portfolio anchor and ETF magnet.**  
Bitcoin remains the top institutional pick. Spot BTC ETFs reportedly took in **>$3.5B** in September, while **corporate treasuries** added around **43,000 BTC** (worth **>$5B** at current prices). Mynt frames BTC as a long-term **“pillar”** thanks to **liquidity**, **network security**, and an **adoption** path that extends beyond retail.

**ETH – backbone of on-chain finance and RWAs.**  
Ethereum continues to lead **on-chain finance**: most **stablecoin volume** and **real-world asset (RWA) tokenization** projects are concentrated on Ethereum. Over the last six months, spot ETFs added about **$11.3B in ETH**, and in September alone corporate treasuries reportedly bought **816,000 ETH** — pointing to sustained institutional demand.

**SOL – speed, cost and DeFi momentum.**  
**Solana** processed **> $100B in DEX volume** for the **third straight month**, while **TVL** topped **$30B** for the first time — signs of deepening **DeFi** liquidity. Potential **spot SOL ETFs** could further drive flows and visibility, on top of **high performance** and **low fees**.

**AVAX – modular stack and institutional use cases.**  
**Avalanche** keeps pushing **subnets** and applications for **institutions** and **gaming/RWA**. Mynt views AVAX as a bet on **modularity** and the expansion of institutional scenarios, with a relatively attractive **risk/reward** versus the broader L1 basket.

**SKY – higher-beta growth play.**  
**SKY** (ticker: **SKY**) is included as a higher-beta option for **high-performance** narratives. It’s a tactical add, but **more volatile** than BTC/ETH.

### The bigger picture
- **Macro:** fading US rate pressure and soft-landing hopes are supportive for risk assets.  
- **ETF wave:** spot ETFs create a **structural inflow channel** — regulated, transparent, with institutional custody.  
- **Liquidity/DeFi:** networks with clear narratives (speed, low fees, RWAs) are outpacing smaller L1s.
