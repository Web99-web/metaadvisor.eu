**Summary**  
In a conversation with Solana Labs (Oct 2), **Matt Hougan** of **Bitwise** argued that **stablecoins** and **tokenization** are moving from vision to **market reality**. Thanks to **latency**, **throughput** and fast **finality**, **Solana** is “extraordinarily attractive.” Over the same session, **SOL** traded in a tight **$228–$237** band — a classic **consolidation**.

## Why the “new Wall Street” label?
- **Tokenization & payments:** Regulators, central banks and asset managers increasingly see **digital assets** reshaping **payments** and **securities** markets.  
- **Technical edge:** Latency reportedly improved from ~**400 μs** to **~150 μs**, which resonates with trading venues where **execution & latency** matter.  
- **Finality & cost:** Fast finality with low fees makes the network a natural fit for **high-frequency** on-chain settlement and micro-payments.

## What it means for stablecoins & RWA
- **Stablecoin rails:** Programmable payments at scale require **low latency** and **cheap settlement**.  
- **RWA/tokenized securities:** Money-market funds, short-duration paper, corporate bonds — **fast, low-cost, programmable** issuance/transfers are a clear edge.  
- **Institutional flows:** As **custody** and **KYC/KYB** connect better to on-chain venues, narrative can turn into **measurable liquidity**.

## SOL technical picture (brief)
- **Range (23h, Oct 3 15:00–Oct 4 14:00 UTC):** **$228.19–$237.04**; high at **$237.04** (~16:00 Oct 3), with **$228–$229** acting as support.  
- **Read:** Tight range after a move implies **consolidation**; a push **above $237–$240** validates momentum, a **loss of $228** risks a lower range.  
*Note: Levels are indicative and vary by venue/timeframe.*

## Risks & caveats
- **Operational reliability:** Consistency under **peak load** (network backpressure, prioritization, MEV).  
- **Regulation:** Tokenized securities need **clear legal mappings** & disclosures.  
- **Centralization risks:** Infra concentration (RPC/validators) can raise **systemic exposure**.
