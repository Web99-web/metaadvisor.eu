**SKY** is the **governance token** of the **Sky** ecosystem, MakerDAO’s successor. Following the rebrand, legacy MKR was **redenominated** into SKY and DAI evolved into **USDS**. The result is a familiar core (collateral, RWA income, risk parameters) under a new identity and with a friendlier per-unit quote that often improves market psychology and liquidity.

Why the Uptober focus? It starts with **narrative and flows**. Expanding **USDS** circulation and rising **RWA** income can lift the ecosystem’s economics, which in turn raises demand to participate in **governance**. Redenomination makes entries easier for smaller tickets, and curated lists that highlight SKY amplify visibility during a seasonally favorable month.

Crucially, a governance token is **not equity**. It’s a **ballot** for protocol decisions—fees, collateral, limits, upgrades. Whether value accrues to the token depends on **tokenomics design** (e.g., buybacks, distribution mechanics) and on the **protocol’s operating performance**.

Material risks remain. **Governance concentration** can skew outcomes; the technical layer (multisig, timelock, scoped pause) must be **transparent and battle-tested**; and after redenomination it’s easy to overfocus on **unit price** instead of **market cap and cash flows**. Story can move price in the short run; over the long run **usage, revenues and risk discipline** decide.

### Our take (Metaadvisor):
- Rebrand + redenomination boost **visibility and access**, but **value capture** stays governance-driven.  
- Watch **USDS/RWA flows**, **risk-parameter changes**, and **on-chain revenue**.  
- Compare with old MKR via **market cap**, not nominal price.  
- Expect volatility; size entries to a defined **risk budget**.
