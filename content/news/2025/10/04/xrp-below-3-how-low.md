**Quick summary**  
XRP fell **below $3** again, raising questions about trend sustainability after a prolonged range. Below are the **key technical zones**, **fundamental catalysts** and **signals** to track—without making price calls.

## Technical picture (approximate)
- **Psychological $3.00**: now resistance; a reclaim would be constructive.  
- **Support zone $2.70–$2.50**: historic range base with liquidity; below that, **$2.20–$2.10** is the next area of interest.  
- **Resistance $3.20–$3.40**: sustained break/hold above could open upside; failure often means a return to sideways.  
*Note: Levels are indicative and differ by venue/timeframe.*

## Fundamental drivers
- **Regulatory track**: outcomes in **SEC vs. Ripple Labs** (rulings, settlements, licensing) shape institutional demand.  
- **XRPL functionality**: rollout of **AMM/DeFi** can lift on-chain usage (liquidity, fees, yield) and support the utility narrative.  
- **Escrow mechanics**: monthly **Ripple escrow** unlocks (traditionally up to 1B XRP) and redistribution affect **net supply**.  
- **Payments use cases**: cross-border corridors and throughput via Ripple partners remain central to utility.

## What to watch next
1) **Volume + holding**: breakouts need **acceptance above** new ranges with healthy spot volume.  
2) **On-chain**: AMM TVL, active accounts, XRPL transactions.  
3) **Regulatory calendar**: deadlines, rulings, settlements—each can quickly shift risk.  
4) **Escrow & treasury policy**: re-escrow vs. circulation.  
5) **Macro & correlations**: **BTC regime**, USD/rates backdrop.

### Our take (Metaadvisor):
- Near term, **defending $2.70–$2.50** is key; a breakdown raises odds of a **longer base-building phase**.  
- Longer term, **utility** must lead: XRPL **AMM/DeFi** and real **payment flows** matter more than single legal headlines.  
- **Escrow transparency** and net issuance remain sensitive for trust and supply perception.  
- For investors: **risk management** (scenarios, levels, sizing) beats trying to nail the exact bottom.
