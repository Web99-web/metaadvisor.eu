A major crypto **whale** has opened a **short position** on **Bitcoin** with a **nominal size of about $235 million**. Based on available market data, the short was initiated **around $111,190 per BTC** with approximately **10× leverage**. The move follows reports that the same actor **booked roughly $200 million** during the recent market sell-off, suggesting expectations of **further weakness** or at least a **retest** of lower levels.

## How and why this short can work
- **If price drops:** additional downside delivers **linear gains**; forced **long liquidations** can amplify moves.  
- **Sideways with a bearish tilt:** even without a big trend, **funding** and volatility can generate carry with disciplined risk control.  
- **As a hedge:** the whale may hold **spot/treasury BTC** while using the short as **protection**.

## Signals the market watches
- **Funding rates & open interest (OI):** rising OI without price progress = position build-up; funding reveals one-sided pressure.  
- **Basis (spot ↔ futures):** shifts into **backwardation** often reflect fear and hedging demand.  
- **Liquidation clusters:** dense zones around psychological levels where stops and margin stress accumulate.  
- **Exchange inflows:** higher BTC inflows to venues often precede sell pressure.

## Quick glossary
- **Short:** a bet on falling prices; profits on downmoves, losses on upmoves.  
- **Leverage:** borrowed capital magnifies exposure; at **~10×**, each price move has a **tenfold** P/L effect.  
- **Funding rate:** periodic payment between longs and shorts on perpetual futures; tethers futures to spot.  
- **Liquidation:** forced close when collateral no longer covers losses.
