### Key points from Citi’s note
- **$485 target** is based on a **12-month BTC forecast of $181,000**, plus **25–35% NAV premium**, consistent with MSTR’s historical **2.5×–3.5×** BTC yield multiple.  
- This setup makes MSTR an **amplified bet** on BTC: **outsized upside in bull runs**, but also **steep losses** if the market reverses.  
- **Bear-case scenario**: BTC −25%, NAV swings from +35% premium to −10% discount → MSTR could **lose ~61%**.  

### Background: Saylor’s Bitcoin treasury pivot
Citi credits the company’s **2020 pivot to Bitcoin**, led by **Michael Saylor**. Since then, Strategy has expanded BTC exposure via **convertible debt, preferred equity, and stock issuance**, depending on NAV premium levels.

- **BTC yield per share** (YTD BTC growth per fully diluted share) has been a **key driver** of the NAV premium.  
- Latest purchase: **168 BTC** at an average price of **$112,051**, taking total holdings to **640,418 BTC**.  

### Why MSTR moves more than BTC
- **Operating leverage on NAV**: rising NAV often commands a **premium** (discount in risk-off phases).  
- **Structured financing**: issuing debt/equity at higher premiums accelerates treasury growth.  
- **Narrative**: MSTR acts as a **proxy for BTC exposure** among investors lacking direct crypto mandates.  

### Risks
- **BTC volatility** (dual dependence: price + NAV premium).  
- **Dilution risk** if capital is raised during weak sentiment.  
- **Regulatory and accounting treatment** of digital assets.
