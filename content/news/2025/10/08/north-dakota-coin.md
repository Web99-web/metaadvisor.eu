The **Bank of North Dakota** announced it will partner with **Fiserv** to launch a USD-backed stablecoin called **Roughrider Coin** next year. The token targets financial institutions and aims to bridge traditional banking with blockchain rails.

According to the announcement, the coin will run on Fiserv’s **FIUSD platform**, built for regulated banking environments. Goals include boosting **bank-to-bank transfers**, speeding up global payments and encouraging merchant acceptance of digital dollars.

#### Bigger picture: Stablecoins as new global payment rails
Over the past year, the stablecoin market has grown to roughly **$293B** — up about 70%. The leaders are **USDT ($175B)** and **USDC ($75B)**, while new entrants continue to expand the landscape.

Cross-border payments are the standout use case. Instead of moving through **SWIFT**, USD stablecoins already enable cheaper, faster transfers — often in seconds rather than days. That creates the potential for a **new global dollar rail**.

North Dakota isn’t first: **Wyoming** launched its Frontier Stable Token earlier this year. Momentum has accelerated since the **GENIUS Act** in July set a clearer federal framework for stablecoin issuance and bank adoption.
