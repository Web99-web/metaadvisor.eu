**Square**, the payments division of **Block (SQ)**, unveiled **Square Bitcoin** — a toolset that lets U.S. small businesses **accept BTC**, **auto-convert** a share of card sales into Bitcoin, and manage **wallet + fiat** in one dashboard.

#### Key features
- **Bitcoin payments** directly through Square terminals.  
- **Auto conversion:** up to **50%** of daily card sales into BTC.  
- **Built-in wallet** inside the Square Seller app.  
- **No processing fees** for BTC acceptance in year one (promo).

The rollout for eligible sellers begins **Nov 10**. Square says pilots have already accumulated **142 BTC** via the conversion feature.

#### Bigger picture
- 📈 **U.S. crypto payments projected +82% (2024–2026)** per cited data.  
- 🛍️ Lower technical barriers bring **Bitcoin to Main Street**.  
- 🧩 Native integration with POS, inventory and payroll reduces **friction**.
