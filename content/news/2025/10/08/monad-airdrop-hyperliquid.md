**Hyperliquid**, a decentralized derivatives exchange, has listed **MON-USD perpetuals**, enabling long/short exposure to **MON**, the native token of the **Monad** blockchain — **ahead of the expected airdrop**.

**Monad** is an **EVM-compatible Layer-1**, easing migration of existing Ethereum dApps. Official channels have been teasing an **“airdrop claim loading”** that reached **~98%** on Oct. 8, suggesting distribution is imminent.

#### Numbers & market dynamics
- **Indicative perps price:** around **$0.13** → **FDV ≈ $13B** (assuming **100B MON**).  
- **Volume:** about **$28M** over the last 24h on Hyperliquid.  
- **Purpose:** pre-TGE listings enable **price discovery** and **hedging** for early participants.

#### Why EVM compatibility matters
EVM L1s accelerate **go-to-market** for DeFi/NFT/infra: contract porting, familiar dev tooling, wallet/oracle integrations. If Monad delivers on performance/finality/costs, its **dApp ecosystem** can expand faster.

#### Listing before the airdrop – implications
- **Early price discovery** with expectations set **before** TGE.  
- **Allocation hedging** via short exposure.  
- **Volatility risk** as funding/liquidations swing without a mature spot float.
