OpenAI’s head of ChatGPT **Nick Turley** outlined a future where **ChatGPT evolves into a platform of third-party apps** — effectively an “operating system” that doesn’t replace Windows or macOS but becomes **the primary workspace** through which users launch AI tools.

Turley **draws a parallel with web browsers**: over the last decade, browsers have become the de facto operating layer by aggregating web apps. By the same logic, ChatGPT could become an **application layer** that orchestrates tools, files and workflows via a **conversational interface**.

> **Product scale:** Since 2022 Turley has focused on commercialization and growth; reports mention **800M+ weekly active users**. The next step is an *apps-first* strategy.

#### Why now?
- **App supply:** OpenAI already experimented with **plugins** and the **GPT Store (2023)**. The new approach aims for **deeper integration** and easier discovery of apps directly inside ChatGPT.  
- **Hardware & browser:** There’s public chatter about an **OpenAI browser**; in parallel, OpenAI is collaborating with **Jony Ive** and veteran Apple designers on **hardware concepts** — hinting at a **broader consumer ecosystem** with a “ChatGPT OS” at the center.  
- **Developer angle:** Positioned around the **third OpenAI developer conference** in San Francisco (Fort Mason), signaling **openness to developers** and new business models.

#### What it means for users & developers
- **Users:** less app-hopping; **AI as orchestrator** (summarize, generate, execute tasks, file search, automations).  
- **Developers:** a potential **in-ChatGPT app marketplace** (distribution, monetization, analytics, identity, sandbox security).  
- **Business model:** subscriptions + **app revenue-share**, enterprise licensing and integrations.  
- **Privacy/security:** crucial questions around **data control**, granular permissions, audits and transparency of model calls.

#### Risks & open questions
- **Ecosystem lock-in:** does an “AI OS” centralize too much?  
- **Standards & interop:** will ChatGPT apps be **portable** across models/platforms?  
- **Regulatory focus:** **privacy, IP, bias**, plus **antitrust** if platform dominance emerges.  
- **Reliability & UX:** how to design **predictable** and **verifiable** AI workflows for mission-critical use?
