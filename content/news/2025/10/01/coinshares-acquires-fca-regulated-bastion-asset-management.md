**CoinShares**, a leading European digital asset manager (approx. $10B AUM), announced the acquisition of **Bastion Asset Management**, an FCA-regulated London-based firm. Financial terms were not disclosed, and the deal remains subject to regulatory approval.

Bastion specializes in **quantitative and market-neutral strategies** for **institutional clients**. Under the agreement, **CEO Philip Scott** and **CIO Fred Desobry** will join CoinShares. The acquisition adds **active management capabilities** to CoinShares’ well-known **ETP products**, creating a more diversified investor offering.

For pension funds and institutions, this means the ability to combine **Bitcoin ETP exposure** with **low-volatility, market-neutral funds**, aiming for steadier returns in the volatile crypto space.

CoinShares already holds an **Investment Advisor license in the U.S.** and plans to launch **actively managed institutional funds**. The Bastion acquisition strengthens its capabilities and accelerates the path to the U.S. market.
