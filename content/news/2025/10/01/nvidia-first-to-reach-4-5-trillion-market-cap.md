**TL;DR:** **Nvidia** has reached a **$4.5T market cap**, a **historic first** among publicly listed companies. Milestones on the way: $1T (2023), $2T (2024), $3T (2024), $4T (07/2025).

### How we got here
- **AI supercycle:** surging demand for **GPU** compute for training & inference.  
- **Hyperscaler capex wave:** multi-GW data center projects and contracts create revenue visibility.  
- **Software moat:** CUDA, networking and platform stack (HGX/NVLink) reinforce leadership.  
- **Index flows:** a higher index weight draws additional passive inflows.

### Why it matters (and the crypto angle)
- **AI infra = core theme:** hardware, energy and networks become the market’s fast lane.  
- **DePIN/crypto spillover:** GPU scarcity invites **decentralized supply models**; the narrative pulls attention and capital into AI×crypto projects.  
- **Competitive response:** peers and foundries must accelerate roadmaps to reduce single-vendor dependence.

### Risks
- **Valuation & capex cyclicality:** overheating risk if macro weakens.  
- **Supply chains & energy:** chip production and power availability constraints.  
- **Customer concentration:** reliance on a handful of mega-buyers.
