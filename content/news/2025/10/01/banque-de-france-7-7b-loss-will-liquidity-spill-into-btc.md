**Context:** France’s central bank disclosed a **~€7.7B net loss in 2024** amid higher rates and negative net interest income. At **TOKEN2049**, **Arthur Hayes** (BitMEX) said these deficits **increase the odds of fresh liquidity** from the **ECB** (e.g., **QE/bond buys**), which has historically aligned with **risk-on** flows into **BTC**.

### Why it matters
- **Balance-sheet & fiscal pressure** often push policymakers toward **cheaper funding** and **bond purchases**.  
- **Excess liquidity** tends to chase **risk assets** — tech and **crypto** included.  
- **Euro area angle:** If the **ECB** re-expands support, **EUR/USD liquidity** could lift broader **risk appetite**.

- **Signal, not a promise:** Central-bank losses **don’t guarantee** QE, but **raise the probability** in stress.  
- **Transmission:** **Bond buying** → lower yields → **hunt for yield** across risk assets (incl. **BTC**).
