**BNB Chain** confirmed its official **X account** was compromised, with attackers posting **phishing links** to lure users to fraudulent crypto pages.

**Changpeng Zhao (CZ)** cautioned via his personal X account **not to follow links** shared from the compromised profile, adding that the team is working to regain full control.

Such attacks on **official social media channels** of major crypto projects are recurring, often exploiting weaknesses in **2FA setups, key management and access control**. Users should avoid suspicious posts and perform sensitive actions only via **bookmarked, verified domains**.
