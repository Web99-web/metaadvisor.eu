**SINGAPORE —** By the next **Token2049**, today’s **perp DEX** leaders (**Hyperliquid**, **Aster**, **Lighter**) may no longer dominate, says **Stephan Lutz** (CEO, **BitMEX**). Incentive-heavy designs are **fragile** and hard to sustain.

- The niche is in a **volume arms race**: Aster recently topped Hyperliquid on 24-hour volume; more DEX launches are chasing share.  
- Lutz likens incentives to an **ad blitz** that “buys attention” (token rewards, fee kickbacks). **Key question:** **what sticks** when the campaign ends?  
- This **boom-and-bust cadence** makes **sticky liquidity** difficult and raises **risk/volatility** for retail chasing yields.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Incentive economics:** If most activity is subsidized, post-campaign we often see **liquidity outflows**. Durable value needs **organic demand**, **deeper books** and **MM agreements** not tied to emissions.  
- **Hybrids likely win:** On-chain execution with **off-chain** risk/clearing or access to **CEX liquidity** may outperform “incentives-only.”  
- **Metrics to watch:** share of non-incentivized volume, **retention** after airdrops, net TVL post-campaigns, robustness of **oracle/LP** design.  
- **Trader playbook:** monitor **funding/basis**, avoid one-sided OI, **scale** entries and keep **tight risk limits**.
