**TL;DR:** In hubs like **Nairobi** and **Lagos**, **stablecoins (USDT/USDC)** have become **everyday tools**: savings, bill payments, **cheaper remittances** and cross-border commerce. **Mobile money** (e.g., *M-Pesa*) and **P2P on/off-ramps** make them familiar and practical. *Image is symbolic.*

## Why stablecoins (not only BTC)?
- **Inflation & FX shocks:** dollar-stable tokens act as a **value anchor**.  
- **Remittances & trade:** **faster/cheaper** than legacy rails.  
- **Mobile-money bridge:** familiar UX without a bank account.  
- **P2P markets:** local dealers ensure **liquidity & pricing** seven days a week.

## How it’s used
- **Freelancers/SMBs** invoice in **USDC/USDT**, then convert to local currency for expenses.  
- **Micro-savings** between paychecks.  
- **Import/export:** stablecoins as a **settlement unit**.  
- **Family support:** send stablecoins + instant local cash-out.

## What about Bitcoin?
- **BTC** increasingly for **long-term savings/hedging**; for **daily spend**, stablecoins dominate due to **nominal stability**.

## Risks & caveats
- **Regulatory uncertainty** (P2P restrictions, KYC).  
- **Reserve quality** of issuers.  
- **Fraud/scams** with on-ramps.  
- **Spreads** on local P2P markets, especially under stress.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Global South as an innovator:** where problems are **more acute** (inflation, capital controls), **adoption** moves faster — bottom-up.  
- **Stablecoins = the on-ramp to the dollar; BTC = reserve/hedge.**  
- **Watch:** on/off-ramp networks (P2P, agents), **spreads**, regulation; products with **mobile-money-like UX** win.  
- **Investment angle:** Rising stablecoin usage boosts demand for **U.S. short-term paper** and increases **liquidity/infra** needs (L2/DePIN).
