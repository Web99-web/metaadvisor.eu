**Overview.** LINK slid about **9%** into the **$16–17** range during broader crypto weakness. Even so, **Caliber** disclosed another **$2M** in LINK, while the **Chainlink Reserve** also added tokens. On the product side, Chainlink rolled out **Data Streams on MegaETH**, aimed at ultra-low latency for real-time DeFi.

### What moved today
- **Price:** a sharp ~9% drop intraday, with a brief late bounce that didn’t change the picture.
- **Dip buyers:** **Caliber** added **$2M** more; the **Chainlink Reserve** increased holdings too, though its average entry remains above spot.
- **Product update:** **Data Streams** are now live on **MegaETH**, a chain tuned for **sub-second latency** — useful for perps, stablecoins and other speed-sensitive DeFi apps.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Jittery tape.** Even when big buyers step in, price can keep sliding short-term if panic dominates.
- **Steady buyers help — no magic wand.** Caliber and the reserve can cushion dips, but sentiment rarely flips overnight.
- **Tech with delayed impact.** Data Streams are a plus for fast DeFi; if integrations follow, fundamentals improve — price impact often lags.
- **Watch next:** pace of new buys (Caliber, reserve), real Data Streams integrations, and signs of calmer market liquidity.
