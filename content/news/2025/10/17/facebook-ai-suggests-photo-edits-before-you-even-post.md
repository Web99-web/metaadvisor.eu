## What’s happening
Facebook is introducing a feature that allows Meta AI to analyze photos stored on your phone — even before they’re posted. Based on that, the app suggests collages, recaps, themed edits or birthday highlights.

To work, the app requests **opt-in permission** for “cloud processing.” If users agree, the photos are uploaded to Meta’s cloud, where AI generates creative variations.

## Rules & privacy
Meta says:  
- Photos are **not** used for ad targeting.  
- AI won’t be trained on all your photos unless you **edit or share** AI-generated content.  
- Accepting AI terms allows Meta to **analyze content and faces** to create suggestions.

## Benefits
- Quick collages & recaps without manual editing  
- Easy sharing to Feed & Stories  
- Consistent aesthetic templates

## Risks
- Deeper cloud analysis of private photos  
- Wider profile of your habits, relationships and routines  
- Once turned on, the feature often stays active
