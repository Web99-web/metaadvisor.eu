**Avalanche Treasury Co. (AVAT)** announced a **$675M+** business combination with **Mountain Lake Acquisition Corp. (MLAC)**, laying the groundwork for a **$1B ecosystem treasury**. An **exclusive arrangement** with the **Avalanche Foundation** includes a **$200M discounted token sale**. A **Nasdaq listing** is targeted for **early 2026**, pending approvals.

At launch, AVAT holds approximately **$460M** in treasury assets (private placements) and offers investors a **~23% discount** via **0.77× mNAV** versus direct token purchases or ETP/ETF alternatives. **MLAC** shares are **+2% premarket**, while **AVAX** trades **flat** over 24h.

**Product thesis:** CEO **Bart Smith** positions the vehicle as **more than passive exposure** — an **active ecosystem partner** investing in **protocol adoption**, **on-chain enterprise activity**, and **institutional blockchain** rollouts. Advisors include **Emin Gün Sirer** (Ava Labs); **John Nahas** (CBO) joins the **board**. The advisory board features **Haseeb Qureshi (Dragonfly)**, **Jason Yanowitz (Blockworks)**, and **Stani Kulechov (Aave)**. **FalconX** will handle execution/credit, while **Monarq** manages assets.

> **Why this model?**  
> - **Discounted entry (0.77× mNAV):** attractive to institutions wary of direct token/ETP exposure.  
> - **Active ecosystem build:** capital allocated to **projects and adoption** across Avalanche.  
> - **Clear capital path:** SPAC structure + privates + potential secondary sources.

### Bigger picture & risks
- **Timing:** rising demand for **ecosystem treasuries** as networks build an **institutional layer** (RWA, enterprise).  
- **Token supply dynamics:** redistributing treasury tokens can lift **TVL/activity** but may add **sell pressure** without disciplined vesting.  
- **SPAC specifics:** **redemptions**, regulatory clearance, and **post-merger liquidity**; the discount is compelling, yet **NAV transparency** and cost control are key.

### Metrics to watch
- **Treasury AUM:** path toward **$1B** (deployment run-rate).  
- **mNAV vs. market:** durability of the **0.77×** relationship.  
- **Ecosystem KPIs:** Avalanche **TVL**, active dApps, **enterprise pipelines**, developer activity.  
- **Market performance:** AVAT’s spread versus AVAX/ETP benchmarks.
