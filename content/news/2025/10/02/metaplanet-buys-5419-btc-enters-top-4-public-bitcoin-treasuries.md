**Metaplanet** disclosed the acquisition of **5,419 BTC** worth roughly **$632.5 million**, reaffirming its **bitcoin-first treasury** strategy. The transaction was financed through a mix of **equity and debt**, continuing the firm’s shift from hospitality into a **corporate bitcoin treasury** profile.

### Why it matters
- **Corporate demand:** Buying at >$110K per BTC signals **long-term conviction** and growing **institutional appetite**.  
- **Signal to Japan’s capital markets:** Few issuers outside the U.S. are scaling a BTC treasury this aggressively — Metaplanet becomes a **regional benchmark**.  
- **Liquidity & structure:** Expansion relies on **market financing** (equity/debt). Key to monitor are **custody arrangements, collateral**, and **audit transparency**.

### Where Metaplanet stands now
- **Newly acquired:** **5,419 BTC** (≈ **$632.5M**).  
- **Total position:** After the purchase, Metaplanet ranks **among the top 4** public companies by BTC holdings.  
- **Strategy:** **Long-term hold (HODL)** with potential for further acquisitions via recurring capital raises.
