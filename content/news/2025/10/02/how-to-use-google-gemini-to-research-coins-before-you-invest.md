**Gemini** can speed up **crypto due diligence** — provided you demand **verifiable sources**, keep a **critical filter**, and **cross-check** its outputs with **primary data** (CoinGecko, DeFiLlama, Token Terminal, GitHub, etc.). Below is a **step-by-step** workflow with ready-to-use **prompts**.

> **Note:** *Image is symbolic.*

## 1) Fast project snapshot
**Prompt:**  
> “Summarize **{COIN/PROJECT}** in 6 sentences: problem solved, target market, key module/protocol, team & funding, network/layer, product status (mainnet/testnet). Add 3 top sources (whitepaper, docs, GitHub).”

**What to check:** live product vs. roadmap; team links (LinkedIn/GitHub); **references over marketing**.

## 2) Tokenomics & distribution
**Prompt:**  
> “Build a tokenomics table for **{TOKEN}**: total/max/circulating supply, unlock schedule (months), allocations (team/investors/treasury/ecosystem), current & projected inflation. Include sources.”

**Checks:** major **unlocks** in 3–12 months; team/investor share (≥30–40% = risk); real **utility** (fees/staking/governance/collateral).

## 3) Liquidity & market profile
**Prompt:**  
> “List top exchanges/pairs for **{TOKEN}**, estimate true liquidity (order-book depth 2%/5%), top-holder concentration, and daily on-chain flow. Sources: CoinGecko/CMC, DEX screener, chain explorer.”

**Checklist:** concentration (top10 >60% supply?); **market-making** without depth; bridge/wrapping risks.

## 4) On-chain health & adoption
**Prompt:**  
> “Give 6-month trends: active users, tx count, TVL (if DeFi), protocol revenue, fee burn/issuance for **{PROJECT/TOKEN}**. Include charts & links (DeFiLlama, Token Terminal, Dune).”

**Interpretation:** sustained **growth** vs. hype; **cash flow** (fees/spreads/MEV); reliance on **incentives**.

## 5) Technical risk (code/security)
**Prompt:**  
> “Assess security for **{PROJECT}**: audits & dates, critical vulnerabilities, open-source status, commit frequency, active contributors, open issues. Sources: GitHub, audit reports, bug bounties.”

**Red flags:** no audit / >12 months old; **admin keys** without multisig; low recent commit activity.

## 6) Regulatory posture & access
**Prompt:**  
> “Summarize regulatory/legal risks for **{TOKEN}** across US/EU/UK/HK: security vs. commodity status, KYC/AML, listing/custody limits, notable legal events.”

**Note:** For EU retail, verify **MiCA** obligations (whitepaper, marketing, stablecoins).

## 7) Portfolio decision matrix
**Prompt:**  
> “Score **{TOKEN}** (0–5) on team, product, tokenomics, liquidity, on-chain adoption, revenue, security, regulatory risk. Propose weights, compute a score, and write bull/bear theses and invalidation.”

**Use it to:** cap **position size**, prefer **DCA** over all-in, and place **stops** at liquidity-supported levels.

## 8) Ready-to-use prompts
- “Create a **one-pager** for {TOKEN} with links to **whitepaper/docs/GitHub**.”  
- “Extract the **vesting schedule** (dates/amounts); estimate **sell pressure** at current liquidity.”  
- “Compare {TOKEN} vs **{PEER1}**/**{PEER2}**: TVL, revenue, active users, **FDV/TVL**, **P/S**; derive **relative valuation**.”  
- “List **top 3 risks** and **3 catalysts** over the next 90 days (mainnet, airdrop, listing, unlock).”

## Common red flags
- High **FDV** with low float (unlock tsunami)  
- **Ponzi-like** yields without real cash flow  
- (Pseudo)anonymous team with no track record  
- Dependence on a **single** bridge/oracle  
- **Sponsored reviews** without independent sources

> **Reminder:** Like any LLM, Gemini can **hallucinate**. Always **open sources** and verify numbers in **primary tools** (CoinGecko/CMC, DeFiLlama, Token Terminal, Dune, explorers).

### Metaadvisor’s Take

- **LLM as copilot, not oracle**: value comes from **data cross-checks** and **disciplined risk management**.  
- **Process = edge**: standardized **prompts + checklists** filter noise and avoid costly mistakes (unlocks, fake liquidity).  
- **Institutional trend**: tokenized data & on-chain reporting are becoming standard; **LLM + data sources** deliver both speed and accuracy.  
- **Limits:** without validating **true volume** (order-book depth) and **sources**, even the best summary is just a story.
