**AlloyX**, a Hong Kong–based stablecoin infrastructure firm, unveiled **RYT**, a **tokenized money market fund (MMF)** debuting **on Polygon** with **Standard Chartered** providing **custody**. Unlike typical tokenized MMFs that offer passive exposure, RYT **integrates DeFi** to pursue **yield-enhancing strategies** within a **compliance-first** framework suitable for auditors and regulators.

> **What makes RYT different?**  
> - **DeFi integration**: controlled looping and strategy rails for **incremental yield**.  
> - **T+1 settlement**: trades settle **one business day** after the trade date.  
> - **On-chain data**: selected fund metrics **disclosed on-chain** for transparency.  
> - **Bank-grade custody**: **Standard Chartered** handles safekeeping with KYC/AML and audit trails.

The **stablecoin ecosystem** has expanded to about **$280B** in market cap, with **USDT** and **USDC** commanding **>80%** share. Tokenized MMFs aim to deliver a **cash-management layer** for crypto — particularly for **institutions** and qualified investors.

**Corporate context:** In **September**, AlloyX was acquired by **Solowin Holdings (SWIN)** for **$350M** to accelerate expansion across **emerging markets** and scale products like RYT.

**Polygon’s role:** Polygon Labs supports **technical integration** and **ecosystem growth**. RYT will be **exclusive to Polygon** during the initial rollout, with plans to **expand to other networks**.

> “With **Polygon-based looping** to amplify utility and **bank-grade tokenized MMF rails** providing the cash component, **RYT** aims to bridge **DeFi liquidity** with a **transparent, audited cash-management layer** — while keeping issuance, custody and reconciliation **within a regulated framework**,” said **Dr. Thomas Zhu**, Co-Founder & CEO of **AlloyX Group**.

---

### What this means for investors
- **Liquidity & UX**: T+1 settlement + token issuance/redemption via familiar EVM tooling = **less friction** in cash management.  
- **Regulatory fit**: a **compliance-first** design helps **auditors/regulators** and may shorten **institutional onboarding**.  
- **DeFi “plus” yield**: integrations are **rule-bound**; emphasis on **controlled risk**.

### Metaadvisor’s Take

- **Tokenized MMF 2.0**: RYT goes beyond passive wrappers — **DeFi integration** + **bank custody** make it relevant for **institutional cash management**.  
- **Key risks**: operational (protocol dependencies), **regulatory** (passporting, marketing rules), **liquidity** (wider spreads in stress).  
- **Metrics to watch**: AUM, **tracking error** vs. target yield, **fee stack**, protocol exposures and **T+1 discipline**.  
- **Competitiveness**: Polygon exclusivity can drive traction; **multi-chain institutions** will expect **swift expansion**.
