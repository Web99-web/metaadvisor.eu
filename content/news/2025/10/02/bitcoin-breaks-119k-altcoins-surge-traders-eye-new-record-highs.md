Crypto markets roared on Thursday as **Bitcoin** broke above **$119,000**, setting its sights on fresh record highs. **Altcoins** joined the rally with double-digit gains.

- **XMR**: $328.63  
- **APT**: $5.06  
- **ZEC**: $144.56 (+58% in 24h)

Drivers included **ETF inflows**, the rally in **gold**, and broad risk-on sentiment.

### Record volumes
Combined **spot and derivatives trading** on centralized exchanges rose 7.58% in August to **$9.72 trillion**, the highest monthly total of 2025.

### Forecasts
According to **JPMorgan**, Bitcoin could climb toward **$165,000**, drawing parallels with gold’s historic run.
