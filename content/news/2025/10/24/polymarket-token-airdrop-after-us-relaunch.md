**What is Polymarket?**

Polymarket is a **prediction market** where users trade “Yes/No” shares on real-world events – from elections and economic data to sports and tech. The share price reflects **probability** (e.g., 0.62 = 62%). If the outcome occurs, each share pays out $1.

## Who is behind it?

The project was founded by **Shayne Coplan** from New York. Legally, operations are routed through offshore entities (e.g., Cayman Islands) to reduce regulatory risk. Backed by major VCs and crypto investors, Polymarket quickly became a reference point in the prediction market space.

> Note: Polymarket previously paid a CFTC fine for running unregistered markets in the U.S. The upcoming relaunch aims to be **more regulator-friendly**.

## Why relaunch in the U.S. now?

- **Regulation**: U.S. rules on prediction markets are becoming clearer.  
- **Demand**: election cycles and macro uncertainty fuel user interest.  
- **Native token**: to align incentives, governance, and ecosystem growth.  
- **Airdrop**: rewards early and active users, boosts liquidity.

## Business model

Polymarket makes money from **fees on profits** (around 2%). Unlike bookmakers, the platform does not set odds – **supply and demand** does.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)

Polymarket represents an attempt to apply **market logic to information and probabilities**. Its U.S. relaunch could legitimize the entire sector but will also bring tighter regulatory scrutiny.  
Whether the new token is designed transparently (clear airdrop criteria, unlock schedule, governance role) will shape trust and adoption. Prediction markets should be seen as a **high-risk tool** – more a signal aggregator than a guarantee of truth.
