🚨 **Trump pardons CZ**

President Donald Trump has officially **pardoned Changpeng Zhao (CZ)**, founder of Binance. This removes the federal criminal penalties tied to his case.

## Why was CZ charged?

- **Background (2023)**: CZ pleaded guilty to violating the **Bank Secrecy Act (BSA)** – Binance failed to implement effective **AML controls**.  
- **Settlement**: Binance paid billions in fines; CZ received a sentence and restrictions.  
- **Focus**: It was about compliance and AML shortcomings, not fraud against users.  

## What does the pardon mean?

- **Legal impact**: Criminal penalties are lifted, but the record of the case remains.  
- **Business impact**: Easier path for CZ to return to leadership or operate in the U.S.  
- **Market sentiment**: Short-term positive; long-term, regulation still dominates.  

---

### Our take (Metaadvisor)

The pardon closes a chapter, but it doesn’t rewrite the rules. The crypto industry will continue to face **strict compliance requirements** – transparency and AML/KYC remain critical for adoption.
