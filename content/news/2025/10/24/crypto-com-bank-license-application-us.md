🚨 **Crypto.com files for U.S. bank license**

Crypto exchange **Crypto.com** has formally applied for a **U.S. Trust Bank Charter**. If approved, it would allow the platform to operate as a **federally regulated bank** – a milestone for the crypto industry.

## Why does this matter?

- **Mainstream access** – ability to offer deposit accounts and custody services under federal oversight.  
- **Investor trust** – a bank license signals stability and compliance, key for institutional adoption.  
- **New precedent** – success could push other exchanges like Coinbase or Gemini to follow suit.
