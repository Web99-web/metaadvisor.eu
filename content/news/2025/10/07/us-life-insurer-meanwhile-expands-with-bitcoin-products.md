## What happened?
**Meanwhile**, a US **life insurer** focused on **Bitcoin-denominated** policies and financial products, has raised **$82 million** to expand its offering — effectively bridging a traditional insurance line with crypto infrastructure.

## Why it matters
- **Institutional signal:** Even life insurance is adopting Bitcoin as a long-term asset.  
- **BTC as unit of account:** Policies/benefits are **denominated in BTC** rather than USD.  
- **Investor demand:** The funding round indicates **market appetite** for Bitcoin-native products.

- **Positive:** Bitcoin is moving beyond exchanges/ETFs into **real-world financial sectors** like insurance.  
- **Trend:** Strengthens the “Bitcoin standard” narrative across traditional products.
