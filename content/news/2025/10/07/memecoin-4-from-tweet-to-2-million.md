**What happened (tl;dr).**  
A trader bought the new memecoin **$4** on BNB Chain very early. After a mention on X (CZ), demand surged and the **$3,000** position ballooned to about **$2,000,000** within hours — a classic hype spike enabled by timing.

---

### On-chain view
- Early buyers within minutes; **shallow liquidity** at the start.  
- Stepwise rally as FOMO buyers piled in; spreads widened at times.  
- Then **heavy profit-taking** → sharp pullback.  
> Takeaway: move was timing/flow-driven — **not** a durable trend.

### Where to trade (now)

### Why did $4 take off?
- **Minimalist brand:** just the digit *4* — meme-friendly and sticky.  
- **High-reach signal:** mention by a widely followed account.  
- **Low initial float:** scarce supply + FOMO demand ⇒ outsized jump.

### Risks (watchouts)
- **Extreme volatility** and **slippage** on entries/exits.  
- **Liquidity can dry up** once early wallets realize profits.  
- **No utility or track record:** price is almost pure sentiment.
