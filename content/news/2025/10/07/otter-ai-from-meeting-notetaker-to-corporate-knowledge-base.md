**Overview.** Otter.ai doesn’t just want to “take notes” anymore. The new enterprise suite aims to convert meeting data — transcripts, notes, slides and action items — into a **searchable, connected and shareable** **knowledge layer** that plugs into the tools teams already use.

**What’s new**
- **API** for custom integrations (e.g., **Jira**, **HubSpot**) and automatic push of tasks/CRM updates derived from meeting summaries.  
- **MCP server** as a secure bridge linking Otter data to external AI models and agents.  
- **AI agent** that searches **notes, transcripts and presentations** in context and returns answers with citations/timestamps.

**Market context**
- Since 2016 the space has exploded (Granola, Circleback, Fireflies; features inside Zoom/Google/Microsoft).  
- The ambition shift matters: from a **meeting tool** to the **source of truth for conversations** — where decisions and tasks originate.

**Why teams care**
- **Faster knowledge retrieval:** one place to search sales calls, product decisions and marketing discussions.  
- **Less manual copy-paste:** action items flow into project/CRM tools; ownership and status are visible.  
- **Measurable impact:** shorter onboarding, less duplication, tighter cross-functional alignment.

**Security & compliance (EU-aware)**
- Critical: **granular access controls**, **audit trails**, and clear **DPAs/SCCs** for cross-border processing.  
- Reduce **GDPR risk** via data-minimization and role-based access.

**Our take (Metaadvisor):**
- **Moving up the value stack:** “meeting scribe” is a feature; a **conversation knowledge base** is a platform with higher budget and stickiness.  
- **Integrations as moat:** If API/MCP + agent reliably create tasks with citations and timestamps, Otter gains leverage over the suites.  
- **Risks:** privacy, summarization accuracy and certifications (SOC 2/ISO). Enterprises will demand strict boundaries between SaaS and models.  
- **Competition:** Microsoft/Google/Zoom/Notion chase the same “source of truth.” Differentiation hinges on **semantic search quality** and **connector depth**.  
- **Metrics to watch:** share of enterprise workspaces using APIs/integrations, agent latency & accuracy, adoption in sales/support.
