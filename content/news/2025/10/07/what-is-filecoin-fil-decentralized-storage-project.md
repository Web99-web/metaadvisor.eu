## Quick explainer
**Filecoin (FIL)** is a **decentralized storage network** created by **Protocol Labs** (the team behind IPFS). Its goal is to provide an **alternative to centralized cloud providers** like Amazon AWS or Google Cloud – but built on an open, peer-to-peer model.

---

## When did it start?
- Announced in 2017 (one of the largest ICOs that year).  
- **Mainnet launched in October 2020.**  
- Since then, the project has been building its **storage market, core protocol and Web3 integrations.**

---

## How does it work?
- Users can **store files on the network** via independent storage providers.  
- Providers lock up **FIL tokens as collateral** and cryptographically prove they are storing the data (**Proof-of-Replication** & **Proof-of-Spacetime**).  
- In return, they receive **FIL rewards** and client payments.  
- FIL token utility:  
  - 💰 pay for storage and retrieval,  
  - 🔒 collateral for storage providers,  
  - ⚡ network fees and incentives.

## Popularity & competition
- Briefly in the **Top 10 by market cap** in 2021.  
- Today less hyped but still relevant in its niche.  
- Competitors:  
  - **Arweave (AR)** – permanent storage (“permaweb”).  
  - **Storj (STORJ)**, **Sia (SC)** – other decentralized storage networks.  
- Why you might not hear about it: less flashy than memecoins or AI tokens, more **infrastructure-focused**.

---

## Why it matters
- Enables cheaper, more resilient storage for large datasets (research, open data, NFT metadata).  
- Provides a **real use case** unlike purely speculative tokens.  
- Success depends on:  
  - ✅ real demand for decentralized storage,  
  - ✅ active clients and data deals,  
  - ✅ competition with centralized cloud providers.
