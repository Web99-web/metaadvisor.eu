**Overview.**  
CleanCore Solutions (**ZONE**) reports a **DOGE treasury exceeding 710M tokens**, with more than **$20M** in unrealized gains. The company aims to **reach 1B DOGE**; the initiative is supported by the **Dogecoin Foundation** and coordinated via **House of Doge**.

---

## What was announced
- Purchases **since Sept 5** in phases, funded by a **$175M private placement**.  
- CEO **Clayton Adams** says the treasury strategy aligns with the long-term vision from insiders/House of Doge — **utility expansion → broader adoption**.  
- On the day of the release, **ZONE shares slightly lower**, while **DOGE -5.8%** intraday (short-term equity/crypto correlation not deterministic).

## Context & numbers
- **Crypto treasuries** on corporate balance sheets are rising (BTC is mainstream; alt treasuries rarer).  
- **710M DOGE** (~0.5% of supply) under custody **reduces free float** and can affect liquidity/order books.  
- The **1B DOGE** target implies continued **spot purchases** over time (subject to market conditions and cash flow).

## Why it matters
- **Demand signal:** a corporate entity **systematically accumulating** a meme asset is different from short-term trade narratives.  
- **Utility narrative:** Foundation/House of Doge backing suggests a push for **real-world usage**, not just hodling.  
- **Concentration risk:** a large treasury with one entity raises **future sell-pressure risk**.
