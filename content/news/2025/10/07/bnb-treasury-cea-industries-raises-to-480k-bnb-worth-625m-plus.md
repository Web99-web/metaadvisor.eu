**Overview.**  
CEA Industries (**BNC**) has increased its **BNB treasury to 480k**. The additional buys of about **$412.8M** (avg **$860/BNB**) are now worth **>$625M** at prices near **$1,303**. Including **$77.5M** in cash, total treasury is approaching **$700M**.

---

## Network & market context
- **BNB Chain activity rising:** TokenTerminal shows ~**58M monthly active addresses (Sept)**.  
- **DeFi driver:** **Aster DEX** surged (TVL ~**$2.4B**; monthly volume near **$200B** — DeFiLlama), supporting demand for BNB as gas/collateral.  
- **Integrations:** partnership with **Chainlink** to bring **US macro data on-chain** strengthens the infrastructure narrative.

## What CEA says
- Goal to accumulate **1% of BNB total supply** by year-end → ongoing spot buy pressure.  
- **Long-only treasury** approach using CEX/OTC liquidity.  
- On the day of the release: **BNC shares ~+7%**, **BNB** just off intraday highs.

## Why it matters
- **Structural demand:** corporate treasuries accumulating L1 assets **reduce free float** and can shape order-book dynamics.  
- **Signal to allocators:** L1 beyond BTC is increasingly considered in institutional risk buckets.  
- **Risks:** concentration with one entity; potential future sell pressure if strategy changes; regulatory perception.
