**Who is BitGo?**  
Founded in 2013 (California), BitGo is a pioneer of **institutional crypto custody**. It popularized **multisig wallets**, offers **cold-storage**, client insurance cover, **OTC liquidity**, and infrastructure for token/stablecoin issuers. Clients include exchanges, funds, corporates and fintechs that require bank-grade key management.

---

## What is VARA — and why does it matter?
**VARA (Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority)** is Dubai’s **virtual-assets regulator**, licensing and supervising **custodians, brokers/dealers, exchanges, lending/staking providers and portfolio managers** across the Emirate (note: DIFC has a separate regime).  
Key aspects:
- **Activity-based licensing** (distinct permissions per service).  
- Strict **AML/KYC**, **Travel-Rule** compliance and **marketing** standards for high-risk products.  
- **Enforcement** via penalties/suspensions for non-compliant firms.  
- **Institutional readiness:** banks and funds prefer **licensed** providers for onboarding and audits.

**BitGo + VARA** ⇒ BitGo can deliver custody and related institutional services **within a recognized regulatory perimeter** in Dubai — meaningful as global oversight tightens.

---

## Why this license matters
- **Quality signal:** licensing eases **due-diligence** and collaboration with **banks/asset managers**.  
- **Regional hub:** Dubai is positioning as a **regulated crypto center** for MENA; the license supports client acquisition.  
- **Operational risk reduction:** standardized processes, asset segregation and access controls mitigate commingling/operational failures.
