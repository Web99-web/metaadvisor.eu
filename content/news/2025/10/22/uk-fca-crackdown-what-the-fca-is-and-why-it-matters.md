The **FCA (Financial Conduct Authority)** is the United Kingdom’s primary **financial regulator** for firms and markets. Its mandate is to **protect consumers**, **ensure market integrity**, and **promote competition**. For crypto businesses this means: if you **target UK users**, you must be **FCA-registered** and follow the **financial promotions rules** to the letter.

## What happened in October
- The FCA sent **hundreds of warnings** to exchanges, brokers and affiliate networks operating **without registration** or running **non-compliant promotions**.  
- Firms were told to display **clear risk warnings**, **remove misleading claims**, and **disable onboarding** for UK users if unregistered.  
- Persistent offenders face **investigations**, **domain blocks**, and **sanctions**.

## Why it matters
- **Consumers:** fewer wild ads, clearer risk disclosure and pricing transparency.  
- **Industry:** greater **legal certainty** for compliant players, plus higher **compliance costs**.  
- **Global platforms:** marketing into the UK without FCA status risks **forced withdrawal** from the market.

## Quick checklist for users
- Check the **FCA Register** (registration & permitted activities).  
- Treat **guaranteed returns/bonuses** as red flags.  
- Consider **self-custody** if you don’t trust a provider; keep seed phrases offline.
