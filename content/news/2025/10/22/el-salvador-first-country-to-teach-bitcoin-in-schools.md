**El Salvador** is officially the first country to integrate **Bitcoin education into public schools**. The Ministry of Education, in partnership with *Mi Primer Bitcoin*, ran a pilot across roughly fifty schools and is now scaling the curriculum nationwide.

The **“What Is Money?”** course covers financial literacy basics, the history of money, and hands-on Bitcoin usage. Students learn how **blockchain** works, how to operate a **BTC wallet**, and what it means to use a global, peer-to-peer payment network without intermediaries.

### Why it matters
- 📚 **Financial literacy**: early, practical knowledge about digital money and personal finance.  
- 🌍 **Global first**: El Salvador again positions itself as a Bitcoin pioneer.  
- 💸 **Economic vision**: aims to lift financial inclusion and attract new investment over time.  

### Criticism and challenges
- ❗ **Bitcoin volatility**: risk of misperceived dangers among younger audiences.  
- 🏫 **Teacher training & resources**: effective rollout needs materials and professional development.  
- ⚖️ **Institutional skepticism**: the program remains under scrutiny by global bodies.
