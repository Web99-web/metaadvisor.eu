### Coinbase rolls out an Amex card with a Bitcoin-first identity

**Coinbase** has launched the **Coinbase One Card** in partnership with **American Express**, available to U.S. users who subscribe to the **Coinbase One** plan (**$49.99/year**). The card offers **up to 4% back in Bitcoin (BTC)** on everyday purchases.

- **Rewards:** up to 4% BTC cashback.  
- **Fees:** no foreign transaction fees.  
- **Bill payment:** from a linked bank account or with crypto held on Coinbase.  
- **Taxes (U.S.):** BTC rewards **don’t appear** on 1099 forms when earned; **selling** those rewards may trigger taxes.  

The physical card leans into Bitcoin’s origin story: it’s etched with data from the **Genesis Block** (the first block created by **Satoshi Nakamoto** on Jan 3, 2009). The brand name “Coinbase” references the **coinbase transaction**, the first entry in each new block that issues newly minted BTC to miners. The positioning is unmistakably **Bitcoin-first**.

### Who is Gemini?

**Gemini** is a U.S. crypto exchange and fintech firm based in **New York**, founded in 2014 by the **Winklevoss twins** (of Facebook notoriety).  
- Gemini is licensed and regulated by the **New York Department of Financial Services (NYDFS)**, giving it a reputation as one of the more tightly regulated U.S. crypto platforms.  
- The company offers a **trading exchange**, **crypto custody**, **staking**, and **rewards credit cards**.  
- Its product philosophy emphasizes **asset choice and flexibility**, contrasting with Coinbase’s **BTC-first** narrative.

### Gemini Credit Card & Solana Edition

- **Gemini Credit Card (2023):** offers **up to 3% cashback** in **multiple cryptocurrencies** (BTC, ETH, stablecoins, and others). Users can **choose** which crypto they receive rewards in.  
- **Gemini Solana Edition (2025):** a branded variant paying rewards in **SOL**:  
  - up to **4% SOL** on gas, **EV charging**, and rideshare,  
  - **3%** on dining, **2%** on groceries, **1%** on other purchases,  
  - plus select merchant offers up to **10%**.  
- **Annual fee:** **none**, which appeals to users who don’t want a fixed yearly cost.

### The key difference: Coinbase vs. Gemini

- **Coinbase + Amex:** a pure **Bitcoin-rewards** proposition (BTC-only), strong **Genesis Block** symbolism and premium branding; in return, there’s an **annual fee** ($49.99).  
- **Gemini:** **multi-asset rewards** with high flexibility (user chooses the crypto), **no annual fee**, and extra product lines such as the **Solana Edition**.
