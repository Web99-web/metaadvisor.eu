### Market Outlook (Oct 22)

**Bitcoin (BTC)** sits at a critical **$107K** support. A break below risks **$100K**, while strength would only show above **$116K**. Projections remain wide – from **$200K by end-2025** to as low as **$60K** if bearish patterns play out.

**Ether (ETH)** rejected at **$4,062 (20-day EMA)**. Breakdown below channel support points to **$3,350**. Bullish only above EMAs and channel top.

**BNB** consolidates between **$1,122 EMA** and **$1,041 SMA**. Below: risk to **~$932**. Above: room toward **$1,198**.

**XRP** stalled at **$2.55 EMA**. Key levels: **2.19 → 2.06 → 1.90 USD**. Bullish reversal only above **$3.38**.

**Solana (SOL)** rejected at **$198 EMA**. Break of channel support risks **$155 → $145**. Bullish case only above channel top.

**Dogecoin (DOGE)** – critical support **$0.18**. Below: **0.16 → 0.14**. Recovery requires above **$0.23**, then test of **$0.29**.

**Cardano (ADA)** – supports at **0.59 → 0.50**. Under **0.50**, target **0.40**. Bullish only above **0.75**.

**Hyperliquid (HYPE)** rejected near **$40 EMA**. Supports: **33.28 → 30.50 → 28**. Strength only above **$46.42 SMA** toward **$51**.

**Chainlink (LINK)** drops toward channel support. Below: retest of **$15.43**, further to **$12.73**. Bullish: above EMA, aiming for channel top.

**Stellar (XLM)** relief rally capped at **$0.34 EMA**. Support at **0.29**, break lower → **0.25**. Strength only above trendline.
