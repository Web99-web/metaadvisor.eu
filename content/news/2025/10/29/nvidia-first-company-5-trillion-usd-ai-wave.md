**In brief**  
Nvidia made history by crossing the **$5 trillion** market cap milestone, driven by unprecedented demand for AI computing. At the center: **Blackwell** systems and **rack-scale** setups like the **GB200 NVL72**, which accelerate training and serving of large language models (LLMs) in data centers.

---

## What happened
- **Milestone:** First company to reach $5 trillion market cap (Oct 29, 2025).  
- **Driver:** Continuous wave of AI chip orders from hyperscalers and AI cloud providers.  
- **Context:** AI megacaps are pulling indices higher, but debate grows over a potential **“AI bubble”** if ROI lags.

---

## Tech primer (for non-experts)
- **AI — Artificial Intelligence:** systems performing tasks resembling human intelligence (e.g., language, vision).  
- **LLM — Large Language Model:** trained on massive text datasets to understand/generate language.  
- **GPU — Graphics Processing Unit:** highly parallel chip ideal for matrix operations in AI.  
- **Blackwell (NVIDIA architecture):** new generation superchips; **GB200 NVL72** unites **72 Blackwell GPUs and 36 Grace CPUs** in one NVLink domain, acting as a “mega-GPU.”

---

## Why it matters
1) **Long investment cycle in infrastructure:** multi-year demand for GPUs, networks, cooling, energy.  
2) **Geopolitics & regulation:** export controls to China shape supply, demand, margins.  
3) **Energy & sustainability:** rack-scale AI pushes debates on efficiency, energy use, and data center locations.

---

## Crypto connection
- **AI infra narrative:** demand for compute boosts interest in decentralized compute and **RWA** (tokenized real assets).  
- **Short-term rotations:** when AI stocks hit records, capital sometimes shifts out of crypto; mid-term, both benefit from “tech risk-on” sentiment.  
- **Security & infra:** redundancy, energy, and data center siting increasingly matter in Web3 too.
