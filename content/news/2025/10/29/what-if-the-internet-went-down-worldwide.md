**In brief**  
The internet underpins modern life. Without it, banking, retail, supply chains and even hospitals would struggle. But the internet is **not a single central switch** — it’s a *network of networks* with countless backbones, subsea cables and data centers. A global blackout is near-impossible; what we actually face are **local and regional disruptions**.

---

## What a true global outage would mean
- **Money:** without online banking and cards, billions would lose access to funds.  
- **Food & fuel:** distribution chains would break as logistics rely on digital systems.  
- **Healthcare:** hospitals would slow down; telemedicine and IT-driven diagnostics would halt.  
- **Communication:** social media and email vanish; radio, TV and word-of-mouth remain.  
- **Markets & politics:** the world economy would lurch backwards, with social unrest likely.

---

## Why a global “off” is unlikely
- The net is composed of **hundreds of thousands of autonomous systems (AS)** connected via peering.  
- If a **subsea cable** or a **cloud provider** (AWS, Azure) fails, traffic reroutes.  
- **DNS, BGP and root servers** are replicated globally.  
- A worldwide outage would require simultaneously knocking out nearly all backbones and energy grids — highly unrealistic.

---

## What actually happens (realistic scenarios)
- **Regional outages** (cable cuts, cyberattacks, cloud incidents).  
- **Government kill switches** — some countries can temporarily isolate the internet.  
- **Cloud dependencies:** incidents at Amazon, Microsoft or Google propagate widely.

---

## How to prepare
- **Households:** keep a week of cash and supplies, charged powerbanks, key contacts on paper.  
- **Businesses:** enable offline sales (cash, paper receipts), runbooks for tills & inventory, supplier agreements.  
- **Society:** invest in redundancy (multiple providers, diverse links, satellite internet, mesh networks).

---

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- A global internet shutdown is unlikely, but **regional blackouts are real** and can paralyze daily life.  
- The answer isn’t fear but **resilience and decentralization** — that’s the internet’s strength.  
- Every major outage (Microsoft, Amazon, card networks) is a reminder that **offline processes** and preparedness are essential.
