Solana just had another **mainstream moment**: Fidelity, one of the world’s largest financial institutions, has officially added **SOL** to its lineup. Clients can now buy, sell and hold Solana across multiple Fidelity platforms — from **Fidelity Crypto** (retail) and **IRAs** to institutional access via **Fidelity Digital Assets**.

For retail investors, this removes friction: instead of going through crypto-native exchanges, they can operate **inside the familiar Fidelity ecosystem** with integrated KYC, reporting and custody. While Fidelity advertises “no commission”, the real trading cost is the **spread** (bid/ask difference, typically up to ~1%). For institutions and advisors, SOL becomes easier to include in **mandates and portfolios** under a **regulated framework**.

### What is Fidelity?
**Fidelity Investments** is a **Wall Street heavyweight** (founded 1946, Boston) managing over **$4T** in assets, offering funds, ETFs and retirement plans.  
- **Fidelity Digital Assets** (since 2018): institutional custody and trading (initially BTC/ETH).  
- **Fidelity Crypto**: the retail app for crypto trading — now including Solana.  

When Fidelity “unlocks” a new crypto asset, it signals that the asset has **escaped the niche** and entered the mainstream for millions of users in a regulated environment.

---

### Bigger picture
In 2025, **TradFi** is moving steadily into crypto. The **CME Group** is posting records in ETH, SOL and XRP derivatives; banks are piloting tokenization; and now a major broker like Fidelity is placing Solana alongside Bitcoin and Ether.  

For Solana — known for **throughput and low fees** — this matters for two reasons:  
1. **Liquidity** → Fidelity channels can funnel long-term capital (including retirement accounts).  
2. **Validation** → a TradFi heavyweight provides **mainstream legitimacy**.
