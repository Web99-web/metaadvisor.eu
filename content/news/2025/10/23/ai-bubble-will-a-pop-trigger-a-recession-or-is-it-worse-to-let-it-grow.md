Artificial intelligence (AI) has become the main engine of growth. Massive investments in data centers, GPU clusters and large models are pushing markets higher and supporting consumption through the so-called “wealth effect”. But cracks are showing: weak job growth, slowing real wages, rising loan delinquencies and collapsing consumer confidence. Political factors (trade wars, migration restrictions, prolonged shutdowns) are adding further pressure.

**What’s worse — a pop now, or letting the bubble inflate further?**  
- If the **bubble bursts now** → market crash, frozen investments and a **recession**. That means **job losses**, higher **unemployment** and **forced home sales**.  
- If it **keeps growing unchecked** → the eventual correction will be **deeper and longer**, with greater financial and social cost.

**Why is the AI boom fragile as the sole growth driver?**  
- **Narrow base:** Growth relies on a handful of big-tech companies and CAPEX with little spillover.  
- **Debt sensitivity:** High interest rates make households and businesses fragile.  
- **Housing pressure:** Mortgages and rents are heavy; defaults and evictions are rising.  
- **Productivity in pockets:** AI boosts niche domains, but broad-based productivity lags behind.

**Glossary:**  
- **AI (artificial intelligence):** computer systems performing tasks resembling human intelligence (language, predictions).  
- **CAPEX:** capital expenditures in long-term assets (data centers, infrastructure).  
- **Wealth effect:** higher asset prices encourage more consumer spending.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- 📉 **System weakness:** Reliance on Big Tech makes cycles fragile.  
- 👷 **Jobs:** Expect **layoffs** in cyclical industries (retail, construction, advertising).  
- 🏠 **Housing:** Rising defaults and **foreclosures** are likely.  
- 🧭 **Policy:** Should aim to spread AI productivity across the broader economy.  
- ⚠️ **For households & investors:** diversify, reduce leverage, keep liquid buffers.
