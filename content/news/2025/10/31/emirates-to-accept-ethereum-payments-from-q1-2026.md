Emirates, one of the world’s largest airlines, plans to accept **cryptocurrency payments** — with a focus on **Ethereum (ETH)** — from **Q1 2026**. The move follows a signed **MoU** (English *Memorandum of Understanding*) with **Crypto.com** to integrate **Crypto.com Pay** into its payments and booking stack.

### What it means for travelers
If timelines hold, customers will be able to choose **crypto checkout** during booking and confirm the transaction from their own wallet (e.g., via QR code). The payment processor — **PSP** (English *Payment Service Provider*) — can settle to Emirates either in **crypto** or via **fiat conversion** (USD/EUR/AED), depending on merchant settings. That reduces volatility exposure for the airline while delivering a smooth ETH payment flow for users.

### Why Ethereum?
Ethereum is widely used for **payments and smart contracts**, offering deep liquidity and broad PSP support. Alongside **BTC**, ETH is often the first choice for crypto pilots because:
- PSP coverage is strong,
- confirmations can be faster via **Layer-2**,
- it fits more easily into existing payment and accounting flows.

**Acronyms explained:**  
- **KYC/AML** (English *Know Your Customer / Anti-Money Laundering*) — identity verification and anti-money-laundering controls required by PSPs.  
- **PSP** (English *Payment Service Provider*) — intermediates card and crypto payments.

### Benefits and challenges
**Benefits for travelers:** more payment choice, faster online settlement, ability to spend crypto without prior conversion.  
**Benefits for Emirates:** reach tech-savvy audiences, potentially lower processing costs in some cases, first-mover marketing in aviation.  
**Challenges:** **ETH** price volatility, tax/accounting rules across markets, and technical integration with legacy booking systems. Hence the **Q1 2026** start — allowing time for testing, compliance, and security.

### Bigger picture: crypto in travel
The travel sector has been exploring crypto payments (accommodation, flights, ancillaries) for years. Key drivers are global demand, simpler cross-border payments, and brand differentiation. The biggest leaps happen when **major brands** plug in established PSPs with crypto support — as in this case.
