### What is a whitepaper and why does it matter?
A **whitepaper** is a technical-concept document that explains how a system works, what problems it addresses and which mechanisms it uses.  
- The Bitcoin whitepaper (author: **Satoshi Nakamoto**) describes in just 9 pages a **peer-to-peer electronic cash system** without intermediaries.  
- Key elements: blockchain, Proof-of-Work, distributed nodes, economic incentives, security and double-spend protection.  
- Although Bitcoin is now global, the whitepaper remains the **design blueprint for the entire network and a model for many other projects**.

### Where is Bitcoin 17 years later?
- From a small cypherpunk idea → a global network with millions of participants.  
- From hobby code → an asset class analysed by banks, exchanges, states and ETFs.  
- Layer-1 remains, and Layer-2 solutions such as the **Lightning Network** have emerged.  
- Price path: from cents to over $100,000 in 2025.

### Today’s focus: $13.4 billion options expiry
What does this mean?

| Term | Explanation |
|------|------------|
| **Options** | Contracts giving the right (not the obligation) to buy/sell an asset at a pre-determined price before a specific date. |
| **Open Interest (OI)** | The number of open contracts that haven’t been settled. |
| **Max Pain Level** | The theoretical price at which most options expire worthless — often observed when big expiries happen. |
| **IV (Implied Volatility)** | Market expectation of future volatility embedded in option prices. |

Large expiries often lead to:  
- **Increased short-term volatility**, as positions get closed or rolled over  
- Market-makers unwinding hedges, which may cause temporary price whipsaws  
- Post-expiry clearer trend signals emerging in the days after

### Why is predicting the direction tricky?
- More **call options** (bullish bets) doesn’t guarantee a price rise — many are hedged.  
- Rising **put option** premiums indicate growing fear — higher IV signals uncertainty.  
- Current data suggests a **slightly bullish positioning**, but **short-term shake-out remains likely**.
