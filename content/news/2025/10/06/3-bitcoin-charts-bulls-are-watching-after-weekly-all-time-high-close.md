Bitcoin ended the week with a **new all-time high weekly close** (spot also briefly reached fresh highs). Bulls are now focused on **three key references** — **without charts here**, to avoid confusion from misleading visuals:

## 1) 200-week moving average (200WMA)
A long-term indicator that moves **slowly upward** and usually sits well below spot during bull phases. The fact that 200WMA remains far under the market price confirms an **intact long-term uptrend** and provides a “floor” in case of larger corrections.

## 2) Realized price
The on-chain average cost basis of all circulating coins. In bull cycles, the **realized price** tends to stay **above the 200WMA** — historically a reliable marker of the **early/mid bull phase**.

## 3) Weekly close at ATH
A **record high weekly close** signals **price discovery** and trend strength — especially when supported by strong liquidity (volume) and ETF inflows. Weekly closes also filter out intraday volatility better than shorter timeframes.

---

## Why these three matter
- **Historical pattern:** Realized price > 200WMA has coincided with months of sustained bullish momentum.  
- **Liquidity flows:** High spot + derivatives volumes, combined with ETF inflows, provide the **fuel** for further upside.  
- **Risk management:** Even in bull runs, **shakeout corrections** are normal; watch funding, open interest, and BTC dominance for overheating signals.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Setup stays bullish**, but the **pace** depends on ETF flows and macro conditions.  
- Different from 2021: deeper liquidity and stronger **institutional participation** shape the market differently now.  
- **Watch next:** strong weekly closes, derivative metrics (funding/OI), and capital rotations between BTC, ETH, and Solana.
