**What is Pump.fun?**  
A platform for **instant memecoin launches** on **Solana** — no complex setup, no manual pool creation. A creator inputs name/symbol, sets parameters, and the token starts on a **bonding curve**. As buyers come in, the **price rises**, and once a threshold is met, a **DEX pool** (e.g., on Raydium) is automatically created.

### How it captured ~80% of Solana’s memecoin scene
- **Frictionless UX:** one-click launch + minimal learning curve = explosion of tokens.  
- **Built-in liquidity:** bonding-curve solves the empty order book problem from day one.  
- **Viral distribution:** meme creators + **KOLs** + **notification bots** drive FOMO.  
- **Solana’s speed & cost:** cheap txs enable rapid-fire micro bets.  
- **Automatic DEX migration:** once the threshold is hit, liquidity pool is live.

### Weak spots and risks
- **Rugpull/dump risk:** fast launches without diligence = pump-and-dump cycles.  
- **Spam & saturation:** too many tokens dilute attention; harder to stand out.  
- **MEV & bots:** snipes/front-running harm retail participants.  
- **Regulatory uncertainty:** easy launches may attract scrutiny (KYC/AML, market abuse).  
- **Project quality:** only a handful survive weeks; most fade as liquidity drains.

### Can it last?
Short term, **yes** — as long as it’s **fun, fast and cheap**, memecoin hype will flow. Long term, sustainability depends on:  
- evolving from pure meme to **utility/narrative** (games, creator tools),  
- stronger **anti-rug** mechanisms and transparency,  
- stable fees and network resilience.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **PMF = fun + speed.** Pump.fun nailed Solana’s sweet spot.  
- **Noise filter:** watch token distribution, team locks, and treasury before entry.
