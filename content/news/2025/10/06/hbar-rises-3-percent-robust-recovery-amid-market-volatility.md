## What is HBAR? (quick explainer)
**HBAR** is the **native token** of the **Hedera Hashgraph** network — a DLT using a **hashgraph/DAG consensus** rather than a traditional blockchain.

- **Hedera’s focus:** **high throughput**, **very low fees** *(typically < **$0.01** per transaction)*, **energy efficiency**, and strong **enterprise adoption**.  
- **Governing Council:** marquee members such as **Google, IBM, Boeing, Deutsche Telekom, LG, Ubisoft** and others participate in **network governance**.  
- **HBAR utility:** paying **transaction/smart-contract fees**, **staking/securing the network** (validator rewards), and fueling **dApps, NFTs & DeFi** across Hedera.

👉 **Bottom line:** **HBAR** is the **fuel of the Hedera network** and a **security/staking asset** that underpins its operations.
