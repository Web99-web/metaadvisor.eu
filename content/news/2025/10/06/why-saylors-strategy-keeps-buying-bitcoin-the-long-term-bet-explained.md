**Why keep buying BTC?**  
For Saylor, **Bitcoin** is a core **treasury reserve asset** with **asymmetric long-term risk/reward**. Instead of timing the market, the approach is **disciplined DCA**, aligned with cash flows and **access to capital markets**.

### Core pillars
- **BTC as “digital gold”:** thesis that it preserves/compounds purchasing power through cycles.  
- **DCA & counter-cyclical buys:** spreading purchases reduces timing dependence.  
- **Corporate financing:** using **convertible notes** and similar tools to scale exposure at acceptable cost of capital.  
- **Balance-sheet framing:** BTC as a **strategic reserve**; tolerance for drawdowns in pursuit of long-term outcomes.  
- **Institutional rails (ETFs):** broader access improves **liquidity** and **price discovery**.

### In practice
- **Embrace volatility:** drawdowns = opportunities to accumulate.  
- **Risk management:** diversify **funding sources**, stagger entries, set clear investor comms.  
- **Default posture:** limited hedging; preference to **hold** through cycles.

---

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- Works for treasuries seeking an **inflation hedge** and able to shoulder **large drawdowns**.  
- **Key risk:** **leverage and refinancing** through cycles — cost of capital must stay **below** expected **real BTC return**.  
- **Operationally:** DCA + long horizon tends to beat market timing.  
- **Catalysts:** sustained ETF inflows, fair-value accounting tailwinds, broader use of BTC as **collateral**.  
- Investors should predefine **>50% drawdown** policy before sizing exposure.
