*Image is symbolic.*

Like other Big Tech players, **Meta** has its own flagship generative AI model: **Llama**. What sets it apart is its **openness** — developers can download and run it locally (with some licensing limits). That contrasts with **Claude (Anthropic)**, **Gemini (Google)**, **Grok (xAI)** and many **ChatGPT** variants (OpenAI), which are chiefly API-only.

### Cloud partners and tooling
To broaden access, Meta partners with **AWS**, **Google Cloud** and **Microsoft Azure** to offer **hosted Llama** options. It also ships **tools, libraries and a “cookbook”** for **fine-tuning**, **evaluation** and **domain adaptation**. With **Llama 3** and **Llama 4**, capabilities expanded to **native multimodal** support (text, code, images) and wider cloud rollouts.

### The model family
**Llama isn’t one model, it’s a family.** The current generation, **Llama 4** (released **April 2025**), ships in three main editions:
1. **Llama 4 Base** — foundation model for research and continued training.  
2. **Llama 4 Instruct** — tuned for **chat/assistant** use; safer, more helpful responses.  
3. **Llama 4 Code** — tailored for **programming** and code reasoning tasks.  

### Why context windows matter
A model’s **context window** determines how much input it can consider at once. **Longer context** reduces forgetting and off-topic drift, but at extreme lengths can **weaken safety guardrails** if not handled carefully.

### Bottom line
**Meta Llama** blends **openness** with strong **capabilities**:  
- **Developers:** freedom to run and adapt locally.  
- **Enterprises:** convenient **cloud availability** across major providers.  
- **Researchers:** open models accelerate **innovation** and **transparency**.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)

- The three editions (Base, Instruct, Code) cover the community’s core needs.  
- Openness nurtures a **fast-growing tooling ecosystem**.  
- The challenge is balancing **openness and safety**, especially as context and capability scale up.
