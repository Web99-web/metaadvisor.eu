*Image is symbolic.*

**Bee Maps** is an app built on the **Hivemapper** network — one of the largest **DePIN** (decentralized physical infrastructure) projects on Solana. Drivers use **AI dash-cams** to capture **real-world changes on roads** (new signs, construction, detours), feeding data into **digital maps** that update in real time. Contributors are rewarded in the native token **$HONEY**.

### $32M for scaling
The new **$32 million round**, led by **Pantera Capital, LDA Capital, Borderless Capital and Ajna Capital**, will fund:  
- **more device deployments** and global coverage,  
- stronger **AI models** for real-time map updates,  
- enhanced **incentives** tied to the $HONEY economy.

### Partnerships
Bee Maps/Hivemapper have teamed up with **Lyft** and **Volkswagen’s robotaxi program**, integrating mapping data into **commercial use cases** such as navigation, logistics, and autonomous driving.

### How it works
- **Devices** send anonymized road footage  
- **AI pipeline** detects changes (construction, signs, limits)  
- **Maps auto-update** in real time  
- **Contributors** earn $HONEY for valid submissions  

With the new **Bee Membership plan**, upfront costs (~$600) drop to just **$19/month** for hardware, software, and contributor benefits.

## Why it matters
- **Real-world data = real utility:** fresh + broad data coverage = better maps.  
- **Solana stack:** high throughput & low fees ideal for **micro-rewards** and scaling.  
- **DePIN momentum:** funding + B2B integrations = clear signal that DePIN is moving into **production-grade services**.
