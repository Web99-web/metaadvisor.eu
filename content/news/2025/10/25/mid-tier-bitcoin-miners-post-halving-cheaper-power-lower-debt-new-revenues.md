**Summary.** After the halving, the mining race isn’t just “the giants vs. everyone else.” Mid-tier operators are building momentum by combining **lower cost per terahash**, **disciplined leverage**, **better power deals** and — increasingly — **additional revenue streams** from their existing infrastructure. That shifts the leaderboard and reframes miners as **digital infrastructure**, not only hashrate machines.

### 1) A second revenue engine from existing infrastructure
More operators dedicate part of their capacity to adjacent services (e.g., space and power for compute-intensive workloads). These projects often deliver **higher revenue per kWh** than pure mining and bring **steadier cash flows** through contracted clients, reducing reliance on volatile block rewards.

### 2) Halving & debt: agility wins
With higher network difficulty and tighter margins, **CAPEX/debt discipline** and **fast fleet rotation** toward more efficient hardware (better performance-per-watt), including smarter cooling, are critical. Mid-tier players often implement upgrades faster because they aren’t bound by the “scale at all costs” imperative.

### 3) Power is the first line of defense
Long-term **power purchase agreements**, participation in **demand-response programs**, and siting in **low-tariff regions** are key to **locking in energy costs**. Operators with that flexibility defend margins more easily and bridge low-revenue periods.

### 4) Markets reward infrastructure, not just EH/s
Investors value miners that, beyond hashrate, show **data-center competence**: facility build-out and operations, **colocation**, and **hybrid revenues** (BTC plus adjacent services). That is driving a **repricing** of the sector and greater interest in mid-tier operators with strong power economics and operating discipline.

---

**Our take (Metaadvisor):**
- Additional revenue streams are becoming a real **margin stabilizer** — while still requiring careful **CAPEX** planning and payback visibility.
- **Power wins**: contracts, location and flexible load management determine margin leaders.
- **Debt** accelerates growth but can **punish inefficiency** harder post-halving.
- Mid-tier wins via **micro-optimization** (efficiency, cooling, fleet refresh) and **smart contracts**, not by chasing EH/s at any cost.
