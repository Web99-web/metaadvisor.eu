**What is fburl.com?**

Fburl.com is a short domain used by Facebook to shorten and redirect URLs across the platform. You’ll often see it in notifications, messages, or internal links.

**Is fburl.com safe?**

✅ If it comes directly from Facebook (e.g., a notification, email, or Messenger), it’s typically legitimate.  
⚠️ Attackers can still abuse short links to hide destinations and funnel users to phishing or malicious pages.

**How to inspect fburl.com links:**

1. Use a service like checkshorturl.com to reveal the final destination.  
2. Verify the sender — be cautious with unknown contacts.  
3. Open in a private/incognito window.
