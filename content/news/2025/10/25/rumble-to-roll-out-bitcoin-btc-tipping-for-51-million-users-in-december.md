Video platform **Rumble (RUM)** plans to introduce **Bitcoin (BTC) tipping** for its **51 million monthly users** by **mid-December**, giving creators another **direct revenue channel** from their audiences.  

While the numbers sound impressive, Rumble remains relatively **unknown outside North America** – especially compared to YouTube. That makes this announcement noteworthy: crypto features are slowly entering alternative video ecosystems.  

The feature is being developed in **partnership with Tether** and will allow viewers to send **BTC tips** via an **integrated digital wallet** inside the Rumble app. The announcement was made at the **Plan ₿ Forum in Lugano, Switzerland**.  

The company said the system is still in **testing phase**. The first BTC tip was sent to Canadian creator **David Freiheit**.  

Rumble has also adopted a **Bitcoin treasury strategy**, having accumulated **211 BTC** according to available data.
