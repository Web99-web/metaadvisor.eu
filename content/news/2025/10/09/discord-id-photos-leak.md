Up to **70,000 Discord users** may be affected after a third-party **age-verification** vendor was compromised.

According to the report, the following may have been accessed:
- **ID photos** submitted during the verification process  
- Names and email addresses  
- IP addresses  
- Messages with Discord customer support  

➡️ **Passwords** and **full credit card details** were **not** included.

The incident has been reported to the U.K. **ICO**, which is assessing the case. The photos were provided during “age appeal” flows for accounts that were temporarily locked. In some countries (e.g., the U.K.), age checks are mandated under the **Online Safety Act**, making such vendors **prime targets** for attackers.

---

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- ⚠️ **High privacy risk:** ID photos can reveal extensive personal details.  
- 🔐 **Third-party weak point:** sensitive workflows require stricter audits, encryption and data minimization.  
- 📉 **Trust hit:** even when the breach is at a vendor, platform reputation suffers.  
- 🧭 **Industry takeaway:** adopt privacy-by-design and prompt data deletion post-verification.
