**Jupiter**, a leading DeFi aggregator on Solana, announced **JupUSD**, a **Solana-native stablecoin**. The move expands Jupiter’s footprint and reinforces Solana’s push toward mainstream usage.

#### Key details
- 📌 **Token:** JupUSD  
- 🌐 **Chain:** Solana  
- 🔗 **Integration:** perps, lending, swap modules  
- 💰 **Goal:** core trading & liquidity unit across Jupiter’s DeFi stack

Jupiter also teased a **Perps AMA** (higher leverage, lower fees, more pairs). Additionally, **53.47M JUP** (≈1.72% of supply) are scheduled to **unlock on Oct 28, 2025**.

#### Why it matters
- 🔥 **Signal for Solana:** more native stablecoins strengthen DeFi infrastructure.  
- 📊 **Liquidity & adoption:** JupUSD could become a default settlement asset within Jupiter apps.  
- ⚖️ **Risks:** maintaining dollar peg and user trust in a new stablecoin.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- 🚀 Jupiter is evolving from DEX aggregator to **infrastructure provider**.  
- 🧩 If JupUSD becomes the Solana “default,” it may displace USDC/USDT in niches.  
- 📉 Near term watch: **JUP token unlock** in late October as potential price pressure.
