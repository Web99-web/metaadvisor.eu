## What happened?
Within **ten minutes**, the market saw a record **flash crash** and rapid rebound. Excessive leverage, thin order books and cascading liquidations dragged prices deep below fair ranges, followed by a mechanical bounce.

> **Exchange note:** We rely on **MEXC** data (24h High/Low/Last). Some **Bybit** traders reported even more extreme wicks — such discrepancies are common during liquidation cascades (order-book depth, feeds, ADL rules).

---

## Summary
- **Wick alts** (e.g. **TIA, SUI, ENA, AVAX, INJ, JTO, WLD, DOGE, PEPE, PUMP, TAO, CRO, ATOM**) dropped **-65% to -85%** then rebounded **+120% to +380%**.  
- **Blue chips** (**BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, XRP**) saw strong but **more orderly** moves.  
- **Relatively stable/positive outliers**: **ZEC, HYPE, ASTER**.

## Metaadvisor take
- **Liquidations ≠ market truth** — they reflect mechanics, not fair value.  
- **Wick alts**: massive potential but extremely risky.  
- **Blue chips**: steadier anchors, but still vulnerable.  
- **Exchange discrepancies** are real; always cite your data source (here: MEXC).
