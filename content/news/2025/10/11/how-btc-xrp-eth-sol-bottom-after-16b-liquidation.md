The crypto market just experienced the **largest liquidation event on record** — about **$16B** in leveraged longs across **BTC, ETH, XRP, SOL** and broader altcoins were force-closed. Many altcoins dropped **20–40%** to **multi-month lows**.

Key question: **V-shaped rebound or slower bottoming?** Market structure and past episodes point to a **gradual reset**.

## The typical post-cascade playbook

**1) Further bleeding / follow-through selling**  
As *forced liquidations* hit order books, prices often **push lower**. We already saw several altcoins (e.g., **XRP**, **DOGE**) print **multi-month lows**.

**2) Market makers step back temporarily**  
Liquidity providers **de-risk** and focus on **arbitraging** — first **spot ↔ perp** mismatches are neutralized rather than aggressively restocking depth.

**3) Arbitrage dampens any instant rebound**  
Until **spot and futures** re-align, a **V-bounce** is unlikely. The result tends to be a **gradual reset**, not a snapback.

**4) Infrastructure stabilization**  
During shocks, **data feeds**, **order-book updates** and execution often face delays/outages. Once **feeds and books** normalize, **spreads** and **liquidity** improve.

## What to watch in the coming days

- **Funding & Open Interest**: Has leverage flushed or is it returning quickly?  
- **Basis (perp vs. spot)**: Fading extremes = cooling conditions.  
- **Order-book depth / spreads**: Liquidity returning at key levels.  
- **CEX/DEX flows**: Net exchange inflows/outflows (pressure gauge).  
- **Volatility**: Falling realized/IV often precedes a sturdier bottom.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- After **extreme cascades**, markets more often carve a **slow bottom** than a clean V-reversal.  
- **Risk discipline** (lower leverage, clear SLs, partial TPs) matters more now than nailing the perfect bottom.  
- **Correlations** (BTC ↔ ETH ↔ alts) can stay elevated until **liquidity** and **confidence** return — expect **wave-like rebounds**.
