Google is introducing a new **Gemini-powered** option in Gmail and Google Calendar called **“Help me schedule.”** It suggests suitable times based on your availability and inserts them into your email. When the recipient picks a slot, the invite automatically lands on both calendars.

### How it works
- Currently optimized for **one-on-one meetings** (not large groups).  
- Click **Help me schedule** under the Gmail compose window → time suggestions appear.  
- Edit/remove slots as needed and insert them into the email.  

### Free for personal users
- **Gemini** also exists in a **free** tier: personal users get basic AI features in the **Gemini app** and some Google services.  
- For scheduling, **personal Google accounts** (Gmail + Calendar) get a **basic version** at **no extra cost**.  
- In short: usable personally — but with **limited scope** vs. Business features.

### Paid for businesses and power users
- **Google Workspace (Business/Enterprise)** plans include expanded AI features.  
- **Gemini AI Pro** (~€22/month) unlocks stronger models and deeper integrations (Docs, Slides, Sheets).  
- **Gemini Enterprise** gives companies the full stack — **licensed per user**.
