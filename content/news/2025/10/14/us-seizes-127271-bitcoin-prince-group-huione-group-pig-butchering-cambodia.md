US authorities have executed the **largest crypto seizure in history**, taking control of **127,271 Bitcoin**, worth around **$14 billion**. The funds were linked to the global criminal network **Prince Group**, which prosecutors describe as one of the biggest operators of online scams and forced labor in Southeast Asia. 

**Chen Zhi**, the founder and chairman of the group, was indicted in New York for conspiracy to launder money and commit wire fraud. The Department of Justice (DOJ) called the seizure historic, while the US Treasury’s OFAC designated **Prince Group** a transnational criminal organization and imposed sweeping sanctions. At the same time, the **Huione Group**, another Cambodian financial conglomerate, was cut off from the US financial system under Section 311 of the PATRIOT Act.

### Pig-butchering scams and forced labor
According to the indictment, the group built a sophisticated fraud network known as **“pig-butchering”**: workers, often victims of human trafficking, were forced under threat of violence to operate scams from fenced compounds in Cambodia. Fraudsters lured victims — mostly in the US — into fake “investments” in crypto platforms, siphoning funds through complex money laundering networks.

US Attorney General Pamela Bondi stated that this was “one of the most significant strikes ever against the global scourge of human trafficking and cyber-enabled financial fraud.”
