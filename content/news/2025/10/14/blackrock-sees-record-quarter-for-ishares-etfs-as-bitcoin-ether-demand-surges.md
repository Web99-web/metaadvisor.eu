**BlackRock** reported a **record quarter** for its **iShares** lineup, driven by accelerating **demand for Bitcoin (BTC) and Ether (ETH)** via ETF products. Robust **inflows** into crypto-focused funds improved **liquidity**, tightened **spreads**, and lifted **assets under management (AUM)** across iShares.

### What’s behind the surge?
- **Institutional normalization of crypto:** ETFs provide a regulated, custodial wrapper that reduces operational friction vs. self-custody.  
- **Cost & efficiency:** rising volumes lower **transaction costs** and make it easier to enter/exit with size.  
- **Portfolio role:** BTC/ETH ETFs increasingly serve as a **diversifier** alongside equities and bonds, with transparent **NAV** and daily settlement.

### Market implications
- **Issuer competition heats up:** pressure on **fees** and focus on **liquidity** — net positive for end investors.  
- **Broader investor base:** capital rotates from **traditional portfolios** that are taking initial crypto exposure via ETFs.  
- **Volatility remains:** a regulated wrapper **doesn’t remove** market swings — sentiment, macro, and news flow still rule.
