Bitcoin sits at a **critical support around $110,000**. The key question: *what if it doesn’t hold?* A breakdown could trigger **cascading liquidations** and open a path toward lower psychological levels.

### What could follow?
- **Cascading liquidations**: A breach often triggers stop-losses and forced liquidations on derivatives, accelerating the move.  
- **Sentiment shift**: The near-term bull case weakens as the market seeks the **next pocket of buyers**.  
- **Altcoin drag**: Historically, alts underperform on BTC drawdowns while BTC dominance rises.  

### Key zones to watch
- **Psychological round numbers**: 105k, 100k, 95k.  
- **Fibonacci retracements** of the prior leg higher: 0.382 – 0.5 – 0.618.  
- **Dynamic supports**: 21-week EMA and 200-day MA as trend anchors.  
- **Orderbook & CME gaps**: prior swing lows and gaps often act like magnets.  

### Signals worth tracking
- **Funding & open interest (OI)**: falling funding + declining OI ≈ a “flush”; rising OI into the drop ≈ fresh shorts.  
- **On-chain gauges**: MVRV, SOPR, realized-price bands — to gauge **capitulation zones**.  
- **Orderbook depth**: do **stepped bids** appear lower?  

If $110k **holds**, BTC may keep consolidating within a bullish framework. If it **breaks**, watch **sub-100k round numbers** and Fibo zones as potential new floors.
