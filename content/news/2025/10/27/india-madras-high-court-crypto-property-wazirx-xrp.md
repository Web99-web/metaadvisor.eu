### What happened?

The **Madras High Court** ruled that cryptocurrencies in India can be treated as **property**.  
In the WazirX case, the use of **3,532.30 XRP** belonging to a user was blocked while arbitration is ongoing.  
This prevented the exchange from reallocating customer assets to cover hack-related losses.

### Context: The WazirX hack (2024)

- The attack targeted a **multisig wallet** — meant to eliminate a single point of failure.  
- Still, the **signature backend was compromised**, allowing funds to be stolen.  
- Media reports linked the breach to the **Lazarus group** (North Korea).

---

### What it means for users

- **Property rights:** crypto assets can now be judicially protected in India.  
- **Segregation of funds:** exchanges must separate client assets from operational funds.  
- **Self-custody signal:** for significant holdings, hardware wallets and multisig remain best practice.

### What to watch next

- Progress on WazirX’s phased restart after restructuring.  
- Further lawsuits citing the “crypto = property” principle.  
- Whether other Indian courts adopt the same reasoning.
