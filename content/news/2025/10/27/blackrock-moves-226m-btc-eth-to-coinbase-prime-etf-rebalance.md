**What happened?**  
According to on-chain data and reports, BlackRock moved **~1,021 BTC (~$118M)** and **~25,707 ETH (~$107M)** to **Coinbase Prime** (total **~$226M**). The firm framed the transactions as **portfolio rebalancing**, i.e., aligning target weights amid ETF flows and price moves.

---

### Why it matters
- **ETF plumbing:** Spot ETFs **buy/sell** BTC & ETH continuously to track NAV and investor flows. With net inflows, custodians execute purchases and move assets to custody accounts.  
- **Liquidity & price:** Large institutional flows signal **active capital**; persistent inflows can support price — outflows the opposite.  
- **Trust signal:** **Coinbase Prime** represents institutional-grade custody & execution — further **standardizing** crypto operations.

---

### What is rebalancing?
Periodic **realignment** to:  
- restore target weights (e.g., 70% BTC / 30% ETH),  
- absorb ETF inflows/outflows,  
- reduce drift from price action.  

At scale, this means **series of transfers & orders** routed via a prime partner (here: Coinbase Prime).

---

### Market implications
- **Near term:** net **inflows → bullish tilt**; net **outflows → bearish tilt**.  
- **Medium term:** steady ETF activity strengthens **institutional infrastructure** (custody, reporting, liquidity).  
- **Volatility:** big transfers can coincide with **short/long liquidations**, amplifying moves.

---

### The quick stats
- BTC: **~1,021 BTC (~$118M)**  
- ETH: **~25,707 ETH (~$107M)**  
- Venue: **Coinbase Prime**  
- Driver: **portfolio rebalancing** (operational, not a macro call per se)
