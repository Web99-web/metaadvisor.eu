**What’s happening.** Based on consistent reporting from major outlets, the **Avalanche Foundation** is pushing a plan to form **two U.S. public companies** that would serve as **AVAX treasuries (institutional reserves)**. The package targets **up to $1B** in fresh capital: one transaction through a **SPAC**, the other via an **already-listed Nasdaq company**. Steps reported so far include merging an **“Avalanche Treasury Co.”** into a **SPAC deal (~$675M)** and a separate **“AVAX One”** project planning **~$550M** and aggressive market purchases of AVAX (sources: FT, CoinDesk, WSJ, BI).

### How the model would work
- **Primary goal:** accumulate **large AVAX positions** and hold them **on public balance sheets** (transparent, quarterly reporting).  
- **Active management:** beyond holding, the firms would **lend AVAX to institutions**, **provide liquidity**, and selectively pursue **DeFi strategies** to generate **yield**.  
- **Token sourcing:** partly **open-market buys**, partly **direct purchases** from the Foundation at a **discount** (common in such arrangements).  
- **Timing:** target **Q1 2026** for completion/listing, subject to approvals.

### Who’s around the table
- **Capital partners:** mentions include **Hivemind Capital**; advisory role for **Anthony Scaramucci** on one track; **Dragonfly** cited as SPAC sponsor on another.  
- **Motive:** offer **regulated, listed AVAX exposure** to funds that **cannot hold tokens directly** but can buy **public-company equities**.

### Why this could matter for the AVAX ecosystem
- **Institutional bridge:** public treasuries create **structural buyers** that **don’t day-trade**, but **accumulate** and **actively monetize** AVAX.  
- **Liquidity & narrative:** steady purchases plus liquidity provision can **reduce friction** and deepen **order books**.  
- **RWA angle:** Avalanche is pushing **real-world asset tokenization**; balance-sheet capital helps **partnerships and M&A**.

### Risks & open questions
- **Discounts/dilution:** discounted token sales can **pressure price** short-term; key are **pace & transparency**.  
- **SPAC execution:** SPAC outcomes are **mixed**; many trade **below NAV**.  
- **Regulatory optics for stablecoins/DeFi:** even “active” treasuries must adapt as **policy frameworks evolve**.  
- **NAV tracking:** treasury equities can **deviate from token NAV** (discount/premium).

### Mini-glossary
- **Treasury/reserves:** institutional **holdings** on a company balance sheet.  
- **SPAC:** special-purpose acquisition company enabling **faster listings** via merger.  
- **NAV (Net Asset Value):** per-share net asset value — reference for **discount/premium**.  
- **Market depth/liquidity:** ability to fill larger orders without big price impact.  
- **RWA (Real-World Assets):** tokenized **traditional assets** (funds, loans, bonds).
