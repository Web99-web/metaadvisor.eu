**A new Solana ecosystem project.** Solana co-founder **Anatoly Yakovenko** has introduced **Percolator**, a **decentralized exchange for perpetual derivatives (Perp DEX)**. The goal: to rival established platforms like **dYdX** and **Hyperliquid** by offering fast execution and low fees powered by Solana’s scalability.

### Key features
- **Perp DEX on Solana:** designed for the perpetual futures market, one of crypto’s most active segments.  
- **Speed & cost:** Solana’s throughput and low fees provide a potential edge.  
- **Competition:** while dYdX and Hyperliquid already dominate, the Solana community sees **tech + branding** as a strong attractor.

### What it means for users
If successful, **Percolator** could become the **new hub for perpetual derivatives** on Solana, delivering **CEX-like speed** with **DEX-level control**.
