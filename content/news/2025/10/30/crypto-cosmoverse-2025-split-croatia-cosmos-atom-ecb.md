If the Champions League is football’s pinnacle, **Cosmoverse** is a flagship moment for crypto—and this year it’s in **Split**. Founders, investors and institutions are debating how **interoperable networks**, **digital money** and **tokenized assets** become tomorrow’s internet infrastructure.

## Cosmos, explained simply
Imagine every app or bank had its own blockchain—and they could **all talk to each other**. That’s **Cosmos**:
- **IBC (Inter-Blockchain Communication)**: the secure “language” to move tokens/data across chains.  
- **Cosmos SDK**: the toolkit for building specialized app-chains.  
- **Cosmos Hub & ATOM**: the central hub and its token for security and governance.

Rather than one monolithic chain, Cosmos is a **network of sovereign chains** that cooperate—faster, cheaper, more flexible.

## Why is the ECB here?
Cosmoverse isn’t just for developers anymore. **Regulators such as the European Central Bank (ECB)** are on site to discuss:
- the **digital euro**, interoperability standards and market infrastructure,  
- **tokenization (RWA)** and multi-chain payments,  
- **operational resilience** beyond single-provider dependencies.

## Who’s on stage — global & regional voices (selection)
- **Ethan Buchman** — co-founder of the Cosmos ecosystem.  
- **Sunny Aggarwal** — co-founder of **Osmosis** (Cosmos DEX).

- **Jose Maria Macedo (Delphi Labs)** — DeFi/VC.

- **Dragan Primorac** — former **presidential candidate** in Croatia; a sign of growing **political interest** in crypto and blockchain.
