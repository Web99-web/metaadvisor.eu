**In brief**  
The Solana Seeker phone was supposed to bring owners **exclusive free apps and perks** via the dApp Store. In practice, many users experienced something else: “free” often came *with strings attached* — from mandatory email registration to surprise charges.

---

## Fitness app as the first “freebie”
For many, the first perk was a fitness app. Yet, using it required **email registration**. For a crypto community that values privacy and self-custody, this felt like the opposite of what was promised.

---

## Free .sol domain – but still 20 USD?
Promotions claimed every owner would get a **free .sol domain**. In practice, when registering through the SNS dApp, many saw a **$20 fee**.  
Why?
- Free applies only to **names with 10+ characters**.  
- Shorter or premium names require payment.  
- Not using the **official Genesis Token flow** pushes users into the normal paid process.  

---

## Helium and geo-limitations
Another perk was a **Helium Mobile free trial**, but available only in the US. For users in Europe or other regions, the perk was useless — adding to disappointment.

---

## Why frustration builds
- **Hype expectations** (BONK airdrop, “new free apps every week”).  
- **Reality of conditions** (signups, geo-restrictions, name length).  
- **Unexpected charges**, despite “free” promotions.  

---

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **(+)** Solana Mobile is trying to build an ecosystem with exclusive perks.  
- **(-)** In practice, “free” often isn’t truly free — terms and limits undermine trust.  
- **(=)** For long-term credibility, clear communication is needed about what’s actually free and what’s a promo hook.  
- **(+)** Lesson for users: always check the fine print and use the official claim process.
