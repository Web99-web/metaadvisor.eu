**In brief:**  
The role of **Meta AI advisers** is once again in the spotlight after claims surfaced that they spread **disinformation about vaccines**. Critics warn that such statements can erode **public trust** in health institutions and AI systems, while tech companies are expected to ensure **strict accountability and transparency**.

## Key points
- **Vaccines & health** are highly sensitive areas where disinformation spreads easily.  
- **Meta AI advisers** must avoid undermining the **scientific consensus**.  
- **Regulators and the public** expect higher levels of **responsibility and oversight**.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **AI + health = high risk**: Vaccine disinformation can have **real-world consequences** for public health.  
- **Advisory roles** in AI ethics must be **clearly defined** and evidence-based, not politicized.  
- **Meta and other platforms** risk **reputational damage** if private views are not separated from institutional roles.  
- **Conclusion**: On matters of public health, AI communication must be **accurate, neutral, and responsible**.
