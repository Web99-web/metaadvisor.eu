**In brief:**  
Today’s equity lending infrastructure relies on **batch files, manual processes and slow custodians**. The result: delays, counterparty risks and shrinking yields. The solution? **Blockchain** and **on-chain equity lending** — where smart contracts enable **instant settlement, automated collateral and transparent audit trails**.

## Problems with the old system
- Slow, manual reconciliations.  
- Counterparty risk and delays in recalls.  
- Erosion of trust due to opaque rules.  

## Benefits of the on-chain model
- **Instant settlement**: no waiting, no batch cycles.  
- **Programmable collateral**: rules and requirements coded, no manual exceptions.  
- **Transparency**: standardized tokens and auditable records.  

## Regulation & framework
- **BIS and ESMA** already exploring tokenization of government bonds and deposits.  
- **EU sandbox** enables testing on-chain solutions under supervision.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- On-chain is a **necessary step** to modernize capital markets.  
- Smart mix of **permissioned blockchains + ZKPs** can balance confidentiality and transparency.  
- The EU could seize a **first-mover advantage**, but overregulation remains a risk.  
- Staying with outdated systems risks **being left behind**.
