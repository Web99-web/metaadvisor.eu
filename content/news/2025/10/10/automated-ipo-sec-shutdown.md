Amid the **U.S. government shutdown** (about **90%** of SEC staff furloughed), companies can list via the rarely used **automatic effectiveness** route. After **20 days**, filings become effective **without** prior SEC review and **without** pricing (no penalties during the shutdown).

#### Key points
- ⏱️ **Automatic effectiveness** after 20 days; no pre-review.  
- 💵 **No price** required in the prospectus (“price-dependent info” waived).  
- ⚠️ **Investor protection weaker**: part of the vetting shifts **post-IPO**.  
- ⚖️ **Liability remains**: SEC can still demand amendments later.

---

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- 🚨 **Investor protection** diluted; higher retail risk.  
- 📊 **Potentially higher volatility** at listing due to less pre-IPO info.  
- 🧭 **Lesson**: political gridlock can quickly reshape market mechanics.
