**What is Phala Network?**  
**Phala (PHA)** delivers **confidential computing** for Web3: apps run inside **Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs)** such as **Intel SGX**, keeping **data private during computation**. This suits **AI workloads**, sensitive DeFi logic and enterprise-grade off-chain processing. The **PHA token** powers fees, staking and network security.

---

### Decision: from Polkadot parachain to Ethereum L2
The community approved a **full migration to an Ethereum L2**, trading the parachain model for **EVM compatibility** and direct access to the **broadest dev ecosystem** in crypto.

#### Why move?
- 📈 **Liquidity & reach**: larger EVM DeFi/NFT/infrastructure, deeper capital pools and user base.  
- 🧰 **Tooling & integrations**: instant access to wallets, oracles, indexers and mature dev stacks.  
- 🧩 **Interoperability**: faster connections to existing dApps and partners.  
- ⚠️ **Trade-offs**: migration/security risks (bridges, address mapping, audits).

#### What users should expect
- Clear **bridge/claim guides**, potential changes to **fees/rewards** and possibly **staking mechanics**.  
- **UX and security** of the migration will make or break adoption.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- 🧭 **Pragmatic call**: EVM network effects are hard to ignore.  
- 🔒 **Privacy mission** remains—key question is how well TEEs + AI operate in an L2 context.  
- 🌐 **Industry trend**: more non-EVM projects gravitating to EVM—ongoing consolidation around Ethereum.
