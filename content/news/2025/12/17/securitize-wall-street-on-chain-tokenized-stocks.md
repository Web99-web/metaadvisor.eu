# Securitize and Wall Street on-chain

Wall Street’s move onto blockchain infrastructure is no longer theoretical. It is happening quietly, methodically, and without spectacle. One of the key companies driving this shift is **Securitize**.

This is not another crypto experiment. It is a structured attempt to bring **real stocks and financial instruments on-chain**, without bypassing existing legal and regulatory frameworks.

## Who Securitize is

Securitize is a **US-based fintech company** focused on the tokenization of securities. Its mission is to enable traditional financial instruments — such as stocks and funds — to exist on blockchain networks as **legally recognized ownership**, not synthetic representations.

The company operates within US regulatory requirements and works closely with institutional participants, which sets it apart from earlier Web3 tokenization efforts.

## What Securitize aims to achieve

Securitize’s core goal is to establish blockchain as **infrastructure for real financial markets**. Tokens issued through its platform represent actual ownership, recorded directly in the issuer’s capitalization table.

This approach replaces exposure-only products with assets that carry real rights, transparency, and compliance.

## Why this matters for Wall Street

For traditional finance, going on-chain is not about disruption — it is about **efficiency and modernization**. Settlement, ownership tracking, and market access can be significantly improved without dismantling existing systems.

Rather than competing, TradFi and DeFi begin to integrate where it makes practical sense.

## From hype to infrastructure

Earlier tokenization attempts were often driven by speculation and short-term narratives. The current model emphasizes **stability, scalability, and legal clarity**.

Blockchain is no longer positioned as an alternative system, but as a foundational layer.

## A new market phase begins

If this model succeeds, Web3 becomes part of the mainstream financial system. Stock exchanges do not disappear — they evolve.

Securitize acts as a bridge between Wall Street and on-chain markets, helping move global finance into its next technological phase.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor):

- Securitize represents an institutional approach to tokenization.  
- Real ownership replaces price-only exposure.  
- Blockchain shifts from experiment to infrastructure.  
- This is a structural, long-term market transition.  
- TradFi moving on-chain signals a more mature crypto ecosystem.
