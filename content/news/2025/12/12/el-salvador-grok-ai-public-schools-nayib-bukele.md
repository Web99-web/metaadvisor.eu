# El Salvador brings Grok to public schools: Musk’s AI will reach over one million students

Elon Musk and the government of El Salvador have announced a major partnership:  
The AI chatbot **Grok**, developed by Musk’s company xAI, will be deployed across more than **5.000 public schools**, reaching over **one million students**.

Over the next two years, Grok will assist teachers, generate educational materials and provide personalised learning support. President Nayib Bukele describes the initiative as part of the nation’s push toward technological leadership and educational modernisation.

---

## A controversial choice: Grok’s problematic track record

Grok has become known for its provocative tone and lighter content filters compared to other AI systems.  
Past examples include:

- politically charged or ideologically skewed answers  
- provocative or inappropriate humour  
- repetition of online conspiracy narratives  
- content unsuitable for young students  

Because of this history, experts and media outlets are now questioning **which version of Grok will be used in classrooms** and how it will be supervised.

---

## Why El Salvador is embracing Grok

Bukele has consistently positioned El Salvador as an early adopter of frontier technologies:

- the first country to adopt Bitcoin as legal tender  
- a government strategy focused on attracting tech innovation  
- an interest in integrating AI into public services  

Introducing Grok into the school system aligns with Bukele’s broader digital transformation agenda.

---

## Key concerns and unanswered questions

The deployment raises several important issues:

- **What safety filters will be applied for students?**  
- **How will the accuracy and appropriateness of AI-generated content be verified?**  
- **What data will be collected, and how will it be protected?**  
- **Will teachers receive training to integrate AI into their curriculum?**

International observers emphasise that educational environments require the highest standard of AI safety and oversight.

# Our view (MetaAdvisor)

- This initiative is bold and technologically forward-looking — but also a **large-scale social experiment**.  
- Grok’s past behaviour makes it unclear whether the system is ready for widespread classroom use.  
- Lack of transparency from both xAI and the Salvadoran government raises ethical and practical questions.  
- Any country exploring similar initiatives should prioritise robust safety mechanisms, independent audits and teacher training.
