# DeFi Future: Finance Is Moving Onchain

Decentralized finance, better known as **DeFi**, is entering a new phase. Rather than positioning itself as an alternative to the traditional financial system, it is increasingly seen as **the foundation of a new financial infrastructure**, where the line between crypto and legacy finance begins to fade.

## What Does “DeFi Is Dead” Really Mean?

The claim that “DeFi is dead” does not signal the collapse of decentralized finance. Instead, it marks the end of a phase where DeFi was treated as a separate experiment. Increasingly, **onchain finance is converging with traditional financial markets**, much like the internet transformed — but did not destroy — commerce.

## Blockchain as the New Financial Infrastructure

Just as the internet became an invisible yet essential layer of everyday life, blockchain is taking on that role in finance. Transaction settlement, issuance of financial instruments, debt markets, and real-world asset tokenization are gradually shifting to public blockchains.

In this context, DeFi protocols are not competitors to banks, but rather a **technology layer** that enables faster, more transparent, and more efficient financial processes.

## From Speculation to Real-World Use

The focus is steadily moving away from pure speculation toward real utility: tokenized real-world assets, onchain capital markets, crypto-backed lending, and financial products designed to integrate with existing systems.

This shift suggests that the future of finance will not be “crypto versus traditional finance,” but rather a **hybrid system combining both worlds**.

## Regulation as a Key Enabler

For DeFi to reach its full potential, clear and functional regulation is essential. Without it, institutional adoption remains limited. Current trends, however, indicate that regulatory frameworks are gradually adapting, paving the way for broader use of onchain financial infrastructure.
