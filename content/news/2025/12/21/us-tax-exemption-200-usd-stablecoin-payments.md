# Tax exemption for small crypto payments in the US

US lawmakers have proposed a **major tax relief measure for everyday crypto users**, potentially paving the way for broader adoption of stablecoins as a payment method. Under the draft proposal, **stablecoin payments of up to $200 would be exempt from capital gains tax**.

## What exactly is being proposed?

The draft legislation would allow users **not to recognize gains or losses** when using stablecoins for everyday purchases — such as groceries, online services, or digital subscriptions — as long as each individual transaction **does not exceed $200**.

The exemption applies only to **regulated, US dollar–pegged stablecoins** that maintain a tight trading range around $1. If a stablecoin deviates significantly from its peg, the tax exemption would not apply.

## Why this matters for crypto payments

Until now, even small crypto payments in the US were technically treated as **taxable events**, making everyday crypto usage impractical.

This proposal marks a clear acknowledgment by lawmakers that **digital assets are increasingly used as a medium of exchange**, not just as speculative investments.

## What about staking and mining rewards?

Beyond payments, the proposal also addresses the long-standing issue of so-called **“phantom income.”** Taxpayers would be allowed to **defer taxation on staking and mining rewards for up to five years**, instead of being taxed immediately upon receipt — even if the assets are not sold.

## A step toward mass adoption

Although the proposal is still in draft form, it clearly signals the direction of US crypto regulation: **reduce friction, enable everyday crypto payments, and integrate stablecoins into the real economy**.

If enacted, this could become one of the most concrete regulatory steps toward mainstream crypto adoption in the United States.
