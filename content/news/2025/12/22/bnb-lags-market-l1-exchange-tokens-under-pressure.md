# BNB lags the market despite a break above $860

BNB rose by just over **1.7% in the past 24 hours**, moving higher alongside the broader crypto market. Over the same period, the **CoinDesk 20 (CD20) index** gained **2.2%**, highlighting that BNB **underperformed the market average** despite the overall rally.

## A technical breakout with limited follow-through

During the session, BNB **broke above the $860 resistance level**, which had capped recent rallies, and briefly touched **$868** before pulling back. The muted follow-through suggests traders remain cautious despite the technical breakout.

## Not just Binance: sector-wide underperformance

BNB’s relative weakness comes at a time when **many Layer-1 and exchange tokens are lagging the broader market**. Capital flows have remained concentrated in Bitcoin and a handful of dominant narratives, while infrastructure and exchange-related tokens have struggled to attract sustained demand.

That said, BNB continues to be closely tied to **sentiment around Binance**. A recent *Financial Times* investigation added pressure by alleging failures to prevent large volumes of suspicious crypto transactions, even after Binance agreed to a multibillion-dollar settlement with U.S. authorities.

## Surging volume and a new support zone

Despite softer price performance, **trading volume surged**, running about **192% above the daily average**, according to CoinDesk Research. Several notable volume spikes appeared as price approached key support levels.

This activity helped establish a **new support zone around $856–$858**, indicating that buyers are still willing to step in at those levels.

## Technical view: recovery without clear trend confirmation

On the hourly chart, BNB showed a **V-shaped recovery** after dipping to roughly **$851**, with buyers quickly pushing price back above **$863**. An **ascending triangle formed between $864 and $865.80**, a pattern often associated with potential continuation.

However, confirmation of a stronger uptrend likely requires **broader market participation** and a reduction in regulatory uncertainty.

## Still far from its all-time high

Even after the recent move, BNB remains **about 32% below its all-time high of $1,360**. By comparison, other exchange tokens such as **KCS** and **LEO** are trading much closer to their peak levels, pointing to relatively stronger performance within that subset.

More broadly, **Layer-1 tokens have underperformed throughout 2025**, despite regulatory progress and growing institutional interest—suggesting that positive fundamentals have yet to translate into consistent price appreciation across the sector.
