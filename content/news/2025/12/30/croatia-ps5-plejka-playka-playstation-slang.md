# Croatians Invented a New Word for PS5: PLEJKA (read: playka)

In Croatia, PlayStation 5 is not just a gaming console.  
It is — **PLEJKA**.

Kids, teenagers and adults alike rarely call the PS5 by its official name in everyday speech. Instead, the invitation sounds simple and instantly understood:  
**“Shall we go play Plejka?”**

That sentence no longer means just video games. It means hanging out, spending time together, competition, relaxation and a break from everyday life.

## PLEJKA is not a brand — it’s a relationship

In an interview with Croatian media, **Raffaele Zeppieri**, one of the executives responsible for PlayStation’s strategy in Europe, said this detail genuinely impressed him.

While in most countries the console is referred to simply as “PS5” or “PlayStation,” in Croatia it gained its own name. Not officially. Not through marketing. But organically.

Words like this are not created by campaigns — they appear only when technology becomes part of home culture.

## How PS5 became PLEJKA

The word *plejka* evolved naturally and phonetically from *PlayStation*.  
But its power lies not in language mechanics — it lies in meaning.

- “I have a Plejka at home.”
- “Come over, let’s play Plejka.”
- “The kids spent the whole afternoon on the Plejka.”

PLEJKA is a noun, a place, an event and an experience — all wrapped into one word.

## Kids and adults speak the same language

What makes PLEJKA particularly interesting is that it’s used equally across generations.  
Children use it instinctively, adults with a touch of nostalgia — but everyone understands it the same way.

This shows how deeply the PS5 has crossed the line from being a “device” to becoming part of the household space — like a TV or a couch.

## Why this matters beyond Croatia

For major technology companies, local slang like this is not trivial.  
It is a signal of deep adoption.

When users stop calling a product by its official name and give it their own — it means the product has truly become theirs.

In that sense, PLEJKA may be the greatest compliment PlayStation 5 could receive.
