## Strategy doubles down on Bitcoin

Strategy, formerly known as MicroStrategy, has once again expanded its Bitcoin position, purchasing **1,229 BTC** for approximately **$109 million**. The acquisition further cements the company’s long-standing commitment to Bitcoin as a core balance-sheet asset.

The average purchase price for this latest batch was around **$88,568 per Bitcoin**, signaling continued confidence despite ongoing market volatility.

## The largest publicly traded Bitcoin holder

Following the transaction, Strategy now holds a total of **672,497 Bitcoin**, acquired for roughly **$50.44 billion** in aggregate. The company’s average purchase price across its entire BTC portfolio stands at approximately **$74,997 per coin**.

This keeps Strategy firmly at the top of the list of publicly traded companies with Bitcoin exposure, far ahead of other corporate adopters.

## Funded through equity markets

The purchase was financed through the sale of **Class A common stock**, a familiar strategy for the company. By tapping traditional capital markets, Strategy continues to convert equity into long-term Bitcoin exposure.

## A clear institutional signal

The renewed buying comes at a time when many investors remain cautious due to macroeconomic uncertainty and year-end positioning. Strategy’s actions send a clear signal: Bitcoin is viewed not as a short-term trade, but as a **strategic reserve asset**.

For institutional observers, the move reinforces Bitcoin’s evolving role as a treasury asset rather than a purely speculative instrument.

## Looking ahead to 2026

While some companies have slowed or paused their crypto strategies, Strategy remains consistent. Its aggressive accumulation policy suggests that Bitcoin will continue to play a central role in the company’s long-term vision heading into 2026 and beyond.
