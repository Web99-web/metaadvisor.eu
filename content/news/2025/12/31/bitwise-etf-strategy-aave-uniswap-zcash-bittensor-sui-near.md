# Bitwise ETF Strategy: AAVE, UNI, ZEC, TAO, SUI and NEAR Move Into Institutional Focus

As 2025 comes to a close, the crypto market is sending a clear signal: institutional attention is no longer limited to Bitcoin and Ethereum. US asset manager **Bitwise** has filed for approval of **strategy ETFs** targeting **six specific altcoins** — Aave, Uniswap, Zcash, Bittensor, Sui and Near.

This move gives institutional investors a regulated and structured pathway into DeFi protocols, privacy technologies, AI-linked blockchain projects and next-generation layer-1 infrastructure — segments that have so far been dominated by crypto-native capital.

## Why these six tokens?

The selection reflects deliberate technological and strategic choices. Each token represents a distinct pillar of the crypto ecosystem.

### AAVE (AAVE)  
Aave is one of the most established DeFi protocols, focused on decentralized lending and liquidity markets. Its long operating history, deep liquidity and real-world usage make Aave attractive to institutions seeking DeFi exposure through mature infrastructure. Increasingly, Aave is viewed as a foundational layer of decentralized finance.

### Uniswap (UNI)  
Uniswap is synonymous with decentralized exchanges and remains a core component of on-chain trading. By enabling token swaps without centralized intermediaries, Uniswap has become the benchmark for DeFi liquidity. Within Bitwise’s ETF strategy, UNI represents institutional access to decentralized market infrastructure rather than centralized venues.

### Zcash (ZEC)  
Zcash stands out for its long-standing focus on privacy, using zero-knowledge cryptography to enable shielded transactions. As regulatory scrutiny and digital surveillance intensify, privacy has re-emerged as a strategic narrative. Including ZEC suggests institutions are once again considering privacy-focused assets as a legitimate portfolio component.

### Bittensor (TAO)  
Bittensor operates at the intersection of blockchain and artificial intelligence. Its network aims to decentralize the creation, evaluation and monetization of AI models. TAO is less a traditional crypto asset and more a bet on decentralized AI infrastructure — a theme increasingly relevant to institutional investors.

### Sui (SUI)  
Sui represents a new generation of layer-1 blockchains designed for high throughput, low latency and scalability. Built with performance and user experience in mind, Sui targets applications that demand speed and reliability. Its inclusion highlights Bitwise’s focus on future-ready blockchain infrastructure.

### Near (NEAR)  
Near Protocol positions itself as a developer-friendly blockchain emphasizing usability and scalability. Its ambition is mass adoption beyond crypto-native users. For institutions, NEAR offers a balance between technical maturity and long-term growth potential.

## What does a “strategy ETF” mean?

Unlike traditional spot ETFs, Bitwise’s products are designed with an active structure. The strategy ETFs may:
- hold up to **60% of assets directly in the underlying token**
- allocate at least **40% to exchange-traded products or related securities**
- use **derivatives** when needed to fine-tune exposure and risk

This approach aligns more closely with conventional institutional investment strategies than with passive buy-and-hold models.

## Why this matters for the market

Many institutional investors are unable to hold tokens directly due to regulatory or operational constraints. ETF structures remove these barriers, providing crypto exposure without wallet management or technical risks. If approved, these products could make **2026 a turning point for institutional adoption of altcoins**.
