# AI on TikTok: Anti-Immigrant Content and Deepfake Videos Are Reaching Billions

## TikTok Has Become a Global Engine of AI-Driven Manipulation

TikTok is no longer just a short-video app — it is one of the world’s most powerful opinion-shaping platforms.  
But new research shows it’s also drowning in a tsunami of AI-generated videos that look real, feel emotional, and spread at lightning speed.

A new investigation by AI Forensics reveals the scale:

- **354 AI-focused accounts**  
- **43,000 AI-generated posts**  
- **4.5 billion views in a single month**

The numbers are staggering — and alarming.

---

## What Researchers Found: Automated Accounts Flooding the Algorithm

Some accounts posted up to **70 AI videos per day**, a clear sign of automated pipelines designed to exploit TikTok’s algorithm.

Even more worrying:  
Although TikTok promises to label AI content, **less than 2%** of reviewed videos had any AI disclosure at all.

Most users have no idea they are watching synthetic media.

---

## How AI Creates Emotion-Optimized Manipulation

Generative AI models don’t need to be politically aware — they optimize for engagement.  
That means maximizing:

- anger  
- fear  
- shock  
- outrage  
- conflict  

TikTok rewards emotional spikes with reach — and AI has learned to weaponize that feedback loop.

---

## Why Anti-Immigrant Narratives Are Exploding

Anti-immigrant themes perform exceptionally well on TikTok, and AI amplifies them even further.

Why?

- high emotional volatility  
- simple visuals  
- easy to fabricate “eyewitness” footage  
- political sensitivity  
- algorithmic preference for polarizing content  

Many of the most viral videos depicting migrants are **entirely fabricated**.

---

## A Moderation Crisis: TikTok Confirms 1.3 Billion AI Videos

Researchers highlight a moderation system overwhelmed by scale:

- bot networks operating for months undetected  
- banned accounts reappearing instantly  
- reuploads faster than removals  
- algorithmic detection years behind generation quality  

TikTok has admitted that the platform already hosts more than **1.3 billion AI-generated videos**.

The real number is likely higher.

---

## Who Is Behind These Videos? No One Knows.

This is where the threat becomes systemic.

The producers could be:

- political actors,  
- foreign influence campaigns,  
- commercial bot farms,  
- ideological groups,  
- or lone operators running automated AI rigs.

The anonymity and low cost of AI production create perfect conditions for mass manipulation.

---

## Why This Is a Democratic Threat

This goes far beyond misinformation — it destabilizes public discourse.

AI manipulation is:

- global  
- instant  
- emotional  
- highly scalable  
- nearly undetectable  

Millions of young users form opinions based on synthetic, emotionally charged content.

We are entering an era where “video proof” is no longer proof.

---

## Conclusion: Visual Uncertainty Is the New Normal

The line between reality and simulation is collapsing.  
AI will define how information is created, distributed, and believed.

The challenge ahead is not merely technological — it is societal.
