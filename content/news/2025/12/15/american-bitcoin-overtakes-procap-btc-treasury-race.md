# American Bitcoin overtakes ProCap: The corporate BTC treasury race accelerates

Despite months of Bitcoin price volatility and persistent selling pressure, corporate demand for BTC continues to grow.  
Fresh data from BitcoinTreasuries.NET shows that **American Bitcoin** — a company connected to Eric Trump — has now surpassed **ProCap Financial** in total Bitcoin holdings.

American Bitcoin added more than **1,000 BTC** in December alone, bringing its reserves to:

### ✔ **5,044 BTC (≈ $443 million)**  

ProCap, founded by Anthony Pompliano, holds around **5,000 BTC**, pushing it down to 22nd place among corporate BTC holders.

---

## How did American Bitcoin emerge?

American Bitcoin was formed through a reverse merger with public Bitcoin miner Gryphon Digital Mining.  
After rebranding, the company was officially introduced by Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump — and has quickly become one of the most aggressive corporate BTC buyers in the U.S.

---

## ProCap: Pompliano’s vision under competitive pressure

ProCap Financial went public following a $750 million SPAC deal, with a mandate to offer broad exposure to Bitcoin and digital assets.  
Although ProCap continues to accumulate BTC, it has now been overtaken by American Bitcoin’s much faster pace of acquisition.

---

## Bigger picture: 2025 as Bitcoin’s “IPO moment”

Bitwise CIO Matt Hougan described 2025 as:

### **“Bitcoin’s IPO moment — a year where corporations adopt BTC at scale.”**

The broader trend is unmistakable:

- More companies are using Bitcoin as part of their treasury strategy  
- BTC is increasingly viewed as a long-term store of value  
- Market dips are seen as accumulation opportunities  
- Institutional adoption continues despite volatility  

American Bitcoin and ProCap illustrate the shift toward **BTC as a strategic corporate reserve asset**.
