BlackRock, the world’s largest asset manager, is accelerating its crypto ambitions.  
The firm has opened **seven new senior digital asset positions** — six in the United States and one in Singapore — marking one of its most significant hiring waves in the sector to date.

## Expanding the ETF and digital asset ecosystem
One of the U.S. roles focuses on expanding the **iShares digital asset ETF lineup**, including support for the massively successful **iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT)**, now one of the largest crypto investment products in the world.

The role includes:

- scaling existing crypto ETFs  
- extending access to institutional and wealth-management clients  
- developing “next-generation products with strong commercial appeal,”  
  which signals an ambition to move deeper into tokenized and on-chain assets.

## Asia becomes a strategic priority
The Singapore-based role has an even broader mandate:  
to develop and oversee BlackRock’s **digital asset strategy across the entire Asia region**, including a multi-year business roadmap.

The role includes identifying:

- early-stage institutional opportunities  
- tokenization pilots  
- regulatory-aligned product expansion in Singapore, Hong Kong, Japan and beyond  

Asia has become one of the fastest-growing hubs for regulated crypto adoption — and BlackRock clearly wants an early lead.

## Why BlackRock is scaling up now
Over the past two years, BlackRock has:

- launched its spot Bitcoin ETF with record inflows  
- publicly promoted **asset tokenization** as the future of capital markets  
- seen growing demand from pension funds, banks, RIAs and institutional allocators  

The seven new senior hires show that BlackRock sees digital assets not as an experiment —  
but as a **core pillar of future financial infrastructure**.
