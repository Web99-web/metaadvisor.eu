# Are Apps Ruining Your Focus at Work?

If you feel like the day ends without real progress, the problem is often not a lack of time — but **constant interruptions**. Notifications, social media, short videos, and endless scrolling have become the biggest enemies of deep work.

That’s why app blockers are evolving from simple productivity tools into **instruments for mental clarity**.

## Why Is Focus So Hard Today?

Smartphones and apps are designed to capture attention. Every notification breaks your train of thought and can take several minutes to recover. When this happens dozens of times a day, productivity drops and mental fatigue increases.

Blocking distractions is not weakness — it’s a **deliberate choice to protect your attention**.

## Freedom: Peace Across All Devices

Freedom allows you to block websites and apps **across all your devices at the same time** — laptop, tablet, and smartphone.

If Instagram is blocked on your computer, it’s also blocked on your phone. This closes the usual escape routes that distractions use to sneak back in.

Key features:
- scheduled focus sessions  
- recurring daily routines  
- full internet blocking  
- “Locked Mode” that prevents early exits  

Freedom is ideal for people with structured workdays who don’t want focus to depend on willpower alone.

## Cold Turkey: No Negotiations With Yourself

Cold Turkey takes a harder approach. The philosophy is simple:  
Once you commit to focus, **there is no way out until the timer ends**.

After starting a block:
- it cannot be stopped  
- there is no “just one quick check”  
- no exceptions  

This tool is often used by people struggling with procrastination or social media addiction. Harsh — but extremely effective.

## Focus Is Not a Productivity Hack — It’s Mental Space

More users are adopting these tools not only for work, but also for:
- quiet evenings without notifications  
- family time  
- reading and learning  
- intentional rest without screen overload  

In a world where attention is constantly monetized, these tools do the opposite: **they give control back to the user**.
