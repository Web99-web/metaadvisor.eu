Bitcoin has fallen against gold to levels analysts describe as a **classic long-term bottom zone**. The BTC/XAU ratio has dropped to around **20 ounces of gold per bitcoin**, marking its lowest levels since early 2024.

Unlike tracking Bitcoin’s price in U.S. dollars, comparing BTC to gold is often seen as a **real, long-term measure of market strength**, particularly during periods of inflation, monetary tightening, and declining risk appetite.

## What does it mean that Bitcoin is “oversold” versus gold?

The key signal comes from the **Relative Strength Index (RSI)**. When RSI drops below 30, a market is considered **oversold**, meaning selling pressure has been unusually intense over a short period.

Historically, in every major Bitcoin cycle:
- A BTC/XAU RSI below 30  
- has coincided with **long-term lows for Bitcoin relative to gold**

The current **weekly RSI sits around 29.5**, levels not seen in nearly three years.

Importantly:  
**Oversold does not mean price must immediately rise**, but it does indicate the market has entered a zone where **further downside becomes statistically less likely**, while the probability of stabilization or recovery increases.

## Bitcoin and gold: two very different roles

Gold is behaving as a **classic safe-haven asset**, trading near all-time highs as risk assets cool off. Bitcoin, by contrast, is undergoing a correction following strong gains in previous years.

Such divergences often create extremes:
- Gold appears **relatively overvalued**
- Bitcoin appears **relatively undervalued**

Over time, one of these assets typically adjusts — either gold loses momentum, or Bitcoin regains strength relative to gold.

## Could this signal a cycle turning point?

Some analysts view the current BTC/XAU ratio as **an accumulation phase rather than the start of a prolonged bear market**. Historical patterns suggest similar levels in the past often preceded multi-year periods in which Bitcoin significantly outperformed gold.

In the short term, however:
- Macro pressures remain  
- Markets can stay oversold longer than expected  

As a result, this signal is best interpreted as a **long-term indicator**, not a call for aggressive short-term trading.

## Conclusion

Bitcoin is currently trading against gold in a rarely seen oversold zone. Historically, such extremes have tended to emerge during periods of deeply negative sentiment but have often marked **important turning points in the market cycle**.

Whether history repeats will depend on macroeconomic conditions, monetary policy, and a return of risk appetite. Still, the message from the BTC/XAU ratio is difficult to ignore.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor)

- Bitcoin versus gold is one of the most reliable long-term cycle indicators.  
- RSI below 30 rarely persists for extended periods.  
- Extreme relative valuations often precede trend reversals.  
- This is a signal for **observation and strategic thinking**, not impulsive action.
