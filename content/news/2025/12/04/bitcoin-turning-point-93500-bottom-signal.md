## Bitcoin rejected at $93.5K as conflicting macro signals shake the market

On December 4, Bitcoin was rejected once again at the critical $93,500 resistance level, sliding back toward $90,000 despite unexpectedly strong US labor-market data.  
Both initial and continuing jobless claims came in below forecasts, signaling renewed economic strength.

Yet markets still expect the Federal Reserve to cut interest rates on December 10.  
According to analysts, the growing disconnect between consumer pressure and risk-asset performance is forcing the Fed into a corner.

As The Kobeissi Letter noted:  
“The Fed has no option. Even with 3% inflation, it MUST cut rates to ‘save’ US consumers.”

## Technical structure still defines the trend

Bitcoin must reclaim several resistance levels to shift the market back into a clear bullish structure.

### Key resistance zones:
- 2025 yearly open  
- $93.5K–$95K area  
- major EMA/SMA trend levels  

Until Bitcoin breaks and holds above 95K, the market remains short-term bearish.

## Negative Nasdaq correlation — historically a bottom signal

The most important indicator today:  
Bitcoin’s correlation with the Nasdaq 100 has turned **negative** again (currently –0.43).

Historically, negative correlation between BTC and Nasdaq frequently appears **near major Bitcoin bottoms**.

### Historical examples:
- **Summer 2021** — BTC drops to 30K, then surges to new ATH  
- **September 2023** — BTC below 30K, then rallies to 40K by year-end  
- **August 2024** — carry-trade unwind pushes BTC to 49K, marking a local bottom  

Today the Nasdaq is only 2% below its all-time high.  
Bitcoin, meanwhile, is still **27% below** its peak — a setup suggesting potential upside if the pattern holds.

## Conclusion: A decisive move is approaching

A confirmed breakout and sustained hold above $93.5K–$95K would strengthen the argument that Bitcoin has formed a bottom.  
If not, volatility will continue to dominate the market.
