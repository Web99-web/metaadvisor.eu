# Solmate to Acquire RockawayX: Abu Dhabi + Prague Form a New Solana Infrastructure Giant

Solmate, an Abu Dhabi–based company focused on Solana infrastructure and treasury management, has signed a **term sheet to acquire RockawayX** — one of the most prominent and active crypto firms from the **Czech Republic**.

The deal would transform Solmate from a pure treasury company into an **integrated platform offering infrastructure, liquidity, and asset-management services**.

---

## What RockawayX Brings to the Merger

RockawayX has been a leading Central European crypto firm since 2018, operating:

- validator infrastructure  
- on-chain liquidity services  
- two venture funds  
- one credit fund  
- over **$1 billion** in invested and staked assets  

Its infrastructure deeply supports the Solana ecosystem, enabling institutional staking, market infrastructure and early-stage ecosystem development.

---

## What Solmate Is Today — and What It Would Become

Solmate, originally Brera Holdings PLC, transitioned into a **Solana-focused treasury platform**.  
After absorbing RockawayX, the new combined entity would:

- manage **over $2 billion AUM**  
- unite infrastructure, staking, venture capital, market liquidity, and asset management  
- deliver institutional Solana services directly from **Abu Dhabi**  
- support latency-sensitive orderflow services for HFT traders and exchanges  

The companies already collaborated in November by launching new Solana validator infrastructure in the **UAE**.

---

## Why Abu Dhabi?

Abu Dhabi is positioning itself as a **global digital-assets hub**, offering regulatory clarity, government backing, and major investment in Web3 infrastructure.

This gives the combined platform:

- access to institutional capital  
- regulatory stability  
- proximity to global HFT centres  
- an ideal environment for high-performance blockchain infrastructure  

---

## Market Reaction

Solmate’s token **SLMT rose over 6%** in early trading after the acquisition news broke.

---

## Our Take (MetaAdvisor)

This merger is a strategic move that:

- **globalizes Solana infrastructure**,  
- gives RockawayX a **path to international expansion**,  
- and strengthens Abu Dhabi’s role as a Web3 epicenter.

If completed, this deal will form one of the **largest Solana infrastructure platforms worldwide**, rooted in both Central Europe and the Middle East.
