# India becomes crypto powerhouse: 118.6 million crypto users and rising

India today has approximately **1.46 billion inhabitants**, and a staggering **118.6 million** of them actively use cryptocurrencies —  
which means **every twelfth person** in the country owns or interacts with crypto assets.  
This makes India the largest crypto market in the world by user base, surpassing the United States and Europe together.  
While in many Western countries only 3–5% of the population uses crypto, in India it’s already over **8%**, and the figure keeps growing rapidly.

## How India evolved into a global crypto powerhouse

The rise happened in three major waves:

### **1) First wave (2017–2018)**
- massive Bitcoin boom  
- first major Indian exchanges achieved hundreds of thousands to millions of users  
- younger generations entered the market in droves  

### **2) Pandemic wave (2020–2021)**
- digitalization accelerated during lockdowns  
- mobile banking and investment apps surged  
- crypto became a popular form of diversification and alternative investment  

### **3) Growth despite strict regulation (2022–2024)**
India introduced heavy taxes — 30% on profits and 1% TDS (tax deducted at source) on each transaction. However:
- interest did not wane  
- user base kept growing  
- growth came especially from smaller cities and rural regions  

India’s advanced digital infrastructure, high mobile penetration and youthful population turned crypto into a mainstream phenomenon — not just a niche interest.

## Which exchanges are dominating among Indian crypto users?

The Indian crypto ecosystem is split between centralized exchanges (CEX) and decentralized exchanges (DEX).

### **Domestic CEX platforms**

- **Bitbns** — at times controlling over **70% of local trading volume**  
- **CoinDCX** — popular especially in smaller towns; strong focus on user education  
- **CoinSwitch Kuber** — mobile-first, widely used by younger investors  
- **WazirX** — once the largest Indian exchange; its reputation plummeted after a major hack in 2024.  
  The full scandal and subsequent court ruling are detailed in **this background article on WazirX and the Madras High Court decision**(https://metaadvisor.eu/news/india-madras-high-court-crypto-property-wazirx-xrp/)

### **Global exchanges**

- **Binance, KuCoin, OKX, Bybit** — popular among experienced traders due to deep liquidity and wide derivative offerings  
- **Coinbase** — after a bumpy first entry in 2022 and exit in 2023, Coinbase is now returning:  
  - App registrations reopened  
  - Crypto-to-crypto trading available immediately  
  - Fiat on-ramp (INR) scheduled for 2026  
  - Company actively working on improving relationships with Indian regulators

## 🔥 The rise of DEX — lower fees, privacy, full user control

Decentralized exchanges (DEX) are gaining massive popularity in India. Because they:

- don’t require KYC  
- operate on **non-custodial models**  
- offer **lower fees** than many CEX platforms  
- provide **stronger privacy and anonymity**  
- often support **perp trading and leverage** with favorable conditions  

they are especially attractive to seasoned traders and crypto-savvy users.  
Because DEX platforms do not store user location or identity data, it’s impossible to precisely know how many Indian users trade on them — but activity levels strongly suggest a **large and growing user base among Indian crypto traders**.

### Key DEX platforms popular among Indian users

- **Uniswap** — the biggest global DEX  
- **1inch** — aggregator delivering optimal pricing  
- **PancakeSwap** — heavily used in Asia due to low fees and fast transactions  
- **GMX** — pioneering decentralized perpetual futures exchange  
- **dYdX** — high liquidity and professional-grade interface  

## ⭐ Hyperliquid — the rising star among perpetual DEXs

**Hyperliquid** has quickly emerged as one of the largest decentralized trading venues worldwide:

- extremely low slippage  
- very competitive trading fees  
- tremendous volume growth since 2024  
- particularly popular among Indian professional traders  

For many experienced Indian users, Hyperliquid is already a **genuine alternative to centralized futures exchanges like Binance Futures — but fully decentralized**.
