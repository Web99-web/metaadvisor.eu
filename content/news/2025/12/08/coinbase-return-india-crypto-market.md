# Coinbase returns to India: A reboot on the world’s largest crypto market

Global crypto exchange **Coinbase** is officially returning to India — the world’s largest and fastest-growing crypto market by number of users.  
This marks the company’s first step back after withdrawing completely from the country in 2023.

## <span style="color:#1e40af;">India becomes a crypto powerhouse — 118.6 million users</span>

Read our full in-depth analysis here:  
**[India — Crypto powerhouse with 118.6 million users](https://metaadvisor.eu/news/india-crypto-powerhouse-118-6-million-users/)**

---

## What does Coinbase’s return mean?

Coinbase has re-enabled **app registrations** for Indian users, giving them immediate access to crypto-to-crypto markets.  
A **full INR fiat on-ramp** is planned for **2026**, signaling a long-term regulatory commitment.

According to **John O’Loghlen**, Coinbase’s APAC director:

> “We had millions of users in India, but we decided to fully off-board them so we could rebuild with a clean regulatory foundation.”

Coinbase’s initial attempt to enter India in 2022 failed due to banking restrictions and regulatory pressure, forcing the company to pause operations in 2023.

---

## What changes in 2025 and 2026?

### ✔️ Registrations open again  
Indian users can once again sign up and trade crypto-to-crypto immediately.

### ✔️ INR on-ramp coming in 2026  
This will finally allow seamless crypto purchases in Indian Rupees.

### ✔️ A new regulatory-first approach  
Coinbase is now:

- participating in Indian parliamentary finance committees  
- strengthening relationships with regulators  
- expanding its India-focused talent base  
- increasing brand presence (Platinum Sponsor at India Blockchain Week)

It is Coinbase’s **first truly compliant entry strategy** for the Indian market.

---

## Why now? Why India?

Because India has become **the most strategically important retail crypto market on Earth**.

Key reasons:

- **118.6 million active crypto users**  
- unmatched mobile-first adoption  
- a young, tech-native population  
- massive growth of DEX, perpetuals and non-custodial finance  
- growing interest from global institutions  

With **1 in 12 Indian citizens using crypto**, Coinbase is re-entering at a crucial moment.
