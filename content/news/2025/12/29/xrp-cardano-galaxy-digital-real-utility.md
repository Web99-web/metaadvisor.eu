# XRP and ADA Enter a Real-Utility Test Phase

The crypto market is entering a more mature phase in which **narratives and loyal communities alone are no longer sufficient** to justify long-term valuations. According to Galaxy Digital founder and CEO Mike Novogratz, this shift is now clearly visible in **XRP and Cardano (ADA)**.

Both projects maintain strong followings, but the market in 2026 is increasingly focused on **verifiable usage, sustainable revenue and consistent on-chain activity**.

## What Galaxy Digital Is Really Saying

Novogratz does not dismiss the historical relevance or technical ambitions of XRP and Cardano. Instead, he highlights how market valuation frameworks are changing.

> Tokens that are not “money” like Bitcoin are increasingly valued like traditional businesses — based on usage, revenue and tangible economic value.

Community enthusiasm alone no longer supports price.

## XRP: Institutional Narrative, Limited Organic Activity

XRP has long been positioned as a bridge asset for **fast, low-cost cross-border payments**, particularly through RippleNet and partnerships with banks and fintech firms. Regulatory uncertainty in the U.S. weighed heavily for years, but 2025 delivered greater clarity.

Still, structural challenges remain:

- relatively low on-chain activity  
- active address counts that lag market capitalization  
- heavy reliance on expectations rather than measurable usage  

With a market capitalization exceeding **$100 billion**, XRP is now firmly under institutional scrutiny.

## Cardano: Strong Community, Slow Real-World Adoption

Cardano is widely known for its academic approach, formal verification methods and long-term development philosophy. Charles Hoskinson has built one of the most loyal communities in crypto.

From Galaxy Digital’s perspective, however, the issues are clear:

- modest levels of user activity  
- a comparatively weak DeFi and application ecosystem  
- slow translation of technical vision into market demand  

ADA’s long-term positioning cannot rely on design quality alone.

## The 2026 Market Demands Metrics, Not Stories

Galaxy Digital’s assessment aligns with broader market trends:

- institutional capital prioritizes measurable indicators  
- regulators demand transparency and accountability  
- investors seek sustainable economic models  

Projects without demonstrable real-world utility risk losing relevance, regardless of brand recognition.

## What This Means for Investors

This is not a blanket bearish call on XRP or ADA, but rather a **warning signal**. To maintain their status among leading cryptocurrencies, both projects must demonstrate:

- increasing real-world usage  
- growing on-chain activity  
- clear and defensible economic models  

Failure to do so may relegate them to legacy-cycle narratives.

## Conclusion

XRP and Cardano are approaching a decisive moment. As crypto markets mature, **storytelling gives way to financial reality**, and 2026 may determine which projects can prove genuine economic relevance beyond loyal fan bases.
