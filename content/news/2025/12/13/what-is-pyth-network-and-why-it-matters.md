Pyth Network is a blockchain oracle that enables smart contracts to access real-world market data in near real time. It acts as critical infrastructure connecting blockchains with off-chain information such as cryptocurrency prices, equities, foreign exchange pairs and commodities — without which DeFi applications could not function reliably.

Unlike traditional oracle solutions, Pyth relies on so-called *first-party* data sources. This means data is not relayed through intermediaries, but sourced directly from major exchanges, market makers and professional trading firms. This structure allows for faster price updates and reduced discrepancies, which is especially important for derivatives, perpetual futures and other price-sensitive financial products.

Originally developed within the Solana ecosystem, Pyth has since expanded to Ethereum and multiple other blockchain networks. Today, it is used by a growing number of DeFi protocols that depend on accurate and timely pricing to maintain system stability and protect users.

At the core of the ecosystem is the PYTH token, which plays a role in protocol governance through a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) and forms part of the network’s broader economic design. Recently, Pyth announced that a portion of protocol revenue will be used to repurchase PYTH tokens on the open market and build a reserve, signaling a longer-term strategic approach.

Despite its technological importance, the PYTH token has seen a significant price decline over the past year. This has intensified discussions about the gap between real-world usage of the network and the market valuation of its token.

Pyth Network is often described as “the Bloomberg terminal for blockchain” — a decentralized, on-chain source of market data aiming to become foundational infrastructure for the next generation of financial applications.
