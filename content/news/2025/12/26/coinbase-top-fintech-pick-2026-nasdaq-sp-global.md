# Coinbase enters the league of core financial infrastructure

Coinbase is increasingly being viewed through a very different lens than just a few years ago. Once seen primarily as a crypto exchange dependent on trading volumes, Wall Street analysts now describe it as a **strategic financial infrastructure platform** with long-term relevance.

That shift is why Coinbase is listed among the **top three fintech picks for 2026**, alongside established institutions such as Nasdaq and S&P Global.

## Why Coinbase is no longer just a crypto exchange

The change is driven by Coinbase’s evolving revenue structure. The company is diversifying away from volatile spot trading and expanding into subscriptions, custody services, on-chain financial products, and stablecoins.

A key component is USDC, the dollar-backed stablecoin increasingly used for payments, transfers, and decentralized finance. Revenue tied to USDC and infrastructure services is less dependent on price cycles and more closely linked to real-world adoption.

In effect, Coinbase is positioning itself as a **financial operating layer**, not merely a trading venue.

## Why the comparison with Nasdaq and S&P Global matters

Nasdaq and S&P Global do not profit from market direction, but from providing the infrastructure, data, and standards that markets rely on to function.

In this context, Coinbase is increasingly viewed as a **blockchain-native equivalent** — a foundational layer for digital assets, stablecoins, and tokenized financial instruments.

## Why 2026 could be a turning point

Analysts believe that 2026 may mark a shift in how crypto-related equities are valued, moving away from hype-driven metrics toward adoption, usage, and structural importance.

If regulatory clarity improves and stablecoins and tokenization continue to expand, Coinbase’s global reach and balance sheet could position it as one of the primary beneficiaries.

- Stablecoin and service-based revenues help smooth crypto market cycles  
- Being ranked alongside Nasdaq and S&P Global signals a major perception shift  
- 2026 may mark the transition from speculation to fundamentals in crypto equities
