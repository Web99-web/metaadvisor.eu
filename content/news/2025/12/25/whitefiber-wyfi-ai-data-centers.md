# WhiteFiber (WYFI): a new AI infrastructure story from Wall Street

**WhiteFiber (WYFI)** is a relatively young US company positioning itself at the core of the **AI revolution** — not through software or consumer applications, but through the foundation that AI depends on: **data centers, power, and physical infrastructure**.

This focus has quickly put WhiteFiber on the radar of investors and Wall Street analysts, as it offers direct exposure to AI growth through **real, tangible assets** rather than abstract technology promises.

## How WhiteFiber was formed

WhiteFiber was created as a **spin-off from Bit Digital**, a company known for its presence in the crypto and infrastructure space. The separation was designed to create a **pure-play AI infrastructure company**, free from the volatility and perception issues often associated with crypto-focused businesses.

The company is led by **Sam Tabar**, founder and CEO, who brings experience across finance, technology, and infrastructure. Under his leadership, WhiteFiber is fully focused on building and operating **AI-optimized data centers**.

Today, WhiteFiber is **publicly listed** and trades under the ticker **WYFI**, making it accessible to both institutional and retail investors.

## What WhiteFiber actually does

WhiteFiber develops and operates **data centers specifically designed for AI and HPC (high-performance computing) workloads**. Its business model is built around two core pillars:

### 1. AI-optimized data centers  
These facilities are designed to support:
- large-scale GPU clusters  
- high power density  
- advanced cooling and operational stability  

### 2. Long-term colocation contracts  
Instead of short-term cloud subscriptions, WhiteFiber focuses on **multi-year colocation agreements** with enterprise clients that require stable, dedicated AI infrastructure.

This approach positions WhiteFiber as an **infrastructure provider**, not a traditional software or hyperscale cloud company.

## The NC-1 campus and why it matters

WhiteFiber’s flagship project is the **NC-1 data center campus** in North Carolina. At this site, the company secured a **10-year colocation agreement** with Nscale Global covering:

- 40 MW of capacity  
- long-term utilization  
- an estimated total contract value of around **$865 million**

For investors, this is a critical milestone, as it demonstrates WhiteFiber’s ability to:
- attract long-term enterprise clients  
- meet demanding infrastructure standards  
- generate predictable future revenue  

## How Wall Street views WYFI

Investment bank **B. Riley** reviewed WhiteFiber following the NC-1 announcement. Analysts **Nick Giles** and **Fedor Shabalin** reiterated a **“buy” rating**, while modestly adjusting the price target:

- previous target: **$44**  
- revised target: **$40**

Even after the adjustment, analysts emphasized that WYFI still offers **meaningful upside potential**, particularly if the company continues to secure long-term infrastructure contracts.

## Why WhiteFiber stands out for investors

WhiteFiber is attracting attention because it offers:
- direct exposure to AI growth  
- physical infrastructure rather than pure software narratives  
- long-term, contract-backed revenue visibility  
- a clear and focused strategic direction  

In a market crowded with AI hype, WhiteFiber is building the **real-world infrastructure** that AI systems actually run on.
