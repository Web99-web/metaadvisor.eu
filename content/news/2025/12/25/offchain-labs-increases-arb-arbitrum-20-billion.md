# Offchain Labs Increases ARB Holdings as Arbitrum Surpasses $20 Billion in Value

**Offchain Labs**, the development company behind the Ethereum layer-2 network **Arbitrum**, has increased its holdings of the **ARB** token, sending a clear signal of long-term confidence in the project at a time when **governance tokens remain under pressure** and competition among layer-2 solutions continues to intensify.

The move comes as Arbitrum reached a major milestone, surpassing **$20 billion in total value locked (TVL)** and reinforcing its position as one of the most important scaling infrastructures in the Ethereum ecosystem.

## Why is Offchain Labs increasing its ARB exposure now?

In a post on X, Offchain Labs said the additional ARB purchases are not a short-term market trade but part of an **approved long-term investment plan**. The company emphasized its commitment to growing the Arbitrum ecosystem “in a meaningful and sustainable way,” despite weaker sentiment toward governance tokens across the broader crypto market.

This decision stands out at a time when the industry is increasingly debating whether **core developers and early stakeholders are reducing exposure to their own governance tokens**. While some projects have seen insiders step back, Offchain Labs has chosen to double down on the ecosystem it is building.

## What is Arbitrum and how does it work?

**Arbitrum** is an **Ethereum layer-2 solution** designed to improve transaction speed and significantly reduce fees. It is built using **optimistic rollup technology**, which processes transactions off-chain and periodically settles them on Ethereum.

This approach preserves Ethereum’s security while enabling faster confirmations and lower costs — a critical advantage for **DeFi applications, decentralized exchanges, NFT platforms, and blockchain-based games**.

## What role does the ARB token play?

The **ARB** token does not represent a direct claim on protocol revenues. Instead, it functions primarily as a **governance token**. ARB holders can vote on:
- network upgrades  
- the allocation of funds from the Arbitrum treasury  
- long-term strategic and ecosystem initiatives  

As a result, ARB’s price does not always move in line with network usage or TVL growth, and is often more influenced by overall market sentiment.

## Why does the $20 billion TVL milestone matter?

Crossing the **$20 billion TVL** threshold shows that Arbitrum is not just technically advanced, but **actively used at scale**. Combined with Offchain Labs’ decision to increase its ARB holdings, the milestone sends a strong message that the project’s long-term value goes beyond short-term token price movements.

In a landscape where layer-2 networks compete for developers, liquidity, and users, **confidence from the builders themselves** often carries more weight than marketing campaigns or temporary market rallies.
