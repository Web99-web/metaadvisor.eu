France is taking another major step into the crypto ecosystem. **BPCE**, the country’s third-largest banking group, is officially rolling out crypto trading directly inside its mobile banking apps. The pilot phase will enable around **2 million customers** to buy and sell digital assets, with plans to expand the service to all **12 million clients** in 2026.

In the first rollout, customers of Banque Populaire and Caisse d’Épargne will be able to trade **Bitcoin (BTC), Ether (ETH), Solana (SOL)** and the stablecoin **USDC**. All trading operations run through a dedicated digital asset account managed by **Hexarq**, BPCE’s crypto-focused subsidiary.

The fee structure is straightforward:  
- **€2.99 per month** for the digital asset account  
- **1.5% transaction fee** (with a minimum of €1 per trade)

BPCE says the phased approach is designed to monitor adoption, security and system performance before scaling to its full user base.

This move positions France as one of the most progressive European markets in banking-integrated crypto services. While similar initiatives exist in Spain (BBVA) and Austria (Raiffeisen’s partnership with Bitpanda), BPCE’s scale is significantly larger.

The expansion signals a broader trend across Europe: banks are no longer ignoring crypto — they are beginning to distribute it.

## Our take (MetaAdvisor):

- BPCE becomes the most significant EU bank to offer crypto trading to mass-market users.  
- Competing banks across Europe will likely follow this model to remain competitive.  
- Fees are high, but they target users who prefer simplicity over lower trading costs.  
- The move may bring a new wave of traditional retail investors into the crypto space.  
- France continues to strengthen its position as a European crypto leader under MiCA.
