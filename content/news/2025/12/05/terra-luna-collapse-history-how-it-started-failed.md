# Terra-Luna: How one of the biggest blockchain projects in history collapsed — and why the world will never forget it

The Terra-Luna ecosystem was **one of the most ambitious and fastest-growing projects in the crypto industry**. In 2021 and early 2022, LUNA ranked among the **top 10 cryptocurrencies**, with a market cap exceeding **$40–50 billion**. UST, its algorithmic stablecoin, was hailed as one of the most innovative experiments in decentralized finance.

Many newcomers today have no idea how massive Terra-Luna once was: global hype, huge VC backing, millions of users, and the fastest-growing app ecosystem in Web3. For a moment, Terra looked like it might become **Asia’s new financial standard**, and founder Do Kwon became one of the most recognizable figures in crypto.

And then everything collapsed in just **72 hours**.

---

## 🌕 How did Terra-Luna begin?

Terra was launched in 2018 by **Do Kwon** and Daniel Shin under Terraform Labs.

It consisted of two main components:

### 1️⃣ LUNA – the investment and governance token  
### 2️⃣ UST – an algorithmic stablecoin

Why UST was unusual:

- USDT and USDC hold **real reserves**  
- UST was **algorithmic**, stabilized through mint/burn mechanisms of LUNA  

A clever yet extremely fragile design.

---

## 🌕 Why did Terra grow so quickly?

The explosive rise came from:

### ⭐ Anchor Protocol — 20% APY  
Anchor offered **20% interest** on UST deposits.  
This attracted billions — up to **17 billion UST**.

### ⭐ Massive venture-capital support  
Including:

- Pantera Capital  
- Coinbase Ventures  
- Galaxy Digital  

### ⭐ Favorable Asian regulatory climate  

### ⭐ Do Kwon’s charismatic but confrontational public persona  

---

## 🌕 Then came the biggest collapse in crypto history

In May 2022, UST began losing its 1:1 peg to the dollar.  
The algorithm failed — panic spread.

Within days:

- LUNA crashed **from $80 to $0.0001**  
- UST fell to **$0.10**  
- **Over $60 billion** in market value evaporated  
- Thousands lost life savings  
- The entire crypto market entered “Crypto Winter”  

U.S. prosecutors later described the event as:

> “One of the largest financial collapses outside of traditional markets.”

## 🌕 The Montenegro arrest: the worst possible hiding place

After the collapse in 2022, Do Kwon spent months fleeing across Europe and Asia using fake passports. His run ended on March 23, 2023, when he was arrested at Podgorica airport while attempting to fly to Dubai using falsified Costa Rican and Serbian documents.

Montenegrin authorities immediately detained him and sentenced him to four months for document fraud. His arrest sparked a race between the U.S. and South Korea, both demanding extradition over multibillion-dollar fraud charges.

Why Montenegro?

Because the former Yugoslav region is known for **extremely strict document control**, strong police structures, and a deeply rooted culture of bureaucracy. Fake passports rarely get far there — and certainly not through airport security.

In other words:  
**If you try to hide with forged documents in Montenegro, you lose. Instantly.**

---

## 🌕 And the most absurd part: how did he think this would work?

The irony only grows when considering that Do Kwon attempted to pass as a Costa Rican or Serbian citizen — in a region where locals immediately notice if someone doesn’t speak any local language.

Even more absurd was choosing Montenegro as the place to “blend in,” given its reputation for rigorous passport checks. A misplaced stamp, unusual hologram color, or tiny inconsistency is enough to trigger suspicion.

It’s as if Do Kwon had never heard of Balkan bureaucracy — legendary for catching even the smallest irregularities.

So while he imagined quietly boarding a flight to Dubai, reality had other plans: appearance, documents and location made his arrest almost guaranteed.

## 🌕 Why Terra-Luna still matters

For new crypto investors, Terra-Luna remains a critical case study:

1️⃣ Algorithmic stablecoins = high innovation, extreme fragility  
2️⃣ High APYs = systemic risk  
3️⃣ Centralized personalities with too much influence = ecosystem failure  

Terra permanently changed how the industry thinks about stablecoins, liquidity, and risk.
