# N3XT — the blockchain bank aiming to fix America’s slow payment infrastructure

N3XT is a brand-new bank built on blockchain rails, launched by former executives of Signature Bank, which was shut down in March 2023.  
Unlike traditional US banks, N3XT **does not lend out deposits**, does not invest with customer funds, and does not operate on a fractional-reserve model.

N3XT is a **full-reserve (“narrow”) bank — every dollar is backed 1:1 by cash or short-term US Treasuries.**

This means:

- no insolvency risk  
- no hidden credit exposure  
- no Silicon Valley Bank–style collapses  
- full transparency over customer assets  

## Why does N3XT exist?
Because the US banking system is still extremely slow.

Even when apps show “instant transfer,” **real settlement often takes 1–5 days**.

ACH, wires, and legacy clearing systems were never built for real-time payments.

The result:

- companies lose liquidity time,  
- fintechs must build expensive workarounds,  
- international payments remain clunky,  
- delays create financial and operational risks.  

Signature Bank had already proven massive demand for **instant enterprise payments**.  
N3XT aims to bring that capability back — only more modern and more secure.

# How N3XT works

## 1) Wyoming SPDI charter — one of the strictest regulatory frameworks in the US
This license requires:

- **100% reserves**  
- no lending  
- permission to run blockchain-based settlement  
- programmable payments via smart contracts  

## 2) Private blockchain for true real-time USD settlement
Payments do **not** run through ACH, SWIFT, or wire networks.  
They settle on a private blockchain.

This enables:

- real, final settlement in seconds  
- 24/7/365 payments  
- automated business logic  
- on-chain auditability  

## 3) Infrastructure for fintech and crypto companies
N3XT targets businesses that need:

- instant, programmable enterprise payments  
- tokenized USD flows  
- trusted on-chain settlement without stablecoins  
- banking infrastructure for exchanges, OTC desks, funds  

Backers include:

- **Paradigm**  
- **Winklevoss Capital**  
- **HACK VC**

It is an infrastructure project for the next generation of finance.

# Why would a business switch to N3XT?

Most European banks advertise “instant payments,” yet many simply send an **instant notification**, not instant settlement.

In the US, the situation is even worse:

- FedNOW exists, but adoption is low  
- Most US banks still do **not** support real-time settlement  

N3XT offers something traditional banking has failed to provide:

### ✔ true, final real-time settlement  
### ✔ programmable payment architecture  
### ✔ full-reserve safety  
### ✔ on-chain transparency  

For companies with large transaction flows, this is a structural advantage.
