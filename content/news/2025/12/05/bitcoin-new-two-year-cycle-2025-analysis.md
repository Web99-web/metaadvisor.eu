# Is Bitcoin abandoning its legendary 4-year cycle? Why 2025 may mark the start of a new 2-year market rhythm

For more than a decade, the **4-year Bitcoin cycle** was treated almost as sacred:

Halving → bull run → peak → crash → accumulation.

But in 2025, the market increasingly behaves as if those rules no longer apply.

In an interview with Cointelegraph, **Jeff Park**, partner and CIO at ProCap BTC, argues that Bitcoin might be transitioning into a **new two-year cycle** driven by forces that did not exist in earlier market phases:

- massive institutional inflows  
- ETF-driven demand  
- new liquidity patterns  
- decreasing influence of the halving  
- competition between Bitcoin and the booming AI sector  

If this framework is correct, investors will need to rethink everything they believe about timing, volatility, and long-term market expectations.

---

## ⭐ Why would Bitcoin’s cycle shorten?

Park highlights that **institutional investors operate very differently** from retail:

- they do not wait for the halving  
- they allocate based on liquidity conditions  
- their buying and selling behavior creates **faster micro-cycles**  

Spot ETFs, macro conditions and risk models generate a market rhythm that is **shorter, sharper, and more fragmented** than the traditional 4-year pattern.

Or in Park’s words:

> “Retail follows the halving.  
> Institutions follow liquidity.”

This shift changes the entire structure of the market.

---

## ⭐ Why the halving matters less than before

During Bitcoin’s early years, the halving was a dramatic supply shock.

Today:

- ETFs buy **more BTC per day than miners produce**  
- exchange reserves are declining  
- macro liquidity has a stronger influence than mining emissions  

The halving still matters — but it **no longer dictates the entire market cycle**.

---

## ⭐ What does a two-year cycle look like?

Park suggests the new structure may follow this pattern:

1️⃣ **Year 1:** strong inflows → aggressive upward trend  
2️⃣ **Year 2:** corrections, redistribution, increased volatility  
3️⃣ capital rotation → cycle restarts  

This would explain:

- shorter bull phases  
- faster and deeper corrections  
- more unpredictable volatility  
- earlier or shifted cycle peaks  

If true, traditional Bitcoin cycle models become obsolete.

---

## ⭐ AI vs. Bitcoin — a new competition for capital

For the first time ever, Bitcoin is competing directly with a new technological megatrend:

**Artificial intelligence.**

Massive flows of capital are being directed toward:

- AI infrastructure  
- AI model development  
- AI stocks  
- hardware manufacturers  

Institutional budgets are finite — and this competition affects Bitcoin’s relative strength.

2025 may be the first cycle where Bitcoin is **no longer the primary magnet for risk capital**.

---

## ⭐ What does this mean for 2026?

If the two-year cycle becomes reality:

- the next major Bitcoin peak may arrive **earlier than the market expects**  
- volatility will increase  
- corrections will be more frequent  
- ETF flows will become the single most important indicator  
- “Just hold until the halving” may no longer be a valid strategy  

Investors will need to track **liquidity, macro trends, and institutional positioning** more closely than date-based narratives.

- Bitcoin is evolving faster than ever — institutional capital now sets the rhythm.  
- If the two-year structure takes hold, old models lose predictive power.  
- The AI sector introduces a new form of competition for capital.  
- 2026 may bring sharper pumps, quicker corrections, and more aggressive capital rotation.  
- ETF flows will be far more important than the halving itself.
