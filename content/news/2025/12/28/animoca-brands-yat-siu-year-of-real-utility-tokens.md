The crypto industry has gone through several turbulent years dominated by hype cycles, memecoins and short-term speculation. According to Animoca Brands and its co-founder Yat Siu, this model is reaching its limits. Their message is direct and uncomfortable for parts of the market: crypto needs to grow up.

## Who are Animoca Brands?

Animoca Brands is a global investment and technology company focused on Web3, blockchain gaming and digital ownership. Unlike typical crypto projects that issue a single token and rely on market hype, Animoca operates more like a combination of a venture capital firm and a game publisher.

The company invests in dozens of blockchain projects, particularly in gaming, digital identity and NFTs, where tokens serve a clear function: access, ownership, participation or the use of specific services. The emphasis is on building infrastructure and long-term ecosystems rather than chasing short-lived price movements.

## Who is Yat Siu and why does his view matter?

Yat Siu is the co-founder and executive chairman of Animoca Brands, with a long background in the gaming and technology sectors. He did not enter crypto for quick profits, but because of the idea of digital property rights and sustainable digital economies.

This is why his perspective carries weight. When he says crypto mispriced political narratives, interest rates and macroeconomic realities, it comes from the viewpoint of a builder focused on products and systems, not a short-term trader.

## Why criticize memecoins and political hype?

Siu argues that much of the market underestimated the impact of tariffs, monetary policy and broader economic forces. Crypto relied too heavily on simplified political stories, while real-world decisions followed entirely different priorities.

Memecoins, in this context, represent an exhausted model: high attention, but little lasting value. These cycles may deliver quick gains for some, but they fail to build trust or attract serious long-term investors.

## What does “real utility” actually mean?

A utility token is not designed as a speculative chip, but as a functional digital tool. This can include:
- access to a platform, game or service  
- governance rights within a project  
- use of specific features in an ecosystem  
- participation in a digital economy with clear rules  

Without such functionality, Animoca believes crypto remains trapped in a speculative niche.

## Why are public markets and institutions important?

Animoca Brands openly targets public markets and institutional capital. Its plans for a public listing are seen as an attempt to create an “altcoin proxy” — a way for traditional investors to gain exposure to crypto without directly trading volatile tokens.

The underlying message is clear: if crypto wants broader adoption and deeper capital pools, it must offer structure, transparency and products that make sense beyond the crypto-native audience.

## What does this mean for everyday crypto users?

If this vision plays out, the coming years may bring fewer explosive hype cycles and more projects built to last. The focus shifts from hunting the next memecoin to understanding what a token actually does.

This does not mean speculation disappears, but it does suggest a more mature and sustainable market.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)

- Animoca Brands is pushing for long-term value over short-term hype.  
- The critique of memecoins reflects market fatigue, not ideology.  
- Utility tokens and clearer rules open the door to larger capital inflows.  
- For retail users, this could mean slower moves, but potentially greater stability.
