Prediction markets are rapidly becoming one of the most dynamic sectors in crypto — and Kalshi’s latest move could redefine the entire industry.  
According to CNBC and Cointelegraph, Kalshi has launched **tokenized versions of its event contracts on the Solana blockchain**, bringing regulated financial products into the Web3 ecosystem for the first time.

This enables political, macroeconomic, social, and even sports-related outcomes to be traded as **on-chain tokenized instruments** with higher speed, deeper liquidity, and enhanced anonymity.

---

## 🔵 What Is Kalshi and Why Does It Matter?

Most non-U.S. readers have never heard of Kalshi, but in the U.S. it is a major regulated platform.

### ✔ Kalshi is a **federally regulated U.S. event-contract exchange**  
Registered with the **CFTC** (Commodity Futures Trading Commission).

This makes it the **only platform in the United States** legally allowed to offer political and macro event contracts as *financial instruments* rather than gambling products.

### ✔ How do Kalshi contracts work?  
Event contracts are yes/no markets paying out **$1 if the event occurs**.  
Examples include:

- Will inflation come in higher than expected?  
- Will the Federal Reserve raise rates at the next meeting?  
- Who will win a specific election?  
- Will U.S. GDP exceed a target?  
- Will Bitcoin reach a price threshold by a certain date?

The price of a contract (e.g., $0.63) reflects the **market-implied probability** (63%).

### ✔ Where is Kalshi based?  
- United States  
- Headquarters: New York  
- Founded: 2018  
- Investors include Sequoia, CapitalG (Google), Charles Schwab, Naval Ravikant, and others

### ✔ Who can use Kalshi?  
- Only U.S. citizens and residents with an SSN  
- International users **cannot** access Kalshi.com  
- Tokenized contracts on Solana offer a *secondary on-chain trading layer* accessible to global users

---

## 🔵 What Exactly Did Kalshi Launch on Solana?

Kalshi has tokenized its existing event markets and deployed them on Solana.

Key benefits include:

### ✔ Greater anonymity  
On-chain trading does not require KYC.

### ✔ Faster settlement  
Solana’s high throughput and sub-second finality are ideal for fast-moving prediction markets.

### ✔ Deeper liquidity  
Protocols such as **Jupiter** and **DFlow** connect Kalshi’s off-chain orderbook with on-chain liquidity.

### ✔ Lower transaction costs  
Solana offers some of the lowest fees of any major blockchain.

### ✔ Open infrastructure  
Developers can build alternative UIs, analytics dashboards, bots, and custom trading front-ends.
