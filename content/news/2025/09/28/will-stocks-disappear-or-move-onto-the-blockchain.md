## Intro — the wrong either/or
Stocks are **legal bundles of ownership rights** (votes, dividends). **Blockchain & AI** mainly change **how** those rights are **recorded, transferred and settled** — not the idea of ownership itself.

## Three scenarios (2025–2050)
1. **Coexistence with on-chain migration (most likely)**  
   Stocks remain stocks, but become **tokenized** (on-chain registry, **T+0** settlement, 24/7 trading, fractional ownership).
2. **Status quo, faster plumbing**  
   Traditional registries with T+1/T+0 rails, no public chain.
3. **Radical crypto dominance**  
   Fully on-chain equities with DAO governance — plausible in niches, slower at scale.

## Why stocks move on-chain (economics)
- **Instant settlement (T+0)** cuts counterparty risk & intermediary costs.  
- **24/7 liquidity** & **fractions** broaden participation.  
- **Programmable rights** (dividends, voting, lock-ups) via smart contracts.  
- **Global reach**: on-chain KYC enables cross-border ownership without complex custody chains.

## Bitcoin’s role in that world
- **Digital reserve** (gold-2.0) for corporate & sovereign balance sheets.  
- **On-chain collateral** for repo/derivatives/credit.  
- **Not a replacement for stocks**, but **base collateral/store of value** alongside tokenized securities.

## What AI changes
- **Automated compliance** (KYC/AML), real-time risk & clearing.  
- **Agent trading**: AI agents trade tokenized stocks 24/7 under on-chain policies.  
- **Transparent data** → easier supervision and audit.

## Regulation — hurdles & bridges
- **Legal status of tokenized equities** must be explicit (dividends, voting, insolvency).  
- **Custody & identity**: regulated custody + verified identity (VC/soulbound).  
- **Interoperability** between **CeFi market infra** and **DeFi protocols** (RWA bridges, permissioned L2s).

## Where the trend is visible already
- **Tokenized MMFs and bonds (RWA)** as regulatory icebreakers.  
- Private equity issuances on permissioned chains (exchange/depository pilots).  
- On-chain voting and cap-table tooling in venture.

## Risks
- **Liquidity fragmentation** across chains/standards.  
- **Smart-contract/oracle** and operational risks.  
- **Regulatory patchwork** by jurisdiction.  
- **Key management/recovery** (UX).

## Timeline (pragmatic)
- **Near term (1–3y)**: RWA/token MMFs boom; larger private equity pilots on-chain.  
- **Mid (3–7y)**: T+0 pilots on permissioned L2; selective 24/7 secondary markets.  
- **Long (7–15y)**: broader equity migration; Bitcoin entrenches as **reserve/collateral**.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Stocks won’t disappear.** Paper and legacy registries will.  
- **Tokenized equities + on-chain infrastructure** will win; **Bitcoin** becomes the **reserve layer**.  
- **Winning setup**: a **hybrid** — tokenized stocks, KYC-aware L2 rails, BTC/ETH as base collateral.  
- **For investors**: watch **tokenization pilots, RWA standards, exchange L2s and custody rules** — that’s the scaffolding of the next 50 years of markets.
