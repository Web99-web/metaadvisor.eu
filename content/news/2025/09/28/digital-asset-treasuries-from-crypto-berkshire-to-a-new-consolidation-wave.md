## DATs enter a new phase
Digital Asset Treasuries (**DATs**) now control **$100B+** in crypto. They are shifting from passive holders to **strategic operators** that **finance, build and govern** the very networks whose tokens they hold — hence the **‘Berkshire Hathaway of crypto’** analogy.

## Where the influence shows up
- **Solana** — larger SOL stakes can improve finality, latency and market liquidity.  
- **Hyperliquid** — staking **HYPE** can reduce user fees and boost operator revenues.  
- **MicroStrategy** — an outlier: **BTC-only** exposure without a broader operational mandate.

## Consolidation spark: Strive–Semler
**Strive’s** announced acquisition of **Semler Scientific** marks the first **DAT→DAT** merger. The combined entity will hold **~11,000 BTC** (including parallel purchases), aiming to lift **bitcoin per share**, unify balance sheets and **improve capital-market access**.

## Why it matters
- **Scale effects**: bigger treasuries signal stronger balance sheets and can secure better terms.  
- **Governance power**: concentrated token holdings mean **real influence** in protocols.  
- **Centralization risk**: transparency and **community alignment** will make or break trust.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Thesis**: DATs are set to become **long-run economic engines** for select chains.  
- **Tactics**: M&A, not only net coin buying, becomes the fastest growth lever.  
- **For investors**: track not just BTC price but **who controls the treasuries**—that’s where protocol direction and value capture increasingly sit.
