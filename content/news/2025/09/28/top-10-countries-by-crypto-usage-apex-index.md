## What’s behind the rankings?
- **UAE**: pro-innovation while **tightening oversight** for exchanges/stablecoins — attract institutions, filter low-quality projects.  
- **Singapore**: fintech hub with clear (often strict) MAS rules and high ownership rates.  
- **USA**: largest ATM network, ETF effects; fragmented regulation but deep markets.  
- **Canada**: ETP pioneer; strong adoption growth.  
- **Turkey & Argentina**: inflation/FX pressure → **real-world usage** (value protection, remittances).  
- **Germany & Switzerland**: TradFi–crypto bridge (banks, ETPs, “Crypto Valley”).  
- **Australia & Indonesia**: broad retail base, active Web3/DeFi scenes.

## Method (ApeX)
Composite score = **ownership rate + adoption growth + search interest + ATM availability**. Not a country market-cap list, not a pure regulation map.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Rules + infrastructure** determine sustainable growth (UAE, Singapore, DE, CH).  
- **Macro stress** creates organic demand (Turkey, Argentina).  
- **Institutional products** (ETF/ETP) boost the US/Canada; EU/APAC likely to follow.  
- **Watch next:** stablecoin frameworks, bank custody, and RWA tokenization.
