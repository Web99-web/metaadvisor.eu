## How the model works
- **USDT** and **USDC** are backed mainly by **U.S. Treasuries** and cash.  
- With high interest rates, those Treasuries generate **billions in yield**.  
- Issuers (Tether, Circle) **keep that yield** – holders of stablecoins earn **nothing**.  
- Result: record corporate profits while users bear the opportunity cost.  

At **DAC 2025**, Wormhole co-founder **Dan Reecer** said: *“If I’m holding USDC, I’m losing money – money Circle is making.”*

---

## Record profits & valuations
- **Tether** reported **$4.9B net profit in Q2 2025**.  
- Company valuation reportedly reached **$500B** in new funding.  

Reecer argued that sooner or later, users will either **demand yield sharing** or **move funds** to competitors.

---

## The challengers: M^0, Agora & tokenized funds
- **M^0** and **Agora** are building systems where **yield flows back to apps or users**, not just issuers.  
- Goal: compete with USDT/USDC while adding a **yield layer**.  

**Tokenized money market funds (MMFs)** are growing as well:  
- Example: **USYC**, launched after Circle’s **$1.3B acquisition of Hashnote**.  
- Idea: let users convert **cash ↔ yield-bearing collateral** directly on-chain.

---

## Why issuers don’t share yield directly
- **Regulation risk**: paying yield could make them **securities**.  
- **Simplicity**: 1 token = 1 USD, no moving parts.  
- **Resilience**: keeping income strengthens reserves and capital buffer.

---

## Glossary
- **Stablecoin** – fiat-pegged token, usually backed 1:1 by USD.  
- **U.S. Treasuries** – government bonds that generate yield.  
- **Money Market Fund (MMF)** – pool of short-term debt instruments, now increasingly tokenized.  
- **RWA** – tokenized real-world assets (bonds, cash, receivables).  
- **M^0 / Agora** – protocols experimenting with yield-sharing stablecoin design.

---

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **USDT/USDC dominate** thanks to liquidity & simplicity. As long as exchanges/merchants prefer them, they stay default money.  
- **But pressure is mounting**: high interest rates make “0% stablecoins” harder to justify.  
- **Most likely future**: a dual ecosystem – *transactional stablecoins* (fast, liquid, 0%) + *yield products* (tokenized MMFs, RWA protocols).  
- **Regulation is key**: direct yield = risk, indirect models win ground.  
- **For users**: treat USDT/USDC as **payment rails**; if you want yield, move into **dedicated yield products**.
