## Why the Terminal still matters
The Bloomberg Terminal is the **control panel of global finance**: real-time prices, prioritized news, **bond & FX curves**, OTC quotes, corporate actions, messaging with **audit trail** — all in one place.  
A typical license costs **≈$30,000 per user/year**, and the Terminal still accounts for the majority of Bloomberg LP’s revenue.

### What you get
- **Reliable tick-by-tick feed** and low latency (hardly any gaps).  
- **Deep fixed-income/FX/derivatives** datasets you won’t find in public sources.  
- **Terminal chat & compliance** (audit trail, permissions, redistribution rights).  
- **Excel/API integrations** that stay stable under load.  
- One window for **risk monitoring, news, and execution**.

## Where crypto & AI fit
Today you can cover a lot with **crypto data + AI + open data**:

- **Crypto data**: Coin Metrics, Kaiko, Glassnode, CryptoQuant, Dune/Flipside, Token Terminal, Nansen.  
- **Equities/fundamentals**: Koyfin, TIKR, Seeking Alpha; more advanced: FactSet / S&P Capital IQ.  
- **Charts/TA**: TradingView.  
- **Macro/open data**: FRED, SEC EDGAR.  
- **AI layer**: data cleaning, fast queries, tables, scripts.

For **retail** and smaller teams this often delivers **80–90% of functionality** for **<5% of the cost**.

## When ~$30,000/yr is worth it
- If you’re a bank, fund, prop desk or treasury team trading **bonds/FX/OTC** daily, needing **reference prices, new issues, curves** and **compliance-safe** messaging.  
- If you require **robust integrations** into risk/portfolio systems and **licensed** news with redistribution rights.  
- If clients/committees expect the **industry standard** (the Terminal is often your ticket to the table).

## When it’s overkill
- **Retail**, educators or a small crypto fund focused on **exchange spot/derivatives**: the modular stack above + AI is usually smarter and **far cheaper**.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Positive:** Bloomberg Terminal remains the **most reliable single source of truth** for institutions — especially in **fixed income & FX**. Integrations, compliance and data depth are hard to replicate.  
- **Practical:** In a crypto-native, AI-augmented world you can build a powerful **modular stack** with excellent value for money.  
- **Bridge, not wall:** It’s not either/or. **Best teams combine** both: the Terminal for heavyweight institutional infra + crypto/AI tools for speed, research and cost efficiency.
