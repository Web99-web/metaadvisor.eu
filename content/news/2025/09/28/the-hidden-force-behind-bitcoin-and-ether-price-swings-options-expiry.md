## Intro — why prices ‘magnetize’ to certain levels
If **Bitcoin** or **Ether** often “stick” to round levels into week/month-end, you’re likely seeing **options expiry (OPEX)** at work. As large **open interest** clusters around a few strikes, **dealers/market makers** adjust **hedges**, which can **pull prices** toward **max pain**, or unleash a **snap move** after expiry.

## The mechanics (plain English)
1. **Open interest** concentrates on popular strikes (e.g., 60k/62k/65k for BTC).  
2. Near **expiry**, dealers dynamically **delta/gamma hedge** as options decay (theta).  
3. Hedging = real **buying/selling** of spot or perps → **moves price**.  
4. Tight OI clustering → **magnet** to **max pain**; asymmetric OI → **mini-squeezes** likelier.  
5. **Post-expiry**, that technical pressure fades and price action often **re-ranges**.

## Example (illustrative numbers)
- Friday: **$3B of BTC options** expire.  
- Largest OI at **call 65k** / **put 60k** → **max pain ≈ 62k**.  
- At 63–64k, dealers **sell** (trim call delta); too low, they **buy** (cover put delta).  
- Effect: **stickiness** near 62k until 16:00 UTC.  
- **Afterwards**: hedging comes off → **new range/volatility**.

## Max pain — useful, not omnipotent
**Max pain** is the level where the **aggregate value** of open calls+puts is **lowest**.  
- **Why it often ‘works’**: dealers, typically **net short options**, hedge toward the **lowest-risk zone**.  
- **When it doesn’t**: when **macro** (ETF flows, news, forced perp liquidations) **overrides**, or OI is **diffuse**.

## BTC vs. ETH — same principle, different flavor
- **BTC**: deeper liquidity; often **cleaner** weekly/monthly magnets.  
- **ETH**: affected by **staking/DeFi** and L2 cross-hedging; **post-expiry** moves can be **sharper**.

## Dates that matter
- **Weekly**: Fridays.  
- **Monthly**: last Friday (larger OI, stronger effect).  
- **Quarterly**: Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec — often the **biggest swings**.

## What to watch (practical)
- **OI concentration by strike** (how tightly clustered).  
- **Put/Call ratios & skew** (risk tilt).  
- **Gamma exposure (GEX)**: positive gamma **dampens**; negative **amplifies** moves.  
- **Funding & basis** (perps/futures).  
- **Macro collisions** (FOMC, unlocks, major headlines).

## Glossary
- **Options expiry (OPEX)** — the day options cease to exist.  
- **Open Interest (OI)** — outstanding contracts.  
- **Max pain** — price where option buyers (calls+puts) lose the most in aggregate.  
- **Delta/Gamma hedging** — dealers’ dynamic risk hedging via spot/perps.  
- **Implied Volatility (IV)** — expected vol baked into option prices.  
- **Skew** — IV difference between calls and puts.  
- **Funding** — periodic fee between longs/shorts on perps.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **OPEX is mechanics, not magic.** With large, clustered OI, expect a magnet — unless macro overrides.  
- **Retail pitfall:** trying to nail the **exact** expiry tick; often better **day-before/day-after**.  
- **Investors:** treat OPEX as **short-term noise**.  
- **Traders:** combine **OI/GEX/funding** with the expiry calendar; **position sizing** beats perfect timing.
