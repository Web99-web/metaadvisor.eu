**TL;DR:** **Bitcoin (BTC)** fell **>3%** this week to around **$111,590**, breaking below the **50-** and **100-day SMAs**. Both have **flattened** for the first time since April — a **caution** signal for bulls. Meanwhile, social chatter around **“buy the dip”** (Santiment) surged — often a **contrarian** hint that the pullback may **deepen**.

### Sentiment vs. market structure
- **Santiment:** rising “buy the dip” mentions across Reddit/Telegram/X often precede **further downside**, as the crowd **buys too early**.  
- **Technical:** below **50/100DMA** with fading momentum, a swift V-recovery is less likely unless **volume** returns and **50DMA** is reclaimed as support.

### Liquidity as gravity: $107k
Per **Hyblock Capital**, the **deepest liquidity cluster** (concentration of limit and stop orders) sits near **$107,000**. Such zones act as **magnets**: price tends to **gravitate** where large orders can be **filled efficiently**, with **bounce potential** only after that test.

**Order book liquidity?**  
It’s the **depth** of bids/asks at each level. Higher concentration → **lower slippage** on size. When the cluster sits below price, markets often **probe** it before finding **equilibrium**.

### Broader context & risks
- **Retail optimism** collides with **flattening trend averages** and a **lower cluster**.  
- For a durable reversal we’d want: (1) **reclaim of 50DMA**, (2) **green-day volume expansion**, (3) **broader breadth** across pairs/timeframes.
