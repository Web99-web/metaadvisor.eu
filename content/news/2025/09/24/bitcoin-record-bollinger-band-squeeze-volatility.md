**TL;DR:** Analysts highlight **the narrowest Bollinger Bands ever recorded for Bitcoin**, signaling **extreme volatility compression**. Such squeezes are often followed by **sharp breakouts**. The key question is not *if* but *where* the next big move unfolds.

### Why the squeeze matters
Bollinger Bands track **standard deviation** around a moving average. When they contract, realized volatility drops. Historically, this “calm” often precedes a **storm** — once a catalyst (macro event, ETF flows, on-chain shift) hits, the bands expand violently.

### Key signals to watch
- **Volume**: true breakouts require **broad participation**.  
- **Derivatives**: **funding & skew** give bias clues; rising IV + rising price = **organic trend**.  
- **Order book liquidity**: clusters of orders can act as **magnets** before the real move.

### Context & risks
- **Fakeouts** are common in thin liquidity regimes.  
- **Macro drivers** (rates, USD, equities) can flip intraday direction.  
- **Crowded positioning** amplifies squeeze→release moves.
