**TL;DR:** India maintains one of the world’s toughest crypto tax regimes in 2025. **30% tax** on realized gains still applies, plus **1% TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)** on **every** transaction. **Loss offsets** remain **disallowed**, putting active traders at a structural disadvantage.

### Key provisions
- **Capital gains tax:** **30%** on realized profits from crypto trades.  
- **1% TDS:** charged on **each transaction**, pressuring liquidity and trader margins.  
- **No loss setoff:** losses from one trade cannot offset gains from another.  
- **Reporting:** keep detailed records across exchanges and wallets.

### Market impact
- **Local liquidity** thins as TDS erodes spread/alpha.  
- **Offshore drift:** users look abroad despite regulatory risks.  
- **Stability vs. innovation:** government prioritizes oversight; industry warns of capital & talent outflows.

### What traders should do in 2025
- **Plan for taxes** — price in 30% + 1% TDS per trade.  
- **Watch for policy shifts** — no relief announced yet, industry pressure is building.  
- **Maintain records** — precise transaction histories and compliant filings are essential.
