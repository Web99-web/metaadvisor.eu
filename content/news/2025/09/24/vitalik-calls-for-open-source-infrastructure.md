**TL;DR:** **Vitalik Buterin**, Ethereum **co-founder**, urges broader adoption of **open-source infrastructure** beyond crypto — in **healthcare**, **finance** and **governance**. Open code supports **trust, interoperability** and **resilience**.

### Why open source in health & finance?
Closed systems risk **opacity** and **vendor lock-in**. **Open source** enables:
- **Interoperability** across platforms and institutions.  
- **Faster innovation** as researchers and developers build on shared code.  
- **Security & resilience** via peer review and quicker fixes.

### Governance applications
**Public institutions** and **NGOs** can build critical rails **openly**, reducing dependency on private vendors and improving **transparency to citizens**.

### Bigger picture
As **blockchain** moves further into **mainstream finance** (ETFs, bank integrations), interest in **decentralized governance** is rising. Buterin’s message: the open-source ethos is **useful far beyond crypto**.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Open source as a public good:** extends “don’t trust, verify” to health/government.  
- **Hurdles:** private IP incentives; security concerns in the public sector.  
- **Opportunity:** global dev community, shared standards, lower lock-in.  
- **Ethereum ethos:** decentralization as **social** as well as **financial** infrastructure.
