**TL;DR:** **Centrifuge** has launched **SPXA**, the first **licensed** S&P 500 index fund on **blockchain rails**. It’s live on **Base** (Coinbase’s Ethereum L2), bringing one of the world’s most watched equity benchmarks **on-chain** with **24/7 trading** and **transparent holdings**.

### Setup & partners
- **License/index:** first tokenized index fund licensed by **S&P Dow Jones Indices**.  
- **Structure:** **Janus Henderson Anemoy S&P 500 Fund (SPXA)**; **Janus Henderson** as **sub-investment manager**, **Anemoy** (Centrifuge AM) as manager.  
- **Infra:** deployed on **Base**; **Wormhole** targeted for future **multichain** expansion.  
- **Anchor:** **FalconX** participated as **anchor investor**.

### Why it matters
- **RWA on-chain:** tokenizing funds/bonds/equities promises **faster settlement**, **24/7 access**, and **on-chain transparency**.  
- **Index breadth:** the **S&P 500** covers ~**80%** of U.S. market cap — **systemic** reach.  
- **DeFi synergies:** potential use as **collateral**, on-chain **margin/financing**, beyond simple buy-and-hold.

### Quotes & thesis
> “**Indexes** are the best way to bring stocks on-chain: **simple**, **collateral-ready**, and they unlock **liquidity** better than single names.” — **Bhaji Illuminati**, Centrifuge CEO.  
For **S&P DJI**, SPXA is a step toward the **future of index-linked products** in a tokenized environment.

### Open questions
- **Policy & tax:** **custody**, taxation, and **cross-border compliance** remain pivotal.  
- **Liquidity/NAV:** early token funds often face **thin books**; robust **MM** and clear **NAV** methodology are key.  
- **Bridges:** **Wormhole** expands reach but adds **operational risk**.
