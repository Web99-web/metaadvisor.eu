**TL;DR:** **Cloudflare** announced **NET Dollar**, a USD-linked stablecoin built for the **agentic web** — where autonomous **AI agents** handle **instant, secure micro-payments** for services, content and APIs.

### Why NET Dollar?
- **A new web business model:** shifting from ads to **pay-per-use** and **fractional payments**.  
- **Microtransactions-first:** direct monetization for developers and creators.  
- **Global Cloudflare network:** fast, low-cost **settlement rails**.

### Standards & interoperability
Cloudflare contributes to **Agent Payments Protocol (APP)** and **x402** to make **agent-to-agent payments** simpler and interoperable across platforms.

### The bigger picture
- **Stablecoin land-grab:** players like **Stripe** (Tempo, Bridge acquisition) are building native rails.  
- **Upside:** stablecoin volumes could approach **$1T by 2030** (institutional adoption, FX settlement, commerce).  
- **Execution risks:** UX, trust, compliance and bank/fintech partnerships will decide outcomes.
