**A new Keyrock report says onchain asset management is having a breakout year.**  
- **Assets under management (AUM)** climbed **118%** in 2025 to about **$35B**.  
- **2026 outlook:** **$64B** base case, up to **$85B** if momentum holds.

**Discretionary strategies** were the standout, up **~738% YTD**. Concentration remains high: **Morpho, Pendle and Maple** now control **~31% of AUM**, signaling both **scaling leadership** and **concentration risk**.  
**Yield vaults** remain the primary entry point with roughly **$18B** in deposits.

**Wallet mix:** while **smaller wallets** dominate in number, **larger accounts** (so-called whales/dolphins) provide **70%–99%** of capital across strategies.  
**Performance:** net returns are **competitive with TradFi** but **not uniformly higher**. **Automated yield vaults** outperformed TradFi peers by about **186 bps after fees**, whereas **structured products** and **onchain credit** slightly lagged once costs were included.  
**Expansion:** Brussels-based **Keyrock** moved into asset & wealth management via its acquisition of **Turing Capital** (Luxembourg).

---

### Plain-English glossary
- **Onchain asset management:** investing via **smart contracts** on a blockchain (transparent, automated).  
- **AUM:** total capital parked in these strategies.  
- **Yield vault:** an **automated savings pot** that routes deposits into yield sources (e.g., over-collateralized lending).  
- **Morpho / Pendle / Maple:** major protocols for **lending/borrowing** (Morpho), **yield/interest trading** (Pendle), and **onchain corporate credit** (Maple).

---

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Flows:** toward **discretionary** (human-led, onchain-executed) and **vaults** (low effort).  
- **Risks:** code/bridges, liquidity, counterparty, policy; watch top-3 concentration.  
- **What to track:** AUM/volume, top-3 share, return vs. risk, audits and transparency.
