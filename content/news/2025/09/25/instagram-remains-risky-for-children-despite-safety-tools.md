A review led by **Arturo Béjar**, former Meta engineer and whistleblower, found that **Instagram still poses serious risks to minors** despite the rollout of new protection tools.

- **64% of the new tools** were found to be **ineffective** or poorly maintained.  
- In 2024 Meta introduced mandatory **teen accounts** under regulatory pressure, but many tools are “ineffective, quietly altered, or removed.”  
- Béjar warns this creates a **false sense of security**, while children remain exposed to harmful content, inappropriate contact and product design choices that promote risky use.

“Kids, including many under 13, are not safe on Instagram. This isn’t only about ‘bad content,’ it’s about **deliberate product design**,” Béjar said.

### Conclusion
Despite Meta’s promises, **risks remain**. Experts and advocacy groups call for greater transparency and regulatory oversight to ensure children are truly protected.
