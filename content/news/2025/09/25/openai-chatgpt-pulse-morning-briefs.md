**TL;DR:** **Pulse** shifts ChatGPT from **reactive Q&A** to a **proactive assistant**. Every morning, users get **5–10 curated briefs** — news, priorities, and tasks — without prompting.

### What’s new
- **Proactive & asynchronous:** Pulse works **while you sleep**, ready by morning.  
- **Context-aware:** prioritizes topics based on **interests, calendar, projects**.  
- **One tab, less friction:** reduces app-hopping across news, tasks, and calendar.

### Availability
- **Launching on Pro ($200/mo):** compute-heavy features ship to higher tiers first.  
- **Plus & beyond:** phased rollout as **compute capacity** and **stability** improve; OpenAI is expanding data centers with **Oracle** and **SoftBank**.

### Why it matters
- **Morning focus:** curated briefs beat doomscrolling.  
- **Assistant, not chatbot:** aligns with **Agents/Codex** direction — always-on support.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Curation over volume:** five to ten high-signal items > info overload.  
- **Guardrails:** require clear **opt-in and source controls** to avoid filter bubbles.  
- **Scaling reality:** **compute & latency** will dictate the timeline for broader access.  
- **Bigger picture:** Pulse is a proving ground for **always-on AI workflows**.
