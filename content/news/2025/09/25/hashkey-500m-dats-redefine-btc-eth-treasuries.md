**In short:** **HashKey** in Hong Kong is launching a **$500M fund** to develop **DAT concepts** (*Digital Asset Treasury*): **structured BTC/ETH treasuries** with clear rules on **risk, liquidity and yield sources**. The idea is to bring **digital assets** closer to **corporate treasury standards**.

### DATs in plain English
- **Core:** **BTC/ETH** as long-term volatile assets.  
- **Liquidity sleeve:** **stablecoins / tokenized T-bills** for **short-term needs**.  
- **Yield sources:** conservative **staking**, restaking/LRT, onchain T-bills.  
- **Hedging layer:** **options, futures, collars** to soften drawdowns.  
- **Rules:** **VaR, max drawdown, liquidity buffers (30–90 days)**, rebalancing triggers.

### Why it matters
- **Board-ready:** measurable metrics, policies, controls like in TradFi.  
- **Transparency:** onchain proof-of-reserves, 24/7 markets.  
- **Flexibility:** add/remove yield or hedge modules without changing the core.

### Target groups
- **Corporate treasuries** seeking partial BTC/ETH exposure.  
- **DAOs/protocols** needing rules for spending and safety nets.  
- **Funds/family offices** looking for structured exposure.

### Regulatory context
Hong Kong aggressively builds **licensed frameworks** for tokenization. DATs fit naturally: **policies, controls, reporting** are standard treasury language.
