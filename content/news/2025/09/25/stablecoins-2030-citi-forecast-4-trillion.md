**TL;DR:** The **stablecoin market** is expanding faster than anticipated. Outstanding supply rose from **$200B** to **$280B** in 2025. **Citi** now projects **$1.9T** in issuance by 2030 (base) and **$4T** in a bull case. At fiat-like velocity, stablecoins could support **$100–200T** in yearly transactions.

### Growth drivers
- **Digital economy & agentic web:** AI agents and digital-native firms drive micro-payments.  
- **Cross-border advantage:** cheaper and faster settlement vs. legacy rails.  
- **USD dominance on-chain:** ongoing demand for Treasuries as collateral.

### But bank tokens loom larger
Citi expects **tokenized deposits/bank tokens** to surpass stablecoin volumes by 2030 — driven by corporates demanding **regulatory clarity, real-time settlement, and embedded compliance**. Even modest migration of banking flows on-chain could mean **>$100T** turnover.

### Geography & currency landscape
- **USD** stays dominant, but **Hong Kong** and **UAE** emerge as testing hubs.  
- **Coexistence model:** stablecoins, bank tokens, and CBDCs will likely share the ecosystem.
