Investors are counting down to the **FED’s** Sept. 17 decision; markets expect a **quarter-point (25 bp)** cut that could trigger short-term volatility but potentially fuel longer-term gains in risk assets.

The macro backdrop highlights the FED’s delicate balancing act.

Per the latest **CPI** report, consumer prices rose **0.4% m/m** in August, lifting the annual rate to **2.9%** from **2.7%**, driven by shelter, food and gasoline. **Core CPI** climbed **0.3%**.

**PPI** data tell a similar story: the headline PPI slipped **0.1% m/m** in August but remained **2.6%** above a year earlier, while **core PPI** advanced **2.8%**, the largest yearly increase since March — underscoring **sticky inflation** even as growth cools.

**Nonfarm payrolls** increased by just **22,000** in August; federal government and energy job losses offset modest health-care gains. Unemployment held at **4.3%** and labor-force participation at **62.3%**.

Revisions showed June and July job growth was weaker than first reported, reinforcing signs of **cooling momentum**. Average hourly earnings still rose **3.7%** YoY, keeping **wage pressures** alive.

Bonds adjusted accordingly. MarketWatch shows the **2-year** Treasury at **3.56%** and the **10-year** at **4.07%**, leaving the curve **modestly inverted**. Futures imply a **93%** chance of a **25-bp** cut, per **CME FedWatch**.

If the FED limits its move to **25 bp**, a **“buy the rumor, sell the news”** response is possible, as much of this is already priced in.

## Our take
- **Base case:** **25 bp** + cautious guidance. Short-term **whipsaws** are likely; medium-term, lower rates and a softer USD are **tailwinds** for risk assets.  
- **Surprises:** **50 bp** or very dovish → stronger risk-on (BTC/ETH first). **No cut** or hawkish → risk-off (higher yields, pressure on crypto/tech).  
- **Crypto:** watch **BTC dominance** and exchange flows; falling dominance with volume often favors alts but volatility is high.  
- **Gold:** benefits from lower **real yields** and a weaker USD.  
- **Equities:** supportive for **growth/tech/small caps**; keep an eye on the **SEP/dot-plot** and earnings guidance.  
- **Tactics:** don’t chase the first move; use **staged entries**, avoid excess leverage around the event, apply **stops** and size positions for volatility.
