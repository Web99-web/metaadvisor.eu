The memecoin sector is firming as social chatter about a fresh **altcoin season** grows — helped by expectations that the **FED** will cut rates next week, a typical boost for risk assets.

Bitcoin’s dominance has slipped about **3.5%** over the past month. Underperformance versus altcoins has pushed **altseason indexes** (top crypto performance vs. BTC) into “altseason” territory.

**Altseason** refers to periods when alternative cryptocurrencies strongly outperform bitcoin, often starting when capital **rotates** from BTC into other tokens as risk appetite increases.

According to **CoinMarketCap** and **CoinGlass** indexes, bitcoin gained just **0.3%** over the last 24 hours, while the **CoinDesk Memecoin Index (CDMEME)** jumped **7.1%**.

Gainers within CDMEME include **SHIB** and **BONE**, which oddly strengthened despite a recent flash-loan exploit on Shiba Inu’s **Shibarium** L2 network.

The stronger altcoin performance stems from **higher risk appetite**: lower rates make safer instruments (e.g., Treasuries) less appealing, prompting flows into riskier assets — including memecoins.

On **Polymarket**, traders now price a **92%** chance of a **25-bp** cut this month and **7%** for **50 bp**. The **CME FedWatch** tool shows **93%** odds for the smaller cut and **6.6%** for the larger.

Against this backdrop, a **wave of altcoin ETFs** could hit U.S. markets in Q4 — if approved. Filings reportedly include a **DOGE ETF** and a **TRUMP ETF**.

If green-lit, such ETFs could draw more mainstream attention, increase liquidity and capital inflows into altcoins — while likely adding to memecoin **volatility**.

## Our take
Macro (rates) + potential ETFs are a tailwind, but risk remains high. Watch **liquidity**, **catalyst calendars** (FED/SEC decisions), and **risk controls** — bursts of euphoria often fade quickly.
