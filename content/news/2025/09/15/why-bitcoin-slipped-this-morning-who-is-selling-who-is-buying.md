Around **10:00 (CEST)** Bitcoin began to slip; as of writing it’s near **$114,867**. When we say “groups are selling,” we don’t mean a secret club — we mean **wallet groups by balance size**. Analytics look at **smaller holders** (under 1 BTC), **mid-sized** (say 1–100 BTC) and **larger** balances (hundreds/thousands of BTC). Today the pattern is similar across those buckets: **net selling**.

Why? After a few strong days many participants are **taking profits**. That’s classic **consolidation** behavior: some sell into strength while others wait lower to buy. We saw the same last week: as price accelerated, larger accounts briefly turned to buying; once momentum faded, they flipped back to **distribution**.

**Time zones** add context. Over the weekend **Asia** nudged price higher; the **European morning** brought today’s pullback. It doesn’t mean “Europe dumps the market,” just that **rhythms and incentives** differ: one cohort buys, another locks in gains.

Levels to watch: the **upper band** around **$117k–$120k** has been **stubborn** — supply tends to thicken there. On the **downside**, support that has set the tempo sits closer to **$113k–$107k**. As long as trade stays **inside** these rails, “**consolidation**” remains the fairest label.

## Our take
If you’re not trading hourly, the good news is today’s dip **doesn’t change the story**. It still looks like a **coiled spring** after a strong stretch. For the longer view, focus on **structural demand** (ETFs, long-term buyers) and whether daily swings **stay inside the range**.

Practical notes:
- **Investors:** stick to plan and allocation. Days like this test nerves, not strategy.  
- **Level-watchers:** a **clean daily close above ~$117k** would favor continuation; **holding ~$113k** on pullbacks preserves the bull case. Only **below ~$107k** would we call a material change.

In one line: today’s move is primarily **profit-taking** across multiple holder groups — more routine than drama.
