**Key takeaways (per Cointelegraph)**  
- Yield-bearing stables come in **three models**: tokens backed by **T-bills/money-market funds**, **DeFi savings wrappers** (e.g., sDAI) and **synthetic dollars** sourcing yield from **derivatives/staking**.  
- **U.S. (GENIUS Act)** and **EU (MiCA)** bar **issuers** from paying yield directly to end-users; access is often **geo-restricted**.  
- **Taxes**: rebase and rewards are generally **taxable income** when credited; keep **rigorous records**.  
- **Risks**: policy shifts, market conditions, smart-contract risk, liquidity and redemption constraints.

---

## What are yield-bearing stablecoins?
Unlike “plain” stablecoins (USDT/USDC) that **don’t pay** anything while idle, yield-bearing designs **pass through** returns from underlying assets/strategies **automatically** — via **rebasing** (your token count rises) or **wrappers** whose **per-token value** accretes over time.

### Three models (2025)
1) **Tokenized T-bills/money-market funds** – digital wrappers for short-dated Treasuries/deposits; ongoing yield flows back to holders.  
2) **DeFi savings wrappers** – e.g., **sDAI** (Sky/Maker): DAI sits in the **Dai Savings Rate**; sDAI tracks balance growth set by governance.  
3) **Synthetic dollars** – stability maintained via **derivative strategies** (e.g., spot-long + perp-short), with potentially higher but variable returns.

## How (per CT) to earn passive income
1) **Pick your lane**: conservative — **T-bills/MMFs**; moderate — **sDAI and peers**; advanced — **synthetics** (more variance).  
2) **Buy or mint**: typically via **CEX** (KYC) or directly with the protocol; many issuers **limit minting** to banks/PSPs or qualified investors.  
3) **Hold in your wallet**: yield accrues via rebase or rising NAV.  
4) **DeFi add-ons (optional)**: lending/liquidity/vault overlays for extra yield — with **more complexity/risk**.  
5) **Track everything**: most jurisdictions treat rebase/rewards as **taxable at credit**; later disposals may add **capital gains**.

---

## Regulation (2025): access defined by the rulebook
- **U.S. – GENIUS Act**: **prohibits** payment stablecoin issuers from paying yield to retail; many yield structures fall under **securities** rules.  
- **EU – MiCA**: EMT issuers **may not** pay interest; stablecoins are **payment instruments**, not savings products.  
- **UK/elsewhere**: similar trajectory; always confirm **eligibility** in your jurisdiction.

---

## Taxes & reporting
- **Rebase/rewards**: often **ordinary income** on the credit date; disposals ⇒ **capital gain/loss**.  
- New regimes (**DAC8/CARF**) add **automatic exchange of information**; U.S. tightens **broker reporting** (e.g., 1099-DA).  
- **Bookkeeping**: serialize transactions, separate wallets (investment vs. spend), archive statements.

---

## Risks (at a glance)
- **Regulatory**, **market** (funding/staking variability), **operational** (bugs, governance), **liquidity** (redemptions, KYC), **concentration** (single issuer/protocol).

## Our take
If you want **passive income** without going full degen, yield-bearing stables are a sensible starting point — but **not** a “risk-free savings account.” Here’s the **framework** we use:

**1) Risk-bucket allocation (three buckets)**  
- **Conservative (30–60%)**: tokenized **T-bills/MMFs** and issuers with clear **fiat redemption**. Focus on **reserve transparency**, audits, clean KYC.  
- **Core/Moderate (20–50%)**: **DeFi wrappers** (e.g., sDAI) and blue-chip protocols. **Diversify smart-contract risk** (several protocols, no all-in).  
- **Advanced (0–20%)**: **synthetic dollars** (funding + staking). Treat as **credit/derivatives risk**: small slice, strict exit rules.

> Ranges are **indicative**, not advice. The goal is **layered risk-stacking**, not chasing the highest APY.

**2) Operational discipline**  
- **Segment wallets**: “vault” (cold), “working” (on-chain), “experimental” (capped).  
- Avoid **APY stacking** (yield-on-yield-on-yield): complexity multiplies **failure points**.  
- **Bridges/oracles** are **single points of failure** — avoid illiquid/exotic chains.  
- **Automate record-keeping** (CSV/tools) and define a **rebalancing policy** (e.g., monthly sweep of excess yield into the conservative bucket).

**3) Taxes & reporting (practical)**  
- **Rebase = taxable** at credit; NAV-style wrappers often realize gain **at disposal** — check local rules.  
- Maintain **lots/cost basis** per token/wallet; don’t mix business and personal funds.  
- Add a **checklist**: (a) token classification, (b) residency & KYC, (c) redemption access, (d) tax treatment, (e) reporting.

**4) Red flags**  
- **High APY with no source**.  
- **Opaque reserves**, “audits” without standards, PoR without hard redemption.  
- **Single bridge/oracle** with no fallback.  
- Governance with **low threshold** for critical parameter changes.

**Bottom line (operational)**  
Start with a **conservative base layer** (T-bills/MMFs or vetted wrappers), add a **DeFi core** (sDAI, etc.), and only then a small **sat** in synthetics. On the **first of each month**, run a **mini-audit**: check **redemptions, reserves, APY source, bugfixes** and **policy changes** — then adjust allocation.
