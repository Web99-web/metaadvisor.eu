**What happened**  
On Sunday, **Shibarium** — Shiba Inu’s L2 — suffered a **flash-loan** attack targeting its **validator system**, with roughly **$2.4M** in **ETH** and **SHIB** siphoned from the bridge to Ethereum. The **CoinDesk Memecoin Index** slid ~**6.6%** over 24 hours, while the broader **CoinDesk 20** fell ~**2.3%**.

**Attack path**  
The exploiter borrowed about **4.6M BONE** (governance token), gained **majority control of validator keys**, and pushed **unauthorized transactions** through the bridge. (A flash loan is a zero-collateral loan borrowed and repaid within the **same** on-chain transaction.)

**Damage control**  
The team said a larger breach was averted because the BONE used to seize control were tied to **validator 1** and remained **locked** under staking rules. Confidence nonetheless took a hit.

**Price action**  
**SHIB** dropped ~**4%** on Sunday (UTC) to **$0.00001369**, then eased toward **$0.00001359** amid elevated volatility; **DOGE** softened alongside memecoins broadly.

## Our take
Validator-key governance and **bridges** remain **prime risk surfaces**. Priorities now: hardened **MPC/HSM key custody**, strict **rotation/multi-sig** policies; bridge **circuit breakers/rate limits**; fast, transparent **post-mortem** with addresses and a concrete **hardening plan**. Near term, expect **choppier** SHIB/BONE price action until forensic details land.
