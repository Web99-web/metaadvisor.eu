**TL;DR:** **Wednesday’s** **Federal Reserve** decision often jolts all markets — Bitcoin included. Price is circling just below **$117,000**. A **clean break above** that area could carry toward **$120,000+**. Another rejection would make a pullback toward **$113,000** a natural stop.

### What could push price higher?
- **ETF demand:** U.S. spot **BTC ETFs** continue to buy on net. In simple terms, **more is being bought than miners create**, which supports price over time.  
- **Big bids on exchanges:** Over the weekend, larger buy orders showed up on major venues — not a guarantee, but a **healthy sign** of interest.  
- **Macro tone:** If the Fed cuts and sounds **dovish**, risk assets like Bitcoin usually get a tailwind.

### …and what holds it back?
- **$117,000 resistance:** That level has been **sticky**. Without a convincing break, bulls lose momentum.  
- **Fed uncertainty:** Markets sometimes do **“sell the news”** — a quick dip on the headline before the real move.  
- **Models aren’t oracles:** Some cycle tools hint we’re **late-stage**, but they can fire **early** or **late**.

### If you’re not a trader
- **Investor lens:** For long-term holders, don’t chase every wiggle. **Plan, allocation and patience** usually beat nerves.  
- **Trader lens (brief):** Two lines matter: **above $117,000** (breakout confirmation) and **around $113,000** (potential dip zone). Without an exit plan and stops, better **not** to jump into the middle of FOMC week.

## Our take
Structurally, the backdrop is friendly: **ETF demand** keeps soaking up supply, and **larger buyers** are visible. Near term, the **Fed** can swing the mood — expect extra noise mid-week. Our simple checklist: (1) a **daily close cleanly above $117,000** favors continuation to 120k+, and (2) **$113,000 holding** on pullbacks keeps the bull case intact. Everything else is just **Fed chatter**.
