Michael **Saylor** has been added to the **Bloomberg Billionaires Index** with an estimated net worth of **$7.37B** (currently **#491**), up nearly **16%** year-to-date, while **MicroStrategy (MSTR)** has gained about **12%** over the same period. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Saylor’s wealth is tightly linked to **MSTR** and broader **bitcoin sentiment**, as MicroStrategy remains the best-known corporate BTC accumulator — implying higher **volatility** versus typical index members. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

Index inclusion is not an endorsement of strategy; it’s a **quantitative snapshot** — but it does refocus attention on the **“bitcoin as corporate treasury”** thesis. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

**Our view:**  
Traditional wealth rankings are increasingly **crypto-sensitive**. With BTC as the main driver, **MSTR** behaves like a **high-beta proxy** for bitcoin — amplifying both upside and downside in Saylor’s net worth.
