The **XUEX** platform (linked to **EG Investment Group**) collapsed on Friday night, leaving thousands of users without access to their funds, according to Croatian media. Ads and Telegram groups promised “stable returns” and a **doubling of deposits**, with users confirming two daily “signals” in the app. Payouts were only possible after **52 days**, sustaining the illusion of profits — a hallmark of **Ponzi** and **“pig-butchering”** schemes.

Economist **Toni Milun** and professor **Miljenko Švarcmajer** had publicly warned about the scam. After the collapse, a group admin nicknamed “Professor” (referred to as **Leo Smith**) told members a “financial inspection” would block withdrawals for **10 months**, while pitching a **new project** — a textbook attempt to extract more from victims.

Similar **sha zhu pan** operations recruit victims via social media and messaging apps and route them to fake investment platforms.

### What victims can do (quickly)
- Stop further transfers and **file a police report**; keep all chats and payment records.  
- If payments were made via **Revolut/bank**, report the transactions and initiate chargeback/APP-fraud procedures.  
- Avoid paying “**recovery**” services that promise to get the money back for a fee.

**Our view:**  
Payout delays plus constant recruitment only hide a Ponzi for a while. Equally important: **you don’t need a middleman**. You can buy and hold crypto **yourself** — no “managers,” no “signals.”

In practice: open an account with a reputable platform, fund it **from your own bank**, never transfer funds to someone who’ll “invest for you,” ignore “guaranteed returns” and signal groups. For long-term holding, consider a **self-custody wallet** (*not your keys, not your crypto*). Prefer **small, regular buys (DCA)** over chasing “quick gains.” If you’re unsure, **don’t send**.
