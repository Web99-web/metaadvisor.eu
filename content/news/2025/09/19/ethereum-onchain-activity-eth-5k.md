**TL;DR:** Ethereum has seen a **clear pickup in on-chain activity** (more **active addresses**, **higher gas**, busier DeFi/stablecoin/L2 traffic). At the same time, **spot ETH ETFs** and some **corporate treasuries** keep **soaking up supply**. With exchange balances trending lower, the setup for a push toward **$5,000** looks increasingly credible.

### What’s happening on-chain
- **More real usage:** The rise in **active addresses** and **gas** points to genuine activity, not just sentiment. DeFi, stablecoins, tokenization and L2 throughput are doing the heavy lifting.  
- **ETF & treasury demand:** Growing **spot ETF AUM** and periodic **treasury top-ups** remove ETH from circulation and support price discovery.  
- **Despite un-staking:** Validator exits and profit-taking occur, but **demand** has been **absorbing** supply so far.

### Why $5,000 is getting more plausible
- **Fundamentals + flows:** When **on-chain metrics**, **ETF inflows** and **declining exchange balances** align, trends often gain **a second leg**.  
- **ETH as a “city”:** More apps and users ⇒ more **gas demand** ⇒ **organic** rather than purely speculative demand.  
- **Relative strength:** Over ~2 months ETH has **outperformed** many large caps — typical of an **early rotation** toward productive ecosystems.

### For non-traders
- **Long-term:** More evidence that **ETH has utility**. Not a guarantee, but **more fire than smoke**.  
- **Active readers:** Watch **ETF flows**, **exchange balances**, and **active addresses/fees** — these often **lead** price.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Usage is the base:** On-chain momentum + ETF/treasury demand = a **constructive mix**.  
- **Risks:** firm USD/real rates, heavier profit-taking or an acceleration in **un-staking**.  
- **$5k level:** psychologically important, but **not a ceiling** if on-chain and inflows persist.  
- **Portfolio view:** **BTC/ETH** as the core; add incrementally, and track **fundamental metrics** not just charts.
