*Image is symbolic.*

**Summary:** FTX’s bankruptcy estate plans to release **$1.6 billion** to creditors on **September 30**, marking the third major payout since the 2022 collapse. Disbursements will be routed via **BitGo, Kraken** or **Payoneer** and are expected to land **within three business days** after processing.

### Who gets what?
- **U.S. customers:** **40%** this round; **95%** cumulative recovery.
- **“Dotcom” (international) customers:** additional **6%**; **78%** cumulative.
- **General unsecured & digital-asset loan claims:** **24%**; **85%** cumulative.
- **Convenience claims:** **120%** (above face value).

### Operational details
Payments begin for creditors who’ve completed verification on the FTX **claims portal**. Funds will be distributed through the listed providers, with expected settlement **within three business days**.

### Broader context
This September distribution follows earlier rounds returning **over $6 billion**. The restructuring aims to maximize returns to users of the once-prominent exchange, which collapsed in **November 2022**. Founder **Sam Bankman-Fried** is serving a **25-year** sentence after conviction on fraud and conspiracy charges.

**Our take (Metaadvisor):**
- **Liquidity to creditors:** Fast T+3 settlement via established providers reduces operational uncertainty.
- **High recovery rates:** 95% (U.S.) and 78% (“dotcom”) exceed early 2023 expectations.
- **Market impact:** Possible sell pressure if recipients liquidate; staggered payouts may diffuse effects.
- **Residual risks:** Ongoing legal matters could shape future rounds.
- **Industry lesson:** Custody, fund segregation and risk controls must be built-in from day one.
