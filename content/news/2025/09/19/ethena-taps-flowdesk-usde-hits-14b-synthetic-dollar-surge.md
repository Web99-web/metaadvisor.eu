**Summary:** Ethena has engaged **Flowdesk** to strengthen **liquidity and market infrastructure** for **USDe**, with circulating supply referenced at **$14B**. The momentum behind “synthetic dollar” instruments continues as users seek dollar exposure beyond traditional stablecoins.

### What the Flowdesk tie-up adds
- Expanded **market-making and OTC** for USDe pairs.
- **Institutional access** (accounts, processes, reporting).
- Potentially **tighter spreads** and deeper books on selected venues.

### Why USDe attracts capital
- **Dollar exposure** without direct fiat deposits.
- **Capital efficiency** in DeFi strategies (yield, collateral).
- Diversification via **synthetic-dollar** approaches alongside stablecoins.

### Watch-outs
- **Model risk:** reliance on **hedging/basis** and market conditions.
- **Liquidity risk:** depth and MM performance during stress.
- **Regulatory uncertainty:** treatment of synthetic dollars varies by jurisdiction.
