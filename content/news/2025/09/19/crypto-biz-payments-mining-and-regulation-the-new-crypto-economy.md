**Summary:** This week’s crypto business landscape was shaped by **payment rails** and Big Tech pilots, **miner economics** under shifting fee regimes and network activity, **stablecoins** as the key bridge to TradFi, and **ETF products** that shorten the institutional path into crypto.

### Payment rails: PayPal P2P & Google experiments
- Global **P2P channels** and super-app integrations drive **faster, cheaper payments**, especially cross-border.  
- **AI-assisted payments** (search, voice, auto-reconciliation) reduce user friction.  
- For merchants: improved **settlement** and fewer chargebacks with tokenized funds and smarter billing rules.

### “Rigs”: state of miner economics
- Miner revenues rely more on **fees** and protocol design (rollups, L2, blobs) — fee volatility directly hits **rig profitability**.  
- **Energy optimization** and **hedging** (hashrate/futures) are becoming standard to bridge low-profit cycles.

### Regulation & stablecoins
- **Stablecoins** remain the main on-ramp: demand for **reserve transparency**, regulated **custody**, and clear **AML/sanctions** policies.  
- The balance between US federal/state frameworks and EU regimes (e.g., MiCA) continues to shape **issuer/exchange constraints**.

### ETFs & capital flows
- Faster approvals and simplified **listing** for crypto **ETF/ETP** products reduce **time-to-market**, intensify issuer competition and often compress **fees**.  
- Price impact is uneven: stronger on **blue-chip tokens** with deep liquidity; weaker across the **mid/long tail**.

---

**Our take (Metaadvisor):**
- **Rails matter:** Less friction boosts real-world usage and maturity.  
- **Miners need stability:** Diversified fee sources (L2/rollups) and energy efficiency are key to resilience.  
- **Stablecoins = UX bridge:** Clear custody and reserve rules make them a trusted **dollar primitive**.  
- **ETFs accelerate mainstreaming:** Expect more institutional interest, but uneven token-level effects.  
- **Operational discipline:** Projects that quantify **actual user value** (not just TVL/volume) will win with partners and regulators.
