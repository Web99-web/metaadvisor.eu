*Image is symbolic.*

The old Wall Street adage — **“escalator up, elevator down”** — played out this week as crypto gave back several days of painstaking gains in Friday trading.

After nearly **pushing through $118,000** on Thursday (one day after the year’s first **Fed rate cut**), **Bitcoin** pulled back to **~$115,600** (**-1.5%/24h**), leaving the **7-day change roughly flat**.  
**Ether (ETH)** retreated from **$4,750** to **~$4,460** (**-2.9%/24h**, **-1.5% w/w**).  
Amid **ETF** excitement and growing institutional adoption, **Solana (SOL)** and **Dogecoin (DOGE)** were the week’s hottest majors, yet both finished **flat on the week** — **SOL -4.5%/24h**, **DOGE -6.3%/24h**.

Technically, BTC has carved a **rising triangle** in recent weeks: a series of **higher lows** along a **rising trendline** while repeatedly **pressing horizontal resistance near $118,000**. Each pullback since early September found buyers at that trendline, signaling **accumulation** and a **mild bullish bias**. Price is **consolidating** around **$115,700** near rising support; **$118,000** remains the **key ceiling** to watch.

> **Note:** **HBAR** slid about **3%**, finding near-term support around **$0.24**.

---

**Our take (Metaadvisor):**
- **Macro catalyst (Fed)** aided the push, but **118k resistance** plus profit-taking capped the move.  
- The **rising triangle** holds as long as **higher lows** persist — a clean break above **$118k** could extend **momentum**.  
- **Alts** show higher beta: they **run faster** on risk-on and **drop faster** when liquidity thins.  
- The **ETF narrative** supports the medium term (inflows) but doesn’t erase **short-term volatility**.  
- For traders: prioritize **risk management** around **$118k/$115k** zones — **level-based reactions** beat prediction.
