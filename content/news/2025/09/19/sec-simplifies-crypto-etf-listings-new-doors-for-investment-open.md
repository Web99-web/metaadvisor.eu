**Summary:** The US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has approved **generic listing standards** for crypto ETFs across Nasdaq, Cboe BZX and NYSE Arca. This eliminates the need for individual rule filings under Section 19(b), allowing new products to reach the market much faster.

### What’s changing?
- No more separate filings for each crypto ETF.  
- If the underlying asset meets **objective criteria** (e.g., traded on an ISG-member market or a CFTC-regulated futures contract with ≥ 6 months history), it qualifies under the standard.  
- This removes the **procedural bottleneck** that long delayed product launches.
