**Summary:** The US Treasury Department has issued an **Advance Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (ANPRM)** — an early formal step to gather input before drafting specific rules. The process aims to implement the newly enacted **GENIUS Act**, the first comprehensive US law on stablecoins. The public and crypto industry can comment until **October 20**.

### Key Questions
- **Issuer restrictions:** Who can and cannot issue stablecoins?
- **Sanctions compliance** (OFAC),
- **AML/KYC** requirements,
- **Balance between federal and state oversight**,
- **Tax considerations**,
- **Clarity** on reserve custody obligations,
- Comparisons to **foreign regimes** regulating payment stablecoins.

### Why It Matters
This ANPRM builds on a prior informal consultation on detecting illicit crypto activity. The GENIUS Act’s implementation will set the standards for reserves, transparency, and oversight, shaping the future of stablecoin issuance and adoption.

- **Positive:** Provides a formal channel for industry input on stablecoin rules.
- **Critical point:** The federal vs. state oversight split could complicate implementation.
- **Issuer risk:** Restrictions and custody rules could raise barriers to entry.

- **Tight timeline:** With only a month to respond, stakeholders must act quickly and precisely.
