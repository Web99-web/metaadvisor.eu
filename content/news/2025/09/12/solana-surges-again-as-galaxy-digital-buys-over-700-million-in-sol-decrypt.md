Solana Surges Again as Galaxy Digital Buys Over $700 Million in SOL

Investment firm Galaxy Digital has purchased over $700 million in Solana within the past two days, as part of its investment in SOL treasury firm Forward Industries. The price of Solana has continued to rise during that period.

Galaxy has been transferring SOL mostly from Binance — and also from Coinbase — across multiple transactions. Data from Arkham Intelligence indicates that it has bought just over 3 million SOL since Wednesday.

The asset manager may be making these purchases on behalf of Forward Industries, in which it has led a $1.65 billion investment, with participation from Jump Crypto and Multicoin Capital.

Incorporated in 1961, Forward is a Nasdaq-listed product design company that is using the proceeds of that week's raise to build what it intends to be the world's largest publicly traded Solana treasury. Galaxy is responsible for acquiring and transferring the bulk of the SOL.

Galaxy's collaboration with the firm comes as it takes a keener interest in Solana, which it described as uniquely positioned to power the next generation of capital markets in a post announcing its investment.

Galaxy founder and CEO Mike Novogratz also discussed Solana in an interview with CNBC's Squawk Box on Thursday. He stated that Solana's blockchain could process 14 billion transactions per day.

Summarizing why Galaxy is excited about Solana, he said the blockchain was now fast enough and tailor-made for financial markets.

He then referred to remarks made by SEC Chair Paul Atkins, who gave a keynote speech on Wednesday about the agency's Project Crypto initiative to encourage markets to move on-chain.
