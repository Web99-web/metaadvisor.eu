# iPhone 17 lineup compared: Which model should you buy?

Apple’s 2025 lineup includes **iPhone 17**, **iPhone Air**, **iPhone 17 Pro**, and **iPhone 17 Pro Max**. Here’s a practical, no-nonsense guide to help you pick.

## What’s new across the lineup
- **ProMotion up to 120 Hz** on every model for smoother UI and gaming.  
- **Bigger base storage (256 GB)** and tougher **Ceramic Shield 2**.  
- **New chips:** A19 on iPhone 17; **A19 Pro** on Air/Pro/Pro Max.  
- **Center Stage front camera (18 MP)** plus revamped 48 MP rear systems.

## Model snapshots

### iPhone 17 — the value pick ($799)
- **6.3" Super Retina XDR, 120 Hz**, A19, 256/512 GB.  
- **48 MP Dual Fusion** rear cameras; **18 MP Center Stage** front.  
- Best for most people who want a modern iPhone without Pro pricing.

### iPhone Air — the ultra-thin all-day iPhone ($999)
- **5.6 mm thin**, titanium frame; **6.5" 120 Hz** display.  
- **A19 Pro**, 256/512 GB/1 TB.  
- Lighter than Pro, still fast; ideal if comfort and battery life matter most.

### iPhone 17 Pro — performance + pro cameras ($1,099)
- New “plateau” design for bigger battery and cooling.  
- **Triple 48 MP** (Main, Ultra Wide, Telephoto), **A19 Pro**, 256/512 GB/1 TB.  
- For creators and power users.

### iPhone 17 Pro Max — the everything phone ($1,199)
- **Largest display (6.9")** and **longest battery life** in the lineup.  
- Up to **2 TB** storage option.  
- If you want the biggest screen and the most flexibility.

## Who should buy which?
- **Everyday users:** iPhone 17.  
- **Commuters/one-hand use, battery first:** iPhone Air.  
- **Mobile photographers/filmmakers and gamers:** iPhone 17 Pro.  
- **Big-screen multitaskers and travelers:** iPhone 17 Pro Max.
