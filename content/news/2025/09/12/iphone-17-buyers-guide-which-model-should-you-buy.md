**Short answer:** iPhone 17 for most people, Air if you prioritize thin design & battery comfort, Pro for performance + pro cameras, Pro Max if you want the biggest screen and endurance.

**What’s new across the lineup**
- **ProMotion 120 Hz** on every model for smoother UI and gaming  
- **Bigger base storage (256 GB)** and tougher **Ceramic Shield 2**  
- **Chips:** A19 (iPhone 17) and **A19 Pro** (Air/Pro/Pro Max)  
- **18 MP Center Stage** front camera; revamped **48 MP** rear systems

### Model snapshots
**iPhone 17 — the value pick ($799)**  
- 6.3" Super Retina XDR, 120 Hz; A19; 256/512 GB  
- 48 MP Dual Fusion rear; 18 MP Center Stage front  
- Best for most buyers who want the latest iPhone without Pro pricing

**iPhone Air — ultra-thin all-day iPhone ($999)**  
- 5.6 mm thin, titanium; 6.5" 120 Hz  
- A19 Pro; 256/512 GB/1 TB  
- Lighter than Pro yet fast; great if comfort and battery life matter most

**iPhone 17 Pro — performance + pro cameras ($1,099)**  
- New “plateau” design for bigger battery & cooling  
- Triple 48 MP (Main/Ultra-Wide/Telephoto); A19 Pro; 256/512 GB/1 TB  
- For creators, power users and mobile gamers

**iPhone 17 Pro Max — the everything phone ($1,199)**  
- Largest display (6.9") and longest battery life  
- Up to **2 TB** storage  
- For big-screen fans and frequent travelers

### Who should buy which?
- **Everyday users:** iPhone 17  
- **Thin & comfy all-day phone, battery first:** iPhone Air  
- **Creators/photographers/gamers:** iPhone 17 Pro  
- **Big-screen multitaskers:** iPhone 17 Pro Max
