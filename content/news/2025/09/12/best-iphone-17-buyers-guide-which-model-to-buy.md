Apple’s 2025 lineup has **iPhone 17**, **iPhone Air**, **iPhone 17 Pro** and **iPhone 17 Pro Max**. Here’s the concise guide.

## What’s new across the lineup
- **120 Hz ProMotion** on all models  
- **256 GB base** storage and tougher **Ceramic Shield 2**  
- **A19** (17) / **A19 Pro** (Air/Pro/Pro Max)

## Model snapshots
**iPhone 17 — best value** ($799)  
6.3" 120 Hz, A19, 256/512 GB; Dual 48 MP. Default pick.

**iPhone Air — thin & long-lasting** ($999)  
Titanium, 5.6 mm, 6.5" 120 Hz, A19 Pro. Comfort and battery first.

**iPhone 17 Pro — creator’s choice** ($1,099)  
Triple 48 MP incl. Telephoto, A19 Pro. For video/photo and gamers.

**iPhone 17 Pro Max — biggest & longest** ($1,199)  
6.9", longest battery, up to 2 TB.

## Who should buy which?
Everyday users → **iPhone 17**.  
Battery/comfort → **Air**.  
Creators/gamers → **Pro**.  
Big-screen multitaskers → **Pro Max**.
