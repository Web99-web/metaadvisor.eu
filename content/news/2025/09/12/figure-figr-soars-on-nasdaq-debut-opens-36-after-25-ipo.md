Figure Technology Solutions (ticker **FIGR**) made a strong public debut on the Nasdaq, **opening at $36 per share after a $25 IPO price** and valuing the company around **$7.6 billion**.

The offering raised **$787.5 million** through the sale of **31.5 million** Class A shares. Goldman Sachs, Jefferies and BofA Securities led the underwriting syndicate.

**What Figure does:** The company runs **Provenance**, a purpose-built blockchain used to originate and process home-equity and other lending workflows. Figure says its stack has been adopted by large mortgage lenders and banks, with **about $6 billion in loans facilitated in the 12 months to June 30**.

**Why it matters:** Figure is a larger fintech/blockchain IPO during a reopened U.S. issuance window. Unlike exchanges or token-price plays, revenue depends more on **loan volumes, take-rates and servicing economics**. Key risks include the **interest-rate path**, **credit performance** and **regulatory scrutiny** across mortgage markets.

**Near-term to watch**
- Stabilization above the IPO price as the **greenshoe** is exercised.
- **Volume mix** (HELOC vs. other products) and margins in the first earnings update.
- Any new partnerships that move more mortgage infrastructure onto Provenance.
