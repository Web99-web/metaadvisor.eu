**BTC is up ~8% in September**, putting this month on track to be the **strongest September since 2012** — a notable break from Bitcoin’s historically soft seasonality. This cycle continues to diverge from old calendar patterns.

For September 2025 to become the **best on record**, BTC would need **roughly another 20%** into month-end. Even without that, the print already suggests a **more mature market** with a different participant mix versus earlier cycles.

Macro tailwinds (rate-cut expectations, post-inflation sentiment) also help risk assets, but the headline remains: **BTC is, for now, outpacing its own seasonal script.**

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Seasonality isn’t destiny:** a strong September challenges the old “sell September” mantra for BTC.  
- **For traders:** watch **volatility** and reactions to macro prints; strong months often see **consolidation** next.  
- **For portfolios:** momentum is nice, but **risk management** matters — especially if chasing the last **~20%**.  
- **Bigger picture:** ETFs and institutional flows are likely reshaping **crypto’s calendar dynamics** versus prior cycles.
