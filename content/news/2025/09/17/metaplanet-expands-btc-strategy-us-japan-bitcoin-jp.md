Japan’s **Metaplanet** just pulled a three-part expansion: it formed **Metaplanet Income Corp.** in **Miami** to drive **bitcoin-linked income** (including derivatives), launched **Bitcoin Japan Inc.** in **Tokyo** to run **media and events**, and acquired the **Bitcoin.jp** domain as a national on-ramp for content and community.

The U.S. arm is designed to **generate returns from bitcoin-related products** while keeping the **core BTC treasury** ring-fenced—cleaner storytelling for investors: **hodl balance sheet** here, **cash-flow engine** there.

In Japan, **Bitcoin Japan Inc.** will oversee **Bitcoin Magazine Japan**, the **Bitcoin Japan Conference**, and **Bitcoin.jp** from a base in **Roppongi Hills**, signaling a bid to become the **local anchor** of the BTC ecosystem.

Context matters: Metaplanet recently crossed **20,000 BTC** and plans a **~¥204.1B (~$1.4B) international share sale** to add more BTC and scale the income business. From hotelier to **media, events & income hub**—with growing global reach.

> Note: **CoinDesk** and **Cointelegraph** reported the subsidiary launches and domain deal; the company confirmed via X and disclosures.

---

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Clear separation** of treasury vs. income reduces style drift and makes the reporting investable.  
- **Bitcoin.jp** is a **brand asset**: owning the generic national domain unlocks education, ads and lead flow.  
- **Risks:** derivatives add operational/regulatory risk; media is cyclical and content-hungry.  
- **Bigger picture:** with **>20k BTC** and fresh capital planned, Metaplanet is vying for the **“Asia’s MicroStrategy”** slot—plus an **income arm** to buffer cycles.
