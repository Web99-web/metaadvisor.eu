**TL;DR:** Lido is a *liquid staking* protocol. Instead of locking 32 ETH and running a validator, you stake any amount and receive **stETH** — a token that rebases daily. **wstETH** is the non-rebasing wrapped form, better suited as collateral.

#### How Lido became the default
The **network effect** did the heavy lifting: **stETH** is widely accepted as **collateral**, so one token provides **yield + working capital**. Each new integration raises **switching costs**, reinforcing dominance.

#### stETH economics
- **Rebasing vs. wrapped:** stETH increases balance; **wstETH** accrues in price.  
- **Liquidity:** deep pools and collateral markets let capital move without waiting in validator exit queues.  
- **Fees & distribution:** rewards flow to users after a protocol fee (operators + treasury).

#### Why institutions care
Custodians now bundle **custody + staking + reporting**. That addresses **operational risk** (signing, segregation) and **accounting/compliance** for yield.

#### The flip side
- **Smart-contract/operational risk**, potential **slashing**.  
- **De-peg** risk (stETH can trade away from ETH in stress).  
- **Validator concentration** & policy pressure.  
- **Governance** (LDO power, fee/policy changes).  
- **Regulation** (treatment of staking income and LSTs).

#### Watch in 2025
Validator diversification, **stETH/wstETH liquidity**, stress events, **DAO fee policy**, and **institutional wrappers** (custody products, funds).

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **stETH is DeFi’s collateral backbone.** As long as acceptance stays broad, Lido remains **default**.  
- **Risks are manageable but real** — our focus is **de-peg** risk and **operator concentration**.  
- **Institutional packaging normalises on-chain yield**, boosting liquidity while adding **centralisation points**.  
- **Portfolio note:** use stETH/wstETH for **yield + collateral** with **exposure limits** and ongoing health checks.
