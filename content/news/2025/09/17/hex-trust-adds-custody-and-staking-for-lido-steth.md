As institutional demand for **ETH yield** grows, the bottleneck is less tech and more **operations & compliance**: who holds the assets, who signs transactions, how is it booked and audited? **Hex Trust**, an institutional-grade digital asset custodian, is adding **Lido’s stETH** as both a **custody asset** and a **staking rail**. The pitch: **on-chain yield** in an institutional wrapper.

### How it works in practice
A client **deposits ETH** with the custodian and, via defined policies (multi-sig, roles, limits), initiates **staking through Lido**. The result is **stETH** (or **wstETH** for non-rebasing) **inside the custody environment**, complete with **asset segregation**, **daily reporting**, **audit trails** and **access controls**. Treasuries and funds avoid building validator ops while retaining governance over approvals.

### Why stETH
**Lido** remains Ethereum’s largest liquid-staking protocol; **stETH/wstETH** are widely accepted as **collateral** across CeFi/DeFi. That lets institutions pair **staking returns** with **operational flexibility** — e.g., using wstETH in repo/credit lines or collateral management without waiting for validator exits.

### Risks and mitigants
Yield comes with **smart-contract/operational risk** (slashing, operator error), **de-peg risk** (stETH deviating from ETH in stress), and **accounting/tax treatment** questions. The custody model addresses part of this via **approval workflows**, **security modules** and **standardised reporting**, but **market and protocol risks persist** and must be actively monitored.

**Net-net:** This is another step in **bridging TradFi processes with on-chain liquidity**. After BTC ETFs and rising demand for **ETH yield**, expect more custodians to package liquid staking into **board-friendly products**.
