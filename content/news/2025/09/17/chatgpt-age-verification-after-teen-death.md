OpenAI says it is building an **age-prediction system** to identify **under-18 users** and, where required, will ask for **ID verification**. If the system **isn’t sure**, it will **default** to the **under-18 experience**. The move follows **legal action** by the family of a 16-year-old who had chatted with the bot for months before his death.

Chief executive **Sam Altman** framed the shift as putting “**safety ahead of privacy and freedom for teens**,” adding that “**minors need significant protection**.” In practice, **graphic sexual content** will be blocked, the bot will **not flirt** with minors, and it will **avoid discussions of suicide or self-harm** — **even in** a **creative-writing** setting.

In cases of **imminent harm**, OpenAI says it will attempt to **contact parents** and, if that fails, **notify authorities**. The company may also, “**in some cases or countries**,” request **government ID** to verify age — a **privacy compromise** for adults that it argues is warranted to protect minors.

Technically, the core is **behaviour-based age estimation**, backed by an **ID fallback**. Critics note risks: **false positives/negatives**, **profiling**, and **jurisdictional mismatches** around data retention and parental contact. OpenAI has previously acknowledged that its systems are **imperfect** and pledged **further safety upgrades**.

Bottom line: ChatGPT is moving toward **age-differentiated experiences**. Whether that saves lives and reassures regulators — or opens new debates over **privacy and surveillance** — will hinge on **implementation**: accuracy, transparency, and robust **escalation pathways** to parents and institutions.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Safety > privacy** for minors is defensible, but **behavioural age-scoring** risks **profiling** and misclassification.  
- **Over- vs under-blocking:** mis-flagged adults face undue limits; missed minors face under-protection.  
- **Operational reality:** “call parents/authorities” is **legally complex** across borders; needs clear protocols and **independent audits**.  
- **Watch items:** model accuracy, **data minimisation/retention**, and third-party **assurance** to earn trust, not just request it.
