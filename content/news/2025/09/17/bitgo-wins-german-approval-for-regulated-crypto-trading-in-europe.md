**BitGo Europe GmbH** has received **BaFin** approval to offer **regulated crypto trading** in addition to **custody** and **staking** for EU clients. The significance: one platform now combines **regulated safekeeping** with **order execution**, removing a key hurdle for institutions that require supervised access to digital-asset markets.

**What is BitGo?**  
BitGo is an **institutional digital-asset platform** (founded 2013; CEO **Mike Belshe**) best known for **multisig security** and **crypto custody**. Through regulated entities across jurisdictions, BitGo provides **custody**, **wallets**, **staking**, and **trading/prime services** (liquidity, collateral, settlement) — all wrapped in workflows that treasuries, funds and banking partners expect.

Germany’s approval layers onto the **MiCA** framework, letting institutions **trade inside the custody perimeter**: orders execute while **assets remain under regulated safekeeping**, reducing operational and **counterparty** risk versus a fragmented multi-exchange/multi-custodian setup. BitGo’s aim is to package **custody + execution + liquidity** with standardized reporting, accelerating TradFi adoption.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **One-stop stack:** custody plus trading removes core frictions (ops, oversight, reporting).  
- **MiCA as safety net:** EU licensing plus BaFin oversight adds predictability for partners.  
- **Risks remain:** operational/technical, regulatory (country-by-country), and **function concentration** at a single provider.  
- **Bigger picture:** Europe is catching up with “board-ready” offerings; expect rising **institutional volumes** via regulated rails.
