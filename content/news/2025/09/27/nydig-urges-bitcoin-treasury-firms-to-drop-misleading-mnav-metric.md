**At a glance**  
**NYDIG (New York Digital Investment Group)** is calling on Bitcoin-treasury companies (**DATs — Digital Asset Treasuries**) to abandon **mNAV**, the “market-based” net-asset-value shortcut (market cap ÷ coins held). The trigger: **Strive Asset Management’s** all-stock acquisition of **Semler Scientific** — the first merger of two public DATs with sizable BTC. NYDIG says mNAV **misrepresents economics** by overlooking **operations, non-crypto assets** and **liabilities**.

---

## Why mNAV got popular — and where it breaks
mNAV (“market-based Net Asset Value”) divides **market capitalization** by the **amount of crypto held**. Investors like its **simplicity** and sometimes read it as a kind of **“yield per coin.”**  
**The catch:** DATs are **not single-asset funds**. They have operating businesses, other assets and **debt**. mNAV **misses** that — producing a veneer of precision that can **mislead**.

---

## NYDIG’s core objections
1) **Ignores businesses and other assets.**  
   Software, IP, cash flows and more don’t show up in mNAV.

2) **“Assumed” shares via convertible debt.**  
   In practice, potential shares from **convertible debt** are folded into the denominator even if **conversion conditions** aren’t met. In stress, creditors demand **cash, not stock** — a **heavier** liability than mNAV implies.

3) **Incentives for “volatility harvesting.”**  
   Convertibles are economically **debt + call option**. Management can be nudged to **amplify equity volatility** to game conversion dynamics — hardly ideal for a “treasury” story.

---

## The catalyst: first merger of two DATs
**Strive Asset Management** is acquiring **Semler Scientific** in an **all-stock deal**. Combined, they hold **>10,900 BTC**. On paper, that lifts **NAV per share**; some interpret it as “yield.” NYDIG warns mNAV may **overstate** such optics while **understating** capital-structure risks.

---

## Market backdrop and implications
- Public DATs now hold **over 1 million BTC**.  
- Many trade **below mNAV**, inviting **more M&A** (valuation arbitrage).  
- Meanwhile, **Tether** and **Circle** mint substantial profits on reserves; **new protocols** like **M^0** and **Agora** are trying to compete with alternative issuance/collateral models.

---

## Glossary
- **NYDIG — New York Digital Investment Group**: U.S. digital-asset investment and research firm.  
- **DAT — Digital Asset Treasury**: public company holding crypto (often BTC) as treasury asset.  
- **NAV — Net Asset Value**: assets minus liabilities, per share.  
- **mNAV — market-based Net Asset Value**: shortcut metric = market cap ÷ coins held.  
- **Convertible debt**: debt that **may** convert into equity; in stress, creditors typically demand **cash**.  
- **Volatility harvesting**: strategies that monetize price swings.  
- **All-stock deal**: transaction settled entirely in shares.

---

## Why investors should care
- **mNAV isn’t “yield”** and says nothing about **cash generation**.  
- **Balance-sheet quality & debt terms** (especially convertibles) drive **real risk**.  
- **Consolidation** is plausible if valuations persist below simplified ratios.

---

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **mNAV is too crude** for public DATs. Prefer **NAV, FCF, net debt, conversion terms** and **covenants**.  
- **Convertibles** can **bite** in stress; mNAV glosses over that.  
- **M&A** will likely pick up; due diligence must go **beyond quotients**.  
- **Practical step:** read the latest **convertible-note prospectus** and the **derivatives footnotes** before buying a “BTC proxy” stock.
