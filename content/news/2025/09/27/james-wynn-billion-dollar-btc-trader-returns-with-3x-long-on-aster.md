## Introduction — James Wynn is back
The pseudonymous trader **James Wynn**, infamous for his **billion-dollar BTC bets**, has returned. Earlier this year, Wynn opened a **$1.1–$1.27B BTC long with 40x leverage** on Hyperliquid. Now, he is back with a **3x long on $ASTER**, the native token of the Aster perpetuals exchange.  

This new position is modest (~**$16,000**, entry ~$1.97, liquidation ~$1.57), but the **narrative** is bigger: Wynn says he is farming the **$ASTER airdrop**, which he believes could be “one of the biggest in crypto history.”

## Recap: billion-dollar BTC bets
- In May 2025, Wynn entered a **BTC long >$1.1–1.27B with 40x leverage**.  
- The position swung between **massive unrealized gains** and **brutal drawdowns**; at one point, his entire **$50M wallet** was at risk.  
- After more than **$100M in liquidations**, Wynn flipped positions — closing, shorting, and finally claiming to walk away with a **net profit of ~$25M**.  

This combination of huge stakes, onchain transparency, and meme-worthy theatrics cemented him as one of crypto’s most notorious pseudonymous whales.

## Why ASTER?
- **3x long on $ASTER:** small in USD, big in signaling.  
- **Motivation:** Airdrop farming & showcasing confidence in a new protocol.  
- **Onchain visibility:** with tools like Onchain Lens, Wynn’s wallets are tracked closely — his trades spread like wildfire.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Leverage is lethal:** Even whales with billions on the line can end up with **nine-figure liquidations**.  
- **Narrative vs. strategy:** For the community, Wynn’s trades are entertainment — for retail, they are a **dangerous temptation**.  
- **For protocols:** whales bring hype, but without **risk frameworks** (OI caps, circuit breakers), onchain perps drift into **casino mode**.  
- **Macro lesson:** James Wynn is an extreme case, but the bigger truth remains: *Onchain perps are powerful innovations — without risk awareness, they are a recipe for disaster*.
