## DATs as new giants
Digital Asset Treasury firms are shifting from speculative wrappers into **strategic operators within blockchain ecosystems**. By holding large amounts of BTC, ETH and other tokens, they wield **direct influence** over the networks whose tokens they control.

DATs already manage more than **$105 billion in assets**, a scale few market participants fully appreciate.

## Key implications
- DATs could become **for-profit counterparts to crypto foundations**, with mandates spanning capital, operations, and governance.  
- Examples in practice:  
  - **Solana**: larger SOL stakes improve transaction finality and liquidity.  
  - **Hyperliquid**: staking HYPE can reduce user fees and boost operator revenue.  
- Unlike MicroStrategy’s bitcoin-only strategy, DATs hold **diverse mandates** and may actively shape ecosystems.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- DATs could evolve into the **“Berkshire Hathaways of crypto”**, if they leverage treasuries strategically.  
- **Centralization risk** looms, as a few firms controlling token supply means influence over governance.  
- Long-term success depends on whether they operate **transparently** and consider **community interests** alongside shareholder returns.
