**Who’s involved — and what’s a ‘family office’?**  
**YZi Labs** is the **private investment firm** (aka *family office*) of **Changpeng “CZ” Zhao** and **Yi He**. A family office is a **professional structure** managing the **personal capital** of ultra-wealthy individuals/families: portfolios, real estate, tax, philanthropy, special projects. Unlike funds that deploy outside LP capital, a family office **invests mostly its own money**, allowing more flexibility and longer time horizons.

**What’s happening at Ethena & USDe**  
Ethena Labs builds **USDe**, a “**synthetic dollar**” that now exceeds **$13B** in circulation, ranking among the largest USD-denominated crypto assets. The team is entering a new growth phase:

- **Expanding to BNB Chain** for broader reach and CeDeFi integrations.  
- **USDtb** — a **fiat-backed stablecoin** in development to complement the product suite.  
- **Converge** — an **institutional settlement layer** with **Securitize** and partners linked to **BlackRock**, aiming to connect tokenized assets with on-chain settlement.

**How USDe works (in brief)**  
USDe aims to hold around **$1** by combining:  
- **spot exposure** (BTC/ETH/SOL as collateral) and  
- **equal-value short perpetual futures** on exchanges.  

This **delta-neutral** setup reduces net market exposure; **yield** primarily comes from **funding rates** and market microstructure. Note the **risks**: funding, liquidity, volatility, basis risk, operational and counterparty risks. Robust **collateral management** and **execution** are critical.

### What does “peg” mean?
In stablecoin parlance, a **peg** is the **link between a token’s value and a reference currency**, most often the US dollar (**1 token ≈ $1**).  
- For **fiat-backed** coins (USDC, USDT), the peg relies on **cash reserves**.  
- For **synthetic/algo** designs (like USDe), the peg is maintained via **hedging mechanisms and market positions**.  
If a peg breaks (say **$0.80**), trust erodes and capital flees. The peg is the **anchor of stability** for any stablecoin.
