**TL;DR:**  
Vitalik advocates focusing on **“low-risk” DeFi**: simple, conservative financial services (savings, payments, stablecoins, fully over-collateralized lending) that generate **predictable on-chain activity** and feed Ethereum’s economy over the long run. Just as **search** was Google’s **quiet revenue engine**, this segment could be Ethereum’s **quiet gas-fee engine**. *Image is symbolic.*

### What counts as “low-risk” DeFi
- **Payments & savings**: straightforward on-chain accounts and savings products with clear, low risk.  
- **Fully over-collateralized lending**: conservative LTVs, transparent liquidation, no aggressive leverage.  
- **Stablecoins & “flatcoins”**: designs with verifiable reserves or reference baskets aimed at minimizing risk.  
- **Limited scope & formal checks**: fewer features = smaller attack surface; audits and, where possible, formal verification.

### Why this matters for Ethereum
- **Consistent fee flow**: everyday financial actions create **steady gas fees**, regardless of hype cycles.  
- **Sustainability & values**: conservative design aligns with **openness, resilience and decentralization** without relying on speculation.  
- **Room for other dApps**: a stable base lets other projects **avoid sacrificing UX and privacy** just to monetize.

### Design guardrails & implications
- **Risk configuration**: conservative LTVs, simple collateral baskets, “circuit-breaker”/pause mechanisms.  
- **Transparency**: **on-chain proof-of-reserves** or cryptographically verifiable third-party attestations; clear yield mechanics.  
- **UX & security**: less complexity, slower feature rollouts, emphasis on reliability and incident recovery.  
- **Regulatory path**: low-risk models **fare better in due diligence**, opening doors to banks/fintech.

### The Google analogy — a “quiet engine”
As **search** funded much of Google for years and subsidized other products, **low-risk DeFi** can **feed Ethereum** without dependence on memecoins or high-risk yield farming.
