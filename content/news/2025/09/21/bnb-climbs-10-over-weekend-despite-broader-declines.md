**At a glance**  
While many tokens drifted lower or went flat, **BNB** **rose about 10%** over the weekend. The move reflects a mix of **structural** forces (deflationary tokenomics, rising on-chain usage) and **mechanical** effects (thin weekend liquidity, derivatives). *Image is symbolic.*

## Why BNB is up

**1) Deflationary tokenomics (Auto-Burn + BEP-95)**  
Continuous fee burning plus the quarterly Auto-Burn create **persistent supply contraction**. With demand firming, the supply-demand balance tilts in favor of price.

**2) Stronger on-chain usage & opBNB**  
Lower costs and faster finality on **opBNB** attract high-throughput apps (gaming, social, micropayments). More transactions ⇒ **more fees burned** ⇒ stronger fundamentals for BNB.

**3) Weekend microstructure**  
Order books are **thinner** on weekends. When **spot buying** aligns with **funding/OI** positioning, small net inflows can trigger **outsized moves** (mini short-squeezes).

**4) A “pragmatic utility” narrative**  
Projects needing **low fees and easy integration** often choose BNB Chain — reinforcing a practical-utility story.

---

## How high can it go? (scenarios, not promises)

**A) Trend continuation (medium probability)**  
With healthy **spot volume** and **neutral→slightly positive funding**, a **further +12–18%** move toward the next supply zones (psych levels, prior swing highs) is plausible. A confirmed **higher-high/higher-low** setup can extend to **+25–35%**.

**B) Cool-off/consolidation (medium-high probability)**  
Typical post-rally pause: **sideways range** with **5–9% pullback** to re-test freshly broken resistance as support.

**C) Risk-off/mean-reversion (lower but relevant probability)**  
A negative macro/reg headline or funding flip could **reverse momentum**, dragging **-10–15%** toward deeper liquidity pockets.

## What to watch in the next 2 weeks

- **Spot vs. perps**: sustainable trends need **rising spot participation**, not just derivatives.  
- **Funding & OI**: prefer **moderate** funding; OI shouldn’t **run ahead of price**.  
- **On-chain metrics**: active addresses, transactions, fee revenue (feeds **BEP-95 burn**).  
- **opBNB pipeline**: new games/social/micro-apps = real demand.  
- **Risk calendar**: regulatory headlines, security incidents, large unlocks.
