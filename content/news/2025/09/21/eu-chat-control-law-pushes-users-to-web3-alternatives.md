**TL;DR:**  
The EU is advancing **Chat Control**, a proposal to scan all digital communications — including encrypted ones — under the banner of child protection. Critics say this amounts to **mass surveillance of all citizens**, while criminals will keep using harder-to-monitor tools. Expect a shift toward **Web3-based alternatives**. *Image is symbolic.*

### What Chat Control entails
- Scanning of all messages, including **end-to-end encrypted chats**.  
- Mandatory filters & algorithms to flag “suspicious content.”  
- Oversight reaching **private chats, emails, and cloud services**.  
- Significant **censorship and abuse risks**.  

### Why Web3
- **Decentralized networks** without central servers.  
- **True end-to-end encryption** without backdoors.  
- **Blockchain identities** with user control.  
- **Censorship resistance** via distributed architecture.  

### Impact
- Undermines the **fundamental right to privacy**.  
- **Chilling effect** and self-censorship.  
- Measures **miss the intended targets** while hitting ordinary users.
