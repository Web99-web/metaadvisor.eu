**Summary:**  
Analysts at **Jefferies** argue that crypto today is roughly where the **internet was in 1996** — meaning it’s **not too late** to get exposure, especially for those looking **beyond BTC** (ETFs, tokenization, equities and “digital asset treasury” companies). Wall Street’s message: **the next leg of growth is just beginning**. *Image is symbolic.*

### What the analysts are really saying
- **The “1996 moment”**: a nod to early-internet dynamics — **early adoption** and **ample room for penetration** among traditional funds.  
- **Institutional interest is rising**: more funds are **formalizing mandates** and allocation models (tokens, **ETFs**, crypto-exposed equities, **DATs**).  
- **Beyond Bitcoin**: focus spans **infrastructure**, **real-world asset (RWA) tokenization**, **payments**, staking/yield layers, plus software/services that **ease adoption**.

### Why it’s “not too late”
- **Low institutional penetration**: only **a minority** of traditional funds have material crypto exposure so far.  
- **Better investment wrappers**: **spot/derivatives ETFs**, regulated custody and accounting standards lower barriers.  
- **Underlying theses**: rising on-chain activity, **tokenized cash flows**, and **TradFi integration** create **real demand** beyond speculation.

### Where the opportunities are (beyond BTC)
- **Infrastructure & middleware**: networks, L2s, validation, data & security layers (audits, monitoring).  
- **RWA & settlement**: tokenized funds, bonds, receivables; on-chain settlement in institutional settings.  
- **Capital bridges**: exchanges, brokers, custodians, **compliance** and analytics — enabling **enterprise-grade** access.  
- **Yield layers**: staking and yield mechanisms where **risk management** and **transparency** are paramount.

### How to approach (frameworks, not advice)
- **DCA & barbell**: pair **low-cost beta exposure** (e.g., ETFs) with **selective alpha** (infra/RWA).  
- **Diversify risk carriers**: tokens, ETFs, equities, private rounds — different risk/liquidity profiles.  
- **Operational discipline**: custody, risk assessments, and **position concentration limits**.

### Risks you can’t ignore
- **Regulatory pivots** and jurisdictional differences.  
- **Volatility** and correlations with tech equities / USD liquidity.  
- **Operational risks**: custody, smart contracts, market microstructure (liquidity, funding).

---

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- The **“still early”** thesis holds while institutional penetration remains low and **investment wrappers** improve.  
- **Beyond BTC** is the right lens: **RWA/tokenization and infrastructure** have the strongest real-economy multipliers.  
- **Practical path**: systematic **DCA**, clear **risk limits**, and a **beta+alpha** mix often beat all-or-nothing bets.
