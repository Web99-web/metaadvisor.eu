**Summary:**  
We’re **not** saying the market will “surely drop more.” Base case is **consolidation**. If you want a plan, use one of two robust paths: **(1) ladder buy on pullbacks** or **(2) buy strength only **after confirmation**—both with clear stops/invalidations, not gut feel.

## Path 1 — Buy weakness, but **in tranches** (ladder)
- Split entries into **3–4 lots** (e.g., 25 % each).  
- Place limits **below** price at logical liquidity/structure (former supports, demand zones).  
- **Invalidation/stop** just beyond the last reasonable support.  
- **Bonus tell**: a brief **flush** with falling **OI** and normalized **funding** is often better than buying a slow bleed.

## Path 2 — Buy strength, but **after confirmation** (trend-follow)
- Don’t bottom-pick. Wait for **2–3 consecutive days** of **ETF inflows**, a **softer USD (DXY)**, and a **reclaim** of a key area.  
- **Enter** on the **retest** (former resistance acting as support).  
- **Stop** just below that retest; **add** only if **spot volume** confirms.

## What **not** to do
- No **all-in** at a single level.  
- Don’t ignore **funding/OI**: persistently high funding with weak **spot** makes buy-the-dip fragile.  
- Don’t **chase green candles**; patience beats FOMO.

## Quick **signal compass**
- **Downside risk**: firmer **USD/DXY** + **ETF outflows** + high **funding/OI** > **spot** → easier **grind-down**.  
- **Upside risk**: softer **USD** + **2–3** days **ETF inflows** + **spot** > **perps** → better odds of a **bounce/trend turn**.
