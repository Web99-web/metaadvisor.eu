**Summary:**  
A new **Deutsche Bank** report argues **Bitcoin** could appear on **central bank balance sheets** by **2030** as a recognized **reserve asset**. **Gold** should retain primacy and the **USD** its dominance, yet signs of **diversification** are emerging. *Image is symbolic.*

### What the report says
- **USD** still ≈ **57%** of global reserves, yet **trim moves** are visible (e.g., China cut Treasuries by **$57B** in 2024).  
- **Gold** sits at **record highs** (around **$3,763**, **>40% YTD**).  
- **BTC volatility is falling**: August 30-day vol hit **historic lows** even as price printed **> $123,500** — pointing to a **maturing profile**.  
- **No dollar dethroning**: neither **BTC** nor **gold** will unseat the **USD** — governments protect **monetary sovereignty**.  
- **Adoption arc**: likely mirrors **gold’s path** — from skepticism to broader acceptance via **regulation** and **time**.

### Why this matters
- **New demand channel**: even **small reserve slices** create **structural, sticky demand**.  
- **From risk-on to reserve-ready**: lower vol + **ETF wrappers**/**custody** make BTC more **balance-sheet-friendly**.  
- **Geopolitical push**: sanctions risk and bloc dynamics favor **diversification** away from single-asset dependence.

### Broader context & numbers
- **Profit rotation**: when **BTC** dipped, **gold** popped — signaling **rotation** into metals.  
- **Regulatory momentum**: clearer **crypto rules** and frameworks like **OECD/CARF** add **legitimacy**.  
- **Corporate balance sheets**: more **public companies** hold **BTC**, normalizing the narrative.

### Risks & caveats
- **Volatility** still **above gold’s** — some CBs will **wait**.  
- **Politics**: electoral cycles & **monetary policy** can **slow** adoption.  
- **Accounting/ops**: **fair-value treatment** and **institutional custody** remain practical hurdles.
