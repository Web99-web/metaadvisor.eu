**TL;DR:**  
A **TGE** is when a project **creates and lists** its token. Trouble starts when the **token arrives before the product**: price pops due to **scarce float**, while **utility & real users** lag behind. Repeated **unlock waves** then weigh on price – and many chase **airdrops** instead of using the product. *Image is symbolic.*

## What a TGE is – with a simple analogy
Think of selling **store coupons** **before** the store opens. Coupons are scarce → price rises. Later, more coupons **unlock** and price drops.  
That’s many TGEs: **tiny free float**, **high FDV (paper valuation)**, **late utility**.

## Where the issues come from
- **High FDV, low free float** → thin books, **wild price swings**, poor discovery.  
- **Vesting/cliffs** → steady **new supply** (team/investors) creates **downward pressure**.  
- **Points/airdrop farming** → activity for **rewards**, not for the **product** (no PMF).  
- **Token before product** → financialization crowds out **UX & build**.  
- **CEX-first hype** → loud day-one, little **on-chain traction** later.

## How it tends to play out
1) **Hype & points** → airdrop hunt.  
2) **TGE + listing** → spike on scarce float.  
3) **Unlock waves** → sell pressure, **fade**.  
4) **Community fatigue** if **utility lags**.

## How strong teams do it better
- **Lower FDV + higher initial float** → fairer spread, steadier pricing.  
- **Metric-gated unlocks** → not just time: **active users, retention, revenue**.  
- **Fair price discovery** → **LBP/auction** instead of pure CEX spectacle.  
- **Lockdrop/retrodrop** → reward **actual usage**, not clicks.  
- **Product-first, token-second** → TGE when **core utility is live**.  
- **Clear decentralization map** → real governance, not a “zombie DAO”.

## For investors (not advice)
- **Check FDV & float**: high FDV + low float = **roller coaster**.  
- **Read the vesting**: who gets how much, and **when**?  
- **Look for real usage**: users, revenue, retention – not just tx counts.  
- Prefer **LBP/auction-based** launches.  
- **Plan liquidity**: book/pool depth, slippage, exit risk.

## Quick glossary
- **FDV**: value **if all tokens were circulating**.  
- **Free float**: **tradable** supply in the market.  
- **Vesting/unlocks**: gradual release of **locked** tokens.  
- **LBP**: launch mechanism for **truer** price discovery.  
- **PMF**: the product solves a **real problem**; users come **without bribes**.
