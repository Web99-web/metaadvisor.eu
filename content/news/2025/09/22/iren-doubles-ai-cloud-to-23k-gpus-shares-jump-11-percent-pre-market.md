**Summary:**  
**IREN** says it has **doubled** its AI cloud to **23,000 GPUs** and lifted its **annualized run-rate** target to **>$500M by Q1/2026**. The stock traded **+11% pre-market**. Management stresses growth funding will **not impair** its **50 EH/s** bitcoin mining operations. *Image is symbolic.*

## Key numbers (what was bought, where it goes)
- **New spend (~$674M):**  
  **7,100** × NVIDIA **B300**, **4,200** × NVIDIA **B200**, **1,100** × AMD **MI350X**.  
- **Expanded fleet (per disclosure):**  
  **1,900** × **H100/H200**, **19,100** × **B200/B300**, **1,200** × **GB300**, **1,100** × **MI350X**.  
- **Sites & scale:** staged deliveries; **Prince George** campus + **Texas** facilities can ultimately host **>60,000 Blackwell GPUs**.  
- **Commercial target:** **annualized run-rate >$500M** by **Q1/2026**.

## Why it matters
- **From mining to compute:** ex-miners are reusing **power/land/cooling** to build **AI/ML cloud** and lift **ROIC**.  
- **Compute supercycle:** demand for **Blackwell** (B200/B300/GB300) raises barriers; fast builders **monetize earlier**.  
- **Revenue diversification:** building an **enterprise** client base reduces BTC price cyclicality.

## What it means for bitcoin mining
- Company is pursuing **financing** tracks to expand **without hurting** the **50 EH/s** hash rate.  
- **Synergies:** power & datacenter moats benefit both **BTC** and **AI**; modular capacity enables **dynamic allocation** by margin.

## Broader context & implications
- **Supply & cash conversion:** staging lowers risk but pushes **revenue realization** into later quarters.  
- **GPU rental pricing:** LT deals add visibility; **pricing pressure** is still possible as supply scales.  
- **Energy & permits:** **cheap, stable power** and permits remain the key **moat**.

## Risks
- **Capex/financing:** heavy upfronts; terms matter.  
- **Utilization:** if **GPU hours** lag plan, **run-rate** targets slip.  
- **Pricing war:** falling **$/GPU-hour** or **$/TFLOP-hour** compress margins.  
- **Supply/integration:** delays in delivery, power, cooling, software.

- **BTC balance:** the explicit **ring-fence** of **50 EH/s** addresses cannibalization fears.  
- **Re-rating trigger:** delivering **>$500M run-rate** (or growing enterprise backlog) could shift multiples toward **AI infrastructure**.
