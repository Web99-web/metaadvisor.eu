**Summary:**  
BTC’s quick move toward **~112k** came from **cascading long liquidations** in **thin liquidity**, amplified by a **stronger USD (DXY)** and **inconsistent ETF flows**. After leverage is flushed, we expect **consolidation** until a clearer **spot bid** emerges or the **USD** softens. *Image is symbolic.*

## What happened (the mechanics)
- **Cascading squeeze:** crowded longs unwound sequentially; stops/algos accelerated the move.  
- **Stronger dollar = headwind:** a **DXY** rebound typically weighs on risk assets, crypto included.  
- **ETF flows not bedrock:** mixed in/out days don’t provide a steady “bid under the market” during shocks.  
- **Thin books:** outside EU/US core hours, order books are shallower — small net selling moves price disproportionately.

## Why ~112k?
That area held **liquidity pockets** (stops/liquidations) below fresh swing supports. Once tapped, **chain liquidations** magnify the move until leverage resets.

## What to watch next 24–72h
- **USD/DXY:** a softer dollar often aids risk-asset recoveries.  
- **ETF net flows (daily):** look for **2–3 consecutive inflow days** to lay a firmer floor.  
- **Spot vs. perps:** durable rebounds need **spot participation**, not just perp bounces.  
- **Funding & OI:** normalization post-flush reduces the odds of another cascade.  
- **Key levels:** reclaim and hold freshly broken resistances as **support**.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Base case:** post-flush **consolidation** (saw-tooth) until spot bid firms or USD eases.  
- **Turn signal:** **softer DXY** + **multi-day ETF inflows** + **spot > perps** often kick off risk-on.  
- **Tactics:** prefer **buying pullbacks into support** or **confirmed reclaim/retest** over catching knives.  
- **Risk of another flush:** if USD stays firm and ETF flows remain choppy, another brief washout is possible before stabilization.
