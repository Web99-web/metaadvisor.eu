**Summary:**  
A new regulatory cycle (EU, U.S. and global) is steering crypto toward **tighter KYC/AML controls**, reporting and **data exchange**. While **centralized VASPs** adapt relatively fast, **DeFi/self-custody** still show gaps — especially on **privacy**, **identity proofs** and the **practical enforcement of the Travel Rule**. *Image is symbolic.*

## What’s changing (rule highlights)
- **FATF Travel Rule:** VASPs must exchange **originator/beneficiary data** above set thresholds.  
- **EU MiCA + AMLA:** oversight for issuers and providers; **source-of-funds** checks, stricter **screening** and **risk scoring**.  
- **U.S. / international:** tougher **sanctions lists**, FinCEN/OFAC requirements and **regtech** that blends **AI** with **on-chain analytics**.
