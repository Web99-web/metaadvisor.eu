### Why it matters
- **Rebound confidence:** whales often accumulate when **books are thin** and **sentiment fragile**.  
- **Different beta buckets:** alongside **BTC/SOL** (large caps), **HYPE/PUMP** target **higher beta** → bigger moves if markets bounce.  
- **Perps dynamics:** **funding** & **open interest** can **amplify** moves; small net inflows move price disproportionately.

### What to watch (next 48–72h)
- **Funding & OI (perps):** look for **normalization** post-flush; elevated funding = **overstretched longs**.  
- **Spot vs. perps:** durable rebounds need **spot participation**, not just perp bounces.

- **PCE (Fri):** higher = **risk-off**; lower = **risk-on**.
