**Summary:**  
The **UAE** has signed on to the **OECD’s Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF)** — a global standard for the **automatic exchange** of tax-relevant crypto data. In practice, **VASPs** (exchanges, custodians, brokers) must **identify clients** and **report** data to tax authorities for cross-border exchange. *Image is symbolic.*

## What is CARF and why it matters
- **CARF** brings crypto assets into a regime similar to **CRS** (Common Reporting Standard) for bank accounts.  
- Goal: reduce **tax evasion** and **regulatory arbitrage** across borders.  
- Result: **less tax privacy** for crypto, especially on cross-border flows.

## What gets reported
- **Identity/residency** (KYC),  
- **Balances and flows** at VASPs,  
- **Transactions** (fiat & crypto deposits/withdrawals),  
- **Token** and **network** identifiers per CARF taxonomy.

## Who’s covered
- **UAE residents** with VASP accounts,  
- **Non-residents** using UAE VASPs,  
- **Institutions** offering custody, exchange, brokerage or token issuance.

## Business & user impact
- **VASPs:** stronger **KYC/AML**, **data mapping**, periodic **CARF reporting**.  
- **Users:** less “anonymous room”; **tax residency** becomes key to treatment of income/cap gains.  
- **Projects/funds:** tokenization and on-chain ops become **tightly linked** to investors’ home-country rules.

## When does it start?
The OECD envisions a **phased rollout**; timelines vary by jurisdiction. By signing, the UAE signals intent for **full implementation**.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Short-term trade-off:** capital that chose the UAE **primarily for secrecy** will likely **re-route** (toward less compliant venues/OTC/P2P).  
- **Medium-term logic:** UAE swaps “hot money” for **institutional, bankable capital** (better reputation, less FATF/OECD heat).  
- **CARF ≠ public database:** it’s **government-to-government exchange**, not public doxxing — acceptable for HNWIs/corporates with proper tax setups.  
- **Signal to the market:** higher **compliance costs** for VASPs but a **larger TAM**; watch **VARA registrations**, banking rails, OTC vs. spot share.
