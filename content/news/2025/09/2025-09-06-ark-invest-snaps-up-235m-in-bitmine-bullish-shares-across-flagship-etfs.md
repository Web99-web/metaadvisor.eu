Cathie Wood’s ARK Invest increased its exposure to crypto-related equities on Friday, buying more than $23.5 million worth of BitMine Immersion Technologies (BMNR) and crypto exchange Bullish, the parent company of CoinDesk, across three of its actively managed ETFs.

Trade disclosures show the ARK Innovation ETF (ARKK), ARK Next Generation Internet ETF (ARKW), and ARK Fintech Innovation ETF (ARKF) collectively picked up 387,000 BitMine shares and 144,000 Bullish shares, valued at over $23.5 million at Friday’s close.

ARKK led the way, adding 257,108 BitMine shares and 81,811 Bullish shares, while ARKW and ARKF split the remainder.

Alongside these additions, ARK trimmed positions in DraftKings, Roku, Roblox, and chipmaker Teradyne.

BitMine is currently the largest “ether treasury” firm, holding 1.87 million ETH worth more than $8 billion. Its stock fell 0.3% during Friday’s trading session and another 1.17% in after-hours trading.

Bullish went public in August through a $1.1 billion IPO after scrapping an earlier SPAC plan. ARK was a major day-one buyer, snapping up $172 million worth of shares. The stock rose 6% on Friday, extending momentum from its debut, but slipped 1.5% in after-hours trading.

Meanwhile, spot Ether ETFs shed $952 million over the past five days as recession fears intensified.

Despite the outflows, ether rose more than 16% in the past month, partly fueled by the passage of the GENIUS legislation.

**Our view:**  
ARK once again demonstrates its aggressive stance toward crypto investments. By doubling down on BitMine and Bullish, the fund signals that major players still want exposure to the ETH ecosystem and crypto exchanges, even amid short-term ETF outflows.
