Bad news has just been bad news over the past 24 hours. Friday’s weak U.S. jobs report bolstered bets on deeper Fed cuts, but Bitcoin (BTC) at $110,617.69 hasn’t responded positively.

The leading cryptocurrency by market value stayed under $112,000 instead of rallying on the prospect of looser monetary policy, as many had anticipated. The lack of upward momentum suggests the potential for a deeper sell-off.

Job seekers struggled in August, with nonfarm payrolls adding just 22,000 jobs – far short of the Dow Jones projection of 75,000. The report also revised down June and July’s combined job creation by 21,000. June’s figure now shows a net loss of 13,000 jobs.

Nine sectors, including manufacturing, construction and professional services, recorded losses, while healthcare and leisure & hospitality provided bright spots.

The Kobeissi Letter called the report “absolutely insane,” describing the revisions as evidence of a broken system and a labor market edging into recession territory.

Following the release, the probability of a Fed rate cut at the Sept. 17 meeting surged to 100%, while odds of a 50-basis-point cut rose to 12%. Expectations for additional cuts in November and December also climbed, pushing Treasury yields lower.

Upcoming revisions to prior jobs reports are expected to fuel more rate-cut bets. “The BLS will announce annual benchmark revisions on Tuesday, and they are expected to point to even weaker job growth earlier. Some surveys suggest between 500,000 and 1 million jobs could be revised away,” said Marc Chandler, Managing Director and Chief Market Strategist at Bannockburn Global Forex.

**Our view:**  
Bitcoin’s failure to rally on dovish Fed expectations suggests the market is waiting for stronger catalysts. Institutional ETF inflows and macroeconomic stability will likely dictate the next move.
