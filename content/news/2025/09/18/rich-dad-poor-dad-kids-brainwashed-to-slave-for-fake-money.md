**Robert Kiyosaki**, author of *Rich Dad, Poor Dad* and a long-time Bitcoin proponent, reiterated that it’s “**criminal**” to teach children to work for **inflationary, ‘fake’ money** (fiat) while overlooking alternatives like **gold, silver, Bitcoin and Ethereum** — his shorthand: **“hard money.”** He accuses the **education system** of **indoctrinating** kids to “work for fake money” instead of understanding **real money and assets**.

Kiyosaki has long criticized the **fiat regime** and how **inflation erodes savings**, urging the **accumulation of real assets**. In recent remarks he repeated his preference for **gold, silver, oil, Bitcoin and Ether**, arguing these are **scarce and/or real-economy-backed stores of value**, unlike “paper money” that can be **issued at will**.

At the same time, he does not ignore **market bubbles**: even while promoting the hard-money narrative, he warns that **prices can drop sharply** — **including BTC**. Over the long run, he says, those who **understand money** and hold **real assets** will fare better than those saving in “fake” money.

Critics note the fear-driven story also doubles as **marketing** for his books and content. Still, Kiyosaki remains an **influential voice** in crypto and hard-money circles, especially among readers skeptical of the **fiat system** and recent **monetary policy**.
