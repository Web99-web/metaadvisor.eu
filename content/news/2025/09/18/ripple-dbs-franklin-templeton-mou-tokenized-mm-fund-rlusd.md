**DBS**, **Franklin Templeton** and **Ripple** have agreed to collaborate on **trading and lending** solutions on the **XRP Ledger**. Franklin Templeton plans to tokenize its **U.S. dollar short-term money market fund**, while **DBS Digital Exchange (DDEx)** will list the **fund token ‘sgBENJI’** **alongside RLUSD**, enabling clients to **rebalance between a stablecoin and a yield-bearing MM fund** — earning yield even through volatile periods.

Ripple calls the setup a **“game-changer”**: pairing a **tokenized MM fund** with a **regulated, liquid medium of exchange** (RLUSD) can unlock **on-chain repo** and more efficient **collateral settlement**. **DBS** adds that tokenized securities can inject **efficiency and liquidity** into global markets and is considering allowing **sgBENJI** holders to **pledge tokens as collateral** within the DDEx ecosystem.

In practice, **XRP Ledger** provides a **public, enterprise-grade settlement layer**, **RLUSD** serves as the **stable unit of account**, and **sgBENJI** represents **tokenized shares** of short-duration USD instruments. The trio enables **T+0 transfers**, **on-chain transparency**, and **NAV-aware rebalancing** between cash (stablecoin) and yield (MM fund).

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **RWA done pragmatically:** a MM fund + stablecoin is a **natural pair** for repo, collateral and treasury workflows.  
- **Distribution & liquidity matter:** a DDEx listing helps, but **market-making** and **redemption mechanics** will determine real activity.  
- **Risks:** **regulatory** (stablecoins, funds), **tokenization ops** (NAV oracles, redemption), and **issuer concentration**.  
- **Bigger picture:** another step in **TradFi → on-chain**; if it works, expect the pattern on other L1/L2s too.
