If crypto sounded like a spaceship, here’s the **coffee-table** version. **Bitcoin (BTC)** and **Ethereum (ETH)** aren’t “the same crypto.” They play **different roles** — two tools for different jobs.

## Bitcoin: like gold in a safe
Picture a **gold bar**. It doesn’t do tricks or pay interest — but it **stores value** and it’s **yours**.  
**Bitcoin** is that, digitally: **fixed supply (21M)**, **no issuer**, **no off-switch** if you self-custody.  
When people worry about inflation, freezes or capital controls, they keep **some savings in BTC**. Not magic — just **hard savings without anyone’s permission**.

**How does BTC “happen”?**  
Through **mining** (computers secure the network). You don’t need the guts to **buy, hold and send** it.

## Ethereum: like a city on the internet
Think **city**: roads, power, buildings, shops, residents.  
**Ethereum** is that **digital city**.  
- **Roads/power** = **Ethereum L1**  
- **Fuel** = **ETH gas** (small fee to make things run)  
- **Buildings/shops** = **apps** (DeFi, stablecoins, NFTs, games …)  
- **Residents/visitors** = **users**  
- **Builders** = **developers**  
- **Utilities** = **validators** (security via **staking**)

In this city, things are **built and used**: send dollar-tokens, borrow/lend, buy tickets, play games … Most of that needs a bit of **gas (ETH)** — so **ETH has demand as “fuel.”**

**How is it secured?**  
Via **staking**: people **lock ETH**, help the network and receive **rewards in ETH**. (Think “crypto time deposit” — with risks but no hardware.)

## Why do prices sometimes move differently?
- **BTC** reacts to the **big picture**: inflation, rates, “safe haven.”  
- **ETH** reacts to **city activity**: more use/building ⇒ more **gas** ⇒ stronger ETH demand.  
Often the **same direction**, but **not the same pace**.

## “So what do I do?”
For a small, simple crypto sleeve:
- Treat **BTC** as your **savings foundation**. No guarantees — but the idea is **“hold, don’t overtrade.”**  
- Treat **ETH** as your **city ticket**. You own it if you believe **more will happen on Ethereum**.

## Common questions
**Do I need to mine BTC?** No. For most, **buy & hold** is plenty.  
**Can I earn on ETH without trading?** Via **staking** — but only with **reputable providers** and a clear view of risks.  
**Why are fees sometimes high?** Like rush-hour tolls: **more traffic = higher tolls**. That’s why **L2 “suburbs”** exist (cheaper & faster atop Ethereum).

## A keep-it-simple start
Say: “I want **a little crypto**, but **sleep well**.”  
- Put a **larger share** of that crypto pocket in **BTC** (issuer-free savings).  
- Put a **smaller share** in **ETH** (a bet on the growing city).  
- Everything else (memecoins, experiments) is **spice, not the main course**.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
BTC and ETH aren’t rivals — they’re **two pillars**: **BTC** as **digital gold** (savings), **ETH** as a **digital city** (a platform where you build and pay). For beginners, it’s enough to grasp the **role**. Foundation (BTC) and city (ETH) give your crypto slice **structure and sense** — add the rest only when it feels right and you know what you’re doing.
