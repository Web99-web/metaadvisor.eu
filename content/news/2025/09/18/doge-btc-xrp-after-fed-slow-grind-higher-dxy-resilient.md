Following the **-25 bps** move and hints the Fed could **speed up easing** over the next 12 months if needed, **Bitcoin** broke out of its multi-day range and **touched ~$117.9k** — the highest since Aug. 17. **Ether (ETH)** is **~+2.7%** but still trading inside a **four-week narrowing triangle**, which keeps the tone “wait for confirmation.”

**Alts** caught a bid: **DOGE, SOL and BNB** are **>4%** on the day, and **XRP ~+3%** after a breakout from a descending triangle. For **SOL**, a kicker is the **CME** scheduling **SOL options** for **Oct 13** (and **XRP options** the same day) — giving institutions better tools to manage exposure and potentially **adding liquidity**.

The **bigger picture** is mixed: the **Dollar Index (DXY)** remains **resilient**, so crypto’s advance is **gradual**, not a “clean” breakout. Also, **futures open interest** is growing faster than **spot demand**, suggesting a **derivatives-led** move; a more durable trend likely needs **broader spot buying**.

---

### Our take (Metaadvisor)

The Fed **eased the brake a notch**, but the dollar **didn’t crack** — hence **stair-step gains** instead of fireworks. **BTC** is the **engine**, while alts (**DOGE** as sentiment gauge, **SOL/XRP** on the CME story) ride the **beta wave**. Until **spot demand** thickens, expect **“two steps up, one sideways”** and a market hungry for **fresh catalysts** (next macro prints, ETF flows, CME open interest). For portfolios: keep a **BTC core**, run **smaller tactical risk** in alts — and keep one eye on the **dollar**.
