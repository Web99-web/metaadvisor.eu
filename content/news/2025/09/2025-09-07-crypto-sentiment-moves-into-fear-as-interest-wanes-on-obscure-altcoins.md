Crypto market sentiment fell into **‘Fear’** as investors temporarily step back from the lower end of the **risk curve**, focusing again on **BTC** and **ETH**. The **Crypto Fear & Greed Index** also shows a move into “Fear.”

Santiment notes traders are “less interested in obscure altcoins,” with debates centering on which **major asset** could lead the next leg higher. Meanwhile, some analysts flag **near-term indecision** at key levels and the possibility of sweeping local lows before any durable bounce.

The broader backdrop is one of **quality rotation**: more liquid, institutionally tracked coins retain leadership while **riskier microcaps** lose momentum until risk appetite convincingly returns.

**Our view:**  
“Fear” regimes are often **contrarian**. Remember the “is ETH dead?” chatter a few months back — followed by a sharp leg up. Near-term ETF outflows and weaker microcap appetite don’t break the medium-term ETH case. Watch **flows & liquidity** (ETF/CEX on-ramps), **derivatives** (funding/basis), and **on-chain** (L2 activity, staking, gas). When those align, “Fear” tends to act as an **opportunity**, not a capitulation signal.
