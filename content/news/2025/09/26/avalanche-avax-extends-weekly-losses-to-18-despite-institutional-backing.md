**Briefly:** **AVAX** slid about **8%** on Friday to **$27.72**, taking **weekly losses** to roughly **18%**. The weakness is part of a broader market pullback — **ETH, SOL and DOGE** show double-digit weekly drops, while **BTC ~−6%**.

### Levels & volume
- **Resistance:** around **$30.28**; multiple failed break attempts.  
- **Support:** near **$27.65**, currently **fragile**.  
- **Volume (early session):** about **121,896 AVAX** — suggests **institutional selling is slowing**, but **no reversal** yet.

### Institutional narrative isn’t lifting price (yet)
- Earlier this week **AgriFORCE** rebranded to **AVAX One** and announced plans to **raise $550M** to **acquire and hold AVAX** — aiming to become the first **Nasdaq-listed** vehicle focused solely on Avalanche and to accumulate **>$700M** in the token.  
- Advisory team led by **Anthony Scaramucci** (SkyBridge) and **Brett Tejpaul** (Coinbase Institutional).  
- For now the market remains **cautious**; **regulatory approvals** for token vehicles are pending and **net inflows** are insufficient.

### Broader context
- Sector-wide **risk-off**; rotations and thinner liquidity amplify **slippage** at resistance bands.  
- Avalanche’s **roadmap** (partnerships, enterprise use cases) is **constructive**, but short-term **selling pressure** dominates.

- The **institutional story** supports the **longer horizon**, but needs **clear regulation** and **steady inflows**.
