**Briefly:** Despite the release of **PCE inflation data** (a key Fed indicator), **BTC** failed to attract buyers and **dropped below $109K**. This broke the short-term market structure. Now, focus shifts to the **liquidity pocket near $107K** and the upcoming **options expiry**.

### Macro signal (*why PCE didn’t help*)
- PCE came in **as expected** → no immediate Fed policy shift.  
- Market returned to **micro drivers**: order-book liquidity, short-term momentum, and option levels.

### Technicals and levels
- **$109K**: repeatedly tested support, now **broken**.  
- **$107K**: liquidity cluster, potential “magnet” level.  
- **$112K–113K**: first resistance if price bounces.

### Derivatives and sentiment
- **Funding** low/positive → reduced demand for **leveraged longs**.  
- **Implied volatility (IV)** compressed → increases risk of **sudden moves** through thin order books.  
- **Options / max pain** near **$110K**, can pull price short term, but liquidity below remains dominant.

### Broader context
- **Gold** and **AI/HPC stocks** continue to attract capital, reducing BTC’s appeal.  
- On-chain perps market is **fragmented**, slippage and liquidity depth vary across venues.

---

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Scenarios:**  
  - **Reclaim $109K** with volume → quick relief rally to **$112–113K**.  
  - **Failed retest of $109K** → eyes on **$107K**; buyer response will decide whether it’s a **local bottom** or a **step down**.  
- **Risk of low IV:** compression often ends with **sharp breakouts**.  
- **Key levels to watch:** **107K / 109K / 112–113K**, plus order-book depth and option flows.
