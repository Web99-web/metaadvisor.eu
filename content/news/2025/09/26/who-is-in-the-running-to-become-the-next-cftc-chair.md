Following Cointelegraph’s reporting, Washington is abuzz with speculation about **who will take the helm of the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC)** after chair **Rostin Behnam** steps down.

👉 **CFTC stands for Commodity Futures Trading Commission — a U.S. federal agency founded in 1974.** Its core mandate is **oversight of derivatives markets** (futures, options, swaps), **investor protection**, and **market integrity**. Over the last decade, the CFTC has also taken on parts of **crypto oversight**, since **Bitcoin and Ethereum** are treated as **commodities** in the U.S., including **crypto derivatives** and the venues that list them.

While **no official short list** has been released, names circulating in D.C. include veterans from **financial institutions**, **former regulators**, and **legal experts** with digital-asset experience. Depending on the pick, the agency could lean **more innovation-friendly** — or **double down on stricter enforcement**.

### Key issues awaiting the next chair
- **Coordination with the SEC** on the line between **commodities vs. securities**.  
- Frameworks for **crypto derivatives** (futures/options) and **stablecoins**.  
- Oversight of **platforms/intermediaries** and standards for **manipulation & market integrity**.
