**Briefly:** When altcoins take the lead, it’s usually **capital rotation**. After a **mature BTC trend** (rally → plateau), profits rotate into **ETH** and major **L1/L2s**, then into **theme pockets** (DeFi, RWA, AI, memecoins). Key levers: **declining BTC dominance**, **rising ETH/BTC**, **net stablecoin issuance**, and **catalysts** (airdrops, launches, narratives).

### 1) BTC dominance & “trend saturation”
- **BTC.D**: alt seasons more often start when **dominance turns down** after BTC-led phases.  
- **Profit structure**: long-only/institutional money books gains in BTC → **seeks beta** in larger alts.

### 2) ETH/BTC as a semaphore
- **Uptrending ETH/BTC** = frequent **green light** for majors rotation.  
- Flow then spills into **L2s**, **DeFi blue chips**, and beyond.

### 3) Liquidity: stablecoins & on-ramps
- **Net issuance of USDT/USDC** signals **fresh buying power**.  
- Better **on-ramps** + stablecoin inflows often **precede** broader alt rallies.

### 4) Derivatives: funding/IV & leverage
- **Low IV** + **moderate/low funding** ⇒ cheaper carry, rotations ignite more easily.  
- **Rising OI** across venues, without extreme funding, implies **healthier demand**.

### 5) Catalysts & narratives
- **Airdrops, L2/L3 launches, big integrations**, and **narratives** (RWA, AI, DePIN, gaming) direct flows.  
- **Listings/partnerships** and **tokenomics** (unlock schedules, sinks, buybacks) can **accelerate or stall** a wave.

### 6) Market structure & depth
- Winners are **pairs with depth**, quality **MMs**, and **low fees** (less slippage).  
- On-chain perps incentives can **inflate** volumes temporarily — **durability** matters.

### 7) Macro
- **Risk appetite** (softer USD, lower real rates) supports alts.  
- **Policy/ETF shocks** can **shut rotations down** and pull capital back to BTC/ETH.

---

## Practical checklist
- **BTC.D** & **ETH/BTC** (daily/3D trend).  
- **Net stablecoin issuance** & on-ramp volumes.  
- **Funding/IV/OI** across CEX + on-chain perps.  
- **Catalyst calendar** (airdrops, network launches, major upgrades).  
- **Depth/spread/fees** (10–50 bps depth).  
- **Tokenomics** (unlocks vs. sinks; real demand).

---

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Rotation, not magic:** without **BTC.D/ETHBTC** signals and **fresh stables**, rallies fade fast.  
- **Layered risk control:** position sizing + unlock calendar + market depth.  
- **Pick durable themes** (RWA, DePIN, infra) for a **better risk/reward** than pure memes.  
- **Pre-plan exits:** alt cycles are **faster & more volatile** than BTC/ETH rotations.
