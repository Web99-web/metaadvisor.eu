**Briefly:** **Flipkart** (owned by **Walmart**) is in late-stage **HQ relocation** from **Singapore to India** to prepare for a **domestic IPO** (potentially **2026**). Per TechCrunch, a Singapore court has granted **in-principle approval**, and multiple **NCLAT** hearings have taken place in India. The transition is targeted for **the coming months**, aligned with India’s **festive season** — a crucial sales window.

### Where things stand
- **Singapore:** court **in-principle** approval for relocation.  
- **India:** several **NCLAT** hearings; **completion by year-end** is the goal.  
- **IPO path:** gearing up for a **domestic IPO**, earliest **2026**, subject to regulators and market conditions.

### Why now — and why India
- **Local capital & valuation:** a domestic listing taps **local institutions & retail**, often with **higher multiples** for the “India growth” story.  
- **Index inclusion & flows:** greater odds of **index inclusion** and **passive inflows**.  
- **Regulatory alignment:** closer alignment with **Indian tax and regulatory** frameworks.

### Wider context: PhonePe, Zepto, Groww
- **PhonePe** (spun out in 2022) already **relocated** and this week **filed confidentially** with **SEBI** (~**₹120B** / **$1.35B** raise).  
- **Zepto** and **Groww** have also **shifted HQ**; **Groww** could be **first** to list domestically after moving.  
- Flipkart’s move follows **Google’s $350M** investment (~**$1B** round) about a year ago.

### What HQ relocation entails
- **Group structure:** shifting the **holdco & SPVs** under **Indian law**.  
- **ESOP & tax:** potential **ESOP restructuring** and careful management of **tax implications**.  
- **Regulatory choreography:** coordinating **Singapore** and **India** bodies (court, **NCLAT**, possibly **MCA/SEBI**) so the IPO stands on **Indian legal footing**.
