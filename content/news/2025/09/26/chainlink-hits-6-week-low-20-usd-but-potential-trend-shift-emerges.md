**Briefly:** **LINK** fell to around **$20**, the lowest since early August, now **~−28%** below its August peak amid broader market weakness. Notably, the drop comes **despite net buying**: wealth manager **Caliber (CWD)** added **~$4M** in LINK (now **~$10M** total), and the **Chainlink Reserve** — a facility that uses integration/service revenues to buy tokens and remove supply from the open market — purchased **~47,903 LINK** (~**$1M**) on Thursday; since August it has accumulated **>370,000 LINK** (**~$7.5M**).

### Market picture & key levels
- **$20 support**: repeatedly tested; buyers are defending the zone for now.  
- **~$20.57 resistance**: first **trigger** for a short-term **trend shift** if reclaimed.  
- **Momentum**: ~**−4%** over 24h; elevated but within recent range.

### Who’s buying into weakness?
- **Caliber (CWD):** another **$4M** as part of a **digital asset treasury** program; **~$10M** LINK in total.  
- **Chainlink Reserve:** **~47,903 LINK** on Thursday; **>370k LINK** (**~$7.5M**) since August → **supply withdrawal** from spot.

### How to read it (technicals + flows)
- **Bull case (near term):** hold **$20** + push through **$20.57** = **signal** of a short-term reversal.  
- **Bear case:** failure at **$20.57** and a fresh break below **$20** brings lower supports into view.  
- **Flows vs. price:** even with **treasury/reserve** bids, broader **risk-off** can dominate short-term direction.
