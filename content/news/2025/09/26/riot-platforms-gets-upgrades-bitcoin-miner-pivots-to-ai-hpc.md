**Riot Platforms (RIOT)** received a **double upgrade** on Friday as **JPMorgan** and **Citigroup** turned more positive amid shifting mining economics and Riot’s push into **high-performance computing (HPC)** and **AI-adjacent services**.

- **JPMorgan:** *neutral → overweight*, target **$15 → $19**; calls Riot the **most attractive** among miners.  
- **Citigroup:** *neutral → buy*, target **$13.75 → $24**.  
- Shares **outperformed** a weak sector, down **1.2% to $16.55** at print.

### Peers and moves
- **IREN:** cut to *underweight* (−9.7% today; still **+300% YTD**).  
- **CleanSpark (CLSK):** to *neutral* (−9.3%; **+34% YTD**).  
- **Cipher Mining (CIFR):** *buy* kept, target **doubled to $12** (−3.5% at **$11.20**).  
- **MARA Holdings (MARA):** *overweight* kept, target **trimmed to $20** (from $22).

### HPC as the near-term driver
**JPMorgan** assigns **50% odds** that **Riot, Cipher and IREN** land **HPC colocation contracts** soon, benchmarking the **800-MW Core Scientific (CORZ) × CoreWeave (CRWV)** deal. Implied valuations: **$3.7–$8.6M per MW**.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **AI/HPC diversification** repurposes existing infra and cushions the **mining cycle**.  
- **Riot** is rewarded for **early positioning**; markets want **revenue beyond pure mining**.  
- **Risks:** execution of HPC contracts, CapEx intensity, and **BTC price volatility** shaping sentiment/funding.
