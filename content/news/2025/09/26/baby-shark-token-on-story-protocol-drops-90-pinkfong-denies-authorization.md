The **Baby Shark token** promoted on **Story Protocol** as an *official* coin tied to YouTube’s most-viewed video **collapsed 90%** after brand owner **Pinkfong** (Seoul) stated on X that it has **no connection** to the project.

The price fell from **$0.35** to below **$0.00064**. Despite a peak valuation near **$200M**, the issuer **IP.World** admitted it relied on **faulty rights** from a purported licensee and said its verification process **blocked creator fees**.

### What is “Baby Shark,” anyway?
**Baby Shark** is a two-minute animated kids’ song released in **2016** by **Pinkfong** (SmartStudy, South Korea). It became a **global phenomenon** with **16B+ views**, the **most-viewed YouTube video** ever, spawning a franchise of **shows, concerts, toys, books and brand deals**.

### Why the confusion?
- **IP.World** believed it had authorization — it turned out **invalid**.  
- Pinkfong says only **two crypto assets are official**:  
  1. *Baby Shark Meme* on **Solana**  
  2. *Baby Shark Universe Token* on **BNB Chain**  
  Everything else is **unauthorized**.

### Market action & on-chain findings
- Influencer endorsements and Story Protocol promotion fueled **hype**.  
- **Bubblemaps** reported one entity **sniped ~7%** of supply (≈ **$10M**) in the **first minute** via multiple fresh wallets.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Big brand ≠ legit token:** with IP-heavy brands, **rights chain and approvals** are non-negotiable.  
- **Investor takeaway:** famous kids’ IPs are frequent **bait** — always verify **“official”** claims (source, license, imprint).  
- **Regulatory angle:** the mix of **IP misuse** and **speculative tokens** invites scrutiny.
