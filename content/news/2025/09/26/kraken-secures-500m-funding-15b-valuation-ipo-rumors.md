U.S. crypto exchange **Kraken** has reportedly raised **$500 million** in fresh capital, pushing its valuation to **$15 billion**. The round has intensified speculation about a potential **initial public offering (IPO)**, a topic long debated in the crypto industry.  

The funds are expected to support Kraken’s **expansion into new markets** and the development of **additional financial products**. Industry observers see the raise as a signal that Kraken aims to strengthen its standing among the world’s leading crypto platforms.  

If realized, an IPO would make Kraken the second major U.S. crypto exchange to go public after Coinbase. Analysts note that the timing is delicate: the market remains volatile, yet institutional demand is steadily rising.
