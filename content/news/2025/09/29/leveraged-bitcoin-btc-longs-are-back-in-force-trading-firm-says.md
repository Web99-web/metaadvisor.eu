**TL;DR:** Traders are re-establishing **aggressive long exposure** in **Bitcoin perpetuals**. **QCP Capital** reports rising **open interest** and **funding rates** across centralized and decentralized exchanges — a clearly **bullish** tilt.

### Key numbers
- **BTC price:** around **$114,142.91** at report time.  
- **Open interest (perps):** up from **$42.8B** to **$43.6B** (modest, but shows **fresh inflows**).  
- **Funding rates:** up to **13% annualized** on **Deribit** and peers — **positive** rates mean **longs pay** shorts to keep positions.  
- **DEX sentiment:** **Hyperliquid** long bias back to **57%** (from **36%** last week).  
- **Last week:** sharp sell-off (dip below **$109k**) triggered **>$700M** long liquidations — **largest single-day** in at least six months (per **Coinglass**).  
- **Recovery:** price back near **$114k**.

### Reading funding & OI
- **Funding > 0:** market skew **to longs**; carrying longs gets **costlier** as rates rise.  
- **Higher OI:** more exposure on the table; if trend flips, **liquidations** can accelerate.

### Why are longs returning?
- **Q4 seasonality:** historically constructive for crypto.  
- **Narratives:** ETFs/policy/AI flows support **risk-on** positioning.  
- **Technical reset:** post-flush leverage is **lighter**, enabling re-build of long risk.

- **Signal vs. cost:** Elevated funding is bullish *and* a drag — often precedes **quick flushes**.  
- **Leverage risk:** After **$700M** in liquidations, position sizing, **stops**, and hedges matter more than the exact top call.  
- **Watch:** abrupt funding swings, basis dislocations, and one-sided **OI** build-ups.
