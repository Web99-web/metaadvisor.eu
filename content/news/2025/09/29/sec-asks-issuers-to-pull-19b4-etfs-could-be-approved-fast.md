The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has asked crypto ETF issuers to withdraw their **19b-4 filings**, opening the door to a much faster approval process after new rules removed a key regulatory hurdle.  

Under the updated framework, exchanges can now **list crypto ETFs without lengthy case-by-case reviews**. Issuers only need to file an **S-1 registration statement**, describing the structure and strategy of the ETF. This could mean approvals in a matter of **days**, though no guarantees exist.  

### What is an ETF?  
An ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund) is a **basket of assets** (stocks, bonds, crypto) traded on exchanges. It allows investors to gain exposure to an asset without holding it directly. **Spot crypto ETFs** track the real price of coins like BTC or ETH, making it easier for institutions to invest.
