**TL;DR:** ChatGPT can dramatically **accelerate** the path from “too many projects” to a **shortlist**. The edge isn’t a “magic answer” but **good filters**, **strong prompts** and **hard validations** (on-chain, tokenomics, liquidity).

## 1) Set the frame (filters)
- **Theme:** DePIN, AI, L2, RWA, gaming, infra …  
- **Stage:** mainnet/testnet; **MC** e.g. $50M–$2B; **liquidity** $5–10M min; **ecosystem:** EVM/Solana/Cosmos …

> Prompt (EN):  
> *“Give me 20 projects in [SECTOR], MC $50M–$2B, active GitHub and real TVL. Group by chain and add 1–2 metrics (TVL, daily volume, active wallets).”*

## 2) Quick digest & red flags
For each candidate ask for **10 bullets** + **risks**:  
admin key, proxy/upgradeable, no audit, shallow pools, top-10 holders > 60% …

> Prompt:  
> *“Summarize project X docs in 10 bullets. List 5 biggest risks (on-chain/legal/operational) and 3 metrics to verify manually.”*

## 3) Tokenomics & unlocks
Table: **circ vs. max**, **FDV/MC**, **unlock schedule**, allocations, **utility**.

> Prompt:  
> *“Compute FDV/MC, show 12-month unlocks and estimate quarterly supply pressure.”*

## 4) Liquidity & micro-signals
Check **DEX/CEX depth**, spread, volume; holder concentration; social anomalies.

> Prompt:  
> *“Write a liquidity checklist: min depth for $X entry with ≤1% slippage, min daily volume, max top-10 concentration.”*

## 5) On-chain security checks
Owner privileges, proxy upgrade, **non-renounced**, audits, similar exploits.

> Prompt:  
> *“List ERC-20 on-chain red flags (owner, proxy, pausable, fee-on-transfer, blacklist) and briefly explain each risk.”*

## 6) Competition & differentiation
Compare with 3–5 peers: price/GPU-hour, TPS/fees, dev activity, partnerships.

> Prompt:  
> *“Compare X vs Y & Z (table). Add key advantage, weakness and KPI.”*

## 7) Scoring & decision
Scorecard (0–5) for team, tokenomics, liquidity, security, traction, competitiveness, narrative. **GO/WAIT/NO-GO** threshold e.g. 22/35.

> Prompt:  
> *“Generate a 0–5 scorecard for X across 7 categories and what would improve the score in 3 months.”*

## 8) Watchlist & risk-first entry
Watchlist **5–10** names; enter **in tranches**; max **1–3%** per high-risk alt; set **invalidations**.

> *Not financial advice. AI can be wrong. Always verify numbers via official sources and on-chain explorers.*
