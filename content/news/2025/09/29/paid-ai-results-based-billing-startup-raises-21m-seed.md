## What Paid is (and isn’t)
**Paid** doesn’t ship AI agents. It ships the **billing rails** for companies that build them. The premise: charge **for delivered outcomes** (“**results-based billing**”) rather than per user or unlimited usage — pricing models that **break** when agent workloads spike and model/cloud costs are variable.

- **Round:** $21.6M **seed** (oversubscribed), on top of a ~€10M **pre-seed** in March → **$33.3M** raised to date.  
- **Valuation:** reportedly **$100M+**.  
- **Why now:** agentic apps are moving real work (ops, sales, CX) but **unit economics** suffer under legacy pricing.

## How it works (at a glance)
- **Outcome tracking** (time saved, cost avoided, revenue created, tasks completed).  
- **Outcome → price mapping** (e.g., % margin saved, per closed deal, per successful workflow).  
- **Background attribution** so “quiet agents” can prove value and bill.  
- **Plumbing** for data, storage, payments and distribution (web/App Store).

## Glossary
- **Results-based billing:** pricing based on **realized value** rather than seats or raw usage.  
- **AI agent:** software that plans, calls tools/APIs, and executes multi-step tasks autonomously.

## Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **Compelling PMF vector:** If Paid makes **value proof + billing** trivial, agent startups can flip from burn to **positive margins**.  
- **Category leverage:** It’s a **picks-and-shovels** layer; less risk than betting on a single agent vertical.  
- **Execution risks:** standardizing outcomes across verticals, **ERP/CRM integrations**, and disputes over **value attribution**.  
- **Industry signal:** monetization is shifting from “all-you-can-eat” to **“pay for what worked.”** Whoever measures and bills that transparently will have **edge**.
