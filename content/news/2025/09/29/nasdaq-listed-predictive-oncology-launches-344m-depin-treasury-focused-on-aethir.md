**TL;DR:** **Predictive Oncology** is launching a **$344 million** **DePIN treasury** focused on **Aethir (ATH)** — a network for **decentralized GPU/AI compute**. DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks) pairs real-world infrastructure (GPU nodes) with token incentives and market liquidity.

### What we know
- **Treasury size:** about **$344M** allocated to **ATH**, node operations and liquidity positions.  
- **Why Aethir:** a distributed **GPU cloud** for AI/ML training and inference.  
- **Potential yields:** staking/locking, **market-making**, and enterprise contracts for **GPU-hours**.  
- **Business synergy:** as a health/AI company, Predictive Oncology can accelerate its own workflows via **cheaper, flexible** access to compute.

### Why it matters
- **DePIN validation:** a **publicly traded** firm stepping in signals mainstream interest beyond crypto natives.  
- **AI × crypto convergence:** chronic GPU scarcity favors **decentralized supply models** with token economics.  
- **ATH liquidity:** a sizable treasury can stabilize **liquidity** and streamline enterprise onboarding.

### Watch next
- **Allocation mix:** **ATH tokens** vs. **hardware/nodes** vs. **liquidity**.  
- **Risk:** token volatility, **lock-ups**, node ops and **SLA** reliability.  
- **Accounting & compliance:** how a listed company books treasury/token positions.
