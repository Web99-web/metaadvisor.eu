## What happened
The world’s largest asset manager, **BlackRock**, now holds approximately **$66M** of Bitcoin exposure in its **Global Allocation Fund** — not directly, but via the **iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT)**, a **spot BTC ETF**. For multi-asset funds, this is the most practical way to gain Bitcoin exposure without introducing direct custody or new operational frameworks.

## Why ETF instead of “real” BTC?
- **Mandates & regulation:** Many funds are allowed to hold **securities (ETFs)**, but not **direct crypto assets**.  
- **Operational reasons:** ETF takes care of custody, valuation, reporting, and liquidity inside the **standard brokerage system**.  
- **Accounting/tax:** Easier mark-to-market and reporting, without managing private keys.

## Market significance
- **Institutional signal:** Bitcoin is moving from “alternative” to a **mainstream portfolio component** (alongside equities, bonds, gold).  
- **Demand growth:** As more mandates allow ETFs, **institutional Bitcoin demand grows**.  
- **Lower entry barrier:** For pensions, endowments, and wealth managers, ETFs are often the **only approved channel**.
