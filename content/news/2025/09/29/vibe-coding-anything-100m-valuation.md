**Anything**, an AI app launched just a month ago, reached a **$100 million valuation** after raising an $11 million funding round. The startup hit a **$2M ARR in just two weeks**, showing the explosive rise of so-called *vibe coding* — building apps and websites through natural language prompts.  

### 🚀 The market context
- **Lovable** hit $100M ARR within 8 months and is aiming for $1B ARR next year.  
- **Replit** skyrocketed from $2.8M to $150M ARR in under a year.  

### ⚡ The gap
Most vibe coding platforms excel at **prototyping**, but fall short when it comes to **production-ready software**.  

### 💡 Anything’s approach
Anything provides a **full-stack toolkit** — databases, storage, payments — enabling users to launch real businesses or publish apps directly.  

### 🔎 Our take (MetaAdvisor)
- Vibe coding may become the **“no-code 2.0 revolution”**.  
- Explosive growth is attracting investors, but sustainability depends on delivering **real-world production value**, not just demos.
