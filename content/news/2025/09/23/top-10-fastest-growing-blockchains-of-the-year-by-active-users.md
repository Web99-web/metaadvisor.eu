**TL;DR:** Looking at **active on-chain users** often gives a more realistic picture of adoption than market cap alone. Networks that let people act **quickly, cheaply and with minimal friction** tend to grow the fastest—especially L2s and high-throughput L1s where micro-transactions (gaming, payments, mini-apps) are common.

### What does “active user” mean on-chain?
Analysts typically track:
- **DAU/MAU** trends and **cohort retention** (new vs. returning),
- **Transaction count & types** (transfers, DEX swaps, NFT mints, payments),
- **Costs & confirmation times** (less friction = more usage),
- **Cross-app activity** (how many dApps a user touches).

Methodologies differ, yet the pattern is similar: networks with **easier onboarding** (social login, MPC/AA wallets), **lower costs** (L2s, cheaper L1s) and **tangible utility** consistently outperform.

### Why these networks grow
- **L2s & low-cost layers:** rollups and performant L1s enable frequent, small transactions without “gas pain”.
- **Payments & stablecoins:** where **real money** moves (P2P, remittance, merchants), DAU/MAU scales steadily.
- **Mini-apps & social distribution:** embedding into super-apps and social platforms onboards millions.
- **Games & on-chain assets:** F2P/NFT economies generate many small tx—perfect for low fees.
- **Incentives (airdrops):** short-term spikes; long-term winners provide value **without** incentives.
- **Tooling ecosystems:** strong SDKs, indexers, RPC and data lakes attract devs → apps → users.

### How to read these rankings (and not get misled)
1. **Retention first:** new addresses are good; returning users create lasting value.
2. **Filter farmers/bots:** the **DAU : tx** ratio and post-incentive activity are key.
3. **Cost + UX:** low fees and seedless onboarding are pivotal for mainstream.
4. **Watch real activity:** payments, games, DEX, mini-apps—each vertical has its own health metrics.

### Top 10 fastest-growing blockchains (by active users)
*(based on the cited overview; ordering and figures shift as measurement windows roll)*

1. **Solana (L1)** — DeFi, NFTs and high-frequency/memecoin flows; *Firedancer* boosts stability.
2. **Near Protocol (L1)** — UX-first, low fees, AI-native integrations; strong organic inflows.
3. **BNB Chain (L1)** — massive base, EVM compatibility, quick blocks; strong CEX on-ramps.
4. **Base (L2, Ethereum)** — ultra-low fees + Coinbase distribution; rapid MAU growth.
5. **TRON (L1)** — cheap stablecoin transfers, high throughput; consistently high tx counts.
6. **Bitcoin (L1)** — ETF narrative + network security; expanding L2/ordinals use cases.
7. **Aptos (L1)** — Move language, fast finality; DeFi expansion, stablecoin partnerships.
8. **Ethereum (L1)** — deepest dev ecosystem; user activity often migrates to L2 for cost reasons.
9. **Polygon (L2/L1 stack)** — interoperability and enterprise deals; ongoing shift toward 2.0.
10. **Arbitrum One (L2, Ethereum)** — low fees, strong DeFi (e.g., perps), Stylus upgrade for devs.
