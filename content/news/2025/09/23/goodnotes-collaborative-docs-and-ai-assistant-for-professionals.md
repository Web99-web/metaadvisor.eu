**TL;DR:** **Goodnotes** expands from education into **professional teams**. New features include a **collaborative whiteboard**, **document creation** (text, images, GIFs, tables) and an **AI assistant** that can summarize meetings, generate visuals (charts/diagrams), proofread text and propose **templates** for notes/documents.

### What the Goodnotes AI does
- **Multiple inputs, one context:** handwriting, typing, **sketches** and **voice**.  
- **Summaries & content prep:** **meeting summaries**, suggested **structure** and **visuals** (charts/diagrams).  
- **Proofreading & style:** grammar/clarity fixes and tone adjustments.  
- **Templates:** quick **templates** for minutes, plans, brainstorming and project boards.

### Collaborative whiteboard & documents
- **Real-time whiteboard:** teams collaborate on a **blank canvas** (text + diagrams).  
- **Rich documents:** **text**, **images**, **GIFs** and **tables** — shifting from notes app to a lightweight **DMS workhorse**.

### Tech from an acquisition
Last year, Goodnotes acquired a South-Korean startup focused on **meeting/video summaries**; that tech now powers **transcripts, summaries and highlights** in the AI feature set.

### Bigger picture: all-in-one productivity
Goodnotes joins **Grammarly** and **Canva** in nudging users to **create documents inside the app**. More in-app context → **better AI assistance**. The goal: be useful **beyond edtech**, across **devices** and in the **office**.

### Who is it for?
- **Teams** wanting a **lightweight, fast** tool for minutes and brainstorming.  
- **Professionals** who mix **handwriting + typing** (planners, creatives, consultants, managers).  
- **Prosumers** needing AI that **adapts to input style** and helps before/during/after meetings.

### Plans & availability
Two new plans — **Goodnotes Essentials** and **Goodnotes Pro** — replace version numbering. Pricing/availability may vary by region and app store; check the **official Goodnotes page** for up-to-date details.
