**TL;DR:** Data shows **calendar week 38** is historically the **third-worst** for **bitcoin**, averaging about **−2.25%** (only weeks **14** and **28** are worse). - **Seasonal pattern:** Weeks **14** (−3.91%), **28** (−2.78%) and **38** (−2.25%) stand out negatively.

- **Perpetual funding** has dropped to about **4% (still positive, but low)** — implying **reduced demand** for **leveraged longs**.  
- **Implied volatility (IV)** is near **historic lows (~37)**, pointing to a **calmer** regime with risk of **sudden breaks** on catalysts.

### Broader context: September and Q3 performance
- Despite the weak week, **September** is still **+4%**, and the **quarter** is **+6%**. Historically, **most remaining weeks** of the year skew **positive** — a possible **calm before volatility**.

### Capital rotations: gold and AI/HPC stocks
- **Gold** keeps rallying (**+1%** Tuesday; **>+42% YTD**), sapping risk appetite away from BTC.  
- **AI/HPC stocks** (e.g., IREN) attract speculative capital, **stealing some shine** from bitcoin short term.
