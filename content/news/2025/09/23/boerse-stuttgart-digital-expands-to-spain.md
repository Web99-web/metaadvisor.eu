**TL;DR:** **Boerse Stuttgart Digital**, the crypto arm of the **Stuttgart Stock Exchange Group**, has opened a **Madrid** office, extending its footprint to **eight European hubs** (including Frankfurt, Zurich and Milan). It provides **crypto trading and custody** for **banks, brokers and asset managers**, aligned with the EU’s **MiCAR** regime.

### MiCAR license & regulatory posture
- Germany’s **BaFin** granted an early **pan-European MiCAR license** to Boerse Stuttgart this year.  
- Institutions get a **compliant** route to roll out **crypto services**.

### Why Spain – why now?
- Studies suggest that by **2025** more than **50%** of Spain’s population could use **crypto**.  
- **Spanish banks** are known for **innovative digital services** — ideal partners for regulated access.  
- The company is already in **talks with major Spanish banks**.

### Modular platform for institutions
- **Plug-in crypto services** for retail and institutional offerings.  
- **Custody + trading** on one platform.  
- **Scalable** design aligned with EU regulation.

### Broader EU expansion
- Beyond Germany/Switzerland, hubs in **Italy, Spain** and other centers.  
- Goal: become a **leading provider** for regulated institutional crypto in the EU.
