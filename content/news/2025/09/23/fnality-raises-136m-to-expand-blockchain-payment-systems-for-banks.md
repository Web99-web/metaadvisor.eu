**TL;DR:** **Fnality** (London) builds **on-chain payment and settlement infrastructure** for banks, using tokenized representations of major currencies **collateralized with central bank money**. The **$136M Series C** is led by **WisdomTree, Bank of America, Citi, KBC Group, Temasek and Tradeweb**, with existing backers **Goldman Sachs, UBS, Barclays** participating. Proceeds will fund **new currencies**, **liquidity tools** and **settlement of tokenized assets** (securities, stablecoins).

### What Fnality actually delivers
- **DLT payment rail:** bank positions on a **permissioned blockchain**; funds are **central-bank-money-like** (CB collateral).  
- **Real-time settlement:** **Delivery-versus-Payment (DvP)** and **Payment-versus-Payment (PvP)**.  
- **Capital & liquidity gains:** faster settlement reduces **RWAs**, **intraday credit** and improves **netting**; e.g., **repo** can close **instantly** rather than T+0/T+1.

### Why banks care
- **Fewer intermediaries & lower risk:** shorter operational chains.  
- **Lower cost & faster capital turn:** cash and securities are **immediately reusable**.  
- **Regulatory alignment:** designed to fit **RTGS/CB** environments required for **wholesale** scale.

### Who’s behind the round — and why
- **New leads** (WisdomTree, BofA, Citi, KBC, Temasek, Tradeweb) signal **institutional distribution**: from **ETF/AM** use cases to **treasury** and **FX**.  
- **Existing investors** (Goldman, UBS, Barclays) reinforce the **standardization** thesis across major dealers.

### What’s next
- **Beyond GBP:** add **new currencies** and **interoperability**.  
- **Liquidity tooling:** **intraday** moves, **auto-sweeps**, smarter **collateral management**.  
- **Tokenized assets:** **DvP/PvP** as the first **tangible use case**.

### Bigger picture: tokenization & infrastructure modernization
Banks and issuers increasingly **tokenize deposits and securities** to enable **24/7 settlement**, finer-grained **risk control**, and **programmable rules** (escrow, conditional payments). Fnality targets the **wholesale** layer (B2B/banks/intermediaries) as a **complement** to future **CBDC rails** and existing **RTGS**.
