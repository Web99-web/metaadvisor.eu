**Quick take (morning of Sept 23):**  
Over 24h the market saw **fresh forced liquidations** (mostly longs) totaling **≈ $1.6–1.9B**. **Bitcoin** **wicked** to **~$111.xk** (venue-dependent) before stabilizing back above **$112k**; **ETH** slipped toward **$4.0–4.2k**. *Image is symbolic.*

### What exactly happened
- **Derivatives pressure:** elevated leverage and **funding** made books fragile — a small nudge triggered **cascades**.  
- **Off-hours liquidity:** thinner books overnight amplified moves.  
- **ETF flows:** one or two **soft net-flow days** sapped the **spot bid** when the slide began.

### Why now (drivers)
1) **Too much leverage:** the flush **reset** parts of **OI** and **funding**.  
2) **Macro tone:** a **firm USD (DXY)**, **Fed** speakers and **Friday’s PCE** kept **risk-off** in play.  
3) **Technical breaks:** loss of fresh support zones tripped **stops**.

### How much was “lost”
- **Perps liquidations:** **≈ $1.6–1.9B** in 24h; **hundreds of thousands** of accounts impacted.  
- **Total crypto mcap:** down by **tens of billions USD** during the sell-off (depending on snapshot).

### What crypto geeks are saying
- A **classic leverage wash** — **painful short-term**, often **healthy** as it clears overstretched longs.  
- **Technical > fundamental:** BTC/ETH narratives intact, but without a **spot bid** and with a **firm USD**, dips run deeper.  
- **Watchlist:** **ETF net flows** (need **2–3 up-days**), **spot vs. perps** mix, **funding/OI** normalization.

### Next 48–72h
- **Funding & OI:** need **normalization** to reduce cascade risk.  
- **Spot participation:** durable rebounds need **spot demand**, not just perp bounces.  
- **USD/Fed/PCE:** a softer **DXY** + calmer Fed tone would ease **risk-on**.

- **Base case:** after the leverage reset, expect **choppy consolidation** until **spot** and **ETF inflows** improve.  
- **Tactics:** prefer **reclaim/retest** entries with tight risk; be cautious with **high-beta** alts.  
- **Strength triggers:** **2–3 days of positive ETF flows** + a softer **USD**.
