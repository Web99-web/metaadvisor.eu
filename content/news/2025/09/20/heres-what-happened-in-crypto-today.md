**At a glance:** The day was defined by **infrastructure and rule-setting**. Custodian **BitGo** moved toward an **IPO**, the U.S. Treasury launched an **ANPRM** to implement the **GENIUS Act** (stablecoins), **Ethereum** scheduled the **“Fusaka”** upgrade for December, and the **SEC** approved **generic listing standards** for crypto ETPs. In parallel, **FTX** prepared another creditor distribution while markets remained **cautious** after the Fed’s rate cut.

### 1) BitGo heads for an IPO
Custody provider **BitGo** readied a **NYSE IPO** (ticker **BTGO**), reporting **$4.2B H1 2025 revenue** and **~$90B** in assets on platform — reinforcing the public-markets turn for crypto infrastructure.

### 2) Stablecoins: GENIUS Act moves into practice
The **U.S. Treasury** opened an **advance notice of proposed rulemaking** on the **GENIUS Act**. Topics include **issuer prohibitions**, **sanctions/AML**, **reserve custody**, **state–federal balance**, and **tax**. Public comments run to **Oct. 20**.

### 3) Ethereum: ‘Fusaka’ in December
**Core developers** set **Dec 3, 2025** for **Fusaka** (12 EIPs), with subsequent plans to **expand blob capacity** — aiming to lower rollup costs and raise throughput.

### 4) ETFs: Faster on-ramps to exchanges
The **SEC** approved **generic standards** for listing **spot crypto ETPs** on **Nasdaq, Cboe BZX and NYSE Arca**. If objective tests are met, an individual 19b-4 filing is no longer required. Expect **many new products**, though flows will likely be **uneven**.

### 5) Market tone: Cautious after the cut
Post-Fed, derivatives desks (e.g., **Deribit**) show rising **put demand** (downside protection). **Blue chips** hold up relatively well while **altcoins** occasionally froth.

### 6) FTX: Third distribution on Sept 30
The **FTX Recovery Trust** targeted **~$1.6B** in new creditor payouts on **Sept. 30**, after earlier rounds returned **>$6B**. Disbursements depend on completed **claims-portal verification**.

### 7) People & headlines
**Kevin Durant** regained access to a long-locked **Coinbase** account, sparking a broader debate on **account recovery/2FA**. Separately, **Anatoly Yakovenko** (Solana) urged **Bitcoin** to prepare **post-quantum** cryptography by **2030** — many view the risk as distant, but the discussion is worthwhile.

---

**Our take (Metaadvisor):** Today was about **pipes and policy**. A custodian IPO and simplified ETF listings widen **capital channels**, while the GENIUS Act aims for **predictable** stablecoin rules. Ethereum’s roadmap keeps **driving costs down**. Markets look **risk-on with seatbelts** — filter short-term noise through **structural trends** in infrastructure and regulation.
