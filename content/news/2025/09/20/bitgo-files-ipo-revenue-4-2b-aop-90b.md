**At a glance:** BitGo, a leading crypto custodian, has filed an **S-1** for an **NYSE IPO** (ticker **BTGO**). The filing shows **$4.19B revenue in H1 2025** (vs. ~$1.12B a year earlier) and **$12.6M net income** (H1 2024: $30.9M). The company reports **~$90.3B** in **assets on platform (AoP)** and **1.14M users**. A **dual-class** structure gives **Class B** holders (incl. CEO **Mike Belshe**) **15 votes** per share, qualifying BitGo as a **controlled company** under NYSE rules.

### Key figures from the filing
- **Revenue:** $4.19B in H1 2025 (~4x YoY).  
- **Profitability:** $12.6M net income (down from $30.9M in H1 2024).  
- **Full-year 2024:** $3.08B revenue; $156.6M net income ($54.1M attributable to common).  
- **AoP:** ~$90.3B; **concentrated** in five assets: **BTC 48.5%**, **SUI 20.1%**, **SOL 5.7%**, **XRP 3.9%**, **ETH 3.0%**.  
- **Listing venue:** **NYSE**, ticker **BTGO**.

### Why it matters
A custodian’s IPO underscores **institutionalization** of digital assets on public markets. The dip in net income points to **cost and investment pressure**, while AoP concentration highlights **asset-specific volatility risk** (notably BTC and SUI).

**Our take (Metaadvisor):** BitGo brings tangible **fee revenue** to crypto infrastructure, but margins hinge on the cycle and compliance spend. For investors, watch **AoP flows**, **revenue mix** (fees, staking, services) and **concentration risk**, rather than day-to-day token moves.
