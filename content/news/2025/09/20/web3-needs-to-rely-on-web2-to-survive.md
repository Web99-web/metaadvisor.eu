*Image is symbolic.*

**Summary:** While Web3 aims to be an open alternative, **most real user flows still run through Web2**: sign-in (email/Apple/Google), **fiat on-ramps**, **app stores** and ad channels, hosting/CDNs, anti-bot and trust-&-safety tooling. Rather than “either/or,” the market favors **dual architectures**: a decentralized core + Web2 UX and distribution that ushers users toward on-chain value.

### Why Web3 still leans on Web2
- **Identity & onboarding:** WebAuthn/passkeys, “Sign in with …,” **social logins**, and **MPC/custodial** wallets reduce friction; self-custody arrives **later** (upgrade path).  
- **Distribution & billing:** **App Store/Play** rules, web payments, email/subscriptions and CRM remain key for reach and monetization.  
- **Payments & on-ramp:** KYC/AML, **fiat↔crypto** bridges and chargeback policies are (still) Web2 territory.  
- **Infrastructure & DevOps:** Cloud + **CDN** + observability (logs/alerting) ensure **uptime** and fast rollbacks; content mirrored on **IPFS/Arweave**.  
- **Security & T&S:** Anti-bot, rate-limit, fraud-scoring and compliance tools come from the Web2 ecosystem.

### A realistic path forward (pragmatic playbook)
1. **Dual stack:** On-chain core (transactions, ownership, verifiability) + Web2 layer for **UX & distribution**.  
2. **Progressive decentralization:** move from **custodial/MPC** to **self-custody** and social recovery as users mature.  
3. **Compliance by design:** build **KYC/AML**, logging and jurisdictional rules in from day one — **invisible** to users.  
4. **Frictionless UX:** 1–2 step login, **embedded wallet**, auto security upgrades (limits/2FA) as value grows.  
5. **Hybrid storage:** public artifacts on **IPFS/Arweave**, but **cache/CDN** for speed; plan for **pinning** and failover.  
6. **Observability & incident response:** 24/7 metrics (latency, tx success, on-ramp conversion), a “kill switch” for bad releases.  
7. **Clear path to self-custody:** education, seed/passkey backups, **account abstraction** and affordable gasless flows.

### Broader implication
Users want **Web2 simplicity** and **Web3 properties** (true ownership, interoperability). Teams that blend both — and measure **real usage** (not just TVL/volume) — will win on acquisition, retention and regulatory durability.

---

**Our take (Metaadvisor):**
- **UX before ideology:** without Web2 channels, there’s no mass adoption; **progressive decentralization** is the pragmatic compromise.  
- **Centralization risks:** reliance on cloud/app-stores and on-ramps creates **failure points** — mitigate via multi-provider strategy and on-chain fallbacks.  
- **Metrics that matter:** track **on-ramp conversion, active on-chain users, retention**, not just price charts.  
- **Regulatory realpolitik:** integrated KYC/AML and geofencing reduce shutdown risk.  
- **Endgame:** once on-chain critical mass forms, parts of the Web2 layer can be **replaced** by decentralized alternatives without sacrificing UX.
