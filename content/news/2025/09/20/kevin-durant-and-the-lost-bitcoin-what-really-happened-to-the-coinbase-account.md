## What actually happened — a timeline
- **2016:** After a dinner at **Ben Horowitz’s (a16z)**, Durant and **Rich Kleiman** buy BTC for the first time (estimated entry ~**$650/BTC**).  
- **2016–2025:** **No access** to the Coinbase account/wallet (**user error**); no selling because **no login**.  
- **Sep 2025:** On **CNBC Game Plan**, Kleiman details the struggle; shortly after, **Brian Armstrong** (Coinbase CEO) posts on X: “**We got this fixed. Account recovery complete.**”  
- **Now:** Access restored; **position size undisclosed**.

## Why can recovery take years?
Even with a **custodial** account (no seed phrase), typical blockers include:
- **Lost password + lost/changed 2FA device** (no backup codes).  
- **Defunct email** (reset not possible).  
- **Failed ID verification** (mismatched data, expired documents).  
- **Security locks** triggered by repeated attempts or device/IP changes.  

Standard steps are **password reset**, **2FA recovery**, and **re-KYC**; exchanges often impose a **24–48h cooldown** after credentials are reset. If you’ve lost **both email and 2FA**, the case turns **manual** — and can drag on.

## Was there VIP treatment?
Because the resolution came **after media attention**, some inferred **preferential handling**. Coinbase shared no process details; what’s clear is the case was **resolved**, amid broader user complaints about **access/support** delays.

## How much money is it?
With BTC near **$116,000** and an estimated 2016 entry around **$650**, that’s **>17,700%** “on paper.” But **stake size is unknown**, so all figures remain **speculative**.

## A practical checklist for users
1. **Password manager** + **2FA backup codes** (consider **passkeys**).  
2. Keep **email/phone** current; **test a reset annually**.  
3. **KYC docs**: valid, legible, and exactly match account details.  
4. For larger balances: **multiple 2FA methods** + a **trusted device**; expect a **cooldown** after resets.  
5. If using self-custody: **seed/SSS/social recovery** and a clear storage/estate plan.

---

**Our take (Metaadvisor):** Access hygiene matters as much as custody security. A lost password, 2FA and an old email can effectively freeze legitimate funds; recovery then depends on patience, solid identity evidence — and sometimes on public visibility.
