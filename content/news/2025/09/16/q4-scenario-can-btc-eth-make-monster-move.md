**TL;DR:** Tom Lee (Fundstrat) is bullish for the next three months. Rather than echo the quote, here’s a **scenario guide**: the **fundamental & liquidity triggers** that could propel BTC/ETH in Q4, and the **risks** that could smother it.

## BULL CASE — What could power BTC/ETH in Q4
- **1) Policy & real yields:** a softer central-bank stance and/or falling real rates → stronger risk appetite.  
- **2) ETF flows:** persistent **net inflows** to spot BTC/ETH ETFs; custody locks coins and **shrinks the float**.  
- **3) Crypto liquidity:** rising **net stablecoin issuance**; declining **exchange reserves** for BTC/ETH.  
- **4) On-chain health:** higher **active addresses**, **L2 volumes** and **gas usage** (ETH) = organic demand.  
- **5) Q4 seasonality & narratives:** “Uptober,” year-end fund windows, weaker USD (DXY) often supports risk.

## BEAR/RISK CASE — What could stop it
- **1) Stronger USD & rising real yields.**  
- **2) Negative ETF flows/outflows.**  
- **3) Regulatory/geopolitical shocks.**  
- **4) Excess leverage → cascade liquidations.**  
- **5) Technical overhang** at key resistance zones.

## 90-Day Watchlist
- **ETF net flows** (BTC first, then ETH).  
- **Net stablecoin issuance.**  
- **Exchange reserves** (downtrend = positive).  
- **Funding/basis** (avoid euphoric extremes).  
- **DXY & real yields** (weakening = plus).  
- **Calendar:** FOMC, CPI/PCE, payrolls; Ethereum L2 & staking.

## Scenarios (at a glance)
- **Bull:** softer macro + positive ETF flows + rising on-chain demand → *trend acceleration*.  
- **Base:** mixed signals, range with slight upward bias.  
- **Bear:** stronger USD/rates + ETF outflows + shock → lower-level tests, risk-off.
