**Nothing**, led by **Carl Pei** (ex-OnePlus), announced a **$200M Series C** round **led by Tiger Global**, putting the company at **~$1.3B valuation**.

Participating investors include **GV**, **Highland Europe**, **EQT**, **Latitude**, **I2BF** and **Tapestry**, with new strategic backing from **Nikhil Kamath** and **Qualcomm Ventures**. This brings Nothing’s **total funding to $450M+**.

Tiger Global partner **Matt Watcher** said Nothing is **reimagining hardware and software with an AI layer** to prepare products for the next era of personal tech.

The company will **open another community round** soon; past community raises totaled **$11.5M**. With its **design differentiation**, Nothing reported **over $1B in cumulative sales** earlier this year and says it’s on a **path to profitability**.

Nothing adds that it has built the **supply chain and infrastructure** to launch a product within months and ship globally. Following the **Phone (3)** and its signature transparent design, the firm is now teasing an **“AI-first” device** as the next step—bringing design, performance and **on-device AI** closer together.
