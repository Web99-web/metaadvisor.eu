**TL;DR:** BTC (**~$115.8k**) defended **$115k** on Tuesday morning in Asia after a strong start to the week. With **spot ETF inflows** and hopes of **rate cuts** next week, sentiment is mixed: **is the recovery fragile or is crypto back on track?**

### Market drivers (last 24–48h)
- **ETF flows:** several consecutive days of **net inflows** to BTC spot ETFs; **ETH** posts its biggest two-week inflow.  
- **Macro expectations:** markets lean **dovish**, boosting risk appetite.  
- **Altcoin rotation:** **XRP** and **SOL** show strength; the **Altcoin Season Index** sits at a **90-day high**.

### What desks say: fragility vs. “back on track”
- **Glassnode — fragility focus:** Despite **ETF inflows** and rising **futures OI**, the **spot bid** looks thin.  
  - With **~92% of supply in profit**, **profit-taking** is rising; **funding** has softened ⇒ **greater vulnerability** to shocks.  
  - **Reduced downside hedging** compresses **implied vols**, but also **raises shock exposure**.  
- **QCP — “back on track”:** After a no-surprises **CPI** and multiple days of **BTC ETF inflows**, the team sees **consolidation above $115k** as a **launchpad** for higher-beta rotation.

### Today’s operating picture
- **Level to watch:** **$115k** as the line to defend; above it, scope for a **$115–118k** range with breakout attempts.  
- **Early risk:** **selling pressure** if spot flows fail to follow derivatives; volatility can expand quickly.  
- **Confirmation:** sustained **ETF net inflows**, lower **exchange reserves**, and **steady funding** without euphoria.

### Our take (Metaadvisor)
- **ETFs set the pace; spot must confirm:** without a visible spot bid, any pullback can be **sharp**.  
- **Sentiment ≠ structure:** a high share of supply in profit is **double-edged** — it invites profit-taking.  
- **Plan for the day:** track **ETF net flows**, **DXY/real yields**, and **net stablecoin issuance**; above $115k the bias is **cautiously positive**, but **data-dependent**.
