**TL;DR:** Michael Saylor, MicroStrategy co-founder, evolved from a public skeptic (2013) into Bitcoin’s most vocal corporate advocate. The turning point came in 2020 as the firm adopted BTC as its **primary treasury reserve asset**, kicking off a multi-year accumulation strategy.

### How it started — context & timeline
- **Early doubts (2013):** Saylor questioned Bitcoin’s durability.  
- **View shift (2018–2020):** As infrastructure matured (custody, regulation, liquidity), institutional interest grew.  
- **Trigger (2020):** With real yields negative, cash eroded; BTC emerged as a store of value.  
- **First buy (Aug 2020):** MicroStrategy acquired **21,454 BTC (~$250M)** and defined Bitcoin as its **primary reserve**.  
- **Financing strategy:** Additional buys via cash, convertibles and ATM equity; **DCA** mitigated volatility.  
- **Effects:** Company became the corporate BTC-treasury reference; Saylor the face of the **digital-gold** narrative.

### Why it matters
1. **Corporate precedent** for BTC in treasury policy.  
2. **Capital structure as a lever** to scale exposure.  
3. **Clear storytelling** enabled broader understanding and adoption.

### Market implications
- **Standardizing practice** of BTC in diversified treasuries.  
- **Liquidity & cycles** amplified by large entrants yet partially “lock” supply.  
- **Long-term > short-term noise** if discipline and liquidity hold.
