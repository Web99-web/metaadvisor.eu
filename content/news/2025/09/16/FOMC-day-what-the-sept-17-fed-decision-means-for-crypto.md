**TL;DR:** On **Wednesday, Sept. 17**, the FOMC releases its rate decision at **18:00 UTC (2:00 p.m. ET)**, followed by **Powell’s press conference** at **18:30 UTC (2:30 p.m. ET)**. The event moves **USD, real yields and liquidity**, so crypto often overreacts. Consensus is a **25 bp cut**, but the **tone** and **projections (dot plot)** usually carry the bigger signal.

### What is the FOMC?
The FOMC is the Fed’s **Federal Open Market Committee**, which sets **U.S. monetary policy**: the target range for the **federal funds rate**, the stance of **QE/QT**, and forward guidance. It comprises **7 Governors** on the Board and **12** regional Federal Reserve Bank presidents; **5 vote** at any time (New York Fed is permanent; four seats rotate). The committee meets **eight times per year** on a regular schedule.

### What’s already priced
- **Cut expectation:** the market **discounts** 25 bp; surprises come via **guidance** and **projections** for the path ahead.

### Scenarios & likely impacts
**1) Dovish cut (25 bp + soft guidance)**  
USD/real yields lower → **risk-on**; in crypto, stronger **spot bid** with scope for rotation into higher beta.

**2) Hawkish cut (25 bp + optionality retained)**  
USD/real yields flat/higher; crypto: initial pop, then cool-off — without spot flows the move fades.

**3) Hold**  
USD/real yields up → **risk-off**; crypto retests lower levels; ETF flows must cushion the blow.

**4) 50 bp**  
If seen as proactive, short-term **risk-on**; if read as reacting to weakness, **risk-off** after the first spike.

### Crypto desk checklist (T-0 to T+48h)
- **DXY & real yields (TIPS 5y/10y)** — weakening = risk-positive.  
- **FedWatch repricing** across upcoming meetings.  
- **Spot vs. derivatives** — is the move **spot-led** (ETF flows, exchange reserves ↓) or **leverage-led** (funding/basis ↑)?  
- **Vol/hedging** — skew and put demand post-presser.  
- **Stablecoin liquidity** — net issuance as a quick gauge of fresh firepower.

### From scenario to plan
- **Reaction > prediction:** the first hour is often a whipsaw; confirm via **DXY/real yields** and **spot flows**.  
- **Step-out logic:** on risk-on, scale out in **steps** rather than chasing the perfect top.  
- **Invalidation:** define before the event (sharp USD/real-yield reversal or a collapse in spot demand).
