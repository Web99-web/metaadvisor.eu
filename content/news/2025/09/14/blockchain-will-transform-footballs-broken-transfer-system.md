**The pitch**  
Using **blockchain** and regulated **crypto rails** (stablecoins, on-chain escrow) could cut transfer settlement from days/weeks to **minutes**, **reduce cross-border costs** and bring **end-to-end transparency** to fees, bonuses and add-ons.

**How it would work**  
- **On-chain escrow** locks funds and releases them when conditions are met (signing, registration, ITC).  
- **Stablecoin settlement** replaces slow SWIFT wires while keeping **KYC/AML**.  
- **Programmable bonuses** trigger **automatic payouts** when performance clauses are hit.

**Why it matters**  
- On **deadline day**, faster settlement lowers the **failure risk** due to banking delays.  
- **Smaller clubs** benefit from cheaper, faster cross-border flows.  
- **Transparency** makes **FFP** supervision and audits easier.

**An FFP “ledger”**  
A shared **on-chain registry** for associations/UEFA would surface the **real-time obligations** behind transfers and fee schedules.

**What’s in the way**  
Clear **regulatory frameworks**, common **data standards**, alignment with **capital controls**, and upgraded **back-office** processes.

## Our take
The realistic path is **hybrid**: **stablecoin settlement via licensed providers + on-chain escrow**, with FFP-grade transparency. If **UEFA/FIFA** and major leagues align, this could evolve into an **industry standard** within a few seasons.
