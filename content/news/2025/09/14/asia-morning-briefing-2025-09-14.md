**1) Japan: Path to a 20% flat crypto tax (by 2026)**  
Reports point to the **FSA** pushing for a **flat 20%** tax on crypto gains, potential re-classification as a financial product, and tweaks to insider-trading/loss-carry rules. Not law yet — but the **policy direction** implies **2026**.

**2) Hong Kong: Stablecoin regime now operational (Aug 1) + early initiatives**  
**Licensing for fiat-referenced stablecoins** is in effect; **HKMA/SFC** have warned against misleading licensing claims as **no official licenses** have been granted yet. Meanwhile, local banks/fintechs are lining up **HKD-stablecoin** efforts.

**3) South Korea: Global crypto data sharing (OECD CARF)**  
Seoul is implementing **CARF**: foreigners’ trades on Korean exchanges will be shared with their home tax authorities; residents’ offshore trades will be reported to the **NTS**. Data collection in **2026**, exchange in **2027**.

**4) Pakistan: Debate over crypto and “hawala”**  
Former FBR chief **Shabbar Zaidi** flagged that crypto could act as a **hawala** substitute, reviving **KYC/AML** concerns in the region.

**5) Market context (overnight)**  
BTC around **$115.7k** ahead of the **FOMC** and after a sticky **CPI** print. Tone remains cautiously positive; in Asia, rotation between **stablecoin/regulatory** themes and **treasury-stock** proxies (e.g., Japan/Metaplanet).

## Our take
- **Japan**: If a 20% flat tax and re-classification land, **Tokyo** could attract **ETF flows** and institutional capital next cycle.  
- **Hong Kong**: Ambitious framework, likely **tight licensing**. Credible **HKD/USD** options would boost **on/off-ramp** capacity in APAC.  
- **Korea**: **CARF** reduces arbitrage and lifts transparency — neutral near-term, **positive** long-term.  
- **Market**: With **FOMC** in focus, Asia remains **policy-driven**; keep an eye on **FSA/HKMA** notices and the first stablecoin licenses.
