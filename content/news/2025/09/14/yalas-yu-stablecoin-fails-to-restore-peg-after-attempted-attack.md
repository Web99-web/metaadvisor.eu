**What happened?**  
On Sunday, September 14, Yala’s stablecoin **YU** **depegged** — it dropped below $1, bounced, but **failed to hold parity**. The team described the event as an **“attempted attack”** on the protocol.

**What are Yala and YU?**  
**Yala** is a DeFi protocol where users mint the **YU** stablecoin against **Bitcoin collateral**, targeting **1 YU ≈ $1**. The design relies on over-collateralization and peg-maintenance mechanisms.

**Immediate actions**  
Some features (e.g., conversions/bridge) were **temporarily paused** as a precaution, and **SlowMist** is assisting with the **investigation**. The team has stated that **user funds are safe**, with a detailed **post-mortem** to follow.

**Where things stand**  
YU is still **trading below $1** with elevated volatility. The market awaits the official findings and a **stabilization plan** (e.g., re-collateralization, buybacks or parameter tweaks).

## Our take
If the project is **substantively built** and the incident is **well-scoped**, there are **credible paths to re-peg** (additional collateral, treasury measures, parameter adjustments). That said, there’s **no guarantee** — outcomes hinge on root-cause analysis and execution. Until clarity arrives, **reducing exposure** and monitoring official updates (Yala/SlowMist) and on-chain flows is prudent.
