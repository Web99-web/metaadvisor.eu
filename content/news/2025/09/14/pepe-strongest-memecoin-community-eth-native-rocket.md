**Why PEPE?**  
Because the **community** doesn’t sleep. What started as a meme became a **cultural + market engine**: memes → attention → liquidity → new participants → more memes. That network flywheel has kept PEPE **at the front of multiple cycles**.

### From hype to enduring format
- Early on, PEPE hit **multi-billion valuations** and set off full-blown meme waves.  
- The “PEPE” brand travels across X/Telegram/Reddit — a **content format** that repeatedly rekindles momentum.

### Liquidity & volume — why it matters
PEPE remains among the **most liquid memecoins**: broad listings, deep books, and steady rotation make **entry/exit** easier than with seasonal memes. That’s why, in every meme up-cycle, **PEPE naturally lines up first**.

### Tied to ETH: often higher beta
PEPE is an **ERC-20** on **Ethereum**. When **ETH** runs (ETF headlines, risk-on), part of the profits and leverage rotates into the **strongest meme narratives** — PEPE frequently delivers **outsized moves**:  
- **ETH up → PEPE often up more** (narrative + perps/leverage).  
- **ETH down → PEPE often down more** (beta cuts both ways).

## Why people say “memecoins are next” — stated smartly
- **Rotation logic:** typical order is BTC → ETH → large-cap alts → **memes**. Once ETH is moving, **PEPE**, as the “meme blue-chip,” often attracts **the first wave**.  
- **Liquidity edge:** for fast beta exposure, capital seeks **deep books** — PEPE outclasses many smaller memes here.  
- **Signal, not a promise:** “Next” doesn’t mean **every day** or **every coin**. Wait for **ETH trend**, **PEPE volume/funding/OI**, and **community rhythm** to align before sizing up.

### Risks
- **Volatility is a feature:** higher beta = bigger swings both ways.  
- **Imitators/clones:** multiple copies on other chains — always verify the **correct ERC-20 contract**.

## Our take
PEPE is a **case study in network effects**: community is the engine, **ETH** is the rail, and liquidity is the fuel. If the “alts then memes” narrative plays out, PEPE has **structural advantages** to lead the pack. Keep FOMO in check: use **staged entries/exits**, watch **liquidity**, and respect **risk**.
