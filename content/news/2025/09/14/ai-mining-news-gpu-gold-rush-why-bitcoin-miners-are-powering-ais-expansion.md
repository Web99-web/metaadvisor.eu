**What’s happening**  
Earlier this year **Core Scientific** inked a **$3.5B** agreement to **host AI data centers** — a pivot from pure ASIC fleets toward **GPU clusters** and steadier, contract-based revenue. Peers such as Hut 8 and TeraWulf are pursuing similar **AI/HPC conversions**.

**Why miners fit**  
- Existing **low-cost power** (hydro, stranded gas) via PPAs.  
- Mastery of **high-density cooling** (immersion/liquid).  
- Pre-built **interconnects, substations and permits**.

**Unit economics**  
- With mining margins thin after the 2024 halving, **AI colocation** offers **multi-year MW contracts** and predictable cash flow.  
- Many sites keep a **hybrid mix**: some capacity on **BTC**, some on **AI** depending on power prices and load.

**Constraints**  
- **GPU supply** and long lead times.  
- **Grid/permit bottlenecks** (transformers, interconnect).  
- **Customer concentration** and AI demand cyclicality.

## Our take
It’s a rational redeployment of strengths — **power, cooling, 24/7 ops**. For investors it’s diversification; for the ecosystem it accelerates AI build-out. Keep **flexibility** (BTC/AI switching) and **CAPEX discipline** front and center.
