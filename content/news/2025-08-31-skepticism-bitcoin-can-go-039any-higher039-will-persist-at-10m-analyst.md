A well-known crypto analyst has said that skepticism about Bitcoin’s price potential will persist no matter how high it goes — even if BTC were to reach **$10 million per coin**.  

He pointed out that throughout history, investors have always found reasons to doubt Bitcoin’s long-term potential. From the days when it seemed unlikely to be worth more than $1, through milestones of $1,000, $100,000 and beyond, the same “overvalued” narrative has echoed.  

“Whether at $100, $1,000, $100,000 or even $1M, the same question comes up: can it go higher? And this won’t change even at $10M,” he said.  

The analyst also emphasized that markets move in cycles and that human psychology plays a decisive role. Skeptics often fuel selling pressure, while long-term investors benefit from accumulation phases.  

His conclusion: skepticism will never disappear — but that very doubt is what, in his view, keeps Bitcoin a healthy and resilient market.
