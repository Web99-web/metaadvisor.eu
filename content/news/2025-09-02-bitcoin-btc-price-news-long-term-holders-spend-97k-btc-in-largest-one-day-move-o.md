Long-term bitcoin holders have stepped up sales in recent weeks, adding to the **bearish momentum** in the market.  

On Friday, they offloaded **97,000 BTC** (nearly $3 billion), marking the largest single-day long-term holder sell-off of 2025, according to **Glassnode**.  

The 14-day moving average of coins spent by long-term holders has risen to almost **25,000 BTC**, the highest since January. Glassnode defines these holders as wallets that have kept coins for more than 155 days.  

Bitcoin’s price dropped over 3.7% to $108,000 on Friday and slid further to $107,400 by Monday. At the time of writing, BTC was trading around $103,330 — still 16% below its all-time high of $124,429.  

Analysts note that the market may need time to adjust to the **$100,000 level as the new normal**, likely resulting in range-bound trading around six figures for some time.  

**Our comment:** Heavy selling by long-term holders pressures the market short term but also reflects a healthy maturation — very few assets in the world trade above $100,000 per unit.
