**Robinhood (HOOD)** shares surged 7% in after-hours trading following the news that the crypto-focused fintech has been added to the **S&P 500**.  

The stock climbed from just above $101 to over $108 per share, with year-to-date gains of more than 150%. According to a press release from **S&P Dow Jones Indices**, Robinhood will join the index on September 22, alongside **AppLovin**.  

By contrast, **Strategy** (formerly MicroStrategy), with a $95 billion market cap and over $70 billion worth of **Bitcoin holdings**, was not included. Its shares dropped nearly 3% after hours.  

The S&P’s decision highlights the growing weight of digital-asset-focused companies, which have benefitted from a more favorable political and regulatory environment in recent months. This shift has fueled institutional interest in cryptocurrencies, boosting the prices of **Bitcoin, Ethereum**, and other leading assets, alongside large inflows into crypto-based ETFs.

## Our take
Robinhood’s inclusion in the S&P 500 underscores its evolution from a simple trading app into a major fintech and crypto player. For Strategy, being left out despite strong results and massive Bitcoin reserves shows that markets remain cautious toward firms whose models depend almost entirely on the volatility of a single asset. The message is clear: **diversification and consistent growth** matter more than pure crypto exposure.
