Dogecoin (DOGE) is testing a critical support zone near **$0.214** after a week of heightened volatility.  

Analysts note that trading volume has expanded during price declines — often a signal of distribution — but strong accumulation by whale wallets has so far defended the support level.  

Speculation about a potential **Dogecoin ETF** in 2025 has fueled short-term rallies, though resistance near **$0.22** and **$0.25** continues to cap upside momentum.  

On-chain data shows DOGE remains one of the most actively traded altcoins, with over 1.2 million daily transactions, keeping it firmly positioned as the leading meme coin.  

Market watchers caution that forming *lower highs* is typically a bearish signal, but any breakout above **$0.25** could trigger fresh momentum, particularly if ETF-related rumors gain credibility.
