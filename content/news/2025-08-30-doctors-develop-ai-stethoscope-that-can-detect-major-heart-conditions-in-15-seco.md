Doctors have created an **AI-driven stethoscope** capable of diagnosing three major heart conditions in just 15 seconds.  

Developed by researchers at **Imperial College London** and the **NHS trust**, the upgraded stethoscope analyzes subtle variations in heartbeat and blood flow undetectable to the human ear, while simultaneously performing a rapid ECG.  

The breakthrough was presented at the **European Society of Cardiology Congress** in Madrid, the world’s largest cardiology meeting.  

In a clinical trial with 12,000 patients, those examined with the AI stethoscope were twice as likely to be diagnosed with heart failure and three times more likely with atrial fibrillation, compared with patients assessed without the tool. Early detection could speed up life-saving treatments and prevent severe illness.
