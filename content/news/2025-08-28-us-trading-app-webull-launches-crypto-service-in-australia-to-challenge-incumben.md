The U.S. trading app **Webull** has launched crypto services for Australian users, giving them access to hundreds of different digital assets. Experts say the move will pressure domestic exchanges to cut fees or risk losing customers.

The launch is facilitated through **Coinbase Prime**, making Australia Webull’s third crypto market after the U.S. and Brazil. The company has promised a 30 basis point spread, according to a statement.

Industry watchers believe Webull’s entry will spark competitive pressure across the Australian crypto exchange sector, where fees have remained high compared to international peers.

Pratik Kala, head of research at Apollo Crypto, told Decrypt:  
> “Australian exchanges have significantly higher fees and spreads than our international peers; in some cases, more than double, even for liquid pairs like Bitcoin and Ethereum. Webull’s entry with 240 coins will greatly expand options for Australians.”

On Monday, Webull also announced it had resumed crypto trading in the U.S., after suspending services in 2023 during its public listing process. CEO Anthony Denier called it part of a “full-throttle” global expansion into digital assets.

James Volpe, founding director of uCubed, said Webull faces entrenched competition:  
> “Established players in Australia will fight to defend their share. But competition in such a young market usually drives down fees and adds new features for users.”

### Context:  
Webull’s expansion could reshape the cost structures in Australia’s crypto market, benefiting retail investors and forcing incumbents to innovate.
