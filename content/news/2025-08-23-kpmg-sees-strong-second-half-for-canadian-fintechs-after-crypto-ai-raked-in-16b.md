Canadian fintechs raised $1.62 billion in H1 2025, with digital-asset and AI startups taking the lion’s share, according to KPMG Canada’s *Pulse of Fintech*.

While global fintech funding cooled, Canadian investors maintained steady support for ventures at the intersection of finance and emerging tech. The report highlights companies building blockchain infrastructure and AI-driven financial tools as leading growth areas.

“If you look at the first half of 2025, it’s clear digital assets re-emerged as a magnet for investor interest despite the broader contraction in VC values,” said Edith Hitt, a partner at KPMG Canada.

A more constructive U.S. regulatory tone and rising institutional participation are also reshaping sentiment. Still, the $1.6B tally is down year-over-year: $2.4B in H1 2024 and $7.5B in H2 2024, reflecting macro headwinds such as tariffs and higher rates.
