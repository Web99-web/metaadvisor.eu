**Scale AI**, a company that helps tech firms prepare data to train AI models, has filed a lawsuit against former employee **Eugene Ling** and competitor **Mercor**.  

The lawsuit alleges Ling stole over 100 confidential documents containing customer strategies and proprietary data. It also claims he attempted to pitch Mercor to one of Scale’s largest clients, referred to as “Customer A,” before leaving his job.  

Mercor co-founder **Surya Midha** denied using Scale’s data but admitted Ling may have had old documents in his personal Google Drive. He said Mercor never accessed them and offered to resolve the matter by destroying the files, but Scale opted to proceed legally.  

Scale argues that the documents contained critical information that would allow Mercor to approach Customer A and other key clients.
