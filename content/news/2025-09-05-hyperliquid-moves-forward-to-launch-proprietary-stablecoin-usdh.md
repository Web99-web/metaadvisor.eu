The decentralized exchange **Hyperliquid (HYPE)** is preparing to launch its own U.S. dollar-pegged stablecoin, according to a Friday announcement from the Hyperliquid Foundation on the platform’s Discord server.  

The protocol has reserved the ticker **USDH**, which validators will soon vote on through an on-chain governance process. Teams interested in deploying USDH can submit proposals, and the winning group will be chosen by validator quorum.  

> “The USDH ticker is well-suited for a Hyperliquid-first, Hyperliquid-aligned, and compliant USD stablecoin,” the announcement said.  

Stablecoins are a critical piece of crypto market infrastructure, providing liquidity and serving as trading pairs to settle most transactions. It’s a **$270 billion asset class**, currently dominated by **Tether’s USDT** and **Circle’s USDC**.  

More players are creating their own tokens: **MetaMask** is launching a stablecoin with infrastructure provider M0, while **Stripe** has built its in-house stablecoin Bridge.  

According to **DefiLlama**, Hyperliquid processed **$398 billion** in perpetual derivatives and **$20 billion** in spot trading volume last month. USDC currently accounts for **95%** of the **$5.6 billion stablecoin supply** on the network.  

By introducing its own stablecoin, Hyperliquid could reduce its dependency on Circle and capture revenue from the assets backing the token.

## Our take
Hyperliquid’s move fits into a broader trend where leading crypto projects are launching proprietary stablecoins to secure greater control over liquidity and revenue. If USDH succeeds, it could reduce USDC’s dominance on the network and spark more competition in the stablecoin market.
