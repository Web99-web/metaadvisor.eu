**Dogecoin (DOGE)** saw a 4% intraday swing but managed to close 1% higher at **$0.213**, despite sharp volatility.

Trading volume surged 21% above weekly averages, reflecting strong market participation. Key drivers include **ETF speculation** and expectations of upcoming **Fed rate cuts**.

Analysts note that DOGE is forming a **triangle pattern**, with a breakout above **$0.22** potentially paving the way for further upside momentum.

On the broader market, traders warn of a potential **12% monthly drop for Bitcoin**, even as **Solana (SOL)** led major altcoins with recent strong gains.

Historically, September has been a weak month for crypto, but renewed institutional interest and ETF chatter could offset seasonal bearishness.

DOGE remains highly sensitive to hype and momentum flows. Clearing the $0.22 resistance could trigger another rally, but Bitcoin’s September risks may still weigh heavily on the broader crypto market.
