**Michael Saylor’s Strategy (MSTR)** was not included in the **S&P 500 Index** on Friday, despite the company posting one of the strongest quarters in its history and meeting all the requirements for inclusion.  

The stock of the largest corporate holder of **Bitcoin (BTC $110,726.08)** fell nearly 3% after hours, erasing the day’s gains. Inclusion in the S&P 500 would have been a major milestone, exposing the company to millions of retail investors and portfolio managers, and serving as a strong signal of confidence for the broader industry.  

Instead, in a surprise move, the digital trading platform **Robinhood (HOOD)** was added to the prestigious index, sending its stock up 7% after the market closed. The changes will take effect on September 22.  

Until now, only two crypto-linked stocks were part of the index: crypto exchange **Coinbase (COIN)** and Jack Dorsey’s **Block**. Robinhood now joins this exclusive list, reinforcing its crypto ambitions and growing influence in financial markets.

## Our take
Robinhood’s inclusion in the S&P 500 shows that markets value its role at the intersection of traditional trading and crypto. For **MSTR** and Michael Saylor, this is a missed opportunity — despite record results and its position as the largest corporate Bitcoin holder, the company remains outside the U.S.’s most important stock index.  

This highlights that **market sentiment and investor perception** matter as much as financial performance. Robinhood is recognized for its broad user base and growth potential, while MSTR remains tied closely to Bitcoin’s volatility.
