**DeFi Development Corp (DDC)** has further increased its position in **Solana (SOL)**, with its treasury now surpassing **$400 million**. This makes DDC one of the largest institutional holders of Solana and signals growing trust in the blockchain.  

### Why Solana?
Over the past two years, Solana has emerged as the **fastest-growing layer-1 blockchain**, known for speed and low fees. It is especially attractive for:  
- **DeFi applications** – DEXs, lending, yield farming.  
- **NFT marketplaces** – Solana is among the top networks for NFT trading.  
- **Developer ecosystem** – with more than 2,000 active developers building on Solana.  

### Key metrics
- **Total Value Locked (TVL)** exceeds **$15 billion**, more than double last year.  
- **SOL price** has surged over **350% in the past 12 months**, holding above **$210**.  
- **Institutional investors** are diversifying into Solana as momentum in **Bitcoin (BTC)** and **Ethereum (ETH)** ETF markets slows.  

### Bigger picture
Bitcoin remains **digital gold**, Ethereum is the **DeFi foundation**, and Solana is positioning itself as the **third force** — faster, cheaper, and more scalable.

DDC’s treasury expansion shows that Solana is no longer a niche project. If this trend continues, Solana could stand alongside Bitcoin and Ethereum as a core component of the global financial system.
