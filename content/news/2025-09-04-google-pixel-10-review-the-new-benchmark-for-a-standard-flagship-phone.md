Google’s new Pixel 10 has been upgraded with more cameras, a faster chip and quality software, lifting it out of the shadow of its pricier Pro siblings to set a new standard for what to expect from a base-model flagship phone.  

The regular Pixel 10 costs £799 (€899/$799/A$1,349) – the same as last year’s Pixel 9 – undercutting the 10 Pro by £200 and matching rivals from Samsung and Apple while offering more for your money.  

The design is almost identical to the Pixel 9, except for some bold new colours and the all-important third camera on the back. The satin aluminium and glass body feels like a quality piece of hardware and stands out in a sea of lookalike phones. The 6.3in OLED screen is sharp, super-bright and smooth with a 120Hz refresh rate.  

The glass back hides another upgrade: Qi2 wireless charging and magnetic accessory support. Qi2 is an open standard and cross-compatible with MagSafe accessories made for Apple, including chargers, wallets and stands.  

Specs:  
- Camera: 48MP + 13MP ultrawide + 10.8MP 5x telephoto; 10.5MP selfie  
- Connectivity: 5G, eSim, Wi-Fi 6E, NFC, Bluetooth 6, GNSS  
- Water resistance: IP68 (1.5m / 30 minutes)  

The new Tensor G5 chip is about 34–38% faster than the previous generation and brings enhanced AI features.
