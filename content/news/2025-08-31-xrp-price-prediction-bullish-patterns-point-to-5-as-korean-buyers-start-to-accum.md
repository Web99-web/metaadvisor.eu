XRP drew analyst attention after sliding from **$3.02 to $2.89** between Aug. 28–29 on heightened volumes, before stabilizing around the **$2.83–$2.89 support zone**. Oversold conditions and whale accumulation helped relieve the selling pressure.  

Technical analysts highlighted the emergence of **bullish patterns** pointing to a potential move toward **$5**, especially as Korean buying activity ramps up.  

Exchange data indicated that large investors have started accumulating XRP again, strengthening the case for a recovery.  

If momentum continues, XRP could retest the **$3.50 level** in the near term, with longer-term models projecting a target around **$5**.
