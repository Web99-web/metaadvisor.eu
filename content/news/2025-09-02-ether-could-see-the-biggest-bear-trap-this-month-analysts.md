Ether (ETH) has seen a sharp price pullback at the start of September, but analysts warn this may form the “biggest bear trap” of the month.  
The sudden drop below key support levels attracted shorts, yet many argue it’s a temporary shakeout before a potential rally.

Technical indicators suggest Ether maintains a medium-term uptrend. A hold above $2,800 and a breakout past $3,200 could quickly send ETH toward $3,500.  
Market sentiment also shows oversized short positions, raising the risk of a sudden short squeeze.

Analysts conclude that while the downturn looks like a bearish continuation, it could actually set the stage for a strong upside move.
