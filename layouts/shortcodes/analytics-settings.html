Cookie policy

This section explains how MetaAdvisor uses browser storage and Google Analytics. You can read our explanation before choosing your settings at the end of this page. Reading articles does not require accepting optional analytics.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text records stored by your browser for a website. They can remember a preference or help an analytics service recognise repeat visits. Session storage and local storage are other browser features we use to remember privacy choices. They are distinct from the article content you read.

Required privacy preferences

MetaAdvisor stores your analytics choice so the site can respect it across pages and languages. These preference records do not enable advertising profiles. Our consent controller uses the names ma_analytics_choice, ma_analytics_visit and ma_consent_v2 for preference storage; a fallback may be used when a storage method is unavailable.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 measures visits, page use and referrals. Where prior permission is required, its script is not loaded until you agree. In previously reviewed regions where our configured analytics may start without prior permission, it can start automatically, with an option to turn it off. Unknown or unreviewed locations use prior permission. Language selection does not determine your location.

When enabled, Google Analytics may create cookies such as _ga and _ga_ followed by a property identifier. Our configuration limits their lifetime to 180 days. Our page commands omit URL query strings, fragments and form contents. Analytics permission does not grant advertising permission.

How long is a choice remembered?

Acceptance is remembered for six calendar months. Outside the regional retention exceptions described above, a refusal does not hide the banner on subsequent page loads. France, the UK and unresolved locations retain refusals for six months. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track keep analytics off. Older undated refusals are converted to an expiring refusal record, never into acceptance.

Changing or withdrawing your choice

Use the optional checkbox below and select Save settings. An empty checkbox keeps analytics off; checking it allows analytics unless your browser sends a privacy signal. Turning analytics off stops future analytics commands and removes the analytics cookies handled by our controller. It does not erase information already processed. Closing the banner does not count as acceptance.

Browser controls

You can also review or delete this website’s cookies and stored data in your browser’s privacy or site-data settings. Blocking preference storage may prevent the site from remembering your choice. Private browsing is not a guarantee that all cookies or analytics are blocked. Deleting cookies does not by itself change data already held by a service provider.

Hosting security logs and third-party services have separate purposes and policies; this analytics control does not claim to manage every third-party cookie. For questions about personal data, use the Contact page.
